# PROMISSORY OATHS ACT 1956

AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ADMINISTRATION OF PROMISSORY OATHS

## 

### s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Promissory Oaths Act 1956.

### s 2 Form of oaths

The several oaths required to be taken under this Act shall be in the forms set forth in Schedule 1.

### s 3 Persons to take and tender oaths

The several oaths set out in the first column of Schedule 2 shall be taken by the respective persons named in the second column of the said Schedule as soon as may be after their appointment to or acceptance of office, and shall be tendered by the respective persons named in the third column of the said Schedule.

### s 4 Avoidance of unnecessary repetition of oaths

Notwithstanding the provisions of section 3, no person shall be required, upon appointment or acceptance of office, to take any oath if he or she shall, on any previous occasion, have taken such oath within Fiji, either in respect of the same or any other appointment or office, unless the President shall otherwise direct.

[s 4 am LN 112 of 1970 O 2, effective 8 October 1970]

### s 5 Consequences of not taking prescribed oaths

Nothing in this Act contained shall render, or be deemed to render or be deemed to have rendered, invalid any act done or which hereafter may be done by a person holding a public office in Fiji in the execution or intended execution of his or her official duties by reason only of the omission by such person to take any such oath which by reason of the provisions of this Act such person should take or have taken, provided that, if any person declines, neglects or omits, where any oath to be taken under this Act is duly tendered, to take such oath he or she shall, if he or she shall have already entered on his or her office, vacate the same, and, if he or she shall not have already entered on his or her office, be disqualified from entering on the same.

### s 6 Cases in which affirmation is allowed in lieu of oath

When an oath is required to be taken under this Act, every person for the time being by law permitted to make a solemn affirmation or declaration instead of taking an oath may, instead of taking such oath, make a solemn affirmation in the form of the oath hereby appointed, substituting the words “solemnly, sincerely and truly declare and affirm” for the word “swear” and omitting the words “So help me God”.

### s 7 Name of the Republic of Fiji to be used in oaths

When in any oath under this Act the name of the State is expressed, the name of the Republic of Fiji shall be substituted from time to time.

[s 7 am Act 14 of 1975 s 2, effective 30 May 1975; Act 31 of 2016 s 171, effective 1 December 2016]

### s 8 Oaths to be recorded and attested

Every oath taken in pursuance of this Act shall be recorded in a book kept for the purpose and the person taking the oath shall at the time thereof be required to place his or her signature in such book and the person tendering the said oath shall attest the same in the book, and such book shall be conclusive evidence for all purposes that any oath therein recorded has been duly taken and recorded.

### s 9 Saving

Nothing in this Act shall affect any other oath required or authorised by law to be taken or made.

### SCHEDULE 1 FORMS OF OATHS

[Sch 1 subst Ordinance 14 of 1966 s 2, effective 20 June 1966; am LN 112 of 1970 O 2, effective 8 October 1970; Act 31 of 2016 s 171, effective 1 December 2016]

**OATH OF ALLEGIANCE**

I, , swear that I will be faithful and bear true allegiance to the people and the Republic of Fiji, according to law. So help me God.

**OFFICIAL OATH**

I, , swear that I will well and truly serve the people and the Republic of Fiji, according to law, in the office of

(here insert the description of the office)

So help me God.

**OATH OF SECRETARY TO THE CABINET**

I, being called upon to exercise the functions of Secretary to the Cabinet, do swear that, except with the authority of the Cabinet, I will not directly or indirectly reveal the business or proceedings of the Cabinet or the nature or contents of any document relating to such business or proceedings or any matter coming to my knowledge in my capacity as Secretary to the Cabinet. So help me God.
