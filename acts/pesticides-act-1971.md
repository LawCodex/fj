# PESTICIDES ACT 1971

AN ACT TO REGULATE THE REGISTRATION AND SALE OF PESTICIDES

**Table of Amendments**

Pesticides Act 1971 (No 41 of 1971) commenced on 1 April 1972, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Pesticides (Amendment) Act 1976 (No 32 of 1976) | 3 December 1976 |

## s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Pesticides Act 1971.

## s 2 Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

**adjuvant** means any product intended for use or used as an aid to the application or effectiveness of a pesticide and includes stickers, spreaders, emulsifying agents and synergists;

**crops** includes trees;

**Director** means the Director of Agriculture;

**pest** means any insect, rodent, bird, fish, mollusc, nematode, fungus, weed, micro-organism, virus or any other kind of plant or animal life which is injurious, troublesome or undesirable to crops and stores products;

**pesticide** means any product intended for use or used for controlling a pest, or any adjuvant intended for use or used in connection with any such product;

**Register** means the Register of Pesticides; and

**Registrar** means the Registrar of Pesticides.

## s 3 Register of Pesticides

(1) The Registrar shall keep a Register of Pesticides, in which shall be entered the trade name of all registered pesticides, their chemical names and percentages of active ingredients or acid equivalents as appropriate, the name and place of business of the manufacturer and the name and place of business of the importer.

(2) Every pesticide entered in the Register shall be given a number to be known as the registered number.

(3) The Director or such other person as the Minister may appoint shall be the Registrar of Pesticides.

[subs (3) am Act 32 of 1976 s 2, effective 3 December 1976]

## s 4 Registration

No pesticide may be used, offered for sale or sold in Fiji unless such pesticide has been registered with the Registrar.

## s 5 Powers of Registrar

(1) Upon application being made for registration of a pesticide, the Registrar may in his or her discretion—

(a) register the pesticide with or without conditions; or

(b) refuse registration of the pesticide and shall, if requested by the manufacturer, state the reasons for refusal.

(2) The Registrar may at any time require a change in the label on any container of pesticide offered for sale.

(3) No change in the formulation of any registered pesticide may be made without prior application to the Registrar who may require a fresh application for registration to be made. Any such application shall be dealt with as if it were an entirely new application.

(4) No change in an approved label may be made without the prior approval of the Registrar.

## s 6 Publication

The Registrar shall cause the trade name, chemical identity of the active ingredients, the name of the manufacturers and the registered number of any pesticide registered under this Act to be published in the Gazette as soon as practicable after registration.

## s 7 Cancellation of registration

(1) Every registration made under this Act shall remain in force until such time as the Registrar shall direct that it be cancelled, which he or she may do upon giving written notice to the holder of the registration and publishing notice of his or her intention to cancel such registration.

(2) Such notice shall be served on the holder of the registration and published in the Gazette not less than 6 calendar months prior to the proposed date of cancellation unless, in the opinion of the Registrar, special circumstances exist to require earlier cancellation.

## s 8 Objection

In the event of notice being given under section 7 the holder of a registration may lodge an objection in writing to the Minister within one calendar month of the date of service of such notice upon him or her and the Minister may uphold or reject such objection or prescribe a fresh date upon which registration shall be cancelled.

## s 9 Repacking

Every pesticide shall be sold in its original container except with the prior written permission of the Registrar, and in accordance with any written instructions given by the Registrar.

## s 10 Offences

Any person who contravenes any of the provisions of section 4, 5 or 9 or of the regulations made under this Act shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to have any registration held by him or her cancelled and to a fine not exceeding $200 and in the case of a continuing offence to a further fine not exceeding $10 for every day during which the offence has continued.

[s 10 am Act 32 of 1976 s 3, effective 3 December 1976]

## s 11 Appointment and powers of inspectors

(1) The Minister may appoint persons to be pesticides inspectors, and any person appointed by the Minister as a pesticides inspector may, for the purposes of enforcing the provisions of this Act—

(a) enter any shop, factory, warehouse or other building or place and inspect and take samples or copies, as the case may be, of any pesticides and the containers, labels, records and books of account relating thereto or to any transactions in connection therewith;

(b) where he or she suspects that any offence has been committed, take into his or her custody the pesticides, containers, labels, records and books of account, together with any other evidence of any such suspected contravention of any of the provisions of section 4, 5 or 9 or of the regulations made hereunder;

(c) institute, prosecute and conduct any legal proceedings arising under this Act even though he or she is not a qualified barrister and solicitor.

(2) Any person who refuses to permit any pesticide inspector to enter any premises or hinders him or her in the course of searching the premises or in the course of otherwise executing his or her duties shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $200.

[s 11 insrt Act 32 of 1976 s 4, effective 3 December 1976]

## s 12 Regulations

The Minister may make regulations for the purpose of carrying out the provisions of this Act and in particular, without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, for—

(a) prescribing forms to be used;

(b) prohibiting or controlling the use of any pesticide;

(c) prescribing any other matter which may be desirable or expedient for the better regulation and control of the sale of pesticides.
