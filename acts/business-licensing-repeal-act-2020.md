# BUSINESS LICENSING (REPEAL) ACT 2020

AN ACT TO REPEAL THE BUSINESS LICENSING ACT 1976

**Table of Amendments**

Business Licensing (Repeal) Act 2020 (No 28 of 2020) commenced on 1 August 2020, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## s 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Act may be cited as the Business Licensing (Repeal) Act 2020.

(2) This Act comes into force on 1 August 2020.

## s 2 Repeal

The Business Licensing Act 1976 and any subsidiary legislation made thereunder are repealed.

## s 3 Transitional provisions

(1) Any money received for or in relation to a pending business licence application before 1 August 2020 must be reimbursed to the applicant.

(2) Any money received for a licence period after 1 August 2020 must be reimbursed on a pro rata basis.

(3) A person required to register a business name by virtue of the consequential amendment to the Companies Act 2015, as provided in the Schedule must, on or before a date appointed by the Minister by notice in the Gazette, apply for registration of the business name under section 32 of the Companies Act 2015.

## s 4 Consequential amendments

The laws listed in the Schedule are amended as set out in that schedule.
