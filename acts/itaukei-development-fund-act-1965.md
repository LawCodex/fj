# ITAUKEI DEVELOPMENT FUND ACT 1965

AN ACT TO CONSOLIDATE AND AMEND THE LAW RELATING TO THE ITAUKEI DEVELOPMENT FUND

[Long title am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

## s 1 Short title and application

This Act may be cited as the iTaukei Development Fund Act 1965 and shall apply to the whole of Fiji except for Rotuma.

[s 1 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

## s 2 Interpretation

In this Act—

**Board** means the iTaukei Development Fund Board established under the provisions of this Act;

[def am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

**coconut** means mature coconuts or coconuts sold or capable of being sold or used for the same purpose as mature coconuts, with or without husk;

**Coconut Board** [def rep Act 31 of 2016 s 109, effective 1 December 2016]

**copra** means the kernel of a coconut grown in Fiji in its dried condition when it has been extracted from the coconut;

**depositor** means an iTaukei, a unit or an iTaukei co-operative;

[def am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

**Fund** means the iTaukei Development Fund established under the provisions of the Fijian Development Fund Ordinance (repealed by this Act) and continued under the provisions of this Act;

[def am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

**iTaukei** bears the meaning assigned to it in the iTaukei Affairs Act 1944;

[def am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

**iTaukei co-operative** means a co-operative registered under the provisions of the Co-operatives Act 1996, of which not less than 85% of the natural persons being members are iTaukei and includes a co-operative all the members of which are co-operatives as aforesaid;

[def am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010; Act 31 of 2016 s 109, effective 1 December 2016]

**licensed buyer** means a person licensed or registered to buy copra under the provisions of the Coconut Industry Development Act 2010;

[def am Act 31 of 2016 s 109, effective 1 December 2016]

**secretary** means the secretary of the Board;

**unit** means a mataqali or other subdivision of the iTaukei people and includes an iTaukei village community; and

[s 1 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

**village community** in relation to any iTaukei village means the body of persons for the time being entitled by iTaukei law or custom to live in such village.

[def am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

## s 3 Establishment of Fund

(1) There shall continue to be established a Fund to be known as the iTaukei Development Fund.

[subs (1) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

(2) There shall be paid into the Fund the following moneys—

(a) any cess paid to the Board in accordance with the provisions of this Act;

(b) any sum received by way of interest on or repayment of principal of loans made under the provisions of section 13;

(c) any income accruing to the Fund from investments made with the moneys of the Fund;

(d) any income accruing to the Fund arising out of the transactions of the Fund;

(e) any sum borrowed under the provisions of section 15;

(f) any sum contributed to the Fund under the provisions of section 10.

[s 3 am Act 19 of 1972 s 2, effective 8 December 1972]

## s 4 Management of the Fund

(1) The Fund shall be managed and controlled by the Board and shall be held by the Board in trust for the payment of the moneys constituting the Fund in accordance with the provisions of this Act.

(2) The Fund or such part thereof as is not for the time being required for the purposes of the Fund shall be invested by the Board in such securities or be employed at interest in such manner as shall be approved from time to time by the Minister or, in the case of moneys remitted to London for investment, by the State Agents.

(3) The Board may, in respect of moneys invested in accordance with the provisions of subsection (2) vary, sell and transfer any such investments as may from time to time be necessary for the proper administration of the Fund.

## s 5 iTaukei Development Fund Board

(1) There shall continue to be established a Board to be known as the iTaukei Development Fund Board which shall continue to be a body corporate under that name and as such have perpetual succession and a common seal. The Board may sue and be sued in respect of matters whether relating to contract or tort or otherwise arising in connection with the exercise of its powers or the carrying out of its functions under the provisions of this Act.

[subs (1) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

(2) The Board shall consist of 9 members, of whom one shall be the Minister and not less than 5 shall be iTaukei to be appointed by the Minister after consultation with the iTaukei Affairs Board.

[subs (2) subst Ordinance 48 of 1966 s 2, effective 23 March 1967; am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

(3) A member of the Board appointed by the Minister under the provisions of subsection (2) shall hold office during the Minister's pleasure and may be paid such remuneration, if any, as may be determined by the Board with the approval of the Minister.

(4) The chairperson of the Board shall be the Minister and in the absence of the chairperson from any meeting the members present shall appoint a deputy chairperson who shall have all the powers of the chairperson.

(5) The chairperson, or in his or her absence the deputy chairperson, and 3 other members shall form a quorum at any meeting of the Board.

[subs (5) subst Ordinance 48 of 1966 s 2, effective 23 March 1967]

(6) All orders given by the Board shall be made or given under the hand of the chairperson.

(7) Service of any document on the chairperson of the Board shall be deemed to be service on the Board.

(8) The Board may make rules governing its own procedure.

[s 5 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

## s 6 Employment of servants

The Board may appoint and employ on such terms and conditions as it may from time to time determine a secretary, accountant and such other officers and servants as it may consider necessary for the proper and efficient carrying out of the functions of the Board.

## s 7 Banking account

(1) The Board may for the purposes of the Fund open and keep a banking account with a bank to be from time to time selected by the Board and cheques and orders for the payment of money shall be signed by at least 2 members of the Board.

(2) The Board may, for the payment of administrative expenses, establish at the bank selected by the Board in accordance with the provisions of subsection (1) an imprest account and shall fix the maximum amount that may be held at any time in such imprest account. Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (1), cheques and orders for payment of money out of the imprest account may be signed by—

(a) the secretary and the accountant of the Board; or

(b) the secretary or the accountant of the Board together with at least one member of the Board.

## s 8 Cess

(1) There shall be deducted by each licensed buyer in the manner hereinafter provided from the purchase price of copra or coconuts bought by him or her from any iTaukei, unit or iTaukei co-operative a cess at such rate as may be fixed by the Board with the approval of the Minister and notified in the Gazette, provided that—

(a) the rate of cess so fixed shall not exceed $20 in respect of—

(i) every ton of copra; or

(ii) every fixed amount of coconuts which shall be such number of coconuts, as in the opinion of the Ministry of Agriculture, is required to make one ton of copra from any particular species of coconut;

(b) in the event of no rate of cess being fixed by the Board such cess in respect of copra shall be at the rate of $20 per ton and proportionally for any part of a ton;

(c) different rates of cess may be fixed in respect of copra or coconuts produced in different areas.

[subs (1) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010; Act 31 of 2016 s 109, effective 1 December 2016]

(2) Each licensed buyer shall, within 14 days of the end of every calendar month or within such greater period as the Board may either generally or in any particular case prescribe, pay to the Board to be paid into the Fund all amounts of cess which he or she was liable under the provisions of this Act to deduct during that month.

(3) The licensed buyer shall at the time of purchase fill in a statement which shall be in a form and contain the particulars prescribed by the Board and at the time of payment of the cess to the Board such licensed buyer shall furnish a copy of such statement to the Board and shall himself or herself retain 2 copies thereof.

(4) The secretary or other officer authorised by him or her in writing shall be entitled to enter upon any lands, buildings or place owned, occupied or in the possession of a licensed buyer for the purpose of inspecting any statement for which provision is made by subsection (3) or any copy of any such statement and may require the production of any such statement or copy by any person in whose custody or control such statement or copy may be.

(5) Upon any licensed buyer ceasing to be licensed or registered under the provisions of the Coconut Industry Development Act 2010, such buyer shall within 14 days after the end of the calendar month during which he or she ceased so to be licensed or within such greater period as the secretary may specify in writing pay to the Board all amounts of cess which he or she was liable to deduct during that month and the Board may by notice in writing require such buyer to deliver up to the Board all forms of statement and all copies thereof, either blank or filled in, within 14 days of the service of such notice upon him or her.

[subs (5) am Act 31 of 2016 s 109, effective 1 December 2016]

(6) Any person who—

(a) being a licensed buyer, fails to deduct cess from the purchase price of copra or coconuts; or

(b) being a licensed buyer, fails to account to the Board for cess collection by him or her within the period prescribed by subsection (2); or

(c) being a non-iTaukei, sells as his or her own, on behalf of an iTaukei, copra or coconuts produced by such iTaukei; or

(d) obstructs or refuses to permit the secretary or other authorised officer to carry out any of his or her functions under the provisions of this section; or

(e) unlawfully and without reasonable cause fails to produce to the secretary or other authorised officer any statement or copy thereof in contravention of the provisions of subsection (5); or

(f) unlawfully and without cause fails to deliver up to the Board all statements and copies thereof, either blank or filled in, within 14 days of the service upon him or her of a notice in accordance with the provisions of subsection (5),

shall be guilty of an offence and shall on conviction be liable to a fine of $200 or to imprisonment for a term of 6 months or to both such fine and imprisonment and for every default in complying with the provisions of subsection (5) to a fine of $2 for every day during which such default continues.

[subs (6) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

(7) Nothing in this Act shall require or authorise the deduction of cess from the purchase price of any copra or coconuts where the cess has already been deducted on a previous purchase of such copra or coconuts.

(8) Nothing in this Act shall require the deduction, by any unit approved by the Board, or any iTaukei co-operative, of cess from the purchase price of any coconuts purchased from any iTaukei or from any other unit or iTaukei co-operative.

[subs (8) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010; Act 31 of 2016 s 109, effective 1 December 2016]

[s 8 insrt Ordinance 48 of 1966 s 3, effective 23 March 1967]

## s 9 Credits in Fund

(1) All cess paid to the Board under the provisions of section 8 shall be credited in the Fund to the depositor from whom the copra or coconuts the subject of the cess was bought unless at the time of sale such depositor directs that the moneys be credited to another depositor, in which case the moneys shall be credited in the Fund in the manner so directed.

(2) Moneys credited in the Fund shall bear interest at the rate of 2% per annum or at such other rate as may be fixed from time to time by the Board, provided that not less than 3 months notice of any change in the rate of interest shall be published in the Gazette and in Na Mata.

(3) Interest shall not be payable on any amount of less than $2 and shall not commence to accrue until the first day of the month next following the receipt of the moneys by the Board and shall cease on the last day of the month preceding that on which such moneys are withdrawn.

(4) Interest on any moneys shall, subject to the provisions of this section, be calculated to 31 July in any year and shall then be added to and become part of the principal sum credited.

## s 10 Voluntary contributions

Any depositor or any person on behalf of a depositor may with the consent of the Board deposit with the Board any sum of money and any sum so deposited shall be dealt with as if it were credited in accordance with the provisions of section 9.

[s 10 am Act 19 of 1972 s 3, effective 8 December 1972; Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010; Act 31 of 2016 s 109, effective 1 December 2016]

## s 11 Withdrawals from Fund

(1) Any iTaukei or any unit may with the consent of the Board and in accordance with the provisions of any regulations made by the Board withdraw the whole or any part of the moneys standing to the credit of such iTaukei or unit, including any interest thereon, for the purpose of expending the sum withdrawn on a work or scheme or for any purpose approved by the Board.

[subs (1) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

(2) Any iTaukei co-operative may with the consent of the Board and for any purpose permitted by the objects of the co-operative as defined in its by-laws, withdraw the whole or any part of the moneys standing to the credit of such co-operative, including any interest thereon, provided that the purpose of the withdrawal shall first be approved by a resolution passed at a general meeting of the co-operative.

[subs (2) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

(3) The grant or refusal of consent to any withdrawal shall be in the discretion of the Board, provided that in the case of refusal, the applicant shall be furnished with a written statement of the grounds of the refusal and shall have a right of appeal to the Minister.

## s 12 Provision on death, cancellation or extinction

(1) When an iTaukei dies leaving moneys in the Fund standing to his or her credit, the Board shall as soon as possible after the death of such iTaukei, pay to his or her personal representative any sum standing to the credit of such iTaukei in the Fund, provided that where such sum does not exceed $200 and probate of the will, or letters of administration to the estate of the deceased iTaukei is not produced to the Board within 6 months of the date of death, it shall be lawful for the Board in its discretion to pay and divide such sum to or among the widow or relatives of the deceased iTaukei in such manner as it may deem proper.

[subs (1) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

(2) When the registration of an iTaukei co-operative is cancelled under the provisions of the Co-operatives Act 1996 the Board shall as soon as possible after the date of such cancellation pay to the liquidator of such co-operative any sum standing to the credit of the co-operative in the Fund.

[subs (2) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010; Act 31 of 2016 s 109, effective 1 December 2016]

(3) When any unit ceases to exist by reason of the extinction of the members thereof any moneys standing to the credit of such unit in the Fund shall accrue generally to the Fund.

## s 13 Loans

(1) The Board may in its discretion make loans out of the moneys in the Fund to any iTaukei or unit, on such terms as the Board considers fit, for the purpose of expending the amount of such loan on a work or scheme approved by the Board, provided that no loan shall be made whereby the total amount of loans outstanding shall exceed 20% of the total amount of the moneys standing to the credit of depositors in the Fund.

[subs (1) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

(2) The Board may in its discretion and subject to such terms and conditions as it may prescribe and for such purposes as it may approve, make loans out of the moneys of the Fund, other than moneys standing to the credit of depositors therein, to officers or servants of the Board.

## s 14 Expenses of administration

(1) Subject to the provisions of subsection (2) all expenses incurred by the Board in the carrying out of its functions under the provisions of this Act or of any regulations made thereunder may be met from moneys in the Fund.

(2) All costs and expenses incurred of or incidental to any application for a withdrawal or loan from the Fund, including any investigation into a proposed work or scheme required under the provisions of this Act for the purpose of determining whether any approval should be given, shall be paid by the applicant.

(3) The Board may in its discretion permit an applicant for a withdrawal or loan to withdraw from the Fund such sums as may be required by the applicant to pay the costs and expenses referred to in subsection (2).

## s 15 Borrowing powers of Board

The Board may borrow for the purposes of the administration of the Fund such sums as may be approved by the Minister and moneys borrowed under the provisions of this section may be secured by deposit of any of the investment securities of the Fund.

## s 16 Payments from the Fund

The Board may pay out of the Fund—

(a) sums withdrawn under the provisions of sections 11, 12 and 14;

(b) sums lent under the provisions of section 13;

(c) sums in repayment of moneys borrowed under the provisions of section 15;

(d) sums in payment of expenses incurred in the administration of the Fund including the remuneration of and the payment of pensions, gratuities or retiring allowances to officers and servants of the Board or their dependants;

(e) sums in payment of remuneration to appointed members of the Board.

## s 17 Audit and accounts

As soon as possible after the close of the financial year of the Board—

(a) the accounts of the Board shall be audited by an auditor, appointed by the Minister, and be published with the auditors’ report thereon in such manner as the Minister shall direct;

(b) the Board shall furnish each depositor credited with any moneys in the Fund with a statement showing the amount standing to the credit of such depositor and of any interest outstanding thereon.

## s 18 Annual Report

The Board shall not later than 31 December in each year submit to the Minister and publish in a newspaper published and circulating in Fiji a report on the operations of the Fund during the 12 months immediately preceding 31 July in that year.

## s 19 Income tax

The income of the Fund shall be exempt from income tax.

## s 20 Regulations

The Board may make regulations—

(a) prescribing the procedure for the deduction, payment and collection of cess and for the forms to be used in connection therewith;

(b) regulating withdrawals from the Fund;

(c) regulating the making of loans from the Fund;

(d) regulating and ensuring the proper expenditure of moneys withdrawn from the Fund or loaned therefrom under the provisions of this Act;

(e) providing for the issue of passbooks to depositors and the making of entries therein;

(f) providing for the charging of a fee not exceeding $1 per annum for keeping the account of a depositor;

(g) prescribing anything required or authorised to be prescribed under the provisions of this Act.

## s 21 Protection of Board

No act or thing done by the Board or by any member thereof or by any officer or servant thereof or by any person acting under the direction of the Board shall if the act was done bona fide and without negligence for the purpose of carrying out the provisions of this Act, subject him or her personally to any liability, claim or demand whatsoever.

## s 22 Moneys not to be assignable etc

Except with the prior approval of the Board, no moneys credited in the Fund to a depositor shall be assignable or transferable by such depositor nor shall any such moneys be attached, sequestrated or levied upon for or in respect of any claim against or debt of such depositor other than a debt due to the Board.

## s 23 Validation of past transactions

Where prior to the commencement of this Act any moneys have been credited in the Fund in the name of an iTaukei village such moneys shall be deemed to be and always to have been validly credited to the iTaukei village community of such village.

[s 23 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]
