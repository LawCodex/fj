# ITAUKEI TRUST FUND ACT 2004

AN ACT TO ESTABLISH A TRUST FUND FOR iTAUKEI AND ROTUMANS, TO PROVIDE FINANCIAL AUTONOMY TO THE GREAT COUNCIL OF CHIEFS, TO EARN INCOME FOR THE PURPOSES OF THE FUND AND FOR RELATED MATTERS

## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [ss 1, 2]

### s 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Act may be cited as the iTaukei Trust Fund Act 2004.

[s 1 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

(2) This Act comes into force on a date appointed by the Minister by notice in the Gazette.

### s 2 Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

**beneficiaries** means persons or institutions that are beneficiaries of the Fund under section 5;

**BLV** [def rep Decree 23 of 2012 s 3, effective 9 March 2012]

**Board** means the iTaukei Trust Fund Board established under section 7;

[def am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

**Council** means the Great Council of Chiefs or Bose Levu Vakaturaga constituted under the iTaukei Affairs Act 1944;

[def insrt Act 25 of 2023 s 7, effective 1 December 2023]

**Fund** means the iTaukei Trust Fund established under section 3; and

[def am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

**iTaukei Affairs Board** means the iTaukei Affairs Board established under the iTaukei Affairs Act 1944.

[def am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

## PART 2 ESTABLISHMENT OF THE iTAUKEI TRUST FUND AND BOARD [ss 3–12]

### s 3 Establishment of the Fund

(1) This section establishes the iTaukei Trust Fund consisting of—

(a) trust property and retained earnings of the iTaukei Trust Fund, an incorporated trust for charitable purposes registered on 18 January 2002;

(b) amounts received by way of dividends and other income from investments of the capital of the Fund as authorised under this Act;

(c) any amount appropriated by Parliament; or

(d) any grant or donation to the Fund.

[subs (1) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

(2) The Board must not transfer or assign to the Council or any person any part of the Fund other than in accordance with this Act.

[subs (2) insrt Act 25 of 2023 s 7, effective 1 December 2023]

(3) Income from the Fund may be applied for the purpose of meeting the operating expenses of the Fund and the Board, including all costs, charges and expenses incidental to the management of the Board or to the exercise of any power, duty or function of the Board.

(4) The Trustee Act 1966 does not apply to the Fund or the Board.

### s 4 Purposes of the Fund

The purposes of the Fund are—

(a) to provide financial independence and autonomy in relation to the operations and administration of the Council;

(b) to provide funding for the undertaking, promotion and sponsoring of programmes in iTaukei and Rotuman languages, culture and the study of ethno-geography and ethno-history;

(c) to provide funding to help develop the management, leadership and entrepreneurial skills of iTaukei and Rotumans;

(d) to sponsor research into languages, art and culture of iTaukei and Rotumans and the better understanding and preservation of their heritage;

(e) any other purposes approved by the Council on the recommendation of the Board as advantageous to the beneficiaries.

[s 4 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010; Decree 23 of 2012 s 5, effective 9 March 2012; Act 25 of 2023 s 7, effective 1 December 2023]

### s 5 Beneficiaries of the Fund

The beneficiaries of the Fund are iTaukei and Rotumans and their institutions, selected under guidelines determined by the Council, selected under guidelines determined by the Board, and registered and issued with certificates as prescribed by regulation.

[s 5 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010; Decree 23 of 2012 s 6, effective 9 March 2012; Act 25 of 2023 s 7, effective 1 December 2023]

### s 6 Application of Fund income

The Board must expend the net income from the Fund for the purposes of the Fund.

### s 7 Establishment of the Board

(1) This section establishes the iTaukei Trust Fund Board.

[subs (1) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

(2) The Board is a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal, which may acquire, hold or dispose of property, enter into contracts and sue and be sued in its corporate name.

(3) The Board consists of 8 members to be appointed by the Minister of whom—

(a) 5 members must have the suitable qualifications and work experience in any of the following areas—

(i) investment management;

(ii) engineering;

(iii) information and technology;

(iv) human capital; and

(v) tourism and hospitality.

(b) one member must be a legal practitioner registered under the Legal Practitioners Act 2009;

(c) one member must be a chartered accountant in public practice registered under the Fiji Institute of Chartered Accountants Act 2021;

(d) one member must be a person with suitable qualifications and work experience as an economist, professional manager, sociologist, historian or similar expertise.

[subs (3) am Decree 32 of 2009 s 2, effective 7 August 2009; Act 25 of 2023 s 7, effective 1 December 2023]

(4) Appointments made under subsection (3)(b), (c) and (d) are not limited to iTaukei and Rotumans.

[subs (4) am Decree 38 of 2009 s 2, effective 11 September 2009; Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

(5) The Minister shall designate a member of the Board as chairperson of the Board.

[subs (5) am Decree 38 of 2009 s 2, effective 11 September 2009]

(6) Unless earlier removed in accordance with this Act, members are appointed for a term of 3 years and are eligible for reappointment for 2 subsequent 3-year term.

[subs (6) am Act 25 of 2023 s 7, effective 1 December 2023]

(7) A member of the Board may be removed from office for inability to perform the functions of a member, whether arising from infirmity of body or mind or any other cause, or for misbehaviour or for absence from 3 consecutive meetings of the Board without reasonable excuse, and may not otherwise be removed.

### s 8 Functions of the Board

(1) The functions of the Board are—

(a) to invest the Fund, as the Board determines, in any investment approved by the Minister responsible for iTaukei affairs or of a kind authorised under the Trustee Act 1966; and

(b) after making provision for the expenses of administration, to expend the net income arising from the investments.

[subs (1) am Act 25 of 2023 s 7, effective 1 December 2023]

(2) The Board may in respect of any moneys invested under subsection (1) vary, sell and transfer any such investments as may from time to time be necessary for the proper administration of the Fund.

### s 9 Meetings of the Board

(1) The Board must meet at least once every 3 months or upon the written request to the chairperson by not less than 5 members.

(2) In the absence of the chairperson at a meeting, the members must appoint a temporary chairperson to exercise the powers and functions of the chairperson for that meeting.

(3) The quorum at a meeting of the Board consists of 4 members.

[subs (3) am Decree 38 of 2009 s 3, effective 11 September 2009]

(4) If there is an equality of votes on any question before the Board, the chairperson shall have a casting vote.

(5) The Board may take independent legal advice on any matter concerning the position of a member of the Board or on the administration of the Fund.

(6) The Board may appoint one or more special purpose subcommittees of the Board to examine specific topics and report their findings to the full Board, and in the performance of such examination, a subcommittee may recruit the assistance of professional and other advisors.

(7) The Board shall determine its own procedure.

### s 10 Powers of the Board

The Board is empowered—

(a) to draw up for approval by the iTaukei Affairs Board, an annual strategic plan and budget, and set performance objectives and implement and monitor performance under such plans and objectives;

(b) to determine the quantum of allocation of funds from the income of the capital of the Fund for beneficiaries selected by the Board, in a manner and under terms and conditions that the Board considers to be in the best interests of the Fund and the recipient beneficiaries;

(c) to require beneficiaries to furnish any information in respect of funds to be allocated and may require an independent audit of the funds allocated;

(d) to terminate funding arrangements for any beneficiary upon non-compliance of any terms or conditions of the funds allocated;

(e) to pay from the income of the Fund any expenses, cost or expenditure, including legal and accounting fees, properly incurred in the discharge of its functions under the Act;

(f) to retain income in the Fund, after paying expenses, costs or expenditure, to be applied for the purposes of the Fund;

(g) to do all acts and things which the Board considers necessary or desirable and proper to further the purposes of the Fund and in the best interests of the beneficiaries.

[s 10 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010; Decree 23 of 2012 s 7, effective 9 March 2012]

### s 11 Disclosure of interest

A member of the Board who has an interest in a matter to be discussed or determined by the Board must declare his or her interest and must not take part in any decision of the Board on the matter.

### s 12 Allowances, fees and expenses

The members of the Board are entitled to allowances, fees and expenses as prescribed by the Minister.

## PART 3 MANAGEMENT AND FINANCIAL PROVISIONS [ss 13–17]

### s 13 Chief executive officer and other staff

The Board may appoint a chief executive officer and other staff as it considers necessary for the proper and efficient carrying out of the functions of the Board.

### s 14 Power to give directions

The Minister, on the recommendation of the iTaukei Affairs Board, may give a general or special policy direction to the iTaukei Trust Fund Board relating to the exercise of its functions under section 8(l)(b) and the Board must comply with the direction.

[s 14 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

### s 15 Power to borrow

The Board may, with the approval of the Minister responsible for iTaukei affairs, borrow any sums required by the Board to exercise its powers or carry out its functions and duties.

[s 15 am Act 25 of 2023 s 7, effective 1 December 2023]

### s 16 Accounts and audit

(1) The Board must keep accounts and records of transactions of the Fund and its operations and must prepare financial statements and accounts for each financial year.

(2) The accounts of the Board must be audited annually by an auditor who is a chartered accountant registered under the Fiji Institute of Accountants Act 1971, and holds a certificate of public practice as required under section 36 of that Act and is approved by the Minister.

### s 17 Annual report

(1) The Board must in each year prepare a report on its activities and on the Fund, including its financial report, and submit the report to the Minister within 6 months from the end of the year to which the report relates.

(2) The Minister must cause a copy of the annual report to be laid before Council at the meeting of Council after the Minister receives the report.

[subs (2) am Act 25 of 2023 s 7, effective 1 December 2023]

## PART 4 MISCELLANEOUS [ss 18–21]

### s 18 Protection from personal liability

A member of the Board or of a committee, the chief executive officer, the Secretary to the Board or other staff member of the Board is not personally liable for any act or default done or omitted to be done in good faith under this Act and any sums of money, damages or costs which may be recovered against the member or staff member in respect of such an act or default are to be paid out of the Fund.

### s 19 Termination

The iTaukei Trust Fund incorporated in Fiji on 20 December 2001 under a Declaration of Trust and a Funding Deed registered with the Registrar of Deeds on 18 January 2002 is terminated.

[s 19 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

### s 20 Regulations

The Minister may make regulations to give effect to the provisions of this Act and in particular to provide for registration as beneficiaries and the issue of certificates and their content.

### s 21 Transitional and savings

(1) At the commencement of this Act, any property, rights, obligations or liabilities vested in or belonging to the iTaukei Trust Fund incorporated in Fiji on 20 December 2001 is vested in or transferred to the Fund without conveyance, transfer or assignment to be held by the Fund for the purposes of the Fund.

[subs (1) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

(2) At the commencement of this Act, the members of the Board of Trustees of the iTaukei Trust Fund incorporated in Fiji on 20 December 2001 continue as if they had been appointed under this Act until their terms of office expire.

[subs (2) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

(3) At the commencement of this Act, the Chief Executive Officer of the iTaukei Trust Fund continues as if he or she had been appointed under this Act until his or her term of office expires.

[subs (3) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]
