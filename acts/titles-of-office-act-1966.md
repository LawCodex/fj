# TITLES OF OFFICE ACT 1966

AN ACT TO EMPOWER THE MINISTER TO CHANGE THE TITLES OF PUBLIC OFFICES AND TO VEST ALL NECESSARY AUTHORITIES IN SUCH OFFICES

**Table of Amendments**

Titles of Office Act 1966 (No 8 of 1966) commenced on 20 June 1966, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Titles of Office (Amendment) Ordinance 1970 (No 22 of 1970) | 3 July 1970 |
| Constitution of Fiji (Amendment) Order (LN 112 of 1970) | 8 October 1970 |

## s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Titles of Office Act 1966.

## s 2 Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires and for all purposes thereof—

**Act** includes this and any other Act and all notices, regulations, rules, proclamations, by-laws or any other law made under or in pursuance of such Acts;

**change of title** includes any change of title necessitated by the abolition of any office;

[def insrt Ordinance 22 of 1970 s 2, effective 3 July 1970]

**functions** includes powers, duties, authority and jurisdiction; and

**office** means an office or post held by any person including that of the Minister charged with responsibility for any Act.

[def insrt Ordinance 22 of 1970 s 2, effective 3 July 1970]

## s 3 Change of title

(1) Whenever any change of title of any office is made, the Minister may order such change and upon the date of coming into force of such order, all references to such office by its former title, or by his or her former title to the officer holding the same in any Act and in all instruments, documents and legal proceedings in force, made or continuing upon the said date shall be construed as references to the new title and all functions that may be performed by the holder of such office or that he or she may have, immediately prior to the date of such order, shall be vested in and may be performed by the holder of the new office.

[subs (1) am Ordinance 22 of 1970 s 3, effective 3 July 1970; LN 112 of 1970 O 23, effective 8 October 1970]

(2) An order made under the provisions of subsection (1) may relate to change of title in an Act or in such parts or sections thereof as may be specified therein whereupon the provisions of subsection (1) shall take effect as shall be necessary to give full force and effect thereto.

[subs (2) insrt Ordinance 22 of 1970 s 3, effective 3 July 1970; LN 112 of 1970 O 23, effective 8 October 1970]
