# METHYLATED SPIRITS ACT 1962

AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION FOR CONTROL OF THE SALE, STORAGE AND USE OF METHYLATED SPIRITS

[Long Title am Act 22 of 1983 s 2, effective 1 January 1984]

## s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Methylated Spirits Act 1962.

## s 2 Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

**licence** means a licence granted under the provisions of this Act;

**licensee** means a person granted a licence under the provisions of this Act;

**licensing authority** means, for the area in which a vendor of methylated spirits carries on his or her business—

(a) in respect of a municipality, the municipal council; and

(b) in respect of all other areas, the permanent secretary responsible for commerce, industry and trade;

[def subst Act 28 of 2020 s 4 and Sch, effective 1 August 2020]

**methylated spirits** means spirits which have been denatured to a prescribed standard under the provisions of the Customs Act 1986; and

[def am Ordinance 42 of 1962 s 2, effective 26 October 1962]

**record book** means the book required to be kept by a licensee under the provisions of section 4.

## s 3 Licence to sell or store for sale methylated spirits

(1) A licence granted under the provisions of this Act shall authorise the licensee to sell or store for sale the quantity of methylated spirits specified in his or her licence.

[subs (1) am Act 22 of 1983 s 4, effective 1 January 1984]

(2) A licensing authority, on being satisfied that the applicant for a licence will be able to observe the provisions of this Act, or, in the case of an application for renewal of a licence, that he or she has observed the provisions of this Act or any Ordinance repealed thereby, may grant or renew such licence on payment of a fee of $1, and, when granting such licence or the renewal thereof, shall specify in the licence the maximum quantity of methylated spirits which may be sold or stored for sale by the licensee and the premises on or from which methylated spirits may be sold or stored.

[subs (2) am Act 22 of 1983 s 4, effective 1 January 1984]

(3) Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (2), no licence shall be granted or renewed to any person in respect of premises in respect of which a licence is in force under which liquor may be sold for consumption on those premises.

(4) Every licence shall expire on 31 December next following the date of issue or renewal.

## s 4 Record of purchase or sale of methylated spirits to be kept

(1) Every licensee shall keep upon the licensed premises a book in the prescribed form in which he or she shall enter legibly in ink or indelible pencil, the date of every purchase or sale of methylated spirits, the quantity of methylated spirits purchased and sold, the name and address of the person from or to whom the purchase or sale was made and the price paid or received for the same.

[subs (1) am Act 22 of 1983 s 5, effective 1 January 1984]

(2) Every such entry shall be in the English language and shall be made at the time of such purchase or sale.

[subs (2) am Act 22 of 1983 s 5, effective 1 January 1984]

(3) When applying for a renewal of a licence, a licensee shall produce his or her record book to the licensing authority for inspection.

(4) A licensee who fails to comply with any of the provisions of subsection (1) or (2) shall be guilty of an offence and, on conviction, shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $100.

## s 5 Power of search

Any police officer may enter, at any reasonable time, any premises licensed under this Act or on which he or she has reasonable ground for supposing that methylated spirits are sold or stored for sale, and may search the said premises and may inspect any record book.

## s 6 Offences

(1) Any person who—

(a) sells or stores for sale methylated spirits without a licence;

(b) obstructs any police officer acting in the course of his or her duty under the provisions of section 5,

shall be guilty of an offence.

[subs (1) am Act 22 of 1983 s 6, effective 1 January 1984]

(2)

(a) Any person, whether or not licensed under this Act, who—

(i) knowingly sells or supplies methylated spirits to any person for consumption as a beverage or sells or supplies to any person any beverage containing methylated spirits;

(ii) prepares or attempts to prepare any methylated spirits for use as a beverage or as a mixture in any beverage or makes or blends any beverage containing methylated spirits; or

(iii) consumes or is found in possession of any beverage containing methylated spirits,

shall be guilty of an offence.

(b) The court by or before which any person is convicted of an offence under this subsection may order all methylated spirits found in his or her possession and the receptacles containing it to be forfeited and to be disposed of in whatever manner the court thinks fit.

(c) It shall be lawful for any police officer to arrest without a warrant any person found in possession of any beverage containing methylated spirits.

(d) For the purposes of this subsection, the expression “methylated spirits” includes crude alcohol, power alcohol, rectified spirits, spirits which have been denatured to a prescribed standard under the Customs Act 1986, industrial spirits or any mixture of such alcohol or spirits whatsoever.

[subs (2) am Ordinance 42 of 1962 s 3, effective 26 October 1962]

(3) Any licensee selling or storing for sale a quantity of methylated spirits in excess of the quantity specified in his or her licence shall be guilty of an offence.

[subs (3) am Act 22 of 1983 s 6, effective 1 January 1984]

(4)

(a) It shall not be lawful to offer for sale or sell methylated spirits to any person in a container of less than 4 gallons, unless such container is clearly labelled or marked “POISON” in letters at least half an inch high, and unless there is a warning thereon in letters at least a quarter of an inch high, in English, iTaukei and Hindustani, that the contents of such container, if drunk, may produce blindness.

(b) Any person offering for sale or selling methylated spirits to any person in containers not clearly labelled or marked in accordance with the provisions of paragraph (a) shall be guilty of an offence.

(c) On request a licensing authority shall supply labels complying with this subsection to any licensee.

[subs (4) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

(5) Any person convicted of an offence against any of the provisions of this section shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $400 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year or to both such fine and imprisonment.

(6) The court by or before which any licensee is convicted of an offence under this section may, instead of or in addition to, any other punishment which it may impose, cancel his or her licence.

## s 7 Regulations

(1) The Minister may make regulations for the better carrying out of the provisions of this Act.

[subs (2) am LN 112 of 1970 O 122, effective 8 October 1970]

(2) In particular, and without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing subsection, the Minister may make regulations prescribing the forms to be used under this Act and the manner of storage of methylated spirits.

[subs (2) am LN 112 of 1970 O 122, effective 8 October 1970]
