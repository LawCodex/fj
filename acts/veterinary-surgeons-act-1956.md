# VETERINARY SURGEONS ACT 1956

AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION FOR THE REGISTRATION OF VETERINARY SURGEONS

## s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1956.

## s 2 Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

**recognised veterinary diploma** has the same meaning as in section 13 of the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1881 (UK);

**register** means the Veterinary Surgeons Register required to be kept under this Act;

**Registrar** means the permanent secretary responsible for agriculture;

[def subst Act 37 of 2022 s 2, effective 1 August 2022]

**Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons** means the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons referred to in the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1881 (UK); and

**veterinary surgery** means the art and science of veterinary surgery and medicine.

## s 3 Unregistered persons not to have title of veterinary surgeon

No person shall be entitled to take or use the name or title of veterinary surgeon (either alone or in combination with any other word or words) or any name, title, addition or description, implying that he or she is registered under this Act or that he or she is a person specially qualified to practise veterinary surgery, unless he or she is registered under the provisions of this Act.

## s 4 Offences by unregistered persons

Any person, not being registered under the provisions of this Act, who takes or uses any such name, title, addition or description as aforesaid whether expressed in words, or by letters, or partly in words and partly in letters, shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200.

## s 5 Keeping of register and publication

(1) The Registrar shall keep a register, in the form contained in Schedule 1, to be styled the Veterinary Surgeons Register, of all persons registered as veterinary surgeons under this Act.

[subs (1) am Act 25 of 1973 s 2, effective 5 October 1973]

(2) The Registrar, in the month of January in each year, shall publish in the Gazette a copy of the register.

## s 6 Persons entitled to registration

(1) A person, on payment of the prescribed fee, is entitled to be registered if the person satisfies the Registrar that he or she is a fit and proper person to be registered and has—

(a) a recognised degree or qualification obtained from a veterinary school or college listed under Schedule 2; or

(b) been registered in the Register of the Veterinary Surgeons kept by the Registrar of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons.

[s 6 subst Act 37 of 2022 s 3, effective 1 August 2022]

## s 7 Registration of persons

(1) A person must submit an application to the Registrar to be registered as a veterinary surgeon under this Act.

(2) An application under subsection (1) must be accompanied by the prescribed fee.

(3) The Registrar must consider an application and, upon assessment of the application, register or refuse to register the applicant.

(4) In considering the application, the Registrar may, in writing, require the applicant to provide such particulars and information relevant to the application, as the Registrar deems necessary.

(5) The Registrar may impose conditions on a person’s registration as the Registrar deems necessary.

[s 7 subst Act 37 of 2022 s 4, effective 1 August 2022]

## s 8 Evidence of registration

A copy of the register for the time being published in the Gazette in accordance with the provisions of section 5(2), shall be evidence in all cases (until the contrary be made to appear) that the persons therein specified are registered under the provisions of this Act and the absence of the name of any person from such copy shall be evidence (until the contrary be made to appear) that such person is not registered under the provisions of this Act, provided that in the case of a person whose name does not appear in such copy, a certified copy under the hand of the Registrar of the entry of the name of such person in the register, shall be evidence that such person is registered under this Act.

## s 9 Erasing name from register

The Registrar may erase from the register the name of any person—

(a) who is convicted of a felony or misdemeanour; or

(b) who by reason of conduct disgraceful to him or her in a professional respect or by reason of a conviction for a criminal offence has been disentitled to practise veterinary surgery in England or in any country in which he or she was granted a recognised veterinary diploma.

## s 10 Correction of register

(1) The Registrar shall from time to time insert in the register any alteration which may come to his or her knowledge in the name or address of any person registered.

(2) The Registrar shall erase from the register the name of every deceased person.

(3) The Registrar may erase from the register the name of a person who has ceased to practise in Fiji or who has been continually absent from Fiji for the space of 3 years, provided that any person whose name has been so erased, shall, on resuming practice or returning to Fiji, be entitled to have his or her name restored to the register.

(4) In the execution of his or her duties, the Registrar shall act on such evidence as in each case appears sufficient.

## s 11 Name may be restored

If the Registrar thinks fit, he or she may in any case restore to the register any name erased therefrom.

## s 12 Appeal

Any person aggrieved—

(a) by a refusal of the Registrar to register him or her in the register;

(b) by a refusal of the Registrar to restore his or her name to the register;

(c) by the erasure of his or her name from the register,

may appeal to the Minister whose decision shall be final.

[s 12 am LN 112 of 1970 O 167, effective 8 October 1970]

## s 13 Penalty for obtaining registration by false representations

Every person who wilfully procures or attempts to procure himself or herself to be registered under this Act, by making or producing or causing to be made or produced, any false or fraudulent representation or declaration, either verbally or in writing, shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding $200 or to imprisonment for 12 months.

## s 13A Regulations

The Minister may make regulations which are not inconsistent with the provisions of this Act prescribing—

(a) the fees to be paid in accordance with this Act;

(b) the forms to be used in accordance with this Act; and

(c) offences and penalties not exceeding a fine of $50,000 or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 10 years or both.

[s 13A insrt Act 37 of 2022 s 5, effective 1 August 2022]

## s 14 Amendment of Schedules

The Minister may by notice in the Gazette amend the Schedules to the Act.

[s 14 subst Act 37 of 2022 s 6, effective 1 August 2022]
