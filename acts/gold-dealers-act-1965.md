# GOLD DEALERS ACT 1965

AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE CONTROL OF THE PURCHASE, SALE AND POSSESSION OF RAW GOLD AND FOR THE LICENSING OF GOLD DEALERS

**Table of Amendments**

Gold Dealers Act 1965 (No 40 of 1965) commenced on 16 December 1966, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Law Revision (Miscellaneous Amendments) (No 2) Ordinance 1966 (No 37 of 1966) | 13 January 1967 |
| Constitution (Statutory Amendments) Order 1970 (LN 112 of 1970) | 8 October 1970 |

## s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Gold Dealers Act 1965.

## s 2 Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

**buyer** includes a dealer and the agent of any buyer or dealer;

**gold** includes gold, gold-bullion, retorted gold, gold-ores, gold-dust, gold amalgam, gold-alloys, and precipitates, slag, concentrates, tailings and residues containing gold, but does not include coin or things manufactured of gold which on view have apparently been worked or manufactured for trade purposes;

**sale** includes exchange or pledge; and

**unwrought gold** means gold in any unmanufactured form and includes amalgam, slimes, slags, blacksands, pots, battery chips, sweepings of reduction work, scrapings and by-products of unrefined gold.

## s 3 Gold dealer's licences

(1) Except as hereinafter provided, no person shall buy or sell gold unless either the buyer or the seller is the holder of a gold dealer's licence and the sale is effected at the registered place of business of the gold dealer and under his or her personal supervision.

(2) Every person who commits a breach of the provisions of this section shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine of $1,000 or to imprisonment for a term of 2 years or to both such fine and imprisonment.

[subs (2) am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 107, effective 13 January 1967]

(3) This Act shall not apply to the purchase of gold-bearing earth or tailings from any registered holder of a mining lease if it is proved by the buyer that the sale was effected by a contract in writing signed by or on behalf of the seller and the buyer setting forth that the earth or tailings were produced from and taken out of the ground comprised in the mining lease of which the seller is the registered holder such lease being sufficiently described in the contract.

## s 4 Possession of unwrought gold

Any person who has in his or her possession any unwrought gold shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine of $1,000 or to imprisonment for a term of 2 years or to both such fine and imprisonment, unless—

(a) he or she is a person exempted under the provisions of section 3(3); or

(b) such unwrought gold has been won by him or her or his or her servant acting on his or her behalf from ground held by him or her under a mining tenement, or on which he or she is lawfully entitled to prospect; or

(c) he or she is in possession of such gold in fulfilment of a contract of service with any person mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b); or

(d) he or she comes into possession of such gold in a lawful manner,

and, in any proceeding against a person for an offence under the provisions of this section, the burden shall lie upon him or her of proving that he or she falls within one of the exceptions hereby provided.

[s 4 am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 107, effective 13 January 1967]

## s 5 Application

Application for a gold dealer's licence shall be made to the Minister.

[s 5 am LN 112 of 1970 O 140, effective 8 October 1970]

## s 6 Grantee to be a fit and proper person

Before granting a gold dealer's licence the Minister shall be satisfied that the applicant is a fit and proper person to hold such licence and that he or she has complied with the provisions of this Act and of the regulations made thereunder.

[s 6 am LN 112 of 1970 O 140, effective 8 October 1970]

## s 7 Limitation of licence

The Minister may in his or her absolute discretion grant a gold dealer's licence to any approved person or incorporated bank on payment of the prescribed fee, and every such licence shall have effect only within the area therein specified and shall be a personal licence not transferable.

[s 7 am LN 112 of 1970 O 140, effective 8 October 1970]

## s 8 Duration of licence

Every gold dealer's licence shall continue in force until the thirty-first day of December next following the granting thereof, but may be annually renewed on payment of the prescribed fee, provided that a licence may at any time be cancelled by the Minister on the licensee being convicted of any offence which, in the opinion of the Minister, renders the licensee unfit to hold a licence.

[s 8 am LN 112 of 1970 O 140, effective 8 October 1970]

## s 9 Register of gold dealers

(1) A register of licensed gold dealers shall be kept in the office of the Minister.

[subs (1) am LN 112 of 1970 O 140, effective 8 October 1970]

(2) The register shall contain the number of each licence and the name in full and the place of business of the licensee.

(3) The register shall be open to public inspection without fee at all times during ordinary office hours.

(4) Every licensee shall be struck off the register on expiration of his or her licence unless a renewal thereof has been previously granted.

## s 10 Change of registered address

(1) The holder of a gold dealer's licence may, on application to the Minister and on payment of the prescribed fee, change his or her registered place of business.

[subs (1) am LN 112 of 1970 O 140, effective 8 October 1970]

(2) An endorsement of any such change shall be made by the Minister on the licence and on the register.

[subs (2) am LN 112 of 1970 O 140, effective 8 October 1970]

## s 11 Gold dealer's book

(1) Every licensee shall keep at his or her registered place of business a gold dealer's book in which, immediately after buying or selling any gold, he or she shall—

(a) make an entry in such book of the name and address of the buyer or seller as the case may be and of such other particulars as may be prescribed; and

(b) furnish to the Minister such particulars as to gold brought or sold as may from time to time be prescribed.

[subs (1) am LN 112 of 1970 O 140, effective 8 October 1970]

(2) Every person who fails to comply with any of the provisions of this section shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine of $200 or to imprisonment for a term of 6 months.

[subs (2) am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 107, effective 13 January 1967]

## s 12 Entries in gold dealer's book to be signed

Every entry in a gold dealer's book shall be signed by the person with whom the dealing is made and every person who makes a false statement therein shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine of $200 or to imprisonment for a term of 6 months.

[s 12 am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 107, effective 13 January 1967]

## s 13 Declaration when gold sent by post

(1) When gold is forwarded by post to an incorporated bank licensed under the provisions of this Act, the sender shall make a declaration in the prescribed form of the name and address of the buyer and seller and of such other particulars as may be prescribed.

(2) Such declaration shall be filed in the gold dealer's book and when so filed shall be in lieu of an entry under the provisions of sections 11 and 12 and shall be deemed a compliance therewith.

## s 14 False entry in gold dealer's book

Every licensee who makes a false entry in a gold dealer's book shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine of $200 or to imprisonment for a term of 6 months.

[s 14 am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 107, effective 13 January 1967]

## s 15 Inspection

(1) Every police officer of or above the rank of inspector and any public officer authorised in writing in that behalf by the Minister may at any time enter the place of business of the holder of a gold dealer's licence or any other place where a gold dealer's book may be and inspect the same and take extracts therefrom.

[subs (1) am LN 112 of 1970 O 140, effective 8 October 1970]

(2) Every person who refuses to produce such book or resists or impedes inspection thereof shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine of $200 or to imprisonment for a term of 6 months.

[subs (2) am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 107, effective 13 January 1967]

## s 16 Inspectors to preserve secrecy

(1) Every person making an inspection under the provisions of section 15 shall keep secret and aid in preserving secrecy with regard to all matters which may come to his or her knowledge in his or her official capacity and shall not communicate any such matter to any other person except in the performance of his or her duties.

(2) Every person who acts contrary to the provisions of this section shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine of $100 or to imprisonment for a term of 6 months.

[subs (2) am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 107, effective 13 January 1967]

## s 17 Powers of search

Every police officer of or above the rank of inspector may—

(a) enter at all times upon any place or works for the reception of unwrought gold, stop and search every vehicle conveying or reasonably suspected or supposed to be conveying unwrought gold, and seal, mark or otherwise secure any package found in such place, works or vehicle;

(b) open any place, works, box or package which is locked if the keys thereof be not produced upon his or her demand;

(c) examine or search every part of such place, works or vehicle for any purpose authorised by the provisions of this section and take an account of all unwrought gold found therein and, if he or she thinks fit, take such unwrought gold into custody;

(d) search any person whom he or she has good reason to believe has unwrought gold secreted about his or her person or in his or her possession, provided that a female shall not be searched by any person other than a female.

## s 18 Temporary licences

(1) The Minister may issue to any fit and proper person a temporary licence to deal in gold in any remote locality.

[subs (1) am LN 112 of 1970 O 140, effective 8 October 1970]

(2) Such licence shall be in force for such period not exceeding 3 months as the Minister thinks fit.

[subs (2) am LN 112 of 1970 O 140, effective 8 October 1970]

(3) The provisions of this Act relating to gold dealers shall apply to the holder of a temporary licence so far as the same are applicable thereto.

(4) The particulars of every temporary licence shall be recorded in the register of licensed gold dealers.

## s 19 Gold exported to be declared

(1) Any banker, gold dealer or other person whosoever exporting any gold, if such gold shall have been found in or procured from the soil of Fiji, shall declare the weight and value of the same at the time of export at customs at the port of export.

(2) Any person failing to comply with the provisions of this section shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $200 or to imprisonment for any term not exceeding 6 months and to forfeiture of the undeclared gold or the value thereof, provided that no person shall be required to make a declaration under the provisions of this section in respect of any coin of the realm or article manufactured of gold or in respect of any alluvial or specimen gold of a less value than $20.

[subs (2) am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 107, effective 13 January 1967]

## s 20 Unlicensed dealers

Every person who, not being a licensed gold dealer, advertises himself or herself as a dealer in gold or in any way invites any person or persons generally to deal with him or her in the purchase or sale of gold shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine of $200 or to imprisonment for a term of 6 months.

[s 20 am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 107, effective 13 January 1967]

## s 21 Gold-dealings to be recorded

(1) Every person within Fiji who receives gold exceeding the value of $20 from any other person for safe keeping, transmission or otherwise howsoever as a bailee shall keep a record in writing of such transaction and shall on demand produce such record for inspection by any public officer authorised in writing by the Minister.

[subs (1) am LN 112 of 1970 O 140, effective 8 October 1970]

(2) Every person who fails to comply with the provisions of this section shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine of $200 or to imprisonment for a term of 6 months.

[subs (2) am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 107, effective 13 January 1967]

## s 22 Regulations

The Minister may make regulations prescribing all matters required or permitted to be prescribed or which are necessary or convenient to be prescribed for carrying out or giving effect to the provisions of this Act and in particular for—

(a) the duties of dealers in gold;

(b) the forms to be used, the returns to be furnished and the fees to be paid by dealers in gold.

[s 22 am LN 112 of 1970 O 140, effective 8 October 1970]
