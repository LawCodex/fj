# TAX FREE ZONES ACT 1991

AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT, MANAGEMENT, CONTROL, LICENSING AND REGISTRATION OF TAX FREE ZONES AND RELATED MATTERS

Tax Free Zones Act 1991 (No 57 of 1991)1 commenced on 4 December 1991, as amended by:

## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [ss 1-3]

### s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Tax Free Zones Act 1991.

### s 2 Acts to be read as one

This Act shall be read as one with the—

(a) Customs Act 1986 and the Customs Tariff Act 1986 in so far as it relates to customs and duties;

(b) Income Tax Act 2015 in so far as it relates to income tax;

(c) Excise Act 1986 in so far as it relates to excise; and

(d) the Exchange Control Act 1950 in so far as it relates to foreign exchange.

### s 3 Interpretation

In this Act unless the context otherwise requires—

**authorised officer** means any person appointed under section 8(c) ;

**company** means a company resident in Fiji, as defined in the Income Tax Act 2015 ;

**duty** means any duty leviable under the Customs Tariff Act 1986 ;

**enforcement officer** means—

(a) any customs officer;

(b) an officer of the Ministry of Economy, the Ministry of Industry and Trade or the Investment Fiji Board designated in writing by his or her respective Minister as an enforcement officer for the purpose of this Act;

[def am Act 31 of 2016 s 210, 1 December 2016]

**excise duty** means any duty leviable under the Excise Act 1986 ;

**export** means to take or cause to take out of Fiji;

**Fiji** includes the internal waters of Fiji, the archipelagic waters of Fiji and the territorial seas of Fiji as defined in the Marine Spaces Act 1977 ;

**Minister** means the Minister responsible for trade and commerce;

**operating licence** means a licence granted by the Minister under section 7(1) of this Act;

**tax** means any levy or tax chargeable under the Income Tax Act 2015 ;

**Tax Free Zone** means any area or building declared to be a Tax Free Zone under section 4(1) of this Act and includes Tax Free Factories approved by the Minister responsible for finance prior to the coming into force of this Act; and

**trade, business or manufacture** means any activity that generates export of goods or services, which the Minister in concurrence with the Minister responsible for finance may authorise any company licensed under section 7 to carry on within a Tax Free Zone.

## PART 2 ESTABLISHMENT OF TAX FREE ZONES [ss 4-5]

### s 4 Declaration, variation of Tax Free Zone and provision of customs facilities

(1) The Minister, in concurrence with the Minister responsible for finance, may on or before 31 December 2000 declare—

(a) that on or after a specified date any area of land and buildings thereon shall be a Tax Free Zone;

(b) a variation in the size of the area already declared as a Tax Free Zone under paragraph (a) .

[subs (1) am Decree 6 of 2001 s 2, 1 January 2001]

(2) Areas that may be declared Tax Free Zones under subsection (1) shall include office and other facilities required for the proper customs supervision of goods entering or leaving the Tax Free Zones.

### s 5 Development, management and control of Tax Free Zones

(1) Responsibility for the development, management and control of Tax Free Zones shall vest in the Minister.

(2) The Minister, in concurrence with the Minister responsible for finance, may delegate responsibility for the development, management, or control of Tax Free Zones to any statutory body, Government department or company.

## PART 3 LICENSING OF TAX FREE ZONE ACTIVITIES [ss 6-10]

### s 6 Application for a licence in Tax Free Zone

(1) Any company may apply to the Minister on or before 31 December 2000 for an operating licence.

[subs (1) am Decree 6 of 2001 s 3, 1 January 2001]

(2) Every application under subsection (1) shall be made in a form as specified in Schedule 1 .

### s 7 Grant of Tax Free Zone licence, criteria for grant of licence and diversion of manufactured goods for sale in Fiji

(1) The Minister, in concurrence with the Minister responsible for finance, may on or before 31 December 2000 grant to any company an operating licence in a form as specified in Schedule 2 authorising the carrying on of any trade, business or manufacture in a Tax Free Zone.

[subs (1) am Decree 6 of 2001 s 4, 1 January 2001]

(2) Any company seeking an operating licence for the carrying on of any trade, business or manufacture in a Tax Free Zone shall be required to—

(a) derive all of its chargeable income from export sales, which may include sales to a company licensed under subsection (1) ;

(b) generate employment opportunities for the people of Fiji;

(c) enhance, expand and improve the technological and trading capability and capacity of the economy of Fiji; and

(d) comply with any other condition deemed by the Minister to be appropriate under the circumstances.

(3) Any company licensed under subsection (1) may divert up to 30% of its production for sale within Fiji. Such products shall become liable for duty at the rate and upon the value subsisting at the time of disposal on the imported articles or raw materials which have been incorporated into such goods and the value for duty shall be calculated in accordance with Schedule 1 of the Customs Tariff Act 1986.

[subs (3) subst Act 7 of 1996 s 2, 28 June 1996am Act 52 of 1998 s 2, 13 November 1998]

### s 8 Conditions of operating licence

The Minister may attach to an operating licence certain conditions including conditions in regard to the following matters—

(a) the date, fixed by the Minister after consultation with the licensee, on or before which the licensee shall commence the trade, business or manufacture authorised by the licence;

(b) the revocation of the licence if the licensee should at any time after the grant of the licence undertake in a Tax Free Zone any trade, business or manufacture not authorised by the licence; and

(c) the appointment by the licensee of an authorised officer who shall be an individual person and who shall be a resident as defined in the Income Tax Act 2015 ;

### s 9 Revocation of licence, variation of conditions and transfer

(1) A licence may be revoked by the Minister—

(a) where there has been a breach of any condition of the licence; or

(b) where the licensee is convicted of an offence against the Customs Act 1986 .

(2) The Minister shall not revoke an operating licence unless a notice in writing is given informing the licensee of the intention to revoke the licence and giving the licensee at least 21 days within which the licensee may make representation to the Minister.

(3) The Minister shall consider any representation made by the licensee and make a decision whether or not to revoke a licence. The Minister’s decision under this subsection shall be final and shall not be challenged in any court.

(4) The Minister, in concurrence with the Minister responsible for finance, may—

(a) vary at any time the conditions of a licence; and

(b) approve the transfer of a licence to another company fulfilling the requirements set out at section 7(2) .

### s 10 Register of licences

(1) The Minister shall establish and maintain a register of the licences granted under section 7(1) .

(2) There shall be entered in the register in respect of each licence—

(a) the date of commencement of the licence;

(b) the name, registered address and the authorised officer of the company to which the licence was granted; and

(c) the trade, business or manufacture to which the licence relates.

(3) The register shall be kept in the Ministry of Industry and Trade in Suva and shall be open to inspection during such times as the Minister may direct, subject to the payment of a fee of $10 for each inspection.

(4) Where a licence is transferred by the licensee in accordance with section 9(4)(b) , the company to which it is transferred shall submit its name, and registered address and authorised officer for inclusion in the register.

## PART 4 DUTY AND TAX EXEMPTIONS [s 11]

### s 11 Duty and tax exemptions for licensed activities in Tax Free Zones

A licence granted by the Minister under section 7(1) shall provide, for a period of 13 consecutive fiscal years from the commencement of such a licence, the exemption of the licensee, in accordance with the Customs Tariff Act 1986 , the Excise Act 1986 and the Income Tax Act 2015 , from the payment of the following—

(a) duties leviable on the importation or purchase ex bond or excise duty leviable on purchase ex-excise factory of machinery and equipment (including parts and materials) in so far as they are required for the establishment and factory operation of the trade, business or manufacture to be carried out in a Tax Free Zone;

(b) taxes normally leviable under the Income Tax Act 2015 in respect of chargeable income of a company licensed under section 7(1) ;

(c) taxes leviable under the Income Tax Act 2015 , excepting dividend tax, on dividends derived by a resident shareholder in companies licenced under section 7(1) ;

(d) withholding taxes leviable on income under the Income Tax Act 2015 , provided that where such income is subjected to tax under the laws of the State of that person, then tax exemption under this paragraph will not apply.

## PART 5 CUSTOMS CONTROL AND DISPOSAL OF GOODS IN TAX FREE ZONES [ss 12-13]

### s 12 Disposal of goods taken into Tax Free Zones

(1) No person shall deal with or otherwise dispose of any goods taken into a Tax Free Zone except in the manner hereinafter provided.

(2) Goods in a Tax Free Zone may—

(a) be removed from such Tax Free Zone for export or sent into another Tax Free Zone either in the original package or otherwise; or

(b) be stored, exhibited, processed or manufactured or put to other uses in accordance with the provisions of this Act; or

(c) be destroyed or be disposed of as the Comptroller of Customs may direct.

### s 13 Utilisation of exempted goods

Subject to the provisions of section 12 all goods exempted under section 11 shall be utilised only in a Tax Free Zone.

## PART 6 MISCELLANEOUS [ss 14-18]

### s 14 Enforcement officers

Enforcement officers shall be responsible for the proper and efficient administration and control of the provisions of this Act.

### s 15 Power to make regulations

The Minister responsible for finance may make regulations for the proper administration of this Act and shall prescribe the places where and the form and manner in which entry of goods under the Customs Act 1986 shall be delivered and customs duties shall be paid.

### s 16 Offences and penalties

(1) Any person who contravenes any provisions of this Act or the provisions of any regulations made in accordance with section 15 , shall be guilty of an offence and shall, notwithstanding the provisions of section 9 , upon conviction, be liable to a fine not exceeding 3 times the value of the goods which are the subject matter of the offence, or $5,000 whichever is the greater; or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years; or to both such fine and term of imprisonment.

(2) Notwithstanding the provision of subsection (1) , any article in respect of which an offence under this Act may have been committed, together with any vehicle or anything whatsoever used for conveyance, handling, deposit or concealment of such article or goods is liable to forfeiture and to be disposed of as the Comptroller of Customs may direct.

### s 17 Application to Tax Free Factories

For the avoidance of doubt, the provisions of this Act shall be deemed to apply to the operations of Tax Free Factories approved by the Minister responsible for finance before the commencement of this Act, as if this Act was in force at the time that Minister’s approval was given for the operation of those Tax Free Factories.

### s 18 Commencement

This Act shall come into force on the day it is signed.
