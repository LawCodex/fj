# PUBLIC HOSPITALS AND DISPENSARIES ACT 1955

AN ACT FOR THE REGULATION OF PUBLIC HOSPITALS AND DISPENSARIES AND FOR MATTERS RELATED THERETO

## s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Public Hospitals and Dispensaries Act 1955.

## s 2 Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Board** means the Board of Visitors;

[def insrt Decree 36 of 2012 s 2, 4 May 2012]

**Chairperson** means the Chairperson of the Board of Visitors;

[def insrt Decree 36 of 2012 s 2, 4 May 2012]

**manager** means the person appointed to such position to be the manager in charge for the management and supervision of a public hospital or public dispensary;

[def insrt Act 22 of 2002 Sch 2, 15 November 2005]

**medical officer in charge** [def rep Act 22 of 2002 Sch 2, 15 November 2005]

**officer in charge** of a public hospital or public dispensary means the officer in immediate charge of the management of a public hospital or public dispensary;

**Permanent Secretary** means the Permanent Secretary for Health; and

**public hospital** and public dispensary means respectively a hospital or dispensary maintained out of public funds and includes a health centre or a nursing station.

[def am Act 4 of 1976 s 5, 26 March 1976]

## s 3 Admission to public hospitals

The admission of patients to, and the continuance of their stay in, a public hospital shall be in the discretion of the officer in charge of the hospital.

## s 4 Guarantee of payment

Every person seeking admission to a public hospital for treatment therein shall, save in the case of those admitted under any of the provisions of section 5 , either deposit with the officer in charge of the hospital or a person authorised in that behalf by him or her a sum sufficient to cover the cost of his or her maintenance and treatment therein for one week or give to the officer or person aforesaid such guarantee of payment of all charges and fees for maintenance and treatment as to such officer or person may appear satisfactory.

## s 5 Urgent cases and poor persons

(1) Persons seeking admission to a public hospital and suffering from the effect of severe accident or from disease threatening speedy death and requiring prompt treatment shall be admitted thereto as soon as may be.

(2) The manager of any public hospital or, in his or her absence, the officer in charge of such hospital may admit to the hospital any person who requires medical care and treatment but who appears to such officer to be unable by reason of poverty to pay therefore.

[subs (2) am Act 22 of 2002 Sch 2, 15 November 2005]

(3) Persons admitted under the provisions of this section shall not be required to make a deposit or give a guarantee of payment but nothing in this section shall be deemed to relieve any person admitted thereunder from liability to pay charges and fees for maintenance and treatment.

## s 6 Regulations

(1) The Minister may make regulations relating to—

(a) the government and management of public hospitals and public dispensaries and the patients therein and the admission and discharge of patients and the admission of visitors;

(b) the charges for the maintenance of patients in public hospitals and the charges for the treatment of patients and for the supply of medicines and services thereto in public hospitals and public dispensaries and the charges for services outside the public hospital or dispensary of staff attached to a public hospital or dispensary and the time and manner of payment thereof;

(c) the powers and duties of the staff including all officers, nurses and servants of public hospitals and public dispensaries;

(d) exemption from the payment of the whole or any part of any charge;

(e) the general purposes of this Act.

(2) Regulations made under the provisions of subsection (1)(c) may prescribe fines for the breach of any regulation made thereunder not exceeding for any one offence the sum of $1.

(3) The manager of a public hospital shall have power, subject to the approval of the Permanent Secretary to inflict any fine prescribed under the regulations and to enforce payment thereof by the deduction of the amount of the fine from the offending officer’s or servant’s salary or wages, provided that no fine shall be inflicted save after due enquiry in the presence of the officer or servant concerned, who shall be given full opportunity to answer any charges made against him or her.

[subs (3) am Act 22 of 2002 Sch 2, 15 November 2005]

[s 6 am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 35, 13 January 1967]

## s 7 Remission of fines and fees

(1) It shall be lawful for the Permanent Secretary in his or her discretion to remit any fine payable under any regulation made under the provisions of section 6 .

(2) The manager of any public hospital or public dispensary may, on being satisfied by a certificate signed by the District officer that a person who is liable to pay any charge payable under the provisions of any regulation made under the provisions of section 6 , is unable by reason of poverty to pay all or any part of such charge remit in whole or in part such charge.

[subs (2) am Act 22 of 2002 Sch 2, 15 November 2005]

## s 8 Appointment of Board

(1) The Minister shall appoint a Board of Visitors for each public hospital or public dispensary.

(2) Each Board shall have a minimum of 3 members and a maximum of 9 members, provided however that the appointment of any member may be terminated by the Minister at any time.

(3) Each member of a Board shall hold office for a term of 2 years and may be reappointed for 2 further consecutive terms.

(4) Any member of a Board may resign at any time by providing 30 days written notice to the Minister.

[s 8 subst Decree 36 of 2012 s 3, 4 May 2012]

## s 9 Particulars of meetings

(1) The Chairperson of a Board shall preside at all the meetings of that Board and if the Chairperson is not present at a meeting, the members present may for that particular meeting appoint a member to preside as the acting Chairperson in the absence of the Chairperson.

(2) Two-thirds of the members of a Board shall constitute a quorum for a meeting.

(3) Any issue raised shall be decided by a majority of the votes of the members of a Board present and in the event of a tie of votes, the Chairperson shall have a casting vote.

[s 9 subst Decree 36 of 2012 s 4, 4 May 2012]

## s 10 Functions of the Board

Each Board shall—

(a) in consultation with the officer in charge of the public hospital or public dispensary, ensure—

(i) the maintenance and repair of the hospital or dispensary under its care and control;

(ii) the beautification of the hospital or dispensary under its care and control; and

(iii) that the hospital or dispensary under its care and control is well equipped and has the necessary technology to provide high quality care for patients;

(b) subject to the approval of the Permanent Secretary, partner with private sector bodies to fulfil the functions given to it under this Act; and

(c) assist and support the Ministry of Health in encouraging good health and preventing communicable and non-communicable diseases.

[s 10 subst Decree 36 of 2012 s 5, 4 May 2012]

## s 10A Accountability of the Board

(1) Each Board appointed under this Act shall—

(a) be accountable to the Minister; and

(b) act in accordance with the laws of Fiji and any directions issued by the Minister to the Board.

(2) Each Board shall keep proper accounts in respect of its operations and shall prepare a statement of accounts for each financial year.

(3) The accounts of each Board shall be audited annually by an independent auditor.

(4) Each Board shall submit to the Minister as soon as practicable at the end of each financial year—

(a) a copy of the statement of accounts; and

(b) a copy of any report made by the independent auditor.

[s 10A insrt Decree 36 of 2012 s 6, 4 May 2012]

## s 10B Disclosure of interest

(1) If any member of a Board is present at a meeting of that Board where a matter is being considered and such a member has any direct or indirect pecuniary interest or other interests in the matter, he or she shall immediately disclose that interest and take no further part in the consideration of the matter.

(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1) , such disclosure of interest by any member of a Board shall not affect the constitution of a quorum.

(3) Any act or omission made in good faith and without negligence in the exercise of a member of a Board’s duties under this Act, or any act or omission made by the Minister, Permanent Secretary, employee of a Board or any committee of a Board, shall not subject that person to any personal legal liability.

[s 10B insrt Decree 36 of 2012 s 6, 4 May 2012]

## s 11 Trespass in hospital grounds

(1) Any person who enters into or upon the premises or grounds of any public hospital without lawful excuse shall be guilty of an offence and upon conviction shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $10.

(2) The provisions of subsection (1) shall not derogate from the provisions of section 387 of the Crimes Act 2009, provided that a person shall not on the same facts be convicted both under the provisions of this section and of section 387 aforesaid.

[subs (2) am Act 31 of 2016 s 176, 1 December 2016]

## s 12 Recovery of fees and charges

(1) All fees and charges made under the provisions of this Act or regulations made thereunder shall when collected or recovered be paid into the Consolidated Fund.

(2) Any fee or charge payable under or by virtue of this Act and remaining unpaid after the due date for payment may, in addition to any other lawful method or recovery, be recovered as a debt due to the State.
