# TOURISM FIJI ACT 2004

AN ACT TO ESTABLISH TOURISM FIJI AND FOR RELATED MATTERS

## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [ss 1, 2]

### s 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Act may be cited as the Tourism Fiji Act 2004.

[subs (1) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 2, effective 6 February 2009]

(2) This Act comes into force on a date appointed by the Minister by notice in the Gazette.

### s 2 Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

**amendment Promulgation of 2009** means the Fiji Visitors Bureau (Amendment) Promulgation (No 5) of 2009;

[def insrt Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 3, effective 6 February 2009]

**Board** means the Board of Directors of the corporation established under section 6;

[def am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 3, effective 6 February 2009]

**Bureau** [def rep Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 3, effective 6 February 2009]

**corporation** means the body corporate mentioned in section 3(1) and known as Tourism Fiji; and

[def insrt Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 3, effective 6 February 2009]

**Chief Executive** means the Chief Executive of the corporation appointed under section 11.

[def am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 3, effective 6 February 2009]

## PART 2 ESTABLISHMENT OF TOURISM FIJI AND THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS [ss 3–10]

### s 3 Establishment of the corporation

(1) The Fiji Visitors Bureau established by the Fiji Tourist Commission and Visitors Bureau Act 1969 is preserved and continues in existence as a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal under this Act, but it is to be known, after the commencement of the amendment Promulgation of 2009, as Tourism Fiji.

[subs (1) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 5, effective 6 February 2009]

(2) The corporation may—

(a) sue and be sued;

(b) acquire, hold or dispose of properties and enter into contracts;

(c) lend money and raise loans, subject to section 14; and

(d) do any other thing, which a legal person can do in connection with its functions.

[subs (2) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 5, effective 6 February 2009]

(3) The corporate identity and the rights and obligations of the body corporate mentioned in section 3(1) are not affected by any change of its name as provided for in that subsection.

[subs (3) subst Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 5, effective 6 February 2009]

### s 4 Object of the corporation

The object of the corporation is to ensure that Fiji is promoted and marketed as a tourist destination for the purpose of maximising sustainable and long-term benefits to Fiji.

[s 4 am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

### s 5 Functions of the corporation

(1) The corporation shall perform its functions—

(a) for the purpose of achieving its object set out in section 4; and

(b) in accordance with its annual statement approved for that financial year under section 13.

[subs (1) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

(2) Subject to subsection (1), the functions of the corporation are—

(a) to develop and implement strategies for the promotion and marketing of tourism; and

(b) to advise the Government and the Fiji tourism industry on matters relating to the development, implementation and promotion of the strategies mentioned in paragraph (a).

[subs (2) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

### s 6 Establishment of the Board

(1) This section establishes the Board which consists of 9 members appointed by the Minister responsible for public enterprises in writing subject to the approval of the Prime Minister.

[subs (1) subst Act 6 of 2019 s 69 and Sch 2, effective 19 July 2019]

(2) The Chief Executive of the corporation is an ex officio member of the Board but does not have the right to vote on any matter before the Board for deliberation.

[subs (2) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

(3) The Minister responsible for public enterprises shall not appoint a person to be a member of the Board under subsection (1) unless in the Minister’s opinion the person has the adequate qualifications, skills, expertise and knowledge to contribute to the functions of the Board under this Act, and in particular for the development of tourism, and general administration and financial management of the Board.

[subs (3) subst Act 6 of 2019 s 69 and Sch 2, effective 19 July 2019]

(4) A member of the Board shall hold office for a term not exceeding 3 years and shall be eligible for reappointment.

[subs (4) subst Act 6 of 2019 s 69 and Sch 2, effective 19 July 2019]

(5) The chairperson of the Board is appointed by the Minister responsible for public enterprises from amongst the members of the Board.

[subs (5) am Act 6 of 2019 s 69 and Sch 2, effective 19 July 2019]

(6) At the first meeting of the new Board, the members must elect a deputy chairperson from amongst its members.

### s 7 Powers of the Board

(1) The Board may, on behalf of the corporation, do all things as are incidental and conducive to the attainment of the object and functions of the corporation.

[subs (1) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

(2) The Board may appoint special committees for such purposes as it deems necessary either from its own members or from within or outside the corporation.

[subs (2) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

(3) The Board shall approve and control all expenditure but it may delegate the control of certain levels of expenditure to the Chief Executive or a special committee in accordance with the directives made by the Board, with the approval of the Minister.

### s 8 Meetings of the Board

(1) The quorum at any meeting of the Board is 5 members.

(2) If the chairperson is absent at a meeting, the deputy chairperson shall preside.

(3) If the chairperson and the deputy chairperson are both absent at a meeting, the members present may elect a member to preside at that meeting.

(4) At any meeting of the Board the member presiding at that meeting has an original vote and a casting vote.

(5) The Board may make rules governing its own procedures.

### s 9 Disclosure of interest

(1) A member of the Board or any special committee or the Chief Executive having any interest in any matter under discussion by the Board or special committee must disclose his or her interest to the Board or special committee.

(2) A disclosure under subsection (1) must be recorded in the minutes of the Board or committee and, after disclosure, the member or the Chief Executive must not take part nor be present in any deliberation or decision of the Board or committee.

(3) An officer or staff member of the corporation must declare any interest relating to any tourism industry to the Chief Executive who must advise the Board or a special committee of the nature of the interest.

[subs (3) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

### s 10 Remuneration of members of the Board and committees

(1) A member of the Board, excluding the Chief Executive, is entitled to remuneration as approved by the Minister responsible for public enterprises.

[subs (1) subst Act 6 of 2019 s 69 and Sch 2, effective 19 July 2019]

(2) Members of any special committee appointed under section 7(2) are entitled to allowances and expenses determined by the Board.

## PART 3 MANAGEMENT AND FINANCIAL PROVISIONS [ss 11–16]

### s 11 Appointment of Chief Executive and other staff

(1) The Board may appoint a Chief Executive and other officers and staff as it considers necessary for the proper and efficient carrying out of the functions of the corporation.

[subs (1) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

(2) The Chief Executive is entitled to remuneration and other allowances determined in accordance with the Higher Salaries Commission Act 2023.

[subs (2) am Act 31 of 2016 s 214, effective 1 December 2016; Act 24 of 2023 s 31 and Sch 2, effective 16 October 2023]

(3) The other officers and staff of the corporation are entitled to remuneration and other allowances determined by the Board.

[subs (3) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

(4) Notwithstanding section 18, the Board may delegate its powers to appoint other officers or staff under subsection (1) to the Chief Executive or other senior officers of the corporation.

[subs (4) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

### s 12 Power to give directions

(1) The Minister may, in writing, give directions to the Board with respect to matters of policy, including directions with respect to the general nature and extent of the operations of the corporation, and the Board shall comply with such directions.

[subs (1) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1), the Board shall, if required by the Minister, formulate and submit to the Minister proposals with respect to the general nature and extent of its future operations.

### s 13 Annual statements

(1) The Board shall, before 1 December in every financial year, submit to the Minister for approval, an annual statement on—

(a) the corporation’s projected outputs during the next financial year;

(b) the corporation’s best estimate of the cost to the corporation of providing those outputs;

(c) the methods (including financial and non-financial performance measures) by which the corporation intends to assess the extent to which it will provide those outputs;

(d) how the corporation intends to report on the extent to which it will provide those outputs; and

(e) liabilities the corporation intends to incur during the next financial year, subject to subsection (5).

[subs (1) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

(2) Every annual statement shall be accompanied by a statement of the corporation’s best estimate of—

(a) the impacts of the outputs described in the annual statement on, and the impacts and consequences of those outputs for, the number of visitors to Fiji in later years, the length of their stay and the amount of money spent; and

(b) the impacts of those outputs on and consequences of those outputs for the number of visitors to Fiji in later years and the amount of money they spent.

[subs (2) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

(3) At any time during a financial year, the Board may submit to the Minister an amended annual statement for that year.

(4) After receiving the corporation’s annual statement for a year or an amended annual statement, the Minister may—

(a) approve the annual or amended statement, subject to subsection (5);

(b) return the annual or amended statement to the Board with directions for amendment; or

(c) refuse to approve the amended statement.

[subs (4) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

(5) The Minister may, with the concurrence of the Minister responsible for economy, approve any part of an annual statement stating the liabilities the corporation intends to incur.

[subs (5) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

(6) If the Minister—

(a) refers to the Board the corporation’s annual statement for any year or an annual statement, for amendment; or

(b) during a financial year, directs the Board, in writing, to amend its annual statement for the year,

the Board shall amend the annual statement and return it to the Minister.

[subs (6) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

(7) When the Board returns to the Minister the amended annual statement for any financial year, the Board may also return the amended statement required under subsection (2) for that year.

### s 14 Power to raise loans

The corporation may, with the consent of, and upon such terms and conditions as may be approved by the Minister with the concurrence of the Minister responsible for economy, raise loans for the purpose of exercising its powers and carrying out its object and functions under this Act.

[s 14 am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

### s 15 Accounts and audit

(1) The corporation shall keep and maintain proper accounts and other records in respect of its operations and shall cause to be prepared a statement of account in respect of each financial year.

[subs (1) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

(2) The Auditor-General shall audit the accounts of the corporation and prepare a report on such accounts.

[subs (2) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

(3) After the end of each financial year as soon as the accounts of the corporation have been audited, the Board shall cause a copy of the statement of accounts to be transmitted to the Minister together with a copy of any report made by the Auditor-General on that statement or on the accounts of the corporation.

[subs (3) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

### s 16 Annual report

(1) The Board shall, within 6 months after the end of each financial year, cause to be made and transmitted to the Minister an annual report on the operations, business and affairs of the corporation.

[subs (1) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

(2) The Minister shall, as soon as practicable, lay before Parliament a copy of the annual report of the corporation.

[subs (2) am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

## PART 4 MISCELLANEOUS [ss 17–20]

### s 17 Exemption of liability

A member of the Board or of a committee, the Chief Executive or an officer or a staff member of the corporation is not personally liable for any act or default done or omitted to be done in good faith under this Act and any sums of money, damages, or costs which may be recovered against the member, officer or staff member in respect of any such act or default is to be paid out of the funds of the corporation.

[s 17 am Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 6, effective 6 February 2009]

### s 18 Delegation of powers

The Board may generally or specifically delegate its powers or functions under this Act to a member of the Board, a member of a special committee or the Chief Executive except the power under this section.

### s 19 Regulations

The Minister may, after consulting the Board, make regulations to give effect to the provisions of this Act.

### s 20 Repeal and savings

(1) The Fiji Tourist Commission and Visitors Bureau Act (Cap. 104) is repealed.

(2) At the commencement of this Act, any property, rights, obligations or liabilities which was vested in or belonged to the Fiji Islands Visitors Bureau vest in and belong to Tourism Fiji without conveyance, transfer or assignment and are to be held by Tourism Fiji for the same purpose as they were held by the Fiji Islands Visitors Bureau at the commencement of the Act.

[subs (2) subst Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 7, effective 6 February 2009]

(3) At the commencement of this Act, a reference to Fiji Visitors Bureau in any written law or any other document is amended and to be replaced and read as Tourism Fiji.

(4) At the commencement of this Act, the members of the Board of Directors of the Fiji Visitors Bureau appointed under the repealed Act continue as if they had been appointed under this Act until their terms of office expire.

(5) As from the commencement of the amendment Promulgation of 2009, any reference to the Fiji Islands Visitors Bureau in any law or other document, other than this Act, is, in respect of the period beginning with that commencement, to be read as a reference to Tourism Fiji.

[subs (5) insrt Promulgation 5 of 2009 s 7, effective 6 February 2009]
