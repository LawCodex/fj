## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [ss 1– 3]

### s 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Act may be cited as the Regulation of Ownership (Fiji Forest Industries Limited) Act 2011.

(2) This Act comes into force on the date of its publication in the Gazette.

### s 2 Interpretation

In this Act, unless the contrary intention appears—

**designated company** means Tropik Wood Industries Limited, a Fiji incorporated limited liability company having its registered office at Vakabuli Road, Drasa, Lautoka;

**Fiji Forest Industries Limited** means a Fiji incorporated limited liability company having its registered office at Vakabuli Road, Drasa, Lautoka;

**Minister** means the Minister responsible for Finance; and

**Registrar of Companies** means the Registrar as defined in section 3 of the Companies Act 2015.

[def am Act 31 of 2016 s 183, effective 1 December 2016]

### s 3 Object of the Act

(1) The object of this Act is to ensure the viability, sustainability and development of the designated company, and to protect the administration, control and management of Fiji Forest Industries Limited and the designated company.

(2) The Act achieves this object by—

(a) providing for the cancellation of shares held in Fiji Forest Industries Limited held by any person other than those shares held by the designated company, which cancellation is caused by the failure to provide consideration for transfer of such shares and in breach of the Memorandum and Articles of Association of the designated company;

(b) providing for the vesting or transfer of such shares in Fiji Forest Industries Limited to the designated company; and

(c) providing for the transfer of all property acquired by any person using the funds of the Fiji Forest Industries Limited, from any such person to Fiji Forest Industries Limited.

## PART 2 VESTING OF SHARES [ss 4– 7]

### s 4 Transfer of shares nullified

(1) The transfer of shares of Fiji Forest Industries Limited held by the designated company on or about 30 April 2009 and 19 November 2009 are hereby cancelled, and any such shares shall wholly vest in the designated company upon the commencement of this Act.

(2) Any document or instrument purporting to transfer the shares of Fiji Forest Industries Limited held by the designated company on or about 30 April 2009 and the 19 November 2009 shall be null and void and of no effect whatsoever.

(3) All transactions, documents, agreements or instruments in writing arising out of and during the course of business pursuant to the transfer of shares of Fiji Forest Industries Limited held by the designated company on or about the 30 April 2009 and the 19 November 2009 shall be null, void and of no legal effect and shall not in any way be binding on the designated company.

### s 5 Liability for transfer of shares

Nothing in this Act shall exempt any person or persons from any civil or criminal liability for any direct or indirect action or omission with respect to the transfer of shares of the Fiji Forest Industries Limited held by the designated company on or about the 30 April 2009 and the 19 November 2009.

### s 6 Registrar of Companies to effect necessary changes

The Registrar of Companies shall make the necessary changes to the entries in the register of shareholders of Fiji Forest Industries Limited to give effect to the provisions of section 4.

### s 7 Minister may exempt stamp duty and other registration fees

Any transfer or other document which may be required to carry into effect the provisions of section 4 shall be exempt from stamp duty or registration fees.

## PART 3 VESTING OF OTHER PROPERTY [s 8]

### s 8 Vesting of other property

(1) Except in the case of shares, all property, whether real or personal and whether covered by registered or unregistered instruments of title, presently held by any person, which were obtained, acquired, transferred or purchased through the monies or to the credit of Fiji Forest Industries Limited between the 30 April 2009 and the 28 May 2010, shall on the date of the commencement of this Act be deemed to have been transferred into the ownership of Fiji Forest Industries Limited.

(2) All documents or instruments brought into existence for the purpose of evidencing, recording or effecting the matters referred to in subsection (1) shall be exempt from stamp duty under the Stamp Duties Act 1920 as amended.

(3) No further consent, permit, certification, ratification, fees or charges, registration fees of any kind, filing fees, lodgement fees or fees of any kind whatsoever shall be required to be given or paid in respect of any document or instrument sought to be registered for the purpose of evidencing, recording or effecting the matters referred to in subsection (1) notwithstanding any provision to the contrary in any written law.

(4) The vesting of any interest in any land in the designated company by virtue of this Act shall be valid and effective notwithstanding any non-compliance with any written law.

(5) The powers and rights conferred upon the designated company by the operation of this Act shall not be limited or restricted in any way by the provisions of or non-compliance with any written law.

## PART 4 MISCELLANEOUS [s 9]

### s 9 Certain decisions not to be challenged

(1) No court, tribunal, commission or any other adjudicating body shall have the jurisdiction to accept, hear, determine or in any other way entertain any challenges by any person or body, or to entertain or grant any remedy to any person or body, which seeks or purports to challenge or question—

(a) the validity, legality or propriety of this Act;

(b) any decision of any Minister or any State official or body, made under this Act;

(c) any decision, action or omission made under or arising out of the provisions of this Act; or

(d) any decision made by the designated company, made pursuant to this Act.

(2) Any proceeding, claim, challenge or dispute of any nature whatsoever in any court, tribunal, commission or before any other person or body exercising a judicial function, in respect of any of the subject matters in subsection (1) that had been instituted before the commencement date of this Act but had not been determined at that date, shall wholly terminate immediately upon the commencement of this Act, and all orders whether preliminary or substantive made therein shall wholly terminate upon the commencement of this Act, and a certificate to that effect shall be issued by the Chief Registrar, tribunal, commission or any other person or body exercising a judicial function.

(3) A certificate under subsection (2) is, for the purposes of any proceedings in a court, tribunal, commission or any other person exercising a judicial function, conclusive of the matters stated in the certificate.

(4) A decision of the Chief Registrar, tribunal, commission or any other person exercising a judicial function to issue a certificate under subsection (2) is not subject to challenge in any court, tribunal, commission or any other adjudicating body.
