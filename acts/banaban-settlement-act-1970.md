# BANABAN SETTLEMENT ACT 1970

AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE SETTLEMENT AND GOVERNMENT OF THE BANABAN COMMUNITY ON RABI ISLAND AND FOR PURPOSES CONNECTED THEREWITH

## s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Banaban Settlement Act 1970.

## s 2 Interpretation

In this Act unless the context otherwise requires—

**Banaban community** means the former indigenous inhabitants of Banaba and their descendants;

[def subst Act 8 of 1996 s 4, effective 30 August 1996]

**Board** means the Banaban Trust Fund Board established under section 6A;

[def insrt Act 3 of 1981 s 2, effective 27 March 1981]

**Council** means the Council of Leaders established by this Act;

**Elders** means a Banaban recognised in accordance with Banaban custom;

[def insrt Act 8 of 1996 s 4, effective 30 August 1996]

**Fund** means the Rabi Island Fund established by this Act;

**Minister** means the Minister responsible for the administration of this Act;

**Resident** means one who is a Banaban from one of the 4 communities specified in section 3(3) of the Act and residing in Fiji;

[def insrt Act 8 of 1996 s 4, effective 30 August 1996]

**Tribunal** means the Rabi Island Tribunal appointed under the provisions of this Act; and

**Trust Fund** means the Banaban Trust Fund established under section 6B.

[def insrt Act 3 of 1981 s 2, effective 27 March 1981]

## s 3 Establishment of Council of Leaders

(1) There is hereby established for Rabi Island a body corporate, with permanent succession and a common seal, to be known as the Council of Leaders.

(2) The Council shall consist of 9 members and shall meet at least 6 times a year.

(3) Each of the 4 Communities on Rabi namely Tabwewa, Uma, Tabiang and Buakonikai, shall elect 2 members to the Council, one of whom shall be an Elder. A chairperson will then be elected from amongst the elected Council by all the 4 communities.

(4) After the election of the chairperson the candidate with the third highest vote from the elected chairperson’s community shall become the ninth member of the Council.

(5) The chairperson shall only have a casting vote.

(6) Subject to section 6C, each member of the Council shall hold office for a term of 4 years and shall be eligible for re-election at the end of any term of office.

(7) Six members of the Council shall form a quorum.

(8) Any Councillor is liable to dismissal by the Minister on the grounds as specified in the regulations.

(9) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Council may determine its own procedures.

[s 3 subst Act 8 of 1996 s 5, effective 30 August 1996]

## s 3A Disqualification for election as member of Council and for appointment as member of the Board

No person shall be qualified to be elected as a member of the Council or appointed as a member of the Board who—

(a) is an undischarged bankrupt, having been adjudged or otherwise declared bankrupt under any law in force in Fiji or in any State outside Fiji;

(b) is under any law in force in Fiji, adjudged or otherwise declared to be of unsound mind;

(c) is under sentence of death imposed on him or her by a court in Fiji or in any State outside Fiji, or is under sentence of imprisonment (by whatever name called) for a term exceeding 12 months imposed on him or her by such a court or substituted by competent authority for some other sentence imposed on him or her by such a court.

[ s 3A insrt Act 8 of 1996 s 6, effective 30 August 1996]

## s 4 Powers of Council

(1) The Council shall have power to hold land in accordance with the provisions of the Banaban Lands Act 1965, to enter into such contracts as are necessary and incidental to the exercise of their powers under this or any other Act or which are for the benefit of the Banaban community, and to sue and be sued in contract or otherwise in relation to the exercise of any power given by this Act.

(2) There shall be an executive director of the Council who shall also be the Council’s Secretary.

[subs (2) subst Act 8 of 1996 s 7, effective 30 August 1996]

(3) Service of any document on the executive director as secretary to the Council shall be deemed to be service on the Council.

[subs (3) insrt Act 8 of 1996 s 7, effective 30 August 1996]

## s 5 Regulations

(1) The Council may, subject to the prior approval of the Minister, make regulations to provide for the peace, order and good government of the Banaban community.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1), regulations made pursuant thereto may provide for—

(a) the imposition of a tax or cess upon any agricultural product;

(b) the imposition of rates by the Council;

(c) the powers, duties and functions of the Tribunal and the jurisdiction, powers and procedure of the Rabi Island Court and the fees which may be charged in connection with the practice and procedure of the Rabi Island Court;

(d) Subject to the provisions of the Public Health Act 1935, keeping the island clean and promoting public health;

(e) the maintenance of peace, order and public safety;

(f) the social and economic betterment of the Banaban community;

(g) the performance of communal works and other communal activities;

(h) the holding of elections and the procedure to be adopted in connection therewith;

(i) the control of livestock;

(j) the prevention or removal of public nuisances;

(k) the care of children and aged persons;

(l) the conservation of food supplies;

(m) fishing and fishing rights;

(n) the island hospitals, prisons and schools; and

(o) the promotion of the general welfare of the Banaban community.

(3) Any person failing to comply with the provisions of any regulation made under this section shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100 or a term of imprisonment not exceeding 2 months or to both such fine and imprisonment.

## s 6 Borrowing powers

(1) The Council may from time to time raise loans for such purposes in such amounts and on such terms and conditions as shall be approved by the Minister responsible for finance.

(2) Such loans shall be secured on the property and revenues of the Council but not including the title of Rabi Island.

[subs (2) subst Act 8 of 1996 s 8, effective 30 August 1996]

[s 6 insrt Act 12 of 1973 s 2, effective 29 June 1973]

## s 6A Establishment of Banaban Trust Fund Board

(1) There is hereby established the Banaban Trust Fund Board.

(2) There shall be 4 members of the Board as follows—

(a) one nominated by the Council from its members;

(b) one nominated by the Minister responsible for finance who shall be a registered and practising chartered accountant;

(c) one Banaban to be selected jointly by the executive director and the Tribunal; and

(d) one chairperson appointed by the Minister.

(3) All appointments which shall be for a term of 4 years, shall be confirmed by the Minister by notice published in the Gazette.

(4) Three members including the chairperson shall form a quorum.

(5) The decisions of the Board shall be by a majority of votes and, in addition to an original vote, in any case where the voting is equal, the chairperson or the person presiding at the meeting shall have and exercise a casting vote.

(6) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Board may regulate its own procedures.

[s 6A subst Act 8 of 1996 s 9, effective 30 August 1996]

## s 6B Establishment of Banaban Trust Fund

(1) There is hereby established a Trust Fund to be known as the Banaban Trust Fund which shall be managed and controlled by the Board.

(2) There shall be paid into the Trust Fund—

(a) the sum of 10 million Australian dollars which shall be a capital payment to the Banaban community arising from mining operations by the British phosphate commissioners on Ocean Island and which shall be exempt from tax;

(b) subject to section 6C(1)(b), the income accruing to the Trust Fund from investments made with the moneys of the Fund.

(3) Nothing in this Act shall authorise the Board to transfer or assign to the Council or any other person, any part or all of the capital or capital gains or increase, referred to in subsection (2).

[subs (3) subst Act 8 of 1996 s 10, effective 30 August 1996]

[s 6B insrt Act 3 of 1981 s 2, effective 27 March 1981]

## s 6C Functions of the Board

(1) The functions of the Board shall be—

(a) to invest the Trust Fund in such securities and on such terms and conditions as the Board may determine;

(b) after making provision for taxation and the expenses of administration, to transmit to the Council all the net income arising from the investments made in terms of paragraph (a);

(c) to hold or refuse the transfer of income to the Council until and unless the Minister had received by 31 October each year, Council estimates for the following financial year;

(d) to hold or refuse the transfer of income to the Council until and unless the Minister had received by 30 June each year audited statement of account for the previous financial year, provided that before exercising the powers specified in paragraphs (c) and (d), the Board shall—

(i) report its intention to the Minister at least 30 days before exercising the power; and

(ii) the Minister shall be empowered in cases where necessary or in the best interest of the Banaban community, to direct supply or part of it under such conditions as he or she considers necessary.

[subs (1) am Act 8 of 1996 s 11, effective 30 August 1996]

(2) The Board may, in respect of moneys invested in accordance with subsection (1)(a) vary, sell and transfer any such investments as may from time to time be necessary for the proper administration of the Trust Fund.

[subs (2) insrt Act 3 of 1981 s 3, effective 27 March 1981]

(3) The Minister’s power to direct supply under this Section shall be limited to 3 months and if the Council is unable to complete and submit the estimates and the audited statement of accounts required in accordance with subsection (1), within that time, the Minister shall dissolve the Council.

[subs (3) insrt Act 8 of 1996 s 11, effective 30 August 1996]

(4) Upon the dissolution of the Council in accordance with subsection (3), the Minister shall appoint an Administrator vested with the powers of the Council to manage the affairs of the Council until a new Council is elected.

[subs (4) insrt Act 8 of 1996 s 11, effective 30 August 1996]

[s 6C insrt Act 3 of 1981 s 3, effective 27 March 1981]

## s 6D Application of trust income

It shall be the duty of the Council to expend the income transmitted to it under section 6C(1)(b) in promoting the social and economic welfare of the Banaban community as a whole.

[s 6D insrt Act 3 of 1981 s 3, effective 27 March 1981]

## s 6E Powers of the Board

The Board may—

(a) enter into such contracts or agreements as are necessary or incidental to the exercise of its functions;

(b) appoint and employ at such remuneration and on such terms and conditions as it sees fit such officers and servants as it considers necessary for the proper carrying out of its functions;

(c) sue and be sued in contract or otherwise in relation to the exercise of any of its functions.

[s 6E insrt Act 3 of 1981 s 3, effective 27 March 1981]

## s 6F Protection from personal liability

No act or thing done by the Board or by any member thereof or by any officer or servant thereof or by any person acting under the direction of the Board shall, if the act was done bona fide for the purposes of carrying out the provisions of this Act, subject him or her personally to any liability, claim or demand whatsoever.

[s 6F insrt Act 3 of 1981 s 3, effective 27 March 1981]

## s 6G Execution and service of documents

(1) All documents to which the Board is a party may be signed on behalf of the Board either by the chairperson or by a member of the Board generally or specially authorised by a resolution passed at a meeting of the Board.

(2) Service of any document upon the Board shall be deemed to be effected by delivering the same or sending it by registered post to the chairperson.

[s 6G insrt Act 3 of 1981 s 3, effective 27 March 1981]

## s 6H Powers of expenditure

The Board may pay from the income of the Trust Fund—

(a) any expenses lawfully incurred by it including legal, accounting and other fees and costs;

(b) any other expenses, cost or expenditure properly incurred by it in the discharge of its functions under this Act.

[s 6H insrt Act 3 of 1981 s 3, effective 27 March 1981]

## s 6I Accounts and audit

The Board shall keep accounts of its transactions to the satisfaction of the Minister and such accounts shall be audited annually by an auditor appointed by the Minister in consultation with the Board.

[s 6I subst Act 8 of 1996 s 12, effective 30 August 1996]

## s 6J Report

(1) The Board shall, not later than 6 months from the end of each calendar year, submit a report to the Minister containing—

(a) an account of its activities throughout such year; and

(b) a statement of the accounts of the Board audited in accordance with section 6I.

(2) The Minister shall lay a copy of the report before Parliament at the next meeting subsequent to the submission thereof.

[s 6J insrt Act 3 of 1981 s 3, effective 27 March 1981]

## s 7 Rabi Island Fund

(1) There is hereby established a Fund to be known as the Rabi Island Fund.

(2) There shall be paid into the Fund—

(a) all tax or cess payable to the Fund;

(b) all fines imposed by the Rabi Island Court;

(c) all fees payable in connection with the practice and procedure of the Rabi Island Court;

(d) all rates imposed by the Council;

(e) all moneys standing to the credit of the Banaban Trust Fund at the date of repeal of the Banaban Funds Ordinance 1948;

(f) all royalties and other moneys accruing to the Banaban community in respect of minerals mined by the British Phosphate Commissioners on Ocean Island;

(g) all other sums from time to time contributed or donated to the Fund;

(h) all net income transmitted by the Banaban Trust Fund Board in accordance with section 6C(1)(b).

[subs (2) am Act 8 of 1996 s 13, effective 30 August 1996]

(3) All moneys paid into the Fund under the provisions of subsection (2)(f) of shall be exempt from income tax.

(4) The Fund shall be under the control of the Council and, subject to subsection (5), may be expended in accordance with regulations made under the provisions of this Act and shall be audited annually by a person appointed by the Minister.

[ subs (4) am Act 12 of 1973 s 3, effective 29 June 1973]

(5) Any moneys forming part of the Fund may from time to time be invested by the Council—

(a) in any security or securities authorised under the provisions of any law relating to trustees for the time being in force; or

(b) in such other security or securities, and on such terms and conditions, as shall be approved by the Minister responsible for finance.

[ subs (5) insrt Act 12 of 1973 s 3, effective 29 June 1973]

## s 8 Rabi Court and Tribunal

(1) The Minister may with the prior approval of the Council appoint any fit and proper person to be the Rabi Island Tribunal who shall hold office for a term of 3 years and shall be eligible for reappointment at the end of any term of office.

(2) There shall be on Rabi a court to be called the Rabi Island Court which shall consist of the Tribunal and such other person or persons as the Minister may appoint and shall be held at such times and places as may be necessary.

(3) An appeal shall lie from the Rabi Island Court to a Resident Magistrate upon the same terms and conditions and in the manner prescribed by the Criminal Procedure Act 2009 for appeals from the Magistrates Court to the High Court, except that whatever the punishment inflicted by the Rabi Island Court no leave to appeal shall be required.

[subs (3) am Act 31 of 2016 s 11, effective 1 December 2016]

(4) The Magistrates Court hearing such appeals shall have the same powers as the High Court when hearing an appeal from a Magistrates Court except the power of summary dismissal.

(5) In applying the aforesaid provisions of the Criminal Procedure Act 2009 to an appeal brought under this section, references to the Chief Registrar shall be construed as references to the Clerk to the Magistrates Court.

[subs (5) am Act 31 of 2016 s 11, effective 1 December 2016]

(6) The decision of a Magistrates Court in relation to appeals under this section shall be final and conclusive.
