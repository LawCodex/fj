# PRESIDENTIAL PENSIONS ACT 1994

AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE PAYMENT OF PENSIONS TO PERSONS WHO HAVE SERVED AS GOVERNORS-GENERAL AND PRESIDENTS OF FIJI OR PRESIDENT OR VICE-PRESIDENT OF FIJI AND FOR MATTERS INCIDENTAL THERETO

[Long title am Act 6 of 1999 s 3, 18 January 1999]

Presidential Pensions Act 1994 (No 15 of 1994) commenced on 1 February 1983, as amended by:

| President’s Pension (Amendment) Act 1999 (No 6 of 1999) | 18 January 1999 |

## s 1 Short title

(1) This Act may be cited as the Presidential Pensions Act 1994.

(2) This Act is deemed to have come into force on 1 February 1983.

[s 1 insrt Act 6 of 1999 s 4, 18 January 1999]

## s 2 Interpretation

(1) In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Governor-General** means a person appointed as Governor-General of Fiji under section 27 of the 1970 Constitution of Fiji;

**President** means a person appointed as—

(a) President of the Republic of Fiji under section 4 of the Appointment of Head of State and Dissolution of Fiji Military Government Decree 1987;

(b) President of the Republic of Fiji under section 2 of the Head of State and Executive Authority Decree 1988;

(c) President of the Republic of Fiji under section 31 of the 1990 Constitution of the Sovereign Democratic Republic of Fiji; or

(d) President of the Republic of Fiji under section 90 of the Constitution Amendment Act 1997; or

(e) President of the Republic of Fiji under section 84 of the Constitution of the Republic of Fiji; and

[def am Act 31 of 2016 s 167, 1 December 2016]

**Vice-President** means a person appointed as Vice-President of the Republic of Fiji under section 90 of the Constitution Amendment Act 1997.

(2) For the purposes of this Act—

(a) Governor-General does not include a person who acted as such under section 28 of the 1970 Constitution of Fiji;

(b) President does not include a person who acted as such under section 33 of the 1990 Constitution of the Sovereign Democratic Republic of Fiji;

(c) President and Vice-President do not include a person who acts as such under section 88(3) of the Constitution Amendment Act 1997; and

(d) a reference to a person ceasing to be President or Vice-President does not include a person ceasing to be such by virtue of a provision relating to removal from office.

[s 2 insrt Act 6 of 1999 s 5, 18 January 1999]

## s 3 Pension payable to Governor-General and President

(1) Every person who, on or after 1 February 1983, ceases to be Governor-General and every person who ceases at any time to be President, shall be paid an annual pension in respect of his or her service as Governor-General or President, as the case may be, in accordance with the following formula—

(a) where he or she has been Governor-General or President for not more than 2 years, 20% of the basic salary payable to him or her at the time he or she so ceases to be Governor-General or President, as the case may be;

(b) where he or she has been Governor-General or President for more than 2 years but not more than 5 years, the aggregate of the following—

(i) 20% of the basic salary payable to him or her at the time he or she so ceases to be Governor-General or President, as the case may be; and

(ii) 10% of such basic salary for each complete year following the expiration of the first 2 years;

(c) where he or she has been Governor-General or President for more than 5 years but not more than 10 years, the aggregate of the following—

(i) 50% of the basic salary payable to him or her at the time he or she so ceases to be Governor-General or President, as the case may be; and

(ii) 5% of such basic salary for each complete year following the expiration of the first 5 years;

(d) where he or she has been Governor-General or President for more than 10 years, 75% of the basic salary payable to him or her at the time he or she so ceases to be Governor-General or President, as the case may be.

[subs (1) am Act 6 of 1999 s 6, 18 January 1999]

(2) Where a person has held the office of Governor-General and subsequently succeeded to the office of the President, the periods during which he or she held the 2 offices shall be aggregated for the purpose of calculating the yearly rate of pension payable to him or her under this section.

(3) In this section, references to the President includes references to the Vice-President with effect from 18 January 1999.

[subs (3) insrt Act 6 of 1999 s 6, 18 January 1999]

## s 4 Right to choose

Notwithstanding section 3 every person who ceases to be President, or Vice-President and who had previously served in the office of Prime Minister for a term that could qualify him or her for pension payable to a Prime Minister under any other law and that his or her entitlement if he or she were to receive that pension would be greater than the entitlement under this Act, shall have the option to receive the annual pension in relation to that person's service as Prime Minister in place of the pension payable under this Act.

[s 4 am Act 6 of 1999 s 7, 18 January 1999]

## s 5 Gratuity payment

(1) A person to whom a pension is payable under this Act shall, where he or she has exercised his or her option as herein provided, but not otherwise, be paid in lieu of the pension, pension at the rate of three-fourths of the pension, together with a gratuity equal to 12.5 times the amount of the annual reduction so made in the pension.

(2) The option referred to in subsection (1) shall be exercisable, and where it has been exercised, may be revoked, not later than the day immediately preceding the date of such person ceasing to be President; but Cabinet may, where it appears equitable to do so, allow him or her to exercise the option or revoke an option previously exercised at any time between that date and the actual date of the first payment of any pension under this Act.

(3) Subject to subsection (2) , where such person has exercised the option referred to in subsection (1) , his or her decision shall be irrevocable so far as concerns any pension paid to him or her under this Act.

(4) Where a person who has not exercised the option dies after he or she has ceased to be President but before a pension has been paid under this Act, there shall be granted to his or her legal personal representatives a gratuity and a reduced pension as provided for in subsection (1) as if that person before his or her death had exercised the option therein referred to, so, however, that such pension shall not be paid in respect of any period later than the date of the death of such person and shall not exceed in the aggregate the total amount due and payable to him or her on the said date.

(5) The date of the exercise of the option by such person shall be deemed to be the date of the receipt of his or her written notification addressed to the Secretary to Cabinet.

(6) A person in receipt of a pension under this Act who again becomes President or Vice-President, is not entitled to receive and shall not be paid pension under this Act—

(a) during such time as he or she is President or Vice-President; and

(b) in respect of any period during which he or she became or again became President or Vice-President, respectively.

[subs (6) am Act 6 of 1999 s 8, 18 January 1999]

## s 6 Gratuity payable to legal personal representatives

(1) Where the President or Vice-President dies while he or she is holding office and does not leave a widow or a widower, a gratuity of an amount equivalent to 12.5 times one-quarter of the 60% of his or her annual pension shall be paid to his or her legal personal representatives on account of the deceased person's estate.

[subs (1) am Act 6 of 1999 s 9, 18 January 1999]

(2) Notwithstanding anything contained in any law, a gratuity payable under subsection (1) is exempt from the payment of estate duty under the Estate and Gift Duties Act 1966.

## s 7 Pension payable to surviving widow or widower of a President

(1) Where a person dies holding the office of President or Vice-President and he or she leaves a widow or a widower, the widow or the widower shall be paid—

(a) a gratuity of an amount equivalent to 12.5 times one-quarter of his or her pensionable emoluments at the date of his or her death; and

(b) a pension at an annual rate equivalent to 60% of his or her pensionable emoluments at the date of his or her death.

[subs (1) am Act 6 of 1999 s 10, 18 January 1999]

(2) Where a person dies after having retired from the office of President or Vice President, there shall be granted to his widow or her widower an annual pension equivalent to 60% of the annual pension to which that person was entitled.

[subs (2) am Act 6 of 1999 s 10, 18 January 1999]

## s 8 Other benefits

(1) The following additional benefits shall apply to a person in receipt of a pension under this Act until his or her demise provided that such person had completed 3 years service—

(i) provision for the installation, connection, maintenance and rental of a telephone in any private residence of his or hers, and to meet all charges in respect of local calls originating from such telephone;

(ii) provision for a Ministerial-type vehicle and a driver, including free travel on scheduled trips of government ships;

(iii) medical treatment, hospitalisation, drugs and all other hospital services locally,

provided that where local specialist treatment and medical treatment overseas including the cost of treatment, accommodation and air travel are necessary, such expenditure may be incurred at the discretion of the Prime Minister;

(iv) the provision of an annual budgetary allocation of an amount of $30,000 per annum and subject to periodic adjustments, to enable the person in receipt of a pension, to hire personal staff as required; and

(v) provision of security protection at the discretion of the Prime Minister.

[subs (1) am Act 16 of 2024 s 2, 10 November 2024]

(2) The additional benefits specified in subsection (1) shall not apply to a surviving widow or widower or children of a person in receipt of a pension under this Act.

## s 9 Further allowances

Cabinet may authorise the payment to a person entitled to a pension under this Act, of such further allowances as Cabinet may deem fit, provided that an allowance payable to any person under this section which may be payable as a capital sum, shall not form part of the person's entitlement to a pension under this Act, unless Cabinet so directs.

## s 10 Source and method of payment of pensions

Any pension payable under this Act—

(a) shall be a charge on the Consolidated Fund; and

(b) shall be paid fortnightly in arrears free of taxation.

## s 11 Repeal of Act

The Governor-General (Pensions) Act is repealed.
