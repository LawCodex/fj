# LAW REFORM (PERSONAL INJURIES) ACT 1953

AN ACT TO ABOLISH THE DEFENCE OF COMMON EMPLOYMENT

**Table of Amendments**

## s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Law Reform (Personal Injuries) Act 1953.

## s 2 Common employment

(1) It shall not be a defence to an employer who is sued in respect of personal injuries caused by the negligence of a person employed by him or her that that person was at the time the injuries were caused in common employment with the person injured.

(2) Any provision contained in a contract of service or apprenticeship or in an agreement collateral thereto (including a contract or agreement entered into before the commencement of this Act) shall be void in so far as it would have the effect of excluding or limiting any liability of the employer in respect of personal injuries caused to the person employed or apprenticed by the negligence of any person in common employment with him or her.

## s 3 Application to State

This Act shall bind the State.
