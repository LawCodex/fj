# 2024–2025 APPROPRIATION ACT 2024

AN ACT TO APPROPRIATE A SUM OF THREE BILLION, NINE HUNDRED FIFTY FIVE MILLION, EIGHT HUNDRED NINETEEN THOUSAND, FOUR HUNDRED AND SEVENTY NINE DOLLARS FOR THE ORDINARY SERVICES OF GOVERNMENT FOR THE YEAR ENDING 31 JULY 2025

**Table of Amendments** 2024–2025 Appropriation Act 2024 (No 3 of 2024) commenced on 1 August 2024, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## s 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Act may be cited as the 2024–2025 Appropriation Act 2024.

(2) This Act comes into force on 1 August 2024.

## s 2 Appropriation of $3,955,819,479

A sum of $3,955,819,479 is to be issued from the Consolidated Fund or other funds of the State and applied to the ordinary services of the Government for the year ending 31 July 2025, and such sum is to be appropriated for the purposes specified in schedules 1 and 2 and also the 2024–2025 Budget Estimates.

## s 3 Government borrowing

The Government borrowing for 2024–2025 must not exceed the sum of $984,609,479.
