## s 6 Pensions

(1) Where a Judge who has attained the age of 60 years retires after serving as Judge for not less than 10 years, he or she shall be entitled to a pension at a rate equal to 60% of his or her salary.

[subs (1) am Act 4 of 1999 s 2, effective 19 March 1999]

(2) Where a Judge, not being a Judge to whom subsection (1) applies, retires, and the President, in the case of the Chief Justice, or the Judicial Services Commission, in the case of any other Judge, certifies that his or her retirement is due to permanent disability or infirmity, he or she shall be entitled—

(a) if his or her retirement occurs before he or she has completed 6 years’ service as a Judge to a pension at a rate equal to 40% of his or her salary; or

(b) in any other case, to a pension at a rate equal to the sum of—

(i) 40% of his or her salary; and

(ii) 2% of his or her salary for each completed year of service other than the first 5 years,

but so that the rate of his or her pension shall not exceed 50% of his or her salary.

[subs (2) am Act 17 of 2002 ss 7, 8, effective 1 January 2002; Act 31 of 2016 s 111, effective 1 December 2016]

## s 7 Widower's or widow’s pensions and other entitlements

(1) Where a Judge dies in office leaving a widower or widow, a pension shall be paid to the widower or widow at a rate equal to 60% of the rate of the pension that would have been payable to the Judge, had he or she been entitled to such pension under section 6, if the Judge had retired on the date of his or her death and, in a case in which subsection (1) of section 6 would not have been applicable in relation to that retirement, the President or the Judicial Services Commission, as the case may be, had certified that that retirement was due to permanent disability or infirmity.

[subs (1) am Act 4 of 1999 s 3, effective 19 March 1999; Act 17 of 2002 ss 7, 8, effective 1 January 2002; Act 31 of 2016 s 111, effective 1 December 2016]

(2) Where a retired Judge dies leaving a widower or widow whose marriage to the retired Judge occurred before his or her retirement, a pension shall be paid to the widower or widow at a rate equal to 60% of the rate of the pension that would have been payable to the retired Judge had he or she been entitled to such pension under section 6 and if he or she had not died.

[subs (2) am Act 4 of 1999 s 3, effective 19 March 1999]

(3) If a widower or widow in receipt of a pension under the provisions of this section remarries, his or her pension shall cease.

(4) In the event of the death of a Judge whilst in office, his widow or her widower shall be entitled to such compensation in lieu of the outstanding leave accruing due to him or her at the date of his or her death as the Judicial Services Commission shall determine.

[subs (4) am Act 31 of 2016 s 111, effective 1 December 2016]

(5) Where a Judge dies whilst in office leaving a widower or widow or other members of his or her family living in Fiji at the date of his or her death, the widower or widow and such other members of his or her family as the Judicial Services Commission shall direct, shall be entitled to such passages or passage allowances as that Commission shall deem fit.

[subs (5) am Act 31 of 2016 s 111, effective 1 December 2016]

## s 8 Pensions not payable in certain circumstances

Unless the President otherwise directs, a pension under this Act shall not be payable by reason of the service of a Judge who has been removed under section 112 of the Constitution of the Republic of Fiji or under any similar provision in any written law.

[s 8 am Act 17 of 2002 ss 5, 8, effective 1 January 2002; Act 31 of 2016 s 111, effective 1 December 2016]
