# FILM FIJI ACT 2002

AN ACT TO ESTABLISH FILM FIJI FOR THE PURPOSE OF DEVELOPING AND PROMOTING THE AUDIO-VISUAL INDUSTRY IN FIJI AND FOR RELATED MATTERS

[Long title am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

## s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Film Fiji Act 2002.

[s 1 subst Decree 48 of 2012 s 2, effective 14 May 2012]

## s 2 Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

**aim of Film Fiji** means the aim described in section 5(1);

[def am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

**audio-visual industry** means the production and distribution of large format films, theatrical feature films, show films, documentary films, television programs, direct to video or video disk programs, radio audio recordings, and computer software and includes the establishment of interactive web sites and other electronic commerce business; and

**Commission** [def rep Decree 48 of 2012 s 3, effective 14 May 2012]

**Film Fiji** means the body established under section 3.

[def insrt Decree 48 of 2012 s 3, effective 14 May 2012]

## s 3 Establishment of Film Fiji

(1) Film Fiji is established under this section as a body corporate with perpetual succession.

[subs (1) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(2) Film Fiji has power to hold land and other property, to enter into agreements, to borrow or lend money, to mortgage, pledge, sell or otherwise encumber or dispose of its property, to employ and dismiss persons, to sue and be sued, and generally to do everything that a legal person can lawfully do to achieve the aim of Film Fiji.

[subs (2) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

## s 4 Composition of Film Fiji

(1) Film Fiji shall consist of the following members to be appointed by the Minister—

(a) a chairperson; and

(b) 7 other members.

[subs (1) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(2) Members of Film Fiji may be appointed for any period up to 3 years and are eligible for reappointment for such further term or terms as the Minister may see fit.

[subs (2) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(3) Members of Film Fiji are entitled to allowances to be fixed by the Minister.

[subs (3) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

[s 4 subst Decree 9 of 2011 s 2, effective 15 February 2011]

## s 5 Aim and functions of Film Fiji

(1) The aim of Film Fiji is to develop and promote the audio-visual industry in Fiji.

[subs (1) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(2) Film Fiji may—

(a) engage in any commercial activities relating to its aim which will, or are intended to, generate more income for Film Fiji;

(b) subject to section 10, receive moneys from any source;

(c) make grants and loans to any individual who is a citizen of Fiji or a company or body wholly owned by a citizen of Fiji and the company or body has its principal place of operation in Fiji for the purpose of achieving its aim;

(d) perform the functions conferred on it by the Income Tax (Film-making and Audio-visual Incentives) Regulations 2016 in connection with film-making and audio-visual tax incentives;

(e) establish an audio-visual collection to record the achievements of the audio-visual industry in Fiji;

(f) establish an audio-visual school to provide training in all aspects of the audio-visual industry in consultation with the Fiji National University;

(g) perform other functions conferred on it by any other written law;

(h) generally, do any other thing necessary to achieve its aim.

[subs (2) am Decree 58 of 2010 s 29, effective 29 November 2010; Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012; Act 31 of 2016 s 85, effective 1 December 2016]

## s 6 Grants and loans

(1) In making grants or loans under section 5(2)(c), Film Fiji must give priority to individuals who are Fijian citizens or companies or bodies wholly owned by a citizen of Fiji whose principal place of operation is in Fiji, which otherwise might have difficulty in raising money for audio-visual productions.

[subs (1) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(2) The conditions of grants and the rate of interest on loans are as determined by Film Fiji generally or in particular cases.

[subs (2) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

## s 7 Meetings of Film Fiji

(1) Film Fiji must meet at least 4 times a year and may meet at other times as the chairperson determines or the members decide.

[subs (1) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(2) A quorum at a meeting of Film Fiji is 4 members.

[subs (2) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(3) Decisions are taken by majority vote of the members present but if the votes are equal the chairperson has the casting vote.

(4) A member of Film Fiji or a person invited under section 8 who has an interest in a matter before Film Fiji must declare his or her personal interest and must not participate in any discussion or voting on the matter.

[subs (4) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(5) Film Fiji may conduct its business, other than decisions on grants or loans, by circulation of papers.

[subs (5) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(6) If the chairperson is absent from a meeting, the members present may elect a member to preside at that meeting.

(7) Film Fiji must have a seal and the fixing of the seal witnessed by the signature of the chairperson is sufficient evidence of any transaction entered into by Film Fiji or if the chairperson is not available, the seal is to be witnessed by the chief executive officer.

[subs (7) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

## s 8 Advice from other persons

Film Fiji may invite a person to advise it on any matters relating to its functions, either as members of a standing advisory panel or on an ad hoc basis.

[s 8 am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

## s 9 Staff of Film Fiji

(1) There shall be a chief executive officer appointed by Film Fiji in consultation with the Minister.

[subs (1) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(2) The chief executive officer is responsible for the overall administration of Film Fiji subject to the directions of Film Fiji.

[subs (2) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(3) Film Fiji may appoint other persons to assist the chief executive officer.

[subs (3) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(4) The terms and conditions of service of persons appointed under this section are determined by Film Fiji.

[subs (4) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(5) Public officers may be seconded to Film Fiji by the Public Service Commission.

[subs (5) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

## s 10 Finances

(1) The funds of Film Fiji consist of—

(a) money appropriated by Parliament to finance the functions of Film Fiji;

(b) money paid to Film Fiji by purchasers of its services or products;

(c) the audio-visual production levy paid to Film Fiji under the Income Tax (Film-making and Audio-visual Incentives) Regulations 2016;

(d) any donation or contribution from any source;

(e) any money borrowed by Film Fiji;

(f) interest on investments.

[subs (1) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012; Act 31 of 2016 s 85, effective 1 December 2016]

(2) The chief executive officer appointed under section 9(1) is the accounting officer for Film Fiji.

[subs (2) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(3) The accounts of Film Fiji must be audited by the Auditor-General each year.

[subs (3) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(4) Film Fiji may pay out as grants or loans 50% of its income in a financial year.

[subs (4) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

## s 11 Annual report etc

(1) Film Fiji must provide to the Minister any information which the Minister requires relating to the activities and the financial position of Film Fiji.

[subs (1) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(2) Film Fiji must send to the Minister within 6 months of the start of each financial year—

(a) a statement of its audited accounts for the preceding year; and

(b) a report on its activities, including details of the recipients of any grants or loans, during the preceding year.

[subs (2) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(3) The reports and accounts referred to in subsection (2) must be tabled by the Minister in Parliament at the next meeting of Parliament after the Minister receives them.

[subs (3) am Act 31 of 2016 s 85, effective 1 December 2016]

## s 12 Power to give directions

The Minister may, in consultation with Film Fiji, give specific or general policy directions to Film Fiji.

[s 12 am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

## s 13 Power to delegate

Film Fiji may delegate to any person as it sees fit any of its functions under this Act except its power under this section or section 5(2)(c).

[s 13 am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

## s 14 Regulations and rules

(1) The Minister may make regulations prescribing—

(a) the procedure for applying for grants or loans;

(b) appeals from a review of decisions of Film Fiji;

(c) matters necessary to regulate the establishment of the audio-visual collection or the audio-visual school;

(d) any other matter necessary to give effect to this Act.

[subs (1) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

(2) Film Fiji may make rules for the conduct of its meetings and for any other matter relating to its aims and functions.

[subs (2) am Decree 48 of 2012 s 4, effective 14 May 2012]

## s 15 Repeal and validation of acts

(1) The Fiji Audio-Visual Commission Decree 2000 (Decree No. 29 of 2000) is repealed.

(2) Any act done or appointment made by virtue of the Fiji Audio-Visual Commission Decree 2000 is deemed to have been validated as if such act had been done under this Act.
