# FIJI LAW REFORM COMMISSION ACT 1979

AN ACT TO ESTABLISH THE FIJI LAW REFORM COMMISSION AND FOR PURPOSES INCIDENTAL THERETO

**Table of Amendments**

## s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Fiji Law Reform Commission Act 1979.

## s 2 Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires, “Commission” means the Fiji Law Reform Commission established by this Act.

## s 3 Establishment of Commission

(1) There is hereby established a Commission to be known as the Fiji Law Reform Commission.

(2) The Commission shall consist of—

(a) a chairperson appointed by the Attorney-General in consultation with the Chief Justice; and

(b) not less than 3 other commissioners who shall be appointed by the Attorney-General for a period not exceeding 3 years on such terms, subject to subsection (7), as may be specified in their instruments of appointment, and be eligible for reappointment.

[subs (2) subst Act 16 of 1985 s 3, effective 31 January 1986; am Act 31 of 2016 s 77, effective 1 December 2016]

(3) The chairperson of the Commission shall be—

(a) a person qualified to be appointed as a Judge of the High Court;

(b) appointed for a term not exceeding 4 years on such terms, subject to subsection (5), as may be specified in his or her instrument of appointment; and

(c) eligible for reappointment,

provided that a person who has reached the retiring age for Judges shall not be precluded from appointment by reason of age.

[subs (3) subst Act 16 of 1985 s 3, effective 31 January 1986]

(4) Neither the chairperson, any commissioner nor any person appointed under subsection (6) shall by virtue of such appointment be deemed to be a public officer.

[subs (4) subst Act 16 of 1985 s 3, effective 31 January 1986]

(5) The chairperson of the Commission shall be entitled to such remuneration and allowances determined by the Attorney-General.

[subs (5) subst Act 31 of 2016 s 77, effective 1 December 2016]

(6) For the purposes of the examination by the Commission of any particular branch of the law, the Attorney-General may appoint on a temporary basis a committee, or a person otherwise than as a member of committee—

(a) to consider any matter which should, in the opinion of the Commission, be referred to such committee or person and to report thereon to the Commission; or

(b) to advise the Commission.

[subs (6) subst Act 16 of 1985 s 3, effective 31 January 1986]

(7) The members of the Commission other than the chairperson, and any person appointed under subsection (6), shall be paid such remuneration or allowances, or both, as may be determined by the Attorney-General.

[subs (7) subst Act 16 of 1985 s 3, effective 31 January 1986; am Act 31 of 2016 s 77, effective 1 December 2016]

(8) There shall be—

(a) a Director of the Commission who shall be legally qualified and who shall be appointed by the Solicitor-General;

(b) such other legally qualified officers appointed by the Solicitor-General as may be required for the proper conduct of the business of the Commission and for the purposes of this Act; and

(c) such other officers appointed by the Solicitor-General as may be so required.

[subs (8) subst Act 16 of 1985 s 3, effective 31 January 1986; am Act 31 of 2016 s 77, effective 1 December 2016]

## s 4 Protection of Commission, powers and procedures

(1) Neither the Commission, any commissioner, nor any person appointed under section 3(6) shall be liable to any action or suit for any matter or thing done by it or him or her in carrying out its or commissioner’s functions under this Act.

(2) The power of the Commission to perform its functions is not impaired by reason of the absence or inability to act of any commissioner, or the director.

(3) Subject to this Act, the Commission shall determine its own procedure.

[s 4 subst Act 16 of 1985 s 4, effective 31 January 1986]

## s 5 Functions of the Commission

(1) The functions of the Commission shall be to take and keep under review all the law applicable of Fiji with a view to its systematic development and reform including in particular—

(a) the codification of such law;

(b) the elimination of anomalies;

(c) the repeal of obsolete and unnecessary enactments;

(d) the reduction of separate enactments;

(e) the making of new laws;

(f) the adoption of new or more effective and economical methods for the administration of the law and the dispensation of justice; and

(g) generally the simplification, improvement and modernisation of the law,

and, subject to section 6, the Commission may in these respects act of its own volition.

(2) For the purposes of carrying out its functions, the Commission shall, subject to section 6 —

(a) consider any subject referred to it by the Attorney-General;

(b) receive and consider any proposal for the reform of the law which may be made or referred to it;

(c) at the request of the Attorney-General, provide assistance to any ministry or department of Government by undertaking the examination of any particular branch of the law and making recommendations for the reform of that branch to bring it into accord with current conditions; and

(d) consider the impact on the statute law of any judicial decision.

(3) The Commission shall, as soon as practicable after 31 December in every year, submit to the Attorney-General a report on the activities and proceedings of the Commission during the year ended on that 31 December, and any such report shall be laid before Parliament.

(4) In addition to the report mentioned in subsection (3) the Commission shall furnish to the Attorney-General in accordance with his directions such reports as the Attorney-General may require in relation to particular matters.

[s 5 subst Act 16 of 1985 s 6, effective 31 January 1986]

## s 6 Approval of work programme

The Commission shall, before setting a work programme for any year, seek the approval of such programme by the Cabinet through the Attorney-General, and the implementation of such programme is subject to that approval and the degree of priority (if any) decided on by the Cabinet and notified by the Attorney-General to the Commission.

[s 6 insrt Act 16 of 1985 s 6, effective 31 January 1986]
