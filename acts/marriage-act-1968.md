## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [ss 1, 2]

### s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Marriage Act 1968.

### s 2 Interpretation

In this Act unless the context otherwise requires—

**civil marriage celebrant** means a person registered as a marriage officer under section 5A;

[def insrt Decree 64 of 2010 s 2, effective 13 December 2010]

**district** means the registration area of a district registry under the provisions of the Births, Deaths and Marriage Registration Act 1975;

**district registrar** has the same meaning as in the Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration Act 1975;

**existing marriage officer** means a person who is registered as a marriage officer under section 4 or 5;

[def insrt Decree 33 of 2009 Sch, effective 1 December 2009]

**Magistrate** means a Magistrate appointed under the provisions of the Magistrates Court Act 1944 and includes every iTaukei Magistrate appointed under the iTaukei Affairs Act 1944;

[def am Decree 31 of 2010 s 3, effective 2 July 2010]

**marriage officer** means the Registrar-General or a district registrar or any person registered as a marriage officer under the provisions of this Act or any other suitable person appointed by the Registrar-General by notice in the Gazette for the purpose of solemnising marriages on his or her behalf;

**minister of religion** means a person recognised by a religious body or organisation of the Christian faith as having authority to solemnise marriages in accordance with the rites or customs of such body or organisation;

**minor** means a person under the age of 18 years;

[def am Decree 26 of 2009 s 2, effective 24 July 2009]

**new marriage officer** means a person who is registered as a marriage officer under section 4 or 5;

[def insrt Decree 33 of 2009 Sch, effective 1 December 2009]

**prescribed information for marriage officers** means written information for marriage officers regarding the prevention of domestic violence, prescribed by regulations made under section 38; and

[def insrt Decree 33 of 2009 Sch, effective 1 December 2009]

**prescribed information for those intending to marry** means a written information for those intending to marry regarding the prevention of domestic violence, prescribed by regulations made under section 38.

[def insrt Decree 33 of 2009 Sch, effective 1 December 2009]

## PART 2 MARRIAGE OFFICERS [ss 3– 11A]

### s 3 Registrar-General or district registrar may solemnise marriage

The Registrar-General may solemnise marriages in any place in Fiji and a district registrar may solemnise marriages within the limits of his or her district.

### s 4 Registration of ministers as marriage officers

The Registrar-General, on application in the prescribed form in the name of any minister of religion under the hand of such minister or that of the head in Fiji of the denomination to which he or she belongs, may register such minister as a marriage officer for solemnising marriages in Fiji.

### s 5 Registration of other marriage officers

Any person, other than a minister of religion, professing any recognised religion may apply to the Registrar-General, in the prescribed manner, for registration as a marriage officer in which event the Registrar-General may, in his or her discretion, upon being satisfied that there is a need for a marriage officer of that particular religion, register such person as a marriage officer for the purpose of solemnising marriages in Fiji.

### s 5A Registration of civil marriage celebrants

(1) Any person other than a public officer may apply to the Attorney-General in the prescribed form for registration as a civil marriage celebrant.

(2) The Attorney-General upon receiving an application must be satisfied, amongst other things, that the applicant—

(a) is a person who is of good character and standing in the community;

(b) will diligently perform the duties of a marriage officer pursuant to this Act; and

(c) is able to satisfy all other relevant conditions applicable to the regulations.

(3) The Registrar-General shall enter all names of persons approved under this section onto the annual list of marriage officers.

(4) The Attorney-General may also specify conditions applicable to the licence issued under this section.

(5) The Attorney-General may revoke a licence if any of the terms, conditions or regulations are breached.

[s 5A insrt Decree 64 of 2010 s 3, effective 13 December 2010]

### s 5B Regulations

The Attorney-General may make regulations to govern and implement this Act.

[s 5B insrt Decree 64 of 2010 s 4, effective 13 December 2010]

### s 6 Change of address etc to be notified

(1) Whenever any marriage officer registered under the provisions of either of sections 4 or 5 changes his or her name, address or designation he or she shall, within one month next after such change, notify the Registrar-General thereof.

(2) The Registrar-General, may, upon receiving notification of any change under the provisions of subsection (1), amend the registration and shall, if he or she deems it necessary, publish particulars of the change in the Gazette.

### s 7 Notification of registration of marriage officers

When the Registrar-General registers a marriage officer, under the provisions of section 5, he or she shall, within one month after such registration, notify in the Gazette the name of the marriage officer so registered.

### s 8 Annual list of marriage officers’ names

The Registrar-General shall, once in every year, publish in the Gazette a list of the names, addresses, designations and denominations of all marriage officers then duly registered and every such annual publication shall be prima facie evidence in all courts of justice that the persons therein named and no others were at the time of such publication duly registered as marriage officers.

### s 9 When names of marriage officers to be omitted

Whenever the Registrar-General shall become aware that any registered marriage officer is dead or has left Fiji or has ceased to officiate as a marriage officer or when the registration of any marriage officer has been cancelled under the provisions of section 10, he or she shall omit the name of such marriage officer from the next annual publication of the names of registered marriage officers.

### s 10 Cancellation of marriage officers’ registration

The Registrar-General may, in any case where he or she in his or her discretion deems such action advisable, cancel at any time the registration of any marriage officer and publication in the Gazette shall be sufficient notification of such cancellation.

### s 11 When marriage officers not compellable to solemnise marriage

Nothing contained in this Act shall be deemed to compel any marriage officer registered under the provisions of sections 4 or 5 to solemnise any marriage between parties either of whom shall not be a member of his or her own religious denomination or otherwise than according to the rites and usages of such denomination nor unless he or she shall be satisfied by the declaration of the parties or otherwise that the proposed marriage is consistent with such rites and usages.

### s 11A Provision of prescribed information to marriage officers

(1) The Registrar-General shall provide the prescribed information for marriage officers and an appropriate number of copies of the prescribed information for those intending to marry to—

(a) each existing marriage officer, within one month of coming into force of this section; and

(b) each new marriage officer, within one month of the Registrar-General registering the person as a marriage officer under section 4 or 5.

(2) The Registrar-General shall, when requested by a marriage officer, promptly provide an appropriate number of additional copies of the prescribed information for marriage officers and the prescribed information for those intending to marry.

[s 11A insrt Decree 33 of 2009 Sch, effective 1 December 2009]

## PART 3 MARRIAGEABLE AGE AND MARRIAGE OF MINORS [s 12]

### s 12 Marriageable age

Any person may contract a valid marriage under the provisions of this Act, if such person is of the age of 18 years or upwards.

[s 12 subst Decree 26 of 2009 s 3, effective 24 July 2009]

## PART 4 MARRIAGES [ss 13– 23]

### s 13 Prohibition of plurality of marriage

Marriage in Fiji shall be the voluntary union of one man to one woman to the exclusion of all others.

[s 13 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 14 Notice of marriage

(1) Where a marriage is intended to be solemnised under the provisions of this Act, notice of marriage in the prescribed form shall be given—

(a) if the parties to be married have resided in the same district for the period of 7 days immediately before the giving of the notice, by either of the parties to the district registrar of that district;

(b) if the parties to the marriage have not resided in the same district for such period of 7 days, by each party to the district registrar of the district where such individual party resides,

provided that the notice of marriage shall, in the district of Suva, be given to the Registrar-General.

(2) Every person giving notice of marriage under the provisions of subsection (1) shall make a solemn declaration in the prescribed form before the Registrar-General or a district registrar to whom such notice is required to be given.

[subs (2) am Decree 26 of 2009 s 5, effective 24 July 2009]

(3) The Registrar-General or a district registrar may require every person giving notice under the provisions of this section to produce documentary evidence to verify the particulars given or statements made in such notice.

[s 14 renum Decree 26 of 2009 ss 5 and 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 15 Filing and posting up of notice

The Registrar-General or the district registrar, as the case may be, shall file in his or her office every such notice and shall forthwith post up in some conspicuous place in or near such office a true copy of every such notice and shall keep the same so posted up during 21 consecutive days before the marriage is solemnised under the notice. At the same time a true copy of every such notice shall also be similarly posted up in some conspicuous place in the registry office nearest to the place of residence of each party to the intended marriage.

[s 15 renum Decree 26 of 2009 ss 5 and 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 16 New notice etc required if marriage not solemnised within 3 months

In the event of the marriage intended to be solemnised not taking place within 3 months after the date of the posting up of the notice under the provisions of section 15, the notice and any certificate for marriage granted in connection therewith shall be void and the intended marriage shall not be solemnised under such notice or certificate.

[s 16 renum Decree 26 of 2009 ss 5 and 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 17 Certificate after 21 days’ notice

(1) At any time not more than 3 months nor less than 21 clear days after the posting up of the copy of the notice referred to in section 15, the Registrar-General or the district registrar, as the case may be, upon request by or on behalf of the party by whom such notice was given, and in the case no lawful impediment shall have been shown to the satisfaction of the Registrar-General or the district registrar, as the case may be, why such certificate should not issue, shall issue under his or her hand a certificate in the prescribed form to the effect that the notice required by section 14 has been duly given and that 21 clear days have elapsed since the date of the posting up of the copy of such notice.

(2) If any person shall knowingly and willfully marry without a certificate for marriage (except as provided in section 19) the marriage of such person shall be null and void.

[s 17 renum Decree 26 of 2009 ss 5 and 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 18 Provision for issue of certificate for marriage where one of the parties resides in England etc

(1) Where a marriage is intended to be solemnised in Fiji between a British subject resident in Fiji and a British subject resident in England, Scotland or Ireland; a certificate for marriage issued by a superintendent registrar in England, and a certificate for marriage issued by a registrar or a certificate of proclamation of banns in Scotland, and a certificate for marriage issued by the registrar in Ireland, shall be deemed to have the same force and effect as a certificate for marriage issued under the provisions of this Act.

(2) Where a marriage is intended to be solemnised or contracted in the United Kingdom between a British subject resident in Fiji and a British subject resident in England, Scotland or Ireland, a certificate for marriage may be issued in Fiji by the Registrar-General or by a district registrar in like manner as if the marriage was to be solemnised in Fiji under circumstances requiring the issue of such a certificate and as if both such British subjects were resident in Fiji, provided that in the case of the issue as aforesaid of a certificate for marriage by a district registrar, the same shall be duly authenticated under the hand and seal of the Registrar-General.

[s 18 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 19 Special licence

Notwithstanding the other provisions of this Act, where a marriage is intended to be solemnised in Fiji, the Registrar-General at any time, on application in the prescribed form by both parties to the intended marriage attested by the Registrar-General or a district registrar upon production of such evidence to prove that there is no lawful impediment to the intended marriage as the Registrar-General\* may require, and upon payment of the prescribed fee, may grant a special licence authorising the solemnisation of the said marriage between the said parties within such time, not exceeding 28 days, as shall be named therein, provided that the Registrar-General may, in such special circumstances as he or she thinks fit, grant a special licence on application by one of the parties to the intended marriage.

[s 19 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 19A Information to accompany certificate for marriage and licence

The Registrar-General must ensure that—

(a) when a certificate for marriage is issued under section 17; or

(b) when a special licence to marry is issued under section 19,

the certificate or licence is accompanied by 2 copies of the prescribed information for those intending to marry.

[s 19A insrt Decree 33 of 2009 Sch, effective 1 December 2009]

### s 20 By whom marriages to be solemnised

(1) No marriage shall be solemnised by a marriage officer to whom the provisions of sections 4 and 5 apply unless his or her name, designation and address shall have been registered and shall then continue registered in the office of the Registrar-General, provided that no marriage in fact shall be void by reason only of the same having been solemnised by a person not being a registered marriage officer if either of the parties to the marriage shall at the time believe in good faith that he or she was such a marriage officer.

(2) Where the parties to be married shall so elect, the marriage may be solemnised between the parties by the Registrar-General in any place within Fiji, or by a district registrar within the limits of the district assigned to him or her, in the prescribed form of words to be repeated and signed by the parties to such marriage respectively.

[s 20 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 21 Declaration of marriage

In every case of solemnisation of marriage under the provisions of this Act, the marriage officer shall require each of the parties in some part of the ceremony in his or her presence and in the presence of the officiating marriage officer and witnesses to say to the other “I call upon these persons here present to witness that I, A.B., do take you, C.D., to be my lawful wedded wife (or husband)”, or words as nearly corresponding thereto as circumstances will permit.

[s 21 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 21A Marriage officer to provide information

A marriage officer who is intending to solemnise a marriage shall before performing the ceremony referred to in section 22 —

(a) ask each person who is a party to the intended marriage whether they have personally received a copy of the prescribed information for those intending to marry; and

(b) if a party has not personally received that information the marriage officer shall provide the prescribed information to that person,

provided that no marriage shall be void by reason only of non-compliance with this section by the marriage officer.

[s 21A insrt Decree 33 of 2009 Sch, effective 1 December 2009]

### s 22 Power of marriage officers to ask for particulars

Any marriage officer may ask of any person married or about to be married the several particulars required under the provisions of this Act touching any such marriage.

[s 22 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 23 Witnesses to marriage and certificate

(1) Every marriage shall be solemnised in the presence of 2 or more witnesses who shall sign a certificate which shall also be signed by the marriage officer solemnising the marriage and by the parties thereto in the presence of such witnesses and shall be legibly written (or partly written and partly printed) in the prescribed form.

(2) The marriage officer solemnising the marriage shall deliver a copy of such certificate immediately after the marriage duly signed as aforesaid, to one of the parties to the marriage and shall within 7 days thereafter transmit the original certificate to the district registrar of the district in which the marriage was solemnised.

(3) Every marriage officer shall on the last day of March, June, September and December in each year or so soon thereafter as may be practicable, transmit to the Registrar-General in the prescribed form a return of all marriages solemnised by him or her during the preceding 3 months.

[s 23 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

## PART 5 MARRIAGE UNDER THE FOREIGN MARRIAGE ACT 1892 [ss 24– 26]

### s 24 Notice of marriage under Foreign Marriage Act 1892

Where a marriage is intended to be solemnised in any foreign country under the Foreign Marriage Act 1892 of the United Kingdom, a notice in the prescribed form of such marriage shall be given by one of the parties intending such marriage who has had his or her usual place of abode for a period of not less than one week (or for such other period as may be hereafter prescribed by the Minister for the purposes of the said Act) immediately preceding in some place within Fiji to the Registrar-General.

[s 24 am LN 112 of 1970 O 179, effective 8 October 1970; renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 25 Filing and posting notice

The Registrar-General shall file every such notice and shall enter a copy thereof in a book to be kept for that purpose and shall publish such notice by forthwith posting up the same in a conspicuous place in his or her office and shall keep the same so posted up during 14 consecutive days. Such book and the copy so posted up shall be open at all reasonable times without fee to the inspection of any person.

[s 25 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 26 Certificate of Registrar-General

The Registrar-General, if he or she is satisfied that the party giving such notice has actually been resident in Fiji for not less than one week (or such other period as the Minister may prescribe) immediately preceding the giving of such notice, and unless he or she is aware of any impediment or objection which should obstruct the solemnisation of the marriage, shall, on payment of the prescribed fee, give a certificate in the prescribed form that such notice has been so given and published as aforesaid.

[s 26 am LN 112 of 1970 O 179, effective 8 October 1970; renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

## PART 6 OFFENCES [ss 27– 33]

### s 27 Persons pretending to be ministers

If any person shall cause his or her name to be registered under the provisions of this Act as a minister of religion, he or she being at the time not such a minister and knowing himself or herself not to be such, he or she shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $400 or to imprisonment for any term not exceeding 2 years.

[s 27 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 28 Penalty on solemnising a marriage without production of certificate

Any person who solemnises any marriage between parties, unless by special licence as hereinbefore provided in this Act, without first having had produced to him or her a certificate or certificates for marriage and having satisfied himself or herself that such certificate is or such certificates are still in full force and effect shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200.

[s 28 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 29 Penalty for marrying a minor

Any person who—

(a) willfully and unlawfully marries a person under the age of 18 years; or

(b) induces or endeavours to induce any marriage officer or other person to solemnise marriage between parties when the person so acting knows that one of the parties to the marriage is a minor; or

(c) abets or assists the principal offender in any such act or endeavour as is described in paragraphs (a) and (b), knowing that a party to the marriage or intended marriage is minor,

shall be guilty of an offence and liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $500 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years.

[s 29 subst Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 30 Penalty for solemnising marriages without being registered

Every marriage officer who solemnises any marriage knowing that his or her name, designation or usual residence has not been registered under the provisions of this Act or is not then duly registered shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100 or to imprisonment for any term not exceeding 6 months.

[s 30 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 31 Penalty for failure to require declaration

Any marriage officer who solemnises any marriage without requiring the parties thereto to make the declaration of marriage referred to in section 23 shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100 and further to have his or her registration as a marriage officer cancelled.

[s 31 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 32 Penalty for omission to transmit certificate etc

Any marriage officer or other person who fails to comply with the provisions of this Act or any of them respecting the certificate of marriage or return of marriages required to be transmitted to the Registrar-General or the district registrar, as the case may be, shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100.

[s 32 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 33 Falsely pretending to be a marriage officer

Any person who falsely pretends to be a marriage officer and knowingly and wilfully acts in such capacity in relation to the solemnisation of any marriage shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to imprisonment for any term not exceeding 7 years.

[s 33 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

## PART 7 MISCELLANEOUS [ss 34– 38]

### s 34 Additional religious ceremony

(1) At any time after the solemnisation of a marriage by the Registrar-General or a district registrar, the parties to such marriage may, if they so desire, upon the production of the certificate of the Registrar-General or district registrar as to the marriage, have a further marriage service performed according to the form ordained or used by the religion or religious denomination to which either or each of such parties belong.

(2) Nothing in the reading or celebration of a marriage service under the provisions of subsection (1) shall supersede or invalidate any marriage previously solemnised nor shall such reading or celebration be entered as a marriage in the register of marriages.

[s 34 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 35 Divorce

Any divorce duly granted to any person by the High Court shall be forthwith notified by the Chief Registrar of the Court to the Registrar-General who shall duly record the fact of such divorce on the entry of marriage, if any.

[s 35 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 36 Validity of registered marriage

Every marriage duly solemnised under the provisions of this Act unless therein expressly declared to be void shall be deemed to be good and valid in law until the contrary be proved.

[s 36 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 11, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 37 Marriage in articulo mortis

(1) Any marriage officer may, without previous publication and without any other formality required by this Act solemnise a marriage between 2 persons one of whom is in articulo mortis and such marriage shall, subject to the following provisions, be as valid as if it had been celebrated by a marriage officer in conformity with the provisions of this Act.

(2) No marriage under the provisions of subsection (1) shall be celebrated unless both parties are able to signify their consent thereto and affix their signatures or marks to the certificate of marriage in the presence of 2 witnesses, both of whom shall mark or sign the certificate.

[subs (2) am Decree 26 of 2009 s 10, effective 24 July 2009]

(3) It shall be the duty of any marriage officer solemnising a marriage under the provisions of this section forthwith to forward the marriage certificate referred to in subsection (2) to the Registrar-General or a district registrar, as the case may be.

[s 37 renum Decree 26 of 2009 s 10, effective 24 July 2009]

### s 38 Regulations

The Registrar-General may, subject to the approval of the Minister, make regulations from time to time for carrying out the provisions of this Act, prescribing the forms to be used, the procedure to be adopted, the information to be provided and the fees to be charged in connection with the provisions of this Act.

[s 40 am LN 112 of 1970 O 179, effective 8 October 1970; Decree 33 of 2009 Sch, effective 1 December 2009]
