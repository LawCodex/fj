# ADVERTISEMENT REGULATION ACT 1931

AN ACT TO CONTROL THE EXHIBITION OF ADVERTISEMENTS

## s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Advertisement Regulation Act 1931.

## s 2 Regulations

(1) The Minister may make regulations for the control, prohibition and removal of advertisements of any kind whatsoever which are situated outside the boundaries of the cities of Suva and Lautoka and which are visible from any street or from the waters of Fiji, or from any other place to which the public have access, or from any land or building not belonging to or in the possession of the owner or occupier of the land or building on or in which the advertisements are exhibited.

[subs (1) am Ordinance 2 of 1945 s 92, effective 1 May 1945]

(2) The city councils of Suva and Lautoka may make regulations for the control, prohibition and removal of advertisements of any kind whatsoever which are situated within the boundaries of the cities of Suva and Lautoka respectively, and which are visible from any street or from the waters of Fiji, or from any other place to which the public have access, or from any land or building not belonging to or in the possession of the owner or occupier of the land or building on or in which the advertisements are exhibited.

[subs (2) am Ordinance 2 of 1945 s 92, effective 1 May 1945]

(3) Any person who fails to comply with any regulations made in pursuance of the provisions of this section shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $20 or to imprisonment for any period not exceeding 2 months or to both such fine and imprisonment.

[subs (3) am Ordinance 2 of 1945 s 92, effective 1 May 1945]

## s 3 Exhibiting liquor advertisements on commercial vehicles

(1) Notwithstanding anything contained in section 2, no owner of any goods or public service vehicle defined in the Land Transport Act 1998 shall exhibit or allow to be exhibited in or upon such vehicle any advertisement or poster of whatsoever kind which induces or is intended or likely to induce any person to purchase liquor as defined in any Act relating to the control of liquor for the time being in force.

[subs (1) am Ordinance 2 of 1945 s 92, effective 1 May 1945 ; Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 66, effective 13 January 1967; Act 31 of 2016 s 3, effective 1 December 2016]

(2) Any person who fails to comply with the provisions of subsection (1) shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $40 or in the event of non-payment to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months.

[subs (2) am Ordinance 2 of 1945 s 92, effective 1 May 1945 ; Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 66, effective 13 January 1967]
