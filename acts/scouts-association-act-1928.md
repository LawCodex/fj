AN ACT RELATING TO THE SCOUTS ASSOCIATION

## s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Scouts Association Act 1928.

[s 1 am Ordinance 15 of 1970 s 3, effective 31 March 1970]

## s 2 Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Association** means the Scout Association formerly known as the Boy Scouts Association incorporated under the Royal Charter granted on 4 January 1912; and

**scout** means a scout recognised as such under the constitution, by-laws or rules of the Association and includes also all officers of the Association.

[def am Ordinance 15 of 1970 s 4, effective 31 March 1970]

## s 3 Restriction on use of uniform etc of the Scouts Association

It shall not be lawful for any person not being under the by-laws or rules of the Association duly authorised and entitled so to do publicly to wear, carry or bear any uniform, badge, token or emblem which under the said by-laws or rules are specifically adopted for use under the authority of the Association in such style or manner as to convey an impression that such person is under the said by-laws or rules entitled so to wear, carry or bear such uniform, badge, token or emblem.

[s 3 am Ordinance 15 of 1970 s 5, effective 31 March 1970]

## s 4 No person falsely to claim connection with Scouts Association

It shall not be lawful for any person to form, organise or work in connection with, or be concerned in forming, organising or in work in connection with, any corps or body which, without due authority granted under the Royal Charter of the Association, claims or purports to be scouts or otherwise to be connected with the Association, or which holds itself out as or passes itself off as scouts or as otherwise connected with the Association.

[s 4 am Ordinance 15 of 1970 s 7, effective 31 March 1970]

## s 5 Penalties

Any person contravening any of the provisions of this Act shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $20 or to imprisonment for any period not exceeding one month or both.

[s 5 am Ordinance 2 of 1945 s 79, effective 1 May 1945 ; Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 33, effective 13 January 1967]
