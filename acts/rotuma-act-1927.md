## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [ss 1– 4]

### s 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Rotuma Act 1927.

### s 2 Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Chairperson** means the Chairperson of the Council;

[def insrt Ordinance 34 of 1970 s 2, effective 8 October 1970]

**Council** means the Council of Rotuma established by this Act;

[def insrt Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 3, effective 11 April 1958]

**District Chief** means a chief of a Rotuman district elected in accordance with Rotuman custom;

[def insrt Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 3, effective 11 April 1958 ; Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 31, effective 13 January 1967]

**District Officer** means the District Officer for Rotuma;

**District Officer's Court** means the District Officer's Court Rotuma;

**former Council** means the Council established by the Rotuma (Council) Regulations 1939;

[def insrt Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 3, effective 11 April 1958]

**former fund** means the fund created by the former Council out of moneys received by the former Council from a cess on copra;

**Fund** means the Rotuman Development Fund established under the provisions of this Act;

[def insrt Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 3, effective 11 April 1958]

**Rotuma** means the island of Rotuma and its dependencies, that is to say all islands, rocks, reefs and fisheries lying between the 12° and the 15° of south latitude and between the 175° and the 180° of east longitude from the meridian of Greenwich;

**Rotuman** means any person of Rotuman or part-Rotuman descent. If any dispute arises as to whether a person is or is not Rotuman the Council shall decide, but any person aggrieved by the Council's decision may appeal to the Minister whose decision shall be final; and

[def insrt Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 3, effective 11 April 1958 ; LN 112 of 1970 O 66, effective 8 October 1970]

**Rotuman community** means the indigenous inhabitants of Rotuma and also any iTaukei resident on Rotuma.

[def insrt Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 31, effective 13 January 1967; am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

### s 3 Application to Rotuma of Acts of Fiji

(1) Except in so far as Rotuma has been expressly excluded from the provisions thereof, all Acts are hereby declared to apply to Rotuma.

[subs (1) subst Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 31, effective 13 January 1967]

(2) In applying any provisions of any Act to Rotuma such Acts shall be construed as containing any variations in respect of Rotuma made necessary by this Act or any other Act specially applicable to Rotuma.

[subs (2) am Act 37 of 1966 s 31, effective 13 January 1967]

(3) All Acts when applied to Rotuma shall be construed to apply only so far as the circumstances of the island and its inhabitants permit and all Acts shall be construed in relation to Rotuma with such verbal alteration not affecting the substance as may be necessary to render the same applicable to the matter before the District Officer's Court, and the District Officer and any other officer having or exercising functions of a like kind or analogous to the functions of any officer referred to in any such Act shall be deemed to be within the enactments thereof relating to such last mentioned officer.

### s 4 District Officer

It shall be lawful for the Minister from time to time to appoint any fit and proper person to be District Officer for Rotuma.

[s 4 am Ordinance 2 of 1945 s 75, effective 1 May 1945 ; LN 112 of 1970 O 66, effective 8 October 1970]

## PART 2 DISTRICT OFFICER'S COURT [ss 5– 11]

### s 5 District Officer's Court

There shall be established for Rotuma a Court of Justice to be styled the District Officer’s Court which shall consist of and be holden by the District Officer.

### s 6 Jurisdiction of District Officer

The District Officer shall, ex officio, be a Magistrate within Rotuma and shall, save as hereinafter set forth, have the same jurisdiction in all civil and criminal suits and matters as a second class magistrate.

[s 6 subst Ordinance 2 of 1945 s 75, effective 1 May 1945]

### s 7 Seal of court

The District Officer's Court shall have a seal bearing an impression of the Coat of Arms with the inscription “District Officer's Court Rotuma” and all process issuing out of the said court shall be stamped or sealed therewith.

[s 7 am Act 31 of 2016 s 187, effective 1 December 2016]

### s 8 Officers of court

There shall be attached to and belong to the District Officer's Court such officers as to the Minister shall from time to time appear to be necessary for the administration of justice and the due execution of the powers and authorities with which the District Officer is hereby vested, and such officers shall have all the powers vested in a police officer by the laws of Fiji.

[s 8 am LN 112 of 1970 O 66, effective 8 October 1970]

### s 9 Accounts of fees etc to be kept

An account of all fees, forfeitures and money penalties levied or paid under the provisions of this Act shall be kept by the District Officer and the District Officer shall at such times as the Minister may direct forward a transcript of such account to the Auditor-General and shall pay into the treasury at such times and in such manner as the Minister may direct all moneys received by him or her on account of such fees, forfeitures and penalties.

[s 9 am LN 112 of 1970 O 66, effective 8 October 1970]

### s 10 Note of evidence to be taken in English

A note of all evidence given in the District Officer's Court shall be taken by the District Officer in English.

### s 11 Procedure in indictable cases

(1) In all cases where a charge or complaint is made before the District Officer that any person has committed or is suspected to have committed any indictable offence within Rotuma or that any person guilty or suspected to be guilty of having committed such offence outside Rotuma is in Rotuma, such charge or complaint shall be made in writing as nearly as possible in the form laid down in the Schedule to the Criminal Procedure Act (Forms) Rules 1945 and the District Officer shall thereafter cause the accused person to be summoned or arrested in the manner provided by the said Act.

[subs (1) am Ordinance 2 of 1945 s 75, effective 1 May 1945 ; Act 31 of 2016 s 187, effective 1 December 2016]

(2) Thereafter the District Officer shall, except where the charge is in respect of an offence punishable by death, proceed to try the charge and at the trial shall, in his or her own handwriting, take down the evidence given upon oath or affirmation of all witnesses, and the evidence of each witness shall be read over and, if necessary, translated to the witness in every case and thereafter signed by the witness and interpreter as being a full and correct statement of the evidence attested by the District Officer as having been taken before him or her on the date of the trial.

[subs (2) am Ordinance 19 of 1962 s 2, effective 19 July 1962]

(3) In all other respects the District Officer shall conduct the trial, and the procedure shall be as nearly as possible the same, as if the trial were for an offence being tried on information before a Judge sitting alone in the High Court until the conclusion of the evidence and of the addresses, if any, of the accused and of the prosecutor.

(4) Thereupon the District Officer shall adjourn the case and as soon as may be dispatch his or her notes of evidence and all documents and exhibits in the case which may reasonably be dispatched to the Chief Justice together with his or her recommendation and any comment upon the evidence he or she may wish to make, and during such adjournment, pending the decision of the case as hereinafter provided, may order that the accused be kept in confinement or admitted to bail and shall inform the Chief Justice accordingly.

(5) The Chief Justice shall then consider the case thus presented to him or her and if he or she considers it necessary may instruct the District Officer to take any further evidence and such evidence shall then be taken in the same manner as evidence in the case which was previously taken and transmitted to the Chief Justice.

(6) If no further evidence is required, or on receipt of the further evidence (if any), the Chief Justice, having considered the case, shall state in writing at the foot of the record of the proceedings the verdict, and, if the verdict is “guilty”, the sentence which to him or her shall seem just and is permitted by law and, shall return the record to the District Officer together with a warrant of commitment.

[subs (6) am Ordinance 19 of 1962 s 2, effective 19 July 1962]

(7) On receipt of the record completed as above the District Officer shall reassemble the court. The verdict and sentence recorded by the Chief Justice shall be read by the District Officer to the accused in open court and if necessary translated to the accused and the fact certified by the District Officer upon the record.

## PART 3 THE COUNCIL OF ROTUMA [ss 12– 20]

### s 12 Constitution of Council of Rotuma

(1) There is hereby established a Council called the Council of Rotuma which shall consist of—

(a) the chiefs of the 7 Rotuma districts;

(b) one representative from each of the Rotuma districts elected in accordance with the provisions of this Act;

(c) as advisory members without any voting powers, the District Officer, the most senior Medical Officer, and the most senior Agricultural Officer resident on Rotuma, provided however they shall cease to be such members upon a resolution duly passed by the Council in that behalf.

(2) The Chairperson of the Council shall be elected by secret ballot by those members of the Council who possess voting power.

(3) All matters decided by the Council other than the election of its Chairperson shall be decided by a majority of those present in open vote.

(4) The Chairperson shall at all times have, in addition to his or her deliberative vote, a casting vote in the case of the deliberative votes being cast equally.

(5) The Council may make rules not inconsistent with this Act governing its own procedure.

(6) The Chairperson and any 9 other members of the Council shall constitute a quorum.

[s 12 subst Ordinance 34 of 1970 s 3, effective 8 October 1970]

### s 13 Meetings of Council

(1) Meetings of the Council shall be held not less frequently than once in each quarter upon a date and at a place to be fixed by the Chairperson.

[subs (1) am Ordinance 34 of 1970 s 4, effective 8 October 1970]

(2) The Chairperson shall direct the order of proceedings at all meetings of the Council in such manner as he or she may deem best fitting for the due despatch of business.

[subs (2) am Ordinance 34 of 1970 s 4, effective 8 October 1970]

(3) Members of the public shall not be admitted to meetings of the Council except at the express invitation of the Council.

(4) The Chairperson may call a meeting at any time and shall call a meeting within 7 days of being requested to do so by at least 8 members of the Council.

[s 13 subst Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 4, effective 11 April 1958 ; am Ordinance 34 of 1970 s 4, effective 8 October 1970]

### s 14 Minutes to be kept

The minutes of every meeting of the Council shall be kept by the Chairperson who shall forward a copy of such minutes to the Minister.

[s 14 subst Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 4, effective 11 April 1958 ; am Ordinance 34 of 1970 s 5, effective 8 October 1970; LN 112 of 1970 O 66, effective 8 October 1970]

### s 15 Duties of Council

It shall be the duty of the Council—

(a) to consider all such questions relating to the good government and well-being of the Rotuman community in the island as may be directed by the Minister or may seem to them to require their attention;

(b) to administer the Rotuman Development Fund established by this Act, and to carry out all such functions as may be required by law or necessary or desirable in connection with such Fund.

[s 15 subst Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 4, effective 11 April 1958 ; am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 31, effective 13 January 1967; LN 112 of 1970 O 66, effective 8 October 1970]

### s 16 Regulations

(1) The Council may, subject to the approval of the Minister, make Rotuma Regulations to be obeyed by all members of the Rotuman community in Rotuma relating to the peace, order and good government of the Rotuman community and, without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, relating to—

(a) the keeping clean of Rotuma and the promotion of public health;

(b) the social and economic betterment of the Rotuman community;

(c) the performance of communal work by members of the Rotuman community and other communal activities of the Rotuman community;

(d) the control of livestock on Rotuma;

(e) the prevention or removal of public nuisances on Rotuma;

(f) the care of children and aged persons;

(g) the conservation of food supplies on Rotuma.

[subs (1) am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 31, effective 13 January 1967; LN 112 of 1970 O 66, effective 8 October 1970; Act 43 of 1998 s 2, effective 18 September 1998]

(2) Regulations made under subsection (1) may prescribe penalties for breach of the regulations not exceeding a fine of $2,000 and imprisonment for 12 months.

[subs (2) subst Act 43 of 1998 s 2, effective 18 September 1998]

[s 16 subst Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 4, effective 11 April 1958]

### s 17 Aiding or abetting an offence

Any person who aids or abets any member of the Rotuman community in committing a breach of any regulation made by the Councillor or who procures or attempts to procure or counsels or advises any act involving the breach or neglect of any regulation shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $40 or to imprisonment for any term not exceeding 4 months.

[s 17 subst Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 4, effective 11 April 1958 ; am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 31, effective 13 January 1967]

### s 18 Election of District Chiefs

(1) District Chiefs shall continue to be elected in accordance with Rotuman custom as heretofore.

(2) The Minister may in his or her discretion by notice in the Gazette remove from office any District Chief.

[subs (2) am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 31, effective 13 January 1967; LN 112 of 1970 O 66, effective 8 October 1970]

(3) Where the Minister has removed from office the chief of any Rotuman district, a new election in accordance with Rotuman custom of a chief for such district shall be held as soon as possible.

[subs (3) am Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 31, effective 13 January 1967; LN 112 of 1970 O 66, effective 8 October 1970]

(4) A person removed from office by the Minister shall not be eligible for re-election as District Chief without the consent of the Minister.

[s 18 subst Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 4, effective 11 April 1958 ; LN 112 of 1970 O 66, effective 8 October 1970]

### s 19 Election of representative

(1) A representative of the Council, in this section referred to as a representative, in respect of each of the 7 districts shall be elected by secret ballot.

(2) Any Rotuman of the age of 21 or over shall be entitled to vote in the election of the representative for the district in which such Rotuman habitually resides.

(3) A representative shall automatically retire after 3 years in office but may offer himself or herself for re-election.

(4) The elections of representatives shall take place in each district during October of every third year, provided that—

(a) if for any reason the, election of representatives cannot take place as stipulated; the Minister shall have the power to appoint to the Council one representative from each of the Rotuma districts to be members thereof and who shall be deemed together with the persons who shall be members of the, Council by virtue of section 12(1)(a) and (c), to be members of the Council and shall, subject to any general or specific directions issued by the Minister, have, perform and discharge all the rights, privileges, powers, duties and functions vested in or conferred or imposed on the members and the Council by this Act or any written law until the election of representatives takes place in the month of October next following;

(b) an appointment made in accordance with paragraph (a) shall be deemed terminated on the day the names of the newly elected representatives are declared;

(c) for the purpose of the next election of representatives after the election of representatives to be conducted in October 1993, the 3 year period between which the elections are to be held, shall begin to run from the date of the last election of representatives due to take place in October 1993.

[subs (4) am Ordinance 34 of 1970 s 6, effective 8 October 1970; Act 14 of 1993 s 2, effective 1 November 1992; LN 112 of 1970 O 66, effective 8 October 1970]

(5) Any person entitled to vote at an election of a representative of a district may, if nominated by 4 persons similarly qualified in respect of such district, be a candidate at an election of a representative for such district. Nomination as a candidate must be made not more than 14 days and not less than 4 days prior to the date fixed for the election.

(6) In the event of the death or resignation of a representative of a district during his or her term of office, a by-election shall be held. A representative elected at a by-election shall hold office for the period remaining of the term of office of the representative whom he or she replaces, provided that where a representative dies or resigns within 3 months of the date on which he or she was due to retire, the District Officer may, with the permission of the Minister, direct that no by-election shall be held in which case the representative shall be deemed to have died or resigned on the date on which he or she would normally have retired.

[subs (6) am LN 112 of 1970 O 66, effective 8 October 1970]

(7) Subject to the provisions of subsection (4), the District Officer shall fix the date when an election or by-election shall take place and, subject to the provisions of this Act, the procedure at any election or by-election shall be in accordance with his or her directions, which may include any direction necessary to give effect to the provisions of this section.

(8) For the purpose of avoiding doubt it is hereby declared that the provisions of subsections (1), (2), (3) and (5) shall also apply to by-elections.

[s 19 subst Ordinance 17 of 1968 s 2, effective 20 January 1969]

## PART 4 ROTUMAN DEVELOPMENT FUND [ss 21– 27]

### s 21 Establishment of Rotuman Development Fund

(1) There is hereby established a Fund known as the Rotuman Development Fund.

(2) The Fund shall consist of—

(a) all moneys, investments, securities or other property comprising the former Fund at the commencement of this Act;

(b) any cess paid to the Council in accordance with the provisions of this Act;

(c) any income accruing to the Fund from placing on deposit, investing or otherwise dealing with the moneys in the Fund in any manner authorised by this Act.

[s 21 insrt Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 4, effective 11 April 1958]

### s 22 Rights and obligations of former Council in former Fund to vest in the Council

All rights, liabilities and obligations in respect of the former Fund which immediately before the commencement of this Act were rights, liabilities and obligations of the former Council shall by virtue of this Act become rights, liabilities and obligations of the Council.

[s 22 insrt Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 4, effective 11 April 1958]

### s 23 Validation of former Fund

It is hereby declared that all moneys heretofore received by the former Council in respect of any cess levied on copra were properly and validly so received as if the said cess had been validly and lawfully imposed, and the former Council and every person from time to time being a member thereof are acquitted, discharged and indemnified against all and every person from all legal proceedings of any kind whatsoever in connection with the imposition or collection of such cess.

[s 23 insrt Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 4, effective 11 April 1958]

### s 24 Objects of Fund

The object of the Fund shall be the promotion of the development, welfare and advancement of Rotumans. Subject to the payment of expenses properly incurred by the Council in the management and control of the Fund or in connection with the imposition and recovery of any cess, the whole of the Fund shall be expended for purposes directed exclusively towards the aforesaid objects, and may be expended outside as well as inside Rotuma.

[s 24 subst Ordinance 22 of 1964 s 2, effective 5 March 1965]

### s 25 Administration of Fund

Subject to such other directions as may be made by the Minister, the Fund shall be managed and controlled by the Council and the following provisions shall have effect with respect to such management and control—

(a) estimates of the revenue and expenditure of the Fund for the following year shall be submitted annually before 1 October in the preceding year, to the Minister responsible for finance for approval, and no expenditure out of moneys in the Fund shall take place except in accordance with such approved estimates unless the special authority of such Minister shall first have been obtained;

(b) the Council shall cause to be kept proper books of account relating to its management and control of the Fund and shall cause details of all moneys received and expended including administration expenses to be entered correctly in such books which books shall be kept separately from any other books of account kept by the Council;

(c) any moneys in the Fund may be invested in such manner as the Council may, with the prior approval of the Minister responsible for finance, from time to time, either generally or in any particular case, determine;

(d) such part of the moneys of the Fund as is not for the time being invested in pursuance of the provisions of paragraph (c) shall be paid into a bank or banks to be selected by the Council and every cheque or order for payment on such bank or banks shall be signed by such person or persons as shall be prescribed by the Minister by rules made under the provisions of this Act, provided that the Clerk of the Council shall be entitled to retain in cash a sum not exceeding such sum as may from time to time be prescribed, for the purpose of making payments from the Fund;

(e) the Council shall not later than 30 June in each year submit to the Minister a report on the operations of the Fund during the year ending on 31 December immediately preceding.

[s 25 subst Ordinance 22 of 1964 s 3, effective 5 March 1965; am Ordinance 34 of 1970 s 8, effective 8 October 1970; LN 112 of 1970 O 66, effective 8 October 1970]

### s 26 Imposition of cess

(1) The Council shall have power to impose a cess to be paid by all Rotuman producers upon primary produce produced by them in Rotuma, provided that no primary produce which is the property of a religious missionary organisation shall be subject to the imposition of cess.

(2) The rate of such cess shall not exceed 10% of the purchase price payable to the producer for the sale of such produce. The Council shall have power to vary the rate of such cess from time to time, but any cess imposed under the provisions of this section shall remain in force until so varied.

(3) Any cess imposed under this section shall be paid to the Council or its agent by the producer of the produce to which it relates, without demand and within 14 days of the sale of such produce. If not so paid the cess shall be recoverable in any manner provided by law for the recovery of civil debts.

(4) For the purposes of this section primary produce means—

(a) copra;

(b) cocoa beans;

(c) such other agricultural or marine produce as may be specified by the Minister by notice in the Gazette.

[subs (4) am LN 112 of 1970 O 66, effective 8 October 1970]

[s 26 insrt Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 4, effective 11 April 1958]

### s 27 The Fund to be exempt from income tax

The former Fund and the Fund shall be exempt from income tax, and the former Fund shall be deemed always to have been so exempt.

[s 27 insrt Ordinance 4 of 1958 s 4, effective 11 April 1958]

## PART 5 ROTUMA AGRICULTURAL AND INDUSTRIAL LOAN FUND [s 28]

### s 28 Rotuma Agricultural and Industrial Loan Fund

(1) The Council may establish a fund which shall be called the Rotuma Agricultural and Industrial Loan Fund which shall be operated, controlled, invested and expended in accordance with the provisions of this Act.

(2) The total amount of moneys paid by the Council into the Rotuma Agricultural and Industrial Loan Fund from the Fund shall not exceed $20,000.

[subs (2) am Ordinance 34 of 1970 s 9, effective 8 October 1970]

(3) The Council may loan moneys from the Rotuma Agricultural and Industrial Loan Fund to any Rotuman for such agricultural and industrial purposes, and upon and subject to such conditions, as the Council, with the prior agreement of the Minister responsible for finance, may from time to time determine.

(4) The manner of applying for such loans and all questions of procedure shall be decided by the Council.

(5) All loans made by the Council from the Rotuma Agricultural and Industrial Loan Fund shall be recoverable at the suit of the Council.

[s 28 insrt Ordinance 22 of 1964 s 4, effective 5 March 1965]

## PART 6 GENERAL [ss 29, 30]

### s 29 Audit of accounts

The accounts of the Council including those of the Fund and of the Rotuma Agricultural and Industrial Loan Fund shall be audited from time to time as the Minister responsible for finance may direct by an auditor to be appointed or approved by the Minister responsible for finance and the audited accounts together with the auditor's report thereon shall be submitted to the Minister responsible for finance within 3 months of any such audit.

[s 29 insrt Ordinance 22 of 1964 s 4, effective 5 March 1965]

### s 30 Saving

The Rotuma regulations lawfully made under the provisions of this Act prior to the establishment of the Council shall continue to have effect but may be amended or revoked by regulations made under the provisions of this Act.

[s 30 subst Ordinance 37 of 1966 s 31, effective 13 January 1967]
