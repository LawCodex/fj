# CRIMINAL PROCEDURE (ALLOWANCES TO WITNESSES) RULES 2016

Criminal Procedure (Allowances to Witnesses) Rules 2016 (LN 86 of 2016) commenced on 11 November 2016, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Criminal Procedure (Amendment) Act 2021 (No 2 of 2021) | 15 February 2021 |

## r 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Rules may be cited as the Criminal Procedure (Allowances to Witnesses) Rules 2016.

[subr (1) am Act 2 of 2021 s 16 and Sch, 15 February 2021]

(2) These Rules come into force on 11 November 2016.

## r 2 Allowances

Witnesses attending trials or inquiries before a Magistrates Court or the High Court or on summons to any court exercising appellate jurisdiction may be granted such allowances as compensation for their attendance and such sums for expenses not exceeding the respective amounts specified in the second column of the Schedule to these Rules, provided that the court may—

(a) disallow any such allowance or expenses in respect of any person in any case, for good cause which shall include non-attendance or late attendance, which reason shall be noted on the record; and

(b) allow an increase in any such allowance not exceeding a total increase of $100 in respect of any one person for any one day where it is satisfied that the witness has suffered other expenses or additional pecuniary loss in excess of the allowance granted under the provisions of this rule.

[r 2 am Act 2 of 2021 s 16 and Sch, 15 February 2021]

## r 3 Revocation

The Criminal Procedure Decree (Allowances to Witnesses and Assessors) Rules 2010 is hereby revoked.
