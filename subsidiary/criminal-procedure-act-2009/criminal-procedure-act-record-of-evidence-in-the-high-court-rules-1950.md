# CRIMINAL PROCEDURE ACT (RECORD OF EVIDENCE IN THE HIGH COURT) RULES 1950

Criminal Procedure Act (Record of Evidence in the High Court) Rules 1950 (LN 17 of 1950) commenced on 17 February 1950, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## r 1 Short title

These Rules may be cited as the Criminal Procedure Act (Record of Evidence in the High Court) Rules 1950.

[r 1 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 54, 1 December 2016]

## r 2 Judge’s notes when shorthand note taken

In cases where shorthand notes of the proceedings, including the evidence, are taken under section 283 of the Criminal Procedure Act 2009, the Judge shall take such notes of the evidence of the witnesses as he or she considers desirable.

[r 2 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 54, 1 December 2016]

## r 3 Where shorthand note not taken

In cases where shorthand notes of the proceedings are not taken, the Judge shall take down in writing in the language of the High Court the substance of the evidence of each witness, or so much thereof as he or she deems material, in narrative form, provided that if he or she so wishes, the Judge may take down the whole or any part of the evidence verbatim.
