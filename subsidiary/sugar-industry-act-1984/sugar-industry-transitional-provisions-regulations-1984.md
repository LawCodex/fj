## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Sugar Industry (Transitional Provisions) Regulations 1984.

## reg 2 Cane growers deemed to be registered

Where, pursuant to section 73(1) of the Act, a person is entitled to be registered as a grower in respect of a farm he or she shall, until such time as he or she is so registered, be deemed to be registered as a grower in respect of that farm.
