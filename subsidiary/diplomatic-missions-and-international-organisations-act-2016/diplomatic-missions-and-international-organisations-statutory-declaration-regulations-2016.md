## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Diplomatic Missions and International Organisations (Statutory Declaration) Regulations 2016.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the date the Act comes into force.

## reg 2 Interpretation

(1) For the purposes of these Regulations “Act” means the Diplomatic Missions and International Organisations Act 2016.

(2) In these Regulations, words and phrases have the same meaning as under the Act, unless the context otherwise requires.

## reg 3 Statutory declaration

For the purposes of making an application under section 5 of the Act, a statutory declaration must be––

(a) made in the form prescribed in the Schedule to these Regulations;

(b) made before a person listed under section 3 of the Statutory Declaration Act 1970; and

(c) accompanied by a copy of the passport of each individual listed in the statutory declaration intending to enter, reside or work in Fiji.
