## r 1 Short title

These Trust Rules shall be cited as the Fiji Pine Trust Rules 2008, and shall come into force upon publication in Gazette.

## r 2 Interpretation

In these Trust Rules unless the context otherwise requires

**beneficiary** means the landowners and extension forest owners and the Government as owner of State land;

**Board of Trustees** means the Board of Trustees constituted under rule 5;

**Company** means Fiji Pine Limited or its successors or assigns;

**directors** means directors of the company appointed by the Board of Trustees herein;

**extension forest** means privately owned pine or other forest plantation, which qualify for participation in the Trust as determined by the Trust from time to time;

**extension forest owners** means owners or associations of owners of extension forest and whose names appear on the Register;

**extension unitholders** means the extension forest owners holding extension units;

**extension units** mean units held by the extension forest owners in accordance with the Register herein;

**forest** means pine or other planted species of forest managed by the company or by a forest-based trust or forest-based company;

**forest-based company** means a company owned by a forest-based trust;

**forest-based trust** means a trust owned by the landowners in any forest area or by the extension forest owners;

**Government** means the Government of the Republic of Fiji;

**Government units** means units held by the Government in accordance with the Register herein;

**landowners** means owners and associations of owners of iTaukei or freehold and leased in any point in time to the company or any forest-based trust for the growing of pine or other species of tree thereon and whose names appear in the Register;

[def am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, effective 1 March 2011]

**landowner unitholders** means the landowners holding landowner units;

**landowners units** mean units held by the landowners not including the Government in accordance with the Register herein;

**Minister** means the Minister responsible for forestry;

[def am LN 99 of 2016 reg 78, effective 1 December 2016]

**Register** means the register of beneficiaries which is kept and maintained by the Trust as provided in these Trust Rules;

**special resolution** means a resolution which is required to be and has been passed by a majority of not less than 75% of the beneficiaries;

**station** means such pine forest or other forest areas which are leased to and recognised by the company and the Trust from time to time and which include their administrative centres;

**Trust** means the Fiji Pine Trust;

**Trust Property** means all the property and assets, whether real or personal, including shares of whatever class in any company held by or under the control of the Trust on behalf and for the benefit of the beneficiaries;

**trustees** means trustees who are elected, appointed or substituted by the beneficiaries in the manner provided in these Trust Rules;

**Unit** means an entitlement under the Trust; and

**vesting day** means the day as determined by the beneficiaries pursuant to rule 35 herein.

## r 3 Non-application of the Trustee Act 1966

The provisions of Trustee Act 1966, shall not apply to these Trust Rules.

## r 4 Legal status and objects of the Trust

The purpose and objects of the Trust shall, in addition to any other contained in other provisions of these Trust Rules, be as follows—

(a) the Trust shall be a body corporate with perpetual succession under a common seal and may in such name sue and be sued, borrow money, and enter into contracts and may acquire, purchase, take, hold, and enjoy real and personal property of whatsoever kind and description and may convey, assign, surrender and yield up, charge, mortgage, transfer or otherwise dispose of or deal with or in real or personal property vested in the Trust on such terms and conditions as the trustees shall think fit;

(b) to provide the opportunity and basis on which to assist the beneficiaries in promoting, coordinating and furthering their interest in accordance with the ultimate objectives stipulated in the Fiji Pine Act 1990 relating to the facilitation of the process of acquisition of pine forests and lands and ancillary industries connected thereto by proprietary units and landowners;

(c) to provide assistance to the beneficiaries in the proper attainment of their development aspirations through advisory, financial, managerial, marketing, training and any other form of assistance connected with the establishment of additional pine forests and the planting, growing, maintenance, logging, transportation and end use production and ancillary industries associated with pine or other forests and their respective forest products;

(d) to hold in trust for the beneficiaries all the property and assets, whether real or personal property of whatsoever kind, including funds and shares vested in the Trust, with power to borrow money and enter into contracts with individual beneficiaries and third parties in respect thereto, and with power to convey, assign, surrender, yield up, charge, mortgage, transfer or otherwise dispose of or deal with such assets and property on such terms and conditions as the trustees shall think fit;

(e) to hold all benefits and income of the Trust intended for distribution among the beneficiaries and to distribute such benefits and income in accordance with these Trust Rules.

## r 5 Board of Trustees

(1) There shall be for the purpose of the Trust a Board of Trustees, which shall consist of—

(a) one trustee to be elected by the beneficiary in each station as follows—

(i) one trustee representing Nabou Forest;

(ii) one trustee representing Nadi Forest;

(iii) one trustee representing Lololo Forest;

(iv) one trustee representing Ra Forest;

(v) one trustee representing Bua Forest;

(vi) one trustee representing Macuata Forest;

(b) one trustee to represent the extension forest owners to be elected by the beneficiary from extension forests in the Central Division;

(c) one trustee to represent the extension forest owners to be elected by the beneficiary from extension forests in the Eastern Division. The Western and Northern Division shall be represented by their respective station representatives as in (a) above.

(2)

(a) Unless earlier removed or replaced in accordance with these Trust Rules, every trustee shall hold office for 3 years and shall be eligible for reappointment, provided that no trustee shall serve more than 2 consecutive terms, but a trustee shall be eligible for reappointment after 3 years from the end of his or her last term.

(b) The trustees shall be entitled to allowances and disbursements incurred by them in connection with the performance of their official duties as such trustees at rates approved by the Board of Trustees.

(c) A person serving as a trustee of this Trust shall not at the same time serve as a trustee of a forest-based trust or as a director of Fiji Pine Limited or a forest-based company.

(3) A person shall be qualified for election or appointment as a trustee if—

(a) in respect of trustee elected under paragraph (1)(a), he or she is a landowner within the station from which he or she is elected;

(b) in respect of trustees appointed under paragraph (1)(b) herein, he or she is an extension forest owner in the division from which he or she is elected;

(c) he or she is a natural person;

(d) he or she is 21 years of age or over;

(e) he or she is not an undischarged bankrupt;

(f) except for the trustees appointed under paragraph (1)(c) herein, he or she is not a member of the senior management staff of the company, of this Trust, or of a forest-based company or trust;

(g) he or she is of sound mind;

(h) he or she has not been convicted of a criminal offence involving dishonesty committed within the last 7 years from the date he or she seeks election as such trustee, or has completed serving his or her last term of imprisonment for such offence 7 or more years from the date of his or her seeking election as such trustee;

(i) he or she has not been previously removed as a trustee or director in accordance with these Trust Rules;

(j) he or she is not a serving trustee in the forest-based trust from the forest area in which he or she seeks election as such trustee; and

(k) he or she is not a serving director in the company or the forest-based company in the forest area in which he or she seeks election as such trustee.

(4) The term of office of a trustee elected or appointed under these Trust Rules shall terminate on the occurrence of one or more of the following events, that is to say that the trustee—

(a) is dead; or

(b) is bankrupt by Order of a court; or

(c) has resigned; or

(d) fails to attend 3 successive meetings of the Board of Trustees without any reason acceptable to the Board of Trustees; or

(e) is insane; or

(f) refuses or is medically unfit to perform the duties of a trustee or is incapable for any reason acceptable to the Board of Trustees of performing such duties;

(g) was convicted within 7 years from the date his or her seeking election as a trustee of a criminal offence which involves dishonesty, or that he or she completed serving a sentence for such offence within 7 years from the date he or she seeks election as such trustee; and

(h) accepts election or appointment as a trustee in a forest-based trust, or as a director in the company or in a forest-based company.

(5) Any trustee whose term has terminated prematurely on any of the grounds specified in paragraph (4) herein may be replaced soon thereafter by the beneficiaries who had elected or appointed him or her, and such replacement trustee shall unless re-appointed, hold office only for the balance of the term of the outgoing trustee.

## r 6 Powers of the Board of Trustees

The Board of Trustees, in addition to other powers conferred upon them under these Trust Rules are empowered to do all or any of the following—

(a) Power to pay costs

To pay out of the Trust or the income thereof all costs, charges and expenses incidental to the management of the Trust or to the exercise of any power, authority or discretion herein contained or in carrying out or performing the Trust hereof which the Board of Trustees may at any time incur including all income tax or other taxes payable in respect of the Trust and all moneys which the Board of Trustees may be required to pay as settlement, probate, estate gift stamp or revenue duties, including stamp, gift or settlement duties payable in respect of the Trust or in these presents.

(b) Power to open and operate bank accounts

To open any account or accounts with any bank or banks as the Board of Trustees shall approve and to operate by and in all usual ways any account or accounts.

(c) Power to issue receipts

To give effectual receipts and discharges for any moneys received by or on behalf of the Trust or otherwise relating to any of acts, matters and things provided for in these presents and to receive either from any person or persons as additions to the Trust and to hold the same upon the Trust herein set forth and to administer such additions under the provision hereof.

(d) Power to appoint managers

To appoint and to determine conditions of a secretary and other staff for the proper management of the Trust and to delegate to them duties without extinguishing the trustees’ own responsibilities.

(e) Power to act to protect the Trust

To take such actions as the Board of Trustees shall think fit for the proper and adequate protection of the Trust, its property or any part thereof.

(f) Power to take an opinion

When necessary, to take and act upon a written opinion from a consultant, including legal and financial advisors, in relation to the interpretation or effect of these presents or any matter arising therefrom and in relation to other documents or statutes relating to the administration of the Trust.

(g) Power to co-opt trustees

To co-opt trustees to attend meetings of the Board of Trustees, but such co-opted trustees shall not have the right to vote at such meetings.

## r 7 Duties of the Board of Trustees

(a) The Board of Trustees in addition to any other duties conferred upon them shall in the manner hereinafter provided—

(i) manage the Trust;

(ii) appoint 3 directors who shall not themselves be trustees under this Trust to the board of directors of the company in accordance with rule 32 of these Trust Rules, or to appoint directors that the Trust may be entitled to appoint in any other company;

(iii) distribute to the beneficiaries such income of the Trust in the manner hereinafter provided; and

(iv) hold meetings of the Board of Trustees for the proper and timely dispatch of any business of the Trust, including any questions or issues the trustees may think fit, and to adjourn and otherwise conduct their meetings and meetings of the beneficiaries in the manner hereinafter provided;

(b) The Board of Trustees shall cause minutes of all their meetings to be kept and maintained and shall make minutes available to any trustee who may request an inspection of the same.

(c) Each trustee shall, except for ill health or bereavement or due to such circumstances beyond his or her control, personally attend every meeting convened by the Board of Trustees in accordance with these Trust Rules and no trustee may attend or vote at these meetings by proxy, agent or attorney.

## r 8 Limitation of liability of trustees

(a) No trustee shall be liable for any loss or damage occasioned by the exercise of any discretion or power hereby or by law conferred on the trustee or by failure to exercise any such discretion or power.

(b) No trustee shall be liable for any breach of duty of trust whatsoever unless it is proved to have been committed, made or omitted by the trustee either fraudulently or in bad faith.

(c) All persons claiming any beneficial interest in over or upon any property subject to this Trust shall be deemed to take the same with notice of and subject to the protection hereby conferred on the trustees hereof.

## r 9 Accounts and audit

(a) The Board of Trustees shall cause proper books of accounts to be kept with respect to—

(i) all sums of moneys received and expended by the Trust and the matters in respect of which the receipts and expenditures relate; and

(ii) the assets and liabilities of the Trust;

(b) The books of accounts shall be kept at such place as the Board of Trustees think fit and shall always be open to the inspection of the trustees;

(c) The Board of Trustees shall for each financial year cause to be prepared income and expenditure accounts and/or profit and loss accounts (hereinafter referred to as the “Income Statements”) and balance sheets and report by the Board of Trustees with respect to the Trust’s affairs and the amounts which they recommend to be distributed;

(d) The accounts which are to be laid aforesaid in the Board of Trustees meeting shall contain all relevant particulars and such other particulars as may be required by the definition of financial statements under the Companies Act 2015 and shall be prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles;

(e) Every balance sheet and report referred to above shall be signed by at least 2 trustees and the auditor’s report shall be attached to the balance sheet and income statements and the report shall be read at the Board of Trustees meeting and shall be open to inspection by any trustee.

[r 9 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 78, effective 1 December 2016]

## r 10 The Trust Property

(a) The Trust shall henceforth stand possessed of all of the property of the Trust and shall receive all dividends, interest and other income accrued or to be accrued or to accrue therefrom and to deal with the same in such manner as provided herein, provided however that the property of the Trust shall not be capable of being distributed, paid or transferred either in whole or in part thereof to or for the beneficiary until the vesting day hereinafter provided.

(b) The income of the Trust shall include all dividends, interest and other income derived from the property of the Trust after deducting costs and other expenditure incurred by the Board of Trustees.

## r 11 Income distribution

(a) The Board of Trustees in each accounting period shall, unit the vesting day, pay the whole of the net income of the Trust for such accounting period to the beneficiaries in proportion to the number of units registered in their respective names for the benefit of the landowners they each represent as at the end of such accounting period, provided however that if the income shall consist of distribution in kind such as shares or other property (personal or real) the Board of Trustees shall hold and deal with the same in the like manner as if it is the property of the Trust. The income therefrom shall only be capable of distribution in the like manner.

(b) The Board of Trustees shall be entitled to make an interim distribution of income at such time or times as the Board of Trustees shall determine during each accounting period.

(c) The distribution of income hereunder to any category of beneficiaries shall be limited from time to time as not to exceed a certain percentage of income available to be distributed to all beneficiaries as shall be decided by the Board of Trustees.

## r 12 Limitation of liability of unitholders

(a) Nothing in these Trust Rules shall constitute or shall be deemed to constitute the relationship of principal and agent between the trustees and the unitholders nor the relationship of partners as between the trustees and the unitholders or as among the unitholders inter se. All income payable to the unitholders in accordance with the provisions of these Trust Rules shall be payable to them separately and income received by the Board of Trustees shall not be received or be construed as received by or on behalf of the unitholders jointly or severally.

(b) Notwithstanding any other provision of these Trust Rules or any provision deemed to be included herein, no unitholder shall, but reason thereof, or by reason of the relationship thereby created with the Board of Trustees, be under any obligation personally to indemnify the Board of Trustees or any creditor of the Board of Trustees in the event of there being any deficiency of the assets and/or property of the Trust as compared with the liabilities of the Board of Trustees in relation thereto, and only valid rights, if any, of indemnity of the Board of Trustees or of such creditor shall be allowed recourse to the assets of the property of the Trust.

## r 13 Beneficial interest of unitholders

(a) The beneficial interest in the Trust as originally constitute and as existing from time to time shall be held by the beneficiaries for the time being in proportion to the units registered in their respective names.

(b) The units hereunder shall be issued in the following manner—

(i) to the landowners (which for this purpose shall not include the Government) on the basis of one unit for each hectare of land leased by them to the Company or their forest-based trust. These shall be referred to as the “landowner units”;

(ii) to the extension forest owners on the basis of one unit for each hectare of land under the extension forests which shall be referred to as “extension units”;

(iii) to the Government on the basis of one unit for each hectare of State land leased to the Company or to the forest-based trust for the forest area within which such State land is situated and which shall be referred to as “Government units”.

(c) Unless otherwise specifically provided herein each unit shall have equal rights.

(d) Each unit shall entitle the registered holder hereof equally with the registered holders of all other units to the beneficial interest in the Trust as an entity but subject thereto shall not entitle the unitholder to any particular security or investment comprised in the property of the Trust or any part thereof and (save as hereinafter expressly provided) no unitholder shall be entitled to transfer to him or her or any one else any property comprised in the unit or other property of the Trust.

(e) In his or her dealing with the Trust or the Trust Property or any part thereof a beneficiary shall not (except as expressly herein provided) be entitled to interfere with the rights or powers of the Board of Trustees nor can the beneficiary exercise any rights, powers or privileges in aspect of the Trust, the Trust Property or any part thereof.

(f) The Trust shall, when necessary under paragraph (b), issue additional units to a beneficiary.

## r 14 Register of Beneficiaries

(a) The Board of Trustees shall cause to be kept and maintained an up-to-date Register of Beneficiaries which shall be subdivided in such manner as to identify properly the categories of beneficiaries set out in rule 4, and the Register shall contain the following particulars—

(i) the names, addresses and descriptions of every beneficiary;

(ii) the number of units registered under each beneficiary, and the distinctive number or letters of the certificates held by them respectively;

(iii) the date on which the name of every beneficiary was entered in the Register in respect of units standing in his or her name;

(iv) any other details considered necessary by the Board of Trustees.

(b) Any change of name or address of any beneficiary shall be notified to the secretary who shall enter the Register accordingly.

(c)

(i) Except as hereinafter provided in this subparagraph no notice of any trust express, implied or constructive shall be entered in the Register and the person from time to time entered in the Register as the beneficiary shall be the only person recognised by the Board of Trustees as entitled to the units registered in his or her name or to exercise the rights and privileges of the registered holder thereof pursuant to these Trust Rules.

(ii) No person shall be recognised by the Board of Trustees as holding any unit upon any trust and the Board of Trustees shall not be bound by or be compelled in any way to recognise (even when having notice thereof) any equitable contingent future or partial interest in any unit or any interest therein or (except only as by the provisions of these Trust Rules as otherwise provided) any other rights in respect of any unit except an absolute right in the unitholder thereof.

(iii) Units held by a trustee in respect of a particular trust may be marked in the Register in such a way as to identify them as being held in respect of that trust but no liability shall be created by any such marking and the Board of Trustees shall not be affected with notice of a trust recorded as aforesaid.

(iv) The Board of Trustees shall not recognise or be bound by any assignments, lien or charges given by any unitholder in respect of the income or any part thereof from the units they hold.

## r 15 Certificates

(a) Every beneficiary whose name is entered in the Register shall be entitled to be issued with a certificate in such form as the Board of Trustees may from time to time prescribe but in respect of units held jointly, the Board of Trustees shall not be bound to issue more than one certificate and delivery of a certificate to one of several joint beneficiaries shall be sufficient delivery to all such beneficiaries.

(b) Each such certificate shall be signed for or on behalf of the Board of Trustees and shall specify the name of the beneficiary and the number of units to which it relates and shall bear distinctive number or letter.

(c) The appearance of the name of a beneficiary on the Register shall be evidence that he or she is a correct beneficiary and shall be final evidence in case of dispute.

(d) Whenever units are cancelled under the provisions hereof, the certificate with respect to such units (or such evidence as to its loss or destruction as the Board of Trustees may require) shall be delivered up to Board of Trustees.

## r 16 Cancellation of units

Units issued shall be cancelled—

(a) in the case of landowner units and Government units when the leases of land in relation to which the said units were issued have been cancelled, or terminated for whatever reason or lapsed or expired and not renewed or disposed of in any way whatsoever by the Company, other than by way of a transfer of the lease to a forest-based trust; and

(b) in relation to the extension units, when extension forests in relation to which such units were issued are no longer used for the plantation of pine or other approved exotic species.

## r 17 Restriction on alienation of units

(a) No units shall be alienated or be capable of alienation whether by way of sale, transfer or assignment or as a result of lunacy, liquidation or bankruptcy of any beneficiary unless, in the case of landowner unitholders, such alienation will vest the unit in accordance with an entity recognised by customary rules recognised by the iTaukei Lands Commission.

(b) No units or any part thereof shall be capable of being charged or mortgaged to any person except with the written approval of the Board of Trustees.

[r 17 am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, effective 1 March 2011]

## r 18 Meetings of the Board of Trustees

(a)

(i) The Meetings of the Board of Trustees shall be held whenever convened by the chairperson on his or her own initiative or upon his or her approval through a written request to the secretary by not less than 3 trustees.

(ii) The Board of Trustees may determine and regulate its own procedures at its meetings.

(iv) The quorum at any meeting of the Board of Trustees shall consist of not less than 50% of all the members of the Board of Trustees.

(v) The members of the Board of Trustees shall appoint from amongst themselves a chairperson who unless earlier removed by the Board of Trustees by a resolution by secret ballot of not less than 50% of all trustees, shall hold office for the remaining term of his or her appointment as a trustee.

(vi) The Board of Trustees may summon to any of its meetings all or any of the directors appointed by the Board of Trustees in the company or any other company in which the Trust has equity for the purpose of obtaining clarification or any other explanations on any matter connected with the interests of the Trust in those companies.

(b) Save as hereinafter specifically provided, a decision by the majority of all members of the Board of Trustees shall constitute a valid and legally effective decision at any meeting of the Board of Trustees; and in the case of an equality of votes the chairperson shall be entitled to a deliberate as well as a casting vote.

(c) A resolution in writing signed by all or a majority of the trustees for the time being shall be valid and legally effective as though it were a resolution made at a duly convened meeting of the trustees.

## r 19 Meetings of landowners/extension unitholders

(a) The trustee or trustees of each station or division shall once every calendar year convene at their respective stations and divisions at a time and place to be determined by the said trustee or trustees and the business at each such annual general meeting shall include the following—

(i) To receive from the trustees of landowners/extension forest owners information concerning the administration and accounts of the Trust.

(ii) To receive from the director representing their interest in the company information concerning the company and to advise the unitholders about the same.

(iii) Subject to rule 5(5), to receive nominations for and to elect trustees to fill vacancies in accordance with rule 5(1).

(b) In addition to the annual general meeting the trustee or trustees of each station or division may in every calendar year convene one or more extraordinary general meetings for landowners/extension forest owners at their respective stations and divisions at such times as shall be determined by the said trustee or trustees, or as required by the Trust upon the written request of the majority of the landowner unitholders/extension unitholders of each such station or division.

(c) The trustee or trustees of each station or division may summon to the annual general meeting or to any such extraordinary general meeting the director appointed in respect of that station or division.

## r 20 Notice of meetings of landowner and extension unitholders

(a) Unless otherwise provided for in this rule, a notice for any meeting of the landowner unitholders or extension unitholders shall be given not less than 14 days from the date of the meeting.

(b) The 14 days specified in paragraph (a) shall exclude the day on which the notice is issued or deemed to be issued, but shall include the day of the meeting for which the notice is given.

(c) A notice given under this rule shall specify the place, the date and the time of the meeting.

(d) Where a meeting is to consider a special business, the general nature of that business shall be included in the notice which shall be given to such persons as are entitled to receive the notice.

(e) Provided that, subject to the requirement for a quorum being satisfied as provided under rule 22 herein, a shorter period of notice than is required under this rule may be approved by the majority of the landowner unitholders or extension unitholders present at and are entitled to receive notice for a particular meeting.

(f) A notice is deemed to be properly given under this rule if the Trust causes an announcement to be broadcast in the iTaukei language over the National Radio Station on not less than 3 separate occasions and not less than 14 days from the date of the meeting informing the landowner unitholders and the extension unitholders of the place, the date and the time of the meeting.

## r 21 Effect of non-receipt of notice of meeting

The non-receipt of notice of a meeting by any person entitled to such notice shall not invalidate the proceedings of that meeting.

## r 22 Quorum

The quorum at any meeting of the landowner unitholders or the extension unitholders shall comprise persons present who hold not less than 50% of the total units held by unitholders at that forest station or division and no business shall be transacted at any meeting without such quorum.

## r 23 Effect of absence of quorum

If within one hour from the time appointed for the meeting a quorum is not present, the meeting, if convened upon the requisition of landowner unitholders or extension unitholders, shall be cancelled; in any other case it shall stand adjourned to the same day in the next week at the same time and place, or to such other day at such other time and place as the secretary of the Trust may determine, and if at the adjourned meeting a quorum is not present within half an hour from the time appointed for the meeting, the meeting shall proceed as if there was a quorum present.

## r 24 Determination of qualification to attend landowner or extension unitholder meeting

Every landowner unitholder and extension unitholder shall be entitled to attend any meeting if prior to the scheduled time of the meeting its duly elected representative is able to satisfy the secretary or Trust that he or she is the elected representative of the landowner unitholder or the extension unitholder duly registered in the Register in accordance with these Trust Rules.

## r 25 Chairperson

The chairperson at a meeting shall be appointed at the meeting by the representatives of the landowner unitholders or extension unitholders.

## r 26 Adjournment

The chairperson may, with the consent of the landowner unitholders or extension unitholders, in any meeting at which a quorum is present (and shall if so directed by the meeting), adjourn the meeting from time to time and from place to place, but no business shall be transacted at any adjourned meeting other than the business left unfinished at the meeting from which the adjournment took place. When a meeting is adjourned for 30 days or more, notice of the adjourned meeting shall be given as in case of an original meeting. Save as aforesaid it shall not be necessary to give notice of an adjournment or of the business to be transacted at an adjourned meeting.

## r 27 Voting

At every meeting of the landowner unitholders or the extension unitholders—

(a) unless a poll is demanded, voting shall be by show of hands and the decision of the meeting shall be declared by the chairperson when carried unanimously or by a majority;

(b) every duly registered landowner unitholder or extension unitholder present in person shall be entitled to one vote for every unit held;

(c) where no poll is demanded, a declaration by the chairperson that a resolution has on a show of hands been carried unanimously or by a particular majority, or lost, an entry to that effect in the book containing the minutes of the proceedings of the meeting shall be conclusive evidence of the fact without proof of the number or proportion of the votes recorded in favor of or against the resolution;

(d) a poll may be demanded either before or on the declaration of the result of the show of hands by the chairperson or by any duly registered landowner unitholder or extension unitholder present at the meeting and holding not less than one-quarter of the total units for that station or division;

(e) where a poll is demanded, it shall be taken in such manner and either at once or after an interval or adjournment or otherwise as the chairperson of the meeting may direct and the result of the poll shall be the resolution of the meeting at which the poll was demanded; but a poll demanded on the election of a chairperson or on a question of adjournment shall be taken forthwith; and

(f) the chairperson shall, in case of equality, have a second or casting vote.

## r 28 Voting rights

Each unit entitles the beneficiary to one vote for each unit held by him or her provided however that in relation to matters and issues which affect only the minority unitholders, the voting entitlement of any of the beneficiaries who is otherwise entitled to more than 50% of the total aggregate voting entitlements for that station or division shall be restricted so as not to exceed 33% of the total aggregate votes of all beneficiaries.

## r 29 Objections

No objection shall be raised to the qualification of any voter except at the meeting or adjourned meeting at which the vote objected to is given or tendered, and every vote not disallowed at such meeting shall be valid for all purposes. Any such objections made in due time shall be referred to the chairperson of the meeting, whose decision shall be final and conclusive.

## r 30 Manager

(a) The Board of Trustees shall appoint on terms and conditions, including remuneration, as the Board of Trustees shall think fit a secretary of the Trust whose duties shall include—

(i) to keep and maintain the Register of unitholders;

(ii) to prepare and maintain books of accounts as required under rule 9 and to keep the minutes of all meetings held by the Board of Trustees, the landowner unitholder and the extension unitholders; and

(iii) to prepare and issue the notice of each meeting of the Board of Trustees, the landowner unitholders and the extension unitholders.

(b) The Board of Trustees may from time to time delegate to the secretary other duties as the Board of Trustees may think fit.

(c) The secretary may employ agents or representatives to carry out any of its duties delegated to it by the Board of Trustees.

(d) The secretary, its agent or representative shall be entitled to attend any meeting held pursuant to these Trust Rules.

## r 31 Appointment of auditors

The Board of Trustees shall at its general meeting convened for the purpose appoint from among 3 nominees submitted to it for the purpose a person who is duly qualified and registered as a chartered accountant in Fiji as auditor of the Trust. The Board of Trustees shall determine the terms and conditions for such appointment.

## r 32 Appointment of directors

(1) The Board of Trustees shall appoint 3 directors to the Board of Trustees of the Company as follows—

(a) Three directors to represent the landowners unitholders appointed in the manner prescribed hereunder—

(i) one director nominated by the landowner unitholders at Nabou to represent Nabou and Nadi Stations and subsequently nominated by the landowner unitholder at Nadi to represent Nabou and Nadi Stations. The term of the trustee shall be alternated between Nabou and Nadi Forest;

(ii) one director nominated by the landowner unitholders at Lololo to represent Lololo and Ra Station; and

(iii) one director nominated by the landowner unitholders at Bua to represent Bua and Macuata Stations.

(2) The qualification of a director shall be the same as that of a trustee, provided however that a serving trustee shall not be entitled to be appointed as a director.

(3) In the event that the directors representing the B Class shares of the Company are reduced to 3, the Board of Trustees shall not be entitled to utilise these directors position by appointing replacements for such directors.

## r 33 Removal of directors

Subject to the Articles of Association of the company and the requirements of the Companies Act 2015, a director appointed by the Board of Trustees shall be removed by the Board of Trustees for misconduct or for conduct unbecoming or upon receipt by the Board of Trustees of a majority resolution of the landowners unitholders or extension unitholders who nominated him or her recommending such removal on the ground that the director had acted in a manner which was prejudicial to and against the interests of such unitholders, or in respect of the Government on receipt of a notice of removal duly signed by an authorised representative of the same.

## r 34 Variations of Rules

(a) The Board of Trustees upon receiving notification of a majority resolution of the beneficiaries obtained through secret ballot, shall vary, alter or amend the Trust Rules herein in accordance with the instructions contained in such resolutions.

(b) A majority resolution shall be an aggregate of not less than 50% of votes of all beneficiaries entitled to cast votes at separate meetings of the landowner unitholders, the Government and the extension unitholders,

provided however that no variation which affects the rights of a particular category of unitholders shall apply to such category unless in the case of the Government and extension forest owners such variation is supported by the consent in writing of the holders of not less than 50% of votes of all unitholders in those categories and in the case of landowner unitholders by a majority resolution passed in each of the separate landowner unitholders meeting called for the purpose.

## r 35 Period of Trust and determination

(a) The Trust created herein shall commence on the date hereof and shall continue until the vesting day.

(b) The vesting day will be such date as determined by the beneficiaries after the full redemption of the B Class and C Class shares in the company have been effected.

(c) The vesting day shall be deemed to be determined upon receipt of special resolution by each category of the beneficiaries approving such determination.

(d) At the vesting day or as soon as practicable after the vesting day, the Board of Trustees shall (subject as hereinafter provided)—

(i) sell, call in and convert into money or cause to be sold, called in and converted into money the investments and property constituting the Trust, and divide the proceeds of such sale and conversion (less all proper outgoings and all proper provision for liabilities) among the unitholders in proportion to the number of units of which they are at the date of determination of the Trust respectively registered as the holders provided however that the Board of Trustees may in their discretion at the request of any unitholder transfer to such unitholder any assets of the Trust fund in species in satisfaction or part satisfaction of the entitlement of the unitholder on the termination of this Trust, provided further that the extension landowner shall not for the purpose of distribution hereunder be entitled to receive any leasehold land and/or forest thereon or proceeds there from which the Trust has received as distribution in kind or in species.

(ii) The Board of Trustees shall give to each beneficiary not less than one month’s notice of the impending distribution herein.

(iii) The Board of Trustees may postpone the sale and conversion of any part of the investments and property of the Trust for such time as it thinks desirable in the interests of the beneficiaries and shall not be responsible for any loss attributed to such postponement.

(e) The Trust shall be deemed to be terminated from the date the Board of Trustees receive in writing evidence that not less than 75% of all the beneficiaries, herein wishing to determine the same provided however that the Trust shall not be determined unless—

(i) A vesting day has already been determined by the beneficiaries pursuant to paragraph (a) herein; and

(ii) That the evidences in writing shall contain the signatures of the requisite percentage of beneficiaries referred to herein.

## r 36 Seal

The Board of Trustees shall provide for the safe custody of the seal which shall only be used by the authority of the Board of Trustees or of a committee of trustees authorised by the Board of Trustees on their behalf and every instrument to which the seal is affixed shall be signed by the chairperson of the Board and the secretary.

## r 37 Jurisdiction

The Trust is subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the laws and the courts of Fiji.

## r 38 Repeal

The Fiji Pine Trust Rules made on 30 November 1990 is hereby repealed.
