## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Valuation Fees By-laws 1986.

## by-law 2 Minimum fees payable to registered valuers

The fees set out in the Schedule shall be the minimum fees to be paid to registered valuers in respect of work done as a registered valuer, with effect from 1 January 1987.
