## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Valuers Regulations 1989.

## reg 2 Application for registration

An application for registration as a registered valuer shall be made in such form as the Board approves.

## reg 3 Certificate of registration

(1) A registered valuer shall display his or her certificate of registration in a prominent place at his or her principal place of business.

(2) If a certificate of registration contains an error or omission, it shall be returned to the Registrar for alteration or replacement by the Registrar free of charge.

## reg 4 Changes to be noted in register

A registered valuer shall promptly notify the Registrar of any change in his or her principal place of business and in any other material particular and such change shall be noted in the register.

## reg 5 Certified copies of register entries

The Registrar shall, on payment of the prescribed fee, provide a certified copy of an entry in the register.

## reg 6 Inquiry by Board

(1) If the Board considers that it may take action under section 14(1) of the Act in respect of any registered valuer, then the Board shall conduct an inquiry.

(2) The Board shall give notice to the registered valuer of its intention to conduct an inquiry and shall state in writing the grounds of complaint giving rise to the inquiry.

(3) The notice under subregulation (2) shall state the time and place of the inquiry and shall be accompanied by a copy of any document (including the complaint of any person) upon which the Board intends to rely.

(4) The registered valuer shall be entitled to appear before the Board on the inquiry, either by himself or herself or with counsel, and to make representations and call evidence to rebut the complaint against him or her.

(5) If the registered valuer wishes to be heard personally or with counsel at the inquiry he or she shall, within 30 days of the service of the notice pursuant to subregulation (2), serve upon the Registrar a notice of intention to appear or a reply to the complaint.

(6) If the registered valuer fails to serve such a notice or reply, then the Board may proceed to hold its inquiry without further reference to him or her.

(7) The Board shall take into account any written representations made by or on behalf of the registered valuer.

(8) The Board may adjourn the hearing of an inquiry and in such an event the registered valuer shall be advised in writing of any adjourned date of an inquiry.

(9) The decision of the Board at an inquiry shall be set out in writing and served upon the registered valuer concerned as soon as possible.

(10) A decision of the Board to remove the name of any registered valuer from the Register or to suspend his or her registration shall, if a notice under subregulation (5) was received, not be acted upon for a period of 30 days after the decision of the Board is served upon the registered valuer.

## reg 7 Application for reinstatement

A person wishing to have his or her name reinstated on the Register after it has been removed, suspended or lapsed shall make application to the Board in a form approved by the Board and shall pay the prescribed reinstatement fee.

## reg 8 Forms

A copy of each form approved by the Board pursuant to these Regulations shall be kept available for inspection and copying at the offices of the Director of Lands and of the Registrar.

## reg 9 Fees and allowances

(1) The fees set out in the Schedule shall be payable for the matters to which they relate.

(2) A witness who appears at an inquiry of the Board shall be entitled to receive the allowances and expenses prescribed in the Criminal Procedure (Allowances to Witnesses) Rules 2016.
