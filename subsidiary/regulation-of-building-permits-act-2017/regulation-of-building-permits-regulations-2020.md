# REGULATION OF BUILDING PERMITS REGULATIONS 2020

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Regulation of Building Permits Regulations 2020.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 30 March 2020.

## reg 2 Prescribed times under section 6 of the Act

For the purposes of section 6 of the Act, the prescribed time for the Committee to—

(a) coordinate with approval agencies to ensure that the relevant approvals in relation to the issuance of a building permit are granted under section 6(c) is 40 working days;

(b) coordinate with an independent person to ensure that an assessment is carried out where an approval agency has failed to provide a response under section 6(d) is 14 working days; and

(c) coordinate with the authority to issue a building permit under section 6(e) is 40 working days.

## reg 3 Prescribed times under section 7 of the Act

For the purposes of section 7 of the Act, the prescribed time for the Committee to—

(a) direct approval agencies to consider and identify the necessary approvals required to issue a building permit under section 7(a) is 5 working days; and

(b) direct an authority to issue a building permit under section 7(d) is 7 working days.

## reg 4 Prescribed form, fee and times under section 9 of the Act

For the purposes of section 9 of the Act—

(a) the prescribed form for an application under section 9(2)(a) is set out in the Schedule;

(b) the prescribed fee for an application under section 9(2)(b) is $500;

(c) the prescribed time for the Committee to consider, send and coordinate the approval or refusal of an application under section 9(3) is 40 working days;

(d) the prescribed time for an approval agency to consider and determine the outcome of an application and notify the Committee and the applicant in writing of its decision under section 9(4) is 35 working days; and

(e) the prescribed time for the purposes of section 9(7) for an approval agency who fails to provide a response as required under section 9(4) is 35 working days.

## reg 5 Prescribed amount and term under section 13 of the Act

For the purposes of section 13 of the Act—

(a) the prescribed amount for the purposes of section 13(a) is $20,000;

(b) the prescribed amount for the purposes of section 13(b) is $20,000; and

(c) the prescribed term for the purposes of section 13(b) is 5 years.
