## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Banking (Amount of Nomination) Regulations 1995.

## reg 2 Maximum amount of nomination

For the purposes of Part 7 of the Banking Act 1995, the maximum amount of nomination shall be $10,000.
