## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Banking (Reporting of Large Credit Exposures) Regulations 1998.

## reg 2 Reports to the Reserve Bank

Information to be submitted to the Reserve Bank by licensed financial institutions pursuant to these regulations shall contain such information and be furnished within such time as is specified in Schedule 1 and shall be reported in accordance with the definitions set out in Schedule 2.

## reg 3 Other information required

The Reserve Bank may require a licensed financial institution to furnish such additional information relating to large credit exposures as the Reserve Bank may specify by notice in writing to the licensed financial institution concerned.
