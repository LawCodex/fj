## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Banking (Application for Licence Fees) Regulations 1995.

## reg 2 Application for licence fee

The prescribed amounts for the purposes of section 8(3) of the Act are—

(a) in respect of every application for a licence to operate as a credit institution, $1,500 (exclusive of Value Added Tax); and

(b) in respect of every application for a licence to operate as a bank, $3,000 (exclusive of Value Added Tax).
