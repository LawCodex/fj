## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Banking (Licence Fees) Regulations 1995.

## reg 2 Amount of annual licence fee

The prescribed amounts for the purposes of section 16(1) of the Act are—

(a) in respect of every licensed credit institution, $7,500 (exclusive of Value Added Tax) per annum; and

(b) in respect of every licensed bank, $15,000 (exclusive of Value Added Tax) per annum.
