## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Town Planning (Suva Rural) Order 1948.

## O 2 Town planning area

The area set out in the Schedule is hereby constituted a town planning area to be known as the Suva Rural Town Planning Area.

[O 2 am LN 97 of 1965 O 2, effective 13 August 1965]

## 

### SCHEDULE SUVA RURAL TOWN PLANNING AREA

[Sch subst LGN 8 of 1977, effective 6 May 1977; am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010; LN 99 of 2016 reg 275, effective 1 December 2016]

All that area of land situated in the Tikina of Suva and Naitasiri in the Provinces of Rewa and Naitasiri, extending from the western boundary of the Province of Rewa to Marshalls Mountain/Wainibuku Creek, in varying widths, from the high water mark of the sea coast to approximately half a mile inland of the proposed Suva-Nadi Highway and half a mile west of Princes Road; including Wailoku Settlement and all the islands within a distance of 2 miles from the said sea coast, but excluding the city of Suva and the town of Lami, as from time to time defined and all iTaukei villages. The area is more particularly delineated and shown verged red on Plan PP 70 deposited in the office of the Director of Lands in Suva and available for inspection at the offices of the permanent secretary responsible for local government, the Director of Town and Country Planning, the Commissioner Central and the secretary, Suva Rural Authority.
