# TOWN PLANNING (LEVUKA) ORDER 1948

**Table of Amendments**

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Town Planning (Levuka) Order 1948.

## O 2 Town planning area

The area comprising the town of Levuka shall be and is hereby constituted a town planning area to be known as the Levuka Town Planning Area.

[O 2 am LN 106 of 1965 O 2, effective 13 August 1965]

## O 3 Boundary limits

The limits of the town planning area hereby constituted shall be the limits and boundaries of the town of Levuka as from time to time declared.

[O 3 am LN 106 of 1965 O 2, effective 13 August 1965]
