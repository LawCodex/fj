## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Town Planning (Lami) Order 1977.

## O 2 Town planning area

The area comprising the town of Lami shall be and is hereby constituted a town planning area to be known as the Lami Town Planning Area.

## O 3 Boundary limits

Limits of the town planning area hereby constituted shall be the limits and boundaries of the town of Lami as from time to time declared.
