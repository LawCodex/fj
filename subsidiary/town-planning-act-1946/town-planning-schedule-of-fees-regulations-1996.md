## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Town Planning (Schedule of Fees) Regulations 1996.

## reg 2 Application form and fee

(1) Every development application submitted to the Director under the Town Planning Act 1946 shall be made on the form set out in Schedule 4 to the Town Planning (Interim Development) Regulations 1960, and shall be accompanied by a fee calculated in accordance with the Schedule to these Regulations.

(2) The development application as well as the fee shall be sent by registered mail to the Director by the local authorities.

(3) In addition, every application to be considered under the Town Planning Act 1946 shall be accompanied by:

(a) the particulars of the instrument of the title under which the land is held; and

(b) a locality diagram sufficient to enable the position of the land to be readily located on existing maps, plans or charts.

#### reg 6 Tourist resort development

A fee of $336 is payable for the processing of any application seeking rezoning for the purpose of tourist resort development.

#### reg 7 Conditional development

A fee of $67.55 per application is payable considered as “conditional development” under Town Planning Act 1946, General Provisions (1999) or other approved Town Planning Scheme General Provisions in respect of a city or town.

#### reg 8 Relaxation of provisions in the approved town planning scheme

A fee of $168 is payable for any application seeking any major relaxation or dispensations from the provisions in a Town Planning Scheme. A major relaxation constitutes invoking of Provisions 7(a), (b) and (c) of the General Provisions (1999) under the Town Planning Act 1946 or other approved Town Planning Scheme General Provisions in respect of a city or town.

#### reg 10 Local authorities administrative costs

A local authority is entitled to recover 2.5% of fees collected to pay for administrative costs.

#### reg 11 Local authorities to reimburse Director

A local authority shall pay back the actual expenses incurred in the preparation of a town planning scheme by the Director of Town and Country Planning unless such expenses are exempted by the Minister.

#### reg 12 Photocopying and sale of Town Planning Act General Provisions

(1) A fee of $1.77 per copy is to be charged on any photocopying on A3 sized paper and $1.24 on an A4 paper.

(2) The cost of a copy of Town Planning Act General Provisions shall be $11.56.

#### reg 13 Building development search fee

A fee of $13.13 is to be charged on any request for search for building (development) plan.

#### reg 14 Other applications

Any other application considered but is not included in any category stated above, a fee of $66.67 is payable on lodgement of such application.

#### reg 15 Value added tax Exclusive Price (VEP)

All fees payable under these Regulations are “VEP”.
