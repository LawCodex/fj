## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Town Planning (Navua Rural) Order 1970.

## O 2 Town planning area

The area set out in the Schedule is hereby constituted a town planning area to be known as the Navua Rural Town Planning Area.

## 

### SCHEDULE NAVUA RURAL TOWN PLANNING AREA

[Sch am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010; LN 99 of 2016 reg 272, effective 1 December 2016]

All that area of land situated in the Tikina of Serua, Nuku and Veivatuloa in the Provinces of Serua and Namosi, extending from the western to the eastern boundaries of the said provinces respectively, in varying widths, from the high water mark of the sea coast to approximately half a mile inland of the proposed Suva-Nadi Highway, except that the boundaries between the Taunovo River and Vunimoli and Waikalou Creeks extend from the high water mark of the sea coast to approximately 4.5 miles inland of the said Highway, including all the islands within a distance of 2 miles from the sea coast, but excluding all iTaukei villages.

The area is more particularly delineated and shown verged red on Plan PP69 deposited in the office of the Director of Lands in Suva and available for inspection at the offices of the permanent secretary responsible for local government, the Commissioner Central and the secretary, Navua Rural Local Authority.
