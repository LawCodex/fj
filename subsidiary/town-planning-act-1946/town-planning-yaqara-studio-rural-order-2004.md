## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Town Planning (Yaqara Studio Rural) Order 2004.

## O 2 Constitution of town planning area

The area specified in the Schedule is hereby constituted a town planning area to be known as the Yaqara Studio Rural Town Planning Area.

## 

### SCHEDULE YAQARA STUDIO RURAL TOWN PLANNING AREA

The area of land situated in the Tikina of Tavua in the Province of Ba, the boundaries of which are as follows—

Commencing at a point where Kings Road intersects the northern boundary of Lot 1, SO 3862; thence easterly for a distance of approximately 1.14 kilometres (km) along the north east boundary of Lot 1, SO 3862 to a point where it meets Naseyani Road; thence for a distance of 90 metres along Naseyani Road and along the eastern boundary Lot 1, SO 3862; thence in a northerly direction for a distance of approximately 0.59 km till it meets Naribaba Creek; thence in a south westerly direction for a distance of approximately 1.66 km to the south eastern tip of Lot 1, SO 3868; thence for a distance of 0.52 km to Nagasaubuca trig; thence along the south western boundary of Lot 1, SO 3868; thence in a west and northerly direction for approximately 2.11 km along the boundaries of Lot 39 part of RR 889 to a point where it meets the south western tip of Lot 48, DP 3403, thence along the western boundaries of DP 2903; thence for an approximate distance of 4km along the western boundaries of Lot 1, SO 3867 and the eastern boundaries of Lots 51 and Lot 23, DP 3403 to a point where it meets the southern boundary of Lot 1 SO 187; thence in a south westerly direction for a distance of 0.56km to a point where it meets Waikatakata Creek; thence along Waikatakata Creek for an approximate distance of 200 metres; thence for an approximate distance of 1.95km along the western boundaries of Lot 1, SO 187 and RR 259 till it crosses the Kings Road; thence along Kings Road for a distance of approximately 161 metres to a point where it meets the south western tip of RR 744; thence in a northern direction along the boundaries of RR 744 and RR 259 until it meets the northern tip of RR 259; thence along the mean high water mark of Tavua Bay to a point where it meets the north western point of Lot 1 SO 3861; thence easterly for a distance of 1.10km along the mean high water mark; thence southerly by a straight line to Kings Road being the commencement point.

The area is more particularly delineated and shown verged red on Proclamation Plan (PP) 687 deposited in the office of the Director of Lands, Suva.

Note (not part of the Schedule): The Plan (PP 687) may be inspected and viewed at the offices of the Director of Lands Suva, the permanent secretary responsible for local government, the Director of Town and Country Planning, Suva, the secretary Tavua Rural Local Authority, Tavua and the Commissioner Western, Lautoka.
