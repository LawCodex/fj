## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Town Planning (Ba Rural) Order 1948.

## O 2 Town planning area

The area set out in the Schedule is hereby constituted a town planning area to be known as the Ba Rural Town Planning Area.

[O 2 am LN 103 of 1965 O 2, effective 13 August 1965]

## 

### SCHEDULE BA RURAL TOWN PLANNING AREA

[Sch am LN 103 of 1965 O 2, effective 13 August 1965; Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, effective 1 March 2011]

The town planning area hereby constituted shall be all that area of land contained within the circle of radius of 2 miles from a point being the western corner of iTaukei Grant No 103 as more particularly delineated on a plan marked “Ba Rural Town Planning Area” and deposited in the office of the Director of Town and Country Planning, provided that if any portion of land containing less than 20 acres and described in one instrument of title is intersected by the said circle, the whole of the lands described in such instrument of title shall be deemed to be within the limits of the said area.

Save and except the whole of the land situated within the boundaries of the town of Ba as from time to time declared.
