## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Town Planning (Savusavu) Order 1969.

## O 2 Town planning area

The area comprising the town of Savusavu shall be and is hereby constituted a town planning area to be known as the Savusavu Town Planning Area.

## O 3 Boundary limits

The limits of the town planning area hereby constituted shall be the boundaries of the town of Savusavu as from time to time declared.
