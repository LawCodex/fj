## O 1 Short title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Town Planning (Rakiraki) Order 2010 and is effective from 1 July 2010.

## O 2 Composition

The area comprising of the town of Rakiraki shall be and is hereby constituted a town planning area to be known as the Rakiraki Town Planning Area.

## O 3 Boundary limits

The limits of the town planning area hereby constituted shall be the limits and boundaries of the town of Rakiraki as from time to time declared.
