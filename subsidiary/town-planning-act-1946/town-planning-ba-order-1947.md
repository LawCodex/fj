## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Town Planning (Ba) Order 1947.

## O 2 Town planning area

The area comprising the town of Ba shall be and is hereby constituted a town planning area to be known as the Ba Town Planning Area.

## O 3 Boundary limits

The limits of the town planning area hereby constituted shall be the limits and boundaries of the town of Ba as from time to time declared.
