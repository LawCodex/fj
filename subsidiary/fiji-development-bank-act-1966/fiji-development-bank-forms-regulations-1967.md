# FIJI DEVELOPMENT BANK (FORMS) REGULATIONS 1967

**Table of Amendments**

Fiji Development Bank (Forms) Regulations 1967 (LN 63 of 1967) commenced on 14 July 1967, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Stamp Duties (Repeal) Act 2020 (No 16 of 2020) | 1 August 2020 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Fiji Development Bank (Forms) Regulations 1967.

## reg 2 Forms

The forms appearing in the Schedule shall be used for the purposes of the Act in respect of the matters specified in them.
