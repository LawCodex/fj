## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Credit Unions (Fees) Order 1959.

## O 2 Fees

Every credit union registered under the provisions of the Credit Unions Act 1954 shall pay to the Registrar of Credit Unions the fees set out in the Schedule at the times specified therein.
