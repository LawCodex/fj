## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration Regulations 1976.

## reg 2 Registration of birth more than 12 months after birth

After the expiration of 12 months from the date of the birth of any person, the Registrar may, upon the production to him or her of satisfactory evidence on oath together with such other proof as he or she may deem necessary of the fact, time and place of birth, and of the particulars of the parents of such person and of the previous issue of each of such parents, duly register such birth.

## reg 3 Fees

The fees specified in Schedule 1 shall be charged by the Registrar in respect of the several matters specified in such Schedule.

## reg 3A Allowances to assistant district registrars

Each assistant district registrar may be paid by the Registrar the sum of $0.25 in respect of each birth registered by such assistant district registrar.

[reg 3A insrt LN 132 of 1979 reg 2, effective 27 July 1979]

## reg 4 Forms

The forms set out in Schedule 2, with such modifications as the Registrar shall from time to time determine to be necessary, shall be the forms to be used for the purposes of these Regulations.

## reg 5 Revocation

The Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration Regulations 1968 are hereby revoked.

## reg 6 Fees

The fees specified in Schedule 3 shall be charged by Post Fiji Limited in the extraction of birth, death and marriage certificates in Post Offices designated by the Minister in the Gazette.

[reg 6 insrt LN 102 of 2008 reg 2, effective 5 September 2008]

## reg 7 Registrar may require additional information

The Registrar may require a person who applies to have his or her name, or his or her child’s name, changed to provide additional information or documents as the Registrar deems necessary.

[reg 7 insrt LN 104 of 2021 reg 2, effective 6 October 2021]

## reg 8 Consent of parents required for change of name of child

The consent of the parents is required for a child’s name to be changed.

[reg 8 insrt LN 104 of 2021 reg 2, effective 6 October 2021]

## reg 9 Use of new birth certificate after change of name

If a person has changed his or her name in accordance with section 15 of the Act and these Regulations, he or she must only use his or her new birth certificate and must not use his or her previous birth certificate.

[reg 9 insrt LN 104 of 2021 reg 2, effective 6 October 2021]
