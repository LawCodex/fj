## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Fiji Public Trustee Corporation Limited (Fees and Charges) Regulations 2008.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Act** means the Fiji Public Trustee Corporation Limited Act 2006.
