# COMPULSORY EDUCATION REGULATIONS 1997

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Compulsory Education Regulations 1997.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on 20 January 1997.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, the term—

**compulsory education** means the compulsory attendance at school of school-age children;

**compulsory school age** means any age between and inclusive of 6 years and 15 years old;

**Minister** means the Minister responsible for education;

**Permanent Secretary** means the permanent secretary responsible for education; and

**School Attendance Officer** means a District Education Officer and other officers who may be nominated by the Permanent Secretary to carry out duties relating to the encouragement and counselling of parents and guardians to send their school-age children to school and also to assist with duties relating to the counselling of students playing truant from school.

## reg 3 Compulsory school attendance requirement

(1) Subject to regulation 5, school attendance at Class 1 level shall be compulsory for all children turning 6 years old by 30 June of any year.

(2) Children shall be required to remain at school until they have completed the Class 8 or the Form 2 level of education, or at the end of the year the child turns 15 years of age.

## reg 4 Parents responsibility

(1) It shall be the duty of the parent or guardian of every child of compulsory school age to cause him or her to receive suitable and relevant education by regular attendance at a school unless one of the following conditions apply—

(a) there is no school within walking distance of the child’s place of residence;

(b) the parent or guardian makes other arrangements deemed by the Minister to be suitable and efficient for the education of the child;

(c) the child has completed the required levels of primary education;

(d) the child is prevented from attending school by sickness or any other unavoidable cause.

(2) Any school age child who is prevented from attending school due to any one of the foregoing conditions shall be deemed to be under special circumstances as defined in regulation 5 below.

## reg 5 Special circumstances

(1) A child deemed to be in special circumstances may be exempted from the provisions of these Regulations at the discretion of the Permanent Secretary.

(2) The parent or guardian of such a school age child deemed to be in special circumstances shall make an application on the appropriate form provided, and shall send it to the Permanent Secretary no later than 1 March of the year, if the child is known to a school community.

(3) If the school age child is “discovered” through the normal routine checks made by School Attendance Officers, the application may be submitted at an appropriate time during the year.

(4) The application shall be supported by such evidence as the Minister may require.

## reg 6 School responsibility

(1) School Managements, Heads of Schools and teachers are required to cooperate with students, their parents, guardians and relatives in the execution of their daily responsibilities in matters relating to compulsory education.

(2) Schools are required to make parents and guardians aware of the responsibility of a parent or guardian under regulation 4 and the special circumstances specified under regulation 5.

(3) Schools are required to cooperate with government in the fulfilment of the provisions of regulation 3 that all children entering Class 1 should remain at school through to Class 8 or Form 2 level or the year the child turns 15 years of age.

## reg 7 School Attendance Officers

(1) District Education Officers, shall do the work of School Attendance Officers and shall as part of their normal duty, make routine checks on the provisions of these Regulations.

(2) School Attendance Officers may after seeking permission, enter a yard, house, village or place between the hours of 9 o’clock in the morning and 3 o’clock in the afternoon of any day of the week except Saturdays, Sundays, public holidays and school holidays, to make enquiries as to any child there residing or employed.

(3) School Attendance Officers may in the conduct of his or her duty, seek information from other sources such as the provincial offices.

(4) School Attendance Officers shall in the conduct of his or her duty, be careful to observe and respect the citizen’s rights and also conduct himself or herself in a courteous and non-aggressive manner.

(5) School Attendance Officers shall conduct his or her duty so as not to apprehend offenders, but rather with a view to counselling parents and guardians of the benefits of education and how they (parents/guardians) might receive assistance from identified sources.

## reg 8 Review of procedures

The Minister may from time to time review the procedures governing the administration of these Regulations.
