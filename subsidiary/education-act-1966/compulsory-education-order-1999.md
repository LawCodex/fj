## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Compulsory Education Order 1999.

## O 2 Appointment of districts

As additional districts in which education for children of compulsory school age is compulsory—

(a) Nausori Education District;

(b) Nadroga/Navosa Education District; and

(c) Ba/Tavua Education District.

## O 3 Relationship to Compulsory Education Order 1997

This Order is to be read as one with the Compulsory Education Order 1997 and the provisions of that Order except for paragraph 5 apply as if set out in this Order.

## O 4 Application of Compulsory Education Regulations 1997

The Compulsory Education Regulations 1997 apply to the 3 districts specified in this Order.
