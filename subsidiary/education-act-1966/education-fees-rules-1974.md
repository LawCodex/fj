## r 1 Short title

These Rules may be cited as the Education (Fees) Rules 1974.

## r 2 Fees: Resident pupils

The fees set out in Schedule 1 shall be charged in respect of pupils who are ordinarily resident in Fiji and who are in full time attendance at the Government Schools specified in such Schedule.

## r 3 Fees: Pupils not ordinarily resident in Fiji

The fees set out in Schedule 2 shall be charged in respect of pupils who are not ordinarily resident in Fiji or who are in part time attendance at the Government Schools specified in such Schedule.

## r 4 Interpretation

For the purposes of these Rules, “a pupil ordinarily resident in Fiji” includes a pupil whose parents or one of whose parents are or is in employment in Fiji.
