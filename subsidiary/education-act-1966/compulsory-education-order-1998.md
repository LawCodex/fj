## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Compulsory Education Order 1998:

## O 2 Appointment of districts

Additional districts in which children of compulsory school age shall have compulsory education with effect from 19 January 1998—

(a) Eastern Division; and

(b) Macuata/Bua Education District.

## O 3 Relationship to Compulsory Education Order 1997

This Order shall be read as one with the Compulsory Education Order 1997 and the provisions of that Order except for paragraph 5 shall apply as if set out in this Order.

## O 4 Application of Compulsory Education Regulations 1997

The Compulsory Education Regulations 1997 apply to the 2 districts specified in this Order.
