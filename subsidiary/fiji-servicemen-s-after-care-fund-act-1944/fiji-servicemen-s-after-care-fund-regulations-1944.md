# FIJI SERVICEMEN'S AFTER-CARE FUND REGULATIONS 1944

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Fiji Servicemen's After-care Fund Regulations 1944.

## reg 2 Regional committee

The Managing Committee may establish such regional advisory committees as it may deem necessary or expedient for the efficient administration of the Fund, and may define the area within which each such regional committee shall exercise its functions, and may in its discretion abolish any regional committee so established.

## reg 3 Duties of regional committee

It shall be the duty of every regional committee—

(a) to investigate to the best of its ability every case referred to it by the Managing Committee and to submit to the Managing Committee with all reasonable dispatch a report on each such case together with any recommendation which it may think fit to make thereon;

(b) to bring to the notice of the Managing Committee any case within the limits of its area which it may consider to be deserving of assistance from the Fund notwithstanding that no application for assistance has been lodged in respect of such case.

## reg 4 Members of regional committee

A regional committee shall consist of not more than 4 members, who shall be appointed by the Managing Committee, together with a chairperson, who shall be appointed by the Minister.

## reg 5 Term of office

The chairperson and members of a regional committee shall hold office for 3 years, but shall be eligible for reappointment, provided that the Minister or Managing Committee, as the case may be, may at any time remove from office the chairperson or any member of a regional committee and may appoint some other person to be chairperson or member in his or her place.

## reg 6 Temporary members

During the illness, incapacity or absence from Fiji of a chairperson or member of a regional committee the Minister or Managing Committee, as the case may be, may appoint any person to act as chairperson or member of such committee in his or her place for so long as the illness, incapacity or absence continues or until such chairperson or member otherwise ceases to be a chairperson or member of the regional committee.

## reg 7 Applications for assistance

All applications for assistance from the Fund shall be made in one of the Forms set out in the Schedule and shall contain the particulars required by such Form, and shall be addressed to the secretary of the Managing Committee. The applicant shall in every case complete and sign the declaration at the foot of such Form.

## reg 8 Forms to be sent to committee

Forms of application, when completed, may either be sent direct to the secretary of the Managing Committee, or may be sent or delivered to a chairperson of a regional committee. In the latter case it shall be the duty of such chairperson to forward the application to the secretary of the Managing Committee with all reasonable dispatch.

## reg 9 Grant of assistance though no application

The Managing Committee may consider and grant assistance in any case notwithstanding that no application for assistance has been made in respect of such case.

## reg 10 Proceedings to be confidential

All applications for assistance, reports of regional committees and all proceedings of the Managing Committee and regional committees shall be treated as confidential and shall not, except so far as may be necessary for the efficient administration of the Fund, be published or otherwise disclosed to any person not concerned with the administration of the Fund.
