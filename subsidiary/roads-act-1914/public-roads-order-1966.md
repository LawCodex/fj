## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Public Roads Order 1966.

[O 1 subst LN 6 of 1986 O 2, effective 31 January 1986]

## O 2 Interpretation

A reference in this Order to a plan by number, or by a letter (or letters) and a number, is a reference to the plan so designated that is kept in the custody of the Surveyor-General.

[O 2 subst LN 6 of 1986 O 2, effective 31 January 1986]

## O 3 Public roads

The roads, streets and thoroughfares specified in the Schedule are public roads for the purposes of the Act.

[O 3 subst LN 6 of 1986 O 2, effective 31 January 1986]
