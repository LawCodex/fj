## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Roads (Wheel) Regulations 1919.

## reg 2 Size of tyre

The tyre of each wheel of all springless carts, wagons and other springless conveyances used on a public road shall be of a minimum width of 2.5 inches.

## reg 3 Wheelless conveyances prohibited

No conveyance without wheels shall be used on or shall be permitted to pass over or along any public road.

## reg 4 Penalty

Any person using or being the owner thereof permitting to be used any conveyance contrary to the foregoing provisions of these Regulations shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $10 for each such offence.
