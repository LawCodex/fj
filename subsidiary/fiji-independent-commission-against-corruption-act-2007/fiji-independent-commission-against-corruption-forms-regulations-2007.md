# FIJI INDEPENDENT COMMISSION AGAINST CORRUPTION (FORMS) REGULATIONS 2007

Fiji Independent Commission Against Corruption (Forms) Regulations 2007 (GN 1627 of 2007) commenced on 14 September 2007, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Fiji Independent Commission Against Corruption (Forms) Regulations 2007.

## reg 2 Forms

The forms set out in the Schedule are prescribed for use in connection with the Fiji Independent Commission Against Corruption Act 2007 .

## 

### SCHEDULE FORMS

#### FORM 1

**FIJI INDEPENDENT COMMISSION AGAINST CORRUPTION ACT 2007**

INFORMATION TO OBTAIN A SEARCH WARRANT

In the Magistrates Court at Fiji.

The information of *(name and description of informant)* of *(address of informant)* who upon oath states:

THAT he or she believes that in *(description of premises or place)* at *(address of premises or place)* of *(name of owner, occupier or person in charge of premises or place, if known)* there are *(description of things)* which are or which contain evidence of *(description of offence)*, being an offence to which the Fiji Independent Commission Against Corruption Act 2007 applies.

AND THAT the grounds of such belief are *(statement of grounds of belief)*.

*(Signature of informant)*

Sworn before me this day of 20

*(Signature and description of witness)*

#### FORM 2

**FIJI INDEPENDENT COMMISSION AGAINST CORRUPTION ACT 2007**

SEARCH WARRANT

In the Magistrates Court at Fiji.

To: *(name of officer of Fiji Independent Commission Against Corruption),* an officer

of the Fiji Independent Commission Against Corruption.

Whereas it is made to appear to me by information laid this day by *(name of informant under the Information)* that there is reason to believe that in *(description of premises or place)* at *(address of premises or place)* of *(name of owner, occupier or person in charge of premises or place, if known)* *(“the specified premises or place”)* there are *(description of things)* which are or which contain evidence of *(description of offence)*, being an offence to which the Fiji Independent Commission Against Corruption Act 2007 applies;

You, and any other officers of the Fiji Independent Commission Against Corruption who may be assisting you, are hereby authorised to enter and search the specified premises or place.

Dated this day of 20

*(Signature of Magistrate)*
