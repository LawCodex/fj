# FISHERIES (RESTRICTION OF USE OF UNDERWATER BREATHING APPARATUS) REGULATIONS 1997

Fisheries (Restriction of Use of Underwater Breathing Apparatus) Regulations 1997 (LN 17 of 1997) commenced on 1 March 1997, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Fisheries (Restriction of Use of Underwater Breathing Apparatus) Regulations 1997.

[reg 1 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 87, 1 December 2016]

## reg 2 Commencement

These Regulations shall come into force on 1 March 1997.

## reg 3 Interpretation

In these Regulations—

(a) fish has the same meaning assigned to it under the Fisheries Act 1941; and

(b) underwater breathing apparatus means and includes scuba, gear, compressors and other mechanical and robotical means in the collection, catching and diving for fish.

## reg 4 Restriction of use of underwater breathing apparatus

(1) Subject to regulation 5 , no person shall—

(a) in any way collect, take or dive for fish using underwater breathing apparatus;

(b) be in possession of underwater breathing apparatus for the purpose of collecting, catching and diving for fish.

(2) Any person who contravenes the provision of this regulation shall be liable to a fine of $400 or imprisonment for a term of 6 months or to both fine and imprisonment.

## reg 5 Exemptions

The permanent secretary responsible for fisheries or any person appointed by him or her in writing, may upon a written request by any person, exempt that person by writing under his or her hand, from the provision of regulation 4(1) if, that person holds a valid licence to fish issued by the Fisheries Department and in addition, the person has satisfied the permanent secretary responsible for fisheries or the person appointed by him or her in that behalf that the person—

(a) holds a valid licence issued by the Fisheries Department to operate underwater breathing apparatus;

(b) holds a certificate of safety in regulation to the underwater breathing apparatus to be used, issued by the Ministry responsible for the administration of labour and industrial relations;

(c) is a certified diving operator being so certified by a reputable diving instructor; and

(d) fish stock and traditional fishing rights in the area of operation will not be adversely affected.

[reg 5 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 87, 1 December 2016]
