## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [regs 1-2]

### reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Fisheries Regulations 1965.

### reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**estuary** means that portion of an estuary specified by the fisheries officer by notice in the Gazette;

**Fiji fishing vessel** means any fishing vessel which is registered in Fiji or the operations of which are based in Fiji and which is wholly-owned by a natural person who is a citizen of, or a company, which is at least 30% owned by Fijian citizens, incorporated in Fiji. For the purpose of avoiding doubt, any fishing vessel which is not a Fiji fishing vessel by this definition shall be considered for licensing under the provisions of the Marine Spaces Act 1977 ;

[def insrt LN 25 of 1990 reg 2, 1 March 1990]

**fishing gear** means all equipment used or intended for the purpose of catching fish and includes fishing lines, hooks, nets, spears, reels and line or net haulers, whether they be manually, hydraulically or otherwise assisted by mechanical or electrical means;

[def insrt LN 25 of 1990 reg 2, 1 March 1990]

**Minister** means the Minister responsible for fisheries matters; and

[def insrt LN 25 of 1990 reg 2, 1 March 1990]

**set net** includes a gill net, drift net, trammel net or any other sort of net which acts by enmeshing or entangling any fish.

[def insrt LN 25 of 1990 reg 2, 1 March 1990]

## PART 2 LICENCES AND REGISTRATION [regs 3-5A]

### reg 3 Fees

The fees to be charged for persons taking fish by way of trade or business or as employees thereof, and for registration of fishing vessels shall be as follows—

(a)

(i) for the captain of every fishing vessel, a fee of $4 per annum;

(ii) for each member of the crew other than the captain and for each person taking fish as an employee of a person carrying on the trade or business of a fisherman, a fee of $1 per annum;

(iii) for each person who takes crustaceans or shellfish by net or hand net for sale, no fee, provided that no further licence shall be required in respect of the wife or husband, and not more than 2 children under the age of 21 years, of the licensee who assist the licensee and, where applicable, use his or her hand nets;

(iv) for any other person carrying on the trade or business of a fisherman, a fee of $4 per annum;

(b)

(i) for each fishing vessel propelled by an engine or sail, an annual fee of $4;

(ii) for each fishing vessel propelled by oars, paddles or poles, whether such hand-propelled vessel is worked independently or in conjunction with a power or sail-driven mother vessel, an annual fee of $1.

[reg 3 am LN 119 of 1968 reg 2, 9 August 1968]

### reg 4 Licences

(1) For the purpose of avoiding doubt, it is hereby declared that a licence under regulation 3 does not permit the holder to take fish on any reef or on any kai (cockle) or other shellfish bed in any area in respect of which the rights of any mataqali or other division or subdivision of the Fijian people have been registered by the iTaukei Fisheries Commission in the Register of iTaukei Customary Fishing Rights, unless he or she shall first have obtained a permit to do so under section 13 of the Fisheries Act 1941 from the Commissioner of the Division in which such area is situated. The holder of any such permit shall produce it to the licensing officer at the time of his or her application for a licence under regulation 3 in order that the particulars thereon may be included in the application and recorded on the licence.

[subreg (1) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, 2 July 2010 Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, 1 March 2011]

(2) Every licence granted under these Regulations shall expire on 31 December next following the date of issue and shall be renewable at the discretion of the licensing officer upon payment of the prescribed fee.

## PART 3 PROHIBITED METHODS AND AREAS [regs 6-11]

### reg 6 Location etc of fish fences, etc

It shall be competent for the fisheries officer—

(a) to determine the location and distance between fish fences or other similar obstructive devices of a permanent or semi-permanent nature; and

(b) to order the removal of a fish fence or other similar obstructive fishing device.

### reg 7 Use of nets in estuaries

No net other than hand nets, wading nets and cast nets shall be permitted for the purpose of taking fish in the estuary of any river or stream or in the sea within 100 m of the mouth of a river or stream.

[reg 7 am LN 87 of 1979 O 2, 4 May 1979]

### reg 8 Use of poison

No person shall take, stupefy or kill any fish in any lake, pool, pond, river, stream or in the sea by the use of any of the following substances or plants—

(a) any chemical or chemical compound;

(b) any substance containing derris;

(c) any substance containing the active principal of derris, namely, rotenone;

(d) any plant or extract of or derivative from any plant, belonging to the *genera Barringtonia, Derris, Euphorbia, Pittosporum or Tephrosia,*

or place any of such substances or plants in any water for the purpose of taking, stupefying or killing any fish.

### reg 9 Spearing of turtle

No person shall harpoon any turtle unless the harpoon is armed with at least one barb of which the point projects not less than 9.5 mm from the surface of the shaft, measured at right angles to the long axis of the shaft.

[reg 9 am LN 87 of 1979 O 2, 4 May 1979]

### reg 10 Fishing in fresh water

No person shall kill or take fish of any kind (excluding shellfish) in fresh water in any manner other than by means of a hand net, portable fish trap, spear, line and hook.

### reg 11 Restricted area

No person, unless he or she is authorised in writing under the hand of the Commissioner of the Division in which the area described in the Schedule 5 is situated shall, within such area, kill or take fish of any kind whatsoever, except by hand net, wading net, spear or line and hook.

## PART 4 MESH LIMITATIONS [regs 12-17]

### reg 12 Stretched measurements for nets

For the purposes of these Regulations, stretched mesh shall be measured by taking 2 diagonally opposite knots of the mesh of the net and drawing them apart until the remaining 2 knots of the mesh just touch each other.

### reg 13 Mesh of hand nets

The mesh of a hand net may be of any size.

### reg 14 Mesh of cast nets

The mesh of cast nets shall not be less than 30 mm, wet and stretched.

[reg 14 am LN 87 of 1979 O 2, 4 May 1979]

### reg 15 Nets for sardines and whitebait

Nets for taking sardines and whitebait may have a mesh size of not less than 30 mm, wet and stretched, but their overall dimensions shall not exceed 10.5 m measured along the cork line and 1.5 m from the cork line to the ground rope. It is prohibited to join 2 such nets together. Such nets may not be used to take fish other than sardines or whitebait.

[reg 15 am LN 87 of 1979 O 2, 4 May 1979]

### reg 16 Meshes of other nets

(1) The meshes of wading nets, gill nets and of all nets not specifically mentioned in these Regulations shall in no part be of a size less than 75 mm wet and stretched, provided that—

(a) the meshes of gill nets may be of a size not less than 30 mm wet and stretched that have been obtained from the Fisheries Division of the Department of MPI and are used only for the purpose of taking fish of the genus *Hemiramphus* (busa) (garfish); and

(2) The meshes of bukiyami net may be of any size which may be used for purpose of taking fish with the prior written permission of the Director.

[reg 16 subst LN 67 of 1992 reg 3, 1 July 1992]

### reg 16A Import of fishing nets

No person shall Import into Fiji fishing nets of any kind whatsoever without the prior written permission of the Director.

[reg 16A insrt LN 67 of 1992 reg 3, 1 July 1992]

### reg 17 Fish fences

In every fish fence constructed with cane or reed screens there shall be at the inmost point in each terminal trap or pan a section not less than 1 m in length, and of the full height of the fence wherein the cane or reeds shall be placed not less than 50 mm apart, or wherein the cane or reed screen shall be replaced by netting or cord, galvanised wire or expanded metal, the mesh of which shall measure not less than 50 mm across the smallest diameter. In fences constructed entirely of wire netting or of expanded metal, the mesh of the netting in the traps or pans shall measure not less than 50 mm along the shortest diameter.

[reg 17 am LN 87 of 1979 O 2, 4 May 1979]

## PART 5 SIZE AND LIMITS OF FISH AND PROHIBITIONS [regs 18-26]

### reg 18 Fish

No person shall kill, take, sell or offer or expose for sale, any fish listed in the Schedule 6 of less than the lengths therein set out, measured in each case from the point of the snout to the middle of the tailfin when the fish is laid flat, provided that this regulation shall not apply to fish caught by children under the age of 16 years when fishing with hook and line from the shore or wading near the shore and not offered or exposed for sale.

### reg 19 Crabs

No person shall kill, take, sell or offer or expose for sale any crab of the species *Scylla serrata* (Swimming Crab or Qari Dina) of less than 125 mm measured across the widest part of the carapace or shell.

[reg 19 am LN 87 of 1979 O 2, 4 May 1979]

### reg 20 Turtle

(1) No person shall at any time dig up, use, take, sell, offer or expose for sale or destroy turtle eggs of any species or in any way molest, take, sell, offer or expose for sale or kill any turtle the shell of which is less than 455 mm in length. No person during the months of January, February, November or December in any year shall in any way molest or take, sell, offer or expose for sale or kill any turtle of any size.

[subreg (1) am LN 140 of 1991 reg 2, 1 January 1991]

(2) No person shall be in possession of, sell, offer or expose for sale or export any turtle shell the length of which is less than 455 mm.

[subreg (2) insrt LN 89 of 1966 reg 2, 24 June 1966am LN 87 of 1979 O 2, 4 May 1979]

### reg 20A Moratorium on molesting, taking or killing of turtles

(1) Notwithstanding regulation 20 , no person shall—

(a) in any way molest, or take or kill turtles of any species; or

(b) sell, offer or expose for sale or export any turtle shell or flesh or derivatives; or

(c) dig up, use, take or destroy turtle eggs of any turtle species; or

(d) use turtle, turtle derivatives, eggs or turtle shells for any purpose including education, research or tourism;

(e) negatively impact turtle habitats.

[subreg (1) am LN 62 of 2010 reg 2, 11 June 2010]

(2) The Minister may, upon a written request by any person, exempt that person by writing under his or her hand, from subregulation (1) .

(3) This regulation expires on 31 December 2018.

[subreg (3) am LN 62 of 2010 reg 2, 11 June 2010]

(4) Subject to subregulation (2) , any person who contravenes subregulation (1) commits an offence and is liable on conviction to the penalty set out in section 10(2)(c) of the Act.

[reg 20A insrt LN 15 of 2004 reg 2, 6 February 2004]

### reg 21 Trocas

No person shall take, be in possession of, sell, offer or expose for sale or export any shell—

(a) of the species *Trochus niloticus (sici)* (trocas shell) measuring less than 90 mm across the whorl;

(b) of the species *Pinctada margaratifera (civa)* (pearl oyster shell) of which the nacre or mother-of-pearl measures less than 100 mm from the butt or hinge to the opposite edge or lip.

[reg 21 am LN 89 of 1966 reg 3, 24 June 1966 LN 87 of 1979 O 2, 4 May 1979]

### reg 22 Davui

No person shall take, sell or offer or expose for sale, or export any shell of the species *Charonia tritonis* (davui).

[reg 22 insrt LN 24 of 1970 reg 2, 13 February 1970]

### reg 23 Giant helmet shell

No person shall take, sell or offer or expose for sale, or export any shell of the species *Cassis cornuta* (giant helmet shell).

[reg 23 insrt LN 24 of 1970 reg 2, 13 February 1970]

### reg 24 Application of regulations 21A and 21B (Repealed)

[reg 24 rep LN 99 of 2016 reg 88, 1 December 2016]

### reg 25 Porpoises and dolphins

No person shall kill, take, sell or offer or expose for sale any porpoise or dolphin of the *genera Phocaena* or *Delphis (babale)*. For the purpose of avoiding doubt, the expression “dolphin” used in this regulation shall not be deemed to refer to the fish commonly known as a dolphin but of the *genus Coryphaena*.

[reg 25 insrt LN 89 of 1966 reg 4, 24 June 1966]

### reg 25A Export of beche-de-mer

No person shall export, either in a natural or processed form, holothurians (beche-de-mer) of the species *Metriatyla scahra (Holothurian scabra)* (dairo) (sandfish) or shall take, be in possession of, sell, offer or expose for sale or export holothurians, either in a natural or processed form, or of any other species whatsoever of a length less than 7.6 centimetres.

[reg 25A subst LN 105 of 1990 reg 2, 1 August 1990]

### reg 25B Export of giant clam meat

No person shall export from Fiji flesh of the *tridacnid* clam *(vasua)* (giant clam), including adductor muscle or mantle tissue, of the following species—

(a) *Tridacna derasa (vasua dina);*

(b) *Tridacna squamosa (cega);*

(c) *Tridacna maxima (katavatu).*

[reg 25B insrt LN 113 of 1988 reg 2, 1 January 1989]

### reg 26 Export of fish

No person shall export from Fiji—

(a) live fish of any kind whatsoever;

(b) turtle flesh;

(c) turtle shell unless worked into jewellery or otherwise processed into a form approved by the permanent secretary responsible for fisheries.

[reg 26 am LN 140 of 1991 reg 3, 1 January 1991 LN 99 of 2016 reg 88, 1 December 2016]

## PART 6 EXEMPTIONS [regs 27-30]

### reg 27 Exemptions by public officers

The permanent secretary responsible for fisheries or any person appointed by him or her may exempt any person, in writing, from any of the provisions of regulations 8 , 10 , 14 , 15 , 16 , 18 , 19 , 20 , 21 , 25 , 25A , 25B and 26 .

[reg 27 am LN 113 of 1988 reg 3, 1 January 1989 LN 99 of 2016 reg 88, 1 December 2016]

### reg 28 Exemption relating to Fijians

Any Fijian assisting a licensed fisherman in a traditional or customary fish drive shall not require a licence to take fish by way of trade or business by reason of the fact that such assistance is given, whether free of charge or on payment.

### reg 29 Exemption relating to punts or skiffs

(1) Where open unpowered punts or skiffs, each not exceeding 7.3 m in length, are employed in fishing by way of trade or business in conjunction with and under the immediate overall control of the captain of a power-driven or sailing fishing vessel, neither the persons in charge nor the crew of such punts or skiffs shall require licences to carry on the trade or business of a fisherman when employed solely as aforesaid.

[subreg (1) am LN 87 of 1979 O 2, 4 May 1979]

(2) For the purposes of this regulation, the expression “sailing vessel” shall mean a vessel propelled solely or mainly by sail whether or not such vessel possesses an engine.
