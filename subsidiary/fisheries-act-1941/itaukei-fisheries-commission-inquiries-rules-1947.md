# ITAUKEI FISHERIES COMMISSION (INQUIRIES) RULES 1947

iTaukei Fisheries Commission (Inquiries) Rules 1947 (LN 105 of 1947) commenced on 9 May 1947, as amended by:

## r 1 Short title

These Rules may be cited as the iTaukei Fisheries Commission (Inquiries) Rules 1947.

[r 1 am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, 1 March 2011]

## r 2 Interpretation

In these Rules, unless the context otherwise requires—

**claimant** means any iTaukei claiming an interest in customary fishing rights of which the ownership is being inquired into.

[def am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, 2 July 2010]

## r 3 Notice of inquiry

When the Commission is about to institute inquiries into the ownership of any fishing right, notice of the time and place at which the first sitting of the Commission for that purpose will be held shall be given to the Roko of the province in which the fishing right is situated.

## r 4 Delivery of notice

The notice shall be delivered to the Roko not less than 30 days before the sitting of the Commission.

## r 5 Publication of meeting

A general notice of the intention to hold a meeting of the Commission giving the time and place of the meeting shall be published in a newspaper published in the iTaukei language and published in Fiji and in the Gazette, and also be broadcast in iTaukei by wireless.

[r 5 am LN 186 of 1977 reg 2, 9 December 1977 Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, 2 July 2010]

## r 6 Mode of publication

The Roko shall cause the time and place of the sitting of the Commission to be publicly proclaimed in each tikina.

## r 7 Witnesses

Any member of a social unit claiming to have any customary fishing rights shall be allowed to give evidence of such claim before the Commission.

## r 8 Claimant’s deputy

Any claimant who, by reason of illness or other cause which the Commission may consider sufficient, is unable to attend the sittings of the Commission, may depute any male member of his or her mataqali to act as his or her representative and such representative shall be entitled to all privileges which might have been exercised by the claimant if present in person at the sitting.

## r 9 Adjournment

The Commission may at its discretion from time to time adjourn the sitting either *sine die* or to a fixed time and place. All claimants to fishing rights within the province in which the Commission is sitting at the time of adjournment shall be deemed to have notice of the adjournment.

## r 10 Language

All proceedings before the Commission shall be conducted in the iTaukei language.

[r 10 Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, 2 July 2010]

## r 11 Forms

The Forms set out in Schedules 1 and 2 shall be used in recording the boundaries and owners of fishing rights.
