## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Coconut Industry (Licences and Fees) Regulations 1966.

## reg 2 Forms of licences

(1) Licences issued by the Ministry to copra makers shall be in Form 1 in Schedule 1 and shall be subject to the conditions specified therein together with such additional conditions as the Ministry may impose.

(2) Licences issued by the Ministry to coconut oil makers shall be in Form 2 in Schedule 1 and shall be subject to the conditions specified therein together with such additional conditions as the Ministry may impose.

(3) Licences issued by the Ministry to copra exporters shall be in Form 3 in Schedule 1 and shall be subject to the conditions specified therein together with such additional conditions as the Ministry may impose.

(4) Licences issued by the Ministry to coconut product makers shall be in Form 4 in Schedule 1 and shall be subject to the conditions specified therein together with such additional conditions as the Ministry may impose.

(5) Licences issued by the Ministry to exporters of coconuts shall be in Form 5 in Schedule 1 and shall be subject to the conditions specified therein together with such additional conditions as the Ministry may impose.

(6) Licences issued by the Ministry to copra traders shall be in Form 6 in Schedule 1 and shall be subject to the conditions specified therein together with such additional conditions as the Ministry may impose.

[reg 2 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 36, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 3 Fees

The fees set out in Schedule 2 shall be payable to the Ministry on the issue of the respective licences specified therein, and thereafter annually in respect of each year or part thereof during which any such licence remains in force.

[reg 3 subst LN 73 of 1966 reg 2, effective 3 June 1966; am LN 99 of 2016 reg 36, effective 1 December 2016]

(hereinafter referred to as “the licensee”) is hereby licensed under the provisions of the Coconut Industry Development Act 2010, to purchase coconuts for copra making subject to the following conditions—

1. This licence shall remain in force until cancelled or suspended by the Ministry or surrendered by the licensee;

2. The licensee shall pay such price for all units of coconuts bought by him or her as may be fixed by the Ministry from time to time for the zone in which he or she purchases such coconuts;

3. The copra drier or driers of the licensee shall be regularly inspected by any person authorised in writing in this regard by the Ministry and shall be maintained in good order and capable of making first grade copra;

4. The licensee shall prominently display on a board outside his or her premises the current prices for units of coconuts as may from time to time be determined by the Ministry;

5. The licensee shall, if a co-operative society, buy from non-members nearest to its drier if so requested up to the full capacity of its drier, and if a professional copra maker, buy from farmers nearer to, in preference to farmers farther from his or her drier;

6. The licensee shall take reasonable steps to satisfy himself or herself that the coconuts offered for sale belong to a coconut farmer entitled to sell them;

7. The licensee shall not purchase at any one time less than one unit of coconuts;

8. The licensee shall comply with the provisions of the Coconut Industry Development Act 2010 and of all regulations made thereunder, and with all lawful orders of the Ministry;

#### FORM 2 COCONUT OIL MAKER’S LICENCE

................ of ................ (hereinafter referred to as “the licensee”) is hereby licensed under the provisions of the Coconut Industry Development Act 2010, to purchase copra for making coconut oil and to make coconut oil, subject to the following conditions—

1. This licence shall remain in force until cancelled or suspended by the Ministry or surrendered by the licensee;

2. The licensee shall pay for all copra bought by him or her at such price as may from time to time be fixed by the Ministry;

3. The licensee shall give the Ministry at least 3 months’ prior notice when proposing to cease making coconut oil or substantially to reduce the quantity of coconut oil to be made by him or her;

4. The licensee shall be required to sell coconut oil in Fiji in preference to exporting it when so directed by the Ministry;

5. The licensee shall be required to buy copra up to the full capacity of his or her mill if the Ministry deems fit and if the Ministry guarantees to provide the licensee with copra up to 75% of the capacity of his or her mill;

#### FORM 3 COPRA EXPORTER’S LICENCE

................ of ................ (hereinafter referred to as “the licensee”) is hereby licensed under the provisions of then Coconut Industry Development Act 2010, to purchase copra for export and to export copra, subject to the following conditions—

1. This licence shall remain in force until cancelled or suspended by the Ministry or surrendered by the licensee;

2. The licensee shall pay to every copra maker from whom he or she purchases copra under the authority of this licence, the current price of copra as may be fixed by the Ministry from time to time;

3. The licensee shall comply with the provisions of the Coconut Industry Development Act 2010 and of all regulations made thereunder and with all lawful orders of the Ministry;

4. The total quantity of copra exported by the licensee under the authority of this licence in any year shall not exceed such tonnage as may be specified by the Ministry in writing for that year;

5. No copra shall be exported by the licensee from the ports of Suva or Lautoka without the permission in writing of the Ministry;

6. No copra may be purchased under the authority of this licence for any purpose other than for export and no copra purchased under the authority of this licence may be disposed of by the licensee in any manner other than by export except with the permission in writing of the Ministry;

7. No copra may be purchased under the authority of this licence at any place other than a grading station as may from time to time be specified in writing by the Ministry or in any grades or quantities other than as may from time to time be specified by the Ministry;

8. The licensee shall comply with the provisions of the iTaukei Development Fund Act 1965;
