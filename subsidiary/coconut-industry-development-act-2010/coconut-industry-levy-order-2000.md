## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Coconut Industry (Levy) Order 2000.

## O 2 Commencement

(1) With effect from 17 March 2000, a levy of $6 per metric tonne is payable on all coconut products traded in Fiji.

(2) The levy of $6 is payable monthly to the Authority.

(3) For the purposes of this Order, 6,000 coconuts are equivalent to one tonne of copra.

(4) This Order replaces any previous Order imposing a levy on the trading of coconut products.
