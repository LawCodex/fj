## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Coconut Industry (Crude Coconut Oil Makers Exemption) Regulations 1966.

## reg 2 Exemption

Persons making crude coconut oil for customary domestic purposes, or for sale, in quantities not exceeding 45 litres in any calendar month, shall be exempt from the provisions of the Act or of any regulations made thereunder, relating to coconut oil makers.
