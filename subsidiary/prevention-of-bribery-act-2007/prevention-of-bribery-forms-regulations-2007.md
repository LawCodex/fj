## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Prevention of Bribery (Forms) Regulations 2007.

## reg 2 Forms

The forms set out in the Schedule are prescribed for use in connection with the Prevention of Bribery Act 2007.

## 

### SCHEDULE FORMS

#### PREVENTION OF BRIBERY ACT 2007

##### FORM 1 EX-PARTE APPLICATION FOR THE ISSUE OF A SEARCH WARRANT

In the Magistrates Court at Fiji.

APPLICATION

Made by (name and description of applicant) of (address of applicant), an investigating officer for the purposes of the Prevention of Bribery Act 2007 (the Act),

Who states that he or she believes that in (description of premises or place) at (address of premises or place) of (name of owner, occupier or person in charge of premises or place, if known) (“the specified premises or place”) there are (description of things) which are or which contain evidence of (description of offence), being an offence under the Act,

For an order under section 17(1A) of the Act for a warrant directed to (name and description of investigating officer to be authorised) of (address of investigating officer to be authorised), an investigating officer for the purposes of the Act, empowering him or her and (particulars of any other investigating officer for the purposes of the Act to be authorised) to enter the specified premises or place, by force if necessary, and search the same.

Dated this day of 20

(Signature of Applicant)

##### FORM 2 SEARCH WARRANT

In the Magistrates Court at Fiji.

WHEREAS on an application made to me this day by (name and description of applicant) of (address of applicant), an investigating officer for the purposes of the Prevention of Bribery Act 2007 (the Act),

I am satisfied that there is reasonable cause to believe that in (description of premises or place) at (address of premises or place) of (name of owner, occupier or person in charge of premises or place, if known) (“the specified premises or place”) there are (description of things) which are or which contain evidence of (description of offence), being an offence under the Act,

I HEREBY BY WARRANT empower (name and description of investigating officer to be authorised) of (address of investigating officer to be authorised), an investigating officer for the purposes of the Act, and (particulars of any other investigating officer for the purposes of the Act to be authorised) to enter the specified premises or place, by force if necessary, and search the same.

Dated this day of 20

(Magistrate)

(Signature of Magistrate)
