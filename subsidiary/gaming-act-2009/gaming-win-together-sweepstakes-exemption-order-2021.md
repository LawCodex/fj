## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming (Win Together Sweepstakes) Exemption Order 2021.

(2) This Order comes into force on 28 August 2021.

## O 2 Exemption

Pursuant to sections 3(1) and 4(b) of the Gaming Act 2009, the Win Together Sweepstakes coordinated by the ministries of communications and civil service as a lottery for Fijians fully vaccinated against COVID-19 is exempted from the provisions of the Gaming Act 2009.
