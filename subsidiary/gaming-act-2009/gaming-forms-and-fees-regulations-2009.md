## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Gaming (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2009.

(2) These Regulations are deemed to have come into force on 1 December 2009.

## reg 2 Application for a gaming licence

(1) An applicant for a gaming licence for Class 1 must fill the approved application form as set out in Schedule 1 Form 1, and pay the appropriate prescribed fee mentioned in Schedule 2.

(2) An applicant for a gaming licence for Class 2 must fill the approved application form as mentioned in Schedule 1 Form 2, and pay the appropriate prescribed fee mentioned in Schedule 2.

(3) Any person applying for a gaming licence must submit, wherever applicable, the details of any other information or document as the licensing authority may require in writing.

## reg 3 Issue of gaming licence by licensing authority

(1) The licensing authority before issuing a gaming licence must be satisfied that the applicant meets the prerequisite and conditions set out in the Act.

(2) The licensing authority must issue a gaming licence in accordance with the form set out in Schedule 1 Form 3.

(3) The licensing authority may annex to the gaming licence issued, special terms and conditions pertaining to each licensee’s operation.

## reg 4 Waiver or variation of gaming licence fees

The Attorney-General may waive or vary the gaming licence fee mentioned in Schedule 2, but such decision must be based on reasonable grounds.

| | (b) | 3% of total value of prize where it is a lottery. |
