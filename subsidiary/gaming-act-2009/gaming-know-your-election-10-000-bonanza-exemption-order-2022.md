## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming (Know Your Election $10,000 Bonanza) Exemption Order 2022.

(2) This Order comes into force on 29 July 2022.

## O 2 Exemption

Pursuant to sections 3(1) and 4(b) of the Gaming Act 2009, the Know Your Election $10,000 Bonanza coordinated by the Fijian Elections Office as a lottery for Fijians who upgrade their voter card or register for a voter card during the Fijian Elections Office’s Know Your Election drive to be held from 30 July 2022 to 3 September 2022 is exempted from the provisions of the Gaming Act 2009.
