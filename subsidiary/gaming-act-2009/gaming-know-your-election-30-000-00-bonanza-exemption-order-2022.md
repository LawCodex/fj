## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming (Know Your Election $30,000.00 Bonanza) Exemption Order 2022.

(2) This Order comes into force on 24 February 2022.

## O 2 Exemption

Pursuant to sections 3(1) and 4(b) of the Gaming Act 2009, the Know Your Election $30,000.00 Bonanza coordinated by the Fijian Elections Office as a lottery for Fijians who upgrade their voter card or register for a voter card during the Fijian Elections Office’s Know Your Election drive to be held from 25 February 2022 to 14 April 2022 is exempted from the provisions of the Gaming Act 2009.
