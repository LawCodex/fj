## reg 1 Short title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Surveyors (Code of Ethics) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 1 January 2008.

## reg 2 Code of Ethics

For the purpose of the Act, the Code of Ethics for surveyors is set out in the Schedule.

## reg 3 Enforcement

A breach of a provision of the Code of Ethics may be a ground for unethical conduct for the purposes of section 9(1)(b) of the Act.

## SCHEDULE CODE OF ETHICS FOR SURVEYORS

### CL 1

Without limiting the ordinary meaning of the expression “unethical conduct” it shall be considered unethical conduct for any Registered Surveyor to act in any manner contrary to the letter or spirit of any paragraph in the following “Code of Ethics”—

1. The surveyor shall carry out his or her professional work in a spirit of fairness to employees, fidelity to clients and employers and devotion to high ideals of courtesy, personal honour and civil service.

2. The surveyor shall not associate himself or herself or allow his or her name to be associated with any enterprise derogatory to the profession of surveying.

3. The surveyor shall treat as strictly confidential all information imparted to him or her as such, concerning the business affairs and technical methods or processes of a client or employer.

4. The surveyor shall inform his or her client or employer of all such business connections, interests or circumstances as could be deemed likely to affect his or her judgment or the quality of professional service he or she is about to render or is in the process of rending.

5. The surveyor shall not accept any financial or other compensation for a particular service from more than one source except with the full knowledge of all interested parties.

6. The surveyor shall not review or undertake to complete the work of another surveyor for the client or employer, except with the full knowledge of such surveyor or unless he or she has ascertained that the connection of such surveyor with the work has terminated satisfactorily.

7. The surveyor shall not advertise in any way whatsoever, the fact that he or she is a surveyor or carries out surveying, save that the following shall be permissible, namely—

(a) a member may display plates or signs at his or her place of business which exhibit his or her name and profession, but in no circumstances shall be characters in such plates, signs or on window displays exceed 20 cm in height;

(b) to advertise, not more than 10 times in each of the daily papers within a period not exceeding 3 months, the commencement of practice in any new or the changing of his or her place of business or the entering into or dissolving of partnership. The advertisement shall occupy the size of a normal business card;

(c) a member may permit, arrange or request his or her name or his or her firm's name to appear as the surveyor of firm of surveyors responsible for the design and survey of any subdivision of any land on any notices or brochures distributed or signs erected by the subdividing owner, whether or not such notice is for the purpose of advertising the sale of the sections comprised in the subdivision, provided that such display of his or her name or his or her firm's name is not used in any way other than as an acknowledgment of his or her professional skill and is not shown in characters exceeding 30 cm in height;

(d) to permit, arrange or request his or her name or the name of his or her firm to be listed in any telephone, business or trade directory and to pay all on any charges thereof at the current ruling rate. Bold type listing may be used in the alphabetical section of the telephone directory, but in any occupational classification uniform small type only may be used;

(e) a member may supply a brochure, on the request of a client. Brochures shall be confined to a factual description of the qualifications of himself or herself and his or her partners or associates, their fields of practice and illustrations of appropriate work or projects already carried out;

(f) branches of the Fiji Institute of Surveyors and other groups approved by the Board may undertake any form of corporate marketing to promote the interest of the profession, including participation in agriculture and other shows, seminars, provision of material to the news media as paid advertisement, and other forms of publicity;

(g) a member may display on the front doors of any vehicle used for surveying, the name of the firm, provided that all wordings must be in capital letters not exceeding 2.5 cm in height. Inclusion of the term Registered Surveyor is not permitted but the word Surveyors together with any of the adjectives-land, hydrographic, aerial, engineering or mining may be used.

8. The surveyor shall not attempt to obtain employment, professional engagement or advancement either by competitive underbidding or by adverse criticism of other corporate or non-corporate member or by any other improper method.

9. The surveyor shall not charge or accept or agree or offer to charge or accept for work carried out or to be carried out by him or her as a surveyor, any remuneration other than that fixed by the Scale of Fees adopted by the Institute and approved by the Minister responsible for lands and shall not give commission to any person interested in the work undertaken by him or her.

10. No member shall carry on business other than—

(a) in his or her own name;

(b) under a firm name which shall consist of the surnames of one or more of the present partners or their predecessors and which may include in addition the Christian names or initials of the person or persons named and such one of the following terms as shall appropriately reflect the composition of the business “and Partner/s”, “and Associate/s”, “and Company”;

(c) as a director or shareholder of a limited liability company the name of which shall consist of the surname of one or more of the present directors or majority shareholders of their predecessors and which may include in addition the Christian names or initials of the person or persons named and such one of the terms set out in (b) above as shall appropriately reflect the composition of the business;

(d) under any other name which in special circumstances the Board may approve;

(e) copies of the Articles of Association of a proposed Company shall be submitted prior to incorporation to the Board.
