## reg 1 Short title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Surveyors (Fees and Charges) Regulations 2007, and come into force on 1 January 2008.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**general works** includes cadastral surveys; and

**principal** means a registered surveyor who is the sole proprietor or a director of a survey entity.

## reg 10 Method of payment

(1) The surveyor must provide the client with monthly accounts for work done.

(2) Any accounts provided under subregulation (1) shall be paid within the 30 days after the date the accounts were provided by the surveyor to the clients.

## reg 11 Records of work

The surveyor shall keep and maintain records of actual survey work done including consultations, travelling time, staff time and other matters.
