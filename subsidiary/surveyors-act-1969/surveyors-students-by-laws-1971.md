# SURVEYORS (STUDENTS) BY-LAWS 1971

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Surveyors (Students) By-laws 1971.

## by-law 2 Students to be registered

(1) Any person wishing to become qualified as a surveyor under the provisions of the Act shall apply, on a form to be obtained from the Registrar, to be registered as a student with the Board.

(2) No person shall be registered as a student, unless he or she satisfies the Board—

(a) that he or she is fully employed in the practice of surveying under a registered surveyor or surveying agency or Government department employing registered surveyors; and

(b) that he or she has such minimum educational qualifications as may, from time to time, be notified by the Board.

## by-law 3 Syllabus to be issued

The Board shall, from time to time, issue a syllabus stating the subjects of examinations to be taken by candidates, the order in which such subjects shall be taken, the minimum marks to be obtained to achieve a pass in such examinations and the manner of application to sit for such examinations.

## by-law 4 Examination fees

A fee of $3 for each examination subject for which the candidate wishes to sit shall be paid by the candidate to the Board.

[s 4 am LN 199 of 1977, effective 1 January 1978]

## by-law 5 Re-registration required in certain cases

Any person registered with the Board as a student who fails, within 9 years, to pass the examinations set by the Board shall, if he or she wishes to remain a student, apply to the Board for re-registration as a student and the Board may, in its discretion, either refuse or permit such re-registration and, in the event of permitting re-registration, may impose such conditions as it deems advisable.

## by-law 6 Reports of experience

(1) Each person registered with the Board as a student shall, on or before 31 January in each year, make a report to the Board, in a form to be obtained from the Registrar, as to the type of work upon which he or she has been engaged during the previous calendar year.

(2) Two weeks before a student takes the final examination, he or she shall submit to the Secretary of the Board a written report giving a summary of his or her job experience since his or her registration as a student.

(3) If the Board is of the opinion that any student has had insufficient training and experience as shown in the report referred to in paragraph (2), it may direct that such student shall undertake further training to be specified by the Board or obtain further experience.

(4) For the purpose of paragraph (3), a period of training of less than 5 years and a proportion of less than 60% of the time occupied during training on the performance of field work shall be regarded as insufficient.

## by-law 7 Students registered before 1 January 1971

The Board shall consider the case of each person registered as a student prior to 1 January 1971, and shall advise him or her of the time he or she will be required to remain as a student and of the subjects in which he or she will be required to pass examinations.
