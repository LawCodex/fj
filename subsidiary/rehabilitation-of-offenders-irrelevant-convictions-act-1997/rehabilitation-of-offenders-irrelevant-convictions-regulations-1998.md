# REHABILITATION OF OFFENDERS (IRRELEVANT CONVICTIONS) REGULATIONS 1998

Rehabilitation of Offenders (Irrelevant Convictions) Regulations 1998 (LN 82 of 1998) commenced on 1 July 1998, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Rehabilitation of Offenders (Irrelevant Convictions) Regulations 1998.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on 1 July 1998.

## reg 2 Application for a Certificate of Rehabilitation

(1) A person who makes an application under section 27(1) of the Act shall provide all the particulars relevant to his or her conviction for which the rehabilitation period has expired to the Commissioner of Police.

(2) Notwithstanding subregulation (1) above, the Commissioner of Police may, for the purpose of satisfying himself that the applicant is entitled to be issued with a Certificate, require the applicant to supply to the Commissioner, if not already provided by the applicant or if already provided, for the purpose of further verification, details as specified in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## reg 3 Application fee

A person who makes an application under section 27(1) of the Act shall pay to the Commissioner of Police a fee of $20 (exclusive of VAT) if the applicant resides in Fiji or $30 (exclusive of VAT) if the applicant resides outside of Fiji.
