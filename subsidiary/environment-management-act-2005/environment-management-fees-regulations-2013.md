## reg 1 Short title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Environment Management (Fees) Regulations 2013, and shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Gazette.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Registrar** means the Registrar of the Tribunal appointed by the Chief Justice under regulation 3;

**Registry** means the Registry of the Tribunal; and

**Tribunal** means the Environmental Tribunal established under section 56 of the Environment Management Act 2005.

## reg 3 Registrar of the Tribunal

The Chief Justice shall appoint the Registrar of the Tribunal.

## reg 4 Fees

The fees for proceedings before the Tribunal are prescribed in the Schedule and shall be payable at the Registry.
