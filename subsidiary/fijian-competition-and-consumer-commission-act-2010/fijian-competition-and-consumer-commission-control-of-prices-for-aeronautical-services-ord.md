# FIJIAN COMPETITION AND CONSUMER COMMISSION (CONTROL OF PRICES FOR AERONAUTICAL SERVICES) ORDER 2022

Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for Aeronautical Services) Order 2022 (LN 78 of 2022) commenced on 23 June 2022, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for Aeronautical Services) Order 2022.

(2) This Order comes into force on 23 June 2022.

## O 2 Definitions

For the purposes of this Order, “aeronautical services” includes the following—

(a) air navigational aided services;

(b) air traffic control services;

(c) landing and parking services for aircraft;

(d) air side activities like incinerator services and aerobridge services; and

(e) other services relating to aircraft movements.

## O 3 Control of prices for the supply of aeronautical services

For the purpose of section 39 of the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission Act 2010, the prices for the supply of aeronautical services in all grades or classes are controlled.

## O 4 Expiration of Order

This Order expires at the start of 23 June 2025 unless it is varied, extended or revoked.
