# FIJIAN COMPETITION AND CONSUMER COMMISSION (CONTROL OF PRICES FOR GHEE PRODUCTS) (EX-FACTORY) ORDER 2024

Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for Ghee Products) (Ex-factory) Order 2024 (LN 93 of 2024) commenced on 25 October 2024, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for Ghee Products) (Ex-factory) Order 2024.

(2) This Order is deemed to have come into force on 25 October 2024.

## O 2 Interpretation

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Ghee** means a semifluid clarified butter; and

**Ghee products** means Ghee as a commercial product sold in the Fijian market.

## O 3 Control of prices for the ex-factory supply of Ghee products

For the purposes of section 39 of the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission Act 2010, the prices for the ex-factory supply of Ghee products in all quantities, qualities, grades and classes within Fiji are controlled.

## O 4 Authorisation

The prices pertaining to the ex-factory supply of Ghee products are authorised by the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission pursuant to section 41 of the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission 2010.

## O 5 Expiration of Order

This Order expires at the start of 25 October 2027 unless it is varied, extended or revoked.
