# FIJIAN COMPETITION AND CONSUMER COMMISSION (CONTROL OF PRICES FOR MECHANICAL HARVESTING SERVICES FOR SUGAR CANE) ORDER 2022

Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for Mechanical Harvesting Services for Sugar Cane) Order 2022 (LN 90 of 2022) commenced on 1 August 2022, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for Mechanical Harvesting Services for Sugar Cane) Order 2022.

(2) This Order comes into force on 1 August 2022.

## O 2 Control of prices for the supply of mechanical harvesting services for sugar cane

For the purpose of section 39 of the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission Act 2010, the prices for the supply of mechanical harvesting services for sugar cane in all quantities, qualities and grades or classes are controlled.

## O 3 Expiration of the Order

This Order expires at the start of 1 August 2025 unless it is varied, extended or revoked.
