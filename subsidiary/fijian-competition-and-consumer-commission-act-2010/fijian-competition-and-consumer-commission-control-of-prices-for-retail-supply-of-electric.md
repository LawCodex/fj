# FIJIAN COMPETITION AND CONSUMER COMMISSION (CONTROL OF PRICES FOR RETAIL SUPPLY OF ELECTRICITY AND ANCILLARY SERVICES) ORDER 2022

Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for Retail Supply of Electricity and Ancillary Services) Order 2022 (LN 75 of 2022) commenced on 25 May 2022, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for Retail Supply of Electricity and Ancillary Services) Order 2022.

(2) This Order comes into force on 25 May 2022.

## O 2 Control of prices for the retail supply of electricity

For the purpose of section 39 of the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission Act 2010, the prices for the retail supply of electricity and services pertaining to the retail supply of electricity in all quantities, qualities and grades or classes are controlled.

## O 3 Expiration of Order

This Order expires at the start of 25 May 2025 unless it is varied, extended or revoked.
