# FIJIAN COMPETITION AND CONSUMER COMMISSION (REQUISITION FOR INFORMATION) REGULATIONS 2017

Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Requisition for Information) Regulations 2017 (LN 102 of 2017) commenced on 8 December 2017, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Requisition for Information) Regulations 2017.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the date of publication in the Gazette.

(3) In these Regulations, the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission Act 2010 is referred to as the “Act”.

## reg 2 Requisition for Information Notice

(1) In accordance with section 119 of the Act, the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission may issue a Requisition for Information Notice to a person to obtain information from that person for the purposes of the Act.

(2) The Requisition for Information Notice is set out in the Schedule.
