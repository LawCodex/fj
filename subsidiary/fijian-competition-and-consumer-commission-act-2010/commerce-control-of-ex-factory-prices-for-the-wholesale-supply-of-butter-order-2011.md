# COMMERCE (CONTROL OF EX-FACTORY PRICES FOR THE WHOLESALE SUPPLY OF BUTTER) ORDER 2011

Commerce (Control of Ex-Factory Prices for the Wholesale Supply of Butter) Order 2011 (LN 95 of 2011) commenced on 26 September 2011, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Commerce (Control of Ex-Factory Prices for the Wholesale Supply of Butter) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 26 September 2011.

## O 2 Revocation

The Commerce (Control of Ex-Factory Prices for the Wholesale Supply of Butter) Order 2010 (LN 115 of 2010) is hereby revoked.
