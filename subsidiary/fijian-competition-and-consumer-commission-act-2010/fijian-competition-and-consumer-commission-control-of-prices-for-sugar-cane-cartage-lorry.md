# FIJIAN COMPETITION AND CONSUMER COMMISSION (CONTROL OF PRICES FOR SUGAR CANE CARTAGE LORRY SERVICES FROM RAKIRAKI TO RARAWAI) ORDER 2022

Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for Sugar Cane Cartage Lorry Services from Rakiraki to Rarawai) Order 2022 (LN 79 of 2022) commenced on 16 June 2022, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for Sugar Cane Cartage Lorry Services from Rakiraki to Rarawai) Order 2022.

(2) This Order comes into force on 16 June 2022.

## O 2 Control of prices for sugar cane cartage lorry services

(1) For the purpose of section 39 of the Act, the prices charged for the cartage of sugar cane in a lorry from Rakiraki to Rarawai are controlled.

(2) The Commission must authorise the prices charged for the cartage of sugar cane in a lorry from Rakiraki to Rarawai pursuant to section 41 of the Act.

(3) Pursuant to section 126(1) of the Act, an officer of the Commission may at all reasonable times enter any premises to obtain any information for the purposes of this Order.

## O 3 Expiration of Order

This Order expires at the start of 16 June 2025 unless it is varied, extended or revoked.
