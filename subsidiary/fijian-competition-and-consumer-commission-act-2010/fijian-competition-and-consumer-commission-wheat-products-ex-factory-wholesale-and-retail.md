# FIJIAN COMPETITION AND CONSUMER COMMISSION (WHEAT PRODUCTS) (EX-FACTORY, WHOLESALE AND RETAIL) ORDER 2022

Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Wheat Products) (Ex-factory, Wholesale and Retail) Order 2025 (LN 11 of 2025) commenced on 28 February 2025, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Wheat Products) (Ex-factory, Wholesale and Retail) Order 2025.

(2) This Order comes into force on 28 February 2025.

## O 2 Control of prices for the supply of wheat products

For the purposes of section 39 of the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission Act 2010, the prices for the ex-factory, wholesale and retail supply of wheat products in all qualities, quantities, grades and classes in Fiji are controlled.

## O 3 Authorisation

The prices pertaining to the factory, wholesale and retail supply of wheat products are authorised by the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission pursuant to section 41 of the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission Act 2010.

## O 4 Revocation

This Order expires on 28 February 2028 unless it is varied, extended or revoked.

## O 5 Expiration of the Order

This Order expires at the start of 28 February 2025 unless it is varied, extended or revoked.
