# FIJIAN COMPETITION AND CONSUMER COMMISSION (ON-SPOT PENALTIES) REGULATIONS 2011

Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (On-Spot Penalties) Regulations 2011 (LN 32 of 2011) commenced on 1 July 2010, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |
| Commerce Commission (Budget Amendment) Act 2017 (No 41 of 2017) | 1 August 2017 |

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (On-Spot Penalties) Regulations 2011 and are deemed to have come into effect on the commencement date of the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission Act 2010.

[reg 1 am Act 41 of 2017 s 25, 1 August 2017]

## reg 2 Repeal

The Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (On-Spot Penalties) Regulations 2010 is hereby repealed.

[reg 2 am Act 41 of 2017 s 25, 1 August 2017]

## reg 3 Form

For the purposes of section 59(1) of the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission Act 2010, the prescribed On-Spot Penalties Form is set out in the Schedule.

[reg 3 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 40, 1 December 2016 Act 41 of 2017 s 25, 1 August 2017]
