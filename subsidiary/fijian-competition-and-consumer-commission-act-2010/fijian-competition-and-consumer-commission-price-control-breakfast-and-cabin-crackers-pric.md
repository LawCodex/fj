# FIJIAN COMPETITION AND CONSUMER COMMISSION (PRICE CONTROL) (BREAKFAST AND CABIN CRACKERS PRICES) ORDER 2024

Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Price Control) (Breakfast and Cabin Crackers Prices) Order 2024 (LN 75 of 2024) commenced on 30 August 2024, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Price Control) (Breakfast and Cabin Crackers Prices) Order 2024.

(2) This Order comes into force on the date of publication in the Gazette.

## O 2 Authorisation

The retail or wholesale prices pertaining to the supply of breakfast and cabin crackers products are to be authorised by the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission pursuant to section 41 of the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission Act 2010.

## O 3 Expiration of Order

This Order expires on 5 September 2029 unless the Order is varied, extended or earlier revoked.

## O 4 Revocation

The Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Price Control) (Breakfast and Cabin Crackers Prices) Order 2019 is revoked.
