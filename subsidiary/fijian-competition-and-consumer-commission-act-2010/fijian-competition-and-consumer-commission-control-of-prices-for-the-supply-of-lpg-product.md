# FIJIAN COMPETITION AND CONSUMER COMMISSION (CONTROL OF PRICES FOR THE SUPPLY OF LPG PRODUCTS FROM BUTANE GAS IN FIJI) ORDER 2024

Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for the Supply of LPG Products from Butane Gas in Fiji) Order 2024 (LN 38 of 2024) commenced on 12 July 2024, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for the Supply of LPG Products from Butane Gas in Fiji) Order 2024.

(2) This Order comes into force on 12 July 2024.

## O 2 Control of Prices for the Supply of LPG Products from Butane Gas in Fiji

For the purpose of section 39 of the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission Act 2010, the prices for the supply of LPG products from Butane Gas in all quantities, qualities, grades or classes in Fiji are controlled.

## O 3 Expiration of Order

This Order expires at the start of 12 July 2027 unless it is varied, extended or revoked.
