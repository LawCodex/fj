# FIJIAN COMPETITION AND CONSUMER COMMISSION (CONTROL OF PRICES FOR REINFORCEMENT STEEL RODS) ORDER 2024

Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for Reinforcement Steel Rods) Order 2024 (LN 98 of 2024) commenced on 14 November 2024, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for Reinforcement Steel Rods) Order 2024.

(2) This Order is deemed to have come into force on 14 November 2024.

## O 2 Interpretation

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires, market means the sale and purchase of reinforcement steel rods through wholesale in Fiji.

## O 3 Control of prices for steel rods

For the purpose of section 39 of the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission Act 2010, the prices for reinforcement steel rods, in all qualities and classes, that are sold in the market including the following grades are controlled—

(a) grade 300 deformed;

(b) grade 300 plain;

(c) grade 430 deformed;

(d) grade 430 plain;

(e) grade 500 deformed; and

(f) grade 500 plain.

## O 4 Expiration of Order

This Order expires on 14 November 2027 unless it is varied, extended or revoked.
