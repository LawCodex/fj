# FIJIAN COMPETITION AND CONSUMER COMMISSION (CONTROL OF PRICES FOR THE SUPPLY OF CEMENT PRODUCTS) ORDER 2024

Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for the Supply of Cement Products) Order 2024 (LN 30 of 2024) commenced on 22 June 2024, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for the Supply of Cement Products) Order 2024.

(2) This Order comes into force on 22 June 2024.

## O 2 Control of prices for the supply of cement products

For the purpose of section 39 of the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission Act 2010, the prices for the ex-factory, wholesale and retail supply of portland cement and blended cement in all qualities, quantities, grades and classes in Fiji are controlled.

## O 3 Expiration of Order

This Order expires at the start of 22 June 2027 unless it is varied, extended or revoked.
