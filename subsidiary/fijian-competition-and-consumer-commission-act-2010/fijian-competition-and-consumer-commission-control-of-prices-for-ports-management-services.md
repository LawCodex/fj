# FIJIAN COMPETITION AND CONSUMER COMMISSION (CONTROL OF PRICES FOR PORTS MANAGEMENT SERVICES) ORDER 2024

Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for Ports Management Services) Order 2024 (LN 15 of 2024) commenced on 29 March 2024, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission (Control of Prices for Ports Management Services) Order 2024.

(2) This Order comes into force on 29 March 2024.

## O 2 Interpretation

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires—

**ports management services** means all services and facilities provided by a port, including—

(a) marine service charges, wharfage, pilotage, dockage, container stevedoring, storage, anchorage, cargo or container handling and machine hiring charges, mooring, cleaning of wharf, fumigation, provision of water or electricity services, reefer charges or commissions;

(b) other services relating to container and ship movement; and

(c) facilities, including shops, office facilities and car parks.

## O 3 Price control

For the purpose of section 39 of the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission Act 2010, the prices or rates for the supply of all ports management services in Fiji are controlled.

## O 4 Expiration of Order

This Order expires on 29 March 2027 unless the Order is varied, extended or revoked.
