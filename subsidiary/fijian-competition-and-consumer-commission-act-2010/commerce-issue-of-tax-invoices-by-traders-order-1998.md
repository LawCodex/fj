# COMMERCE (ISSUE OF TAX INVOICES BY TRADERS) ORDER 1998

Commerce (Issue of Tax Invoices by Traders) Order 1998 (LN 93 of 1998) commenced on 10 July 1998, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Commerce (Issue of Tax Invoices by Traders) Order 1998 and comes into force on 10 July 1998.

[O 1 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 50, 1 December 2016]

## O 2 Interpretation

In this Order—

**service** means anything which is not goods or money and includes—

(a) any service supplied or carried on for hire or reward by any person engaged in trade or business;

(b) any rights or privileges for which remuneration is payable in the form of royalty or tribute; and

(c) any rights under an agreement for the provision of board or lodging; and

**trader** means any person who in connection with any business carried on by him or her sells, has sold, or proposes to sell any goods, or who supplies or carries on any service.

## O 3 Traders to issue tax invoices

Any registered trader under the Value Added Tax Act 1991 who sells by wholesale or retail any goods or charges for a service supplied by him or her except those exempted by the Value Added Tax Act 1991 which exceeds $10 must issue a tax invoice in accordance with the Value Added Tax Regulations 1991 .

[O 3 am am LN 99 of 2016 reg 50, 1 December 2016]

## O 4 Value Added Tax (VAT)

(1) Any trader mentioned in order 3 who sells by wholesale must state Value Added Tax (VAT) separately in the invoice at the time of sale;

(2) Any trader mentioned in order 3 who sells by retail, or charges for services, must give a tax invoice inclusive of VAT at the time of sale or service provided.

## O 5 Records

(1) Any trader mentioned in order 3 selling goods or supplying services must keep, to the satisfaction of the Commision, the following records—

(a) a register to record all tax invoices printed in numerical order;

(b) the name of the printer;

(c) a record of cash registers and computers in use daily; and

(d) a record of daily sales—

(i) not exceeding $10; and

(ii) exceeding $10.

(2) Tax invoices printed by cash registers and computers must be numbered in numerical order.

(3) Tax invoices, whether used or unused, should be kept for inspection purposes.

[O 5 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 50, 1 December 2016]
