# MARITIME (SHIP REGISTRATION) REGULATIONS 2014

Maritime (Ship Registration) Regulations 2014 (LN 103 of 2014) commenced on 1 January 2015, as amended by:

| --- | --- |

## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [regs 1-3]

### reg 1 Short title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Maritime (Ship Registration) Regulations 2014 and shall come into force on a date appointed by the Minister in the Gazette.

### reg 2 Purpose

These Regulations prescribe the requirements that must be fulfilled in order to register ships in Fiji and which entitles the ship to fly the Fiji flag.

### reg 3 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Act** means the Ship Registration Act 2013 ;

**Fijian national** means—

(a) a Fijian citizen; or

(b) a body corporate established by or under the law of Fiji;

**official number** means the registration or registry number issued to the ship during registration;

**qualified person** means a ship’s agent appointed in accordance with section 15 of the Act;

**surveyor** shall have the same meaning as defined under the Act; and

**unlimited voyage** means a voyage, trade or operation of a ship from any port or place in Fiji beyond the Exclusive Economic Zone of Fiji to another port or place outside Fiji or conversely.

## PART 2 TONNAGE OF SHIPS [regs 4-5]

### reg 4 Tonnage measurement

(1) The tonnage of a ship that is to be registered shall be determined or recognised in accordance with the maritime regulations and the International Convention on Tonnage Measurement of Ships 1969 as amended.

(2) The owner or master of a ship that is to be measured shall—

(a) fill in the application form prescribed in the maritime regulations and pay the prescribed fees;

(b) make the ship available for measurement by a Surveyor; and

(c) provide all necessary facilities for its inspection and measurement by the surveyor.

(3) The owner or master of a ship shall produce any plans, drawings, specifications and other documents the surveyor requires for the surveyor’s use and retention.

(4) Any person who contravenes subregulation (3) commits an infringement offence and shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $500.

### reg 5 Tonnage certificates

(1) In the case of a ship to which the Tonnage Measurement Convention applies, a tonnage certificate shall be in the Form TM. 1 which complies with Annex II of the Tonnage Convention.

(2) In any other case a tonnage certificate shall be in Form TM. 2 which complies with the Maritime (Fiji Maritime Code) Regulations 2014 .

### reg 6 Marking of ship

(1) Except as otherwise provided by subregulation (7) , a ship shall be marked on—

(a) each side of its bow with its name;

(b) its stern with its name with the word “SUVA” below its name;

(c) each side of its stem and on its stern by a scale of decimetres or of metres and decimetres denoting its draught; and

(d) its main beam with its official number and net tonnage.

(2) The marking referred to in subregulations (1)(a) and (b) shall be in—

(a) white or yellow letters on a dark background; or

(b) black letters on a light background,

being letters of a length of 10 centimetres or more and of proportionate breadth.

(3) The marking referred to in subregulation (1)(c) shall be in—

(a) white or yellow figures on a dark background; or

(b) black figures on a light bvvackground, being figures that are—

(i) of a length of 10 centimetres and of proportionate breadth;

(ii) if the scale is in decimetres, at each interval of 2 decimetres; and

(iii) if the scale is in metres and decimetres, at each metre interval and at each intervening 2 decimetres interval.

(4) In the case of a figure denoting the scale of draught of a ship—

(a) the capital letter “M” shall be placed after each metre;

(b) the top most figure of the scale shall show the metres and except where that figure marks a full metre interval, the decimetres denoted by the figure; and

(c) the lower line of each figure shall coincide with the draught line denoted by each figure.

(5) The marking referred to in subregulation (1) for the following ships shall be—

(a) pre-cut components welded on to the hull or by cutting in or centre punching, for steel and aluminium ships;

(b) cutting in to a depth of 3mm into the wooden planking for wooden ships; and

(c) pre-cut component fixed on the ship with structural glue for fibre reinforced Plastic ships.

(6) Ships of 15 metres or less in registered length shall be marked with the number of passengers it is certified to carry on its port and starboard side and as prescribed in its survey certificate.

## PART 4 REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS [regs 7-15]

### reg 7 Ships to be registered

(1) Prior to registration of Fiji Ships engaged in voyage on coastal waters, the following statutory certificates or ship documentation shall be provided to the Registrar—

(a) Survey Certificate;

(b) Safe Manning Certificate;

(c) Radio Certificate;

(d) Fumigation (Sanitary) Certificate;

(e) Safe Ship Management Certificate;

(f) Life Raft Certificate;

(g) Fire Fighting Appliance Certificate; and

(h) Compass Deviation Card.

(2) Prior to registration of Fiji Ships engaged in international voyage, the certificates or ship’s documentation referred to in Part 4 of the Schedule depending on the type of ship shall be provided to the Registrar.

(3) Any person who contravenes subregulation (1) commits an infringement offence and is liable to a fine not exceeding $1,000.

(4) All Fiji Ships of 15 metres and less in length shall be registered in Part B of the Register.

(5) Any person who contravenes subregulation (3) commits an infringement offence and shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $500.

### reg 8 Registration of Fiji Government ships

An application for registration of a Fiji Government ship shall be made to the Registrar by the respective head of that Department or Agency or by a person who holds the ship on behalf of, or for the benefit of, the Government of the Republic of Fiji in accordance with Part 2 of the Act.

### reg 9 Application for registration

(1) An application for registration of a ship by the owner of the ship shall be made through Form REG 1A and shall be accompanied by the documents specified under section 5(2) of the Act.

(2) An application for registration of a ship by the ship’s agent on behalf of the owner of the ship, shall be made through Form REG 1B .

(3) The following documents shall also be provided in addition to the documents referred to under subregulation (1) —

(a) if the ship has been registered under a law of a country other than Fiji, evidence from the Flag State administration that registration in that country has ceased in the form of a deregistration certificate;

(b) evidence that the ship is in a seaworthy condition;

(c) evidence that the applicant is the owner of the ship or the ship’s agent of the owner, as the case may be;

(d) a list of not less than 3 alternative names, on Form REG 6 , numbered in order of reference, under which the ship is sought to be registered; and

(e) a declaration of ownership.

(4) Where any certificates referred to under this regulation cannot be obtained, the applicant shall—

(a) make a statutory declaration to that effect; and

(b) attach the statutory declaration to the application.

### reg 10 Declaration of ownership

A declaration of ownership of a registered ship shall be in accordance with Form REG 2 .

### reg 11 Registration of ships

(1) If the Registrar is satisfied that all the requirements stipulated in Part 2 of the Act and these Regulations relating to the registration of a ship have been complied with, the Registrar shall—

(a) allot an official number to the ship; and

(b) enter in the Register—

(i) the number allotted under paragraph (a) ;

(ii) the name approved by the Registrar for the ship;

(iii) such particulars of the construction or origin of the ship as appear to the Registrar to be relevant; and

(iv) the proportions in which its owners, if more than one, own the ship.

(2) On the registration of a ship the Registrar shall retain all the originals of the documents specified under section 16(3) of the Act.

### reg 12 Change of ownership

(1) Where the ownership of a registered ship changes, the new owner of the ship or qualified person shall make an application in the Form REG 9 for change of ownership.

(2) Upon receiving an application under subregulation (1) , the Registrar shall—

(a) enter details of the change in the Register;

(b) endorse details of the change on the certificate of registry or certificate of registration whichever is applicable to the ship; and

(c) retain the Bill of Sale and declaration of ownership.

(3) The prescribed fee for the change of ownership is $203.83 (VEP).

### reg 13 Registration of alterations

Where an application is made by the owner or ship’s agents for registering an alteration to their ship, the Registrar shall—

(a) enter into the Register details of alteration corresponding with the particulars relating to the new tonnage certificate or any particulars to the ship contained in the Register;

(b) issue a new certificate of registry or certificate of registration whichever is applicable; and

(c) ensure that the new tonnage is marked on the ship in accordance with regulation 6(1)(d) .

### reg 14 Change of name of registered ships

(1) An application for a change in name of a registered ship shall be made by the owner in Form REG 5 , with the prescribed fees of $62.52 (VEP).

(2) Where the Registrar is satisfied that the applicant is the owner of the ship and that the proposed name is not a name that would be refused under section 19 of the Act, the Registrar shall notify the owner or the ship’s agent of the approval of the application.

(3) Where the Registrar is satisfied that the ship has been re-marked in accordance with these Regulations he or she shall—

(a) enter the new name of the ship in the Register; and

(b) endorse the new name of the ship on its certificate of registration or certificate of registry, whichever is applicable.

### reg 15 Proportion of the crew to be Fijian nationals

For the purposes of section 91 of the Act, the proportion of the crew of a Fiji ship required to be citizens shall be as follows—

(a) in the case of a Fiji ship engaged on an international voyage, 70% of the total crew shall be Fijian nationals;

(b) in the case of a Fiji ship in respect of which a coasting trade licence is held, 90% of the total crew shall be Fijian nationals; and

(c) in case of fishing ships engaged in fishing in Fiji’s territorial waters, Economic Exclusive Zone and on the high seas, 70% of the total crew shall be Fijian nationals.

## PART 5 CERTIFICATION [regs 16-17]

### reg 16 Certificate of Registry and Certificate of Registrations

A certificate of registry for ships registered in Part A of the Register shall be in accordance with form REG 3A and a certificate of registration for ships registered in Part B of the Register shall be in accordance with form REG 3B .

### reg 17 Provisional Certificate of Registry and Provisional Certificate of Registration

A provisional certificate of registry for ships registered in Part A of the Register shall be in accordance with form REG 4A and a provisional certificate of registration for ships registered in Part B of the Register shall be in accordance with form REG 4B .

## PART 6 TRANSFERS, TRANSMISSION AND MORTGAGES OF SHIPS [regs 18-19]

### reg 18 Documents accompanying a transfer of ship or share in a ship

(1) A ship or any share in a ship shall be transferred by a bill of sale and a declaration of transfer.

(2) The Registrar shall on receipt of a bill of sale and the declaration of transfer—

(a) register the bill of sale in the register; and

(b) endorse on the bill of sale the date and time of making the entry.

(3) Every declaration of transfer or declaration of transmission shall be made before any person before whom a declaration may be made under section 3 of the Statutory Declarations Act 1970, and where the transferee or person entitled under the transmission is not the Government of the Republic of Fiji, shall include the following particulars—

(a) a statement specifying the full name, address, and nationality of the transferee or person entitled under the transmission;

(b) the grounds on which nationality is claimed;

(c) the extent of that person’s interest in the ship; and

(d) a statement that, to the best of the knowledge of the person making the statement, the ship will continue to be a Fiji-owned ship or will be controlled by a Fijian national or Fijian nationals.

(4) The Transferee of any bill of sale shall lodge the bill of sale to the Registrar within 3 months after the bill of sale becomes effective and bills of sale lodged under these Regulations shall be registered in the order of their lodgment.

### reg 19 Documents accompanying a transmission of ship or shares in a ship or of interest in mortgage

When a transmission of a ship or shares in a ship, or any mortgage of a ship is in accordance with section 42 or 43 of the Act, the evidence of transmission—

(a) if the transmission is a consequence of bankruptcy, shall be accompanied by such evidence as is for the time being receivable in a court as proof of title of persons claiming under a bankruptcy;

(b) if the transmission is a consequence of the death of a joint owner or joint mortgagee, the remaining joint owners or mortgagees or their representative shall produce to the Registrar in the form REG 11A —

(i) a certificate of death or burial of the deceased, probate or letters of administration of the estate, or a certified copy of any such documents; and

(ii) a declaration made by a person well acquainted with the deceased stating that the deceased named in the Register is a joint owner or mortgagee;

(c) if the transmission is a consequence of the death of a sole owner or sole mortgagee, the executor, administrator or the legal representative of the estate of the deceased owner, shall produce to the Registrar either—

(i) a probate, letter of administration or other instrument proving that he or she is the legal representative of the estate of the deceased owner or mortgagee; and

(ii) evidence that he or she is a qualified person;

(d) in the case of transmission by a court order, the Registrar shall be furnished with a copy of that order and evidence that the person to whom the transmission is made is a qualified person in the form REG 11B ;

(e) in the case of a non-qualified person or persons, that person or persons shall provide the Registrar with evidence of an appointed qualified person as an agent of the ship endorsed by a barrister and solicitor in the roll of the High Court of Fiji; and

(f) in the case of forfeiture of a ship, provide to the Registrar the court order stating that the ship has been forfeited and the ship under these circumstances may be de-registered in form REG 11C .

## PART 7 FEES AND CHARGES [regs 20-23]

### reg 20 Annual registration fees

(1) Under these Regulations, the gross tonnage of a ship means the gross tonnage of the ship as recorded in the Register.

(2) The annual registration fee for a vessel is an amount calculated at the rate of $0.63 for each gross ton of the vessel with a minimum fee of $25.

### reg 21 Initial registration fees

(1) In these Regulations, the gross tonnage of a ship means the gross tonnage of the ship as specified in its tonnage certificate.

(2) The fee payable on the registration of a vessel is an amount calculated at the rate of $1.25 for each gross ton of the vessel with a minimum fee of $100.

(3) The registration fee payable in respect of the registration of a vessel includes the issue of a Carving Note, provision of forms and documents and the issue of a provisional registration certificate, where necessary, and a registration certificate.

(4) The prescribed fee for the issue of a copy of a registration certificate that has been defaced, lost, misplaced or destroyed is $107.80.

### reg 22 Miscellaneous fees

For the purposes of these Regulations the miscellaneous fees set out in Part 1 of the Schedule shall be charged for the purposes of the administration of ship registration.

### reg 23 Exemption clause

The Minister may, in consultation with the Chief Executive Officer, by written notice, exempt any person or categories of ships from the payment of any fees prescribed under these Regulations.
