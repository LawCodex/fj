# TAX FREE ZONES (DELEGATED RESPONSIBILITY) REGULATIONS 1998

Tax Free Zones (Delegated Responsibility) Regulations 1998 (LN 74 of 1998) commenced on 22 May 1998, as amended by:

## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [regs 1-2]

### reg 1 Short title

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Tax Free Zones (Delegated Responsibility) Regulations 1998.

(2) These Regulations apply only to tax free zones, the responsibility for the development, management or control of which has been delegated under section 5(2) of the Act.

### reg 2 Interpretation

(1) In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**activity** includes any trade, business or manufacture and any other activity or operation;

**Authority,** in relation to any tax free zone, means the statutory body, government department or company to which responsibility for the tax free zone has been delegated under section 5(2) of the Act;

**Customs laws** includes any written law relating to the Customs and includes the Act;

**operator** means a company to which an operating licence has been granted under section 7 of the Act;

**Permanent Secretary** means the permanent secretary to the Minister and if there is more than one permanent secretary, the permanent secretary designated by the Minister from time to time;

**proper officer of the Authority** means any officer or employee of the Authority acting in the fulfilment of his or her duties, whether such duties are assigned to him or her specially or generally, or expressly or by implication;

**proper officer of the Customs** means any officer authorised, whether specially or generally or expressly or by implication, by the Comptroller, to carry out any of the provisions of these Regulations:

**public holiday** means a public holiday as provided for under the Employment Relations Act 2007 ;

[def am LN 99 of 2016 reg 259, 1 December 2016]

**tax free zone** means any area or building declared to be a tax free zone under section 4(1) of the Act, the responsibility for the development, management or control of which has been delegated under section 5(2) of the Act; and

**the Act** means the Tax Free Zones Act 1991 and includes these Regulations and any other subsidiary legislation made under the Act.

(2) The expressions “export”, “Fiji”, “Minister”, “operating licence”, “tax” and “trade, business or manufacture” have the meanings assigned to them by section 3 of the Act.

(3) The expressions “Comptroller”, “Customs”, “duty”, “import”, “officer” and “vehicle” have the meanings assigned to them by section 2 of the Customs Act 1986.

## PART 2 ADMINISTRATION [regs 3-15]

### reg 3 Responsibilities of Authority

It is the responsibility of the Authority in any tax free zone for which it has been delegated responsibility—

(a) to ensure that the tax free zone includes such fencing and other security arrangements and such office and other facilities as are necessary for the due supervision and control of all goods entering or leaving such zone;

(b) to provide secure sheds or other facilities for the loading, unloading and examination of goods in the tax free zone;

(c) to provide suitable office accommodation for the exclusive use of Customs officers employed in the tax free zone;

(d) to provide adequate enclosures to segregate the tax free zone for the protection of the public revenue, together with suitable supervision of all persons, vehicles, vessels and goods entering or leaving the tax free zone;

(e) to take all such measures as the Minister may consider necessary for the development, security and proper functioning of the tax free zone; and

(f) to take such measures which the Minister responsible for finance considers necessary for the purpose of protecting the public revenue in relation to the zone.

### reg 4 Directions of Ministers

(1) The Minister may, after consultation with the Authority, give the Authority general or special directions, not inconsistent with the Act or any other law, to be followed by the Authority in the exercise of its responsibilities and powers under these Regulations, and the Authority must give effect to any direction so given.

(2) The Minister responsible for finance may, after consulting with the Minister and the Authority, give the Authority general or special directions, which he or she considers necessary for the purpose of protecting the public revenue in relation to the zone and which are not inconsistent with the Act or any other law, to be followed by the Authority in the exercise of its responsibilities and powers under these Regulations, and the Authority shall give effect to any direction so given.

### reg 5 Records and annual report

(1) The Authority must keep such records of its activities in a tax free zone, and in such form, as the Minister may from time to time direct.

(2) The Authority must present to the Minister annually, and at such other times as the Minister may require, a report containing a full account of the activities, receipts and expenditure of the Authority in respect of a tax free zone, and such records and other information in respect of the zone as the Minister may require.

### reg 6 Authority to erect perimeter fences or walls

(1) For the purpose of—

(a) segregating a tax free zone; and

(b) the protection of the public revenue,

the Authority must, except where the Comptroller agrees otherwise, erect and maintain 2 parallel perimeter fences or walls along the boundary of a tax free zone with provisions for exit and entry points for the movement of persons, vehicles, vessels and goods, as the case may be, entering or leaving the tax free zone.

(2) The Authority must, except where the Comptroller agrees otherwise, illuminate—

(a) the perimeter fences or walls erected under subregulation (1) ;

(b) the exit and entry points on the perimeter fences or walls; and

(c) the area along the perimeter of the tax free zone,

from 5.30 pm on each day to 7.00 am the day following and during such other hours as the Comptroller may deem necessary.

(3) Nothing in this regulation limits regulation 3 .

### reg 7 Promotion of public health and safety

(1) For the purpose of safety or promotion of public health in a tax free zone, the Authority may by itself or an agent—

(a) establish, maintain and carry out sanitary, fire and security services;

(b) build and maintain roads;

(c) regulate the flow of traffic;

(d) undertake the planting, trimming and removal of trees; and

(e) do such other acts that the Authority deems necessary and expedient,

in the tax free zone.

(2) The Authority may, in carrying out any function or act pursuant to subregulation (1) , issue reasonable instructions to the operators in the tax free zone and such instructions must be complied with.

### reg 8 Authority may designate areas for different activities in tax free zones

(1) The Authority may designate different areas within a tax free zone for different activities.

(2) Notwithstanding subregulation (1) , the Authority may allow an existing activity to be carried out at, from or on its present premises.

### reg 9 Permission to erect buildings or structures or to hold leases and tenancies in tax free zone

(1) A person must not—

(a) erect any building or other structure; or

(b) hold or give any tenancy or lease or subtenancy or sublease of any movable or immovable property in a tax free zone for the purpose of carrying out any activity in the zone,

except with the permission of the Authority.

(2) A person may apply for the permission required under subregulation (1) by making an application to the Authority in writing in a form approved by the Authority.

(3) Upon receiving an application under subregulation (2) the Authority may grant or refuse the permission in writing.

(4) Without limiting subregulation (3) the Authority may, before granting or refusing its permission, do any or all of the following—

(a) inspect—

(i) the site where the building or other structure is proposed to be erected;

(ii) the place or premises in respect of which a tenancy or lease or subtenancy or sublease is proposed;

(iii) the place or premises where the activities are to be carried out;

(b) require the application to submit a site or building plan;

(c) require the applicant to make an alteration to any plan of any building or structure submitted with the application in order to comply with any written law relating to buildings or structures in the tax free zone.

(5) The permission granted under this regulation must be in writing and may be subject to such reasonable terms and conditions as the Authority deems necessary to impose, and a copy of the permission granted must be sent to the Comptroller and the Permanent Secretary.

(6) No alteration or addition may be made—

(a) to any existing building or structure in a tax free zone; or

(b) to any plan of a building or structure proposed to be erected in a tax free zone in respect of which a permission under subregulation (3) has been granted,

without the written permission of the Authority.

(7) Nothing in this regulation authorises the Authority to permit any building or structure to be erected, within a tax free zone, less than 6 meters from that perimeter of the zone.

(8) A permission granted under this regulation to any person does not relieve that person from complying with the requirements of any written law in respect of anything done or proposed to be done pursuant to that permission.

### reg 10 Business hours of the Authority in tax free zone

(1) The offices of the Authority within a tax free zone must be opened for business to the public as follows—

(i) from 8.00 am to 4.30 pm on Monday to Thursday of every week; and

(ii) from 8.00 am to 4.00 pm on every Friday,

and may be so opened at such other times as the Authority may decide.

(2) Unless the Authority otherwise decides, the office of the Authority within a tax free zone must be closed on a public holiday.

### reg 11 Requirements to comply with written law and licence

(1) Any person who intends to carry on or carries on any activity in a tax free zone must comply with all the requirements of any written law relating to that activity.

(2) Without limiting subregulation (1) , a person must not carry on any trade, business or manufacture in a tax free zone except in accordance with a valid operating licence.

### reg 12 Authority to keep record of operator’s licences within tax free zone

(1) The Authority must keep a record of the operating licences of all operators within a tax free zone.

(2) The record must, in respect of each operating licence, contain details of—

(a) the date of commencement and expiry of the operating licence;

(b) the name, registered address and the authorised officer of the operator;

(c) the trade, business or manufacture to which the licence relates;

(d) the conditions attached to the operating licence;

(e) any variation or revocation of the operating licence by the Minister; and

(f) any transfer of the licence approved by the Minister under section 9 of the Act.

(3) The Minister must keep the Authority informed regarding any entry made in the register kept by him or her under section 10 of the Act in respect of any operator within a tax free zone.

### reg 13 Authority to report non-compliance

The Authority must inform the Minister and the chief executive officer of the Fiji Revenue and Customs Service of any non-compliance or suspected non-compliance with the terms and conditions of an operating licence or with the Customs laws of which it becomes aware.

[reg 13 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 259, 1 December 2016 Act 38 of 2017 s 7, 1 August 2017]

### reg 14 Authority may order removal or exclusion of goods from or discontinuance of activity in tax free zone

(1) Where a person—

(a) has taken, or proposes to take, any goods into a tax free zone; or

(b) is carrying on, or proposes to carry on, any activity in a tax free zone,

which in the opinion of the Authority is dangerous or prejudicial to the public interest, health or safety, or contrary to, or not authorised by, an operating licence or the Act, the Authority may give an order in writing to the person—

(i) in respect of goods that have been taken into the tax free zone, to exclude the goods from the zone;

(ii) in respect of goods proposed to be taken into a tax free zone, to exclude the goods from the zone;

(iii) in respect of any activity that is being carried on in a tax free zone, to discontinue the activity; or

(iv) in respect of any activity proposed to be carried out in the tax free zone, to exclude the activity from the zone.

(2) Any order given under subregulation (1) —

(a) in respect of paragraph (b)(i) or (ii) , takes effect immediately on the date the order is served on the person;.

(b) In respect of paragraph (b)(iii) or (iv) , takes effect only after the determination of any appeal, if any, in favour of the Authority.

(3) The Authority must inform the Comptroller and the Minister in writing of any order which it makes under subregulation (1) and of the compliance or otherwise with the order.

(4) An operator may, within 14 days after being notified of an order against it under subregulation (1) , appeal in writing to the Minister.

(5) The Minister may, after giving the Authority and the operator an opportunity to be heard, affirm, set aside or vary an order made under subregulation (1) .

### reg 15 Restrictions of entry into and departure of persons from tax free zone

(1) A person must not enter or reside in a tax free zone except as authorised by the Authority, or remain therein when requested to leave by the Authority.

(2) The Authority may, after consultation with the Comptroller, give its authorisation under this regulation in such form and manner as it deems expedient and appropriate in the circumstances.

(3) Where access to a tax free zone is restricted, the Authority must display at every point of entry of the zone, the limits and conditions of entry, and any person entering the tax free zone must comply with the limits and conditions so specified.

## PART 3 HANDLING OF GOODS IN TAX FREE ZONES [regs 16-28]

### reg 16 Interpretation and application

In this Part, unless the context otherwise requires—

**approved form** means a form from time to time approved by the Comptroller; and

**suspect goods** means any goods in respect of which there is reasonable cause to believe that they may be the subject of an offence under the Customs laws or the Excise Act 1986 .

### reg 17 Restriction of entry and removal of goods into and from tax free zone

Goods must not be taken into, or taken out from, or kept or used in a tax free zone except in accordance with the Customs laws.

### reg 18 Customs control

(1) For the avoidance of doubt, all goods in or intended for or removed from a tax free zone on which all duties and charges have not been paid and which have not yet been released from the control of the Customs are subject to the Customs laws and Customs control until the time of their export.

(2) All goods entering or leaving any tax free zone are liable to inspection and control by the proper officer of the Customs.

### reg 19 Customs access

(1) A proper officer of the Customs has for the purposes of the Customs laws at all reasonable times free access to a tax free zone and to any premises, vehicle or vessel therein, and in any such zone may—

(a) require the occupant of the premises to produce and permit him or her to examine any records relating to any activity on the premises;

(b) seize and detain any records relating to any offence under the Customs laws and require any person to answer questions relating to the records;

(c) stop and examine any vehicle or vessel for the purpose of ascertaining whether any suspect goods are contained in it and request any person in control or in charge of the vehicle or vessel to open all parts of the vehicle or vessel for examination by the officer;

(d) stop and search any person entering or leaving the zone for the purpose of ascertaining whether any suspect goods are being concealed by that person;

(e) open any package or receptacle and examine any suspect goods which are being taken into or removed from, or are intended to be taken into or removed from a tax free zone;

(f) take and retain reasonable samples of any goods necessary for the performance of his or her duties of enforcement under the Customs laws, provided that an official receipt shall be given for any sample so taken; and

(g) lock up, mark, seal or otherwise secure any premises, vehicle or vessel, goods, material or plant, for the purpose of enforcing the Customs laws.

(2) Any person or vehicle or vessel entering or leaving a tax free zone if so required by a proper officer of the Customs or the Authority must stop for search or examination.

(3) Every person required by a proper officer of Customs to give information or to produce any document which it is the duty of such officer to investigate and which it is within the power of such person to give or produce must give such information or produce such documents.

### reg 20 Permitted goods

(1) Subject to these Regulations and the Customs laws, goods of any description which will be used in a tax free zone by an operator directly or indirectly for any activity permitted by an operating licence may be brought into a tax free zone.

(2) No goods other than those referred to in subregulation (1) may, without the approval of the Comptroller, be brought into a tax free zone.

(3) The Comptroller may give his or her approval under this regulation in such form and manner as he or she deems expedient and appropriate in the circumstances.

### reg 21 Authority may require operator to provide storage and safety measures for goods etc

(1) The Authority may, after consultation with the Comptroller, and must, if required by the Comptroller, require an operator in a tax free zone—

(a) to provide—

(i) separate storage space for raw materials;

(ii) separate storage space for finished products; and

(iii) separate storage space for waste products,

in any place or on any premises where the activity is carried out;

(b) to provide separate storage for dangerous or inflammable goods and to ensure adequate safety measures are taken in relation to such goods;

(c) to provide identification marks for goods meant for a particular activity;

(d) to comply with any other obligation the Authority or the Comptroller considers necessary to impose in order to ensure adequate protection of the public revenue.

(2) An operator must comply with any requirement under subregulation (1) .

### reg 22 Use of goods manufactured in tax free zone

(1) Goods manufactured within a tax free zone may be used or consumed in the zone only with the written permission of a proper officer of the Customs.

(2) An operator must apply for permission under subregulation (1) in the approved form.

### reg 23 Goods must not be destroyed without approval of Customs

(1) An operator must not, unless he or she is exempted under these Regulations, destroy any goods in any tax free zone except with the written approval of and in the presence of a proper officer of the Customs.

(2) The approval under subregulation (1) may be obtained by applying in writing to a proper officer of the Customs stating the type of goods to be destroyed, the quantity and the date it is proposed to destroy the goods.

(3) Where goods are destroyed in accordance with these Regulations, the proper officer of the Customs present must issue a certificate to the owner of the goods or his or her representative certifying that the goods have been destroyed and the certificate must be kept as a record by the operator.

### reg 24 Authority may require operator to maintain record of activities

(1) For the purpose of protecting the public revenue and such other purposes which are deemed necessary and expedient for the proper functioning of a tax free zone, the Authority may require, and if so directed by the Minister responsible for finance or the Comptroller must require, operators to maintain full and proper records of all activities carried out by them in a tax free zone.

(2) A record referred to in subregulation (1) must, *inter alia*, contain details of—

(a) all goods received at the place or premises where the activities are carried out;

(b) all goods released for consumption, sale or manufacture within the tax free zone, in another tax free zone, elsewhere in Fiji or for export;

(c) waste stocks and the manner of their disposal or destruction;

(d) losses through spillage, evaporation and other causes; and

(e) the balances of all goods stocked at the place or premises where the activities are carried out.

(3) Unless exempted by the Authority, an operator must comply with all directions given by the Authority in respect of matters specified in subregulation (2) .

(4) Every record kept under subregulation (1) —

(a) must be made in an indelible fashion and must not be altered;

(b) must, where any entry is incorrect, be corrected by drawing a line through such entry so that it remains legible, and the correct entry shall be made as close to the incorrect entry as possible;

(c) must be made available to the proper officer of the Authority or the Customs upon request at any reasonable time;

(d) must not be destroyed unless approved in writing by the Authority with the concurrence of the Comptroller.

(5) An operator must, if required, furnish to the Authority and the Comptroller not later than the tenth day of each month a return in such form as the Authority acting with the concurrence of the Comptroller may require, giving details as to the matters specified in subregulation (2)(a) to (e) for the month immediately preceding.

(6) The Authority may, with the concurrence of the Comptroller or the Minister responsible for finance, as the case may be, exempt any operator within a tax free zone from any provision of this regulation.

(7) Nothing in this regulation affects any other obligation to keep any record under the Income Tax Act 2015 , the Value Added Tax Act 1991 or any other law.

### reg 25 Times for movements of goods into or out of tax free zone

(1) Goods must not be taken into or out of a tax free zone except at the following times—

(a) from 8.00 am to 5.00 pm on Monday to Thursday of every week; and

(b) from 8.00 am to 4.00 pm on every Friday,

other than a public holiday.

(2) Notwithstanding subregulation (1) the Comptroller may direct the Authority to vary the commencement and closing times for movement of goods in respect of any particular tax free zone to such times as the Comptroller may specify in the direction.

(3) The times specified in subregulation (1) do not affect any activity (other than movement of goods) carried out in a tax free zone.

(4) Any operator may apply in writing to a proper officer of the Authority for an extension of the times specified in subregulation (1) or the times specified pursuant to the Comptroller’s direction under subregulation (2) .

(5) An application under subregulation (4) must be submitted at least 2 hours before the closing time specified, and the officer of the Authority may—

(a) approve the application with the concurrence of the Comptroller; or

(b) refuse the application on the following grounds—

(i) that the application was not submitted within the stipulated time;

(ii) that in the circumstances of the case it is not possible to approve the application; or

(iii) that the application has not been approved by the Comptroller.

(6) When goods are permitted by the Comptroller to be transported outside the prescribed working days and hours, the operator must pay the Comptroller for the services of the Customs officers involved at the rates for the time being prescribed under the Customs laws.

### reg 26 Customs forms

The provisions of the Customs laws relating to the use of forms apply for the purposes of regulations 27 and 28 .

### reg 27 Customs requirements for goods entering tax free zones

A person, or his or her agent, who takes goods into a tax free zone must—

(a) submit a duly completed Customs entry or entries in the approved form;

(b) produce the goods for inspection; and

(c) pay forthwith any duties, taxes or other charges leviable on the goods,

to the proper officer of the Customs at the Customs office at the tax free zone.

### reg 28 Customs requirements for goods being taken out of tax free zones

A person who takes goods out of a tax free zone must, by himself or herself or an agent—

(a) submit a duly completed Customs entry or entries in the approved form;

(b) submit in duplicate a sales docket for the goods in the approved form;

(c) produce the goods for inspection; and

(d) pay forthwith any duties, taxes or other charges leviable on the goods,

to the proper officer of the Customs at the Customs office at the tax free zone.

## PART 3 MISCELLANEOUS [regs 29-30]

### reg 29 Contravention is an offence

A person who contravenes any provision of these Regulations commits an offence and, on conviction, is liable to the penalty prescribed in section 16 of the Act.

### reg 30 Disposal of abandoned or undeclared goods

(1) Subject to subregulations (2) and (3) , where goods brought into a tax free zone have been abandoned, the Authority may after a period of 6 months from the date the goods are brought into the tax free zone dispose of the goods by public auction.

(2) The Authority must not dispose of any goods (other than perishable or inflammable goods) pursuant to subregulation (1) unless—

(a) it has given notice in writing not less than 30 days before the date of the proposed disposal to the owner of the goods, if the name and the address of the owner is known; or

(b) it has published a notice in the Gazette 30 clear days before the date of the disposal, if the owner and the address of the owner of the goods is unknown.

(3) The Authority may without notice to the owner or in the Gazette—

(a) dispose of any perishable goods at any time if they are not declared and collected by the owner forthwith;

(b) dispose of any inflammable goods if they are not declared and collected by the owner after 14 days of their arrival at the tax free zone.

(4) Any proceeds of public sale carried out pursuant to this Regulation must be paid into the Consolidated Fund less any expenses incurred by the Authority in carrying out the sale.
