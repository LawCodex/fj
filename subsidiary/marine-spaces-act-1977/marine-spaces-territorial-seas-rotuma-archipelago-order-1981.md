# MARINE SPACES (TERRITORIAL SEAS) (ROTUMA ARCHIPELAGO) ORDER 1981

**Table of Amendments**

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Marine Spaces (Territorial Seas) (Rotuma Archipelago) Order 1981.

[O 1 am LN 83 of 2018 O 2, effective 2 November 2018]

## O 2 Territorial seas baselines

The points between which straight baselines are to be drawn for the purpose of determining the innermost limits of the territorial seas of the Rotuma archipelago are declared to be the geographical coordinates, based on the International Terrestrial Reference System 2005 (ITRS2005) Geodetic Datum, specified in Schedule 1.

[O 2 am LN 82 of 2012 O 3, effective 9 November 2012; LN 83 of 2018 O 3, effective 2 November 2018]

## O 3 Outer limits of territorial seas

The outer limits of the territorial seas of the Rotuma archipelago are declared to extend to a line drawn between, and joining in numerical sequence, the geographical co-ordinates, based on the International Terrestrial Reference System 2005 (ITRS2005) Geodetic Datum, specified in Schedule 2.

[O 3 insrt LN 83 of 2018 O 4, effective 2 November 2018]

Footnotes:

1 This was rectified by the Rectification of Errors Order 2019 (LN 13 of 2019).
