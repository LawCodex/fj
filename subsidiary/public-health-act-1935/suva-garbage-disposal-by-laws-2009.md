## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [by-law 1-4]

### by-law 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These By-laws may be cited as the Suva (Garbage Disposal) By-laws 2009.

(2) These By-laws come into force on a date published in the Gazette.

### by-law 2 Definitions

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Director Health Service** means the Director Health Services of the local authority and his or her representatives who are authorised to act on his or her behalf;

**garbage** means all trade waste, house garbage, litter, paper, plastic materials, food scrap, rubbish and any noxious, offensive or unwholesome matter or substance. It does not include Yard and Garden Waste (as defined within this regulation), hazardous waste, rocks, stumps or large metal objects. It does not include any object that is too large to be enclosed in a regulation garbage receptacle as hereinafter defined;

**garbage collector** means any persons appointed from time to time by the local authority or designated by the local authority’s Director Health Services to collect and remove garbage;

**hazardous waste** means gaseous, liquid and solid waste which, because of its inherent nature and quantity, requires special disposal techniques to avoid creating health hazards, nuisances or environmental pollution. Hazardous wastes are toxins or poisons, corrosives, irritants, strong sensitisers, flammables, explosives, infectious wastes, condemned foods and asbestos, sharps (hypodermic needles, etc) animal excrement, soiled diapers and used personal hygiene products;

**health inspector** means the health inspector of the local authority and his or her representatives who are authorised by the local authority to act on his or her behalf;

**local authority** means the local authority for the urban sanitary district of Suva and includes any persons authorised by that body;

**occupier** means any person occupying and dwelling, habitation, place of residence or trade premises but does not include a boarder, roomer or lodger therein;

**person** includes a corporation, partnership or party, and the personal or other legal representatives of a person to whom the contract can apply according to law;

**regulation garbage receptacle** means a watertight galvanised iron or rigid plastic receptacles, equipped with 2 handles set opposite to each other and drip-proof cover, and having a capacity of not less than 42 litres and not more than 85 litres;

**tyres** means the outer pneumatic rubber covering of wheels of passenger vehicles, light service trucks and motor cycles with an inner diameter of less than 42 centimetres;

**white goods** means metal appliances and includes refrigerators, freezers, clothes washers, dish washers, cloths dryers ranges, stoves, air conditioners and hot water tanks or parts thereof; and

**yard and garden waste** means organic materials, substances or objects including, but not necessarily limited to, grass, lawn and hedge clippings, grass sod, flowers, weeds, leaves, vegetables stalks, shrubs and tree branches less than 75 mm (3 inches) in diameter but does not include—

(a) material disposed of from inside the home, such as vegetable scrapings, fruit peelings, leftover food waste, house plants or floral arrangements.

### by-law 3 Application

These By-laws apply to the urban sanitary district of Suva.

### by-law 4 Administration and enforcement

These By-laws shall be administered and enforced by the local authority.

## PART 2 COLLECTION, REMOVAL AND DISPOSAL [by-law 5-16]

### by-law 5 Garbage receptacles to be provided

Every person being the owner or occupier of lands or premises within the local authority shall provide on and for such premises a sufficient number of regulation garbage receptacles, to contain all garbage from such land and premises and maintained for garbage purposes in accordance with the provisions of these By-laws.

### by-law 6 Accumulation on land owned or occupied

No person being the owner or occupier of any land within the local authority’s boundary limits shall suffer or permit the accumulation upon lands or premises belonging to or occupied by him or her, of any animal or vegetable refuse, garbage, ashes, kitchen or trade refuse or other filth or offensive thing except in a regulation garbage receptacle or receptacles.

### by-law 7 Accumulation on vacant land

No person shall deposit or permit to be deposited in or upon any vacant land, street, road or highway with the local authority’s boundary limits, or deposit or permit to be deposited on any foreshore or in waters adjacent to the local authority’s boundary limits, any animal or vegetables refuse, ashes, garbage, kitchen, trade refuse or other filthy or offensive thing as aforesaid except with the permission in writing of the Director Health Services.

### by-law 8 Regulation garbage receptacle

(1) Regulation garbage receptacle must be a watertight galvanised iron or rigid plastic receptacle, equipped with 2 handles set opposite to each other and drip-proof cover, and having a capacity of not less than 42 litres and not more than 85 litres.

(2) Regulation garbage receptacles are to be—

(a) maintained in a clean and sanitary condition;

(b) kept in good repair;

(c) shall be replaced immediately when they become unserviceable; and

(d) properly marked with the number associated with the premises being served;

(e) covered and closed when depositing garbage therein.

### by-law 9 Liquids in garbage receptacles

No liquid or free water shall be kept in or permitted to remain in or accumulate in any garbage receptacle and receptacles shall, at all times, be kept securely covered with a drip-proof cover.

### by-law 10 Location of receptacles

All regulation garbage receptacle shall at all times, be kept on the premises, conveniently accessible to the garbage collector, provided, however, that the distance of the regulation garbage receptacles from the highway, shall not exceed 6.l m (20 ft).

### by-law 11 Availability of garbage receptacle

(1) All regulation receptacles shall be accessible to the garbage collector by 7.00 am on the scheduled days of collection as may be determined from time to time by the local authority.

(2) No person shall at any time, place their own garbage into the local authority’s vehicle without the authorisation of the local authority or Director of Health.

### by-law 12 Prohibited materials

(1) No person shall place any of the following materials or substances in a regulation garbage receptacle—

(a) hazardous waste, excluding animal excrement, soiled diapers and used personal hygiene products that are appropriately bagged and sealed;

(b) white goods;

(c) tyres; and

(d) yard and garden waste.

(2) Garbage will not be collected from any regulation garbage receptacle containing hazardous waste as particularised in by-law (1)(a) , (b) , (c) and (d) .

### by-law 13 Household hazardous waste

(1) No person shall put household hazardous waste in a regulation garbage receptacle places out for collection by the garbage collector.

(2) Any person who fails to comply with paragraph (1) , commits an offence.

### by-law 14 Notification

It shall be the duty of the owner or occupier of the land within the local authority’s boundary limits to immediately notify the health inspector of the local authority in cases where the local authority’s garbage collector has failed to collect garbage on the scheduled day of collection.

### by-law 15 Discontinued garbage collection

(1) Where a condition exists on any property which prevents the garbage collector from carrying out his or her duties by virtue of hazard, potential danger, difficulty of access or other abnormal or dangerous condition, the Director of Health Service shall—

(a) give written notification to the owner or occupier that the condition must be abated and in default of abatement; and

(b) direct that garbage collection service be discontinued until the condition is abated.

(2) Responsibility for disposal of garbage during discontinuance of service as aforesaid shall rest with the owner or occupier.

### by-law 16 Inspection

(1) The Director of Health Services may enter, at all reasonable times, upon any property in order to ascertain whether the by-laws set out in these By-laws are being obeyed.

(2) No person shall prevent or obstruct, or attempt to prevent or obstruct, the entry of the Director of Health Services upon any property as authorised under paragraph (1) .

## PART 3 FEES AND GARBAGE COLLECTION SERVICE [by-law 17-18]

### by-law 17 Fees

For the collection of the contents of each regulation garbage receptacle, from any premises a charge of $25 per year is to be levied and shall be paid in advance by the owner or occupier no later than 31 December of every year.

### by-law 18 Fees recoverable by action

(1) The local authority may resolve that the fees may be paid in instalments in which case a demand notice shall state when each instalment is due and payable.

(2) The liability of any person for fees shall not be affected by reason only of the fact that notice has not been given to such person.

(3) The fees stipulated in by-law 17 and collected by the local authority including fines and costs shall be paid into a special fund to be called the Garbage Collection and Disposal Fund to manage and sustain garbage collection and disposal.

(4) The local authority may recover the fees which have been levied under by-law 17 and are payable to the local authority and payment of which is in arrears and also the cost of proceedings, if any, for their recovery from a person liable to pay them, by action at the suit of the council in a court of competent jurisdiction.

(5) For the purpose of this by-law, fees shall be deemed to be overdue—

(a) where payment is to be made otherwise than by instalments, on the expiration of 30 days from the date of service of the demand notice; or

(b) where payment may be made by instalments in terms of a resolution of the local authority under paragraph (1) , on the expiration of 14 days from the due date of each instalment.

## PART 4 REPEAL [by-law 19]

### by-law 19 Repeal

The Suva (Garbage Disposal) By-laws are repealed.

## PART 5 PENALTIES [by-law 20]

### by-law 20 Penalties

(1) Any person shall be deemed to have committed an offence under this by-law and shall be liable upon conviction to a fine not less than $100 and the costs of prosecution, if such person—

(a) contravenes or does any act or thing which contravenes any provision of this by-law;

(b) suffers or permits any act or thing to be done in contravention of any of the provisions of this by-law; or

(c) neglects or refrains from doing anything required to be done by any of the provisions of this by-law.

(2) A person convicted under paragraph (1) shall, in the case of a continuing offence, be liable, to a further fine not less than half of $5 for each day the offence continues.
