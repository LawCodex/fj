# NAUSORI (GARBAGE REMOVAL AND DISPOSAL) BY-LAWS 2012

Nausori (Garbage Removal and Disposal) By-laws 2012 (LN 29 of 2012) commenced on 6 March 2012, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## by-law 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These By-laws may be cited as the Nausori (Garbage Removal and Disposal) By-laws 2012.

(2) These By-laws shall be deemed to have come into force on 6 March 2012.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**garbage** includes household refuse, empty bins, rubbish, refuse, house sweepings, ashes or any filth;

**local authority** means the local authority of the urban sanitary district of Nausori; and

**sanitary inspector** means any officer for the time being holding the appointment of sanitary inspector to the local authority or his or her deputy or assistant or any person authorised by the local authority for the purpose of these By-laws.

## by-law 3 Application

These By-laws shall apply to the urban sanitary district of Nausori.

## by-law 4 Responsibility of local authority

The local authority shall be responsible for the administration and enforcement of these By-laws.

## by-law 5 Works to be undertaken by local authority

The local authority may undertake itself or by contract the collection, removal and disposal of garbage from houses and premises.

## by-law 6 Garbage pans

Every owner or occupier of any house, residence, shop, building, premises or other premises in the urban sanitary town of Nausori shall provide a sufficient number of garbage pans each of a maximum capacity of 3 cubic feet and in accordance with the requirements of the Public Health Regulations 1937, and shall—

(a) at all times keep such garbage pans covered except when depositing garbage in them; and

(b) place the garbage pan in a position easily accessible to the Nausori Town Council’s garbage collector and, when more than one is necessary, they shall be placed together.

## by-law 7 Fees to be charged for garbage collection, removal and disposal services

(1) The local authority may charge a maximum of $85 per garbage pan per annum for residential and commercial/industrial and such fee shall be paid in advance to the local authority by the owner of the house, building or other premises in respect of which any garbage pan is used or has been prescribed whether such garbage pan is used or not.

(2) Any fee charged under paragraph (1) shall be by demand and shall be served upon the owner or the premises stating the period in respect of which the fee is charged and the date by which it shall be paid.

(3) If any fee due and payable under this by-law is not paid to the local authority within the time specified in the notice required under paragraph (2) , a Magistrate may, upon application made by the local authority issue a warrant levying the amount with costs by distress and sale of goods and chattels of the owner found on the premises.

(4) A warrant issued under paragraph (3) authorises the person executing it to remain on the premises during the interval, between the distress and sale whether or not such authority is expressed in the warrant.

(5) Paragraph (4) is without prejudice to the rights of the local authority to recover such fee by civil proceedings.

(6) The local authority with the approval of the Board may through a resolution, waive any fee due under these By-laws and any costs incurred for the purpose of that fee, if it appears to the local authority that such fee is irrecoverable or that it be written off on the grounds of poverty of the owner.

(7) If a resolution is made under paragraph (6) in respect of fee due in which a warrant under paragraph (4) , the warrant is deemed to be cancelled.

(8) All fees collected by the local authority under these By-laws shall be paid into its municipal fund.
