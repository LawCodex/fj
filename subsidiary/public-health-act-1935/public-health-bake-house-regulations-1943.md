# PUBLIC HEALTH (BAKE HOUSE) REGULATIONS 1943

Public Health (Bake House) Regulations 1943 (GN 52 of 1944) commenced on 28 January 1944, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Health (Bake House) Regulations 1943.

## reg 2 Doors to be screened

No person shall use or cause or suffer to be used as a bake house any premises unless the doors and windows are protected against flies and dust by means of self-closing wire gauze screens of approved mesh and material.

## reg 3 Hands to be cleansed

Every person engaged in the work of preparing or handling flour, bread, cakes or pastry or the ingredients entering into the composition thereof, shall immediately before commencing such work, and upon every occasion after visiting any sanitary convenience and before resuming work in the bake house, wash his or her hands thoroughly with soap and clean water.

## reg 4 Basins etc to be provided

The occupier of a bake house shall, for the purpose of facilitating the fulfilment of the preceding regulations provide sufficient wash basins and a supply of soap and clean towels for the use of his or her employees.

## reg 5 Clothing

The occupier of a bake house shall cause every person working in the bake house to wear a white apron and clean and sufficient clothing, and shall himself or herself observe this requirement if he or she takes any part in any operation conducted therein or connected therewith.

## reg 6 Storage of flour

The occupier of such bake house shall cause flour stocks to be stored only in dry, well-ventilated places, and shall cause the same and all other bake house stores to be kept in such a place, and manner as to ensure their protection from dust, rats, mice and other vermin and any other cause liable to cause spoilage or deterioration of any such goods.

## reg 7 Bread etc to be kept on shelves

No bread, cakes, pastry or other food shall be placed upon the ground or floor or any other place than a bench, shelf or rack well above the ground or floor and kept for the purpose.

## reg 8 Use of tables etc

No person shall in any bake house sit or lie on any table, bench or trough, or upon any other place on which food is prepared, handled or kept.

## reg 9 Use of bake house

No person shall use any bake house for any other purpose than that connected strictly with the preparation and baking of food.

## reg 10 Animals prohibited in bake house

No person shall keep or have in, or suffer or permit to be in, any bake house, any dog, animal or poultry.

## reg 11 Yeast

The occupier of such bake house shall cause yeast to be manufactured only in an enclosed place to the satisfaction of an inspector and stored only in approved receptacles, which shall be kept securely covered when not in actual use.

## reg 12 Containers for yeast

No person shall use any kerosene, petrol or other tin receptacle of a like description for the purpose of holding yeast.

## reg 13 Covering cloths

No person shall use, or suffer to be used, for covering dough or for working upon, any bag or sack or material other than that approved by the local authority. Such bag, sack or material shall be kept solely for such purposes and shall be maintained at all times in a clean condition.

## reg 14 Cleanliness of employees

Any employee who fails to observe due cleanliness of person or habits, or who neglects to wash his or her hands before resuming work after visiting the closet or urinal, or who neglects to use, or who misuses, any convenience provided by the occupier of a bake house for the protection of food from contamination, shall be guilty of an offence against this regulation.

## reg 15 Animals in yard

The occupier of a bake house shall not permit any animal or poultry to be at large in any yard, and where any horse is stabled on bake house premises, he or she shall provide a properly constructed and approved covered manure bin into which shall be placed daily all animal or poultry droppings.

## reg 16 Penalty

Any person who acts in contravention of or fails to comply with these Regulations shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $10 for each offence and in the case of a continuing offence to a further fine not exceeding $4 for each day during which the offence continues after conviction.
