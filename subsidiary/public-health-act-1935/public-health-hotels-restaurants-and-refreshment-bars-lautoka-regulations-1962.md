# PUBLIC HEALTH (HOTELS, RESTAURANTS AND REFRESHMENT BARS) (LAUTOKA) REGULATIONS 1962

Public Health (Hotels, Restaurants And Refreshment Bars) (Lautoka) Regulations 1962 (LN 149 of 1962) commenced on 1 October 1962, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Health (Hotels, Restaurants and Refreshment Bars) (Lautoka) Regulations 1962.

## reg 2 Modified application to Lautoka

The Public Health (Hotels, Restaurants and Refreshment Bars) Regulations 1960 were applied to the town of Lautoka with effect from 1 October 1962 except that, in regulation 3(a), the words “and the city of Lautoka” are deleted.
