# NAVUA RURAL LOCAL AUTHORITY (GARBAGE REMOVAL AND DISPOSAL) BY-LAWS 2012

Navua Rural Local Authority (Garbage Removal and Disposal) By-laws 2012 (LN 36 of 2012)1 commenced on 23 March 2012, as amended by:

| --- | --- |
| Business Licensing (Repeal) Act 2020 (No 28 of 2020) | 1 August 2020 |

1
This was rectified by the Corrigendum published on 20 July 2012 (LN 57 of 2012).

## by-law 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These By-laws may be cited as the Navua Rural Local Authority (Garbage Removal and Disposal) By-laws 2012.

(2) These By-laws shall commence on the date of its publication in the Gazette.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Board** means the Navua Rural Local Authority Board;

**garbage** includes household refuse, empty tins, rubbish, refuse, house sweeping ashes or any filth;

**Local Authority** means the Navua Rural Local Authority;

**Public Service Vehicles** for the purposes of these By-laws includes but is not limited to—

(a) licence hire vehicles (LH);

(b) licence carrier (LC);

(c) rural service licence (RSL);

(d) licence minibus (LM); and

(e) road contracted licences (RCL); and

**Sanitary Inspector** means an officer appointed under section 11 of the Public Health Act 1935, and includes the deputy or assistant sanitary inspector, together with the enforcement officers.

## by-law 3 Application and Administration

These By-laws apply to the areas within Navua Sanitary District declared and constituted under the Local Government Act 1972.

## by-law 4 Responsibility of Local Authority

The Local Authority is responsible for the administration and enforcement of these By-laws.

## by-law 5 Garbage collection and disposal fees

(1) The Local Authority shall charge a fee of $60 per annum for residential premises, $120 per annum for any commercial premises per garbage pan and $5 per month for Public Service Vehicles and mobile businesses for the collection, removal and disposal of garbage.

[para (1) am Act 28 of 2020 s 4 and Sch, 1 August 2020]

(2) The Local Authority shall charge $15 per permit to all commercial outlets that are operational on public holidays and all parties interested in carrying out other function in public places within the Navua Subdivision.

(3) The owner of any premises shall pay in advance to the Local Authority the fee charged under paragraphs (1) and (2) for the purposes of collection, removal and disposal of garbage from such premises regardless of whether or not a garbage pan is used.

(4) The Local Authority shall issue and serve a written notice to the owner of the premises demanding payment of an amount due and specify the—

(a) amount to be paid;

(b) month(s) or period for which the amount relates; and

(c) date and place by which the amount must paid.

(5) If any premises remains unoccupied for less than 3 months, the Local Authority may upon written application by the owner, make a proportionate adjustment of the fee payable or a proportionate of the fee paid.

(6) All fees collected by the Local Authority under these By-laws must be paid into its Local Authority account.

## by-law 6 Warrant for distress and sale of goods

(1) If the owner or occupier of any premises fails to comply with the notice issued under by-law 5(4) the Local Authority may apply to a Magistrate to issue a warrant levying the amount with costs by distress and sale of the goods and chattels of the owner found on the premises.

(2) Regardless of whether such authority is expressed, a warrant issued under paragraph (1) shall authorise the person executing the warrant including any other person so authorised, to remain on any premises during the time of the distress and sale of goods found on that premises.

(3) Notwithstanding paragraph (1) , the Local Authority may recover such fee by other remedies, including civil proceedings in court.

## by-law 7 Waiving of fees

(1) Where the Local Authority is satisfied that the fees due and payable—

(a) cannot be recovered; or

(b) should be written off on the ground of poverty of the owner,

the Local Authority may, with the approval of the Board, waive or write off whole or part of any fee due under these By-laws and any costs incurred for the purpose recovering that fee.

(2) A warrant issued under by-law 6 is deemed to be cancelled, if the fee in respect of which the warrant was issued is waived pursuant to paragraph (1) .

## by-law 8 Garbage pans

(1) The owner or occupier of any premises must provide one or more garbage pans, each of a minimum capacity of—

(a) 1 cubic metre for residential areas; and

(b) 3 cubic metres for commercial and industrial areas,

in accordance with the requirements of Part 10 of the Public Health Regulations 1937.

(2) The owner or occupier of any premises must at all times—

(a) keep the lid of the garbage pan closed except when depositing garbage in the garbage pan;

(b) keep the garbage pan and its cover clean and in good condition;

(c) place the garbage pan in a position easily accessible for collection by employers or the Local Authority’s garbage collectors; and

(d) where more than one garbage pan is necessary, place such garbage pans together close to the road.

(3) The owner or occupier of premises must cause all the garbage arising from such premises to be deposited in garbage pans.

## by-law 9 Compulsory use of garbage pans

Every owner or occupier of premises must cause all garbage arising from such premises to be deposited in garbage pans.

## by-law 10 Supply of garbage pans

(1) The Local Authority may supply garbage pans to premises within its areas for the purpose of standardising and improving its service delivery.

(2) If garbage pans are supplied under paragraph (1) , the owner of such premises must accept, pay for and use such garbage pans.

## by-law 11 Garbage pans to be easily accessible

Garbage pans must be placed on the footpath adjoining each property adjacent to the road used by the collection vehicles on the day specified by the Local Authority, unless there is no footpath then immediately in front of the property or at a cul-de-sac from where access is provided to the premises.

## by-law 12 Frequency of service

(1) The Local Authority must arrange for the removal of all garbage pans from the Navua sanitary district at least twice a week.

(2) The garbage collector may not collect and remove any garbage pan placed at a distance of more than 6 metres from the road side.

## by-law 13 Prohibition on disposal of garbage on vacant lands etc

(1) No person shall dispose of or deposit garbage on any pavement, roadway, vacant land, foreshore, stream or creek within the Local Authority area.

(2) Any person who contravenes paragraph (1) commits an offence and shall be liable to a fine issued by the Local Authority under by-law 15 .

(3) The owner of premises commits an offence if any accumulation of garbage other than that contained in a garbage pan is found on such premises.

(4) For the purpose of paragraph (4) , if there are 2 or more owners, the owners jointly and severally commit the offence.

## by-law 14 Notification of non-removal of garbage

If garbage is not removed by the Local Authority for more than 4 days, the owner of premises shall inform the Secretary, Navua Rural Local Authority forthwith.

## by-law 15 Penalties

Any person who contravenes a provision of these By-laws commits an offence and is liable to a fine not exceeding $200 and, if the offence is a continuing one to a further fine not exceeding $80 each day or part of a day during which the offence has continued.
