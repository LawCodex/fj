# NASINU (GARBAGE REMOVAL AND DISPOSAL) BY-LAWS 2000

Nasinu (Garbage Removal and Disposal) By-laws 2000 (LN 112 of 2000) commenced on 26 October 2000, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Nasinu (Garbage Removal and Disposal) (Amendment) By-laws 2010 (LN 23 of 2010) | 26 February 2010 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Nasinu (Garbage Removal and Disposal) By-laws 2000.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**garbage** includes household refuse, empty tins, rubbish, refuse, house-sweepings, ashes or any filth;

**local authority** means the local authority of the urban sanitary district of Nasinu; and

**sanitary inspector** means any officer for the time being holding the appointment of sanitary (health) inspector to the local authority or his or her deputy or assistants or any person authorised by the local authority for the purposes of these By-laws.

## by-law 3 Application

These By-laws apply to the urban sanitary district of Nasinu.

## by-law 4 Responsibility of local authority

The local authority is responsible for the administration and enforcement of these By-laws.

## by-law 5 Works to be undertaken by local authority

The local authority may undertake itself or by contract, the collection, removal and disposal of garbage from houses and premises.

## by-law 6 Fees to be charged for garbage collection, removal and disposal services

(1) The local authority may charge a maximum fee of $18 per garbage pan for every 3 months and such fee shall be paid in advance to the local authority by the owner of the house, building or other premises in respect of which any garbage pan is used or have been prescribed whether such garbage pan is used or not.

[para (1) am LN 23 of 2010 by-law 2, 26 February 2010]

(2) Any fee charged under paragraph (1) must be by demand and must be served upon the owner of the premises stating the period in respect of which the fee is charged and the date by which it must be paid.

(3) If any premises have remained unoccupied for not less than 3 months, the local authority may on application of the owner, make a proportionate adjustment of the fee payable, or a proportionate refund of the fee paid.

(4) If any fee due and payable under this by-law is not paid to the local authority within the time specified in the notice required under paragraph (2) , a Magistrate may, upon application made by the local authority, issue a warrant levying the amount with costs by distress and sale of the goods and chattels of the owner found on the premises.

(5) A warrant issued under paragraph (4) authorises the person executing it or his or her assistant to remain on the premises during the interval between the distress and the sale whether or not such authority is expressed in the warrant.

(6) Paragraph (4) is without prejudice to the rights of the local authority to recover such fee by ordinary civil proceedings.

(7) The local authority, with the approval of the Board may by resolution, waive the whole or any part of any fee due under this by-law and any costs incurred for the purpose of recovering that fee, if it appears to the local authority that such fee is irrecoverable or that it should be written off on the grounds of poverty of the owner.

(8) If a resolution is made under paragraph (7) in respect of a fee due in which a warrant issued under paragraph (4) , the warrant is deemed to be cancelled.

(9) All fees collected by the local authority under this by-law must be paid into the municipal fund.

## by-law 7 Garbage pans

Every owner or occupier of any house, residence, shop, building, premises or other place in the urban sanitary district of Nasinu must provide a sufficient number of garbage pans, each of a maximum capacity of 3 cubic feet and in accordance with the requirements Part 10 of the Public Health Regulations 1937, and must—

(a) at all times keep such garbage pans cover closed except when depositing garbage in them;

(b) keep the garbage pans and its covers clean and in a good condition;

(c) place the garbage pan in a position easily accessible to the Nasinu Town Council’s garbage collector and, when more than one is necessary, they must be placed together.

## by-law 9 Frequency of service

All garbage must be removed at least twice weekly.

## by-law 8 Compulsory use of garbage pans

Every occupier of premises must cause all garbage arising from such premises to be deposited in garbage pans.

## by-law 10 Garbage pans to be easily accessible

Garbage pans must be placed on the footpath adjoining each property adjacent to the road taken by the collecting vehicles on the days as the local authority prescribes or if there is no footpath then immediately in front of the property or at the *cul-de-sac* from where access is provided to the property.

## by-law 11 Penalties

Any person who contravenes these By-laws commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine of $10 and in the case of a continuing offence to a further fine of $4 for each day during which the offence continues after such conviction.
