# LAMI (GARBAGE REMOVAL AND DISPOSAL) BY-LAWS 2008

Lami Town (Garbage Removal and Disposal) By-laws 2008 (LN 124 of 2008) commenced on 31 October 2008, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Lami (Garbage Removal and Disposal) By-laws 2008.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Council** means the Lami Town Council;

**garbage** includes household refuse, empty tins, rubbish, refuse, house sweepings, ashes or any filth; and

**sanitary inspector** means an officer appointed under section 11 of the Public Health Act 1935, and includes the deputy or assistant sanitary inspector.

## by-law 3 Application and administration

(1) These By-laws apply to the areas within Lami town declared and constituted under the Local Government Act 1972.

(2) The Council is responsible for the administration and enforcement of these By-laws.

## by-law 4 Garbage collection fees

(1) The Council may charge a fee of $50 for every 3 months for the collection, removal and disposal of garbage for any premises.

(2) The owner of any premises is liable to pay in advance to the Council the fee charged under paragraph (1) for the purposes of collection, removal and disposal of garbage from such premises regardless on whether or not a garbage pan is used.

(3) The Council must issue and serve a written notice to the owner of premises—

(a) specifying the amount to be paid pursuant to paragraph (1) ;

(b) stating which 3 month period the amount relates;

(c) the date and place by which the amount must be paid; and

(d) demanding the payment of such amount.

(4) If any premises remain unoccupied for less than 3 months, the Council may upon application by the owner, make a proportionate adjustment of the fee payable or a proportionate refund of the fee paid.

## by-law 5 Warrant for distress and sale of goods

(1) If the owner of any premises fails to comply with the notice under paragraph (3) , the Council may apply to a Magistrate to issue a warrant levying the amount with costs by distress and sale of the goods and chattels of the owner found on the premises.

(2) A warrant issued under paragraph (5) authorises the person executing, including any other person assisting the person so authorised to remain on the premises during the interval between the distress and the sale whether or not such authority is expressed in the warrant.

(3) Despite paragraph (4) , the Council may opt to recover such fee by other remedies, including civil proceedings in court.

## by-law 6 Waiving of fees

(1) If the Council is satisfied that—

(a) a fee due and payable cannot be recovered; or

(b) the fee due and payable should be written off on the grounds of poverty of the owner.

the Council may, with the approval of the Board, waive or write off whole or any part of any fee due under these By-laws and any costs incurred for the purpose of recovering that fee.

(2) A warrant issued under by-law 5 is deemed to be cancelled, if the fee in respect of which the warrant was issued is waived under by-law 6.

## by-law 7 Fees payable to Council fund

All fees collected by the Council under this by-law must be paid into its municipal fund.

## by-law 8 Owner to provide and use garbage pans

(1) The owner of any premises must—

(a) provide one or more garbage pans, each of a minimum capacity of one cubic metre and in accordance with the requirements of Part 10 of the Public Health Regulations 1937;

(b) at all times keep the lid of the garbage pan except when depositing garbage in the garbage pan;

(c) keep the garbage pan and its covers clean and in good condition; and

(d) place the garbage pan in a position easily accessible for collection by employees or the Council’s garbage collectors and, when more than one is necessary, such pans must be placed together close to the road curb.

(2) The owner of premises must cause all garbage arising from such premises to be deposited in garbage pans.

## by-law 9 Council may provide garbage pans

(1) The Council may supply garbage pans to premises within its areas for the purpose of standardising and improving its service delivery.

(2) If garbage pans are supplied under paragraph (1) , the owner of such premises shall accept, pay for and use such pans.

## by-law 10 Garbage pans to be easily accessible

Garbage pans must be placed on the footpath adjoining each property adjacent to the road used by the collection vehicles on the days specified by the Council, unless if there is no footpath then immediately in front of the property or at a cul-de-sac from where access is provided to the premises.

## by-law 11 Frequency of service

(1) The Council must arrange that all garbage must be removed by it at least twice a week.

(2) The garbage collector may not collect and remove any garbage pan placed at a distance of more than 6 metres from the road curb.

## by-law 12 No disposal on vacant lands etc

(1) No person shall throw or deposit upon any, pavement, roadway, vacant land, foreshore, stream or creek within the Council area any garbage.

(2) The owner of premises commits an offence if any accumulations garbage other than that contained in a garbage pan is found on such premises.

(3) For the purpose of subsection (2) , if there are 2 or more owners, the owners jointly and severally commit the offence.

## by-law 13 Notification of non-removal of garbage

If garbage is not removed by the Council for more than 4 days, the owner of premises shall inform the town clerk or Health Section of the council forthwith.

## by-law 14 Penalties

Any person who contravenes a provision of these By-laws commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200 and, if the offence is a continuing one to a further fine not exceeding $4 for each day or part of a day during which the offence has continued.
