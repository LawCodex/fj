## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Public Health (Infectious Diseases) Regulations 2020.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the date of publication in the Gazette.

## reg 2 Failure to comply

Any person who fails to comply with an order, prohibition, declaration or directive issued pursuant to section 69(1)(c) or (3) of the Public Health Act 1935 commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $10,000 or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 5 years or both.

## reg 3 Fees

(1) The fees charged in relation to Part 7 of the Act, except the accommodation fees for persons quarantined or isolated in designated centres, hospitals, camps or stations, are specified in the Schedule.

(2) Any person quarantined or isolated in a designated centre, hospital, camp or station, following entry into Fiji must pay the cost of accommodation to the designated centre, hospital, camp or station.

(3) In this regulation, the cost of accommodation includes any related costs of the designated centre, hospital, camp or station.

[reg 3 subst LN 17 of 2021 reg 2, 5 March 2021]

## reg 4 Exemptions

(1) Any person who is a citizen of Fiji and is ordinarily resident in Fiji is exempt from paying any fees under these Regulations on entry into Fiji if—

(a) the person travelled outside of Fiji before 28 March 2020;

(b) the person is a patient that was referred for medical treatment outside of Fiji by the Ministry of Health and Medical Services; or

(c) the person is endorsed by the Ministry of Health and Medical Services to accompany the patient under paragraph (b).

(2) Any person who is a citizen of Fiji is exempt from paying any fees under these Regulations on entry into Fiji if—

(a) the person is a student studying outside of Fiji on a Government scholarship or Government endorsed scholarship, returning to Fiji on the completion of the student’s studies;

(b) the person and the student under paragraph (a) are financially dependent on each other or the person is an immediate family member of the student returning to Fiji with the student on the completion of the student’s studies; or

(c) the person is employed under a government to government arrangement that the Fiji Government is a party to.

[reg 4 insrt LN 17 of 2021 reg 3, 5 March 2021]

## reg 5 Waiver of fees

The Permanent Secretary may, on application, waive or refund the whole or any part of a fee under these Regulations subject to the conditions he or she considers necessary.

[reg 5 insrt LN 17 of 2021 reg 4, 23 July 2020]
