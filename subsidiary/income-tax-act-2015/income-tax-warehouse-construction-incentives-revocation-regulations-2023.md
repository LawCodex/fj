## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Income Tax (Warehouse Construction Incentives) (Revocation) Regulations 2023.

(2) These Regulations are deemed to have come into force on 1 July 2023.

## reg 2 Revocation

The Income Tax (Warehouse Construction Incentives) Regulations 2019 are revoked.

## reg 3 Savings and transitional

Notwithstanding the revocation of the Income Tax (Warehouse Construction Incentives) Regulations 2019, applications received prior to 1 July 2023 will still be processed.
