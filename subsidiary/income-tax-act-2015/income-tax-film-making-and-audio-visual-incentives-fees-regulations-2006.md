# INCOME TAX (FILM-MAKING AND AUDIO-VISUAL INCENTIVES) (FEES) REGULATIONS 2006

**Table of Amendments**

Income Tax (Film-making and Audio-visual Incentives) (Fees) Regulations 2006 (LN 28 of 2006) commenced on 1 January 2006, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Income Tax (Film-making and Audio-visual Incentives) (Fees) Regulations 2006 and are deemed to have come into force on 1 January 2006.

[reg 1 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 109, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 2 Income Tax (Film-making and Audio-visual Incentives) (Fees)

The fees (exclusive of VAT) payable for the purposes of the Income Tax (Film-making and Audio-visual Incentives) Regulations 2016 are set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

[reg 2 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 109, effective 1 December 2016]
