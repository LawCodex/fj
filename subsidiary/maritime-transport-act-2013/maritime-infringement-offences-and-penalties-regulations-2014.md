## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [regs 1–5]

### reg 1 Short title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Maritime (Infringement Offences and Penalties) Regulations 2014 and shall come into force on a date or dates appointed by the Minister by notice in the Gazette.

### reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Act** means the Maritime Transport Act 2013;

**Authorised Officer** shall have the same meaning as in section 2 of the Act;

**Authority** means the Maritime Safety Authority of Fiji;

**cargo manifest** means the document containing the number of tonnage of cargo carried on board a ship which is usually checked before departure and upon arrival;

**Chief Executive Officer** means the Chief Executive Officer of the Authority;

**infringement notice** means the notice set out in Schedule 2;

**infringement offence** shall have the same meaning as in section 262(1) of the Act; and

**letter of dispensation** means a letter issued by the Authority to any shipping company that applies for dispensation.

### reg 3 Objective

The objective of these Regulations is to prescribe the infringement offences under the Act, the maritime regulations and the marine environment protection regulations.

### reg 4 Infringement offences and penalties

In accordance with section 262 of the Act, Schedule 1 sets out the infringement offences and penalties.

### reg 5 Infringement notice

In accordance with section 262 of the Act, an authorised officer may issue an infringement notice set out in Schedule 2, to a person who commits an infringement offence under the relevant provisions in the maritime regulations.

Statement of rights

Note: If, after reading these notes, you do not understand anything in the notes, you should consult a lawyer immediately.

#### reg 1.

This notice sets out one or more alleged infringement offences. Each offence that is identified by a number is a separate infringement offence. You may decide what to do in respect of each alleged offence individually, and may act in the same way in respect of all alleged offences or in different ways in respect of different alleged offences, as set out below.

#### reg 2. Payments

If you pay the infringement penalty for an alleged offence within 28 days of the service on you of this notice, no further enforcement action will be taken for that offence. Payments should be made at places indicated on the front page of this notice.

#### reg 3. Defence

You have a complete defence against proceedings for an alleged offence if the infringement penalty for that offence has been paid to the enforcement authority at the address shown on the front page of this notice within 28 days after service on you of a reminder notice. Late payment or payment made to any other address will not constitute a defence.

#### reg 4. Further action

If you wish to—

(a) raise any matter relating to the circumstances of an alleged offence for consideration by the enforcement authority;

(b) deny liability for the offence and request a court hearing (refer to paragraphs 5 and 9); or

(c) admit liability for the offence, but wish to have a court consider written submissions as to penalty or otherwise (refer to paragraphs 6 and 9),

you should write to the enforcement authority at the address shown on the front page of this notice. Any such letter should be personally signed.

#### reg 5.

If you deny liability for the offence and request a hearing, the enforcement authority will, unless it decides not to commence court proceedings in respect of the offence, serve you with a notice of hearing setting out the place and time at which the matter will be heard by the court.

Note: That if the court finds you guilty of the offence, costs will be imposed in addition to any penalty.

#### reg 6.

If you admit liability for the offence but want the court to consider your submissions as to penalty or otherwise, you should in your letter to the enforcement authority—

(a) request a hearing;

(b) admit liability; and

(c) set out the written submissions you wish to be considered by the court.

The enforcement authority will then file your letter with the court (unless it decides not to commence court proceedings in respect of the offence). There is no provision for an oral hearing before the court if you follow this course of action.

Note: That costs will be imposed in addition to any penalty.

#### reg 7. Non-payment of penalty

If you do not pay the infringement penalty and do not request a hearing in respect of an alleged offence within 28 days after the service on you of this notice you will (unless the enforcement authority decides otherwise) be served with a reminder notice.

#### reg 8.

If you do not pay the infringement penalty and do not request a hearing in respect of the alleged offence within 28 days after being served with the reminder notice, you will become liable to pay COSTS IN ADDITION TO THE INFRINGEMENT PENALTY, unless the enforcement authority decides not to commence proceedings against you.

#### reg 9. Queries/correspondence

When writing or making payment of an infringement penalty, please indicate—

(a) the date of the infringement offence;

(b) the infringement notice number;

(c) the identifying number of each alleged offence and the course of action you are taking in respect of it (if this notice sets out more than one offence and you are not paying all the infringement penalties for all the alleged offences); and

(d) your address for replies (if you are not paying all the infringement penalties for all the alleged offences).

If it is not clear which alleged offence any payment relates to, your payment may be treated as relating to the alleged offences in the order in which they are set out on the front page of this notice.

Note: All queries and/or correspondence regarding the infringement offence(s) must be directed to the enforcement authority named in this notice at the address shown.
