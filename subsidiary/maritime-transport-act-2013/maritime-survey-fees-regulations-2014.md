## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [regs 1–3]

### reg 1 Short title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Maritime (Survey Fees) Regulations 2014 and shall come into force on a date appointed by the Minister by notice in the Gazette.

### reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Act** means the Maritime Transport Act 2013;

**Authority** means the Maritime Safety Authority of Fiji;

**surveying service** means a surveying service as specified under Schedule 1; and

**surveyor** means a person appointed or recognised by the Chief Executive Officer of the Authority.

### reg 3 Purpose

The purpose of these Regulations is to specify the fees to be paid in respect of surveying services provided by the Authority.

## PART 2 SURVEY FEES REQUIREMENTS [regs 4–13]

### reg 4 Survey fees

(1) The fees charged for a surveying service specified in Schedule 1 shall be calculated according to the number of hours a surveyor is employed in undertaking a surveying service.

(2) For any surveying service provided, a minimum number of hours as shown in Schedule 1, shall be charged and must be paid before a surveying service is undertaken.

(3) If a surveyor is engaged in undertaking a surveying service for more than the minimum number of hours required under subregulation (2), an additional charge for each additional half hour, or part thereof, during which he or she is so engaged, shall be charged and must be paid before any certificate or other document can be issued as a result of the survey.

### reg 5 Surveyor’s hourly rate

(1) Subject to subregulation (2), the charge for each half hour that a surveyor is engaged in undertaking a surveying service specified in Schedule 1 or in travelling in connection with a surveying service shall be as follows—

(a) $17.39 (VEP) in the case of a—

(i) registered ship or a ship that may be registered; or

(ii) a ship under construction that is to be or could be registered on completion; and

(b) $21.74 (VEP) in any other case.

(2) Where a surveyor—

(a) carries out a surveying service outside normal working hours; or

(b) travels in connection with a surveying service specified, outside normal working hours,

the hourly rate in respect of the service or travel carried out outside normal working hours shall be double that rate as prescribed under subregulation (1).

### reg 6 Travelling time

(1) Where a surveyor travels from his or her normal place of employment to another place to carry out a surveying service, there shall be charged, in addition to the fee specified for that surveying service, a charge for the surveyor’s travelling time.

(2) The travelling time referred to in subregulation (1) shall be charged in accordance with the following—

### reg 7 Travel and subsistence expenses

A person for whom a surveying service is carried out shall pay to the Authority a charge equal to any cost incurred by the Authority in respect of the travel, meals and accommodation of the surveyor while carrying out that surveying service.

### reg 8 Inspections in respect of hulls, engines and safety equipment

(1) Subject to subregulation (2), the survey fee payable in respect of an inspection of a ship’s hull, engines or safety equipment shall be in respect of one visit to the ship by a surveyor.

(2) If, on a visit by a surveyor in accordance with subregulation (1), a ship is not ready for completion, the surveyor shall issue a defect list to the ship’s owner and shall make a second visit to the ship to ascertain whether the defects specified in the list have been rectified.

(3) A visit in accordance with subregulation (2) shall be free however, any subsequent visit shall charge a fee in accordance with Schedule 1.

### reg 9 Progressive surveys of machinery

(1) Prior to the construction of a ship, a ship owner shall submit for assessment and approval all required ship plans and drawings.

(2) The construction of a ship shall be surveyed progressively with the hull and each unit of machinery being surveyed by way of a special survey shall incur a fee in accordance with Item 6 of Schedule 1.

(3) A Certificate of Survey in respect of a ship must not be granted in respect of any period which extends beyond the estimated date that any unit of machinery on the ship becomes out of survey.

### reg 10 Tonnage measurement surveys

(1) The fees specified in Schedule 2 shall apply in respect of a tonnage measurement survey of a ship.

(2) Before a tonnage measurement survey is carried out, a fee calculated at the rate of $3.48 (VEP) for each metre of the overall length of the ship must be paid.

(3) The fee paid under subregulation (2) shall be deducted from the fee payable on completion of the survey as calculated in accordance with Schedule 2.

(4) Where adequate ship plans are not available during the time taken in carrying out any necessary extra measurement of the ship, the ship owner or operator shall incur a fee at the rate applicable to a special survey in accordance with Item 6 of Schedule 1.

### reg 11 Load line surveys

(1) The fees specified in Schedule 3 shall apply in respect of a load line survey carried out on a ship whose flag State is not a party to the Load Line Convention and, must be paid before the survey is carried out.

(2) Where adequate ships’ plans are not available, any time taken in carrying out any extra measurement of the ship, shall be charged to the ship owner or operator at the rate applicable to a special survey in accordance with Item 6 of Schedule 1.

### reg 12 Inclining test

(1) The fees specified in Schedule 4 shall apply in respect of the provisions of a surveyor to supervise an inclining test on a ship and the provision of any approved stability information in respect of the ship.

(2) The fees specified in Schedule 4 shall not cover the provision of any weight or personnel, other than the surveyor.

### reg 13 Exemption

Notwithstanding the provisions of these Regulations, the Chief Executive Officer may, after consultation with the Minister, by written notice grant an exemption in accordance with section 33 of the Act for the payment of prescribed fees under these Regulations.
