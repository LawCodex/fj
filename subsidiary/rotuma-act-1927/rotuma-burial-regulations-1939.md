# ROTUMA (BURIAL) REGULATIONS 1939

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Rotuma (Burial) Regulations 1939.

## reg 2 Cemeteries

The places in Rotuma where the dead may be buried (hereinafter referred to as cemeteries) shall be determined by and be under the control and supervision of the Council.

[reg 2 subst LN 113 of 1964 reg 2, effective 13 November 1964]

## reg 3 Cemetery boundaries

The boundaries of such cemeteries shall be marked in a suitable manner.

[reg 3 am LN 113 of 1964 reg 3, effective 13 November 1964]

## reg 4 Depth of graves

All graves shall be excavated to a depth of not less than 4 feet.

## reg 5 Finding of dead body

Any person who finds a dead body shall forthwith report the fact to the chief of the district who shall thereupon examine the body and report the result to the medical officer and the District Officer, and the District Officer shall give permission in writing for the body to be buried.

## reg 6 Building inside burial ground

The erection of any dwelling house within the boundaries of a cemetery in use is hereby prohibited.

[reg 6 am LN 113 of 1964 reg 4, effective 13 November 1964]

## reg 7 Re-opening of graves

No grave shall be re-opened for the purpose of burying another body until the expiry of 10 years from the date of the earlier burial.

[reg 7 insrt LN 113 of 1964 reg 5, effective 13 November 1964]

## reg 8 Penalty

Any person contravening the provisions of these Regulations shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $10 and in default to imprisonment not exceeding 2 months.
