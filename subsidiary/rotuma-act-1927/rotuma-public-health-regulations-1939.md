## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Rotuma (Public Health) Regulations 1939.

## PART 1 DWELLING HOUSE [regs 2–11]

### reg 2 Houses to be above ground

The floor level of every dwelling house shall be not less than one foot above the ground.

[reg 2 subst LN 111 of 1964 reg 2, effective 13 November 1964]

### reg 3 Flooring of houses

Every dwelling house shall be provided with either a wooden floor or a concrete floor or a floor upon which coral or shingle has been laid. In the case of a coral or shingle covered floor, coconut leaves and mats shall be used as additional covering.

[reg 3 subst LN 111 of 1964 reg 3, effective 13 November 1964]

### reg 4 Refuse not to be used for filling

No refuse shall be used in filling in any house foundation.

[reg 4 subst LN 111 of 1964 reg 4, effective 13 November 1964]

### reg 5 Kitchen

Every dwelling shall be provided with kitchen accommodation which, if separate from the dwelling house, shall not be less than 12 feet long and 8 feet wide with walls not less than 5 feet high.

[reg 5 am LN 32 of 1961 reg 2, effective 21 April 1961; LN 111 of 1964 reg 5, effective 13 November 1964]

### reg 6 Land to be kept clean

It shall be the duty of the owners or occupiers of land within any village at all times to keep such land clean and free from weeds, rubbish and offensive matter.

### reg 7 Latrine

Every dwelling house shall be provided with a latrine and no latrine shall be erected within 30 feet of any dwelling house or road or in any other place to which the medical officer may object. No latrine may be constructed on a beach except with the express prior approval of the medical officer.

[reg 7 am LN 111 of 1964 reg 6, effective 13 November 1964; LN 235 of 1966 reg 2, effective 23 December 1966]

### reg 8 Specifications for latrine

Each latrine shall have a floor area of not less than 12 square feet being provided with a removable pan or being built over a pit over which a concrete slab or wooden seat adequately covered and fly-proofed shall be placed.

[reg 8 subst LN 111 of 1964 reg 7, effective 13 November 1964]

### reg 9 Specifications for dwelling house

Every dwelling house shall have at least 2 doors and 2 windows and shall be not less than 24 feet long and 15 feet wide with walls not less than 5 feet high.

[reg 9 subst LN 111 of 1964 reg 8, effective 13 November 1964]

### reg 10 Erection of dwelling houses

No person shall erect a dwelling house without the prior approval of the chief of the district in which it is proposed that the dwelling house shall be erected and, before approval shall be given by such chief to the proposed erection, he or she shall consult with the medical officer.

[reg 10 subst LN 111 of 1964 reg 9, effective 13 November 1964]

### reg 11 Penalty

Any person who fails to carry out any order so given to him or her or who fails to comply with any of the provisions of this Part shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $4 and in default to imprisonment for any period not exceeding one month.

## PART 2 SANITATION [regs 12–18]

### reg 12 Food plantations

There shall be no food plantations within any village.

### reg 13 Bush and scrub

All bush and scrub shall be cleared for a distance of at least 20 yards, round every village.

### reg 14 Space around dwelling house

There shall be a free and unobstructed space between the sides and backs of all dwelling houses of not less than 20 feet.

### reg 15 Guttering

All houses roofed with material other than thatch shall be provided with guttering which shall be so constructed that water can flow freely into the down-pipes. Gutters and down-pipes shall be kept in good repair and free from obstruction.

### reg 16 Mosquito control

Every occupier of premises on which is situated any barrel, tank or cistern, or other receptacle for the collection or storage of water shall fit such receptacle with a sufficient cover or screen so as to prevent the ingress of mosquitoes and shall at all times maintain the said barrel, tank, cistern or other receptacle in a clean condition to the satisfaction of the District Officer or medical officer.

### reg 17 Surface drains

In every village such drains shall be provided for carrying off surface water from any part of such village as the District Officer or medical officer may think fit.

### reg 18 Penalty

Any person who fails to comply with any of the provisions of this Part shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $4 and in default to imprisonment for any period not exceeding one month.

## PART 3 INFECTIOUS DISEASES [regs 19–29]

### reg 19 “Infectious disease” defined

In this Part, “infectious disease” means cholera, plague, yellow fever, small-pox, diphtheria, typhoid fever, croup, puerperal fever, dysentery, pulmonary tuberculosis, measles, mumps, whooping-cough, venereal disease and any other disease which the Minister may see fit by order to declare temporarily or permanently to be an infectious disease within the meaning of this Part.

### reg 20 Notification of infectious disease

All infectious diseases shall be at once notified, in the case of a sick child by the parent or guardian and in the case of a sick adult by the householder in whose house the illness occurs or by any other adult inmate of the house, to the medical officer, who shall inform the District Officer.

### reg 21 Isolation of person suffering from infectious disease

The District Officer or medical officer shall have power to order the isolation or removal to hospital of persons suffering from any infectious disease and no person so isolated or removed to hospital shall leave or be taken from the place where he or she has been isolated or leave the hospital or be taken from it unless ordered to do so by the medical officer.

### reg 22 Isolation of person who has been in contact with infectious disease

Any person who has been in contact with a person suffering from any infectious disease or who is known to have rendered himself or herself liable in any other way to infection by any infectious disease shall, if so ordered by the medical officer, remain in his or her town or in such place as the medical officer may direct for such period not exceeding 14 days as the medical officer may direct.

### reg 23 Destruction of infected house or article

The District Officer or medical officer may order the removal, disinfection or destruction of houses or anything therein that has been exposed to infection.

### reg 24 Children with disease

No person, being the parent or having the care of a child who is or has been suffering from infectious disease, shall permit the child to attend school unless the child has been certified to be free from infection by the medical officer.

### reg 25 Body to be removed for burial only

If any person shall die from any infectious disease in any hospital or place of isolation for the sick, the body shall not be removed from the hospital or place of isolation aforesaid, except for the purpose of immediate burial, without the authority of the medical officer in charge.

### reg 26 Restriction on custom

When a person dies from an infectious disease, the custom of kissing or handling the body more than is absolutely necessary is prohibited.

### reg 27 Burial to be without delay

There shall be no delay in the burial of bodies after death from infectious disease. The District Officer or medical officer shall have power to order the immediate burial of bodies of persons who have died from infectious disease and to direct where such bodies shall be buried. Any person who, being responsible for the burial of any such body, refuses or neglects to comply with any such order as aforesaid shall be guilty of a breach of these Regulations.

### reg 28 Quarantine of area

The District Officer or medical officer may order any locality or district to be quarantined or isolated if in his or her opinion the spread of any infectious disease can thereby be checked and any persons entering or leaving such locality or district against the orders of the District Officer or the medical officer shall be guilty of a breach of these Regulations.

### reg 29 Penalty

Any person who knowingly commits a breach of any of the provisions of this Part shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $40 or in default of payment to imprisonment for any period not exceeding 4 months.
