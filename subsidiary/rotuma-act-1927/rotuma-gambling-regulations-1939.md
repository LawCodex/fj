## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Rotuma (Gambling) Regulations 1939.

## reg 2 Penalty for gambling

Any person who plays any game for money or for property or for anything representing money or property whether with cards or with any contrivance used for any such purpose shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $2 or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 2 weeks.
