# ROTUMA (COCONUT PLANTATION) REGULATIONS 1939

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Rotuma (Coconut Plantation) Regulations 1939.

## reg 2 Weeding

During such periods as the Council shall annually, by resolution, order a general clearing of weeds and bush between the trees of coconut plantations is to be carried out and completed during the said periods by all owners of plantations and all those who have family claims on such plantations.

[reg 2 am LN 52 of 1964 reg 2, effective 26 June 1964]

## reg 3 Extent of weeding

During such clearing periods all weeds and bush between the coconut trees shall be cut to ground level.

[reg 3 am LN 52 of 1964 reg 3, effective 26 June 1964]

## reg 4 Persons to assist in weeding

Every able-bodied man between the ages of 16 and 60 years who is the owner of a coconut plantation or who has by family rights a claim on the said plantation shall assist in this general clearing.

## reg 5 Coconut husks to be burnt or buried

Unless the District Officer shall otherwise order, all coconut husks shall be burnt or buried.

[reg 5 insrt LN 52 of 1964 reg 4, effective 26 June 1964]

## reg 6 Penalty

Every person who contravenes or fails to comply with any provision of these Regulations shall on complaint of the chief of his or her district be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $4 and in default to imprisonment for any period not exceeding 6 weeks.
