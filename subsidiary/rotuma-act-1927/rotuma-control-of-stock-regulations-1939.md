## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Rotuma (Control of Stock) Regulations 1939.

## reg 2 Definition

For the purposes of these Regulations the term “pigs” shall not include young pigs of less than 6 months of age.

## reg 3 Pig at large may be killed

Any person who finds a pig at large may kill it and any person who kills a pig as above sanctioned shall, if he or she knows the owner, tell him or her to remove the carcass; if, however, he or she cannot ascertain who the owner is, he or she may eat the carcass.

## reg 4 Failure to inquire for owner

Any person who, having killed a pig as above sanctioned, eats the same without making reasonable inquiry as to the ownership of such pig shall, on complaint of the owner, pay to such owner the value of the pig or such compensation as the court may award.

## reg 5 Restriction on keeping of pigs

All pigs shall be kept within fences not less than 4 feet high. No enclosure for keeping pigs shall, with effect from 1 January 1965, be built within 200 yards of any dwelling. Any person erecting a pig fence within 200 yards of any dwelling may be ordered to remove the same by the District Officer and in addition shall be liable to any fine or penalty provided in these Regulations. It shall be the duty of any person owning pigs to ensure the cleanliness of the pens and good condition of the fences. Each pen shall have a suitable shed for shelter.

[reg 5 am LN 33 of 1961 reg 2, effective 21 April 1961; LN 110 of 1964 reg 2, effective 13 November 1964]

## reg 6 Compensation for damage and impounding of animals

Horses, goats and cattle found at large may be impounded and any person whose property has been injured by horses, goats, cattle or pigs may recover damage from the owner of the animal.

[reg 6 am LN 110 of 1964 reg 3, effective 13 November 1964]

## reg 7 Penalty

Any person who contravenes or fails to comply with any provision of these Regulations shall on conviction be liable to a fine not exceeding $2 and in default to imprisonment for any period not exceeding one month.
