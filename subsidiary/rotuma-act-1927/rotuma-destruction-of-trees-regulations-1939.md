## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Rotuma (Destruction of Trees) Regulations 1939.

## reg 2 Destruction of trees near the beach

Any person who cuts down useful trees within 100 yards of the beach without the consent of the District Officer shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $2 and in default to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 2 weeks.

## reg 3 “Useful trees” defined

For the purpose of these Regulations “useful trees” are defined as “hefau”, “pinau”, “toa”, “niu”, “ifi” and “ulu”.
