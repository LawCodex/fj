## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Fiji National Training (Trade Testing) Regulations 1976.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**appropriate form** means a form approved by the Minister for use in any particular case; and

**trade test** means such test approved by the Minister, after consultation with the Council, as he or she may consider desirable for the purpose of determining the competence of a person engaged in any trade specified in Schedule 1.

## reg 3 Application

A person wishing to take a trade test shall apply on the appropriate form and shall pay the fees set out in Schedule 2, provided that no application shall, without the written approval of the Director, be accepted unless—

(a) in the case of an applicant for a Class III trade test, he or she has completed at least one year’s employment in the trade;

(b) in the case of an applicant for a Class II trade test, he or she has completed at least 3 years employment in the trade, and he or she has passed his or her Class III trade test;

(c) in the case of an applicant for a Class I trade test, he or she has completed either a contract of apprenticeship in the trade under the Act or at least 5 years employment in the trade and has passed his or her Class II trade test.

## reg 4 Trade test

A trade test shall be in the form and subject to the conditions prescribed by the Minister, after consultation with the Council.

## reg 5 Certificate

An applicant, after satisfying the Director that he or she has reached the required standard in a trade test, shall be granted a certificate in the appropriate form signed by the Director or by an officer authorised by him or her in writing, and by the officer examining the applicant in the trade test.

## reg 6 Register of certificates

The Director shall cause a register or registers to be kept in the appropriate form of certificates issued under regulation 5.

## reg 7 Offences

Any person who—

(a) makes use of a certificate to which he or she is not entitled under these Regulations;

(b) being entitled to a certificate under these Regulations, parts with possession of it to a person not so entitled;

(c) alters or defaces a certificate issued under these Regulations, or makes any model, likeness or facsimile of such certificate with intent to deceive or defraud; or

(d) fails to produce his or her certificate without good cause when the Director or a trade testing officer requires him or her to do so,

shall be guilty of an offence under the Act.

### APPROVED TRADE TESTS

**Prescribed by the Minister** [LN 14 of 1997 am LN 98 of 1978, effective 21 July 1978]

Note on general safety

In the theoretical test, questions will be asked on safety topics of the relevant syllabus. Marks will also be awarded for the correct observance of safety procedures during the practical test. Candidates are therefore expected to have a thorough knowledge of the current safety regulations in force in their workshops or sites, and should be able to take the necessary precautionary measures against the hazards of using tools, machines, lifting gears and other equipment related to their trades.

Candidates will be expected to pay particular importance to cleanliness and tidiness when performing the various operations during the practical test, and all tools to be employed in the test should be in good condition.

System of measurement

The imperial system of measurement will be used in all the practical tests until further notice.

However, trade tests using the metric system could be arranged if specifically requested.
