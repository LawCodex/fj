## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Fiji National Training Levy (Designation) Order 1988.

## O 2 Designation

I have designated the following co-operatives to be co-operatives to which the provisions of order 3(6) of the Fiji National Training Levy Order 1988 shall not apply—

Rotuma Co-operative Association;

Fiji Co-operative Association Limited.
