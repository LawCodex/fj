## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fiji National Training (Remuneration of Apprentices) Order 2003.

(2) This Order is deemed to have come into force on 3 February 2003.

## O 2 Designated trades or occupations

All trades or occupations are designated trades or occupations for the purposes of this Order.

## O 3 Minimum remuneration

The minimum rates of remuneration of apprentices in any designated trade or occupation shall be as follows—
