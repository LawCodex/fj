# MEDICAL ASSISTANTS REGULATIONS 1978

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Medical Assistants Regulations 1978.

## reg 2 Postings

A medical assistant shall discharge his or her duties only within the defined limits of the area to which he or she is posted from time to time.

## reg 3 Certificates for incapacity for work

In any case where there is no medical practitioner available, a medical assistant may issue a certificate of temporary incapacity for work, provided that a medical assistant shall not issue to any one person a certificate or certificates of incapacity for work for a period exceeding in the aggregate 7 days in any month.

## reg 4 Certificates of death

In any case where there is no medical practitioner available, a medical assistant may issue a certificate of death.

## reg 5 Reference to medical practitioner

A medical assistant shall without delay refer to a medical practitioner any person—

(a) in respect of whom he or she is unable to establish a diagnosis;

(b) whose condition demands immediate surgical intervention;

(c) whose condition does not respond to treatment within a reasonable time.

## reg 6 Duties of medical assistants

A medical assistant shall perform such medical and administrative functions as are assigned to him or her by the Permanent Secretary and, without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, may be required to undertake any or all of the following duties—

(a) in the absence of a medical practitioner, to undertake medicolegal work of a non-specialist nature, including the giving of medical evidence before any court, tribunal, commission or other body duly constituted under the provisions of any written law;

(b) to perform simple laboratory tests;

(c) to operate any portable x-ray unit and to interpret simple films;

(d) to assist registered nurses and midwives in the discharge of their duties;

(e) to provide advice, guidance and assistance in matters relating to family planning;

(f) to promote and participate in programmes relating to health education, disease control and sanitation;

(g) to form and maintain health records;

(h) to exercise administrative control over any medical unit or part thereof.

## reg 7 Issue of certificates etc

Every certificate or other document issued by a medical assistant in his or her professional capacity shall be signed by him or her with his or her usual signature and shall bear thereunder the words “Medical Assistant”.

## reg 8 Responsibilities of medical assistant

Every medical assistant shall accept and comply with the legal, ethical and moral responsibilities associated with the practice of medicine.
