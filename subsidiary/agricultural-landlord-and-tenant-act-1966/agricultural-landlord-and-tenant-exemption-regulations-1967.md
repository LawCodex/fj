## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Agricultural Landlord and Tenant (Exemption) Regulations 1967.

## reg 2 Exemptions from provisions of the Act

The provisions of the Act shall not apply—

(a) to any agricultural land—

(i) occupied by or let to any person by reasons solely of his or her being a full-time bona fide employee of the landlord;

(ii) occupied by any person under an agreement not to pay rent whether in legal currency or in kind or in labour or in any other form whatsoever;

(iii) situated within the boundaries of any Government aerodrome (as delineated on Plan No PP39 and on Plan No PP40 held by the Director of Lands), a licensed aerodrome or radio communication station;

(iv) situated within the boundaries of the Lomaivuna settlement scheme at Lomaivuna in the Central Division;

(v) situated within such part or parts of—

(A) the tikina of Nuku and Serua in the province of Serua;

(B) the tikina of Namosi and Wainikoroiluva in the province of Namosi; and

(C) the tikina of Koro in the province of Lomaiviti, as have not been surveyed for the purpose of demarcating the boundaries of the holdings therein of land-holding units;

(vi) situated within the areas included in Lots 19A, 19B, 19C, 19D, 20A, 20B, 3B, 3C, 3D, 3E, 48A, 48B, 48C and 48D on iTaukei Lands Commission sheets Koro 1, 2 and 3;

(vii) situated within any area set aside as an iTaukei reserve by the iTaukei Land Trust Board notwithstanding that such area has not been proclaimed as such;

(viii) situated in the islands of Rabi or Rotuma;

(ix) required for use by light railways or tramways;

(x) required for use in any pilot irrigation scheme in the Rewa delta as delineated on Plans Nos PP51 and PP33 held by the Director of Lands;

(xi) situated in the area at Waila containing 1,289 acres and 16 perches being the residue of the land comprised in Certificate of Title Volume 58 Folio 5740 and being all the land comprised in Certificate of Title Volume 123 Folio 2273;

(xii) situated in the areas listed in the Schedule.

(b) to any contract in respect of agricultural land which is—

(i) a contract of agistment or a grazing licence for the grazing of sheep or goats;

(ii) a contract of agistment or a grazing licence for the grazing of horses and bovine animals where the number of animals grazed, excluding animals under the age of 12 months, does not exceed 6;

(iii) a contract of agistment under which the charge is calculated on the basis of each animal grazed and the party accepting animals for grazing accepts full responsibility for their safekeeping;

(iv) a licence granted in respect of any forest or any part thereof for any purpose and for any period.

[reg 2 am LN 62 of 1968 reg 2, effective 17 May 1968; LN 57 of 1969 reg 2, effective 28 March 1969; LN 57 of 1970 reg 2, effective 19 June 1970; LN 8 of 1973 reg 2, effective 26 January 1973; LN 42 of 2004 reg 2, effective 28 May 2004; Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, effective 1 March 2011]

## reg 3 Exemption from the provisions of sections 7 and 13 of Act

The provisions of sections 7 and 13 of the Act shall not apply to any contract of tenancy which has an unexpired term of 30 years or more to run from the commencement of the Act.

## reg 4 Exemptions from the provisions of sections 6, 7 and 13 of Act

The provisions of sections 6, 7 and 13 of the Act shall not apply to any agricultural land—

(a) situated within the boundaries of any city or town;

(b) situated outside such boundaries which the Director of Lands may, by notice published in the Gazette, declare to be land required for non-agricultural purposes;

(c) situated within any area outside the cities of Suva and Lautoka delineated on Plans Nos PP41 and PP42 held by the Director of Lands;

(d) approved by the Director of Town and Country Planning for subdivision for residential, industrial or commercial purposes.

## reg 5 Exemption from the provisions of sections 6, 7, 13 and 45 of Act

The provisions of sections 6, 7, 13 and 45 of the Act shall not apply to any agricultural land held in trust under a will or on intestacy—

(a) let or leased for a term not exceeding 10 years, such letting or leasing to commence within a period of 5 years from the date of death of the deceased; or

(b) let or leased under such a trust subsisting at the commencement of the Act, such letting or leasing having an unexpired term not exceeding 10 years from the latter date.

[reg 5 am LN 132 of 1977 reg 2, effective 1 September 1977]

## reg 5A Exemption from a provision of section 9 of the Act

Section 9(1)(e)(ii) of the Act shall not apply to any contract of tenancy of an agricultural holding subsisting at or after 29 December 1967 in relation to land where the Government or the iTaukei Land Trust Board is the landlord—

(a) for the purposes of dealing with the contract of tenancy by way of mortgage, charge, pledge or caveat; or

(b) if there is no breach of any lease condition or where such application to deal with the land is in accordance with any law.

[reg 5A insrt LN 95 of 2021 reg 2, effective 22 September 2021]

## reg 6 Exemption from the provisions of sections 22 and 24 of Act

The provisions of—

(a) section 22(1)(a);

(b) subsection (2) of that section; and

(c) section 24,

of the Act shall not apply to any lease issued in respect of any forest or any part thereof.
