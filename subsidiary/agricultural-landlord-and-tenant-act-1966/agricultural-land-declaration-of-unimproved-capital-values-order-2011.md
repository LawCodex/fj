# AGRICULTURAL LAND (DECLARATION OF UNIMPROVED CAPITAL VALUES) ORDER 2011

**Table of Amendments**

Agricultural Land (Declaration of Unimproved Capital Values) Order 2011 (LN 14 of 2012) commenced on 28 October 2011, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Agricultural Land (Declaration of Unimproved Capital Values) Order 2011 and shall be deemed to have come into force on 28 October 2011.

## O 2 Unimproved capital values

The different classes of agricultural land subject of an agricultural holding set out in the Schedule are declared to have the unimproved capital values specified therein.

## O 3 Repeal

The Agricultural Land (Declaration of Unimproved Capital Values) Order 1997 is hereby repealed.
