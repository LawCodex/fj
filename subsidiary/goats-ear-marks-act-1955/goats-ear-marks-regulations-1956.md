## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Goats (Ear-Marks) Regulations 1956.

## reg 2 Forms

Every application for registration of an ear-mark, certificate of registration, memorandum for the transfer of registration and application by a personal representative of a deceased owner for registration of an ear-mark shall be made in the appropriate form set out in Schedule 1.

## reg 3 Position of registered ear-mark

The position on the ear where a goat may be marked with a registered ear-mark shall be as follows—Reading round the ear from the head commencing at the front or tip of the ear—

| | | | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | Position A … | Front of the ear | |
| | Position B … | Tip of the ear | |
| | Position C … | Back of the ear | |
| | Position D … | Centre of the ear | |

[reg 3 am LN 79 of 1956 reg 2, effective 14 September 1956]

## reg 4 Distinguishing sexes

Male goats shall be marked in the left ear and female goats in the right ear.

## reg 5 Size of mark

The size of the ear-mark shall be sufficiently large to be clearly identifiable but shall in no case remove a greater portion than one-third of the ear.

## reg 6 Fees

The fees set out in Schedule 2 shall be paid in regard to the respective matters therein contained.
