# ITAUKEI LAND (TRUST FUNDS INVESTMENT) REGULATIONS 2004

**Table of Amendments** iTaukei Land (Trust Funds Investment) Regulations 2004 (LN 37 of 2004) commenced on 14 May 2004, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Fijian Affairs (Amendment) Decree 2010 (No 31 of 2010) | 2 July 2010 |
| Native Land Trust (Amendment) Decree 2011 (No 8 of 2011) | 1 March 2011 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the iTaukei Land (Trust Funds Investment) Regulations 2004.

[reg 1 am Decree 8 of 2011 s 3, effective 1 March 2011]

## reg 2 Power to invest and lend

Subject to section 14 of the act, the Board, may lend or invest any trust funds including rents, royalties or premiums in respect of leases, licences, concessions or any other binding agreements on iTaukei land, provided that there is always a legal security by way of a mortgage, charge or any other collateral arrangement that normally qualify under any normal bona fide commercial investment or lending transactions.

[reg 2 am Decree 8 of 2011 s 3, effective 1 March 2011]

## reg 3 Purpose to invest and lend

The purpose for such investment and lending is to utilise the trust funds to attain profit and guarantee maximum returns for the benefit of the iTaukei owners.

[reg 3 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

## reg 4 Type of trust funds to be invested

The Board may consider investment or lending in any of the following trust funds—

(a) unclaimed deposits in the Unclaimed Deposits Reserve fund as provided for under the iTaukei Land (Unclaimed Deposits) Regulations 1975;

(b) any other trust funds available as the Board considers appropriate in line with its functions under the Act;

(c) notwithstanding paragraph (a), if iTaukei owners have requested in writing for their trust funds to be invested and borrowed; or

(d) notwithstanding paragraph (b), any other trust funds that the Board is not satisfied will procure viable mortgage or collateral security, provided that the Board must first apply to the High Court under the Trustee Act 1966 for approval to invest or lend.

[reg 4 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010; Decree 8 of 2011 s 3, effective 1 March 2011]

## reg 6 Distribution of interest accrued

(1) The Board shall distribute the interest accrued from investment or lending of any trust funds after deduction of its administrative expenses in the same manner as stipulated under regulation 11 of the iTaukei Land (Leases and Licences) Regulations 1984.

[subreg (1) am Decree 8 of 2011 s 3, effective 1 March 2011]

(2) The administrative expenses under subregulation (1) is up to 25% on the total interest accrued from such trusts funds investments or lending and any other related ancillary costs incurred by the Board.
