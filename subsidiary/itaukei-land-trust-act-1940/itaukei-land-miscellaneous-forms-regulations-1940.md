## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the iTaukei Land (Miscellaneous Forms) Regulations 1940.

[reg 1 am Decree 8 of 2011 s 3, effective 1 March 2011]

## reg 2 Form of consent of iTaukei owners

The consent of the iTaukei owners to any matter or thing in respect of which such consent is required to be given under the Act or any regulations made thereunder shall be given in such manner and evidenced in such form as the Board may consider appropriate and such consent shall be deemed to have been given if a majority of the adult iTaukei owners shall have signified their consent.

[reg 2 subst LN 66 of 1965 reg 2, effective 11 June 1965; am Decree 8 of 2011 s 3, effective 1 March 2011]

## reg 3 Form of certificate

A certificate under section 6 of the Act shall be as prescribed in Form 2 in the Schedule or as near thereto as the circumstances will permit.

## reg 4 Form of notice

A notice under section 19(3) of the Act shall be as prescribed in Form 3 in the Schedule or as near thereto as the circumstances will permit.

[reg 4 am LN 66 of 1965 reg 2, effective 11 June 1965]

## reg 5 Form of order

An order under section 19(6) of the Act shall be as prescribed in Form 4 in the Schedule or as near thereto as the circumstances will permit.

[reg 5 am LN 66 of 1965 reg 2, effective 11 June 1965]

#### FORM 4 ORDER OF THE iTAUKEI LAND TRUST BOARD

Whereas the mataqali was the owner of that piece of land containing more or less and being Lot on plan No deposited with the Registrar of Titles:

And whereas the said mataqali has been reported to have ceased to exist by the extinction of its members.

And whereas notice has been published in a newspaper published in the iTaukei language and circulating in Fiji and in the Gazette stating that it has been reported to the iTaukei Land Trust Board that the said mataqali has ceased to exist by the extinction of its members.

And whereas a copy of the said notice has been served on the roko tui of the province in which the said land is situate.

And whereas no objection has been received to such report within the time prescribed: or

And whereas the objections received to such report have been duly investigated and disallowed and notice of such disallowance given as prescribed.

And whereas the said Board is satisfied that the said mataqali has ceased to exist by the extinction of its members.

And whereas it is provided by section 19(1) of the iTaukei Land Trust Act 1940 that the land of any extinct mataqali shall vest in the Board to be dealt with as therein provided.

Now therefore the iTaukei Land Trust Board doth hereby order that the above-described land shall be retained and dealt with by the State in terms of section 19(1) of the iTaukei Land Trust Act 1940.

In witness whereof the Seal of the Board is hereunto affixed this day of, 20 .
