## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the iTaukei Land (iTaukei Reserves) Regulations 1940.

[reg 1 am Decree 8 of 2011 s 3, effective 1 March 2011]

## reg 2 Appointment of Commissioner and assessors

(1) The Minister may appoint a Commissioner, who shall be charged with the duty of holding inquiries for the purpose of ascertaining the boundaries of portions of iTaukei land which the iTaukei owners desire should be set aside and notified as iTaukei reserves, and may appoint a person or persons to assist such Commissioner, and may, by general or special order published in the Gazette, confer on any such person or persons all or any of the powers of the Commissioner under these Regulations; and the term “Commissioner” wherever it is used in these Regulations shall include a person duly appointed under this subregulation to assist the Commissioner.

[subreg (1) am LN 20 of 1958 reg 2 effective 7 March 1958 ; LN 112 of 1970 O 253, effective 8 October 1970; Decree 8 of 2011 s 3, effective 1 March 2011]

(2) The Minister may appoint for the inquiries in each province a person being an iTaukei to sit as assessor with the Commissioner appointed as aforesaid.

[subreg (2) am LN 112 of 1970 O 253, effective 8 October 1970; Decree 31 of 2010 s 4 effective 2 July 2010; Decree 8 of 2011 s 3, effective 1 March 2011]

## reg 3 Power to summon witnesses etc

For the purposes of any inquiry the Commissioner shall have the same powers as those vested in Magistrates to summon and examine on oath all owners of any land the reservation of which is being inquired into and any person whom he or she may think able to give relevant evidence.

## reg 4 Notice of sittings

(1) When the Commissioner is about to institute inquiries as aforesaid notice of the time and place at which the first sitting will be held shall be given to the buli of the tikina by the Commissioner of the division in which the inquiries are to be held.

(2) The notice shall be delivered to the buli not less than 14 days before the sitting. In the absence of any buli from his or her place of residence the notice shall be delivered to the tikina scribe or such other person as the Commissioner of the division may appoint for the purpose.

(3) The buli or other recipient of the notice shall cause the time and place of the sitting of the Commissioner to be publicly proclaimed in each village within his or her tikina.

(4) A general notice of the intention to hold an inquiry giving the time and place of the meeting shall be published in the Gazette and in a newspaper published in the iTaukei language and circulating in Fiji.

[subreg (4) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 124, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 5 Meeting of tikina council

On receipt of the notice provided for in regulation 4(1) by the buli or other recipient of the notice shall convene a special meeting of the tikina council within 48 hours, and call upon the heads of the land owning units within the tikina to cause the members thereof to prepare particulars of the boundaries of reserves desired by them.

## reg 6 Sittings

The statement of particulars prepared under the provisions of regulation 5 shall be examined by the Commissioner, who may accept it as a basis for his or her recommendation in respect of reservations within the tikina, and the Commissioner shall, when he or she deems it advisable to do so, call and examine witnesses and make such alterations and amendments in the statements as are justified by the evidence elicited.

## reg 7 Adjournment of sittings

The Commissioner may at his or her discretion from time to time adjourn the sitting either sine die or to a fixed time and place. All members of land owning units within the tikina in which the Commissioner is sitting at the time of adjournment shall be deemed to have notice of the adjournment.

## reg 8 Language of proceedings

All proceedings before the Commissioner shall be conducted in the iTaukei language.

[reg 8 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 1 March 2010]

## reg 9 Attendance of lessee affected

Any lessee of iTaukei land affected by any proposal of the iTaukei owners shall be entitled to attend in person before the Commissioner to give evidence and to make representations regarding such proposal. If any such lessee does not understand the iTaukei language the proceedings shall be interpreted to him or her in a language which he or she understands.

[reg 9 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 1 March 2010; Decree 8 of 2011 s 3, effective 1 March 2011]

## reg 10 Report of Commissioner to Board

(1) Upon the conclusion of the inquiries in each tikina the Commissioner shall forward an English translation of statements prepared under regulation 5 and his or her notes of evidence and his or her recommendations thereon in respect of each proposed reserve to the Secretary, who shall place the same before the Board.

[subreg (1) am LN 53 of 1946 reg 2, effective 8 March 1946]

(2) The Commissioner shall cause the description of the boundaries and situation of land which are claimed for reserve to be entered in a form as set out in the Schedule.

[subreg (2) am LN 65 of 1965 reg 2, effective 11 June 1965]

(3) In the event of the owners of any iTaukei lands refusing or neglecting when called upon to give such full and proper information to the Commissioner as will enable him or her to carry out the provisions of regulations 2 and 5 the Commissioner shall have the power to make such recommendation to the Board as may seem to him or her just and equitable.

[subreg (3) am Decree 8 of 2011 s 3, effective 1 March 2011]
