# ITAUKEI LAND (UNCLAIMED DEPOSITS) REGULATIONS 1975

**Table of Amendments** iTaukei Land (Unclaimed Deposits) Regulations 1975 (LN 129 of 1975) commenced on 5 December 1975, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Fijian Affairs (Amendment) Decree 2010 (No 31 of 2010) | 2 July 2010 |
| Native Land Trust (Amendment) Decree 2011 (No 8 of 2011) | 1 March 2011 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the iTaukei Land (Unclaimed Deposits) Regulations 1975.

[reg 1 Decree 8 of 2011 s 3, effective 1 March 2011]

## reg 2 Advertisement of unclaimed deposits

The iTaukei Land Trust Board, hereinafter referred to as the Board, may at any time cause to be advertised, in each of the English and iTaukei languages, at least twice at intervals of 14 days in newspapers published and circulating in Fiji details of all rents, royalties and premiums in respect of leases or licences in respect of iTaukei land received by the Board and remaining unclaimed 3 months after the date of deposit with the Board.

[reg 2 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 1 March 2010; Decree 8 of 2011 s 3, effective 1 March 2011]

## reg 3 Unclaimed Deposits Reserve Fund

Any such rents, royalties or premiums so deposited with the Board to which no valid claim is made at the expiration of 6 months from the last date of advertisement referred to in regulation 2 may be transferred to a reserve fund to be known as the Unclaimed Deposits Reserve Fund.

## reg 4 Disposal of unclaimed deposits

The Unclaimed Deposits Reserve Fund shall be disposed of by the Board from time to time in such manner as the Board shall deem appropriate for the furtherance of the purposes of the Act.

## reg 5 Ex gratia payments

For the avoidance of doubt it is hereby expressly declared that, notwithstanding any of the provisions of these Regulations, the Board may in its absolute discretion agree to make an ex gratia payment in full or partial settlement of any claim for the refund of a deposit submitted to the Board at any time.
