## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Rabi Island Regulations (Application) Order 1957

## O 2 Application

The Regulations contained in the first column of the Schedule shall be obeyed by Fijians who are not members of the Banaban community during such time as they are on Rabi Island, to the extent shown in the second column.
