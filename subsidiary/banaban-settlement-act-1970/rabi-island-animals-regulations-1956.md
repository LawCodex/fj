# RABI ISLAND (ANIMALS) REGULATIONS 1956

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Rabi Island (Animals) Regulations 1956.

## reg 2 Pigs to be confined

(1) No person shall keep pigs on Rabi Island unless the pigs are confined within a place approved for that purpose by the Council and such place is fenced to the satisfaction of the Council.

(2) Any person contravening the provisions of subregulation (1), shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding $1.

(3) The occupier of any cultivated land may kill any pigs found upon such land.

## reg 3 Animals not to stray

Any person who grazes or allows to wander in any village on Rabi Island any cattle, horses or goats shall be guilty of an offence and liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $20.

[reg 3 am LN 81 of 1957 reg 2, effective 1 November 1957]

## reg 4 Riding communally owned horses

Any person who, without the permission of the Council, rides a communally owned horse shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding $20.

[reg 4 am LN 81 of 1957 reg 2, effective 1 November 1957]

## reg 5 Keeping dangerous dogs

(1) Any person who keeps a dangerous dog shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding $20.

[subreg (1) am LN 81 of 1957 reg 2, effective 1 November 1957]

(2) Where any person is convicted on an offence against this regulation, the court shall order the dog to be destroyed.
