## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Banaban (Copra) Regulations 1958.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**copra** means the kernel or meat of the coconut in its dried condition when it has been extracted from the shell;

**green copra** means copra which is not dried so as to be fit for export;

**manager** means the owner of a retail store; and

[def subst Act 28 of 2020 s 4 and Sch, effective 1 August 2020]

**village store** means a store on the Island of Rabi owned by one or other of the following co-operative societies—

Tabwewa Village Co-operative Society Limited;

Buakonikai Village Co-operative Society Limited;

New Uma Village Co-operative Society Limited;

Tabiang Village Co-operative Society Limited.

[def subst LN 50 of 1958 reg 2, effective 20 June 1958]

## reg 3 Copra licences

(1) The Council may issue copra licences annually, covering the period 1 January to 31 December, to male or female members of the Banaban community between 14 and 60 years of age who are registered members of one or another of the 4 village communities on Rabi Island.

(2) The fee for a copra licence shall be $0.50 cents.

(3) The Council shall not refuse to issue a copra licence to any such member of the Banaban community as aforesaid who pays the requisite fee.

[subreg (3) am LN 90 of 1977 reg 2, effective 15 July 1977]

(4) It shall be unlawful on Rabi Island for any member of the Banaban community between 14 and 60 years of age unless he or she is in possession of a current licence, and for any other person whatsoever except a member of the Banaban community under the age of 14 years, to collect coconuts in copra making.

Any person contravening the provisions of this subregulation shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $50.

## reg 4 Prohibition of making copra with immature nuts

Any person who makes or assists in making copra from green or immature coconuts shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100 and the Council may suspend the copra licence of any person so convicted for a period not exceeding 6 months.

## reg 5 Village copra areas

(1) The Council may define village copra areas on Rabi Island and shall publish within Rabi Island details of the boundaries of such areas and any alterations thereof which may from time to time be made.

(2) It shall be unlawful for any member of the Banaban community to collect coconuts in a village copra area other than the area allotted to the Community in which he or she is registered, except with the permission in writing of an Island Councillor of that other community.

Any person who contravenes the provisions of this subregulation shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $50.

[subreg (2) am LN 90 of 1977 reg 3, effective 15 July 1977]

## reg 6 Prohibition of export of green copra

(1) No member of the Banaban community shall export green copra from Rabi Island to any place outside that island.

(2) Any member of the Banaban community contravening the provisions of this regulation shall be liable upon conviction to a fine not exceeding $200, and shall also be liable to pay any cess which would have been payable had he or she sold to the manager of a village store an amount of copra equal in weight to the amount of green copra exported.

## reg 7 Sale and export of copra

(1) No member of the Banaban community shall sell copra except to the manager of a village store at such a store.

(2) No member of the Banaban community shall export copra from Rabi Island to any place outside that island.

(3) Any member of the Banaban community contravening the provisions of this regulation shall be liable upon conviction to a fine not exceeding $200, and shall also be liable to pay any cess which would have been payable had he or she sold the copra to the manager of a village store.

(4) Nothing in this regulation shall apply to the sale or export of copra by any of the co-operative societies specified in regulation 2 through the manager of a village store.

[subreg (4) am LN 50 of 1958 reg 3, effective 20 June 1958]

## reg 8 Buying of copra

(1) All copra fit for export which is offered for sale to the manager of a village store shall be bought by him or her on behalf of the cooperative society owning the store at a price not less than the minimum fixed price for copra sold at that store.

[subreg (1) am LN 50 of 1958 reg 4, effective 20 June 1958]

(2) The minimum price of copra to be paid on any sale to the manager of a village store shall be fixed from time to time by the manager of such store subject to the approval of the Banaban Adviser.

(3) A statement in the Gilbertese language of the minimum price of copra for the time being in force at a village store shall be posted up and kept posted up in a conspicuous place in the store.

(4) If any manager of a village store fails to comply with any of the provisions of this regulation he or she shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100.

[subreg (4) am LN 50 of 1958 reg 4, effective 20 June 1958]

## reg 9 Saving

Nothing in the foregoing regulations shall be deemed to authorise or require the manager of a village store to buy copra in contravention of any of the provisions of the Coconut Industry Development Act 2010.

[reg 9 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 21, effective 1 December 2016]
