# RABI ISLAND (BUILDINGS, VILLAGES AND SANITATION) REGULATIONS 1956

**Table of Amendments**

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Rabi Island (Buildings, Villages and Sanitation) Regulations 1956.

## reg 2 Buildings not to be erected without permission

Any person who builds or erects any house, bure, hut or copra drier on Rabi Island without first obtaining the permission of the Council shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding $20.

[reg 2 am LN 80 of 1957 reg 2, effective 1 November 1957]

## reg 3 Water supplies not to be altered without permission

Any person who without first obtaining the permission of the Council alters or interferes with any installation forming part of a public piped water supply on Rabi Island shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $20.

[reg 3 insrt LN 44 of 1961 reg 2, effective 28 April 1961]

## reg 4 Electrical connections not to be interfered with without permission

Any person who without first obtaining the permission of the Council alters or interferes with any electrical installation forming part of a public electricity supply system on Rabi Island shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $20.

[reg 4 insrt LN 44 of 1961 reg 2, effective 28 April 1961]

## reg 5 Filling of site

Any person who fills in the site of any house, bure or hut on Rabi Island with refuse, filth or dung shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding $20 or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 2 months.

[reg 5 am LN 80 of 1957 reg 3, effective 1 November 1957]

## reg 6 Kitchen and floors

Every dwelling house on Rabi Island shall be provided with—

(a) a kitchen separate from the main room or rooms of the dwelling house;

(b) a floor and floor coverings of a material satisfactory to the Council.

## reg 7 Removal of refuse

Refuse and rubbish shall be removed daily from each dwelling house on Rabi Island and taken to an approved place.

## reg 8 Cleanliness of buildings

All houses, huts, bures and latrines on Rabi Island shall be kept clean and in adequate repair to the satisfaction of the Council.

## reg 9 Fencing of wells etc

All wells, open pits and receptacles on Rabi Island shall be adequately fenced and covered to the satisfaction of the Council.

## reg 10 Offence

(1) Any householder who fails to comply with the provisions of any of regulations 7 to 10 inclusive, shall be guilty of an offence and liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $20.

[subreg (1) am LN 80 of 1957 reg 4, effective 1 November 1957]

(2) Any householder who fails to carry out an order made by the Council or any person authorised by it for the purpose of enforcing the provisions of any of regulations 7 to 10, inclusive, shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of $20 or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 2 months.

[subreg (2) am LN 80 of 1957 reg 4, effective 1 November 1957]

## reg 11 Sleeping on earth floors prohibited

Any person who sleeps on the uncovered earth floor of any house, bure or hut on Rabi Island shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of $20.

[reg 11 am LN 80 of 1957 reg 5, effective 1 November 1957]

## reg 12 Polluted water supplies not to be used

Any person who draws water from a well, spring or any source of fresh water on Rabi Island which has been condemned by a resolution of the Council shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of $20 or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 2 months.

[reg 12 am LN 80 of 1957 reg 6, effective 1 November 1957]

## reg 13 Safeguard of water supplies

Any person who places any refuse, filth or excreta anywhere whereby a well, spring or any source of fresh water on Rabi Island may be contaminated shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of $20 or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 2 months.

[reg 13 am LN 80 of 1957 reg 7, effective 1 November 1957]

## reg 14 Refuse etc not to be placed in latrines

Any person who throws or places dead animals, refuse, food or fish in a latrine on Rabi Island shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding $20.

[reg 14 am LN 80 of 1957 reg 8, effective 1 November 1957]

## reg 15 Latrines to be used

Any person who defecates on Rabi Island within 500 yards of any habitation or within 50 yards of any well, spring, stream or source of fresh water except in a latrine shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding $20 or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 2 months.

[reg 15 am LN 80 of 1957 reg 9, effective 1 November 1957]

## reg 16 Bathing etc near sources of water

Any person who bathes, washes clothes or cleans food on Rabi Island within 100 feet of a well, spring, stream or source of fresh water shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding $20 or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 2 months.

[reg 16 am LN 80 of 1957 reg 10, effective 1 November 1957]

## reg 17 Occupier to keep land clean

Any occupier of land on Rabi Island who fails to keep his or her land or the area of beach opposite his or her land, or that part of a road which passes through his or her land, in a clean and tidy condition to the satisfaction of the Council shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding $20.

[reg 17 am LN 80 of 1957 reg 11, effective 1 November 1957]

## reg 18 Villages to be kept clear of vegetation

(1) All undergrowth, plants, trees and shrubs growing in or adjacent to a village on Rabi Island or growing at the side of any road passing through a village on Rabi Island shall be either cut or shall be kept cleared and tidy to the satisfaction of the Council.

(2) Any member of a village who fails to clear or cut plants, trees and shrubs growing in or adjacent to his or her village upon being ordered to do so by the Council or by any person authorised by it shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding $20.

[subreg (2) am LN 80 of 1957 reg 12, effective 1 November 1957]
