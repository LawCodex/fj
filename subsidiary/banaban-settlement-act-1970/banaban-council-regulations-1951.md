## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Banaban Council Regulations 1951.

## reg 2 Election of Council

(1) A general election of the Council shall take place during November 1996, and thereafter the general election shall take place during January of each succeeding fourth year, provided that the Council may with the prior approval of the Minister postpone the holding of a general election for a period not exceeding 6 months.

(2) The District Officer Savusavu on the directions of the Commissioner Northern Division shall in December of the year preceding the year in which a general election is to be held in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, fix the date in the following January when such election shall take place, and subject to the provisions of these Regulations, the procedure at such election shall be in accordance with the directions of the Commissioner Northern Division.

(3) Any casual vacancy in the Council shall be filled by a by-election on a day fixed by the District Officer Savusavu on the direction of the Commissioner Northern Division and the procedure shall be similar to that at a general election.

(4) Election of members to the Council shall be by secret ballot.

(5) Any member of the Banaban community, male or female, who is a resident and who is of or above the age of 21 years shall be qualified to vote at an election.

(6) Any qualified elector who is a member of one of the 4 communities specified in section 3(3) of the Act may, if nominated by at least 2 qualified electors in his or her community and if he or she consents thereto, be a candidate for election as a member of the Council for that community.

(7) Candidates shall pay a deposit of $50.

(8) Members of the Council shall, unless they resign or dismissed by the Minister or their office otherwise becomes vacant, hold office until the day next preceding the day upon which the next succeeding general election is held.

(9) Members of the Council shall consult with their communities after each sitting of the Council.

(10) The Minister may dismiss a member of the Council including the Chairperson on any of the following grounds—

(a) when a member comes under one of the grounds of disqualification specified in section 3A of the Act;

(b) that a member has failed to attend 3 consecutive meetings of Council without the prior permission of the Council;

(c) that the Minister is of the opinion that the member is incapable of discharging his or her duties as Councillor;

(d) that a petition for the member's dismissal on the grounds of misconduct or unsatisfactory performance, signed by not less than 60% of electors in the community that the member represents or by 60% of all registered voters in the case of the Chairperson, has been received by the Minister. The Minister shall on receipt of the petition be empowered to initiate such enquiries as he or she may think fit and act in his or her considered judgment. In any such dismissals, the Minister should within 3 months, order a by-election to be held to fill the vacancy.

(11) No person holding the office of Tribunal shall be a member of the Council.

[reg 2 subst LN 112 of 1996 reg 3, effective 11 October 1996]

## reg 3 Chairperson of the Council etc

(1) The Chairperson shall hold office until the day next preceding the date of the next succeeding general elections, unless he or she resigns or his or her office becomes vacant for any reason.

(2) If the office of the Chairperson becomes vacant for any reason, a general election to elect a successor shall be held as soon as practicable.

(3) The Chairperson or in his or her absence or during a vacancy, a member elected from amongst those present, shall preside at meetings of the Council.

(4) Questions coming before the Council shall be decided by a majority of members in an open vote.

(5) A member shall not vote on any question in which he or she has a pecuniary interest.

[reg 3 subst LN 112 of 1996 reg 4, effective 11 October 1996]

## reg 4 Meetings

(1) All meetings of the Council shall be held on Rabi Island.

(2) The first meetings of a newly elected Council shall be called by the Executive Director as Secretary to the Council.

(3) The Chairperson may call a meeting at any time and shall call a meeting within 7 days of being requested so to do by at least 6 members of the Council.

(4) The Executive Director as Secretary shall notify members of the time and place of meetings of the Council. He or she shall keep the minutes of each meeting which shall record the names of those present, the subjects discussed, any decisions made and any other matters of importance. Such minutes shall be authenticated by the Chairperson and kept in a book at the office of the Executive Director as Secretary.

(5) The first business of any meeting after the election, if any, of the Chairperson or presiding member, shall be the confirmation of minutes.

(6) The Council may with the approval of the Minister make rules providing for the summoning of meetings, the regulation and conduct of debates, the maintenance of order, the suspension of members and all such other matters as may be deemed necessary and proper to ensure the efficient functioning of the Council.

[reg 4 subst LN 112 of 1996 reg 5, effective 11 October 1996]

## reg 5 Act not invalidated by vacancy

No act or proceeding of the Council or of any person acting as a member of the Council shall be invalidated in consequence of there being a vacancy in the number of members of the Council at the time of such act or proceeding.

## reg 6 Executive director

(1) The Executive Director shall be appointed by the Minister in consultation with the Council and shall hold office during the Minister’s pleasure.

(2) The Executive Director, other than being Secretary to Council, shall carry out duties under the Act and this Regulation, and shall be responsible for the carrying out of all necessary administrative work.

[reg 6 subst LN 112 of 1996 reg 6, effective 11 October 1996]

## reg 7 Admission of public

Members of the public shall not be admitted to meetings of the Council except at the express invitation of the Council.

## reg 8 Reports

The Council shall consider reports made to it by members of the Council relating to the peace, order and good government of the Banaban community.

## reg 9 Appointment of officers

The Council may appoint such officers and servants as may be required for the efficient discharge of its duties and responsibilities and for the proper conduct and administration of the affairs of the Banaban community.

## reg 10 Powers of council

The Council shall have power to do all or any of the following things—

(a) to erect, maintain and keep in repair such offices and buildings as may be required for Council purposes or for the communal purposes of the Banaban community;

(b) to do all things necessary for carrying out all the purposes for and in regard to which the Council is empowered to make Regulations and for carrying such regulations into effect;

(c) subject to the other provisions of these Regulations to incur all expenditure necessary for the carrying out of any purpose of the Act, or of these Regulations which the Council is authorised to carry out or for any purpose not specially provided for which the Minister may determine to be a purpose incidental to the exercise of its powers under the Act or these Regulations.

## reg 11 Rabi Island Fund

(1) The monies constituting the Rabi Island Fund shall be held in a general account which shall be credited with all income due to the Council.

(2) Separate accounts shall be kept in respect of monies received or collected or raised or levied for, or allocated to or held in trust for any particular purpose or benefit or for any particular section of the Banaban community, which monies shall be expended only for such particular purpose or benefit or for the benefit of such particular section of the Banaban community.

(3) No expenditure from the accounts referred to in subregulation (1) or (2) shall be incurred without the approval of the Minister as provided for in regulation 19.

[reg 11 subst LN 112 of 1996 reg 7, effective 11 October 1996]

## reg 12 Application of Fund

Subject to the provisions of these Regulations, the Fund shall be applied to the payment of—

(a) fees and allowances for the payment of members of the Council as approved by the Minister through the annual budget;

(b) such officers as may be appointed by the Council under these Regulations; and

(c) expenditure which the Council is authorised to incur under approved annual budget.

[reg 12 subst LN 112 of 1996 reg 8, effective 11 October 1996]

## reg 13 Power to impose annual rate

The Council shall have power by resolution, subject to the approval of the Minister, to impose an annual rate to be called the island rate on every adult male member of the Banaban community, who, on or before 1 January of the year in respect of which the island rate is imposed, is between the ages of 18 and 60 and who is not excepted by regulation 14. Every such resolution shall be published in Rabi Island and shall state the amount of the rate and the method and time of payment.

## reg 14 Exemptions

(1) Any member of the Banaban community who—

(a) resides outside Fiji for a continuous period of 6 months or more in the year in respect of which the island rate is levied; or

(b) is undergoing treatment as a patient in any mental hospital or as a person suffering from leprosy in any hospital or institution,

shall be exempt from paying the island rate until such time as he or she returns to Fiji or is discharged from such hospital or institution as the case may be.

(2) If the Council recommends that a member of the Banaban community shall be exempted from paying the whole or part of the island rate, and the Minister responsible for finance approves such recommendation, the member shall be exempt from paying a part or the whole of such rate for such period as may be specified in the recommendation or during the life time of such member, as the case may be.

## reg 15 Registration

(1) Every male member of the Banaban community shall within 2 months of the date of these Regulations or of his reaching the age of 17, whichever shall be the latter, register as a ratepayer at the office of the Executive Director.

[subreg (1) am LN 112 of 1996 reg 9, effective 11 October 1996]

(2) Every such person who without lawful excuse fails to register in accordance with the provisions of this regulation shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $4 or in default to imprisonment for any period not exceeding 2 weeks.

## reg 16 Failure to pay rate — penalty

Any member of the Banaban community who without just cause fails to pay to the Executive Director the island rate before 31 July of any year shall be liable on conviction to pay such rate and in addition to pay a fine not exceeding $10 and in default of payment of such rate or of such fine to imprisonment for any period not exceeding one month.

[reg 16 am LN 112 of 1996 reg 9, effective 11 October 1996]

## reg 17 Money to be paid into Fund

The Executive Director shall pay to the Fund all moneys received by him or her in payment of the island rate.

[reg 17 am LN 112 of 1996 reg 9, effective 11 October 1996]

## reg 18 Books

The Council shall keep proper books of account in which shall be correctly entered all moneys received and expended.

## reg 19 Statement of account

(1) The Chairperson of the Council shall, before 30 June in each year, submit to the Minister a statement of account, together with the certificate and report of the auditor appointed by the Minister in respect of the 12 months ended on the preceding 31 December.

[subreg (1) am LN 112 of 1996 reg 10, effective 11 October 1996]

(2) The Chairperson of the Council shall, before 31 October of each year, submit to the Minister a detailed estimate of the revenue and expenditure of the Council for the year commencing 1 January next following. No expenditure may be incurred by the Council except in accordance with the estimate as approved by the Minister or with the special sanction of the Minister.

[subreg (2) am LN 112 of 1996 reg 10, effective 11 October 1996]

## reg 20 Island Rate Register

The Executive Director shall keep a book to be known as the “Island Rate Register” in which he or she shall enter the names and ages of all persons registering as ratepayers in accordance with regulation 15.

[reg 20 am LN 112 of 1996 reg 9, effective 11 October 1996]
