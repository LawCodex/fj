## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [regs 1–3]

### reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Ginger Regulations 1997.

### reg 2 Commencement

These Regulations shall come into force on the day the Ginger Council of Fiji Act (Act No 1 of 1996) hereinafter called “Act”, comes into force.

### reg 3 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms and expressions have the meaning assigned to them under the Act, “Board”, “Council”, “fresh ginger”, “ginger exporter”, “ginger grower”, “ginger processor”, “Permanent Secretary” and “processed ginger”.

## PART 2 RULES AND CONDITIONS PERTAINING TO FEES AND LEVIES [regs 4–7]

### reg 4 Fresh ginger export levy

A fresh ginger export licensee must pay an export levy to the Council as prescribed below:

(a) $0.01 per lb or $0.022 per kg for all fresh ginger exported to North America;

(b) $0.005 per lb or $0.011 per kg for all fresh ginger exported to market destinations other than North America.

### reg 5 Ginger processor export levy

A ginger processor must pay an export levy of $0.005 per lb or $0.011 per kg for all processed ginger exported to all destinations.

### reg 6 Review of export levy

Any change to the prescribed export levy must be approved by the Board and is reviewable on an annual basis.

### reg 7 Payment of levy and consequence for non-payment in time

(1) Levies must be paid in full to the Council at the end of each quarter.

(2) A licensee who fails to pay export levies as and when due will be liable to pay interest on the outstanding amount and their licence may be revoked for the next shipment or for the season.

## PART 3 RULES AND CONDITIONS PERTAINING TO THE ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO THE GINGER COUNCIL BOARD [regs 8, 9]

### reg 8 Registration requirement

(1) Ginger growers who are also ginger exporters and/or ginger processors or vice versa must register either as a grower or an exporter or a processor for the purpose of electing members to the Board.

(2) Only ginger growers who have sold a minimum of 1.0 tonne of export ginger in the previous season are eligible to register. Ginger exporters are required under the Act to provide the Council information on growers export tonnage in the previous season before the end of February each year.

### reg 9 Election of members to the Ginger Council Board

The Board shall consist of 9 members and elected as follows—

(a) Fresh ginger growers representatives, 4 members.

(i) These members shall be elected each year at the annual general meeting.

(ii) One vote shall be cast for only one member.

(iii) The person who received the fifth highest number of votes shall be the alternate member to represent growers in the absence of one of their elected members.

(b) Fresh ginger exporters representatives, 3 members.

(i) These members shall be elected each year at the annual general meeting.

(ii) One vote shall be cast for only one member.

(iii) The person who receives the fourth highest amount of votes will be the alternate member for the exporters and will serve on the Board when one of the other members is unable to attend.

(c) Ginger processor’s representative, one member.

(i) The member shall be elected each year at the annual general meeting.

(ii) One vote shall be cast for one member.

(iii) The person who received the second highest amount of votes shall be the alternate member for the processors and shall serve on the Board when the elected member is unable to attend.

(d) Ministry of Agriculture Representative, one member.

The Permanent Secretary or his or her nominee shall be the Ministry’s representative on the Board.

## PART 4 RULES AND CONDITIONS PERTAINING TO THE ISSUING OF GINGER EXPORT LICENCES [regs 10]

### reg 10 Conditions of fresh ginger exporters licence

Licences issued by the Council to fresh ginger exporters shall be subject to the following conditions—

(a) the licensee will be allowed to export fresh ginger (including baby and mature ginger) to—

(i) all destinations; or

(ii) all destinations except North America (including Hawaii and Canada);

(b) a licensee must supply to the Council a list of its existing ginger growers, where they are located and ginger planted by each grower;

(c) the licensees must not purchase ginger from growers who have entered into agreement with, or are listed as owing money to other exporters unless prior approval in writing is obtained from such other exporters;

(d) the licensee must pay ginger farmers no later than 30 days after the date of shipment;

(e) the purchase price will be the price agreed to with the grower prior to harvest or shipment;

(f) the licensee must issue the grower on delivery a receipt or Payment Advice Invoice clearly stating the quantity and price (as agreed to in (e) above) paid or to be paid to the grower. If the price is not stated then the method that price is determined, must be clearly stated. Any such method must be easy to calculate and verify, and the exporter must agree to provide copies of all relevant documents to the Council when the Council is requested by the grower to verify the price;

(g) new applicants for an export licence must furnish to the Council a bank guarantee to ensure that the full financial commitment to growers will be met. Grower or exporter applying for a licence to export his or her own ginger will not require to furnish bank guarantee;

(h) licensee must grade ginger for export according to standards imposed by the Council or Ministry of Agriculture for the intended export market;

(i) the licensee must have adequate facilities for curing, inspection, grading, and packing of ginger. These include:

(i) adequate space to hold ginger according to fanner lots until it is inspected and graded;

(ii) adequate air flow to ensure proper drying and curing to avoid mould problems;

(iii) adequate light for inspection and grading of ginger at night or the licensee must specify that all grading and inspection be performed only between 7.00 am–5.00 pm;

(iv) the scales used by the licensee are of commercial quality and have been recently certified by the Department of National Trade Measurement and Standards;

(v) the facilities is kept clean at all times and have adequate facilities for employees to wash and maintain the required standard of cleanliness;

(j) all ginger exported must be packed in new clean cartons or crates or bags. The exporting company name (or code) must be clearly marked on the packaging;

(k) that all air freighted mixed-container shipments to North America (including Hawaii) must have no more than 30 boxes or 900 lbs of ginger;

(l) based upon the capacity of licensee drying and packing facilities, the licensee is authorised to export a maximum number of containers as determined by the Council on any one shipment. The licensee may apply to the Council to increase this limit should he or she increase the capacity of his or her packing facilities during the season;

(m) the licensee must furnish a monthly return to the Council consisting of a copy of shipment invoice detailing quantity of ginger shipped, date of shipment and destination at the end of each month;

(n) the licensee must pay prescribed levy and fees to the Council;

(o) this licence is non-transferable and expires on 31 December of each year or until it is cancelled or suspended by the Council;

(p) licensee who fails to pay producers in accordance with the purchase agreement referred to in (e) and (f) above or who significantly breaches the terms of the export licence and does not rectify the breach is subject to $500 fine and will be required to pay all outstanding amounts due to his or her growers within 30 days from the date the incident was reported to the Council. In the event the same exporter continues to breach the purchase agreement with his or her growers, he or she may lose his or her licence for one or 2 seasons at the discretion of the Arbitration Committee;

(q) a licensee who fails to comply with any of the above conditions is liable to $500 fine and in the event the licensee continues to breach any of the above conditions his or her licence may be suspended or cancelled at the discretion of the Arbitration Committee;

(r) any person or persons other than the licensed fresh ginger exporter who buys and exports fresh ginger will be liable to a fine of $500;

(s) the Council, in any licence issued, may impose conditions additional to any condition to which such licence is subject under the provision of the Act.

## PART 5 RULES AND CONDITIONS PERTAINING TO THE CONDUCT OF THE COUNCIL’S ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING [regs 11–15]

### reg 11 Annual general meeting

(1) The supreme authority of the Council shall be vested in the annual general meeting of its members and this authority shall be exercised by the Board elected by the Council in accordance with the Act.

(2) There shall be an auditor to the Council who shall audit the annual accounts and make written report to the annual general meeting. The annual general meeting shall be held annually as soon as the annual accounts have been audited or no later than 30 April each year.

### reg 12 Voting and quorum

(1) The annual general meeting shall be a meeting which registered ginger growers, exporters and processors shall attend and vote on all motions. Each member shall have one vote only which shall be exercised in person and not proxy.

(2) The quorum of the annual general meeting shall be two-thirds of all registered ginger growers, exporters and processors present in person or by proxies.

### reg 13 Notice of meeting

The Secretariat shall give 14 days written notice on the date, time and place of the annual general meeting and shall advertise the same in the daily newspaper.

### reg 14 Chairperson

(1) The chairperson of the Board shall preside as chairperson of the annual general meeting or if he or she is not present within 30 minutes after the time appointed for the holding of the annual general meeting, or is unwilling to act, the vice-chairperson who is present and willing shall be the chairperson or if the vice-chairperson is not present or unwilling to act as chairperson, the delegates present shall elect one of their members to be chairperson at that annual general meeting.

(2) In the case of equality of votes, the chairperson or acting chairperson of the general meeting shall be entitled to a second or casting vote.

### reg 15 Special general meeting

Twenty five per cent of the financial members of the Council may at any time convene a special general meeting provided that 30 days notice of such special general meeting shall be given in writing to the executive director and only matters for which due notice has been given shall be discussed.
