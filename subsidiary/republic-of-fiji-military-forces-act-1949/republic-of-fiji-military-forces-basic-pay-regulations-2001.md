## reg 1 Short title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Republic of Fiji Military Forces (Basic Pay) Regulations 2001 and are deemed to have commenced on 1 January 2001.

## reg 2 Basic Pay

The basic pay for—

(a) Regular officers on the Active List and soldiers of the Regular Force on military service;

(b) Territorial officers and soldiers in the territorial force;

(c) the Reserves; and

(d) Contracted Service personnel,

are set out in Schedule 1.

[reg 2 am LN 78 of 2013 reg 2, effective 1 January 2014]

## reg 3 Service allowances

A member of the force is entitled to the service allowances set out in Schedule 2.

[reg 3 insrt LN 108 of 2006 reg 2, effective 22 September 2006]
