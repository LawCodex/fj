## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Republic of Fiji Military Forces (Leave) Regulations 1988.

[reg 1 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 75, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations unless the contrary intention appears—

**Act** means the Republic of Fiji Military Forces Act 1949;

**Commander** means the Commander of the Forces appointed under the Act;

**excused duty leave** means sick leave;

**Fiji Military Forces** [def rep LN 99 of 2016 reg 75, effective 1 December 2016]

**leave year** means the period of 12 months service, excluding any of the period for which non-leave earning service was enjoyed by the officer;

**officer** means any commissioned officer appointed to the Forces by the President and Commander in Chief;

**pay** means basic pay; and

**soldier** means any warrant officer, non-commissioned officer or person enlisted in or belonging to the Forces and includes ratings in any naval unit of the Forces.

## reg 3 Coming into force and application

(1) These Regulations shall come into force on 1 April 1988 and shall apply to all ranks of the Forces other than officers or soldiers appointed under an agreement of service (contract) who will be governed by their terms of agreement.

(2) No officer or soldier shall be granted leave with salary within the first 3 months of his or her appointment to the Forces.

## reg 4 Authority for the grant of leave

(1) The authority to grant leave including the rescheduling of leave under these Regulations vests with the Commander.

(2) The Commander may delegate this authority in writing to any officer of the Forces and may specify the terms and limits of such authority.

## reg 5 Leave etc to be right

Leave entitlements accrued and due under these Regulations shall be deemed to be the right of all ranks of the Forces and cannot be forfeited.

## reg 6 Leave on resignation etc

In the event of resignation, termination of appointment on any grounds, or death of an officer or soldier he or she or his or her legal representative shall be paid pro rata compensation for—

(a) annual leave, due to the officer up to the date of his or her resignation, death etc;

(b) leave allowance;

(c) in addition to the above officer or soldier or his or her legal personal representative shall be paid compensation for long service leave which shall be in accordance with his or her entitlement at the time of his or her resignation, termination or appointment on any grounds or death etc.

## reg 7 Leave on retirement

In the event of retirement from the Forces whether at the officer's or soldier's own request or at the instigation of the Commander, the officer or soldier shall be granted—

(a) annual leave on a pro rata basis;

(b) leave allowance on pro rata basis if applicable, provided that in the event the officer or soldier has completed 21 years 6 months Regular Force service he or she shall be granted the full amount of leave allowance;

(c) in addition to (a) and (b) above, an officer or soldier who has completed 12 years or more continuous Regular Force service shall be paid the full long service leave allowance due to him or her under regulation 13(1)(a) and (b) provided he or she has not already taken it.

## reg 8 Annual leave before due date

In the event of an officer or soldier being authorised to proceed on leave before the due date whether—

(a) on medical grounds if recommended by a medical board;

(b) on grounds of urgent private affairs; or

(c) if required by the Commander to take leave,

he or she may be granted annual leave on a pro rata basis.

## reg 9 Leave categories

For the purpose of the these Regulations, officers or soldiers will be divided into 4 categories as follows—

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | Category A: | | Privates to Corporals or equivalent naval rank |
| | Category B: | | Sergeants to Lieutenants or equivalent naval rank |
| | Category C: | | Captains and Majors or equivalent naval rank |
| | Category D: | | Lieutenant Colonels or equivalent naval rank and above. |

## reg 10 Annual leave

(1)

(a) Annual leave shall be granted as follows—

(i) Category A, 24 consecutive days;

(ii) Categories B, C and D, 29 consecutive days;

(iii) the leave referred to in (i) and (ii) exclude public holidays.

(b) Annual leave when due may be taken on a daily basis or in multiples;

(c) Subject to the Commander's approval, officers or soldiers may, if they so desire, be allowed to carry over up to a maximum of 7 consecutive days leave entitlement from one leave year to another;

(d) officers/soldiers may use up to 7 consecutive days leave from the following leave year's entitlement during the final 6 months of any leave year, but this may not be linked with a long leave of absence (14 consecutive days or more) save in exceptional circumstances and with the prior approval of the Commander;

(e) Grant of annual leave shall take effect from different dates because of exigencies of service.

(2) The grant of leave to officers or soldiers may be rescheduled by the Commander for operational reasons and due to the exigencies of the service.

(3) Any leave entitlement granted in any overseas mission area by that mission's leave regulations and taken by a member of the Forces, is deemed to be part of the annual leave entitlement under these Regulations.

[subreg (3) insrt LN 107 of 2008 reg 2, effective 12 September 2008]

## reg 11 Long service leave

(1) In addition to annual leave as prescribed above, officers or soldiers shall be granted long service leave as follows—

Categories A to D—

(a) on completion of 12 continuous years service 30 consecutive days;

(b) on completion of 15 continuous years service 42 consecutive days;

(c) on completion of 20 continuous years service 56 consecutive days;

(d) on completion of 25 continuous years service 84 consecutive days;

(e) on completion of 30 continuous years service 112 consecutive days.

(2) Long service leave shall normally be taken together with an officer's or soldier's annual leave due on completion of the officer's or soldier's leave year, but in exceptional cases or in case of retirement it may be granted before the end of the officer's or soldier's leave year. Long service leave shall be regarded as non-leave earning service for annual leave purposes.

(3) An officer or soldier who has taken long service leave on his or her twelfth, fifteenth, twentieth or twenty-fifth year of service shall be entitled to be granted the difference between the long service leave he or she has already taken and the leave prescribed for completing 15, 20, 25 or 30 years service.

## reg 12 Application for leave

(1) All applications for annual leave shall save in exceptional circumstances be submitted to the Commander not later than 7 days in advance of the date the leave is intended to commence in duplicate on form FMF Z3.

(2) All applications for long service leave shall be submitted to the Commander in duplicate on form FMF Z3 not later than 30 days in advance of the date the leave is intended to commence.

## reg 13 Leave allowance

(1) Officers or soldiers shall be paid leave allowance no later than 7 days before it becomes due, as follows—

(a) Category A—

(i) $230 payable 7 days before long service leave is taken;

(ii) $105 every third leave year after qualifying for long service leave;

(iii) if long service leave is taken with annual leave when annual leave is due and the officer or soldier qualifies for the leave allowance under (i) and (ii) then both allowances ($335) shall be payable.

(b) Category B—

(i) $230 payable 7 days before long service leave is taken;

(ii) $230 every third leave year after qualifying for long service leave;

(iii) if long service leave is taken with annual leave when annual leave is due and the officer or soldier qualifies for the leave allowance under (i) and (ii) then both allowances ($460) shall be payable.

(c) Category C—

$574 payable every third leave year;

(d) Category D—

$690 payable every third leave year;

(e) Leave allowance for long service leave under Categories A and B shall be payable once only. No such allowance shall be payable when an officer or soldier utilises the balance of his or her long service under regulation 11(3).

(2) The leave allowance shall be brought in line with such similar allowances in the civil service whenever there is a adjustment in these allowances in the civil service.

## reg 14 Sick leave, outpatient and inpatient sick leave, excused duty

(1) Sick leave on full pay shall only be granted if the sickness is not occasioned by the officer's or soldier's own neglect or misconduct and shall be in accordance with the following regulations.

(2)

(a) An officer or soldier required to undergo treatment as outpatient shall be entitled to 21 days excuse duty leave in any one year of service. This leave cannot be accumulated.

(b) Excused duty leave shall be supported by a medical certificate obtained from the Regimental Medical Officer (RMO), Visiting Medical Officer (VMO) or any medical practitioner registered under the Medical and Dental Practitioner Act 2010.

[subreg (2) am LN 105 of 2007 reg 2, effective 13 November 2007; LN 99 of 2016 reg 75, effective 1 December 2016]

(3)

(a) An officer or soldier required to undergo treatment as an inpatient of a hospital or required by the RMO or VMO to be confined to his or her home on grounds of illness shall, in addition to his or her outpatient sick leave, be entitled to a further period of up to 42 days excused duty leave on full pay in any one year of service. This leave cannot be accumulated.

(b) On the recommendation of a medical board, inpatient sick leave on full pay may be extended up to a maximum of 180 days. Thereafter excused duty with or without full salary shall be at the discretion of the Commander.

[subreg (3) am LN 105 of 2007 reg 2, effective 13 November 2007]

(4) The Commander may at any time require an officer or soldier who is absent on sick leave to be examined by a RMO or VMO.

## reg 15 Maternity leave

(1)

(a) An officer or soldier will be entitled to maternity leave on the first 3 such occasions on full salary up to a maximum absence of 84 days covering the period before and after confinement;

(b) in the event of the officer or soldier being unfit to return to duty as a result of illness arising out of pregnancy or confinement at the end of 84 days, she may be granted by the Commander leave of absence without salary for a further period of up to 60 days;

(c) further extension of leave in addition to (a) and (b) above may be granted by the Commander if considered necessary by a medical board.

(2)

(a) After the first 3 occasions as provided for in regulation 15(1)(a), officers or soldiers will be granted leave without salary up to a maximum absence of 84 days covering the period before and after confinement;

(b) during the period of leave without salary, the officer or soldier will receive an allowance of $1.50 per day including Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays and will be paid in advance in one lump sum;

(c) in the event of the officer being unfit to return to duty as a result of illness arising out of pregnancy or confinement at the end of this period, the Commander may grant her leave of absence without salary for a further period of up to 60 days. No allowances will be paid during this period. No further extension of leave may be granted without the prior approval of the Commander.

(3)

(a) An officer or soldier who has rejoined the service and had maternity leave on at least 3 occasions during her previous service, will be subject to the provisions of regulation 15(2);

(b) If she had such leave on less than 3 occasions she will be subject to the provisions of regulation 15(1).

(4) Any leave arising out of pregnancy before the officer or soldier takes her maternity leave will be counted against sick leave for which the officer or soldier may be eligible. It will be counted against maternity leave only if the officer or soldier has taken all sick leave for which she may be eligible.

(5) An officer or soldier will not be permitted to resume duty before the expiry of her maternity leave.

(6) Any annual leave due to the officer or soldier before the commencement of the maternity leave shall not be absorbed in the maternity leave.

(7) An officer or soldier taking maternity leave shall be granted annual leave on a pro rata basis for the number of months service from the date of resumption of duty after maternity leave to the end of the officer's or soldier's leave year.

## reg 16 Conferences, courses and attachments

(1) When an officer or soldier is required to attend any conference, course of instructions or attachments, with the prior approval of the Commander, the times spent thereon shall not absorb any annual leave, or long service leave due to the officer or soldier.

(2) An officer or soldier sitting examinations in connection with a course approved by the Commander will be granted leave of absence for such examinations and such leave shall not count against any leave due to the officer or soldier.

(3) An officer or soldier who is granted leave with pay under this regulation shall be required to sign an undertaking to serve the Forces for a further period in accordance with the rules governing the recipients of “Return of Service following Specialised Training”.

(4) An officer or soldier who is sent overseas for training will normally travel by air and by the most direct route. Where the normal period of the air journey is less than 24 hours, travel leave shall not be granted. In other cases travel leave shall be limited to the period of the voyage up to a maximum of 2 days (based on air travel to the United Kingdom). A similar period for the return journey will be allowed.

## reg 17 Leave for sporting tour

An officer or soldier who is selected to be an official or a member of a Fiji national sporting team shall be granted leave on full pay up to 60 days in any one year, such period to include local training sessions. Leave in excess of 60 days shall be debited to the officer's or soldier's future annual leave.

## reg 18 Leave without pay

Subject to regulations 19 and 20, no leave without pay shall be granted to any officer or soldier except in very special circumstances.

## reg 19 Leave without pay in the interest of the Forces

(1) The Commander may in his or her discretion grant an officer or soldier leave without pay in the interest of the Forces.

(2) Without limiting the generality of the Commander's discretion the following situations are deemed to be in the interest of the Forces—

(a) an officer or soldier granted leave without pay for duty with a Government department, recognised international or regional organisation;

(b) an officer or soldier granted leave without pay for duty with a statutory authority as defined in the Financial Management Act 2004;

(c) an officer or soldier granted leave without pay to undertake a course of instruction deemed to be of value to him or her in his or her work;

(d) an officer or soldier granted leave without pay to accompany a spouse posted overseas in the service of Fiji;

(e) an officer or soldier granted leave without pay to accompany a spouse undertaking a Government sponsored course of instruction, attachment or visit on full pay whether in Fiji or overseas; and

(f) an officer or soldier granted leave without pay to accompany a spouse granted leave under (a) and (c) above.

[subreg (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 75, effective 1 December 2016]

(3) Leave granted under these Regulations shall be deemed to be on the grounds of public policy for the purposes of the Pensions Act 1983 and the Pensions (Amendment) Act 1986, but shall not earn annual or long service leave or any other leave, or count as a merit increase or increment earning period.

(4) An officer or soldier granted leave under these Regulations for a period which is to extend beyond 12 months shall be transferred to the reserve list, whereupon his or her post will become vacant.

(5) Upon resumption of duty the officer or soldier will be eligible to be transferred to a suitable vacancy in his or her substantive or lower rank.

## reg 20 Leave without pay not in the interest of the Forces

(1) The Commander may grant an officer or soldier leave without pay not in the interest of the service, for a period not exceeding 12 months.

(2) Leave granted under these Regulations shall not count as service for any purpose.

## reg 21 Bereavement leave

The Commander may grant an officer or soldier bereavement leave of up to 3 days in any one leave year. This leave shall be additional to any other leave to which an officer or soldier is entitled under these Regulations.

## reg 22 Unexpired leave

(1) Unless the Commander decides otherwise officers or soldiers shall enjoy the full leave earned by them prior to resuming duties.

(2) An officer or soldier may with the prior authority of the Commander be recalled from leave where the exigencies of the service dictates.

(3) An officer or soldier who is so recalled shall, if he or she so desires be paid appropriate compensation for the total of his or her unexpended leave at the time of resumption of duty or enjoy his or her unexpended leave at a later date mutually convenient to the officer or soldier and the Commander.

## reg 23 Extension of leave

(1) Extension of leave may be granted only with the prior approval of the Commander and shall be on any of the following grounds—

(a) on full pay on grounds of ill health in accordance with regulation 14;

(b) on grounds of public interest which shall be on full pay.

(2) Extension of leave granted on any grounds other than those set out above shall be without pay unless decided otherwise by the Commander.

## reg 24 Leave schedules

A leave schedule in the form Fiji Military Forces Z3 shall be kept in respect of every officer or soldier and included in the officer's or soldier's personal file. On the leave schedule will be entered records of all leave granted to the officer or soldier commencing from the date he or she first joined the Forces. This leave schedule shall be produced upon demand for inspection by the Commander.

## reg 25 Leave in respect of statutory holiday

(1) An officer or soldier who is required to work on a public holiday, shall be granted a day off in lieu of that public holiday.

(2) The day(s) granted in lieu of the public holidays shall be in addition to the officer's or soldier's annual leave entitlement.

## reg 26 Special provision for officers or soldiers serving on pre 1983 leave condition

(1) Officers or soldiers who were enlisted before 1 April 1983 and are still in the Force as at the date of this regulation would qualify for compensation as follows—

Years of service to date20 x 1811 = No of days

(compensation at the rate of pay for the rank held at the material time).

(2) An officer or soldier who had taken “retirement leave” under the leave regulations in force since 1983 would only be compensated for the difference between (1) above and what he or she had already taken.

(3) This compensation would be paid once only.

## reg 27 Commander’s leave

(1) Notwithstanding the other provisions of these Regulations, the Commander shall not proceed on annual or vacation leave without prior written approval of the Minister.

(2) Before granting such approval, the Minister shall satisfy himself or herself that suitable arrangements have been made for the command of the Republic of Fiji Military Forces during the absence of the Commander.
