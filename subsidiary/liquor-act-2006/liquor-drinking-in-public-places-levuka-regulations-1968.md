# LIQUOR (DRINKING IN PUBLIC PLACES) (LEVUKA) REGULATIONS 1968

Liquor (Drinking in Public Places) (Levuka) Regulations 1968 (LGN 2 of 1968) commenced on 9 February 1968, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Liquor (Drinking in Public Places) (Levuka) Regulations 1968.

## reg 2 Prohibition

No person shall, whether or not in or on any vehicle, drink liquor within the town—

(a) on or in any road, street or lane; or

(b) except with the consent of the person or authority in control, within any public park, reserve, playground or garden.
