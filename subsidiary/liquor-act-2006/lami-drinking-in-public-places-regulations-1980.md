# LAMI (DRINKING IN PUBLIC PLACES) REGULATIONS 1980

Lami (Drinking in Public Places) Regulations 1980 (LN 176 of 1980) commenced on 21 November 1980, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Lami (Drinking in Public Places) Regulations 1980.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations—

**liquor** means any spirit, wine, ale, beer, porter, cider, perry, hop beer or any liquor of a strength exceeding 3% of proof spirit, but does not include methylated spirits.

## reg 3 Prohibition

No person shall, whether or not in or on any vehicle, drink liquor within the town of Lami—

(a) on any street; or

(b) except with the consent in writing of the authority in control, within any public park, reserve, playground or garden or on any land owned or controlled by the council of the said town.
