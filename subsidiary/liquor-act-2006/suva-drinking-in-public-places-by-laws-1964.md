# SUVA (DRINKING IN PUBLIC PLACES) BY-LAWS 1964

Suva (Drinking in Public Places) By-laws (LN 136 of 1964) commenced on 24 December 1964, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Suva (Drinking in Public Places) By-laws 1964.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws—

**liquor** means any spirit, wine, ale, beer, porter, cider, perry, hop beer, or any such liquor of a strength exceeding 3% of proof spirit but does not include methylated spirits.

## by-law 3 Prohibition

No person shall, whether or not in or on any vehicle, drink liquor within the city of Suva—

(a) on any street; or

(b) except with the consent in writing of the authority in control, within any public park, reserve, playground or garden or on any land owned or controlled by the council.
