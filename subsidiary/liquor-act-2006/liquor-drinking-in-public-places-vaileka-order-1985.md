# LIQUOR (DRINKING IN PUBLIC PLACES) (VAILEKA) ORDER 1985

Liquor (Drinking in Public Places) (Vaileka) Order 1985 (LN 14 of 1985) commenced on 8 March 1985, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Liquor (Drinking in Public Places) (Vaileka) Order 1985.

## O 2 Prohibition

No person shall, whether or not in or on any vehicle, drink liquor—

(a) on or in any road, street or lane; or

(b) except with the consent of the person or authority in control, within any public park, reserve, playground or garden,

within a radius of 2 kilometres of the Vaileka Post Office.
