# LIQUOR REGULATIONS 2008

Liquor Regulations 2008 (LN 60 of 2008) commenced on 1 April 2008, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Fiji Islands Trade and Investment Bureau (Amendment) Decree 2011 (No 28 of 2011) | 21 June 2011 |
| Liquor (Amendment) Regulations 2018 (LN 62 of 2018) | 1 August 2018 |
| Investment Act 2021 (No 5 of 2021) | 1 August 2022 |

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Liquor Regulations 2008.

(2) These Regulations are deemed to have come into force on 1 April 2008.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**fees** means the fees set out in Schedule 1 ; and

**Form** means a Form with a number set out in Schedule 2 .

## reg 3 Application for liquor licence

(1) For the purpose of section 25 of the Act—

(a) the prescribed application form is set out as Form 1 ; and

(b) the application fee is prescribed in Schedule 1 .

(2) For the purposes of section 25(1)(c) of the Act—

(a) an application for on-premises use and off-premises use shall include the following particulars—

(i) the certificate of registration of the company or other legal entity issued by the Registrar of Companies; and

(ii) the original or a certified copy of the birth certificate of the person to whom the licence will be issued;

(b) an application by a foreigner, in addition to subparagraphs (a)(i) and (ii) must also include—

(i) [Repealed]

(ii) a copy of the work permit issued by the Immigration Department;

(c) an application for club use must include the valid certificate of registration, as required under the Registration of Clubs Act 1932 .

[subreg (2) am Decree 28 of 2011 s 4, 21 June 2011 ; am Act 5 of 2021 s 25(5), 1 August 2022]

## reg 4 Plans

(1) For the purpose of section 14 of the Act, a certified copy of location plan and the detailed block plan of the premises from the Central Board of Health or a person or body appointed by the Central Board of Health or relevant city or town council of the area, where the business of selling or consumption of liquor will take place must be attached to the application.

[subreg (1) am LN 62 of 2018 reg 2, 1 August 2018]

(2) The detailed block plan required by subregulation (1) must specify the location of

(a) the bar area and the space for consumption of liquor, “restricted” and “supervised” areas if the application is made for on premises use licence;

(b) strong room for the storage of liquor;

(c) toilet facilities for separate use by males, females and disabled people; and

(d) dining and other areas required for the purpose of the licence.

## reg 5 Advertisement and hearing of applications

(1) Any application for a licence shall be advertised as required under section 13 of the Act and a reasonable time shall be given after such advertisement by an Authority for the hearing of the application.

(2) Notice of the application required under section 25(4) of the Act is set out as Form 2 in Schedule 2.

## reg 6 Objections

(1) The Secretary of the Authority must—

(a) upon receipt of any objection to any application, record on the objection the date the objection was received and his or her signature; and

(b) advise the objector that the objection has been received.

(2) An objector must be given 14 clear days written notice of the date and place for hearing the objection.

## reg 7 Preliminary provisional approvals

The preliminary provisional provided approval under section 12 of the Act, does not permit the selling, supplying or consumption of liquor unless the conditions of such preliminary provisional approval have been fully met and duly accepted by the agencies mentioned in section 27 of the Act.

## reg 8 Forms of licence

(1) A licence issued under section 19 of the Act shall be in Form 3 in Schedule 2.

(2) If a licence is suspended or cancelled, the annual licence fee covering the period of the suspension or cancellation until the expiry of the licence shall not be refunded but forfeited to the State unless the licence is restored before its date of expiry.

## reg 9 Appeal against the decision of an Authority

(1) An appeal pursuant to section 18 of the Act must be filed within 21 days from the date of notification of the decision to the licensee.

(2) An appeal pursuant to section 18 or 23 of the Act must—

(a) be in writing;

(b) set out the grounds of appeal;

(c) be submitted to the Secretary of the Board;

(d) be accompanied by the application fee as set out in Schedule 1 .

(3) The appellant as the case may be, must within 7 days after filing an appeal with the Board, provide a copy to the Authority and obtain an acknowledgement and date of receipt in writing from the Secretary of the authority, and send the acknowledgement to the Secretary of the Board.

(4) Upon receipt of the appeal, the Authority shall within 21 days—

(a) respond to the grounds of appeal and file a copy with the Secretary to the Board; and

(b) thereafter serve a copy of its response on the licensee or applicant or objector as the case may be.

(5) Both parties are entitled to be represented by a legal practitioner or by a person authorised in writing by the appellant or respondent in an appeal.

(6) When hearing and determining an appeal, the Board must—

(a) accord natural justice to the parties;

(b) be bound by rules of evidence of a court of law;

(c) keep a written record of its proceedings;

(d) state the reasoning for its decision on the appeal.

(7) The Board may decide not to proceed with an appeal if it is satisfied that the ground of appeal is frivolous or vexatious, and must advise the appellant of its decision.

## reg 10 Attempt to influence the Board

(1) A person who attempts to influence the Board or a member of the Board in an appeal commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine of $400 or to imprisonment not exceeding 6 months.

(2) Nothing in subregulation (1) prohibits a person (including a witness, licensee, applicant, objector or the representative of a party) from giving information or making representations in respect of an appeal at the request or invitation of the Authority or the Board, at the hearing of an appeal.
