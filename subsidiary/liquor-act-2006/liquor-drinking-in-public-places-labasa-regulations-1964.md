# LIQUOR (DRINKING IN PUBLIC PLACES) (LABASA) REGULATIONS 1964

Liquor (Drinking in Public Places) (Labasa) Regulations 1964 (LN 3 of 1965) commenced on 15 January 1965, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Liquor (Drinking in Public Places) (Labasa) Regulations 1964.

## reg 2 Prohibition

No person shall, whether or not in or on any vehicle, drink liquor within the town—

(a) on or in any road, street or lane; or

(b) except with the consent of the person or authority in control, within any public park, reserve, playground or garden.
