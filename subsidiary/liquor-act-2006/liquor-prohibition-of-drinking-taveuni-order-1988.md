# LIQUOR (PROHIBITION OF DRINKING) (TAVEUNI) ORDER 1988

Liquor (Prohibition of Drinking) (Taveuni) Order 1988 (LN 89 of 1988) commenced on 2 September 1988, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Liquor (Prohibition of Drinking) (Taveuni) Order 1988.

## O 2 Prohibition

Any person found drinking liquor in the places described in the Schedule hereto shall be guilty of an offence and on conviction shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $40.

## 

### SCHEDULE PROHIBITED BOUNDARIES

Taveuni Coastal Road

(a) South end of Wairiki Catholic Mission School boundary with Koroni No 1 to the northern end of Lamini bridge;

(b) all other roads which branch off from the main Taveuni Coastal road between points at (a) above including Waiyevo Government Station Road and ground, Tagimaucia Road, Vione Road, Tavuki Road, Government Wharf Road, Waiyevo landing for small crafts and Nagara Shopping Centre.

Taveuni Coastal Road From Maravu resort and Matei boundary to the end of Naselesele bridge.
