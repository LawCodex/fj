# NASINU TOWN (DRINKING IN PUBLIC PLACES) REGULATIONS 2003

Nasinu Town (Drinking in Public Places) Regulations 2003 (LN 62 of 2003) commenced on 7 November 2003, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Nasinu Town (Drinking in Public Places) Regulations 2003.

## reg 2 Prohibition

A person who, whether or not on any vehicle, drinks liquor within the town—

(a) on or in any road, street or lane; or

(b) except with the consent of the person or authority in control, within any public park, reserve, playground or garden,

commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine of $40.
