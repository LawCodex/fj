## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These regulations may be cited as the National Bank of Fiji Restructuring (Dissolution) Regulations 1998.

(2) These regulations are deemed to have been made on 29 June 1998.

(3) These regulations are deemed to have come into force on 1 July 1998.

## reg 2 Dissolution Day

For the purposes of the Act, the dissolution day is 2 September 1998.

## reg 3 Corporatisation Day

For the purposes of the Act, the corporatisation day is 1 September 1998.
