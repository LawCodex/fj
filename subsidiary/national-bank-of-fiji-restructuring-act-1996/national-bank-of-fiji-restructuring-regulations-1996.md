## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [regs 1, 2]

### reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Bank of Fiji Restructuring Regulations 1996.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on the day that they are published in the Gazette.

### reg 2 Interpretation

(1) In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**the Act** means the National Bank of Fiji Restructuring Act 1996;

**AMB** means NBF Asset Management Bank;

**Bank** means NBF or AMB, as the case may be;

**branch**, in relation to the Bank, includes the head office of the Bank;

**document** includes a book, account, roll, record, register, paper and a microfilm, print, tape, disc and computer roll; and

**NBF** means the National Bank of Fiji.

(2) Subject to any express provisions in these Regulations, expressions defined in the Act have the same meanings in these Regulations.

## PART 2 PROVISIONS RELATING TO EACH BANK [regs 3–22]

### reg 3 Information to be furnished

The Bank may require a person who wishes to transact banking business with the Bank to furnish to the Bank particulars of his or her name, address and occupation and such other particulars as, in the opinion of the Bank, are necessary to enable the Bank to conduct that business properly.

### reg 4 Bank not bound to inquire as to manner of operating accounts etc

The Bank shall not be under any liability by reason of its failure to ascertain that the method in which a firm, company, society, local authority, club or other body of persons operates an account, deals with any security or transacts any other business with the Bank is in accordance with any deed, articles, rules or other instrument of any such body.

### reg 5 Powers of attorney etc

When any business is to be transacted with the Bank under a power of attorney, probate of a will or letters of administration to an estate, the Bank may require the original power of attorney, probate or letters of administration to an estate, the Bank may require the original power of attorney, probate or letters of administration to be produced to the Bank and may require a copy of the power of attorney, probate or letters of administration to be lodged with the Bank.

### reg 6 Deposits may be refused in certain cases

(1) The Bank may, if it considers that to do so would be in accordance with established bank practice—

(a) refuse to open an account for a person; or

(b) refuse to continue accepting a person as a depositor.

(2) Where the Bank refuses to continue to accept a person as a depositor, the Bank shall pay to that person any money standing to the credit of his or her account, including accrued interest.

### reg 7 Joint accounts

Where money has been deposited with the Bank in an account in the names of 2 or more persons, the Bank may, on the death of any of those persons, pay the money standing to the credit of the account to the survivor or survivors, and the receipt of the money by that survivor or those survivors shall be a good and complete discharge to the Bank.

### reg 8 Trustees

(1) Where money has been deposited with the Bank by a person or persons in his or her or their names but in trust for any other person—

(a) the Bank may allow the name of another person, as a trustee, to be substituted for the name of an existing or deceased trustee;

(b) the Bank may pay any amount standing to the credit of the account to the trustee or trustees and the receipt of the money by the trustee or trustees shall be a good and complete discharge to the Bank; and

(c) the Bank may, on the death of a sole trustee or the last surviving trustee, pay the money standing to the credit of the account of the person or persons entitled to the benefit of the trust funds and the receipt of the money by such beneficiary or beneficiaries shall be a good and complete discharge to the Bank.

(2) The Bank may, if it thinks fit, before it makes any payment to a trustee or trustees under subregulation (1), require the prior written consent of all persons entitled to any interest in the trust funds.

(3) Where money has been deposited with the Bank by a person or persons in trust for any other person, no person other than a trustee of the money may claim against the Bank in respect of the money.

(4) Subregulation (3) does not operate to relieve any trustee from any liability to account for or apply money held in trust by the Bank in accordance with the law.

### reg 9 Accounts opened by proxies

Where a person opens an account as proxy for another person, the Bank may withhold the passbook for the account, and refuse to allow withdrawals from the account, until a specimen of the depositor's signature, witnessed by the proxy or another person approved by the Bank, has been received by the Bank.

### reg 10 Accounts of local authorities etc

(1) Where a local authority, company, friendly society, co-operative society, or any other society, body or club opens an account with the Bank, it shall furnish to the Bank through its secretary, treasurer or other appropriate officer—

(a) an authority in a form required by the Bank specifying the names of the persons who may operate and draw cheques on the account; and

(b) any information required by the Bank.

(2) Where a local authority, company, friendly society, co-operative society, or any other society, body or club, desires to make a change in the persons authorised to operate its account, a further authority in a form required by the Bank shall be furnished to the Bank.

(3) Where the Bank—

(a) pays an amount standing to the credit of an account referred to in subregulation (1) to a person whose name is specified in an authority furnished in relation to the account in accordance with any of the provisions of this regulation; or

(b) pays a cheque drawn on an account referred to in subregulation (1) by a person whose name is specified in an authority furnished in relation to the account in accordance with any of the provisions of this regulation,

the receipt of the amount by the person or the payment of the cheque, as the case may be, shall be a good and complete discharge to the Bank.

### reg 11 Authority to operate an account

Where a depositor wishes his or her account with the Bank to be operated by a person other than himself or herself, the Bank may require him or her to furnish an authority in accordance with a form required by the Bank.

### reg 12 Passbooks

(1) Where the Bank issues a passbook to a depositor, the passbook shall remain the property of the Bank and shall be delivered to the Bank whenever so required by the Bank.

(2) Whenever a deposit or withdrawal is made in relation to an account for which a passbook has been issued to a depositor, the passbook shall be produced to the Bank unless, in its discretion, the Bank determines otherwise.

### reg 13 Production of passbook for inspection

(1) Where the Bank issues a passbook to a depositor, the depositor shall, at least once in each year, produce his or her passbook for examination and verification against the books of the Bank at such time and place as the Bank shall determine.

(2) The Bank shall give notice of the time and place at which a depositor's passbook is to be so produced.

### reg 14 False entries in passbooks

No person (unless he or she is specifically authorised by the Bank to do so) shall—

(a) make any entry whatsoever in any passbook; or

(b) erase or alter in any way an entry in a passbook.

### reg 15 Lost passbooks

(1) If a depositor loses his or her passbook, he or she shall forthwith give notice in writing of the loss to the Bank.

(2) If a depositor wishes to have a new passbook issued to him or her, or to close the account on the loss of his or her passbook, the Bank may require him or her—

(a) to make a statutory declaration as to the loss of the passbook;

(b) to satisfy the Bank as to his or her identity;

(c) to give an indemnity satisfactory to the Bank.

(3) The Bank may, after the expiration of 14 days from the date of receipt by the Bank of the notice of the loss of the passbook, and subject to compliance with any requirements made under subregulation (2), issue to the depositor a new passbook showing the amount standing to the credit of the depositor in the records of the Bank, or pay that amount to him or her.

### reg 16 Deposits to be made at offices or agencies of the Bank during opening hours

A depositor shall not have any claim against the Bank in respect of any deposit unless the deposit was made at the Bank during the hours during which the Bank was open for the receipt of deposits.

### reg 17 Calculation of interest

(1) Interest on deposits may be calculated in such manner as the Bank shall from time to time determine.

(2) Interest so calculated shall be credited to deposit accounts at such times as the Bank shall from time to time determine.

### reg 18 Withdrawals

(1) Before any money standing to the credit of an account is withdrawn, the Bank may require a withdrawal form, signed by the depositor or other person to whom the money is lawfully payable, to be presented to the Bank.

(2) Any money to be withdrawn from the Bank may be paid to the person presenting the withdrawal form, and if that person is not the depositor or other person to whom the money is lawfully payable, the receipt of the money by the person presenting the withdrawal form shall, for all purposes, have the same effect as the receipt of the money by the depositor or other person to whom the money is lawfully payable.

### reg 19 Deposits by infants

(1) The Bank may receive and accept deposits from or for the use or benefit of infants.

(2) The Bank may pay to an infant who can sign his or her name any money standing to the credit of that infant in the Bank in the same manner and upon the same conditions as are applicable to depositors other than infants, and the receipt of the money by the infant shall be a good and complete discharge to the Bank.

(3) The Bank may pay to the parent or guardian of an infant who cannot sign his or her name any money standing to the credit of that infant in the Bank, and the receipt of the money by such parent or guardian shall be a good and complete discharge to the Bank.

### reg 20 Married women's deposits

Every deposit in the name of a woman, whether made before or after her marriage shall, as against her husband and any person claiming through or under him, be deemed to be her separate property and to belong to her in her own right as if she were unmarried.

### reg 21 Death of depositors

The Bank shall not be under any liability by reason of the withdrawal of, or other dealing with, any money standing to the credit of the account of a deceased depositor before it has received formal notice of his or her death.

### reg 22 Charges on deposits and withdrawals

The Bank may require a depositor to pay a charge, at such rate as the Bank shall from time to time determine, on any amount transferred or remitted from one place to another, whether by way of deposit or withdrawal.

## PART 3 PROVISIONS RELATING TO THE FUNDING OF AMB [regs 23]

### reg 23 Provisions in deeds or agreements

For the purposes of section 65(2)(g) of the Act, any deed or agreement entered into under section 65(1) of the Act may include provisions providing for or giving effect to any one or more of the following:

(a) the transfer from time to time of any money in the trust account to the settlement account for the purpose of providing AMB with funds to meet its operating costs;

(b) the terms upon which transfers of money from the trust account to the settlement account, or from the settlement account to the trust account, shall be made, including (where a transfer is made by way of a loan) a term that no interest shall be payable;

(c) the raising of a loan or other borrowing by the State, which loan or borrowing shall not exceed the sum of $220 million.

## PART 4 TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS [regs 24, 25]

### reg 24 Access to documents

Where either NBF or AMB reasonably requires any document from the other in order to carry out its functions under the Act—

(a) an employee of NBF or AMB (as the case may be) shall make a written request for the document to the other, including the reason and the length of time for which the document is required;

(b) on receiving a request that complies with paragraph (a), NBF or AMB (as the case may be) shall provide the document (or, where necessary, a copy of that document) to the other as soon as practicable;

(c) where NBF or AMB provides a document under paragraph (b), it may charge the other any reasonable costs of providing the document;

(d) any document requested and provided under this regulation shall be returned to NBF or AMB (as the case may be) as soon as practicable after use.

### reg 25 AMB permitted to accept NBF cheques

AMB may accept as a cheque drawn on it, a cheque which states that it is drawn on NBF (whether that cheque is drawn on, before or after the date on which the Minister approves the principal restructuring plan), provided that the cheque relates to a customer's account which has been transferred to AMB by virtue of this Act.
