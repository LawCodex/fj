# BRANDS REGULATIONS 1965

**Table of Amendments**

Brands Regulations 1965 (LN 173 of 1965) commenced on 3 December 1965, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Brands (Amendment) Regulations 1974 (LN 211 of 1974) | 1 January 1975 |
| Brands (Amendment) Regulations 1979 (LN 24 of 1979) | 1 February 1979 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Brands Regulations 1965.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, the expression “the Act” means the Brands Act 1928.

## reg 3 Form of register, certificate, applications and notices

(1) Every register, certificate, application and notice under the Act shall be kept, issued, made or given in the appropriate form set out in Schedule 1.

(2) Any authority or person having power to issue any certificate under the provisions of the Act shall, on it being proved to his or her satisfaction that such certificate has been lost or destroyed and on payment of the prescribed fee, issue a duplicate of the certificate to the person entitled thereto.

## reg 4 Register of brands

The particulars to be recorded in the register shall be those specified in Form Aof Schedule 1.

## reg 5 Notice of registration of brands

The notice referred to in section 7 of the Act shall be published in the Gazette in Form B of Schedule 1.

## reg 6 Advertisement of brand

For the purpose of advertising particulars of registration of any brand under the provisions of sections 7 and 8 of the Act, the registrar may require the applicant to furnish a wood block or electrotype of the brand, of such dimensions as may be directed by the registrar.

## reg 7 Date of registration

When a brand is registered the date on which the application was received by the registrar shall be deemed to be the date of registration.

## reg 8 Certified copies

Any person may upon application and payment of the prescribed fee receive a certified copy of any application made under the Act.

## reg 9 Fees

The fees set out in Schedule 2 shall be payable to the registrar in respect of the several matters specified therein.
