# TRAFFIC LIGHTS (ESTABLISHMENT) (LABASA) ORDER 1991

Traffic Lights (Establishment) (Labasa) Order 1991 (LN 1 of 1991) commenced on 18 January 1991, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## O 1 Short title and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Traffic Lights (Establishment) (Labasa) Order 1991.

(2) In this Order, the “Act” means the Land Transport Act 1998 .

[para (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 291, 1 December 2016]

## O 2 Establishment of traffic lights

The traffic lights and related road markings situated at the location, in the town of Labasa, described in the Schedule to this Order are hereby established as traffic lights for the crossing of Nasekula Road and Jaduram Street at that location.

## O 3 Traffic lights to be obeyed

Every driver of a vehicle, pedestrian or other person using the roads mentioned in paragraph 2, or either of them at the aforesaid location shall obey the signals conveyed by the traffic lights established by this Order, as signified in regulation 19 of the Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1975.

## O 4 Penalty for contravention

Any person who fails to obey the signals conveyed by the traffic lights established by this Order shall be guilty of an offence under the Act and shall be punished accordingly.

## 

### SCHEDULE LOCATION

Nasekula Road — 55 metres west of its junction with Jaduram Street.
