## O 1 Short title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Parking Meters (Sigatoka) Order 1991 and shall come into force on a date to be notified by the Council in the Gazette.

## O 2 Interpretation

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires—

**commercial vehicle** means any vehicle used principally for the carriage of goods or in the course of trade;

**Council** means the Sigatoka Town Council;

**exempt period** means any of the following periods—

(a) Sunday;

(b) a public holiday;

(c) Saturday before 8.00 am or after 1.00 pm;

(d) any other day before 8.00 am and after 5.00 pm;

[def am LN 67 of 1994 O 3, 1 July 1994]

**loading zone** means an area established and set aside by council and marked out on the surface of a street or road and designated by signs including the words “Loading Zone” as a place for a commercial vehicle or for commercial vehicles to stand whilst being loaded or unloaded;

**metered space** means a space in a metered zone marked out pursuant to the provisions of this Order by painted lines for the accommodation of a vehicle and at which a parking meter has been installed;

**metered zone** means any street or portion of a street or any piece of land prescribed under the provisions of this Order as a place where vehicles may park and at which parking meters may be established and maintained;

**parking meter** means a mechanical apparatus designed for the purpose of measuring and indicating the time within which a vehicle is or may be parked at a metered space and installed thereat pursuant to the provisions of this Order and includes the stand to which the meter is affixed;

**prescribed coin** means a coin of Fiji legal tender prescribed as the fee payable for parking at a metered space;

**prescribed indication** means the red flag or other device contained in a parking meter, which shows that the time for which a motor vehicle may be parked in a metered space has expired or that no payment for its parking has been made;

**registered owner** means the person who is recorded in the records of the Land Transport Authority as the owner of the vehicles;

[def am LN 99 of 2016 reg 195, 1 December 2016]

**town clerk** means the town clerk for the time being of the Council and includes a deputy town clerk or any person duly authorised to act as town clerk; and

**traffic attendant** means an officer of the Council holding the office of the traffic attendant or any other officer duly authorised in that behalf.

## O 3 Metered zones

Each of the areas specified in the Schedule to this Order is hereby designated a metered zone for motor vehicles.

## O 4 Fees

(1) The fee for the occupation by the motor vehicle of a metered space in a—

(a) high density traffic as specified in Part A of the Schedule, $0.20 in respect of every 15 minutes; and

(b) low density area as specified in Part B of the Schedule, $0.20 in respect of every 30 minutes.

(2) Any person may, without payment of any fee park—

(a) (for so long as is reasonably necessary for the purpose) a commercial vehicle in a loading zone for the purpose of delivering goods from such vehicle or to loading goods on to such vehicles; and

(b) a motor vehicle in a metered space during any exempt period, subject to such parking time limits and restrictions as are prescribed by a traffic order or by-law of the Council for the time being in force.

[O 4 subst LN 38 of 2010 O 2, 26 March 2010]

## O 5 Parking of motor vehicle

(1) A driver of a motor vehicle shall park the vehicle in a metered space in such a position that the vehicle shall be entirely within the metered space, unless by reason of its size such vehicle cannot be completely contained therein.

(2) If a parked vehicle, by reason of its size, occupy or encroach on more than one metered space, the driver of such vehicle shall pay a parking fee in respect of each metered space so occupied or encroached on.

(3) No driver of a motor vehicle shall park such vehicle in a metered space already occupied by another vehicle.

## O 6 Parking of motorcycles

(1) It shall be lawful for any number of motorcycles to occupy any metered space at the same time without payment of more than one parking fee.

(2) If a metered space is parallel to the kerb or footpath, no motorcycle shall be parked in such a manner that any part of it lies within 120 centimetres of the rear extremity of the metered space.

(3) Where more than one motorcycle occupy a metered space no one of such motorcycle shall remain therein when the time for which payment has been made has expired.

## O 7 Direction parked vehicles

A driver of a vehicle, when parking in a metered space, shall park the vehicle so that it shall be headed in the general direction of the movement of traffic on the side of the street on which the vehicle is parked.

## O 8 Operation of meters

(1) The driver of a vehicle parked in a metered space shall forthwith deposit or cause to be deposited in the parking meter provided at such space the prescribed coin, as indicated on the parking meter and, thereafter the metered space may be lawfully occupied by the vehicle only during the period indicated on the parking meter, provided that a driver of a vehicle may without payment of any fee park the vehicle during any time indicated on the parking meter as being unexpired from its previous use.

(2) Unless the total period allowed for the parking of a vehicle in a metered space has been exceeded, the driver of the vehicle may, upon the expiration of the authorised period of parking deposit or cause to be deposited further the prescribed coin in the parking meter to set the parking meter in operation and thereafter the vehicle maybe lawfully parked in the metered space for the further period indicated on the parking meter.

(3) No driver of any vehicle shall cause, allow, permit or suffer such vehicle to remain parked in a metered space while the parking meter at such metered space shows that the authorised parking period has expired.

## O 9 Excess charge

(1) If a motor vehicle is parked in a metered space during the prescribed hours whilst the parking meter for that metered space is displaying the prescribed indication, an excess charge (hereinafter referred as the ‘excess charge’) shall be payable as follows—

(a) excess charge of $2 if such excess charge is paid within the prescribed period in order 10(1) and before demand notice issued to the registered owner of the motor vehicle in terms of order 10(2);

(b) excess charge of $10 if such excess charge is paid after the issue of demand notice to the registered owner of the motor vehicle in terms of order 10(2) .

[para (1) subst LN 1 of 1993 O 2, 1 January 1993am LN 34 of 1996 O 3, 22 March 1996]

(1A) For the purposes of the preceding subsection, the demand notice to the registered owner shall be deemed to be the date of issue of the demand notice.

[para (1A) insrt LN 1 of 1993 O 2, 1 January 1993]

(2) Where excess charge is incurred in respect of any vehicle, a traffic attendant shall attach to the vehicle, in a conspicious position, a notice which shall include the following particulars—

(a) the registration number of the vehicle;

(b) the date and the time at which he or she first noticed that the parking meter displayed the prescribed indication;

(c) a statement that an excess charge of $4 is payable;

(d) a statement as to where and by what date such excess charge is payable;

(e) a statement that it is an offence under the Land Transport Act 1998 for the driver of a motor vehicle who has parked the vehicle in a metered space to fail duly to pay any excess charge incurred;

(f) the number of the parking meter and that of the stand on which it is for the time being mounted.

[para (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 195, 1 December 2016]

(3) Where a notice has been attached to a vehicle in accordance with the provisions of paragraph (2) it shall be an offence for any person, not being the driver of the vehicle or a traffic attendant, to remove the notice unless authorised to do so by the driver.

(4) Where a traffic attendant has taken the action prescribed by paragraph (2) the vehicle may remain parked in the metered space for a period of 2 hours from the time specified in the notice attached under paragraph (2) without becoming liable to pay a further excess charge.

## O 10 Payment of excess charge

(1) The excess charge shall be delivered or sent by post so as to reach the town clerk's office at the Sigatoka Town Council, Sigatoka no later than 4 o'clock in the afternoon of the seventh day after the day on which the excess charge was incurred.

(2) In the event of the excess charge not being paid in accordance with paragraph (1) , the Council shall serve on the registered owner of the vehicle a demand in writing requiring payment of the charge within a period of 14 days after the service of such demand.

(3) If a registered owner upon whom a demand has been served under paragraph (2) fails to pay the sum of money demanded within the period therein specified, the Council may institute proceedings against such registered owner in accordance with the provisions of the Act.

## O 11 Unauthorised dealing with parking meters

No person shall without due authority—

(a) affix or attempt to affix any placard, advertisement, notice, list, document, board or any such thing on any parking meter; or

(b) paint, write upon or disfigure any parking meter.

## O 12 Meters to be installed and maintained

This Order shall be of no force or effect in relation to any street or road specified in the Schedule unless parking meters have been installed there and shall be of no force or effect in relation to any metered space if the parking meter installed thereat is out of order.

## O 13 Temporary discontinuance of metered space

(1) Whenever a traffic attendant or other authorised officer is of the opinion that any metered space or spaces should be temporarily discontinued as a parking space, the said officer may place or erect, or cause to be placed or erected, a sign or other device indicating “NO STOPPING” at such metered space or spaces, and it shall be unlawful for any person to park, stand or stop a vehicle at such metered space or spaces while such sign or device is so placed or erected.

(2) Whenever the use of a metered space is temporarily discontinued pursuant to paragraph (1) by reason of any building or contracting operations on any land adjacent thereto, by any contractor or other person at whose request or by reason of whose operations on such land the temporary discontinuance has occurred, such fee as shall be fixed from time to time by the Council shall be paid by the contractor or such other person.

(3) Whenever the use of any metered space is temporarily discontinued pursuant to paragraph (1) by reason of any such building or contracting operations, any authorised officer of the Council may permit the use of any space for the parking, standing, or stopping of vehicles in connection with the building or contracting operations and such authorised use shall not be an offence under this Order.

## O 14 Exemption

The provisions of this Order shall not apply to drivers—

(a) while engaged in operating vehicles on the site of road maintenance or construction or maintenance work on power or telephone lines or equipment;

(b) conveying members of the Fiji Police Force on urgent police duty;

(c) of vehicles of any fire brigade at the scene of any fire or alarm of fire;

(d) of ambulances answering an urgent call;

(e) operating vehicles in accordance with directions given to them by a police officer;

(f) operating vehicles assigned in garbage collection or in connection with the installation or maintenance of water supplies.

## O 14A Offences in respect of traffic attendant

A person who—

(1) obstructs, prevents, hinders or interferes with the traffic attendant in the performance of any duty under these Orders;

(2) personates or falsely pretends to be a traffic attendant;

(3) gives or agrees to give or offers to a traffic attendant any gift or consideration as an inducement or reward for any act done or to be done or for any forbearance observed or to be observed or for any favour shown or to be shown by him or her or who, being such a traffic officer, accepts or agrees to accept or solicits any such gift or consideration;

(4) offers violence to, assaults, threatens, intimidates, uses threatening language towards, or behalves in a threatening manner to any traffic attendant while he or she is acting in the exercise of his or her powers or the discharge of his or her duties under these Orders;

shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $50 or in case of second or subsequent conviction to a fine not exceeding $100.

[O 14A insrt LN 2 of 1993 O 2, 1 January 1993]

## O 15 Offences

(1) The driver of any vehicle who contravenes or fails to comply with any of the provisions of this Order shall be guilty of an offence under the Land Transport Act 1998 .

[para (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 195, 1 December 2016]

(2) Any person, whether or not the driver of a vehicle, who contravenes or fails to comply with the provisions of order 9(3) or 11 shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $50.
