# TRAFFIC LIGHTS (ESTABLISHMENT) (LAUTOKA) ORDER 1981

Traffic Lights (Establishment) (Lautoka) Order 1981 (LN 32 of 1981) commenced on 10 April 1981, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Traffic Lights (Establishment) (Lautoka) Order 1981.

## O 2 Establishment of traffic lights

The traffic lights and related road markings situated at the location in the city of Lautoka described in the Schedule are established as traffic lights for the crossing of the roads of Vitogo Parade and Namoli Avenue at that location.

## O 3 Obeying of signals

Every driver of a vehicle, pedestrian or other person using the road mentioned in order 2 , or either of them, at the aforesaid location, shall obey the signals conveyed by the traffic lights established by this Order as signified in regulation 19 of the Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1975.

## 

### SCHEDULE LOCATION

At the intersection of Vitogo Parade and Namoli Avenue in the city of Lautoka.
