# WAINIVULA (NO STOPPING) ORDER 1982

Wainivula (No Stopping) Order 1982 (LN 101 of 1982) commenced on 24 September 1982, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Wainivula (No Stopping) Order 1982.

## O 2 Interpretation

In this Order—

**stop** or stopping means the coming to rest of a vehicle except when necessary to avoid a collision or in compliance with the direction of a police officer or a traffic sign.

## O 3 Prohibition on stopping

No person shall at any time of the day or night stop any vehicle on that part of King’s Road at Wainivula described in the Schedule and signified by a “No Stopping” traffic sign.
