# KOROVOU (TAILEVU) TRAFFIC ORDER 1986

Korovou (Tailevu) Traffic Order 1986 (LN 1 of 1986) commenced on 3 January 1986, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |
| Korovou (Tailevu) Traffic (Amendment) Order 2018 (LN 54 of 2018) | 1 August 2018 |

## O 1 Short title and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Korovou (Tailevu) Traffic Order 1986.

(2) This Order shall apply to the area within the boundaries of the Korovou (Tailevu) rural town planning area.

## O 2 Interpretation

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires—

**bus** means a public service vehicle constructed or adapted to carry more than 6 passengers excluding the driver;

**stop** in respect of a vehicle, means the coming to rest of the vehicle except where necessary—

(a) to avoid a collision; or

(b) to comply with the direction of a police officer or a traffic sign; and

**taxi** means a public service vehicle licensed to carry not more than 6 passengers excluding the driver.

## O 3 Public stands for goods vehicles

(1) The places specified in Schedule 1 are goods vehicle stands.

(2) A person shall not stop or park a vehicle, other than a goods vehicle, on a goods vehicle stand.

## O 4 Taxi stands

(1) The places specified in Schedule 2 are taxi stands.

(2) A person shall not stop or park a vehicle, other than a taxi, on a taxi stand.

(3) A person shall not stop or park a taxi on a taxi stand if that stand is already occupied by the number of vehicles indicated on a traffic sign relating to that stand.

(4) When a taxi vacates a position on a taxi stand where taxis stand front to rear, the driver of the taxi in the position behind the taxi vacating the position shall move his or her taxi into that position without delay.

(5) The driver of a taxi on a taxi stand shall remain constantly in attendance on that taxi while it is on the stand.

## O 5 Bus stands

(1) The places specified in Schedule 3 are bus stands.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3) , a person shall not stop or park a vehicle on a bus stand.

(3) Subject to paragraph (4) , the driver of a bus may allow his or her vehicle to stand on a bus stand for the purpose of loading or unloading passengers and their luggage.

(4) The driver of a bus shall not stand his or her bus on a bus stand—

(a) more than 15 minutes before the departure time of the bus where the intended journey of the bus ends a distance of less than 50 kilometres from the stand; or

(b) more than 30 minutes before the departure time of the bus where the intended journey of the bus ends a distance of 50 kilometres or more from the stand.

(5) The driver of a bus standing on a bus stand for the purpose of unloading passengers and their luggage shall not allow the bus to remain there longer than is necessary for that purpose.

(6) The driver of a bus standing on a bus stand shall remain constantly in attendance of that bus while it is on the stand.

(7) A person shall not drive a vehicle, other than a bus, on a bus stand.

(8) A person shall not drive a bus on a bus stand at more than 10 kilometres an hour.

## O 6 Exemptions

(1) This Order does not apply to—

(a) a vehicle of a registered medical practitioner who is in the course of rendering his or her professional services to any person at any place other than his or her normal place of practice, which has affixed to its windscreen a sticker supplied by the relevant municipal council identifying such vehicle as that of a registered medical practitioner;

(b) a vehicle in use by members of the Fiji Police Force on urgent police duty;

(c) a fire brigade vehicle dealing with any fire or alarm of fire;

(d) an ambulance dealing with an urgent call or emergency;

(e) a vehicle on the site of road maintenance or construction or maintenance work on power or telegraph lines or equipment while engaged on such work;

(f) a vehicle operating in accordance with directions given by a uniformed member of the Fiji Police Force; and

(g) a vehicle engaged in garbage collection or operating in connection with the installation or maintenance of water services.

(2) Notwithstanding any other provisions of this Order, at any place where vehicles are assembled, any uniformed member of the Fiji Police Force may appoint a temporary stand for such vehicles or any class thereof and every person shall work such stand as directed by such member of the Fiji Police Force.
