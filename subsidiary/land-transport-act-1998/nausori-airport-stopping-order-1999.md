# NAUSORI AIRPORT (STOPPING) ORDER 1999

Nausori Airport (Stopping) Order 1999 (LN 96 of 1999) commenced on 23 July 1999, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Nausori Airport (Stopping) Order 1999.

## O 2 Interpretation

In this Order—

**stop** in relation to a vehicle means bringing the vehicle to rest other than as necessary to avoid a collision or in compliance with the directions of a police officer, an authorised officer or a traffic sign.

## O 3 Prohibition on driving

No person driving a vehicle must at any time stop the vehicle on the parts of Wainibokasi Road, Bau/Kuku and Visama Roads at Luvu Luvu described in the Schedule and signified by traffic lights.
