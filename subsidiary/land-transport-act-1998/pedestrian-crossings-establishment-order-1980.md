## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Pedestrian Crossings (Establishment) Order 1980.

## O 2 Establishment of crossings

The locations specified and described in the Schedule are established as crossings for pedestrians.

## O 3 Application of traffic regulations

The crossings established by this Order shall be pedestrian crossings for the purpose of regulation 60 of the Land Transport (Traffic) Regulations 2000.

[O 3 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 199, 1 December 2016]
