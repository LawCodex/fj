# MAKOI ROUNDABOUT (NO STOPPING) ORDER 1994

Makoi Roundabout (No Stopping) Order 1994 (LN 78 of 1994) commenced on 30 August 1994, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Makoi Roundabout (No Stopping) Order 1994.

## O 2 Interpretation

In this Order—

**stop** or **stopping** means the coming to rest of a vehicle except when necessary to avoid a collision or in compliance with the direction of a police officer or a traffic sign.

## O 3 Prohibition on stopping

No person shall at any time of the day or night stop any vehicle on that part Makoi roundabout described in the Schedule and signified by a “No Stopping” traffic sign.
