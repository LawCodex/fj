## reg 1 Short title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Land Transport (Fees and Penalties) Regulations 2000 and comes into force on 10 July 2000.

## reg 2 Prescribed fees

(1) The fees payable under the Act and regulations made under it are as prescribed in Schedule 1 .

(2) The following are exempted from paying the fee prescribed under the Act or any regulations made under it—

(a) the President of the State;

(b) the government of another country or an institution, an international organisation or a person to which or to whom section 6 , section 12(1) or section 12(2) of the Diplomatic Privileges and Immunities Act 1971 applies;

(c) the Government of the Republic of Fiji;

(d) any fire brigade established under the provisions of any Act in respect of motor vehicles operated by such fire brigade; and

(e) any authority, institution or person exempted under any other written law.

[subreg (2) am LN 43 of 2003 reg 2, 18 July 2003 LN 99 of 2016 reg 139, 1 December 2016]

(3) An authority, institution or person exempted from payment of fees under subregulation (2) must advise the Authority within 7 days of any change in circumstances as a result of which subregulation (2) no longer applies.

(4) Any authority, institution or person may be exempted from the payment of the Road User Levy by the Minister responsible for Transport after consultation with the Authority.

[subreg (4) insrt LN 121 of 2010 reg 2, 1 January 2010]

(5) Unless the cancellation of registration of a vehicle has been by way of a court order, an amnesty period on and from 1 August 2021 to 31 July 2022 is given to allow for the registration of a vehicle that has been off the road on and from 4 July 2016 without the payment of any charges or fees, including the Road User Levy and levies payable under the Accident Compensation Act 2017, for the period that the vehicle has not been on the road.

[subreg (5) subst LN 67 of 2021 reg 2, 31 July 2021]

(5A) Unless the cancellation of registration of a vehicle has been by way of a court order, an amnesty period on and from 1 August 2024 to 31 July 2025 is given to allow for the registration of a vehicle that has been off the road on and from 4 July 2016 to 31 July 2023 without the payment of any charges or fees, including the Road User Levy and levies payable under the Accident Compensation Act 2017, for the period that the vehicle has not been on the road.

[subreg (5A) insrt LN 73 of 2024 reg 2, 1 August 2024]

(5B) For the avoidance of doubt, subregulation (5A) does not apply to any registration of a vehicle that expired after 31 July 2023.

[subreg (5B) insrt LN 73 of 2024 reg 2, 1 August 2024]

(6) The late payment fee for any Traffic Infringement Notice issued on and from 1 August 2021 to 31 July 2022 for a fixed penalty offence carrying a liability for a fixed penalty of $60 or less may be waived by the Authority for an unemployed individual on and from 1 August 2021 to 31 July 2022.

[subreg (6) insrt LN 67 of 2021 reg 2, 31 July 2021]

(7) The fees payable under the Act and regulations made under it, not including the fees charged by the Authority to defray the Authority’s expenses for a motor vehicle’s seizure and storage, are not payable for any motor vehicle seized by the Authority during the period the motor vehicle is kept in a place of safe custody by the Authority.

[subreg (7) insrt LN 67 of 2021 reg 2, 31 July 2021]

## reg 3 Road User Levy prescribed fees

(1) The following are exempted from paying the “Road User Levy” prescribed under the Act or any other regulations made under it—

(a) the President of the State;

(b) the government of another country or an institution, or to whom section 12(1) or section 12(2) of the Diplomatic Privileges and Immunities Act 1971 applies;

(c) National Fire Authority;

(d) St John Ambulance;

(e) Red Cross Association;

(f) all registered non-governmental organisations;

(g) crippled children societies;

(h) boarding homes operated by registered churches; and

(i) owners of agricultural machineries which do not operate or rarely operate on public roads, where such machineries operate on public roads at a maximum of 50% of its entire operation.

[subreg 1 am LN 63 of 2015 reg 3, 4 July 2015 LN 99 of 2016 reg 139, 1 December 2016]

(2) Any authority, institutions or person exempted under any other written law.

(3) For the purpose of sub-regulation (1)(i) , “agricultural machineries” means any machinery which does not operate or rarely operates on public roads and is used specifically for agricultural purposes.

[subreg (3) insrt LN 63 of 2015 reg 3, 4 July 2015]

[reg 3 insrt LN 20 of 2009 reg 2, 6 February 2009am LN 63 of 2015 reg 3, 4 July 2015]

## reg 4 Prescribed penalties and fixed penalties

(1) A penalty prescribed in column 4 and column 5 of Schedule 2 is the fixed penalty payable on the issue of a Traffic Infringement Notice in respect of an offence against the section of the Act or regulation respectively prescribed in column 3 of Schedule 2 .

(2) The penalty prescribed in columns 6 and 7 of Schedule 2 is the maximum penalty for an offence against the regulation respectively prescribed in column 3.

(3) Where the prescribed penalty is shown “$……/……months” or similar in column 7 of Schedule 2 , the court may impose a fine up to the maximum shown or a term of imprisonment up to the maximum period shown or both such fine and such imprisonment.

(4) The ‘TIN Code’ mentioned in column 2 of Schedule 2 is to be used—

(a) by the Authority, police and courts in administering Traffic Infringement Notices; and

(b) in the records of the Authority in relation to the issue of such notices.

## reg 5 Revocation of Order

The Motor Vehicle Licence Fees (Exemption) Order is revoked.
