## TAXIS [Os 8-12]

### O 8 Location of taxi stands and taxis bays (Schedule 6)

(1) The roads or parts thereof or places specified in Schedule 6 to this Order shall be taxi stands.

(2)

(a) The roads or parts thereof or places specified in Part 2 of Schedule 6 shall be taxi stands for out-of-base taxis.

(b) No person shall stop or park any vehicle, other than out-of-base taxis on any stand designated for out-of-base taxis.

(3) The roads or parts thereof specified in Part 3 of Schedule 6 shall be taxi bays particularly for loading and unloading of goods by the hirer of such taxi.

[O 8 subst LN 39 of 1996 O 3, 19 April 1996]

### O 9 Prohibition on parking of other vehicles on taxi stands

No person shall stop or park any vehicle, other than a taxi on any taxi stand.

### O 10 Restriction on the stopping of taxis

Subject to other provisions of this Order, the driver of a taxi shall not permit the taxi to stop or park on any road or part thereof other than a taxi stand or when hired on a taxi bay except—

(a) for the purpose of taking on or discharging passengers and their baggage or while waiting for passengers who shall have engaged such taxi, or while the driver is otherwise attending to the requirements of the hire of the taxi, and in any such case for a period of no longer than 15 minutes; or

(b) while the driver of a taxi is obtaining a meal, but for no longer than 30 minutes.

[O 10 subst LN 39 of 1996 O 4, 19 April 1996am LN 115 of 2006 reg 2, 6 October 2006]

### O 11 Restrictions on taxi drivers at taxi stands

The driver of a taxi shall not permit the taxi to stand on any taxi stand if such stand is occupied by the number of vehicles indicated on the traffic sign in relation to that stand.

### O 12 Taxi to move into vacated positions

Where a taxi vacates any position on a taxi stand where taxis stand front to rear, the driver of the taxi behind the taxi vacating the position shall move his or her taxi into it without delay.

## BUSES [Os 13-18]

### O 13 Appointment of bus stops

No bus operating routes specified in the road service licence shall stop to put down or pick up passengers except at places marked or indicated by the appropriate signs and specified in Schedule 10 .

### O 14 Restriction on stopping of buses

Subject to other provisions of this Order, no driver of any bus shall stop or park the bus on any road except at a bus stop for the purpose of loading or unloading passengers and luggage, or at a stand or area appointed for the assembly of buses and if the use of any such stand or area shall have been restricted to certain times then only during those times, provided that a contract carriage which is also by bus may stop and pick up and set down passengers subject to the provisions of this Order in the discretion of the drivers, at places other than a bus stop or bus stand.

### O 15 Bus stands (Schedule 7)

The area specified in Schedule 7 to this Order shall be stands for buses specified in that schedule.

### O 16 Restriction on parking of buses at bus stands

No person shall park any bus on a bus stand except for the purpose of loading or unloading passengers and their luggage and in any such case for not longer than—

(a) 15 minutes immediately prior to scheduled time of departure from the said stand of a bus on a journey terminating within a radius of 50 kilometres therefrom; or

(b) 30 minutes immediately prior to the scheduled time of departure from the said stand on a journey terminating beyond a radius of 50 kilometres therefrom; or

(c) as is reasonably necessary for the purpose of setting down passengers and their luggage at the end of a journey.

### O 16A Restriction on picking up and setting down

When driving a bus into the bus stand, the driver must—

(a) park the bus at the bay provided for its route;

(b) not pick up or set down a passenger when the bus is in motion.

[O 16A insrt LN 98 of 1999 O 2, 23 July 1999]

### O 16B Switching off bus engine

A driver of a bus who is driving the bus into the bus stand must switch off the bus engine as soon as the bus is parked at the appropriate bay and must not restart the engine until immediately before taking the bus out of the bus stand.

[O 16B insrt LN 98 of 1999 O 2, 23 July 1999]

### O 17 Only buses on the bus stand

No person shall drive a vehicle other than a bus on a bus stand.

### O 18 Speed limit for buses on the bus stand

No person shall drive a bus upon a bus stand at a speed greater than 8 kilometres an hour.

## STANDS AND PARKING PLACES [Os 19-22]

### O 19 Provision for temporary stands

Notwithstanding any other provision of this Order, at any place where vehicles are assembled, any uniformed member of the Fiji Police Force may appoint a temporary stand for such vehicles or any class thereof and every person shall work such stand as directed by such members of the Fiji Police Force.

### O 20 Attendance upon vehicles

The driver of—

(a) a bus shall be constantly in attendance upon the same while the bus is on any bus stand;

(b) a goods vehicle shall be constantly in attendance upon the same while the vehicle is on any goods vehicle stand between the hours of 6.00 am and 6.00 pm on Monday to Saturday of any week;

(c) a taxi shall be constantly in attendance upon the same while the taxi is on any taxi stand.

### O 21 One-way streets (Schedule 8)

(1) The roadways or parts thereof specified in Schedule 8 to this Order shall be one-way streets.

(2) A vehicle shall move upon a one-way street only in the direction specified in that schedule.

### O 22 Maximum speed

The maximum speed limit at which a vehicle shall be driven in the town of Labasa shall be 50 kilometres per hour.

## EXEMPTIONS [Os 23-26]

### O 23 Medical practitioners

The provisions of orders 3 , 4 and 5 shall not apply to a registered medical practitioner in the course of rendering his or her professional services to any person at any place other than his or her normal place of practice, provided that the exemption hereby conferred shall only apply in the case of a vehicle to the windscreen of which is affixed a sticker supplied by the Labasa Town Council identifying such vehicle as that of a registered medical practitioner.

### O 24 Exemption

The provisions of this Order shall not apply to the drivers—

(a) conveying the members of the Fiji Police Force on urgent police duty;

(b) of vehicles of a fire brigade proceeding to any fire or alarm of fire;

(c) of ambulances answering an urgent call or proceeding to hospital with any sick or injured person requiring emergency treatment.

### O 25 General

With the exception of the provision of order 21 , the provisions of this Order shall not apply to the drivers—

(a) operating vehicles on the site of road maintenance or construction or maintenance work on power or telegraph lines or equipment while engaged on such work;

(b) operating vehicles in accordance with directions given to them by any uniformed member of the Fiji Police Force;

(c) operating vehicles engaged in garbage collection or in connection with the installation or maintenance of water services.

### O 26 Revocation

The Labasa Traffic Order 1973 is hereby revoked.

[O 26 am LN 57 of 1991 O 3, 1 November 1991]

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as Labasa Traffic Order 1991 and shall apply to the area within the proclaimed boundaries of Labasa Town.

## O 2 Interpretation

In this Order unless the context otherwise requires—

**bus** means a public service vehicle constructed or adapted to carry more than 6 passengers excluding the driver;

**footpath** means that area between the kerb lines or lateral lines of a roadway and the adjacent property lines improved by the laying or formation of concrete, gravel, grass or other material or any roadside reserve for the use of pedestrians;

**out-of-base taxi stands** means stands provided for taxis other than those authorised to operate from Labasa Town base for the purpose only of picking passengers for the return journey or for onward journey to other bases in accordance with the regulations;

[def am LN 99 of 2016 reg 133, 1 December 2016]

**pedestrians** means any person on foot upon a road, and includes any person in an invalid chair not propelled by mechanical power and any person travelling in or on a vehicle, tricycle or scooter designed principally for use by children for the purpose of play; and

**roadway** means that portion of a street improved, designed or actually used or reasonably usable for the time being, by vehicles.

## O 3 Prohibition on stopping of vehicles (Schedule 1)

No person shall stop a vehicle on any road or part thereof specified in Schedule 1 to this Order at any time indicated on a traffic sign as a time at which stopping is prohibited on such road or part thereof.

## O 4 Complete prohibition on parking (Schedule 2)

No person shall park a vehicle at any time of the day or night on any road or part thereof specified in Schedule 2 to this Order.

## O 5 Restriction on parking (Schedules 3 and 4)

(1) No person shall park a vehicle on a road specified in Schedule 3 at a time specified in that schedule.

(2) No person shall park a vehicle on a road specified in Schedule 4 at a time specified in that schedule for a period of time longer than the period specified in that schedule.

## O 6 Loading zones (Schedule 9)

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) a person shall not park or stop a vehicle in a loading zone specified in Schedule 9 .

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply in respect of a goods vehicle parked or stopped in a loading zone for the purpose of loading or unloading goods.

## O 7 Goods vehicle (Schedule 5)

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Order relating to goods vehicles, no person shall park any goods vehicles exceeding 1,780 kg net weight on any road except for the purpose of loading and unloading such vehicle, provided that the restriction contained in this paragraph shall not apply to roads or parts thereof specified in Schedule 5 to this Order appointed as public stands for goods vehicles.

(2) The driver of a goods vehicle shall not permit his or her vehicle to stand on a public stand if such stand is already occupied by the number of vehicles indicated on a traffic sign relating to such stand.
