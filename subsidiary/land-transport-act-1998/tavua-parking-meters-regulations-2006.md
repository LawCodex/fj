# TAVUA PARKING METERS REGULATIONS 2006

Tavua Parking Meters Regulations 2006 (LN 140 of 2006) commenced on 15 December 2006, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Tavua Parking Meters (Amendment) Regulations 2010 (LN 40 of 2010) | 26 March 2010 |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Tavua Parking Meters Regulations 2006.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**authorised officer** means an employee of the Council being authorised by the Land Transport Authority under the Land Transport Act 1998 to carry out a duty being delegated to the Council;

[def am LN 99 of 2016 reg 255, 1 December 2016]

**coin** means a Fijian currency coin specified as the fee payable for parking at a metered space;

**commercial vehicle** means any vehicle used principally for the carriage of goods or in the course of trade;

**Council** means the Tavua Town Council;

**enforcement officer** means an employee appointed as such by the Council;

**exempt period** means any of the following periods—

(a) Sundays;

(b) public holidays;

(c) Saturdays before 8.00 am or after 12.30 pm;

(d) other days before 8.00 am or after 4.30 pm;

**high density area** means an area designated as such in the Schedule ;

**indication** means the red flag or other indication shown in a parking meter, stating the expiration of time for which a motor vehicle may be parked in a metered space;

**loading zone** means an area established under regulation 3 ;

**metered space** means a space in a metered zone marked out pursuant to the provisions of these Regulations by painted lines for the accommodation of a vehicle and at which a parking meter has been installed;

**parking meter** means any mechanical appliance designated for the purpose of measuring and indicating the time within which a vehicle is or may be parked at a metered space and installed thereat pursuant to the provisions of these Regulations and includes the standard to which the meter is affixed;

**Town Clerk** means the appointed person to be the Town Clerk of the Council and includes the Deputy Town Clerk or any other person duly authorised to act as the Town Clerk; and

[def am LN 99 of 2016 reg 255, 1 December 2016]

**traffic attendant** means an employee appointed as such by the Council.

## reg 3 Loading zones

(1) The Council may establish loading zones for standing by commercial vehicles whilst being loaded or unloaded.

(2) Any area established under subregulation (1) shall be marked out with the appropriate signs including “LOADING ZONES”.

## reg 4 Metered zones

The areas listed in the Schedule are declared as a metered zone for motor vehicles.

## reg 5 Fees

(1) The fee for occupying a metered space by a motor vehicle is—

(a) $0.20 for every 15 minutes in a high density traffic area; and

(b) $0.20 for every 30 minutes in a low density traffic area.

[subreg (1) subst LN 40 of 2010 reg 2, 26 March 2010]

(2) Parking fees are not payable in the following cases—

(a) a commercial vehicle in a loading zone for the purpose of delivering goods from such zone or for loading on to such vehicle and, in such case, only for so long as is reasonably necessary for such purpose; or

(b) a motor vehicle in a metered space during any exempt period subject to such time limits and restrictions as are prescribed by law;

(c) if the fee has been paid for, as indicated in the unexpired parking meter.

## reg 6 Parking

(1) A driver of a motor vehicle shall not park a motor vehicle in such a position that is outside the metered space, unless by reason of size such vehicle cannot be completely parked therein.

(2) If the parked vehicle by reason of its size occupies or encroaches on more than one metered space, the driver of such vehicle is liable to a parking fee in respect of each metered space so occupied or encroached upon.

## reg 7 Motorcycle parking

(1) One or more motorcycles may occupy any metered space at the same time without payment of more than one parking fee.

(2) If the metered space is parallel to the kerb or footpath, no such motorcycle shall be parked in such a manner that any part thereof lies within one metre of the rear extremity of the metered space.

(3) If 2 or more than one motorcycle occupy a metered space and remain in a metered space when the time for which payment made has expired, all the drivers of such motorcycles are liable for the payment of the fee for the metered space.

## reg 8 Direction of vehicle

The driver of any vehicle occupying a metered space shall park a vehicle headed towards the general direction of the movement of traffic on the side of the street on which the vehicle is parked.

## reg 9 Operation of meter

(1) The driver of any vehicle who parks the vehicle in a metered space shall deposit or cause to be deposited in the parking meter of such metered space a coin as indicated on the parking meter for the purpose of occupying the metered space only during the paid period indicated in the parking meter.

(2) If the period paid for the use of the metered space has expired, the owner or the driver of the vehicle is liable to deposit the coin for the purpose of continuing use of the metered space occupied.

(3) The driver or owner of any vehicle shall not cause, allow, permit or suffer such vehicle to remain parked in a metered space if the paid period indicated on the parking meter has expired.

## reg 10 Excess charge

(1) If a motor vehicle is parked in a metered space whilst the parking meter for the metered space is displaying that the period has expired, an excess charge (thereinafter referred to as the excess charge) of $20 shall be payable.

(2) If a motor vehicle has incurred an excess charge, a traffic attendant, enforcement officer or an authorised officer shall attach in a conspicuous position of the vehicle a notice which shall include the following particulars—

(a) the registration number of the vehicle;

(b) the date and time in which the parking meter displayed the prescribed indication;

(c) a statement that an excess charge of $20 is payable;

(d) a statement as to where and by what date such excess charge is payable;

(e) a statement that it is an offence under the Land Transport Act 1998 for the driver of a motor vehicle who has parked the vehicle in a metered space to not pay any excess charge incurred;

(f) the number of the parking meter and that of the stand on which it is for the time being mounted.

(3) If a notice has been attached to a vehicle under subregulation (2) , it shall be an offence for any person, not being the driver of the vehicle or a traffic attendant, enforcement officer or authorised officer, to remove the notice unless authorised to do so by the driver.

(4) If a traffic attendant, enforcement officer, authorised officer has taken the action under subregulation (2) , the vehicle may remain parked in the metered space for a period of 2 hours from the time specified in the notice attached under subregulation (2) without becoming liable to pay a further excess charge.

## reg 11 Payment excess

(1) The excess charge shall be delivered or sent by post so as to reach the town clerk's office at the Tavua Town Council, Tavua no later than 4 o'clock in the afternoon of the seventh day after the day on which the excess charge was incurred.

(2) If the excess charge is not paid in accordance with subregulation (1) , the Council shall serve on the registered owner of the vehicle a demand in writing requiring payment of the charge within a period of 14 days after the service of such demand.

(3) If the registered owner fails to pay the sum of money demanded under subregulation (2) within the period specified, the Council may institute proceedings against the registered owner in accordance with the Land Transport Act 1998 .

## reg 12 Disfiguring meter

No person shall without authority affix or attempt to affix any placard, advertisement, notice, list, document, board or thing, or paint, write upon or disfigure any parking meter.

[reg 12 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 255, 1 December 2016]

## reg 13 Meters to be installed

(1) These Regulations shall be of no force or effect in relation to any street or road specified in the Schedule unless parking meters have been installed.

(2) A driver or owner of a vehicle is liable to pay charges for the use of a metered space for which the installed parking meter is defective.

## reg 14 Exemption

These Regulations do not apply to—

(a) a driver operating a vehicle on the site of road maintenances or construction or maintenance work on power or telegraph lines or equipment while engaged on such work;

(b) a driver whilst conveying members of the Fiji Police Force for urgent police duty;

(c) a driver of vehicles of fire brigade at the scene of any fire or alarm of fire;

(d) a driver of ambulance answering an urgent call;

(e) a driver operating a vehicle in accordance with directions given to them by a police officer;

(f) a driver operating a vehicle engaged in garbage collection or in connection with the installation or maintenance of water supplies.

## reg 15 Offences

(1) The owner or driver of any vehicle who contravenes or fails to comply with any of the provisions of these Regulations commits an offence.

(2) Any person, whether or not a driver of a vehicle, who contravenes or fails to comply with the provisions of regulation 9(3) or 10 shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200 or a term of imprisonment of 30 days.
