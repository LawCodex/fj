## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Lami Parking Meters Regulations 2009.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Authorised Officer** means an employee at the council being authorised by the Land Transport Authority under the Land Transport Act 1998 to carry out duties being delegated to the council;

**commercial vehicle** means any vehicle used principally for the carriage of goods in the course of trade;

**Council** means the Lami Town Council;

**enforcement officer** means an employee appointed as such by the council;

**exempt period** means any of the following periods—

(a) Sundays;

(b) public holidays;

(c) Saturdays before 8.00 am or after 12.30 pm;

(d) other days before 8.00 am or after 4.30 pm;

**high density area** means an area designated as such in Part A of the Schedule;

**loading zone** means an area specified in Schedule 3 of the Lami Traffic Order 1982;

**metered zone** means any street or portion of a street or any piece of land prescribed under the provisions of these Regulations as a place where vehicles may park and at which parking meters may be established and maintained;

**parking meter** means any mechanical application designated for the purpose of measuring and indicating the time within which a vehicle is or may be parked at a metered space and installed there pursuant to the provisions of these Regulations and includes the standard to which the meter is affixed;

**prescribed coin** means a coin of Fijian currency prescribed as the fee payable for parking at a metered space;

**prescribed indication** means the red flag or print out tickets in a parking meter, which shows that the date and time for which a motor vehicle may be parked in a metered space has expired or shows that no payment for its parking has been made;

**town clerk** means a clerk of a municipality council; and

**traffic attendant** means an employee or person appointed as such by the council.

## reg 3 Metered zones

The areas listed in the Schedule are declared as a metered zone for motor vehicles.

## reg 4 Fees

(1) The fee for occupying a metered space by a motor vehicle is $0.20 for every 15 minutes in a high density traffic area.

(2) Parking fees are not payable in the following cases—

(a) a commercial vehicle in a loading zone for the purpose of delivering goods from such zone or for loading on to such vehicle and, in such case, only for so long as is reasonably necessary for such purpose; or

(b) a motor vehicle in a metered space during any exempt period subject to such time limits and restrictions as are prescribed by law;

(c) if the fee has been paid for, as indicated in the unexpired parking meter.

## reg 5 Parking

(1) A driver of a motor vehicle shall not park a motor vehicle in such a position that is outside the metered space, unless by reason of size such vehicle cannot be completely parked therein.

(2) If the parked vehicle by reason of its size occupies or encroaches on more than one metered space, the driver of such vehicle is liable to a parking fee in respect of each metered space so occupied or encroached upon.

## reg 6 Motorcycle parking

(1) One or more motorcycles may occupy any metered space at the same time without payment or more than one parking fee.

(2) If the metered space is parallel to the kerb or footpath, no such motorcycle shall be parked in such a manner that any part thereof lies within one metre of the rear extremity of the metered space.

(3) If 2 or more than one motorcycle occupy a metered space and remain in a metered space when the time for which payment made has expired, all the drivers of such motorcycle are liable for the payment of the fee for the metered space.

## reg 7 Direction for vehicles

The driver of any vehicle occupying a metered space shall park a vehicle headed towards the general direction of the movement of traffic on the side of the street on which the vehicle is parked.

## reg 8 Operation of meter

(1) The driver of any vehicle who parks the vehicle in a metered space shall deposit or cause to be deposited in the parking meter of such metered space a coin as indicated on the parking meter for the purpose of occupying the metered space only during the paid period indicated in the parking meter.

(2) If the period paid for the use of the metered space has expired, the owner or the driver of the vehicle is liable to deposit the coin for the purpose of continuing use of the metered space occupied.

(3) The driver or owner of any vehicle shall not cause allow, permit or suffer such vehicle to remain parked in a metered space if the paid period indicated on the parking meter has expired.

## reg 9 Excess charge

(1) If a motor vehicle is parked in a metered space whilst the parking meter for the metered space is displaying that the period has expired an excess charge (thereinafter referred to as excess charge) of $20 shall be payable.

(2) If a motor vehicle has incurred an excess charge, a traffic attendant, enforcement officer or any authorised officer shall attach in a conspicuous position of the vehicle a notice which shall include the following particulars—

(a) the registration number of the vehicle;

(b) the date and time in which the parking meter displayed the prescribed indication;

(c) a statement that an excess charge of $20 is payable;

(d) a statement as to where and by what date such excess charge is payable;

(e) a statement that it is an offence under the Land Transport Act 1998 for the driver of a motor vehicle who has parked the vehicle in a metered space to fail duly to pay any excess charge incurred;

(f) the number of the parking meter and that of the stand on which it is for the time being mounted.

(3) If a notice has been attached to a vehicle in accordance with subregulation (2) , it shall be an offence for any person, not being the driver of the vehicle or a traffic attendant, enforcement officer or authorised officer, to remove the notice, and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200 or a term of imprisonment of 30 days, unless authorised by the driver of the vehicle.

(4) If a traffic attendant, enforcement officer or authorised officer has taken the action under subregulation (2) , the vehicle may remain parked in the metered space for a period of 2 hours from the time specified in the notice attached under subregulation (2) without becoming liable to pay a further excess charge.

## reg 10 Payment of excess charge

(1) The excess charge shall be delivered or sent by post so as to reach the town clerk's office at the Lami Town Council, Lami, not later than 4 o'clock on the afternoon of the seventh day after the day on which the excess charge was incurred.

(2) If the excess charge is not paid in accordance with subregulation (1) , the Council shall serve on the registered owner of the vehicle a demand in writing requiring payment of the charge within a period of 14 days after the service of such demand.

(3) Failure to comply with subregulation (2) , the registered owner shall be guilty of an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200 or a term of 30 days imprisonment.

(4) The Council may also institute proceedings against the registered owner of the vehicle in accordance with the Land Transport Act 1998 .

## reg 11 Disfiguring meters

(1) No person shall without the authority affix or attempt to affix any placard, advertisement, notice, list, document, board or thing on, or paint, write upon or disfigure, any parking meter.

(2) Any person who contravenes subregulation (1) shall be guilty of an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200 or a term of imprisonment of 30 days.

## reg 12 Meters to be installed

(1) These Regulations shall be of no force or effect in relation to any street or road specified in the Schedule unless parking meters have been installed.

(2) A driver or owner of a vehicle is liable to pay charges for the use of a metered space for which the installed parking meter is defective.

## reg 13 Exemptions

These Regulations do not apply to—

(a) a driver operating a vehicle on the site of road maintenance or construction or maintenance work on power or telegraph lines or equipment while engaged on such work;

(b) a driver whilst conveying members of the Fiji Police Force for urgent police duty;

(c) a driver of vehicles of fire brigade at the scene of any fire or alarm of fire;

(d) a driver of an ambulance answering an urgent call;

(e) a driver operating a vehicle in accordance with directions given to them by a police officer;

(f) a driver operating a vehicle engaged in garbage collection or in connection with the installation or maintenance of water supplies.
