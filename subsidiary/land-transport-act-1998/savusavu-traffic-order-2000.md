# SAVUSAVU TRAFFIC ORDER 2000

Savusavu Traffic Order 2000 (LN 7 of 2000) commenced on 4 February 2000, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Savusavu Traffic (Amendment) Order 2009 (LN 20 of 2010) | 26 February 2010 |
| Savusavu Traffic (Amendment) Order 2009 (LN 21 of 2010) | 26 February 2010 |
| Savusavu Traffic (Amendment) Regulations 2011 (LN 104 of 2011) | 7 October 2011 |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Savusavu Traffic Order 2000.

## O 2 Interpretation

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires—

**bus** means a public service vehicle constructed or adapted to carry more than 15 passengers excluding the driver;

**bus stand** means the bus stand at Savusavu maintained and controlled by the Council situate at Savusavu and contained in State lease CL11197, Lot 1 and 2 (part of);

**Council** means the Savusavu Town Council;

**goods vehicle stand** means any goods vehicle stand at Savusavu maintained and controlled by the Council;

**taxi** means a public service vehicle carrying passengers for hire or reward either at separate fares or under a contract, express or implied, for the use of the vehicle as a whole, or for a fixed or agreed rate or sum and duly authorised by a taxi permit; and

**taxi stand** means a taxi stand at Savusavu maintained and controlled by the Council.

## O 3 Parking prohibition

No person may park any vehicle on any road listed in Schedule 1 at any time of the day or night.

## O 4 Limited parking

No person may park any vehicle on any road listed in Schedule 2 except for the period specified in that Schedule.

## O 5 Bus stops

The driver of a bus may stop only at the places listed in Schedule 3 and only for the purpose of dropping off or picking up passengers and their luggage.

## O 6 Bus stands and stopping restrictions

(1) The area listed in Schedule 4 is designated as a bus stand.

(2) The driver of a bus may stop the bus in the bus stand only for the purpose of dropping off or picking up passengers and luggage and for not longer than—

(a) 15 minutes immediately prior to the scheduled time of departure from the stand of a bus on a journey of less than 50 km;

(b) 30 minutes immediately prior to the scheduled time of departure from the stand of a bus on a journey of 50 km or more; or

(c) the time reasonably needed to set down or pick up passengers and their luggage.

(3) A bus operating under contract carriage licence may stop at the bus stand only for as long as is necessary for the purpose of picking up or dropping off passengers and their luggage.

## O 7 Only buses on the bus stand

No person may drive a vehicle other than a bus on to the bus stand.

## O 8 Speed limit for buses on the bus stand

No person may drive a bus upon or within a bus stand at a speed greater than 8 km per hour.

## O 9 Temporary stands

A police officer may specify a temporary stand for vehicles or a class of vehicles and the drivers or such vehicles must follow the directions of the police officer.

## O 10 Attendance upon vehicles

The driver of—

(a) a bus, must be present in the bus while the bus is on a bus stand;

(b) a goods vehicle, must be present on the vehicle while it is on a goods vehicle stand between 6.00 am and 6.00 pm from Monday to Saturday;

(c) a taxi, must be present in the taxi while the taxi is on a taxi stand.

## O 11 Speed limit

The maximum speed at which any vehicle may be driven in the town of Savusavu is 50 km per hour.

## O 12 Loading zones

No person may park or stop a vehicle in a loading zone listed in Schedule 5 except a goods vehicle and only for the purpose of loading or unloading goods.

## O 13 Parking of goods vehicles and location of goods vehicle stands

(1) No person may park any goods vehicle exceeding 1,788 kg net weight on any road except—

(a) for the purpose of loading or unloading the vehicle; or

(b) on a public goods vehicle stand listed in Schedule 6 .

(2) The driver of a goods vehicle must not permit the vehicle to stand on a public goods vehicle stand if the stand is fully occupied by the authorised number of goods vehicles for the particular stand.

## O 14 Location of taxi stands and parking prohibition

No person may stop or park any vehicle other than a taxi on a taxi stand.

## O 15 Restriction on stopping of taxis

(1) The driver of a taxi must not permit the taxi to stop or park on any road except—

(a) on a taxi stand;

(b) for the purpose of picking up or discharging passengers;

(c) while waiting for a passenger who has hired the taxi;

(d) while the driver is attending to the requirements of the hirer; or

(e) while the driver is obtaining a meal.

(2) For the purpose of order (1)(a) , (b) and (c) , the period of permitted stopping or parking is no longer than 15 minutes.

## O 16 Restriction on taxi stands

The driver of a taxi must not permit the taxi to stand at a taxi stand if the taxi stand is fully occupied by the number of vehicles specified in Schedule 7 to use the stand.

## O 17 Taxi to move into vacated position

If a taxi stand is designed for straight-line parking, the driver of a taxi stopped behind a vacated position must without delay move into it.

## O 18 One-way streets

(1) A street listed in Schedule 8 is declared a one-way street.

(2) The driver of a vehicle on a street specified in Schedule 8 must drive the vehicle in the direction specified.

## O 19 Medical practitioners

(1) Orders 3 , 4 , 11 , 12 and 14 do not apply to a medical practitioner rendering professional services to any person at any place other than his or her normal place of practice.

(2) This order applies only to a medical practitioner issued with an identity sticker by the Council, which is affixed to the medical practitioner’s vehicle.

## O 20 Other exemptions

(1) This Order does not apply to a driver—

(a) of a vehicle conveying members of the Fiji Police Force on urgent duty;

(b) of a fire brigade vehicle proceeding to any fire or alarm of fire;

(c) of an ambulance answering an urgent call or proceeding to hospital with a sick or injured person requiring emergency treatment.

(2) Orders 3 , 4 , 12 and 13 do not apply to a person—

(a) operating a vehicle on a road maintenance or construction site or for maintenance work on power or telegraph lines or equipment while engaged in such work;

(b) operating a vehicle under the directions of a police officer;

(c) operating a vehicle engaged in garbage collection or in connection with the installation or maintenance of water services.

## O 21 Repeal

The Savusavu Traffic Order (Legal Notice No. 1 of 1974) is repealed.
