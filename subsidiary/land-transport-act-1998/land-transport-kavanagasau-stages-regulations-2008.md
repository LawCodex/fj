# LAND TRANSPORT (KAVANAGASAU STAGES) REGULATIONS 2008

Land Transport (Kavanagasau Stages) Regulations 2008 (LN 70 of 2008) commenced on 23 May 2008, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Land Transport (Kavanagasau Stages) Regulations 2008.

## reg 2 Route stages

(1) The stage services for Kavanagasau Road is now 15 stages.

(2) The location of the 15 stage services for the Kavanagasau route is annexed in the Schedule, with the new bus fare for each stage.

## reg 3 Transitional

Any criminal proceedings instituted are deemed to have been instituted under these Regulations.

## reg 4 Revocation

These Regulations revoke all other regulations relating to the stage services affecting Kavanagasau Road.

## reg 5 Schedule

The Schedule to these Regulations contains the new Kavanagasau route stages, its new location and the imposed fare for each stage.
