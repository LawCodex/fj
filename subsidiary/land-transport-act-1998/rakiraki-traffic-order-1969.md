## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [Os 1-2]

### O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Rakiraki Traffic Order 1969, and shall apply to the area within the boundaries of the Ra Rural Town Planning Area.

### O 2 Interpretation

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires—

**bus** means a public service vehicle constructed or adapted to carry more than 6 passengers excluding the driver;

**one-way street** means a roadway or any part of a roadway specified in Schedule 6 ;

**park** or parking shall mean the standing of a vehicle whether attended, occupied or otherwise upon a roadway, otherwise than temporarily for the purpose of or while actually engaged in loading or unloading merchandise or taking on or discharging passengers;

**roadway** means that portion of a street improved, designed or actually used or reasonably usable for the time being, by vehicles;

**stop** or stopping means the coming to rest of a vehicle except when necessary to avoid a collision or in compliance with the direction of a police officer or traffic sign; and

**taxi** means a public service vehicle constructed or adapted to carry not more than 6 passengers, excluding the driver.

## PART 2 STOPPING AND PARKING VEHICLES [Os 3-4]

### O 3 Prohibition on stopping of vehicles

No person shall stop a vehicle on any road or part thereof specified in Schedule 1 at any time indicated on a traffic sign as a time at which stopping is prohibited in such road or part thereof.

### O 4 Complete prohibition on parking in certain places

No person shall park any vehicle at any time of the day or night on any road or part thereof specified in Schedule 2 .

## PART 3 GOODS VEHICLES [Os 5-5A]

### O 5 Parking and location of public stands for goods vehicles

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Order relating to goods vehicles, no person shall park any goods vehicle on any road except for the purpose of loading or unloading such vehicle, provided that the restrictions contained in this paragraph shall not apply to the roads or parts thereof specified in Schedule 3 appointed as public stands for goods vehicles.

(2) The driver of a goods vehicle shall not permit the vehicle to stand on a public stand if such stand is already occupied by the number of vehicles indicated on the traffic sign relating to such stand.

### O 5A Loading zone

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) , a person shall not park or stop a vehicle in a loading zone specified in Schedule 7 between the hours of 7.00 am and 6.00 pm Mondays to Fridays inclusive and between the hours of 7.00 am and 1.00 pm on Saturdays; excluding public holidays.

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply in respect of a goods vehicle parked or stopped in a loading zone for the purpose of loading or unloading goods.

## PART 4 TAXIS [Os 6-10]

### O 6 Location of taxi stands

The roads or parts thereof or places specified in Schedule 4 shall be taxi stands.

### O 7 Prohibition on parking of other vehicles on taxi stands

No person shall stop or park any vehicle, other than a taxi, on any taxi stand.

### O 8 Restriction on the stopping of taxis

Subject to the other provisions of this Order, the driver of a taxi shall not permit the taxi to stop or park on any road or part thereof other than a taxi stand except—

(a) for the purpose of taking on or discharging passengers and their baggage, or while waiting for passengers who shall have engaged such taxi, or while the driver is otherwise attending to the requirements of the hirer of the taxi and, in any such case, for a period of no longer than 15 minutes; or

(b) while the driver of a taxi is obtaining a meal, and then only between the hours of 1.00 pm and 2.00 pm and between the hours of 6.00 pm and 7.00 pm.

### O 9 Restrictions on taxi drivers at taxi stands

The driver of a taxi shall not permit the taxi to stand on any taxi stand if such stand is occupied by the number of vehicles indicated on the traffic sign in relation to that stand.

### O 10 Taxi to move into vacated positions

When a taxi vacates any position on a taxi stand where taxis stand front to rear, the driver of the taxi behind the taxi vacating the position shall move his or her taxi into it without delay.

## PART 5 BUSES [Os 11-16]

### O 11 Appointment of bus stops

All buses operating routes specified in a road service licence shall not stop to put down or pick up passengers except at places marked or indicated by the appropriate traffic signs.

### O 12 Restriction on stopping of buses

Subject to the other provisions of this Order, no driver of any bus shall stop or park the bus on any road except at a bus stop for the purpose of loading or unloading passengers and luggage, or at a stand or area appointed for the assembly of buses and, if the use of any such stand or area shall have been restricted to certain times, then only during those times, provided that a contract carriage which is also a bus may stop and pick up and set down passengers in accordance with any other provisions of this Order in the discretion of the driver.

### O 13 Bus stands

The area specified in Schedule 5 shall be a stand for buses.

### O 14 Restriction on parking of buses at bus stands

No person shall park any bus on a bus stand except for the purpose of loading or unloading passengers and their luggage and, in any such case, for not longer than—

(a) 15 minutes immediately prior to scheduled time of departure from the said stand of a bus on a journey terminating within a radius of 30 miles therefrom; or

(b) 30 minutes immediately prior to the scheduled time of departure from the said stand of a bus on a journey terminating beyond a radius of 30 miles therefrom; or

(c) as is reasonably necessary for the purpose of setting down passengers and their luggage.

### O 15 Only buses on the bus stand

No person shall drive a vehicle other than a bus on to any bus stand.

### O 16 Speed limit for buses on the bus stand

No person shall drive a bus upon or within a bus stand at a speed greater than 5 miles per hour.

## PART 6 STANDS AND PARKING PLACES [Os 17-18]

### O 17 Provision for temporary stands

Notwithstanding any other provision of this Order, at any place where vehicles are assembled, any police officer may appoint a temporary stand for such vehicles or any class thereof and every person shall work such stand as directed by a police officer.

### O 18 Attendance upon vehicles

The driver of—

(a) a bus shall be constantly in attendance upon the same while the bus is on any bus stand;

(b) a goods vehicle shall be constantly in attendance upon the same while the vehicle is on any goods vehicle stand between the hours of 6.00 am and 6.00 pm on Monday to Saturday of any week;

(c) a taxi shall be constantly in attendance upon the same while the taxi is on any taxi stand.

## PART 7 ONE-WAY STREETS [Os 19]

### O 19 One-way streets

(1) The roadways or parts thereof specified in Schedule 6 shall be one-way streets.

(2) A vehicle shall move up or down a one-way street only in the direction specified in that Schedule.

## PART 8 EXEMPTIONS [Os 20-21]

### O 20 Medical practitioners

The provisions of orders 3 and 4 shall not apply to a registered medical practitioner in the course of rendering his or her professional service to any person at any place other than his or her normal place of practice, provided that the exemption hereby conferred shall only apply in the case of a vehicle to the windscreen of which is affixed a sticker supplied by the relevant municipal council identifying such vehicle as that of a registered medical practitioner.

[O 20 am LN 58 of 2018 O 2, 1 August 2018]

### O 21 General

The provisions of this Order shall not apply to drivers—

(a) operating vehicles on the site of road maintenance or construction or maintenance work on power or telegraph lines or equipment while engaged on such work;

(b) conveying members of the Fiji Police Force on urgent police duty;

(c) of vehicles of a fire brigade proceeding to any fire or alarm of fire;

(d) of ambulances answering an urgent call or proceeding to hospital with any sick or injured person requiring emergency treatment;

(e) operating vehicles in accordance with directions given to them by any police officer;

(f) operating vehicles engaged in garbage collection or in connection with the installation or maintenance of water services.
