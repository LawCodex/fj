# NASINU TRAFFIC REGULATIONS 2010

Nasinu Traffic Regulations 2010 (LN 126 of 2010) commenced on 17 December 2010, as amended by:

## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [regs 1-3]

### reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Nasinu Traffic Regulations 2010.

### reg 2 Application

These Regulations apply to all the areas within the Nasinu Town.

### reg 3 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**base** means a specific area allocated and approved by relevant authority where PSV vehicles park and wait for passengers;

**bus** means a public service vehicle constructed or adapted to carry more than 15 passengers, excluding the driver;

**carrier** means a motor vehicle constructed and equipped for the safe carriage of passengers and goods such that the majority of passengers are located separate from the driver’s compartment;

**carriageway** means a part of the public street reserved for the movement of vehicles;

**council** means Nasinu Town Council as the local authority responsible for the Nasinu land boundary;

**footpath** means the part of the public street provided to be used by pedestrians and includes footpath lane, thoroughfare or any other part of the street habitually used by pedestrians and not by vehicles;

**one-way street** means a public street with carriageway designed or configured for traffic heading in one direction;

**park** or **parking** means the standing of a vehicle whether attended, occupied or otherwise upon a roadway, otherwise than temporarily for the purpose of, or while actually engaged in, loading or unloading merchandise or taking on or discharging passengers;

**pedestrian** means a person on foot on a public street and includes a person on a public tricycle, scooter, roller skates, skateboard, any other device designed principally for use for children at play and any baby in a pram or a wheel chair; and

**taxi** means a motor vehicle usually fitted with a taximeter that may be hired along with its driver, to carry not more than 4 passengers to any specified destination.

## PART 2 TAXIS [regs 4-12]

### reg 4 Locations of taxi stands

Schedule 1 sets out the areas for taxi stands.

### reg 5 Prohibition on stopping of other vehicles on taxi stands

No person shall stop or park any vehicle, other than a taxi, on any taxi stand.

### reg 6 Restriction on the stopping of taxis

The driver of a taxi shall not stop or park the taxi on any road or part thereof other than a taxi stand except—

(a) for the purpose of taking on or discharging passengers and their baggage; or

(b) while waiting for passengers who shall have engaged such taxi; or

(c) while the driver is otherwise attending to the requirements of the hirer of the taxi, and in any such a case to stop, for a period of not more than 15 minutes; or

(d) while the driver of a taxi is obtaining a meal.

### reg 7 Restrictions on taxi drivers at taxi stands

The driver of a taxi shall not permit such taxi to stand on any taxi stand if the stand is occupied by the required number of vehicles indicated on a traffic sign in relation to that stand.

### reg 8 Taxis to move into vacated positions

When a taxi vacates a position on a taxi stand, the driver of the taxi behind the taxi vacating the position shall move the taxi into the vacated position without delay.

[reg 8 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 175, 1 December 2016]

### reg 9 Duty of driver to accept engagement

If a taxi is upon a taxi stand and not already engaged and any person desires to engage it for any reasonable journey, the driver must not refuse such engagement unless—

(a) the acceptance of it shall be impracticable by reason of prior engagement or for any other reasonable cause; or

(b) the proposed hirer is exhibiting signs of threatening, uncontrolled, dangerous, undesirable, offensive and other similar behaviour and by reason thereof is not in a fit and proper state to be carried as a passenger.

### reg 10 Taxi fee and charges structure

The taxi fee structure shall be accordance to that set out in Schedule 4 .

### reg 11 Base approval for council base

Base approval for bases managed by the Council shall not be issued unless the base applicant undertakes to—

(a) pay the base application fee which shall be the first fee to enable the processing of the taxi base application;

(b) pay the base leasing fee which is set out in Schedule 4 to effect base approval for 3 years;

(c) monthly base service fee shall be charged by the council for the use of the base and this fee must be paid in advance on a quarterly basis till the end of the base leasing period;

(d) [Repealed]

[reg 11 am Act 28 of 2020 s 4 and Sch, 1 August 2020]

### reg 12 Base approval for private base

(1) The base owner of each private base must pay to the council the car park fees as set out in Schedule 4 ;

[subreg (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 175, 1 December 2016]

(2) Private bases shall only be renewed annually after the approval by Town Planning Office and also requires the full consideration of the Nasinu Town Council.

## PART 3 CARRIER [regs 13-17]

### reg 13 Appointment of carrier stops

No carrier operation within the routes specified in its Road Route licence shall stop to drop or pick up passengers except at places marked or indicated by the appropriate traffic signs.

### reg 14 Restriction on other vehicles

No person shall stop or park any vehicle, other than a carrier, on any carrier stand.

### reg 15 Carrier stands

Schedule 2 sets out the area for carrier stands.

### reg 16 Restriction on parking

The driver of a carrier shall not stop or park the carrier on any road or part thereof other than a carrier stand except—

(a) for the purpose of taking on or discharging passengers and their baggage; or

(b) while waiting for passengers who shall have engaged such service; or

(c) while the driver is otherwise attending to the requirements of the hirer of the carrier and in any such a case to stop, for a period of not more than 15 minutes; or

(d) while the driver of a carrier is obtaining a meal.

### reg 17 Carrier to move into vacated positions

When a carrier vacates a position on a carrier stand, the driver of the carrier behind the carrier vacating the position shall move the carrier into the vacated position without delay.

[reg 17 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 175, 1 December 2016]

## PART 5 STANDS AND PARKING PLACES [regs 26-27]

### reg 26 Provision for temporary stand

Notwithstanding any other provisions of these Regulations, at any place where vehicles are assembled, any uniformed member of the Fiji Police Force or authorised officer may appoint a temporary stand for such vehicles or may class thereof and every person shall work such as directed by such member of the Fiji Police Force or authorised officers.

### reg 27 Attendance upon vehicle

The driver of—

(a) a bus shall be constantly in attendance upon the same while the bus is on any bus stand;

(b) a goods vehicle, shall be constantly in attendance upon the same while it is on any goods vehicles between the hours of 6.00 am and 6.00 pm on Monday to Saturday of any week;

(c) a taxi, while it is on any taxi stand.

## PART 6 ONE-WAY STREETS [regs 28]

### reg 28 Location of one-way street

(1) The carriageway set out in Schedule 5 shall be one-way streets.

(2) The driver on a one-way street shall follow the direction specified in Schedule 5 .

## PART 7 MAXIMUM SPEED [regs 29]

### reg 29 Speed limits

The maximum speed at which a vehicle shall be driven in the area within the Nasinu Town boundaries inclusive of the Kings Road is 50 km per hour.

## PART 8 EXEMPTIONS [regs 30-31]

### reg 30 Exemptions

The provisions of these Regulations shall not apply to the following—

(a) a registered medical practitioner in the course of rendering professional services to any person at any other place than his or her normal place of practice;

(b) a police vehicle conveying members of the Fiji Police Force on urgent police duty;

(c) a fire brigade vehicle proceeding to any fire alarm or fire; and

(d) an ambulance vehicle answering an urgent call or proceeding to hospital with any sick or injured person requiring emergency treatment;

(e) a driver operating vehicles on the site of road maintenance or construction or maintenance work on power or telephone lines or equipment while engaged on such work;

(f) a driver operating vehicles in accordance with directions given to them by any uniformed member of the Fiji Police Force or authorised officers; and

(g) a driver operating vehicles engaged in garbage collection or in connection with the installation or maintenance of water services.

### reg 31 Identification stickers

Each registered medical practitioner shall affix and keep affixed to the windscreen of his or her car the identification sticker supplied by Nasinu Town Council.
