## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Parking Meters (Nausori) Order 1997.

## O 2 Interpretation

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires—

**commercial vehicle** means a vehicle used principally for the carriage of goods or in the course of trade;

**Council** means the Nausori Town Council;

**exempt period** means—

(a) Sunday;

(b) a public holiday;

(c) Saturday before 8.00 am or after 5.00 pm; or

(d) any other day before 8.00 am or after 5.00 pm.

**loading zone** means an area established and set aside by the Council and marked out on the surface of a street or road and designated by signs including the words “loading zone” as a place for a commercial motor vehicle or commercial motor vehicles to stand whilst being loaded or unloaded;

**metered space** means a space in a metered zone marked out pursuant to the provisions of this Order by painted lines for the accommodation of a motor vehicle and at which a parking meter has been installed;

**metered zone** means any street or portion of a street or any piece of land prescribed under the provisions of this Order as a place where motor vehicles may park and at which parking meters may be established and maintained;

**parking meter** means a mechanical appliance designed for the purpose of measuring and indicating the time within a motor vehicle is or may be parked at a metered space and installed thereat to the provisions of this Order and includes the standard to which the meter is affixed;

**prescribed coin** a coin to Fiji prescribed as the fee payable for parking at a metered space;

**prescribed indication** means the red flag or other device contained in a parking meter, which shows that the time for which a motor vehicle may be parked in a metered space has expired or that no payment for its parking has been made;

**town clerk** means the Town Clerk for the time being of the Council and includes a deputy clerk or any person duly authorised to act as Town Clerk; and

**traffic attendant** means an officer of the Council holding the office of traffic attendant.

## O 3 Metered zones

Each of the areas specified in the Schedule is designated/declared a metered zone for motor vehicles.

## O 4 Fees

(1) The fee for the occupation by a motor vehicle of a metered space in a—

(a) high density traffic area specified in Part A of the Schedule are—

(i) $0.20 in respect of each and every 15 minutes;

(ii) $0.50 in respect of each and every 40 minutes.

(b) low density area specified in Part B of the Schedule is $0.20 in respect of each and every 30 minutes.

[para (1) subst LN 39 of 2010 O 2, 26 March 2010]

(2) Notwithstanding any of the provisions of this Order, a person may, without payment of any fee—

(a) park a commercial motor vehicle in a loading zone for the purpose of delivering goods from that zone or for loading goods onto that motor vehicle and such case only for so long as is reasonably necessary for that purpose;

(b) park a motor vehicle in a metered space during any exempt period, subject to parking time limits and restrictions as are precribed by a traffic order or by-law of the Council for the time being in force.

## O 5 Parking

(1) A person shall not park a motor vehicle in a metered space in a position that the motor vehicle shall not be entirely within the metered space unless by reason of its size that motor vehicle cannot be completely contained therein.

(2) Where a parked motor vehicle by reason of its size occupy or encroach on more than one metered space the owner or driver of that motor vehicle is liable for the parking fee for each metered space so occupied or encroached on.

## O 6 Multiple parking

A person shall not park a motor vehicle in a metered space already occupied by another vehicle, provided that—

(a) it shall be lawful for any number of motorcycles to occupy any metered space at the same time without payment of more than one parking fee;

(b) where the metered space is parallel to the kerb or footpath no such motorcycle shall be parked in such a manner that any part thereof lies within 120 centimetres of the rear extremity of the metered space;

(c) where more than one motorcycle occupies a metered space no such motorcycle shall remain therein when the time for which payment has been made has expired.

## O 7 Direction of vehicles

The driver of a motor vehicle occupying a metered space shall park that motor vehicle so that it is headed substantially in the general direction of the movement of traffic on the side of the street on which the motor vehicle is parked.

## O 8 Operation of meters

(1) The driver of a motor vehicle parked in a metered space shall forthwith deposit or cause to be deposited in the parking meter at the space the prescribed coin as indicated on the parking meter. Thereupon the metered space may be lawfully occupied by the motor vehicle only during the period indicated on the parking meter, provided that the driver of the motor vehicle may without any payment park the motor vehicle during any time indicated on the parking meter as being unexpired from its previous use.

(2) Unless the total period allowed for the parking of a motor vehicle in any metered space has been exceeded the driver of a motor vehicle may immediately upon the expiration of any authorised period of parking again deposit or cause to be deposited the prescribed coin in the parking meter to set the parking meter in operation and thereafter the said motor vehicle may lawfully occupy the metered space for the further period indicated on the parking meter.

(3) No owner or driver of a motor vehicle shall cause, allow, permit or suffer the motor vehicle to remain parked in a metered space which the prescribed indication at such metered space shows that the time has expired.

## O 9 Excess charge

(1) Where a motor vehicle is parked in a metered space during the prescribed hours whilst the parking meter for that metered space is displaying the prescribed indication, an excess charge (hereinafter referred to as the “excess charge”) of $2 shall be payable.

(2) In the case of a motor vehicle in respect of which an excess charge may have been incurred, a traffic attendant shall attach to the motor vehicle in a conspicuous position a notice which shall include the following particulars—

(a) the registration number of the motor vehicle;

(b) the date and time at which he or she first noticed that the parking meter displayed the prescribed indication;

(c) a statement that an excess charge of $2 is payable;

(d) a statement as to where and by what date such excess charge is payable;

(e) a statement that it is an offence under the Land Transport Act 1998 for the driver of a motor vehicle who has parked the motor vehicle in a metered space to fail duly to pay any excess charge incurred;

(f) the number of the parking meter and that of the stand on which it is for the time being mounted.

[para (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 194, 1 December 2016]

(3) Where a notice has been attached to a motor vehicle in accordance with the provisions of paragraph (2) it shall be an offence for any person not being the driver or the owner of the motor vehicle or a traffic attendant, to remove the notice unless authorised to do so by the driver or the owner.

(4) Where a traffic attendant has taken the action prescribed by paragraph (2) without becoming liable to pay a further excess charge.

## O 10 Payment of excess charge

(1) The excess charge shall be delivered or sent by post so as to reach the town clerk's office at Nausori Town Council, Nausori no later than 4 o'clock in the afternoon of the seventh day after the day on which the excess charge was incurred.

(2) Where the excess charge is not paid in accordance with paragraph (1) , the Council shall serve on the registered owner of the vehicle a demand in writing requiring payment of the charge within a period of 14 days after the service of such demand and the excess charge payable upon second notice shall be $10.

(3) Where a registered owner of a motor vehicle upon whom a demand has been served under paragraph (2) fails to pay within the period therein specified the sum of money demanded, the Council may institute proceedings against such registered owner in accordance with the provisions of the order.

## O 11 Disfiguring meters

No person shall without due authority affix or attempt to affix any placard, advertisement, notice, list, document, board or thing on or paint, write upon or disfigure any parking meter.

## O 12 Meters to be installed as per Schedule

This Order shall be of no force or effect in relation to any street or road specified in the Schedule unless parking meters have been installed therein and shall be of no force or effect in relation to any metered space if the parking meter installed thereat is out of order.

## O 13 Exemptions

The provisions of this Order shall not apply to a driver—

(a) operating motor vehicles on the site of road maintenance or construction or maintenance work on power or telegraph lines or equipment while engaged on such work;

(b) conveying members of the Fiji Police Force on urgent police duty;

(c) of motor vehicles of a fire brigade at the scene of any fire or alarm of fire;

(d) of ambulances answering an urgent call;

(e) operating motor vehicles in accordance with directions given to them by a police officer;

(f) operating motor vehicles engaged in garbage collection or in connection with the installation or maintenance of water supplies.

## O 14 Offences

(1) A driver of a motor vehicle who contravenes the provisions of this Order commits an offence.

(2) A person who contravenes the provisions of orders 9(3) or 11 commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $50.
