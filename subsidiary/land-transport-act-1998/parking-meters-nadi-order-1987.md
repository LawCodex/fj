## O 1 Short title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Parking Meters (Nadi) Order 1987 and shall come into force on a date to be notified by the Council in the Gazette.

## O 2 Interpretation

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires—

**commercial vehicle** means any vehicle used principally for the carriage of goods or in the course of trade;

**Council** means the Nadi Town Council;

**exempt period** means any of the following periods—

(a) Sunday;

(b) a public holiday;

(c) Saturday before 8.00 am or after 1.00 pm;

(d) any other day before 8.00 am or after 5.00 pm;

[def am LN 60 of 1991 O 3, 8 November 1991]

**loading zone** means an area established and set aside by the Council and marked out on the surface of a street or road and designated by signs including the words “Loading Zone” as a place for a commercial vehicle or commercial vehicles to stand whilst being loaded or unloaded;

**metered space** means a space in a metered zone marked out pursuant to the provisions of this Order by painted lines for the accomodation of a vehicle and at which a parking meter has been installed;

**metered zone** means any street or portion of a street or any piece of land prescribed under the provisions of this Order as a place where vehicles may park and at which parking meters may be established and maintained;

**parking meter** means a mechanical appliance designed for the purpose of measuring and indicating the time within which a vehicle is or may be parked at a metered space and installed thereat pursuant to the provisions of this Order and includes the standard to which the meter is affixed;

**prescribed coin** means a coin of Fiji prescribed as the fee payable for parking at a metered space;

**prescribed indication** means the red flag or other device contained in a parking meter, which shows that the time for which a motor vehicle may be parked in a metered space has expired or that no payment for its parking has been made;

**private motor vehicles** means a motor vehicle other than a goods vehicle, motor tractor or public service vehicle;

[def insrt LN 41 of 1997 O 3, 18 April 1997]

**registered owner** means the person who is recorded in the records of the Land Transport Authority as the owner of the vehicle;

[def am LN 99 of 2016 reg 193, 1 December 2016]

**town clerk** means the town clerk for the time being of the Council and includes a deputy town clerk or any person duly authorised to act as town clerk; and

**traffic attendant** means an officer of the Council holding the office of traffic attendant or any other officer duly authorised in that behalf.

## O 3 Metered zones

Each of the areas specified in the Schedule hereto is hereby appointed a metered zone for motor vehicles.

[O 3 am LN 60 of 1991 O 4, 8 November 1991]

## O 4 Fees

(1) The fee for the occupation by a motor vehicle of a metered space in a—

(a) high density traffic as specified in the Schedule are—

(i) $0.20 in respect of each and every 15 minutes;

(ii) $0.50 in respect of each and every 40 minutes.

(b) low density area specified in the Schedule is $0.20 in respect of every 30 minutes.

(2) Any person may, without payment of any fee park—

(a) (for so long as is reasonably necessary for the purpose) a commercial vehicle in a loading zone for the purpose of delivering goods from such vehicle or to loading goods on to such vehicles; and

(b) a motor vehicle in a metered space during any exempt period, subject to such parking time limits and restrictions as are prescribed by a traffic order or by-law of the Council for the time being in force.

[O 4 subst LN 37 of 2010 O 2, 26 March 2010]

## O 5 Parking

(1) No driver of a motor vehicle shall park such vehicle in a metered space in such a position that such vehicle shall not be entirely within such metered space, unless by reason of its size such vehicle cannot be completely contained therein.

(2) Should a parked vehicle by reason of its size occupy or encroach on more than one metered space the driver of such vehicle shall be liable for a parking fee in respect of each metered space so occupied or encroached on.

(3) No goods vehicle, motor tractor or public service vehicle shall be parked on any metered space designated for private motor vehicles only.

[para (3) insrt LN 41 of 1997 O 4, 18 April 1997]

## O 6 Multiple parking

No driver of a motor vehicle shall park such vehicle in a metered space already occupied by another vehicle, provided that—

(a) it shall be lawful for any number of motorcycles to occupy any metered space at the same time without payment of more than one parking fee;

(b) if the metered space is parallel to the kerb or footpath no such motorcycle shall be parked in such a manner that any part thereof lies within 120 centimetres of the rear extremity of the metered space;

(c) where more than one motorcycle occupies a metered space no such motorcycle shall remain therein when the time for which payment has been made has expired.

## O 7 Direction of vehicles

The driver of any vehicle occupying a metered spaced shall park such vehicle so that it shall be headed substantially in the general direction of the movement of the traffic on the side of the street on which the vehicle is parked.

## O 8 Operation of meters

(1) The driver of any vehicle parked in a metered space shall forthwith deposit or cause to be deposited in the parking meter at such space the prescribed coin as indicated on the parking meter. Thereupon the metered spaced may be lawfully occupied by the vehicle only during the period indicated on the parking meter, provided that the driver of such vehicle may without any payment park the same during any time indicated on the parking meter as been unexpired from its previous use.

(2) Unless the total period allowed for a parking of the vehicle in any metered space has been exceeded, the driver of such vehicle may immediately upon the expiration of any authorised period of parking again deposit or cause to be deposited the prescribed coin in the parking meter to set the parking meter in operation and thereafter the said vehicle may lawfully occupy the metered space for the further period indicated on the parking meter.

(3) No driver of any vehicle shall cause, allow, permit or suffer such vehicle to remain parked in a metered spaced while the prescribed indication at such metered space shows that the time has expired.

## O 9 Excess charge

(1) If a motor vehicle is parked in a metered space during the prescribed hours whilst the parking meter for that metered space is displaying the prescribed indication an excess charge (hereinafter referred to as the “excess charge”) shall be payable as follows—

(a) excess charge of $2 if such excess charge is paid within the prescribed period in order 10(1) and before demand notice issued to the registered owner of the motor vehicle in terms of order 10(2) ;

(b) excess charge of $10 if such excess charge is paid after the issue of demand notice to the registered owner of the motor vehicle in terms of order 10(2) .

[para (1) subst LN 58 of 1989 O 2, 6 October 1989am LN 33 of 1996 , O 3, 22 March 1996]

(1A) For the purposes of the preceding subsection, the date of postage of the demand notice to the registered owner shall be deemed to be the date of issue of the demand notice.

[para (1A) insrt LN 58 of 1989 O 2, 6 October 1989]

(2) In the case of a motor vehicle in respect of which an excess charge may have been incurred, a traffic attendant shall attach to the vehicle in a conspicuous position a notice which shall include the following particulars—

(a) the registration number of the vehicle;

(b) the date and time at which he or she first noticed that the parking meter displayed the prescribed indication;

(c) a statement that an excess charge of $2 is payable;

(d) a statement as to where and by what date such excess charge is payable;

(e) a statement that it is an offence under the Land Transport Act 1998 for the driver of a motor vehicle who has parked the vehicle in a metered spaced to fail duly to pay any excess charge incurred;

(f) the number of the parking meter and that of the stand on which it is for the time being mounted.

[para (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 193, 1 December 2016]

(3) Where a notice has been attached to a vehicle in accordance with the provisions of paragraph (2) it shall be an offence for any person, not being the driver of the vehicle or a traffic attendant to remove the notice unless authorised to do so by the driver.

(4) Where the traffic attendant has taken the action prescribed by paragraph (2) the vehicle may remain parked in the metered space for a period of 2 hours from the time specified in the notice attached under paragraph (2) without becoming liable to pay a further excess charge.

## O 10 Payment of excess charge

(1) The excess charge shall be delivered or sent by post so as to reach the town clerk’s office at the Civic Centre Nadi no later than 4 o’clock in the afternoon of the seventh day after the day on which the excess charge was incurred.

(2) In the event of the excess charge not being paid in accordance with paragraph (1) , the Council shall serve on the registered owner of the vehicle a demand in writing requiring payment of the charge within a period of 14 days after the service of such demand.

(3) If a registered owner upon whom a demand has been served under paragraph (2) fails to pay the sum of money demanded within the period therein specified, the Council may institute proceedings against such registered owner in accordance with the provisions of the Act.

## O 11 Disfiguring meters

No person shall without due authority affix or attempt to affix any placard, advertisement, notice, list, document, board or thing on or paint, write upon or disfigure any parking meter.

## O 12 Meters to be installed

This order shall be of no force or effect in relation to any street or road specified in the Schedule unless parking meters have been installed therein and shall be of no force or effect in relation to any metered space if the parking meter installed thereat is out of order.

## O 13 Temporary discontinuance of metered space

(1) Whenever a traffic attendant or other authorised officer is of the opinion that any metered space or spaces should be temporarily discontinued as a parking space, the said officer may place or erect, or cause to be placed or erected, a sign, signs or other device indicating “NO STOPPING” at such metered space or spaces, and it shall be unlawful for any person to park, stand or stop a vehicle at such metered space or spaces while such sign or device is so placed or erected.

(2) Whenever the use of any metered space is temporarily discontinued pursuant to this order, by reason of any building or contracting operations on any land adjacent thereto by any contractor or other person at whose request or by reason of whose operations on such land has caused such temporary discontinuance, such fee as shall be fixed from time to time by the Council shall be paid by that Contractor or other person.

(3) Whenever the use of any metered space is temporarily discontinued pursuant to this clause by reason of any such building or contracting operations any authorised officer of the Council may permit the use of any such space for the parking, standing or stopping of vehicles in connection with such building or contracting operations, and such use shall not be deemed to be an offence under this Order.

## O 14 Exemptions

The provisions of this Order shall not apply to drivers of—

(a) operating vehicles on the site of road maintenance or construction or work on power, telephones or water or sewerage pipes;

(b) vehicles operated by the Fiji Police Force;

(c) vehicles operated by any fire brigade;

(d) ambulances;

(e) Council vehicles.

## O 14A Offences in respect of traffic attendant

A person who—

(1) obstructs, prevents, hinders or interferes with the traffic attendant in the performance of any duty under these Orders;

(2) personates or falsely pretends to be a traffic attendant;

(3) gives or agrees to give or offers to a traffic attendant any gift or consideration as an inducement or reward for any act done or to be done or for any forbearance observed or for any favour shown or to be shown by him or her or who, being such a traffic officer, accepts or agrees to accept or solicits any such gift or consideration;

(4) offers violence to, assaults, threatens, intimidates, uses threatening language towards or behalves in a threatening manner to any traffic attendant while he or she is acting in the exercise of his or her powers or the discharge of his or her duties under these Orders; shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100 or in case a of second or subsequent conviction to a fine not exceeding $100.

[O 14A insrt LN 5 of 1993 O 2, 1 January 1993]

## O 15 Offences

(1) The driver of any vehicle who contravenes or fails to comply with any of the provisions of this Order shall be guilty of an offence.

(2) Any person, whether or not the driver of a vehicle, who contravenes or fails to comply with the provisions of orders 9(3) or 11 is guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $50.
