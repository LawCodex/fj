## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Engineers Registration (Prescribed Qualifications) Regulations 1982.

## reg 2 Prescribed qualifications

The qualifications cited in the first column of the Schedule obtained from the institution cited in the second column of the Schedule shall be recognised for the purposes of section 7(1)(a) of the Act.
