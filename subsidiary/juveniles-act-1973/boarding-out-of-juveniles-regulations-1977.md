# BOARDING OUT OF JUVENILES REGULATIONS 1977

Boarding Out of Juveniles Regulations 1977 (LN 91 of 1977) commenced on 6 July 1977, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Boarding Out of Juveniles Regulations 1977.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations unless the context otherwise requires—

**foster parent** means any person with whom a juvenile is or is proposed to be boarded out and includes any society or voluntary institution approved by the Minister in terms of section 35 of the Act; and

**home-master** or **home-mistress** means a member of the staff of the Nasinu Boys Centre and includes a member of the staff of the Girls Home.

## reg 3 Application

(1) These Regulations shall apply to the boarding of a juvenile by the Director with a foster parent to live in his or her dwelling as a member of the family and the boarding of a juvenile to which these Regulations apply is hereinafter referred to as “boarding out” and “board out” and “boarded out” shall be construed accordingly.

(2) For the purposes of these Regulations a juvenile who is delivered into the care and custody of a person proposing to adopt him or her under the Adoption of Infants Act 1944 shall not be regarded as boarded out.

## reg 4 Duty to terminate boarding out

(1) The Director shall not allow a juvenile to remain boarded out if it appears to him or her that the boarding out is no longer in the juvenile's best interests.

(2) Where a welfare officer, whose duty it is under these Regulations to supervise the welfare of a juvenile, considers that the conditions under which the juvenile is boarded out endanger his or her health, safety or morals the juvenile may be removed forthwith from the place at which he or she is boarded out.

(3) Where a juvenile is boarded out in an area other than the one in which he or she usually resides the welfare officer responsible for the area in which the juvenile is to be boarded out shall be notified forthwith and he or she shall perform supervisory duties in respect of the juvenile.

## reg 5 Medical examination

The Director shall arrange for a juvenile to be medically examined by a medical practitioner, registered in terms of the Medical and Dental Practitioner Act 2010 , before such juvenile is boarded out, and a report or that medical examination shall be placed on the juvenile's record at the Department of Social Development and Welfare.

[reg 5 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 25, 1 December 2016]

## reg 6 Reports

(1) The Welfare Officer shall submit a report to the Director at intervals of not more than 3 months on each boarded out juvenile under his or her supervision.

(2) The report submitted in terms of subregulation (1) shall state the health, welfare, progress and conduct of the juvenile to which it refers.

(3) The Director shall at intervals of not more than 6 months review the position of each juvenile boarded out and consider whether the boarding out in each case shall continue or be terminated.

## reg 7 Register

(1) The Director shall maintain a register of all juveniles boarded out.

(2) The register maintained in terms of subregulation (1) shall contain the following information—

(a) The name, sex, date of birth and religious persuasion of each juvenile; and

(b) The name and address of the foster parent with whom the juvenile is boarded out.

## reg 8 Suitability of foster parent

(1) No juvenile shall be boarded out unless the Director is satisfied that, in all the circumstances, the foster parent with whom the juvenile is to be boarded out is suitable.

(2) For the purposes of subregulation (1) , the Director shall consider—

(a) the reputation, religious persuasion, age, character and health of the foster parent;

(b) the domestic facilities present in the dwelling of the foster parent in which the juvenile will reside;

(c) the physical and mental health of the members of the household of the foster parent;

(d) the criminal record (if any) of the foster parent and other members of his or her household;

(e) the number, sex and ages of the members of the household of the foster parent;

(f) the history of the juvenile; and

(g) any other matter appearing to the Director to be relevant.

## reg 9 Undertaking to be given

(1) A foster parent with whom any juvenile is boarded out shall be required to give an undertaking in respect of the juvenile to the Director.

(2) The undertaking shall be in the form specified by the Minister.

## reg 10 Visits to juvenile

A welfare officer or home master shall visit the juvenile at his or her place of boarding out—

(a) within the month from the date of the commencement of the boarding out;

(b) upon the receipt of any complaint by or concerning the juvenile;

(c) 2 weeks before the termination of the period of boarding out; and

(d) at any other time he or she is required by the Director to do so.
