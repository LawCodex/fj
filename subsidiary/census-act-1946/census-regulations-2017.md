## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Census Regulations 2017.

(2) These Regulations are deemed to have come into force on 1 April 2017.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Act** means the Census Act 1946;

**area coordinator** means a person appointed under regulation 4(1) or 11;

**assigned area** means the area assigned to an area coordinator by the Census Commissioner for which the area coordinator must carry out his or her functions under these Regulations;

**authorised officer** means any person appointed by the Census Commissioner to assist in the collection of statistical information in relation to the census;

**census** means the official count of the inhabitants of the Republic of Fiji—

(a) who are present in the Republic of Fiji on census night; and

(b) which takes place in accordance with the Census Order 2016;

**Census Commissioner** means the Census Commissioner appointed under section 2 of the Act;

**census night** means 12.00 am on 17 September 2017;

**dwelling** means any place in which a person is present on census night and any place which is wholly or partly used for human habitation on census night, including—

(a) any building, erection, tenement or part thereof used wholly or partly for human habitation;

(b) any hotel, lodging house, boarding house or club;

(c) any prison, police station, barracks, school and any public or private charitable, religious, educational, health or other institution; and

(d) any ship within the exclusive economic zone of Fiji;

**enumerator** means a person appointed under regulation 5(1);

**GPS** means the Global Positioning System;

**head of household** means any person who is the head of a household and who may be present at the dwelling on census night;

**headquarters** means the office of the Fiji Bureau of Statistics in Suva, Nadi, Ba or Labasa;

**occupier** means the person who has overall responsibility of a non-private dwelling, including—

(a) the manager, director, secretary or other executive officer of a hotel, lodging house, boarding house or club;

(b) the head or other person in charge of an institution; or

(c) the master or other person in charge of a ship;

**superintendent of census** means a person appointed under regulation 3(1); and

**supervisor** means a person appointed under regulation 6(1).

## reg 3 Superintendent of census

(1) The Census Commissioner may, with the approval of the Minister, appoint any of the following persons as a superintendent of census—

(a) provincial administrators;

(b) assistant provincial administrators;

(c) district officers;

(d) assistant district officers; and

(e) any other civil servant.

(2) A superintendent of census has overall administrative responsibility of the census in his or her assigned district or province.

## reg 4 Area coordinators

(1) The Census Commissioner may, with the approval of the Minister, appoint any person to be an area coordinator.

(2) An area coordinator must—

(a) ensure that any supervisor or enumerator allocated to the area coordinator’s assigned area is adequately trained to conduct the census;

(b) ensure that an enumerator allocated to the area coordinator’s assigned area collects statistical information in relation to the census from each dwelling in the assigned area; and

(c) provide administrative and operational support to the superintendent of census of the area coordinator’s assigned area.

## reg 5 Enumerators

(1) The Census Commissioner may, with the approval of the Minister, appoint any person to be an enumerator.

(2) An enumerator must collect statistical information in relation to the census as instructed by the area coordinator.

## reg 6 Supervisors

(1) The Census Commissioner may, with the approval of the Minister, appoint any person to be a supervisor.

(2) A supervisor must oversee the work carried out by any enumerator, under his or her responsibility and, as instructed by the area coordinator.

## reg 7 Census questionnaire

The census must be undertaken by means of the census questionnaire prescribed in the Schedule.

## reg 8 Particulars to be provided

(1) Subject to regulation 9, a person must—

(a) to the best of his or her knowledge and belief, provide particulars under the provisions of these Regulations to any authorised officer; or

(b) take all reasonable steps to ensure that such particulars are provided to any authorised officer on the person’s behalf by another person.

(2) Any occupier or head of household must, to the best of the knowledge and belief of the occupier or the head of household, provide the particulars in respect of the occupier or the head of household, and any other person who—

(a) is present in the dwelling on census night; or

(b) returns to the dwelling on or before 12.00 pm on 18 September 2017 and has not been enumerated.

## reg 9 Failure or inability to provide particulars

A person who is required to provide particulars under the provisions of these Regulations but fails or is unable to do so, must report to a superintendent of census or any authorised officer, who must then record the required particulars of such person.

## reg 10 Power to give directives

The Census Commissioner may give directives to any person exercising any function or power under these Regulations in order to—

(a) give effect to the provisions of the Act and these Regulations; and

(b) ensure the efficient and effective undertaking of the census.

## reg 11 Transitional

Any area coordinator appointed under the Census Regulations 2007 and currently employed by the Fiji Bureau of Statistics is deemed to have been appointed under regulation 4(1).

## reg 12 Revocation

The Census Regulations 2007 is revoked.
