## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Meat Industry (Slaughterhouse Regions) Order 1983.

## O 2 Slaughterhouse regions

The following areas are declared to be slaughterhouse regions from the severally specified dates—

(a) the area comprising the whole of the Central Division (13 September 1976);

(b) the area comprising the whole of the Western Division (1 January 1984).
