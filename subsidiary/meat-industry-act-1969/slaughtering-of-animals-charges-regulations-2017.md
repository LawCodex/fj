## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Slaughtering of Animals (Charges) Regulations 2017.

(2) These Regulations are deemed to have come into force on 1 March 2007.

## reg 2 Schedule of charges

The charges payable to the Fiji Meat Industry Board in respect of the slaughtering of animals shall be as set out in the Schedule.

## reg 3 Repeal

The Slaughtering of Animals (Charges) Regulations 1987 are repealed.
