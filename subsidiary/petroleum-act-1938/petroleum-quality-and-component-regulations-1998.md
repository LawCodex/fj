## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Petroleum (Quality and Component) Regulations 1998.

## reg 2 Minister to determine quality or component

Subject to regulations 3 and 4, the Minister may, by notification in the Gazette, after consulting any interested party, determine the quality or component of motor spirit imported into or to be used in Fiji.

## reg 3 Restrictions

Notwithstanding regulation 2, no person shall, until 31 December 1999, import or use motor spirit having a lead content exceeding 0.3 g/l lead.

## reg 4 Prohibitions

Notwithstanding regulation 3, no person shall import or use motor spirit having a lead content exceeding 0.013 g/l lead at any final delivery point with effect from 1 January 2000.
