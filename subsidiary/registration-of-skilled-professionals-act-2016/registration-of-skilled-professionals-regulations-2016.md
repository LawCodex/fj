# REGISTRATION OF SKILLED PROFESSIONALS REGULATIONS 2016

Registration of Skilled Professionals Regulations 2016 (LN 27 of 2016) commenced on 21 March 2016, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |
| Registration of Skilled Professionals (Amendment) Regulations 2021 (LN 28 of 2021) | 22 March 2021 |

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Skilled Professionals Regulations 2016.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the date the Act comes into force.

## reg 2 Interpretation

(1) In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Act** means the Registration of Skilled Professionals Act 2016 ; and

**special registration** means the registration of a skilled professional by the Committee.

(2) In these Regulations, words and phrases have the same meaning as under the Act unless the context otherwise requires.

## reg 3 Professions

For the purpose of the definition of “skilled professional” in section 2 of the Act, a “profession” means a paid occupation that requires a formal qualification or specialised areas of training.

## reg 4 Application for special registration

(1) For the purpose of section 9 of the Act and notwithstanding any other written law, any individual may make an application to the Committee for special registration as a skilled professional.

(2) An application under subregulation (1) must be—

(a) made in the prescribed form set out in Schedule 1 ; and

(b) accompanied by the prescribed fee set out in Schedule 2 .

[subreg (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 223, 1 December 2016 LN 28 of 2021 reg 2, 22 March 2021]

(3) The Committee must immediately disregard any application that does not comply with subregulation (2).

(4) When considering an application for special registration, including any supporting documentation, the Committee may have regard to the following—

(a) the profession within which the applicant is qualified or has expertise in;

(b) the qualification or training of the applicant;

(c) the country where the applicant obtained his or her qualification or training;

(d) the relevant professional experience of the applicant;

(e) the country or countries where the applicant has worked in the profession;

(f) the standards of any relevant professional body in Fiji;

(g) whether the applicant has any pending criminal charges or is a party to any current civil proceedings in any country; and

(h) whether the applicant has any previous criminal conviction or has been subjected to any order made against him or her in any civil proceedings in any country.

(5) The Committee may invite any person or organisation to provide advice or information on an application for special registration or any other matter.

(6) The Committee may, at any time, make necessary amendments to the form in Schedule 1 .

[subreg (6) am LN 28 of 2021 reg 2, 22 March 2021]

## reg 5 Lodgement of appeal

Pursuant to section 12(2) of the Act, an appeal against the decision of the Committee must—

(a) be in writing;

(b) clearly and concisely set out the grounds for the appeal;

(c) be accompanied by the prescribed fee set out in Schedule 2 ; and

(d) be sent to the Prime Minister.

[reg 5 insrt LN 28 of 2021 reg 3, 22 March 2021]
