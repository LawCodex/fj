## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Poisons (Industrial and Agricultural) Regulations 1962.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, “agricultural” includes “horticultural”.

## reg 3 Form of licence

The form of licence to import poisons required by section 35(1) of the Medicinal Products Act 2011 shall be as set out in Schedule 1.

[reg 3 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 203, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 4 Use of poisons for control of pests

The permanent secretary responsible for agriculture or his or her duly authorised officer may use or issue to agriculturists the poisons set out in Schedule 3 for the purpose of controlling noxious weeds or pests.

[reg 4 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 203, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 5 Register

A Register of Receipts and Issues of all poisons used for industrial or agricultural purposes shall be kept by the person responsible for the custody of the poison in the form as set out in Schedule 2.

## reg 6 Storage of poisons

When it is necessary to store poisons for industrial or agricultural purposes the following conditions shall apply. They shall be stored—

(a) in a cupboard or drawer which is used solely for that purpose; or

(b) in a room or building used solely for that purpose.

## reg 7 Cupboards etc to be kept locked

(1) All cupboards, drawers, rooms or buildings used for the storage of poisons as aforesaid shall be kept locked and the key shall be in the sole possession of some responsible person who shall be acquainted with the nature of the poison and who is capable of recognising the symptoms peculiar to that poison and is aware of and is capable of administering the appropriate antidote.

(2) The front external part of a drawer or the external side of any door or cupboards, rooms and buildings used solely for such purpose as aforesaid shall bear a notice in a prominent position with the word “POISON” thereon in the English, iTaukei and Hindi languages, the letters of which—

(a) on a notice on the front external part of any drawer shall be at least 1.5 inches in height; and

(b) on a notice on the doors of cupboards, rooms and buildings shall be at least 3 inches in height.

[subs (2) am LN 37 of 1964 reg 2, effective 8 May 1964; Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, effective 2 July 2010]

## reg 8 Containers

No poison shall be issued unless in a container impervious to the poison, and distinctly labelled “POISON” with the name of the poison and the purpose for which it is to be used.
