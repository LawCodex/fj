## reg 1 Short title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Real Estate Agents (Fees, Forms and Appeals) Regulations 2008 and commences on 1 July 2008.

[reg 1 am LN 6 of 2024 reg 2, effective 1 February 2024]

## reg 2 Prescribed forms and fees

(1) The forms required to be prescribed under the Act are set out in Schedule 1.

(2) The fees required to be prescribed under the Act are set out in Schedule 2.

## reg 2A Rate of commission on residential property (Revoked) (Repealed)

(Repealed)

## reg 2B Rate of commission in contract of agency

Any commission charged by a real estate agent, an officer of a licensee that is a company, a salesperson or a branch manager on the sale of any property must—

(a) be agreed to in writing in the contract of agency between the real estate agent, officer, salesperson or branch manager and the principal; and

(b) only be paid on the completion of the service or work specified in the contract of agency.

[reg 2B insrt LN 76 of 2022 reg 2, effective 26 May 2022]

## reg 3 Procedure of appeals

(1) An appeal made to the Minister under section 77 of the Act must—

(a) be in writing;

(b) set out the grounds of appeal;

(c) be accompanied by the prescribed fee.

(2) The appellant must first serve a copy of the appeal to the Board and send the appeal to the Minister.

(3) When an appeal is received by the Board, the Board shall, within 14 working days send to the Minister its records of proceedings relating to the appeal, including the decision that is subject to appeal and the reasons for the decision.

(4) The Minister must determine the appeal within 15 working days after receiving the records of proceedings from the Board under subregulation (3).
