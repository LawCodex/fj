# CUSTOMS TARIFF CLASSIFICATION OPINION FEES REGULATIONS 1991

Customs Tariff Classification Opinion Fees Regulations 1991 (LN 95 of 1991) commenced on 1 January 1992, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Customs Tariff (Classification Opinion Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 (LN 104 of 2004) | 1 January 2005 |
| Customs Tariff (Classification Opinion Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2012 (LN 2 of 2012) | 1 January 2012 |
| Customs Tariff Classification Opinion Fees (Amendment) Regulations 2016 (LN 68 of 2016) | 1 August 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Customs Tariff Classification Opinion Fees Regulations 1991.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on 1 January 1992.

## reg 2 Application and fees

(1) Applications for Tariff Classification Opinions shall be made in writing accompanied by the fee prescribed in these Regulations.

(2) A fee of $50 shall be payable to the Comptroller of Customs for each written request for tariff classification opinion.

[subreg (2) am LN 104 of 2004 reg 2, 1 January 2005 LN 2 of 2012 reg 2, 1 January 2012 LN 68 of 2016 reg 2, 1 August 2016]

(3) Where before the Comptroller has prepared a tariff classification opinion the applicant notifies in writing to the Comptroller that the opinion is no longer required, the Comptroller may, at the Comptroller’s discretion, refund or remit in whole or in part the fee paid or payable pursuant to these Regulations.
