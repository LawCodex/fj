## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Suva (Advertisement) Regulations 1933.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**advertisement** means any word, device or pictorial representation intended to promote the sale of any commodity or to induce persons to attend any place of entertainment or meeting for any purpose whatsoever and includes a sky sign and an occulting sign;

**building surveyor** means the building surveyor appointed by the Council;

**Council** means the Suva City Council;

**sky sign** means any advertisement situated within the boundaries of the city of Suva which, or any part of which, shall be visible against the sky from some point in any public place in Fiji or in the waters thereof, and includes any aircraft, kite, parachute or similar device employed wholly or in part for the display of any advertisement; and

**Town Clerk** means the town clerk appointed by the Council.

## reg 3 Exhibition of advertisement

No person shall within the city of Suva exhibit or cause to be exhibited any advertisement, sky sign or occulting sign which is visible from any place to which the public have access or from the waters of Fiji or from any land or building not belonging to him or her or not in his or her possession without the licence of the Town Clerk.

## reg 4 Approval of building surveyor

A person making application for a licence shall produce to the Town Clerk a certificate in writing from the building surveyor that the proposed advertisement and the place and manner in which it is proposed to be exhibited meet with his or her approval.

## reg 5 Licences may be cancelled

A licence issued under the provisions of these Regulations may be for such time as the Council may decide and may permit the exhibition of any advertisement, sky sign or occulting sign either unconditionally or subject to conditions to be endorsed thereon, and such licence may be cancelled by the Town Clerk in writing upon the breach of any such condition. A licence issued as aforesaid may be renewed from time to time by the Council.

## reg 6 Removal of advertisement

The Council shall have an unrestricted right from time to time to require any advertisement, sky sign or occulting sign to be removed, and the owner or occupier of the building on which the same is erected shall forthwith on receiving notice from the Council to that effect remove or take down such advertisement, sky sign or occulting sign.

## reg 7 Discretion to refuse licence

The Council may without disclosing any reason refuse to grant any licence to any person or persons under these Regulations.

## reg 8 Fees

There shall be paid to the Town Clerk for every licence under these Regulations and for the renewal of any licence an annual fee of $0.50.

## reg 9 Removal of unauthorised advertisement

Any advertisement, sky sign or occulting sign exhibited in contravention of these Regulations may be removed by order of the Council and all expenses of such removal may be recovered in a summary manner from the exhibitor.
