# COMPANIES (PENALTIES) REGULATIONS 2015

Companies (Penalties) Regulations 2015 (LN 108 of 2015) commenced on 1 January 2016, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Stamp Duties (Repeal) Act 2020 (No 16 of 2020) | 1 August 2020 |

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Companies (Penalties) Regulations 2015.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on 1 January 2016.

## reg 2 Interpretation

(1) In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, “Act” means the Companies Act 2015 .

(2) In these Regulations, words and phrases have the same meaning as under the Act unless the context otherwise requires.

## reg 3 Maximum penalty

Pursuant to section 626 of the Act, the maximum penalty for an offence under the Act is prescribed under Schedule 1 .

## reg 4 Penalty notice

(1) The Registrar and the Reserve Bank of Fiji may issue a penalty notice indicating the fixed penalty for an offence under the Act in accordance with Schedule 2 .

(2) A penalty may be payable in addition to any late fee prescribed under the Act.

## reg 5 Schedule 6 replaced

Pursuant to section 713(2) of the Act, these Regulations replace Schedule 6 to the Act.
