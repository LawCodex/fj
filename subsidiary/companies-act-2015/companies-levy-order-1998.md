# COMPANIES (LEVY) ORDER 1998

Companies (Levy) Order 1998 (LN 79 of 1998)1 commenced on 29 May 1998, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

1
This was rectified by the Corrigendum published on 5 June 1998.

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Companies (Levy) Order 1998.

## O 2 Levies

The levies specified in the Schedule shall apply to every purchase and every sale of securities recorded on the stock exchange or notified to it under its rules.

## O 3 Revocation

The Capital Markets Development Authority Levy Order 1997 (LN 115 of 1997) is hereby revoked.
