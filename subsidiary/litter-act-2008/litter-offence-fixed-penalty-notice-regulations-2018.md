## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Litter Offence (Fixed Penalty Notice) Regulations 2018.

(2) These Regulations are deemed to have come into force on the date the Litter Act 2008 came into force.

## reg 2 Interpretation

(1) In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, “Act” means the Litter Act 2008.

(2) In these Regulations, words and phrases have the same meaning as under the Act unless the context otherwise requires.

## reg 3 Fixed penalty notice

(1) A fixed penalty notice to be served under section 22(1) of the Act is to be in the form prescribed in the Schedule.

(2) Nothing in subregulation (1) affects the validity of a fixed penalty notice issued prior to the commencement of these Regulations and any such fine is deemed to be paid or payable in accordance with the law applicable at the time such fixed penalty notice was issued.

## reg 4 Repeal

The Litter Offence (Prescribed Notice) Regulations 1991 and the Litter (Prescribed Form) Regulations 1997 are revoked.
