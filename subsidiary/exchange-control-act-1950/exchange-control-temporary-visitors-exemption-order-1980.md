## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Exchange Control (Temporary Visitors’ Exemption) Order 1980.

## O 2 Exemption

(1) There shall be exempted from the obligations imposed by the provisions of section 4 of the Act any person in Fiji who is not resident in Fiji.

(2) The Minister may, at any time, direct that the exemption conferred by this Order shall not apply to such person as may be specified in the direction.

## O 3 Revocation

The Exchange Control (Temporary Visitors Exemption) Order, published at page 6329 of Volume X of the Laws of Fiji 1967 Edition, is revoked.
