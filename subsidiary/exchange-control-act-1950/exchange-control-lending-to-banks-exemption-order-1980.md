# EXCHANGE CONTROL (LENDING TO BANKS EXEMPTION) ORDER 1980

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Exchange Control (Lending to Banks Exemption) Order 1980.

## O 2 Exemption

(1) There shall be exempted from the provisions of section 32(3) of the Act the lending of any money or securities by any person resident in Fiji to any office or branch in Fiji of a bank or any financial institution licensed under the Banking Act 1995 and which is, by any means, controlled (whether directly or indirectly) by persons resident outside Fiji.

(2) The Minister may, at any time, direct that the exemption conferred by this Order shall not apply as may be specified in the direction.

## O 3 Revocation

The following are revoked—

(a) Exchange Control (Lending to Banks Exemption) Order 1970 (Legal Notice No 62 of 1970); and

(b) Exchange Control (Lending to Banks Exemption) Order 1975 (Legal Notice No 116 of 1975).
