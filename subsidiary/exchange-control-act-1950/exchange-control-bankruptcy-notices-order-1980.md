## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Exchange Control (Bankruptcy Notices) Order 1980.

## O 2 Form of notice

Any bankruptcy notice issued in accordance with the Bankruptcy Rules 1915 of the United Kingdom at the request of a person (or persons) residing outside Fiji, being a creditor for an amount due under a judgment, order or award, shall (with such variations as circumstances may require)—

(a) in a case in which permission of the Minister has not been obtained for payment of the amount due, be in a form set out in Part 1 of the Schedule;

(b) in a case in which permission of the Minister for payment into a blocked account has been obtained, be in a form set out in Part 2 of the Schedule.

## O 3 Revocation

The Exchange Control (Bankruptcy Notices) Order, published at pages 6331 and 6332 of Volume X of the Laws of Fiji 1967 Edition, is revoked.

(Title)

Take notice that whereas CD (or CD and Co) of

a person (or persons) residing outside Fiji, has (or have) obtained judgment against you for the sum of $ and whereas permission of the Minister for Finance for payment of the said sum by you to him or her (or them) has not been obtained you must within 7 days after service of this notice on you excluding the day of such service pay into Court in accordance with rules of court made pursuant to the Schedule 4 to the said Act the said sum of $ the amount of the said judgment whereon execution has not been stayed or you must secure or compound for the said sum to his or her (or their) satisfaction or to the satisfaction of of or his or her (or their) duly authorised agent or to the satisfaction of the court in some manner not prohibited in the circumstances set out above by the said Act or you must satisfy this Court that you have a counterclaim, set off or cross demand against him or her (or them) which equals or exceeds the amount claimed by him or her (or them) which you could not set up in the action or other proceedings in which the judgment (or order) was obtained.

#### PART 2 BANKRUPTCY NOTICE FOR USE WHEN THE MINISTER HAS GRANTED PERMISSION FOR PAYMENT INTO A BLOCKED ACCOUNT

(Title)

To AB (or AB and Co) of

Take notice that whereas CD (or CD and Co)

of a person (or persons) residing outside Fiji has (or have) obtained a final judgment (or order) against you for the sum of $ and whereas permission of the Minister responsible for finance for payment of the said sum by you to him or her (or them) has not been obtained but the Minister responsible for finance has directed that the said sum shall be paid to a blocked account and the said CD (or CD and Co) has (or have) nominated an account in his or her (or their) name at the Bank of pursuant to paragraph 6 of Schedule 3 to the said Act you must within 7 days after service of this notice on you excluding the day of service pay the said sum of $ being the amount of the said judgment (or order) on which execution has not been stayed either into the said blocked account or to the satisfaction of the said CD (or CD and Co) or to the satisfaction of of his or her (or their) duly authorised agent or to the satisfaction of the court in manner not prohibited by the said Act or you must satisfy this Court that you have a counterclaim set off or cross demand against him or her (or them) which equals or exceeds the amount claimed by him or her (or them) and which you could not set up in the action or other proceedings in which the judgment (or order) was obtained.
