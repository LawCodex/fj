## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Exchange Control (Gold Coin Exemption) Order 1980.

## O 2 Exemption from provisions of the Act

(1) Gold coin shall be exempted from the provisions of sections 3, 4 and 5 of the Act.

(2) The Minister may, at any time, direct that the exemption conferred by this Order shall not apply as may be specified in the direction.

## O 3 Revocation

The Exchange Control (Collectors’ Pieces Exemption) Order, published at page 6329 of Volume X of the Laws of Fiji 1967 Edition, is revoked.
