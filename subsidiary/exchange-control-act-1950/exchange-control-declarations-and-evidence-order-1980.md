## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Exchange Control (Declarations and Evidence) Order 1980.

## O 2 Prescribed evidence

(1) The prescribed evidence, for the purposes of section 10(1)(b) of the Act, to be produced to the person issuing the security, is evidence that the person to whom the security is to be issued is not resident outside Fiji and, from facts known to the person giving the evidence or from enquires made by that person, is not, to the best of his or her belief, acquiring the security as the nominee of any person resident outside Fiji.

(2) The evidence shall be given by a declaration in writing signed by or on behalf of the person to whom the security is to be issued.

## O 3 Prescribed declaration to transferee

The prescribed declaration, for the purposes of section 11(1)(b) of the Act, to be delivered to the transferee at or before the time of the transfer, is a declaration in the form set out in the Schedule 1 or in a form to the like effect made by an authorised depositary.

## O 4 Prescribed evidence by person keeping register

The prescribed evidence, for the purposes of section 15(a) of the Act, to be produced to a person concerned with the keeping of a register in Fiji, is—

(a) in the case of the issue of securities, the evidence prescribed by order 2;

(b) in the case of the transfer of securities, the declaration prescribed by order 5 and evidence given by a declaration in writing signed by an authorised depositary that the transferee is not resident outside Fiji and, from facts known to the person giving the evidence or from enquiries made by that person, is not, to the best of his or her belief, acquiring the security as the nominee of any person resident outside Fiji; and

(c) in the case of the registration of securities transferable by means of bearer certificates, the evidence prescribed by order 2 given by a declaration in writing signed by an authorised depositary.

## O 5 Prescribed evidence to authorised depositary

The prescribed evidence, for the purposes of section 17(5) of the Act, to be produced to an authorised depositary, is the evidence prescribed in the case of the transfer of securities by order 4(b).

## O 6 Prescribed declaration to authorised depositary

The prescribed declaration, for the purposes of section 18(1)(a) of the Act, to be delivered to an authorised depositary, is a declaration in a form set out in Schedule 2 or in a form to the like effect made by an authorised depositary.

## O 7 Revocation

The Exchange Control (Declarations and Evidence) Order, published at pages 6324 and 6325 of Volume X of the Laws of Fiji 1967 Edition, is revoked.

### SCHEDULE 1 DECLARATION UNDER SECTION 11

The holder of the abovementioned security is not resident outside Fiji and, from facts known to us or from enquiries we have made, is not, to the best of our belief, holding the security as the nominee of a person resident outside Fiji.

### SCHEDULE 2 DECLARATION UNDER SECTION 18

We hereby declare that, to our knowledge, the security specified in the attached Schedule has not been acquired by the present owner in contravention of any Exchange Control legislation in force in Fiji since 2 September 1939 and has not been imported into Fiji in contravention of such legislation.
