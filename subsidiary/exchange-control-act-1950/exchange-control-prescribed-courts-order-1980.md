## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Exchange Control (Prescribed Courts) Order 1980.

## O 2 Prescribed court

The court prescribed for the purposes of paragraph 4 of Schedule 4 to the Act is the High Court.

## O 3 Revocation

The Exchange Control (Prescribed Courts) Order, published at page 6334 of Volume X of the Laws of Fiji 1967 Edition, is revoked.
