## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Exchange Control (Blocked Accounts) Order 1980.

## O 2 Investment of sums in blocked accounts

The investments in which sums standing to the credit of a blocked account may subject to the provisions of paragraph 4 of Schedule 3 to the Exchange Control Act 1950 be invested are any securities which are issued or guaranteed as to both principal and interest by the Government.
