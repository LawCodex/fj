## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Exchange Control (Authorised Dealers) Order 1999.

## O 2 Authorised dealers

Authorised dealers listed in Part 1 of Schedule 1 are authorised to act for the purposes of the Act as dealers in gold and all foreign currencies and may for those purposes do all or any of the things listed in Schedule 2.

## O 3 Authorised restricted dealers

Authorised dealers listed in Part 2 of Schedule 1 are authorised to act for the purposes of the Act as dealers in all foreign currencies and may for those purposes do all or any of the things listed as items (a) to (f) in Schedule 2.

## O 4 Authorised money changers

Authorised dealers listed in Part 3 of Schedule 1 are authorised to act for the purposes of the Act as money changers in all foreign currencies for travel related purposes only and may for those purposes do all or any of the things listed as items (a) to (c) in Schedule 2.

## O 5 Conditions

An authorisation under this Order is conditional on compliance with any reasonable conditions imposed by the Reserve Bank of Fiji in writing from time to time.

## O 6 Orders revoked

The following Orders are revoked—

### SCHEDULE 2 PERMITTED ACTIVITIES

(a) the purchase, sale and repatriation of travellers cheques and foreign currency notes;

(b) the remittance of the proceeds of repatriated Fijian curreny notes;

(c) the issue of bank drafts;

(d) the issue of telegraphic or electronic transfers for payment and receipt of funds;

(e) the issue of Letters of Credit for trade related payments; and

(f) any other type of related transactions approved by the Reserve Bank of Fiji.
