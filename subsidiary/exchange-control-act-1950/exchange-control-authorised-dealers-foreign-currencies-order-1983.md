## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Exchange Control (Authorised Dealers) (Foreign Currencies) Order 1983.

## O 2 Authorised dealer

Offices in Fiji Thomas Cook Financial Services Limited are authorised to act for the purposes of this Act as authorised dealers in relation to all foreign currencies subject to the restrictions specified in the Schedule.

## 

### SCHEDULE RESTRICTIONS

#### cl1

Dealings restricted to—

(1) The purchase, sale and repatriation of travellers cheques and foreign currency notes.

(2) The remittance of the proceeds of repatriated Fijian currency notes.
