## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Exchange Control (Authorised Dealers) Order 1995, and shall come in force on 1 May 1995.

## O 2 Authorised dealers Schedule

Officers in Fiji of Deak International Limited are authorised to act for the purposes of the Act as authorised dealers in relation to all foreign currencies subject to the restrictions specified in the Schedule.

## 

### SCHEDULE DEALINGS RESTRICTED TO

#### CL 1

(1) The purchase, sale and surrender to authorised Banks of travellers cheques and foreign currency notes for travel related transactions only.
