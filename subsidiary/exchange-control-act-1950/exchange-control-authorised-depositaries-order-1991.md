## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Exchange Control (Authorised Depositaries) Order 1991, and shall be deemed to have come into force on 25 February 1991.

## O 2 Authorised depositaries Schedule

Offices in Fiji of the persons specified in the Schedule to this Order are authorised to act as authorised depositaries for the purposes of Part 4 of the Act.

## O 3 Revocation

The Exchange Control (Authorised Depositaries) Order 1986 is revoked.
