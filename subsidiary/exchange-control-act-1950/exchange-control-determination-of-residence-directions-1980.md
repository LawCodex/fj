## r 1 Short title

These Directions may be cited as the Exchange Control (Determination of Residence) Direction 1980.

## r 2 Determination of residence

A person or body of persons carrying on in Fiji a branch of any business shall, as respects such business as is carried on by that branch, be treated for all purposes of the Act as resident in Fiji.

## r 3 Saving

These Directions may be varied by subsequent directions as respects any particular person or body of persons.

## r 4 Withdrawal of direction

The Exchange Control (Determination of Residence) Direction, published at page 6333 of Volume X of the Laws of Fiji 1967 Edition, is withdrawn.
