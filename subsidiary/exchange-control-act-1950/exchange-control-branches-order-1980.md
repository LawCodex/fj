## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Exchange Control (Branches) Order 1980.

## O 2 Branch to be deemed body corporate

For the purposes of the Act, any transaction with or by a branch of any business, whether carried on by a body corporate or otherwise, shall be treated in all respects as if the branch were a body corporate resident where the branch is situate.

## O 3 Interpretation

References in this Order to a branch of a business shall be deemed to include a reference to the head office of that business.

## O 4 Revocation

The Exchange Control (Branches) Order, published at page 6332 of Volume X of the Laws of Fiji 1967 Edition, is revoked.
