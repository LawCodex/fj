# EXCHANGE CONTROL (SPECIFIED CURRENCY AND PRESCRIBED SECURITIES) ORDER 1980

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Exchange Control (Specified Currency and Prescribed Securities) Order 1980.

## O 2 Specified currency

All foreign currency is hereby specified as foreign currency to which section 4 of the Act applies.

## O 3 Specified securities

Section 19 of the Act shall apply to all securities on which capital moneys, dividends or interest are payable in any foreign currency or as respects which the holder has an option to require payment of any capital moneys, dividends or interest thereon in any foreign currency.

## O 4 Revocation

The Exchange Control (Specified Currency and Prescribed Securities) Order 1968 is revoked.
