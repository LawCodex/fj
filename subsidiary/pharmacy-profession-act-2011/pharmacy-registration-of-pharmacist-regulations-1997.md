# PHARMACY (REGISTRATION OF PHARMACIST) REGULATIONS 1997

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Pharmacy (Registration of Pharmacist) Regulations 1997.

## reg 2 Interpretation

For the purposes of section 22 of the Act—

(a) prescribed examinations means—

(i) any examination in the Diploma in Pharmacy prescribed by the Fiji School of Medicine; and

(ii) any examination set by the Pharmacy and Poisons Board of Fiji;

(b) prescribed course of practical training means a period of 52 weeks of employment comprising 2,000 hours under the supervision of a registered pharmacist at, the Colonial War Memorial Hospital, Lautoka Hospital, Labasa Hospital or an approved private pharmacy.

## reg 3 Practical training

For the purpose of regulation 2, the prescribed course of practical training shall include a minimum 1000 hours in direct contact with patients including dispensing, patient counselling and clinical pharmacy.

## reg 4 Proof of practical training

The Board may accept as proof of completion of the 2000 hours prescribed course of practical training a declaration duly completed and signed by the registered pharmacist responsible for the training.

## reg 5 First aid

The Board may accept satisfactory completion of a course in First Aid currently provided by the St John Ambulance Brigade in Fiji, the Fiji Red Cross Society or other body approved by the Board.

## reg 6 Evidence of foreign certificate

Any certificate of identity as a pharmacist from any foreign country shall be supplied directly to the Board from the body responsible for the issuing of the certificate in that foreign country.

## reg 7 Recognised qualifications

The Board may recognise qualifications from a country specified in the Schedule for the purpose of registration in Fiji of a person as a pharmacist.

## reg 8 Proof of character

(1) Any evidence of good fame and character from a foreign country in support of an application for registration as a pharmacist in Fiji shall be in the English language.

(2) If the evidence is in a foreign language, the evidence shall be translated in the English language and the translation to be certified by a legal practitioner of that foreign country.

## reg 9 Fees

The prescribed fees payable for an examination conducted by the Board shall be $9.09 (VAT EXCLUSIVE) for each application.

## reg 10 Board examination

The examination to be set by the Board shall be held twice a year in June and December.

## reg 11 Approved practical training institutions

The Board may, by notice in the Gazette, approve any other hospital, institution or private pharmacy business where the prescribed course of practical training may be undertaken.
