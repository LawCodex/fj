# PORTABLE ELECTRICAL APPARATUS EXEMPTION ORDER 1971

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Portable Electrical Apparatus Exemption Order 1971.

## O 2 Interpretation

(1) In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires—

**all-insulated tool** means an electrically driven tool in which all metal parts (except any chuck or other tool-holder, name plate, screw, rivet and other small part which is isolated from all other metal parts by reinforced insulation or by insulation no less effective than reinforced insulation) are covered with insulating material in the form of a durable and substantial continuous enclosure of insulating material;

**double-insulated cables** means cables with double insulation of the approved standard; and

**double insulated tool** means an electrically driven tool of which all the metal work which a person may handle is, where practicable, so separated from the conductors by double insulation of the approved standard as to prevent such metal work from becoming electrically charged or, where in the case of any such metal work such double insulation is impracticable, of which that metal work is so separated as aforesaid by reinforced insulation.

(2) Reference in this Order to a British Standard shall be construed as a reference to the British Standard of the number specified which is current at the date of the making of this Order and reference in this Order to an Australian or New Zealand Standard shall be construed in a similar manner.

## O 3 Exemption

(1) Subject to the conditions set out in order 4 and except as provided in paragraph (3), all-insulated tools and double-insulated tools are hereby exempted from the requirements of regulation 17 of the Factories (Electricity) Regulations 1971 as to the efficient earthing of metalwork of portable apparatus.

(2) Subject to the conditions set out in order 4 and to the provisions of paragraph (3), where a single portable apparatus only is supplied at low voltage through double-insulated cables from the unearthed secondary windings of an isolating transformer, the exposed metal work of the portable apparatus need not be earthed.

(3) The exemption granted by this Order shall not apply to any flexible metal covering of conductors.

## O 4 Conditions

(1) The conditions referred to in order 3(1) and (2) are—

(a) that the apparatus or isolating transformer shall be clearly and indelibly marked on the outside with the name or trade mark of the maker and his or her model or type reference;

(b) that the apparatus or isolating transformer shall be clearly and indelibly marked on the outside so as to certify—

(i) that the apparatus or isolating transformer complies with, in the case of the apparatus, British Standard 2769, 1964 or Australian Standard c100 and, in the case of an isolating transformer, with Australian Standard cl67 Ap or New Zealand Standard NZ51379;

(ii) that the apparatus or isolating transformer has been subjected by the maker to a routine test in accordance with the said Standard;

(c) that the insulation of the apparatus or isolating transformer shall be maintained so as to prevent danger.

## O 5 Offence

Any person who sells or lets on hire, or as agent of the seller or hirer causes or procures to be sold or let on hire, any apparatus or any isolating transformer in contravention of any of the provisions of this Order shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable upon conviction to a fine not exceeding $200.

## O 6 Exemption by certificate

The chief inspector may by certificate exempt any class or description of apparatus or isolating transformer from so much of the provisions of this Order as may be indicated in such certificate for such period and on such conditions as he or she may think fit.
