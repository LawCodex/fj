## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Copyright (Border Protection) Regulations 2003.

## reg 2 Form of notice

A notice required under section 128 must be in the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## reg 3 Evidence in support of claim

(1) A person giving a notice under section 128 must provide to the Comptroller evidence in support of the claim that an item is a work in which the person owns the copyright.

(2) The Comptroller may direct that the evidence be provided at the time the notice is given or at any later time.

## reg 4 Particulars in support of claim that item is a pirated copy

(1) A person who has given a notice under section 128 may provide to the Comptroller particulars in support of the claim that an item is a pirated copy.

(2) The Comptroller may request the person to submit further information if necessary.

## reg 5 Notice of transmission and other matters

A person giving a notice under section 128 must inform the Comptroller, in writing, of—

(a) a transmission of copyright in the work to which the notice relates;

(b) a change in the particulars contained in the notice or in an evidence given to the Comptroller in support of the claim that an item is a work or items are works as the case may be in which the person owns the copyright.

## reg 6 Security and indemnity

(1) A person giving notice under section 128 must give security or an indemnity of an amount and on terms and conditions as the Comptroller determines.

(2) The Comptroller may direct that security or indemnity be given at the time the notice is given or at a later time.

(3) A person to whom an item is to be or has been released from detention under section 132 must give security or an indemnity of amount and on terms and conditions as the Comptroller determines.

(4) If the Comptroller is required under section 128(3) to advise, or cause a notice to be served on the person who has given a notice under section 128, that advice or notice may be served on the appointed agent.

## reg 7 Agents

(1) Notice under section 128 may be given to the Comptroller by an agent of the claimant appointed in writing by the owner of the copyright in the work.

(2) The Comptroller’s advice to the claimant under section 128(3), may be served on the appointed agent appointed under subregulation (1).
