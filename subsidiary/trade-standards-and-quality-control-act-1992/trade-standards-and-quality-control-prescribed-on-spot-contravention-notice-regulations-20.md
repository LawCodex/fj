## reg 1 Short title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Trade Standards and Quality Control (Prescribed On-Spot Contravention Notice) Regulations 2011 and come into force upon gazettal.

## reg 2 Prescribed notice

For the purpose of section 17(1A) of the Trade Standards and Quality Control Act 1992, the prescribed notice is in the Schedule.
