## O 1 Short title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Trade Standards (Household Electric Refrigerating Appliances) Order 2007, and comes into force on 1 April 2008

## O 2 Interpretation

In this Order—

**AS/NZS 4474.1:2007** means the Australian/New Zealand Standard on the performance of household electrical appliances — Refrigerating appliances (Part 1: Energy consumption and performance) published on 15 August 2007 and reissued incorporating the following amendments—

(a) Amendment No. 1 (October 2008); and

(b) Amendment No. 2 (March 2011);

[def subst LN 92 of 2020 O 2, effective 23 October 2020]

**AS/NZS 4474.2:2009** means the Australian/New Zealand Standard on the performance of household electrical appliances — Refrigerating appliances (Part 2: Energy labelling and minimum energy performance standard requirements), 4th Edition 2009, and reissued incorporating the following amendments—

(a) Amendment No.1 (March 2011); and

(b) Amendment No. 2 (January 2014);

[def subst LN 92 of 2020 O 2, effective 23 October 2020]

**FS AS/NZS 4474.1:2020** means the AS/NZS 4474.1:2007, as adopted by virtue of this Order, subject to the amendments set out in this Order;

[def subst LN 92 of 2020 O 2, effective 23 October 2020]

**FS AS/NZS 4474.2:2020** means the AS/NZS 4474.2:2009, as adopted by virtue of this Order, subject to the amendments set out in this Order;

[def subst LN 92 of 2020 O 2, effective 23 October 2020]

**refrigerating appliances** means any electrical household refrigerating appliances covered under the Standard, and includes refrigerators, refrigerator and freezers or freezers covered under the Standard which—

(a) operate using the vapour compression cycle; and

(b) use mains electricity (230/240 Volts at 50 Hz) as the primary power source;

**Regulator** means the Director of energy; and

**Standard** means the FS AS/NZS 4474.1:2020 and the FS AS/NZS 4474.2:2020.

[def subst LN 92 of 2020 O 2, effective 23 October 2020]

## O 3 Application

(1) The FS AS/NZS 4474.1:2020 sets out the methods for determining performance characteristics of electric refrigerating appliances intended for household and similar use, and are to be read in conjunction with FS AS/NZS 4474.2:2020.

[para (1) am LN 92 of 2020 O 3, effective 23 October 2020]

(2) The FS AS/NZS 4474.2:2020 sets out the energy labelling and minimum energy performance standard requirements for electric mains powered refrigerating appliances of the vapour compression type intended for household and similar use, falling within the scope of AS/NZS 4474.1:2007 and AS/NZS 4474.2:2009.

[para (2) am LN 92 of 2020 O 3, effective 23 October 2020]

## O 4 FS AS/NZS 4474.1:2020

[para (1) subst LN 92 of 2020 O 4, effective 23 October 2020]

(2) The AS/NZS 4474.1:2007 is deemed to have been reissued under this Order as FS AS/NZS 4474.1:2020.

[para (2) am LN 92 of 2020 O 4, effective 23 October 2020]

(3) Any amendments to the AS/NZS 4474.1:2007 shall not become part of FS AS/NZS 4474.1:2020 unless such amendments are adopted under this Order.

[para (3) am LN 92 of 2020 O 4, effective 23 October 2020]

## O 6 Responsibilities of importers and sellers

A person who imports or sells any refrigerating appliances regulated under this Order must comply with this Order and the Standard.

## O 7 Registration

(1) A person who intends to import or sell any refrigerating appliances may apply to the Regulator for the registration of such appliances to ascertain conformity with the Standard.

(2) The Regulator may require the applicant to provide evidence that the refrigerating appliances comply with the Standard.

(3) Upon receiving an application for registration, the Regulator must process and decide the application as soon as practicable, and notify the applicant in writing, within 3 working days of making the decision.

[para (3) am LN 92 of 2020 O 6, effective 23 October 2020]

## O 8 Labelling and standards guidelines

(1) The Regulator has the power to make, amend, suspend or revoke guidelines under this Order in relation to energy labeling and standards.

(2) For the purpose of this Order, the Fiji Appliance Energy Standards and Labelling Program Operational Procedures Guideline (available from the Regulator directly) which is in existence before the coming into force of this Order is deemed to be a guideline made pursuant to this Order.

[para (2) am LN 92 of 2020 O 7, effective 23 October 2020]

## O 10 Inspections

(1) The Regulator may, by written notice, require a person who imports or sells refrigerating appliances to make available one or more units of such appliances to be examined or tested to determine whether the product complies with MEPS levels set out under the Standard, and with the efficiency claims stated in the registration of such appliances.

(2) Upon receipt of such notice, the importer or seller must make all requested unit(s) available to the Regulator free of charge within 10 working days after receipt of a notice.

(3) The Regulator must return the refrigerating appliances in saleable condition.

(4) The guideline made under order 8 will provide further information on check-testing procedures.

(5) After the examination or testing of refrigerating appliances under this order the Regulator must notify the importer or seller to collect such appliances within 20 days from the date of the notice.

(6) If the importer or seller fails to comply with paragraph (5), the Regulator may return such appliances to the importer or seller, at the costs to be borne by the importer or seller.

## O 11 Legal proceedings and offences

(1) Offences set out in Part 4 of the Act apply to this Order or the Standard.

(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1), the Regulator may—

(a) direct, in writing, an importer or seller of refrigerating appliances to recall any such appliances on market in Fiji that do not comply with the Standard;

(b) use the naming and shaming protocols specified in the guidelines made under order 8.

(3) An inspector is responsible for facilitating and coordinating legal proceedings for the purposes of this Order or the Standard.

## O 12 Transitional and savings

(1) The Standard does not apply to refrigerating appliances imported before 1 January 2012, which may be sold without complying with this Order or compliance with the MEPS and labelling requirements of the Standard.

[para (1) am LN 92 of 2020 O 9, effective 23 October 2020]

(2) However, the importer or seller of the refrigerating appliances must prove that such appliances were imported before 1 January 2012.

[para (2) am LN 92 of 2020 O 9, effective 23 October 2020]
