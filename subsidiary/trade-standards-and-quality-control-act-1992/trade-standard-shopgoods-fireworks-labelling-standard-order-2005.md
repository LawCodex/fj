## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Trade Standard (Shopgoods Fireworks Labelling Standard) Order 2005.

(2) This Order comes into force on 1 January 2006.

## O 2 Shopgoods fireworks labelling standard

For the purposes of the Act, the standard for labelling shopgoods fireworks imported, manufactured and sold in Fiji is set out in the Schedule.

## 

### SCHEDULE SHOPGOODS FIREWORKS LABELLING STANDARD

1. The following labelling standard is adopted from Australian Standard (AS 2187.3-1999) Explosives — Storage, transport and use; Part 3: Pyrotechnics — Shop goods fireworks — Design, performance and testing.

2. Definition

shopgoods fireworks are fireworks in the list of permitted fireworks in the Schedule to the Declaration of Fireworks as Dangerous Goods (LN 45 of 2004).

3. Labelling

Shopgoods fireworks that are too small to carry a label may be labelled only on an outer pack, provided they are sold in the outer pack only and not as, unlabelled single items. Shopgoods fireworks sold individually shall be labelled individually.

4. Information on labels

Labels on shopgoods fireworks shall contain the following information—

(a) the words ‘Shopgoods firework’;

(b) instructions for use;

Examples of instructions for use as appropriate to the particular firework are—

(i) Light at arm's length;

(ii) Light at arm's length and retire;

(iii) Light fuse and get away;

(iv) Straighten fuse, light tip of free end at arm's length and retreat immediately at least 5 metres;

(v) Do not hold in hand;

(vi) Place flat on ground;

(vii) Place upright on level ground;

(viii) Place on hard open surface;

(ix) Place boat on water;

(x) Insert upright in soft ground;

(xi) Stick end in ground and point away from people or flammable material;

(xii) Attach securely to an upright post;

(xiii) Attach securely to an upright post with nail provided;

(xiv) Ensure firework will not fall over;

(xv) Ensure firework is free to rotate;

(xvi) Fuse is located under tissue cover;

(xvii) Remove cover to expose fuse.

(c) appropriate warnings;

Examples of warnings—

(i) Light fuse and retreat at least 5 metres;

(ii) Do not hold in hand when ignited;

(iii) Use only under adult supervision;

(iv) Do not fire near eyes or ears;

(v) Keep away from eyes;

(vi) Keep away from eyes and skin;

(vii) Do not put in mouth;

(viii) Do not inhale smoke;

(ix) Keep away from clothing etc;

(x) Do not dismantle;

(xi) Spent sparklers remain very hot;

(xii) Never position any part of the body over the firework;

(xiii) Do not put ignited item into confined area;

(xiv) Do not direct or point towards people, animals or buildings.

(d) a description of the principal effect of the firework after ignition;

Examples of descriptions of the principal effects are contained in the phrases below and in the column titled ‘Principal Effects(s)’ in the table below—

(i) Ejects/emits bang(s)/report(s);

(ii) Ejects/emits star(s);

(iii) Ejects/emits showers of sparks;

(iv) Emits bang(s)/report(s) and showers of sparks;

(v) Emits ground spinners;

(vi) Shoots flaming balls;

(vii) Produces bangs/reports;

(viii) Produces whistles and showers of sparks;

(ix) Ejects whistles;

(x) Produces novelty effect;

(xi) Spins on ground;

(xii) Shoots upwards;

(xiii) Moves on ground;

(xiv) Spins on string.

Examples of descriptions of fireworks are contained in the column titled ‘Description’ in the Table in (d) above.

5. Form of information

Labels shall effectively and unambiguously convey the required information by words printed with a minimum print size of 3 mm on the firework and on outer packs in plain English on a contrasting background, by graphics or by both.
