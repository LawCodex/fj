# LEVY ON WATER METER ORDER 1995

Levy on Water Meter Order 1995 (LN 85 of 1995) commenced on 1 August 1995, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Levy on Water Meter Order 1995.

## O 2 Levy

The prescribed levy on each water meter shall be $0.50 per month and shall come into effect from 1 August 1995.
