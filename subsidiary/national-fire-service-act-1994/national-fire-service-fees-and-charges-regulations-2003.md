# NATIONAL FIRE SERVICE (FEES AND CHARGES) REGULATIONS 2003

National Fire Service (Fees and Charges) Regulations 2003 (LN 48 of 2003) commenced on 8 August 2003, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| National Fire Service (Fees and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 (No 67 of 2008) | 2 May 2008 |
| Business Licensing (Repeal) Act 2020 (No 28 of 2020) | 1 August 2020 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the National Fire Service (Fees and Charges) Regulations 2003.

## reg 2 Fees and charges

The fees and charges for the purposes of the Act are set out in the Schedule .

## reg 3 Recovery of fees and charges payable

Section 45 of the Act applies to the recovery of fees and charges under these Regulations.
