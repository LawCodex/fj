# INSURANCE POLICY LEVY ORDER 1995

Insurance Policy Levy Order 1995 (LN 83 of 1995) commenced on 14 July 1995, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Insurance Policy Levy Order 1995.

## O 2 Levy

The prescribed fire service levy or individual contribution shall be charged for each class of insurance policy specified below, where the inception date is on or after 1 August 1995—

(a) commercial fire policies— All policies covering commercial (non-domestic) property for the peril of fire. This will include fire, industrial all risks and contractors all risks policies. Prescribed levy — 0.06% of the policy sum insured or total value of the property, whichever is the greater;

(b) domestic fire policies— All policies covering domestic property for the peril of fire. This will include houseowners, householders and contract works policies. Prescribed levy — 0.06% of the policy sum insured or total value of the property whichever is the greater.

## O 3 Determination of “total value of property”

For the purpose of order 2, in determining the “total value of property”, the following criteria shall be used—

(a) where the basis of settlement in respect of any property insured by the policy is the property’s replacement value the levy shall be applied to such actual total replacement value plus the amount of professional fees and escalation provision also insured;

(b) where the basis of settlement in respect of any property insured by the policy is the property’s indemnity value the levy shall be applied to such actual total indemnity value plus the amount of professional fees and escalation provision also insured;

(c) for the purpose only of removing any doubt as to the meaning and intention of (a) and (b) above, where any policy has a sum insured which is less than the actual total value of property insured by such policy (commonly referred to as a first loss policy), the levy shall be applied to the actual total value in accordance with (a) and (b) above and not the policy sum insured.

## O 4 Payment of levies

All levies charged under this order shall be paid to the Authority within 21 days of the end of the month during which the policy is incepted. This requirement for payment and particular responsibility applies to—

(a) all insurers or reinsurers licensed or incorporated in Fiji;

(b) all licensed brokers in respect of all policies placed by or through them with any insurer or reinsurer outside of Fiji;

(c) any person or corporation who places his or her insurance with an insurer or reinsurer outside of Fiji whether by himself or herself or through an agent other than a licensed broker.
