# RETURN BY INSURANCE COMPANIES REGULATIONS 1995

Return by Insurance Companies Regulations 1995 (LN 131 of 1995) commenced on 17 November 1995, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Return by Insurance Companies Regulations 1995.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, “Authority” means the National Fire Authority.

## reg 3 Return by insurance companies

(1) Each insurance company licensed by the Commissioner of Insurance of Fiji and Fiji Reinsurance Corporation shall before 31 October each year lodge with the Authority a return in the form of Schedule 1 showing the total gross sums insured of all policies in force as at the preceding 30 September. Where a policy is written on a first loss basis, the sum insured shall equate to the total value at risk. All inwards reinsurance is to be excluded.

(2) Each insurance broker licensed by the Commissioner of Insurance shall before 31 October each year lodge with the Authority a return in the form of Schedule 2 showing the gross sums insured of all policies placed overseas by them during the preceding year ending 30 September. Where a policy is written on a first loss basis, the sum insured shall equate to the total value at risk.

(3) The return shall include a statutory declaration in the form of Schedule 3 made by the manager, secretary, agent or other person for the time being performing the duties of the manager, secretary or agent for the company to the effect that according to the duties of the company and to the best of his or her knowledge and belief, the return contains a true statement of the gross sum insured by that company.

## reg 4 Failure to transmit return an offence

(1) Any insurance company which fails to transmit a return within the specified time or which transmits an incorrect or incomplete return shall be guilty of an offence against these Regulations and the Act and shall be liable to a penalty of not more than $500 provided under section 47 of the Act.

(2) If the failure to transmit a return is due to the act or omission of any person specified in regulation 3(3) , that person shall be liable to the penalty provided under section 47 of the Act.

## reg 5 Return by the Commissioner of Insurance

(1) The Insurance Commissioner shall by 31 October in each year lodge with the Authority a return in respect of all policies covering the peril of fire placed with an overseas unlicensed, insurer other than through a licensed insurance broker, during the preceding year ending 30 September.

(2) The return shall list all such policies and in respect of each show the following—

(i) the name of the insured;

(ii) the policy due date; and

(iii) the total sum insured as described by order 3 of the Insurance Policy Levy Order 1995.
