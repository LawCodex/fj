## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Public Enterprise (Civil Aviation Authority of Fiji) Regulations 1999.

(2) These Regulations come into force on a date to be appointed by the Minister and published in the Gazette.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations—

**Act** means the Public Enterprise Act 1996; and

**Civil Aviation Authority of Fiji** means the Civil Aviation Authority of Fiji established under section 3 of the Civil Aviation Authority of Fiji Act 1979.

## reg 3 Transfer of assets

For the purpose of the Act, the assets of the Civil Aviation Authority of Fiji specified in the Schedule are vested in Airports Fiji Limited.
