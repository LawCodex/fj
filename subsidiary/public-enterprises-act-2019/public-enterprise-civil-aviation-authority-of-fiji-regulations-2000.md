## ] reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Enterprise (Civil Aviation Authority of Fiji) Regulations 2000.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations “Authority” has the meaning given in the Civil Aviation Authority of Fiji Reform Act 1999.

## reg 3 Transfer of assets

The assets of the Authority specified in Schedule A are vested in Airports Fiji Limited and assets of Airports Fiji Limited specified in Schedule B are vested in the Authority.
