## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Enterprise (Fiji Broadcasting Commission) Regulations 1998.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Act** means the Public Enterprise Act 1996; and

**Fiji Broadcasting Commission** means the Fiji Broadcasting Commission established under section 3 of the Broadcasting Commission Act 1952.

## reg 3 Transfer of assets

For the purpose of the Act, the assets of the Fiji Broadcasting Commission specified in Part 1 of the Schedule are vested in the Island Networks Corporation Limited.

## reg 4 Transfer of liabilities

For the purpose of the Act, the liabilities of the Fiji Broadcasting Commission specified in Part 2 of the Schedule are vested in the Islands Networks Corporation Limited.
