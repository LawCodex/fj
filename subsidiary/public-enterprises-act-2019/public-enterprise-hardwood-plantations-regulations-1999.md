## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Enterprise (Hardwood Plantations) Regulations 1999.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, “Act” means the Public Enterprise Act 1996.

## reg 3 Transfer of assets

(1) For the purpose of the Act, forest assets currently owned by the State specified in Part 1 of the Schedule are vested in Fiji Hardwood Corporation Limited.

(2) For the purpose of the Act, non-forest assets currently owned by the State specified in Part 2 of the Schedule are vested in Fiji Hardwood Corporation Limited.
