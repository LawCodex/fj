# PUBLIC ENTERPRISE (PORTS TERMINAL LIMITED AND MARITIME PORTS AUTHORITY OF FIJI) REGULATIONS 2005

**Table of Amendments**

| --- | --- |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Enterprise (Ports Terminal Limited and Maritime Ports Authority of Fiji) Regulations 2005 and come into force on 1 July 2005.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, “Authority” means the Maritime Ports Authority of Fiji.

## reg 3 Transfer of assets

(1) The assets of the Authority specified in Schedule A and the assets of Ports Terminal Limited specified in Schedule B are vested in Fiji Ports Corporation Limited.

(2) The assets of the Authority specified in Schedule C are vested in Fiji Islands Maritime Safety Administration.
