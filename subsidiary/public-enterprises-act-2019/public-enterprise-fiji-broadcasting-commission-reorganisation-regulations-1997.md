## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Public Enterprise (Fiji Broadcasting Commission Reorganisation) Regulations 1997.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on a date to be appointed by the Minister and published in the Gazette.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Act** means the Public Enterprise Act 1996.

**Fiji Broadcasting Commission** means the Fiji Broadcasting Commission established by section 3 of the Fiji Broadcasting Commission Act 1952;

**Islands Network Corporation Limited** means the New Radio Company established under the Fiji Broadcasting Commission Charter; and

**Minister** means the Minister responsible for public enterprises.

## reg 3 Transfer of assets

For the purpose of this Act, assets currently owned by Fiji Broadcasting Commission specified in the Schedule shall be vested in the Islands Network Corporation Limited.
