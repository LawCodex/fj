## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Enterprises (Yaqara Pastoral Company Limited) Regulations 2004.

## reg 2 Transfer of assets

For the purpose of the Act, the following Government assets with the Ministry for Agriculture are transferred to and vested in Yaqara Pastoral Company Limited—

(a) one only Caterpillar Bulldozer D3C, Registration No GL 832;

(b) one only Mazda 4x4 Twin Cab B2500, Registration No GM 338;

(c) only 4 wheel Drive Massey Tractor 350, Registration No GL 416.
