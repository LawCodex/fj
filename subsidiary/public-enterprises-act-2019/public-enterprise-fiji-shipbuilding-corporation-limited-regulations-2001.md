## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Enterprise (Fiji Shipbuilding Corporation Limited) Regulations 2001.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations—

**Act** means the Public Enterprise Act 1996;

**Company** means the Fiji Shipbuilding Corporation Limited.

## reg 3 Transfer of assets

For the purpose of the Act, assets specified in Schedules A and B are vested in the Company.
