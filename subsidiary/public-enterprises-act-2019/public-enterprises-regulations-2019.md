## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Public Enterprises Regulations 2019.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 19 July 2019.

## reg 2 Percentage of shares

For the purposes of section 43(2)(e) of the Public Enterprises Act 2019, the prescribed percentage of the shares of the corporation or company is 50%.
