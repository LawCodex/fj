# PUBLIC ENTERPRISE (PORTS AUTHORITY OF FIJI) REGULATIONS 1998

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Enterprise (Ports Authority of Fiji) Regulations 1998.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Act** means the Public Enterprise Act 1996;

**Authority** means the Maritime and Ports Authority of Fiji;

**Minister** means the Minister responsible for public enterprises; and

**Ports Authority of Fiji** means the Ports Authority of Fiji established under section 4 of the Ports Authority of Fiji Act 1975.

## reg 3 Change of name

(1) For the purpose of the Act, the Ports Authority of Fiji is renamed “Maritime and Ports Authority of Fiji”.

(2) The Director General of the Authority is to be appointed by the Authority by notice in the Gazette.

## reg 4 Transfer of assets

(1) For the purpose of the Act, the assets specified in Schedule 1 are vested in Ports Terminal Limited at the values specified in that Schedule.

(2) For the avoidance of doubt, it is declared that the assets specified in Schedule 2 (being assets currently vested in Ports Authority of Fiji), remain vested in Maritime and Ports Authority of Fiji, at the values specified in that Schedule.

(3) Assets specified in Schedule 3 are transferred and vested in the Fiji Ports Corporation Limited.

[subreg (3) insrt LN 81 of 2004 reg 2, effective 8 October 2004]

## reg 5 Repeal

The Public Enterprise (Ports Authority of Fiji Reorganisation) Regulations 1997 (Legal Notice 144 of 1997) is repealed.
