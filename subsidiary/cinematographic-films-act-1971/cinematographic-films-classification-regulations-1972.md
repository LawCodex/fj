## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Cinematographic Films (Classification) Regulations 1972.

## reg 2 Classification of films

A film classified by the censor in accordance with section 13(1) of the Act may be displayed or announced whenever required by the Act by using the following capital letters which shall have the following meanings—

**G** shall mean that a film is approved for general exhibition;

**Y** shall mean that a film is approved for exhibition to persons over the age of 13 years;

**A** shall mean that a film is approved for exhibition to adult audiences only;

**R** shall mean that a film is approved for restricted public exhibition subject to conditions imposed by the censor, which conditions shall be specified in the display or announcement.

## reg 3 Films in a programme

When more than one film is exhibited in a programme of films, every film shown during such programme shall be deemed to be classified in the category of the most restricted film in the programme, and no person shall be admitted to the programme or any part of the programme unless he or she may lawfully attend the exhibition of the most restricted film in the programme.
