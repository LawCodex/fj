# CINEMATOGRAPHIC FILMS (FEES) REGULATIONS 1971

**Table of Amendments**

| --- | --- |
| Cinematographic Films (Forms and Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1974 (LN 220 of 1974) | 1 January 1975 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Cinematographic Films (Fees) Regulations 1971.

## reg 2 Prescribed fees

The fees appearing in the Schedule shall be the prescribed fees for the purposes of the Act in respect of the matters specified therein.
