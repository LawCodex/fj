# CINEMATOGRAPHIC FILMS (FORMS) REGULATIONS 1972

**Table of Amendments**

Cinematographic Films (Forms) Regulations 1972 (LN 20 of 1972) commenced on 11 February 1972, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Cinematographic Films (Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 1973 (LN 63 of 1973) | 15 June 1973 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Cinematographic Films (Forms) Regulations 1972.

## reg 2 Forms

The forms appearing in the Schedule shall be used for the purposes of the Act in respect of the matters specified therein.

## reg 3 Dimensions of notice — section 15 of the Act

The notice of certificate referred to in section 15 of the Act shall be not less than 210 millimetres in height and not less than 148 millimetres in width.

[reg 3 subst LN 63 of 1973 reg 3, effective 15 June 1973]
