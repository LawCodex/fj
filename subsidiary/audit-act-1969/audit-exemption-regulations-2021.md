## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Audit (Exemption) Regulations 2021.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the date of publication in the Gazette.

## reg 2 Exemption

The following off-budget State entity is exempted from audit by the Auditor-General—

ENERGY FIJI LIMITED
