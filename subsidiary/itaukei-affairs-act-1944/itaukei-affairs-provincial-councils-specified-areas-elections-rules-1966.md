# ITAUKEI AFFAIRS (PROVINCIAL COUNCILS) (SPECIFIED AREAS ELECTIONS) RULES 1966

iTaukei Affairs (Provincial Councils) (Specified Areas Elections) Rules 1966 (LN 216 of 1966) commenced on 9 December 1966, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Fijian Affairs (Amendment) Decree 2010 (No 31 of 2010) | 2 July 2010 |

## r 1 Short title

These Rules may be cited as the iTaukei Affairs (Provincial Councils) Regulations (Specified Areas Elections) Rules 1966.

[r 1 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 3, 2 July 2010]

## r 2 iTaukei Affairs (Provincial Councils) Regulations to apply

Subject to the provisions of these Rules, the provisions relating to the election of members to Provincial Councils contained in the iTaukei Affairs (Provincial Councils) Regulations 1996 , shall *mutatis mutandis* apply to the election to Provincial Councils of members to represent residents in closely settled areas specified in the Part 3 of Schedule 1 to such Regulations (such areas being hereinafter referred to as “specified areas”).

[r 2 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 3, 2 July 2010]

## r 3 Towns etc to be deemed tikina

For the purpose of electing members to Provincial Councils to represent specified areas, the specified areas specified in the first column of the Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the iTaukei Affairs (Provincial Councils) Regulations 1996, shall collectively be deemed to constitute a tikina of each of the provinces specified in the second column of such Part.

[r 3 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 3, 2 July 2010]

## r 4 Registration and returning officers

The Minister shall appoint such number of registration officers, assistant registration officers, returning officers and assistant returning officers as may be necessary for the purpose of registering electors and of conducting the election to Provincial Councils of members to represent specified areas.
