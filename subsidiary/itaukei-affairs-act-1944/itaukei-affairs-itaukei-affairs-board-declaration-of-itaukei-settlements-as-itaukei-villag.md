# ITAUKEI AFFAIRS (ITAUKEI AFFAIRS BOARD) (DECLARATION OF ITAUKEI SETTLEMENTS AS ITAUKEI VILLAGES) BY-LAWS 2010

iTaukei Affairs (iTaukei Affairs Board) (Declaration of iTaukei Settlements as iTaukei Villages) By-laws 2010 (LN 117 of 2010) commenced on 22 October 2010, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Native Lands (Amendment) Decree 2011 (No 7 of 2011) | 1 March 2011 |
| iTaukei Affairs (iTaukei Affairs Board) (Declaration of iTaukei Settlements as iTaukei Villages) (Amendment) By-laws 2012 (LN 23 of 2012) | 1 January 2012 |

## by-law 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These By-laws may be cited as the iTaukei Affairs (iTaukei Affairs Board) (Declaration of iTaukei Settlements as iTaukei Villages) By-laws 2010.

[para (1) am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, 1 March 2011]

(2) These By-laws shall commence on 22 October 2010.

## by-law 2 Application

These By-laws shall be applied in the registration of iTaukei settlements as a village.

[by-law 2 am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, 1 March 2011]

## by-law 3 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Board** means the iTaukei Board;

[def am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, 1 March 2011]

**Chairperson** means the Chairperson of the iTaukei Lands Commission;

[def am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, 1 March 2011insrt LN 23 of 2012 by-law 2, 1 January 2012]

**Commission** means the iTaukei Lands Commission established under section 4 of the iTaukei Lands Act 1905;

[def insrt LN 23 of 2012 by-law 2, 1 January 2012]

**Commissioner** means a Demarcation Commissioner appointed under by-law 6;

[def insrt LN 23 of 2012 by-law 2, 1 January 2012]

**iTaukei Settlement** means a place where a group of iTaukei families have come to live and make their homes; and

[def am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, 1 March 2011]

**village** means any settlement established in iTaukei land according to the custom or law or any settlement declared to be a village under a By-law duly approved by the Board, or in such other manner as may be prescribed by the Board and published in a newspaper published in the iTaukei language and circulated in Fiji.

[def subst LN 23 of 2012 by-law 2, 1 January 2012]

## by-law 4 Criteria of eligibility

(1) The Board shall have the power to register iTaukei settlements to be a village.

[para (1) am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, 1 March 2011]

(2) When assessing an iTaukei settlement’s suitability for registration as a village, the Board must ensure that the—

(a) proposed site must be on iTaukei land within the land boundary of its parent Yavusa;

(b) proposed village must be sited on iTaukei land and where majority of the members of the landowning unit of the land concerned has agreed to surrender in the traditional manner, ownership rights to such land, which would, after the registration, be owned communally by the new village;

(c) inhabitants of the village are from the Yavusa and have recognised and accepted a leader of the new village;

(d) new village, when registered, will be the new village of the inhabitants and will be accepted for such purpose for all official transactions including the Vola ni Kawa Bula which shall go through the processes as required by written law and the new village will also be registered as such by the iTaukei Lands Commission;

(e) site has been certified by the health authorities to be posing no threat to the health of the inhabitants and that it has a reliable source of fresh water;

(f) site has been certified by the Director of the Disaster Management Office as safe from natural hazards such as flooding, landslides or erosions; and

(g) request for registration must be made in writing with the endorsement and support of the relevant Tikina and Provincial Council;

(h) the boundaries of the proposed village has been demarcated by the iTaukei Lands Commission.

[para (2) am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, 1 March 2011 LN 23 of 2012 by-law 3, 1 January 2012]

## by-law 5 Declaration of villages by the Board

(1) The Board may by notice in the Gazette, declare any place to be a village and in a like manner, declare that any village shall cease to be a village.

(2) For the purpose of paragraph (1) the Board may declare those settlements currently in use as villages to be villages within the meaning of this by-law.

[by-law 5 insrt LN 23 of 2012 by-law 4, 1 January 2012]

## by-law 6 Appointment of Demarcation Commissioners

(1) The Chairperson shall appoint 3 Demarcation Commissioners who shall be responsible for the—

(a) demarcation of iTaukei villages; and

(b) confirmation of the iTaukei village boundaries by giving sworn evidence.

[para (1) Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, 1 March 2011]

(2) The appointment of Demarcation Commissioners shall be published in the Gazette.

(3) The Chairperson may revoke the appointment of a Demarcation Commissioner at any time the Chairperson deems necessary and appoint another Demarcation Commissioner in that person’s place.

[by-law 6 insrt LN 23 of 2012 by-law 4, 1 January 2012]

## by-law 7 Register of iTaukei villages

(1) There is to be a Register of iTaukei villages maintained by the iTaukei Lands Commission.

[para (1) am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, 1 March 2011]

(2) The Commission shall record on the Register of iTaukei Villages all villages which have been declared by the Board to be villages within the meaning of these By-laws.

[by-law 7 insrt LN 23 of 2012 by-law 4, 1 January 2012]
