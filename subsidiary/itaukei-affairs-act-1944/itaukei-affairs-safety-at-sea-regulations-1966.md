# ITAUKEI AFFAIRS (SAFETY AT SEA) REGULATIONS 1966

iTaukei Affairs (Safety at Sea) Regulations 1966 (LN 79 of 1966) commenced on 10 June 1966, as amended by:

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the iTaukei Affairs (Safety at Sea) Regulations 1966.

[reg 1 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 3, 2 July 2010]

## reg 2 Certificate of seaworthiness

(1) No iTaukei shall undertake a voyage in a vessel or canoe of under 2 tons measurement when travelling beyond the protection of the reefs, unless the owner of such vessel or canoe is in possession of a valid certificate of seaworthiness in respect of such vessel or canoe, issued in accordance with the provisions of subregulation (2) .

[subreg (1) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 3, 2 July 2010]

(2) Any person authorised by the Minister to act as a certifying officer under the provisions of these Regulations, shall, on application by the owner of a vessel or canoe of under 2 tons measurement, inspect such vessel or canoe, and, if he or she is satisfied that such vessel or canoe is sufficiently seaworthy to travel beyond the protection of the reefs, shall issue a certificate as in the form set out in the Schedule. Such a certificate shall be valid for a period of one year from the date of issue.

[subreg (2) am LN 112 of 1970 O 243, 8 October 1970]

(3) The owner and the master of a vessel or canoe of under 2 tons measurement shall not undertake or permit to be undertaken a voyage in such vessel or canoe beyond the protection of the reefs, unless a valid certificate has been issued in respect of such vessel or canoe under the provisions of subregulation (2) .

## reg 3 Equipment of vessels

(1) The owner and the master of a vessel or canoe of under 2 tons measurement, other than an outrigger canoe, shall ensure that, when such vessel or canoe travels at any time beyond the protection of the reefs, it shall be equipped with a bailer, a rudder or steering oar, at least 2 oars, in addition to the steering oar, if any, and at least 2 rowlocks.

(2) The owner and the master of an outrigger canoe of under 2 tons measurement shall ensure that, when such a canoe is travelling at any time beyond the protection of the reefs, it shall be equipped with a bailer and at least 2 vertical sculling oars *(a i sau)*.

(3) If any vessel or canoe travelling under the provision of subregulation (1) or (2) is being used for hire or reward, the owner and master shall further ensure that such vessel or canoe is equipped with sufficient lifebelts, lifejackets or other similar buoyancy aids for the number of persons on board.

## reg 4 Number of passengers

(1) The owner of a vessel or canoe in respect of which a valid certificate has been issued under the provisions of regulation 2 shall ensure that the maximum number of passengers permitted under such certificate to be carried in such vessel or canoe when travelling beyond the protection of the reefs is carved or painted in a legible fashion in a prominent position of such vessel or canoe.

(2) The owner and master of a vessel or canoe of under 2 tons measurement shall ensure that, at any time such vessel or canoe travels beyond the protection of the reefs, the number of persons on board does not exceed the maximum number permitted under a valid certificate issued under the provisions of regulation 2 .

## reg 5 Supplies of food and drink

Each person travelling on a vessel or canoe of under 2 tons measurement beyond the protection of the reefs shall ensure that he or she has a sufficient supply of food and drink for his or her own use during the voyage.

## reg 6 Minimum length of vessels to travel outside reef

No vessel or canoe of less than 12 feet in length shall travel beyond the protection of the reefs.

## reg 7 Penalty

Any person contravening or failing to observe the provisions of these Regulations shall be guilty of an offence and liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100 or to imprisonment not exceeding 6 months or to both such fine and imprisonment.
