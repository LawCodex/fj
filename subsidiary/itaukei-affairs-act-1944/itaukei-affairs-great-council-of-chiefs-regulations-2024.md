# ITAUKEI AFFAIRS (GREAT COUNCIL OF CHIEFS) REGULATIONS 2024

iTaukei Affairs (Great Council of Chiefs) Regulations 2024 (LN 11 of 2024) commenced on 26 February 2024, as amended by:

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the iTaukei Affairs (Great Council of Chiefs) Regulations 2024.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the date of publication in the Gazette.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**chief** means the head of the *vanua* who has been traditionally installed and recorded as such in the Register of iTaukei Landowners administered by the iTaukei Lands Commission;

**Constitution** means the Constitution of the Republic of Fiji;

**Council** means the Great Council of Chiefs;

**Minister** means the Minister responsible for iTaukei affairs; and

**Permanent Secretary** means the Permanent Secretary responsible for iTaukei affairs.

## reg 3 Membership

(1) The Council consists of the following members—

(a) 3 *ex-officio* members—

(i) the President;

(ii) the Prime Minister; and

(iii) the Minister;

(b) 6 members nominated by the Minister;

(c) 42 chiefs representing the 14 provinces with each province represented by 3 members nominated in accordance with regulation 4; and

(d) 3 members nominated by the Council of Rotuma.

(2) The Minister may, before a meeting of the Council, cause the names of members of the Council to be published in the Gazette.

## reg 4 Nomination of representatives from the provinces

(1) The nomination of members representing the provinces is to be carried out by the *Bose Vanua* and where applicable, the nomination of female members is to be encouraged.

(2) A nominee under subregulation (1) must be a chief who has been traditionally installed as *Turaga iTaukei*, *Marama iTaukei*, *Liuliu ni Yavusa* or *Liuliu ni Mataqali* and recorded as such in the Register of iTaukei Lands administered by the iTaukei Lands Commission, possesses exemplary leadership qualities and has served in his or her province.

(3) The nomination of members representing the provinces must be held at a date, time and place as may be determined by the chairperson of each provincial council.

## reg 5 Tenure

A member of the Council other than an *ex-officio* member holds office for 3 years unless earlier revoked or terminated.

## reg 6 Disqualification of members

A person is not eligible to be a member of the Council if he or she—

(a) is an undischarged bankrupt;

(b) is an in-mate or extra-mural prisoner;

(c) is a person of unsound mind; or

(d) is an office holder of a political party.

## reg 7 Chairperson

(1) The Chairperson of the Council must be elected by the Council from amongst its members and the Secretary of the Council must preside over the election of the Chairperson.

(2) The Chairperson of the Council holds office for a term of 3 years and is eligible for re-election.

## reg 8 Secretary of the Council

The Permanent Secretary is the Secretary of the Council.

## reg 9 Resignation of members

A member of the Council under regulation 3(1)(b), (c) or (d) may resign from his or her membership by notice in writing and such resignation takes effect on the receipt of the notice by the Secretary of the Council.

## reg 10 Meetings

The Council must meet at the time and place appointed by the Minister by notice in the Gazette.

## reg 11 Rules of procedure

The Council may make rules regulating its proceedings at any of its meetings and the mode of conducting its business.

## reg 12 Quorum

The quorum of the Council is 36.

## reg 13 Attendance by invitation

The Council may invite any person to attend a meeting, and such person may take part in the proceedings of the meeting but is not entitled to vote.

## reg 14 Duties of the Council

(1) The duties of the Council are to—

(a) deliberate, review, formulate and determine issues, policies and laws that relate to the rights, interests, health, welfare, order and governance of the iTaukei people as the Minister or iTaukei Affairs Board may refer to the Council;

(b) consider any proposal or report that relates to the matters as set out under paragraph (a);

(c) proactively advocate and have oversight on iTaukei interests and make decisions on iTaukei matters;

(d) maintain and strengthen advisory functions in nation building, promote racial harmony, champion social cohesion, and acceptance;

(e) advocate and set strategic directions on the following—

(i) good governance and leadership;

(ii) economic empowerment;

(iii) culture, tradition and heritage;

(iv) resource ownership;

(v) education;

(vi) health and well-being;

(vii) environment;

(viii) climate change; and

(ix) violence against women and children; and

(f) undertake any other duty or function imposed on it by written law.

(2) Before making any recommendation on any matter, the Council may appoint a committee consisting of a chairperson and members of the Council or co-opted members as the Council deems suitable, to consider the matter and report back to the Council.

## reg 15 Report of proceedings

The Secretary of the Council must submit a report of each meeting of the Council to the Minister as soon as practicable after the meeting.

## reg 16 Revocation

The iTaukei Affairs (Great Council of Chiefs) Regulations 2023 is revoked.
