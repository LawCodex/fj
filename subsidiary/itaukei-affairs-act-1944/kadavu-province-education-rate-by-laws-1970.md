# KADAVU PROVINCE (EDUCATION RATE) BY-LAWS 1970

Kadavu Province (Education Rate) By-laws 1970 (LGN 3 of 1971) commenced on 12 February 1971, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Fijian Affairs (Amendment) Decree 2010 (No 31 of 2010) | 2 July 2010 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Kadavu Province (Education Rate) By-laws 1970.

## by-law 2 Application

These By-laws shall apply only to iTaukei males between the ages of 21 and 60—

(a) who are members of a mataqali registered as a land owning unit in the province of Kadavu;

(b) who own or lease land within the province of Kadavu and request to be included;

(c) who live on iTaukei land in the province of Kadavu and request to be included.

[by-law 2 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 3, 2 July 2010]

## by-law 3 Registration

(1) Every male referred to in by-law 2(a) who has not attained the age of 21 years before 12 February 1971, shall, within 2 months after he reaches the age of 21, register himself at the Provincial Office or Central iTaukei Treasury as an education rate-payer of the Province.

[para (1) am Decree 31 of 2010 s 3, 2 July 2010]

(2) Any person over the age of 21 years referred to in by-law 2(a) and any person who requests to be a rate-payer as provided in paragraph (b) or (c) of such by-law shall register himself at the Provincial Office during the first 60 days of the year in respect of which the rate is levied.

## by-law 4 Education rate

(1) The Council shall, by resolution, levy an education rate of not more than $5 per annum which rate shall be paid by each person referred to in by-law 2 .

(2) Such resolution shall specify a date on or before which the education rate levied shall be paid.

(3) The education rate shall be calculated so as to cover all expenditure on education approved by the Council.

(4) As soon as possible after the Council has approved the rate at which the education rate is to be levied, details of such rate shall be published in Na Mata and by other means available to the Council.

(5) Such moneys as are paid by ratepayers under the provisions of these By-laws shall be deposited monthly into an account in the National Bank of Fiji to be called “the Kadavu Education Rate Account”.

## by-law 5 Interest

Any amount of education rate not paid within 30 days of the date upon which payment of such rate is due shall bear interest at the rate of 7.5% per annum.

## by-law 6 General

Moneys standing to credit of the account referred to in by-law 4(5) shall be withdrawn and paid out in accordance with the estimates on education approved by the Council, and separate accounts shall be kept in respect thereof.

## by-law 7 Responsibility for enforcement

The Roko or such persons as may be authorised by the Council in that behalf shall be responsible for the enforcement of these By-laws.

## by-law 8 Exemptions

The provisions of regulation 42 of the iTaukei Affairs (Provincial Councils) Regulations 1996 shall apply to the education rate as if such rate were a provincial rate.

[by-law 8 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 3, 2 July 2010]

## by-law 9 Recovery of education rate

The provisions of regulation 37 of the iTaukei Affairs (Provincial Councils) Regulations 1996 shall apply to the education rate as if such rate were a land rate.

[by-law 9 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 3, 2 July 2010]

## by-law 10 Service of notices etc

Any notice, demand or other document required by these By-laws to be served, shall be served in accordance with the provisions of regulation 44 of the iTaukei Affairs (Provincial Councils) Regulations 1996.

[by-law 10 am Decree 31 of 2010 s 3, 2 July 2010]
