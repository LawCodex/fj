## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Stock Improvement Regulations 1966.

## reg 2 Fees

The fees specified in Schedule 1 shall be charged in respect of the several matters specified in such Schedule.

## reg 3 Forms

The forms set out in Schedule 2 shall be the forms to be used for the purposes of these Regulations.
