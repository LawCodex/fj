## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Bovine Tuberculosis) Regulations 1965.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Assistant Director** [def rep LN 99 of 2016 reg 14, 1 December 2016]

**bovine animal** or animal means a bull, cow, ox, heifer or calf;

**cattle dealer** means any person who is engaged in the business of buying and selling bovine animals for slaughter or for any other purpose;

**eradication area** means the whole of Fiji;

[def subst LN 31 of 1991 reg 3, 1 June 1991]

**holding** means land held by a person under any form of tenure;

[def insrt LN 31 of 1991 reg 31 June 1991]

**infected area** means any holding declared by the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji to be infected with bovine tuberculosis (*Mycobacterium boris*) and includes any public roads or water course adjacent to the infected area as the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji may determine;

[def insrt LN 31 of 1991 reg 3, 1 June 1991am LN 99 of 2016 reg 14, 1 December 2016]

**inspector** means any veterinary officer, livestock officer, veterinary assistant, or any other person appointed by the Minister, in writing, to be an inspector;

**milk** means the normal sweet secretion obtained from the udder of a cow;

**owner** includes the manager or agent of an owner;

**reactor** means any bovine animal showing a positive reaction to a test;

**test** with its grammatical variations and cognate expressions means a tuberculin test for bovine tuberculosis; and

**whole milk supply herd** means a herd of bovine animals from which milk or cream is sold or supplied to the public.

## reg 3 Tuberculin testing

(1) Any veterinary officer may, by delivery of a notice in the form set out in Schedule 1 upon the owner of any herd of bovine animals or of any individual bovine animal, require that such owner shall submit his or her herd or individual animal, as the case may be, or any animals permitted to remain on the same premises on a date not less than 7 days after delivery of such notice and at such place as may be specified, for testing in the manner required by the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji.

[subreg (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 14, 1 December 2016]

(2) The Biosecurity Authority of Fiji shall arrange with the owners of all whole milk supply herds for every animal in such herds to be tested in the manner required by it, at least once in every 12 months.

[subreg (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 14, 1 December 2016]

## reg 4 Introduction of untested animals into herds

Whenever the owner of a whole milk supply herd wishes to introduce into his or her herd an animal which has not been tested he or she shall forthwith and, in any case, within 7 days, inform an inspector who shall arrange for an early test of such animal to take place and, until such test has been made, the owner of the herd shall keep such animal in reasonable isolation and shall not allow any milk from such animal to be sold or supplied to the public. Any owner of a whole milk supply herd failing to comply with the provisions of this regulation shall be guilty of an offence.

## reg 5 Owner to muster animals

When notified of a test, or when arrangements have been made for a test under the provisions of either of regulations 3 or 4 , the owner shall arrange for all his or her animals or such animal or animals as is or are required to be tested together with all other animals permitted to remain on the same premises as the inspector may require also to be tested to be mustered and shall have all such animals confined at the place at the time directed. The owner or his or her agent shall give all reasonable assistance to the inspector to enable the testing to be carried out.

## reg 6 Marking of tested animals

(1) All animals which have been tested shall be identified by means of serially numbered tags inserted in the right ear of such animals and a record of tested animals and the serial numbers allotted to such animals shall be kept by the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji.

[subreg (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 14, 1 December 2016]

(2) All reactors shall be identified by means of a broad arrow punch in the lower edge of the right ear of each reactor and a record of such reactors shall be kept by the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji.

[subreg (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 14, 1 December 2016]

(3) Any person, other than an inspector carrying out his duties under the provisions of this regulation defacing in any way the right ear of any tested animal shall be guilty of an offence.

## reg 7 Disposal of reactors

(1) The disposal of any reactor shall be at the discretion of a Veterinary Officer who shall direct the means and place of isolation and the time of final destruction of such reactor.

(2) No reactor may be removed from any premises for any purpose whatsoever except upon the written order of an inspector given in the form set out in Schedule 2 .

## reg 8 Eradication area

(1) The Biosecurity Authority of Fiji shall arrange for the testing in an eradication area of all bovine animals and for the disposal of all reactors within such area.

[subreg (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 14, 1 December 2016]

(2) The Biosecurity Authority of Fiji may also arrange for the testing, within an eradiction area and in a manner approved by it, of goats and the disposal of reactors in the same manner as bovine reactors.

[subreg (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 14, 1 December 2016]

(3) All bovine animals within an eradication area which, in the opinion of the inspector, are too wild to be mustered for testing may be disposed of or destroyed in the manner directed by the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji.

[subreg (3) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 14, 1 December 2016]

[reg 8 subst LN 105 of 1979 reg 3, 11 May 1979]

## reg 9 Movement of cattle in eradication areas

(1) Without the express written authority of an inspector given in the form set out in Schedule 3, no untested animal may be brought into an eradication area.

(2) Where permission has been granted in accordance with the provisions of subregulation (1) , any untested animal shall be taken to the destination specified on the form set out in Schedule 3 and shall not be moved from such destination without the prior permission of an inspector.

(3) The provisions of subregulations (1) and (2) shall not apply—

(a) to any animal or animals in the course of transit through an eradication area on defined roads or tracks with all possible despatch in which case all reasonable steps shall be taken to ensure that there is no contact between such animal or animals and tested animals within the eradication area;

(b) to the animals of a cattle dealer or the owner of a registered slaughterhouse if the premises of such dealer or owner are adequately fenced and maintained to the satisfaction of the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji so as to isolate all animals therein from contact with tested animals, provided that where the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji is of the opinion that it is desirable to erect additional or double fencing around such premises, it may so direct and the dealer or owner shall carry out such directions.

[subreg (3) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 14, 1 December 2016]

## reg 10 Offences

(1) Any person being the owner of a bovine animal who—

(a) refuses or neglects to muster or confine all animals as required by the provisions of regulation 5 ;

(b) fails to give all reasonable assistance to an inspector to enable testing to be carried out;

(c) removes a reactor from its directed place of isolation otherwise than in accordance with the written order of an inspector;

(d) save as provided under the provisions of regulation 9(3) , introduces any bovine animal into an eradication area without the prior permission of an inspector;

(e) save as provided under the provisions of regulation 9(3) , removes any untested bovine animal from an eradication area without the prior permission of an inspector;

(f) in an eradication area, permits any untested bovine animal to enter any enclosure in which there are tested animals;

(g) fails to comply with any order of an inspector for the movement of any reactor,

shall be guilty of an offence.

[subreg (1) am LN 85 of 1969 reg 2, 30 May 1969]

(2) Any person being the owner of a whole milk supply herd who—

(a) fails to inform an inspector within 7 days of the entry into his or her herd of any untested bovine animal;

(b) permits milk or cream from untested bovine animals to be sold or supplied to the public,

shall be guilty of an offence.
