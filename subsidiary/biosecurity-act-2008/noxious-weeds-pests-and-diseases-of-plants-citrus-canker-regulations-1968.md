# NOXIOUS WEEDS, PESTS AND DISEASES OF PLANTS (CITRUS CANKER) REGULATIONS 1968

Noxious Weeds, Pests and Diseases of Plants (Citrus Canker) Regulations 1968 (LN 61 of 1968) commenced on 17 May 1968 as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Noxious Weeds, Pests and Diseases of Plants (Citrus Canker) Regulations 1968.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations—

**citrus canker** means the disease known as *xanthomonas citri;*

**citrus plant and citrus fruit** mean citrus plant and citrus fruit grown or produced in Fiji;

**quarantine area** means the area specified in the Schedule; and

**vessel** includes aircraft and **master of a vessel** includes the commander of an aircraft or, if there is no commander, the pilot thereof.

## reg 3 Quarantine area for citrus

No person shall, except with the permission in writing of the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji or an inspector, bring or attempt to bring into the quarantine area any citrus plant or citrus fruit from any other part of Fiji.

[reg 3 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 185, 1 December 2016]

## reg 4 Inspectors’ powers

(1) An inspector, with such assistants as he or she may require, shall be entitled to board, at any place—

(a) any vessel voyaging or intending to voyage to the quarantine area from any other part of Fiji; or

(b) any such vessel arriving at any place in the quarantine area or in the territorial waters surrounding such area,

in order to ascertain if any citrus plant or citrus fruit is being carried in such vessel.

(2) The master of any such vessel shall give the inspector all reasonable facilities for the proper exercise of his or her powers under the provisions of this regulation.

(3) Any citrus plant or citrus fruit found on any such vessel may be seized by the inspector and destroyed or disposed of in such other manner as the inspector may consider appropriate.

(4) No compensation shall be payable in respect of any citrus plant or citrus fruit destroyed or disposed of under the provisions of this regulation.

## reg 5 Offences

Any person who contravenes the provisions of regulation 3 and the master of any vessel who, without reasonable cause, fails to comply with the provisions of regulation 4(2) is guilty of an offence.

### SCHEDULE QUARANTINE AREA

Rotuma and the islands of Vanua Levu, Taveuni, Yadua, Yaqaqa, Galoa, Cikobia, Kia, Mali, Macuata-i-wai, Druadrua, Toto, Rabi, Kioa, Yanuca, Yavu, Cobia, Matagi, Laucala, Qamea, Maqewa and Qelelevu.
