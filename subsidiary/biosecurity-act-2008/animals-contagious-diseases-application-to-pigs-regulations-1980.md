# ANIMALS (CONTAGIOUS DISEASES) (APPLICATION TO PIGS) REGULATIONS 1980

Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Application to Pigs) Regulations 1980 (LN 8 of 1980) commenced on 4 January 1980, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Application to Pigs) Regulations 1980.

## reg 2 Application of Act to pigs

All sections and provisions of the Biosecurity Act 2008 in so far as they can be made to do so shall apply to pigs.
