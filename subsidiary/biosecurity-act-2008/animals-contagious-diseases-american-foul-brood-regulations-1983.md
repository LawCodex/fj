# ANIMALS (CONTAGIOUS DISEASES) (AMERICAN FOUL BROOD) REGULATIONS 1983

Animals (Contagious Diseases) (American Foul Brood) Regulations 1983 (LN 101 of 1983) commenced on 2 December 1983, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals (Contagious Diseases) (American Foul Brood) Regulations 1983.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, “bee” means any living stage of the honey bee.

## reg 3 Infected areas

The areas specified in the Schedule are declared to be areas infected with the contagious disease known as American Foul Brood *(bacillus larvae).*

## reg 4 Restriction on movement

The movement of bees, beehives and beekeeping equipment into, through or out of an infected area is prohibited.

## reg 5 Penalty

A person who contravenes regulation 4 shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200.

## reg 6 Repeal

The Animals (Contagious Diseases) (American Foul Brood) Regulations 1980 is repealed.

### SCHEDULE INFECTED AREAS

The tikina of Rewa, tikina of Suva, tikina of Naitasiri and tikina of Nadi.

The island of Ovalau.
