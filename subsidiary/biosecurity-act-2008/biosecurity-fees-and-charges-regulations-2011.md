# BIOSECURITY (FEES AND CHARGES) REGULATIONS 2011

Biosecurity (Fees and Charges) Regulations 2011 (LN 57 of 2011) commenced on 1 July 2011, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Biosecurity (Fees and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 2015 (LN 76 of 2015) | 20 October 2011 |
| Biosecurity (Fees and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 2021 (LN 68 of 2021) | 1 August 2021 |

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Biosecurity (Fees and Charges) Regulations 2011 and comes into force on 1 July 2011.

## reg 2 Fees and charges

The fees and charges of the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji are listed in the table in the Schedule.

## reg 3 Repeal

All regulations and legal instruments relating to any fees and charges that were enacted in the Animals Importation Act (Cap. 159), the Animals (Contagious Diseases) Act (Cap. 160) and the Plant Quarantine Act (Cap. 156) are now repealed.
