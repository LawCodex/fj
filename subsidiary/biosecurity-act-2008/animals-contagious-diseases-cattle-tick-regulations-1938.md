# ANIMALS (CONTAGIOUS DISEASES) (CATTLE TICK) REGULATIONS 1938

Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Cattle Tick) Regulations 1938 (FRG No 4 of 1938) commenced on 19 January 1938, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Cattle Tick) Regulations 1938.

## reg 2 Restriction on movement of cattle etc

No cattle or other animals may be shipped from the island of Viti Levu to other islands within Fiji unless they have been inspected, treated to the satisfaction of an officer of the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji and found free from ticks.
