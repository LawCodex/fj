# ANIMALS (CONTAGIOUS DISEASES) (BOVINE BRUCELLOSIS) REGULATIONS 1984

Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Bovine Brucellosis) Regulations 1984 (LN 113 of 1984) commenced on 21 December 1984, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Bovine Brucellosis) (Amendment) Regulations 1991 (LN 30 of 1991) | 1 June 1991 |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Bovine Brucellosis) Regulations 1984.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Assistant Director** [def rep LN 99 of 2016 reg 13, 1 December 2016]

**bovine animal** or animal means a bull, cow, heifer, ox, steer or calf;

**bovine brucellosis** means the infection suffered by bovine animals caused by bacterium of the genus Brucella;

**eradication area** means the whole of Fiji;

[def insrt LN 30 of 1991 reg 3, 1 June 1991]

**holding** means land held by a person under any form of tenure;

[def insrt LN 30 of 1991 reg 3, 1 June 1991]

**infected area** means any holding declared by the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji to be infected with brucellosis (Brucella abortys) and includes any public roads or water course adjacent to the infected area as the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji may determine;

[def insrt LN 30 of 1991 reg 3, 1 June 1991am LN 99 of 2016 reg 13, 1 December 2016]

**reactor** means a bovine animal which, being affected by bovine brucellosis, shows a positive reaction to a test; and

**test** means a test, of a type approved for the purpose by the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji, carried out on the milk or blood of a bovine animal for the purpose of diagnosing brucellosis and includes a retest.

[def am LN 99 of 2016 reg 13, 1 December 2016]

## reg 3 Testing of animals

The Biosecurity Authority of Fiji shall arrange for all bovine animals which it considers to be susceptible to bovine brucellosis to be tested and, at such intervals as it may determine, retested.

[reg 3 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 13, 1 December 2016]

## reg 4 Destruction etc of animals not capable of being mustered

Where, in the opinion of an inspector, a bovine animal is too wild to be mustered for testing the owner of the animal shall destroy or otherwise dispose of the animal in such manner as the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji directs.

[reg 4 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 13, 1 December 2016]

## reg 5 Notice to be served

(1) An inspector may require the owner of a bovine animal to make the animal available to be tested by the inspector.

(2) A request under subregulation (1) shall—

(a) be in writing;

(b) state the time, date and place where the animal is to be tested; and

(c) be served on the owner of the animal.

(3) The owner of an animal requested to make an animal available for testing in accordance with this regulation shall not fail to muster that animal and make it available for testing at the time, date and place specified in the request, and to give all reasonable assistance to the inspector to enable the test to be carried out.

## reg 6 Tested animals to be tagged

(1) Where an animal has been tested an inspector shall insert a numbered tag in the right ear of the animal.

(2) The Biosecurity Authority of Fiji shall keep a record of the number appearing on a tag inserted in accordance with subregulation (1) .

[subreg (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 13, 1 December 2016]

(3) A tag inserted in accordance with subregulation (1) shall not be removed except by an inspector acting in the course of his or her duties.

## reg 7 Destruction etc of reactors

(1) Where a test establishes an animal to be a reactor the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji may, in addition to exercising its powers under subsection 7(4) of the Act, if the animal forms part of a herd—

(a) order that all or any of the remaining animals in the herd be vaccinated with a vaccine—

(i) of a type; and

(ii) in a manner, approved by it; and

(b) prohibit the sale of removal of all or any of the remaining animals in the herd for such period as it specifies.

[subreg (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 13, 1 December 2016]

(2) Subregulation (1)(b) shall not apply in respect of—

(a) a steer or ox; or

(b) an animal which the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji is satisfied is to be removed for immediate slaughter.

[subreg (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 13, 1 December 2016]

## reg 8 Reactors to be identified

(1) Where an animal has been tested and has proved to be a reactor an inspector shall identify the animal by means of a punch in the form of an R in the lower edge of its left ear.

(2) The Biosecurity Authority of Fiji shall keep a record of all reactors.

[subreg (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 13, 1 December 2016]

## reg 9 Use of certain vaccine restricted

(1) Subject to subregulation (2) , a person shall not vaccinate a bovine animal against brucellosis using the vaccine known as Strain 19.

(2) Subregulation (1) shall not apply where the vaccine is used—

(a) with the written permission of the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji and under the supervision of an inspector; or

(b) on a heifer calf which is over the age of 3 months but under the age of 6 months.

[subreg (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 13, 1 December 2016]

## reg 10 Vaccinated animals to be identified

Where an animal has been vaccinated in accordance with regulation 7(1)(a) or subregulation 9(2) an inspector shall identify the animal by means of a diamond shaped punch in the centre of the left ear of the animal.
