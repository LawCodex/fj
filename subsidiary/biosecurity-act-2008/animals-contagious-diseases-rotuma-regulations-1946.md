# ANIMALS (CONTAGIOUS DISEASES) (ROTUMA) REGULATIONS 1946

Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Rotuma) Regulations 1946 (LN 84 of 1946) commenced on 26 April 1946, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Rotuma) Regulations 1946.

## reg 2 Restriction on entry

No cattle or pigs shall be permitted entry into the island of Rotuma without first having been, within 14 days of shipment, subjected to the tuberculin test by an inspector and found to give a negative reaction.
