# ANIMALS (CONTAGIOUS DISEASES) (RINGWORM OF HORSES) REGULATIONS 1945

Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Ringworm of Horses) Regulations 1945 commenced on 9 March 1945, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Ringworm of Horses) (Amendment) Regulations 1946 (LN 221 of 1946) | 13 December 1946 |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Ringworm of Horses) Regulations 1945.

## reg 2 Infected area

The area defined in the Schedule (hereinafter called the “infected area”) is hereby declared to be an area infected with the contagious disease known as ringworm of horses (infection with the fungus *Trychophton*).

## reg 3 Restriction on movement of animals

All horses, cattle and other animals in the infected area on 9 March 1945 and all horses, cattle and other animals which shall come into such area after that date shall not be permitted to leave such area during the continuance of these Regulations except with the written consent of the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji or an inspector.

[reg 3 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 16, 1 December 2016]

## reg 4 Mustering of animals for inspection and treatment

Any horses, cattle and other animals within the infected area shall be mustered in such areas at such times and places and under such conditions for any purpose required by or necessary for inspection or treatment in respect of such contagious disease as the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji or an inspector may direct.

[reg 4 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 16, 1 December 2016]

## reg 5 Treatment

All horses, cattle and other animals within the infected area shall be subject to such treatment in respect of ringworm of horses as the Biosecurity Authority of Fiji or an inspector shall direct.

[reg 5 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 16, 1 December 2016]
