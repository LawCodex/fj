# ANIMALS (CONTAGIOUS DISEASES) (APPLICATION TO DOMESTICATED POULTRY) REGULATIONS 1980

Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Application to Domesticated Poultry) Regulations 1980 (LN 4 of 1980) commenced on 4 January 1980, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Application to Domesticated Poultry) Regulations 1980.

## reg 2 Application of Act to domesticated poultry

All sections and provisions of the Biosecurity Act 2008 in so far as they can be made to do so shall apply to domesticated poultry.

[reg 2 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 7, 1 December 2016]

## reg 3 Revocation

The Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Application to Domestic Fowls) Regulation, published at page 5533 of Volume 9 of the Laws of Fiji (1967 and 1978 Revised Editions), are revoked.
