# ANIMALS (CONTAGIOUS DISEASES) (APPLICATION TO BOVINE ANIMALS) REGULATIONS 1925

Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Application to Bovine Animals) Regulations 1925 (FRG No 57 of 1925) commenced on 6 November 1925, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals (Contagious Diseases) (Application to Bovine Animals) Regulations 1925.

## reg 2 Application of Act to bovine animals

All sections and provisions of the Biosecurity Act 2008 in so far as they can be made to do so shall apply to bovine animals.

[reg 2 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 4, 1 December 2016]

## reg 3 Land to be fenced

The occupiers of infected areas and of adjoining lands shall be required to fence and maintain the fencing of their land to the satisfaction of an inspector under the Act.
