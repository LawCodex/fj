## reg 1 Short title

This Notice may be cited as the Birds and Game Protection (Killing or Taking of Pigeons) Notice 2012.

## reg 2 Maximum number of pigeons

The maximum number of pigeons which may be taken or killed by any person holding a licence to kill or take game under section 9(1) of the Act in any one day not included in the close season specified in Schedule 3 of the Act is 5.
