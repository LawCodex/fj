# BIRDS AND GAME PROTECTION (LICENCE FEE) REGULATIONS 1974

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Birds and Game Protection (Licence Fee) Regulations 1974.

## reg 2 Licence fee

The licence fee for the killing of game shall be $2 per annum.

## reg 3 Revocation

The licence fee previously prescribed and published at page 5560 in Volume 9 of the Laws of Fiji (1967 Revised Edition) is hereby revoked.
