## r 1 Short title

These Rules shall be cited as the Fiji Mahogany Trust Rules 2005.

## r 2 Interpretation

In these Rules, unless the context otherwise requires—

**beneficiaries** mean all the members of every land-owning unit of iTaukei land and the government in respect of state land leased to the company for mahogany plantation forests throughout Fiji, and include their representatives whose names are registered in the Register of Units maintained by the Trust secretariat;

[def am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, effective 1 March 2011]

**Board of Trustees** means the Board of Trustees as constituted under rule 5;

**company** means the Fiji Hardwood Corporation Limited or its successors and assigns;

**directors** means directors of the company appointed by the Board of Trustees under rule 32;

**forest based company** means a company owned by a forest based trust;

**forest based trust** means a trust owned by the landowners in any forest area or by the extension forest owners;

**forests** means mahogany or other planted species of hardwood forests owned or managed by the company;

**Government units** means units held by the Government in accordance with the Register;

**landowners** means owners and associations of owners of iTaukei land leased to the company or to any forest based trust for mahogany plantation forests;

[def am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, effective 1 March 2011]

**landowner units** means units held by the landowners through their representatives and registered in the Register of Units;

**landowner unitholders** mean the landowners holding landowner units as registered in the Register of Units;

**Minister** means the Minister responsible for the administration of the Act;

[def am LN 99 of 2016 reg 74, effective 1 December 2016]

**Register** means the Register maintained by the Board of Trustees under and in accordance with rule 14;

**special resolution** means a resolution that is required to be and has been passed by a majority of not less than 75% of the votes of the beneficiaries;

**station** means such areas of land leased to and recognised by the company and the Trust as mahogany plantation forests with their respective administrative centres;

**termination day** means the day as determined by the beneficiaries pursuant to rule 35;

**Trust** means the Fiji Mahogany Trust;

**Trust Property** means all the property and assets, whether real or personal, held by or under the control of the Trust on behalf and for the benefit of the beneficiaries, including shares of whatever class in any company;

**Trust Rules** means the rules of this Trust called the Fiji Mahogany Trust and include any amendments thereto;

**trustees** mean trustees who are elected, appointed or substituted by the beneficiaries in the manner provided in these Trust Rules; and

**unit** means an entitlement under the Trust as recorded in the Register of Units.

## r 3 Non application of the Trustee Act 1966

The provisions of the Trustee Act 1966 shall, in accordance with section 6 of the Fiji Mahogany Act 2003, not apply to these Trust Rules.

## r 4 Legal status and objects of the Trust

The purposes and objects of the Trust, in addition to any other contained in the provisions of these Trust Rules and the Fiji Mahogany Act 2003, shall be as follows—

(a) the Trust shall be a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal and may in such name sue and be sued, borrow money, and enter into contracts and may acquire, purchase, take, hold and enjoy real and personal property of whatsoever kind and description and may convey, assign, surrender and yield up, charge, mortgage, transfer or otherwise dispose of or deal with in any real or personal property vested in the Trust on such terms and conditions as the Board of Trustees shall think fit;

(b) to provide the opportunity and basis on which to assist the beneficiaries in promoting, co-ordinating and furthering their interests as shareholders in the company and as resource owners in relation to mahogany plantation forests;

(c) to provide assistance to the beneficiaries in the proper attainment of their development aspirations through advisory, financial, managerial, marketing, training and any other form of assistance connected with the establishment of additional mahogany forests and the planting, growing, maintenance, logging, transportation and end use production and ancillary industries associated with mahogany or other hardwood forests and their respective forest products;

(d) to hold in trust for the beneficiaries all the property and assets, whether real or personal property of whatsoever kind, including funds and shares vested in the Trust, with power to borrow money and enter into contracts with individual beneficiaries and third parties in respect thereto, and with power to convey, assign, surrender, yield up, charge, mortgage, transfer or otherwise dispose of or deal with such assets and property on such terms and conditions as the Board of Trustees shall think fit;

(e) to hold all benefits and income of the Trust intended for distribution among the beneficiaries and to distribute such benefits and income in accordance with these Trust Rules.

## r 5 Board of Trustees

(1) There shall be for the purpose of the Trust a Board of Trustees which shall consist of—

(a) one trustee to be elected by the beneficiaries in each Station;

(b) one trustee to be appointed by the Minister at his or her discretion to represent all State land that is leased to the company for the cultivation of mahogany.

(2) Unless earlier removed or replaced in accordance with these Rules, every trustee shall hold office for 3 years and shall be eligible for reappointment, provided that no trustee shall serve for more than 2 consecutive terms, but a trustee shall be eligible for appointment after 3 years from the end of his or her last term.

(3) The trustees shall be entitled to allowances and disbursements incurred by them in connection with the performance of their official duties as such trustees at rates approved by the Board of Trustees.

(4) A person serving as a trustee of this Trust shall not at the same time serve as a director of the Fiji Hardwood Corporation Limited.

(5) A person serving as a trustee of this Trust may also serve as a trustee in a forest based trust.

(6) A person serving as a trustee of this Trust may also be a director of a forest based company.

(7) A person shall be qualified for election or appointment as a trustee if—

(a) in respect of trustees elected under paragraph (1)(a) and (b), he or she is a landowner within the Station from which he or she is elected;

(b) he or she is a natural person;

(c) he or she is 18 years of age or over;

(d) he or she is not an undischarged bankrupt;

(e) he or she is not a member of the senior management staff of the company save for any trustee appointed under this paragraph;

(f) he or she is of sound mind;

(g) he or she has not been convicted of a criminal offence involving dishonesty committed within the last 7 years from the date he or she seeks election as such trustee, or he or she has completed serving his or her last term of imprisonment for such offence 7 or more years from the date of his or her seeking election as such trustee;

(h) he or she has not been previously removed as a trustee or director in accordance with these Trust Rules;

[para (7) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 74, effective 1 December 2016]

(8) The term of office of a trustee elected or appointed under these Trust Rules shall terminate on the occurrence of one or more of the following events, that is to say that the trustee—

(a) is dead;

(b) is a declared bankrupt by Order of a court;

(c) has resigned;

(d) fails to attend 3 successive meetings of the Board of Trustees without any reason acceptable to the Board of Trustees;

(e) is insane;

(f) refuses or is medically unfit to perform the duties of a trustee or is incapable for any reason acceptable to the Board of Trustees of performing such duties;

(g) was convicted within 7 years from the date his or her seeking election as a trustee of a criminal offence which involves dishonesty, or that he or she completed serving a sentence for such offence within 7 years from the date he or she seeks election as such trustee.

(9) Any trustee whose term has terminated prematurely on any of the grounds specified in paragraph (4) herein may be replaced soon thereafter by the beneficiaries who had elected or appointed him or her, and such replacement trustee shall, unless re-appointed, hold office only for the balance of the term of the outgoing trustee.

## r 6 Powers of the Board of Trustees

The Board of Trustees, in addition to other powers conferred upon them under these Trust Rules, are empowered to do all or any of the following—

(a) to pay out of the Trust or the income thereof all costs, charges and expenses incidental to the management of the Trust or to the exercise of any power authority or discretion herein contained or in carrying out or performing the trusts hereof which the Board of Trustees may at any time incur including all income tax or other taxes payable in respect of the Trust and all costs in any way connected with the preparation and execution of these presents and all moneys which the Board of Trustees may be required to pay as settlement, probate estate gift stamp or revenue duties, including stamp, gift or settlement duties payable in respect of the Trust or in these present;

(b) to open any account or accounts with any Bank or Banks as the Board of Trustees shall approve and to operate by and in all usual ways any such account or accounts;

(c) to give effectual receipts and discharges for any moneys received by or on behalf of the Trust or otherwise relating to any of the acts, matters and things provided for in these presents and to receive property by gift inter vivos or by will or under the provisions of any other trust or trusts or otherwise either from any person or persons as additions to the Trust and to hold the same upon the trust herein set forth and to administer such additions under the provision hereof;

(d) to appoint and to determine conditions of appointment of a Secretary and other staff for the proper management of the trust and to delegate to them duties without extinguishing the trustees’ own responsibilities;

(e) to take such actions as the Board of Trustees shall think fit for the proper and adequate protection of the Trust, its property or any part thereof;

(f) when necessary, to take and act upon a written opinion from an advisor or consultant, including legal and financial advisors, in relation to the interpretation or effect of these presents or any matter arising therefrom and in relation to other documents or statutes relating to the administration of the Trust;

(g) to co-opt trustees to attend meetings of the Board of Trustees, but such co-opted trustees shall not have the right to vote at such meetings.

## r 7 Duties of the Board of Trustees

(1) The Board of Trustees in addition to any other duties conferred upon them shall in the manner hereinafter provided—

(a) manage the Trust;

(b) appoint 2 directors to the board of directors of the company in accordance with rule 32 of these Trust Rules and the said directors shall not themselves be trustees under this Trust or to appoint directors that the trust may be entitled to appoint in any other company;

(c) distribute to the beneficiaries such income of the Trust in the manner hereinafter provided; and,

(d) hold meetings of the Board of Trustees for the proper and timely despatch of any business of the Trust, including any questions or issues which the trustees may think fit, and to adjourn or otherwise conduct their meetings and those meetings of beneficiaries in the manner hereinafter provided.

(2) The Board of Trustees shall cause minutes of all their meetings to be kept and maintained and shall make minutes available to any trustee who may request an inspection of the same.

(3) Each trustee shall, except for ill health or bereavement or due to such circumstances beyond his or her control, personally attend every meeting convened by the Board of Trustees in accordance with these Trust Rules and no trustee may attend or vote at these meetings by proxy, agent or attorney.

## r 8 Limitation of liability of trustees

(1) No trustee shall be liable for any loss or damage occasioned by the exercise of any discretion or power hereby or by law conferred on the trustee or by failure to exercise any such discretion or power.

(2) No trustee shall be liable for any breach of duty of trust whatsoever unless it is proved to have been committed, made or omitted by the trustee either fraudulently or in bad faith.

(3) All persons claiming any beneficial interest in or upon any property subject to this Trust shall be deemed to take notice of and subject to the protection hereby conferred on the trustees.

## r 9 Accounts and audit

(1) The Board of Trustees shall cause proper books of accounts to be kept with respect to—

(i) all sums of moneys received and expended by the Trust and the matters in respect of which the receipts and expenditures relate; and,

(ii) the assets and liabilities of the Trust.

(2) The books of accounts shall be kept at such place as the Board of Trustees think fit and shall always be open to the inspection of the trustees.

(3) The Board of Trustees shall for each financial year cause to be prepared income and expenditure accounts and/or profit and loss accounts (hereinafter referred to as the “income statements”) and balance sheets and report by the Board of Trustees with respect to the Trust’s affairs and the amounts, which they recommend to be distributed.

(4) The accounts, which are to be laid as aforesaid at the Meeting of the Board of Trustees, shall contain all relevant particulars and such other particulars as may be required by the definition of financial statements under the Companies Act 2015 and shall be prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.

[para (4) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 74, effective 1 December 2016]

(5) Every balance sheet and report referred to above shall be signed by at least 2 trustees and the auditor’s report shall be attached to the balance sheet and income statements and the report shall be read at the Board of Trustees Meeting and shall be open to inspection by any trustee.

## r 10 The Trust Property

(1) All property of the Trust shall henceforth remain in the Trust’s possession and all dividends, interests and other income that have accrued or are to accrue therefrom shall be dealt with by the Trust in such manner as provided under these Rules, provided however that the property of the Trust shall not be capable of being distributed, paid or transferred either in whole or in part thereof to or for the beneficiary until the vesting day hereinafter provided.

(2) The income of the Trust shall include all dividends, interest and other income derived from the property of the Trust after deducting costs and other expenditure incurred by the Board of Trustees.

## r 11 Income distribution

(1) The Board of Trustees in each accounting period shall, until the vesting day, pay the whole of the net income of the Trust for such accounting period to the beneficiaries in proportion to the number of units registered in their respective names for the benefit of the landowners they each represent as at the end of such accounting period, provided however that if the income shall consist of distribution in kind such as shares or other property (personal or real) the Board of Trustees shall hold and deal with the same in the like manner as if it is the property of the Trust. The income therefrom shall only be capable of distribution in the like manner.

(2) The Board of Trustees shall be entitled to make an interim distribution of income at such time or times as the Board of Trustees shall determine during each accounting period.

(3) The distribution of income hereunder to any category of beneficiaries shall be limited from time to time as not to exceed a certain percentage of the income available to be distributed to all beneficiaries as shall be decided by the Board of Trustees.

## r 12 Limitation of liability of unitholders

(1) Nothing in these Trust Rules shall constitute or shall be deemed to constitute the relationship of principal and agent between the trustees and the unitholders nor the relationship of partners as between the trustees and the unitholders or as among the unitholders inter se. All income payable to the unitholders in accordance with the provisions of these Trust Rules shall be payable to them separately and income received by the Board of Trustees shall not be received or be construed as received by or on behalf of the unitholders jointly or severally.

(2) Notwithstanding any other provision of these Trust Rules or any provision deemed to be included herein, no unitholder shall, by reason thereof, or by reason of the relationship thereby created with the Board of Trustees, be under any obligation personally to indemnify the Board of Trustees or any creditor of the Board of Trustees in the event of there being any deficiency of the assets and/or property of the Trust as compared with the liabilities of the Board of Trustees in relation thereto, and only valid rights, if any, of indemnity of the Board of Trustees or of such creditor shall be allowed recourse to the assets of the property of the Trust.

## r 13 Beneficial interest of unitholders

(1) The beneficial interest in the Trust as originally constituted and as existing, from time to time shall be held by the beneficiaries for the time being in proportion to the units registered in their respective names.

(2) The units hereunder shall be issued in the following manner—

(a) to the landowners (which for this purpose shall not include the Government) on the basis of one unit for each hectare of land leased by them to the company or their forest based trust and these shall be referred to as the “landowner units”;

(b) to the Government on the basis of one unit for each hectare of State land leased to the company or to the forest based trust for the forest area within which such State land is situated and which shall be referred to as “Government units”,

unless otherwise specifically provided herein each unit shall have equal rights.

(3) Each unit shall entitle the registered holder hereof equally with the registered holders of all other units to the beneficial interest in the Trust as an entity but subject thereto shall not entitle the unitholder to any particular security or investment comprised in the property of the Trust or any part thereof and (save as hereinafter expressly provided) no unitholder shall be entitled to transfer to himself or to herself or any one else any property comprised in the unit or other property of the Trust.

(4) In his or her dealing with the Trust or the Trust Property or any part thereof a beneficiary shall not (except as expressly herein provided) be entitled to interfere with the rights or powers of the Board of Trustees nor can the beneficiary exercise any rights, powers or privileges in respect of the Trust, the Trust Property or any part thereof.

(5) The Trust shall, when necessary under paragraph (2) of this rule, issue additional units to a beneficiary.

## r 14 Register of beneficiaries

(1) The Board of Trustees shall cause to be kept and maintained an up-to-date Register of beneficiaries which shall be subdivided in such manner as to identify properly the categories of beneficiaries set out in rule 13, and the Register shall contain the following particulars—

(a) the name of Tokatoka or Mataqali, Yavusa and location (Village, District and Province) of the landowning, and the name and address of its duly appointed representative;

(b) the number of units registered under each beneficiary, and the distinctive numbers or letters of the certificates held by them respectively;

(c) the date on which the name of every beneficiary representative was entered in the Register in respect of units standing in his or her name;

(d) the date on which the beneficiaries representative ceases to be such and the name of his or her replacement; and,

(e) any other details considered by the Board of Trustees to be necessary.

(2) Any change of name or address of any beneficiary representative shall be notified to the secretary who shall alter the Register accordingly.

(3) Except as provided in this paragraph no notice of any trust express implied or constructive shall be entered in the Register and the person from time to time entered in the register as the beneficiary representative shall be the only person recognised by the Board of Trustees as entitled to the units registered in his or her name or to exercise the rights and privileges of the registered holder thereof pursuant to these Trust Rules.

(4) No person shall be recognised by the Board of Trustees as holding any unit upon any trust and the Board of Trustees shall not be bound by or be compelled in any way to recognise (even when having notice thereof) any equitable contingent future or partial interest in any unit or any interest therein or (except only as by the provisions of these Rules as otherwise provided) any other rights in respect of any unit except an absolute right in the unitholder thereof.

(5) Units held by a trustee in respect of a particular trust may be marked in the register in such a way as to identify them as being held in respect of that trust but no liability shall be created by any such marking and the Board of Trustees shall not be affected with notice of a trust recorded as aforesaid.

(6) The Board of Trustees shall not recognise or be bound by any assignments, lien or charges given by any unitholder in respect of the income or any part thereof from the units they hold.

## r 15 Certificates

(1) Every beneficiary representative whose name is entered in the Register shall be entitled to be issued with a certificate in such form as the Board of Trustees may from time to time prescribe but in respect of units held jointly the Board of Trustees shall not be bound to issue more than one certificate and delivery of a certificate to one of several joint beneficiaries shall be sufficient delivery to all such beneficiaries.

(2) Each such certificate shall be signed for or on behalf of the Board of Trustees by the secretary of the Board and shall specify the name of the beneficiary and the number of units to which it relates and shall bear a distinctive number or letter.

(3) The appearance of the name of a beneficiary on the Register shall be evidence that he or she is a correct beneficiary and shall be final evidence in case of dispute.

(4) Whenever units are cancelled under the provisions hereof, the certificate with respect to such units (or such evidence as to its loss or destruction as the Board of Trustees may require) shall be delivered up to the Board of Trustees.

## r 16 Cancellation of units

In respect of landowner units and Government units, such issued units shall be cancelled when the leases of land in relation to which the said units were issued have been cancelled, or terminated for whatever reason or lapsed or expired and not renewed or disposed of in any way whatsoever by the company, other than by way of a transfer of the lease to a forest based trust.

## r 17 Restriction on alienation of units

(1) No units shall be alienated or be capable of alienation whether by way of sale, transfer or assignment or as a result of the lunacy, liquidation or bankruptcy of any beneficiary unless, in the case of landowner unitholders, such alienation will vest the unit in accordance with an entity recognised by customary rules recognised by the iTaukei Lands Commission.

[para (1) am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, effective 1 March 2011]

(2) No units or any part thereof shall be capable of being charged or mortgaged to any person except with the written approval of the Board of Trustees.

## r 18 Meetings of the Board of Trustees

(1) The meetings of the Board of Trustees shall be held whenever convened by the chairperson on his or her own initiative or upon his or her approval through a written request to the secretary by not less than 5 trustees from different forest stations.

(2) The Board of Trustees may determine and regulate its own procedures at its meetings.

(3) The quorum at any meeting of the Board of Trustees shall consist of not less than 50% of all the members of the Board of Trustees with not less than 50% members from Viti Levu and Vanua Levu present.

(4) The members of the Board of Trustees shall appoint from amongst themselves a chairperson who, unless earlier removed by the Board by a resolution by secret ballot of not less than 50% of all trustees, shall hold office for the remaining term of his or her appointment as a trustee.

(5) The Board of Trustees may summon to any of its meetings all or any of the directors appointed by the Board of Trustees in the company or any other company in which the Trust has equity for the purpose of obtaining clarifications or any other explanations on any matter connected with the interests of the Trust in those companies.

(6) Save as hereinafter specifically provided, a decision by the majority of all members of the Board of Trustees shall constitute a valid and legally effective decision at any meeting of the Board of Trustees, and in the case of an equality of votes the Chairperson shall be entitled to an ordinary as well as a casting vote.

(7) The chairperson may at his or her discretion require that certain issues be decided by the Board of Trustees by a resolution in writing signed by all or a majority of the trustees for the time being and such resolution shall be valid and legally effective as though it were a resolution made at a duly convened meeting of the trustees.

## r 19 Meetings of landowners

(1) The trustee or trustees of each station or division shall at least once every calendar year before the end of September convene at their respective stations an annual general meeting for landowners of each such station or division at a time and place to be determined by the said trustee or trustees, and the business at each such annual general meeting shall include the following—

(a) to receive from the trustees of landowners information concerning the administration and accounts of the Trust;

(b) to receive from the director representing their interest in the company information concerning the company and to advise the unitholders about the same;

(c) subject to rule 5(5) of these Rules, to receive nominations for and to elect trustees to fill vacancies in accordance with rule 5(1).

(2) In addition to the annual general meeting the trustee or trustees of each station or division may in every calendar year convene one or more extraordinary general meeting for landowners/extension forest owners at their respective stations and divisions at such times as shall be determined by the said trustee or trustees, or as required by the Trust upon the written request of the majority of the landowner unitholders of each such station or division.

(3) The trustee or trustees of each station or division may summon to the annual general meeting or to any such extraordinary general meeting the director appointed in respect of that station or division.

## r 20 Notice of meetings of landowner

(1) Unless otherwise provided for in this rule, a notice for any meeting of the landowner unitholders shall be given not less than 14 days from the date of the meeting.

(2) The 14 days specified in paragraph (1) shall exclude the day on which the notice is issued or deemed to be issued, but shall include the day of the meeting for which the notice is given.

(3) A notice given under this rule shall specify the place, the date and the time of the meeting.

(4) Where a meeting is to consider a special business, the general nature of that business shall be included in the notice which shall be given to such persons as are entitled to receive the notice.

(5) Subject to the requirement for a quorum being satisfied as provided under rule 22 herein, a shorter period of notice than is required under this Rule may be approved by the majority of the landowner unitholders present at and are entitled to receive notice for a particular meeting.

(6) A notice is deemed to be properly given under this rule if the Trust causes an announcement to be broadcast in the iTaukei language over the National Radio Station on not less than 3 separate occasions and not less than 14 days from the date of the meeting informing the landowner unitholders of the place, the date and the time of the meeting.

## r 21 Effect of non-receipt of notice of meeting

The non-receipt of notice of a meeting by any person entitled to such notice shall not invalidate the proceedings of that meeting.

## r 22 Quorum

The quorum at any meeting of the landowner unitholders shall comprise persons present who hold not less than 50% of the total units held by unitholders at that forest station or division and no business transacted at any meeting without such quorum shall be valid unless ratified at a subsequent meeting or by flying minutes which satisfy the quorum requirements.

## r 23 Effect of absence of quorum

If within one hour from the time appointed for the meeting a quorum is not present, the meeting, if convened upon the requisition of landowner unitholders, shall be cancelled; in any other case it shall stand adjourned to the same day in the next week at the same time and place, or to such other day at such other time and place as the secretary of the Trust may determine, and if at the adjourned meeting a quorum is not present within half an hour from the time appointed for the meeting, the meeting shall proceed as if there was a quorum present, and resolution reached at such a meeting shall be deemed to be valid.

## r 24 Determination of qualification to attend landowner meeting

Every landowner unitholder shall be entitled to attend any meeting if prior to the scheduled time of the meeting its duly elected representative is able to satisfy the secretary of the Trust that he or she is the elected representative of the landowner unitholder duly registered in the Register in accordance with these Trust Rules.

## r 25 Chairperson

The chairperson at a meeting shall be appointed at the meeting by the representatives of the landowner unitholders. If none is appointed, then the trustee of the forest station present shall preside at the meeting.

## r 26 Adjournment

The chairperson may, with the consent of the landowner unitholders in any meeting at which a quorum is present (and shall if so directed by the meeting), adjourn the meeting from time to time and from place to place, but no business shall be transacted at any adjourned meeting other than the business left unfinished at the meeting from which the adjournment took place. When a meeting is adjourned for 30 days or more, notice of the adjourned meeting shall be given as in the case of an original meeting. Save as aforesaid it shall not be necessary to give notice of an adjournment or of the business to be transacted at an adjourned meeting.

## r 27 Voting

At every meeting of the landowner unitholders—

(a) unless a poll is demanded, voting shall be by show of hands and the decision of the meeting shall be declared by the chairperson when carried unanimously or by a majority;

(b) every duly registered landowner unitholder or present in person shall be entitled to one vote for every unit held;

(c) where no poll is demanded, a declaration by the chairperson that a resolution has on a show of hands been carried or carried unanimously, or by a particular majority, or lost, an entry to that effect in the book containing the minutes of the proceedings of the meeting shall be conclusive evidence of the fact without proof of the number or proportion of the votes recorded in favour of or against the resolution;

(d) a poll may be demanded either before or on the declaration of the result of the show of hands by the chairperson or by any duly registered landowner unitholder present at the meeting and holding not less than one-quarter of the total units for that station or division;

(e) where a poll is demanded, it shall be taken in such manner and either at once or after an interval or adjournment or otherwise as the chairperson of the meeting may direct, and the result of the poll shall be the resolution of the meeting at which the poll was demanded; but a poll demanded on the election of a chairperson or on a question of adjournment shall be taken forthwith; and,

(f) the chairperson shall, in the case of equality, have a second or casting vote.

## r 28 Voting rights

Each unit entitles the beneficiary representative to one vote for each unit held by him or her, provided however that in relation to matters and issues which affect only the minority unitholders, the voting entitlement of any of the beneficiaries who is otherwise entitled to more than 50% of the total aggregate voting entitlements for that station or division shall be restricted so as not to exceed 33% of the total aggregate votes of all beneficiaries.

## r 29 Objections

No objection shall be raised to the qualification of any voter except at the meeting or adjourned meeting at which the vote objected to is given or tendered, and every vote not disallowed at such meeting shall be valid for all purposes. Any such objections made in due time shall be referred to the chairperson of the meeting, whose decision shall be final and conclusive.

## r 30 Secretary of the Trust and the Board of Trustees

(1) The Board of Trustees shall appoint on terms and conditions, including remuneration, as the Board shall think fit a secretary of the Board and the Trust as a whole whose duties shall be to provide the secretariat functions of the Board and to manage the affairs of the Trust as directed by the Board, including the following—

(a) to keep and maintain the Register;

(b) to prepare and maintain books of accounts as required under rule 9 and to keep the minutes of all meetings held by the Board of Trustees, the landowner unitholder and the extension unitholders; and

(c) to prepare and issue the notice of each meeting of the Board of Trustees, unitholders.

(2) The Board of Trustees may from time to time delegate to the secretary other duties as the Board may think fit, and in the performance of his or her duties the secretary shall be responsible to the chairperson of the Board of Trustees.

(3) The secretary may employ agents or representatives to carry out any of its duties delegated to it by the Board of Trustees.

(4) The secretary, its agent or representative shall be entitled to attend and participate in the proceedings of any meeting held pursuant to these Trust Rules, but shall not be entitled to vote in any such meeting.

## r 31 Appointment of auditors

The Board of Trustees shall at its general meeting convened for the purpose appoint from among 3 nominees submitted to it for the purpose a person who is duly qualified and registered as a chartered accountant in Fiji as auditor of the Trust. The Board shall determine the terms and conditions for such appointment.

## r 32 Appointment of directors

(1) The Board of Trustees shall appoint to the Board of the company as many directors as are permitted under the Articles of Association of the company.

(2) The qualifications of a director shall be the same as that of a trustee, provided however that a serving trustee shall not be entitled to be appointed as a director.

## r 33 Removal of directors

Subject to the Articles of Association of the company and the requirements of the Companies Act 2015, a director appointed by the Board of Trustees shall be removed by the Board of Trustees for misconduct or for conduct unbecoming or upon receipt by the Board of Trustees of a majority resolution of the landowners unitholders who nominated him or her recommending such removal on the ground that the director had acted in a manner which was prejudicial to and against the interests of such unitholders.

## r 34 Variation of Rules

(1) The Board of Trustees upon receiving notification of a special resolution of the beneficiaries obtained through secret ballot, shall vary, alter or amend these Rules in accordance with the instructions contained in such special resolutions.

(2) A special resolution shall be an aggregate of not less than 75% of votes of all beneficiaries entitled to cast votes at separate meetings of each category of unitholders, provided however that no variation which affects the rights of a particular category of unitholders shall apply to such category unless in the case of the Government such variation is supported by the consent in writing of the holders of not less than 50% of votes of all unitholders in those categories and in the case of landowner unitholders by a special resolution passed in each of the separate landowner unitholders meetings called for the purpose.

## r 35 Termination of Trust

(1) The Trust created herein shall commence on the date hereof and shall continue until the Trust is determined and dissolved by the Board of Trustees upon receipt of special resolutions by each category of the beneficiaries approving such determination.

(2) As soon as practicable after the date determined by special resolution of the beneficiaries in accordance with paragraph (1), the Board of Trustees shall subject as hereinafter provided—

(a) sell, call in and convert into money or cause to be sold, called in and converted into money the investments and property constituting the Trust, and divide the proceeds of such sale and conversion (less all proper outgoings and all proper provision for liabilities) among the unitholders in proportion to the number of units of which they are at the date of determination of Trust respectively registered as the holders, provided however that the Board of Trustees may in its discretion at the request of any unitholder transfer to such unitholder any assets of the trust fund in species in satisfaction or part satisfaction of the entitlement of the unitholder on the termination of this Trust.

(b) The Board of Trustees shall give to each beneficiary not less than one month’s notice of the impending distribution herein.

(c) The Board of Trustees may postpone the sale and conversion of any part of the investments and property of the Trust for such time as it thinks desirable in the interests of the beneficiaries and shall not be responsible for any loss attributable to such postponement.

(3) The Trust shall be deemed to be terminated from the date the Board of Trustees receive in writing evidence that not less than 75% of all the beneficiaries herein wishing to determine the same, provided however that the Trust shall not be determined unless—

(a) a termination date has already been determined by the beneficiaries pursuant to paragraph (1); and,

(b) that the evidence in writing shall contain the signatures of the requisite percentage of beneficiaries required for a special resolution.

## r 36 Seal

The Board of Trustees shall provide for the safe custody of the common seal of the Trust which shall only be used by the authority of the Board of Trustees or of a committee of trustees authorised by the Board of Trustees on their behalf and every instrument to which the common seal is affixed shall be signed by the chairperson of the Board and the secretary on behalf of the Board of Trustees.
