# UNIT TITLES REGULATIONS 1985

**Table of Amendments**

Unit Titles Regulations 1985 (LN 102 of 1985) commenced on 1 January 1986, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Unit Titles (Amendment) Regulations 1986 (LN 122 of 1986) | 1 January 1987 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Unit Titles Regulations 1985.

## reg 2 Commencement

These Regulations shall come into force on 1 January 1986.

## reg 3 Prescribed person

A registered valuer is a prescribed person for the purposes of section 7 and section 53 of the Act.

[reg 3 subst LN 122 of 1986 reg 3, effective 1 January 1987]

## reg 4 Form 1 — Application to deposit unit plan

An application to deposit a unit plan shall be in Form 1 of Schedule 1.

## reg 5 Form 2 — Certificate of Title — unit estate — general

The certificate of title for a unit estate shall be in Form 2 of Schedule 1.

## reg 6 Form 3 — Certificate of Title — future development unit

The certificate of title for a unit estate in a future development unit shall be in Form 3 of Schedule 1.

## reg 7 Forms 4, 5 and 6 — Cancellation of unit plan

An application to the Registrar for the cancellation of a unit plan shall—

(a) if made by the lessor pursuant to section 36(2), be in Form 4 of Schedule 1;

(b) if made by consent of the proprietors, be in Form 5 of Schedule 1; and

(c) if made pursuant to section 56, be in Form 6 of Schedule 1.

## reg 8 Form 7 — Certificate of body corporate

The certificate of the body corporate pursuant to section 45 of the Act shall be in Form 6 of Schedule 1.

## reg 9 Form 8 — Certificate of expiry of lease

A certificate of expiry of a lease, deposited pursuant to section 37(3) of the Act, shall—

(a) if lodged by the lessor, be in Form 8(A) of Schedule 1; and

(b) if lodged by the persons referred to in section 37(3)(b) of the Act, be in Form 8(B) of Schedule 1.

## reg 10 Form 9 — Change of rules

The notification by a body corporate of an amendment to, repeal of, or addition to a rule applicable to the body corporate shall be in Form 9 of Schedule 1.

## reg 11 Form 10 — Certificate of principal officer — unit plan

The certificate of the principal officer of a Local Authority given pursuant to section 5(1)(e) of the Act, shall be in Form 10 of Schedule 1.

## reg 12 Form 11 — Certificate of principal officer — future development plan

The certificate of the principal officer of a Local Authority, given pursuant to section 24(3)(c) of the Act, shall be in Form 11 of Schedule 1.

## reg 13 Fees

The fees set out in Schedule 2 shall be charged by the Registrar for those dealings under the Act which are specified in Schedule 2.
