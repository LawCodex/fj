## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Water Supply (Free Allowance) By-laws 1955.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, “free allowance” means a free allowance of water.

## by-law 3 Application

Any person desirous of obtaining a free allowance under these By-laws shall—

(a) make application therefore in writing to the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji; and

(b) give full particulars as to—

(i) the type of premises;

(ii) the purposes for which the premises are used;

(iii) the number of persons normally resident or attending such premises; and

(iv) the number and type of privies provided.

[by-law 3 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 299, effective 1 December 2016]

## by-law 4 Amount of free allowance

The amount of the free allowance shall be determined by the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji whose decision shall be final, provided however that no free allowance to any premises or institution shall exceed 900 cubic metres per annum.

[by-law 4 am LN 126 of 1978 by-law 2 and Sch, effective 8 September 1978; LN 99 of 2016 reg 299, effective 1 December 2016]

## by-law 5 Discontinuance of free allowance

(1) A free allowance to any institution or premises under these By-laws may be reduced or discontinued altogether, if the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji is satisfied that the owner of the premises obtained such free allowance by giving false or inaccurate information to the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji.

[para (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 299, effective 1 December 2016]

(2) The consumer to whom a free allowance has been granted under these By-laws shall notify the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji—

(a) when the premises for which the supply has been granted have been demolished or removed; or

(b) where the nature of the premises has been changed.

[para (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 299, effective 1 December 2016]

## by-law 6 Offences

Whosoever shall—

(a) obtain a free allowance by giving false or inaccurate information to the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji; or

(b) fail to notify the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji when—

(i) the premises for which the free allowance has been granted have been demolished or removed; or

(ii) the nature of the premises has been changed,

shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable, on conviction, to a fine not exceeding $400 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months, or to both such fine and imprisonment.

[by-law 6 am LN 21 of 1979 by-law 2, effective 19 January 1979; LN 99 of 2016 reg 299, effective 1 December 2016]
