## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Water Supply By-laws 1955.

## by-law 1A Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires,

**appropriate form** means a form approved by the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji.

[by-law 1A insrt LN 23 of 1977 by-law 2, effective 4 February 1977; am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

## by-law 2 Application

Any person desirous of obtaining a domestic, trade or building supply from the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji shall make application therefor, under and subject to the terms of these By-laws, on the printed form contained in Schedule A, which may be obtained from the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji.

[by-law 2 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

## by-law 3 Fees

The application form for a new domestic, trade, industrial or commercial supply shall be accompanied by a fee of $21.95, and, for a new building supply, a fee of $100.98, which will be returned if, for any reason, the application is refused.

[by-law 3 am LN 45 of 1978 by-law 2, effective 1 April 1978; LN 83 of 1978 by-law 2, effective 1 July 1978; LN 185 of 1979 by-law 2, effective 1 November 1979; LN 158 of 1980 by-law 2, effective 1 November 1980; LN 31 of 1984 by-law 3, effective 1 April 1984; LN 4 of 1997 by-law 3, effective 1 January 1998]

## by-law 4 Control

If it shall appear to the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji that—

(a) an applicant will be likely to use such a quantity of water as will prejudice the supply to other consumers; or

(b) for any other reason, the approval of the application will adversely affect, or is likely adversely to affect, the efficiency of the system generally,

the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji may refuse the application or specify the quantity of water which such applicant may draw from the mains during any period, and the use of any quantity of water in excess of the amount specified shall be deemed to be a breach of these By-laws.

[by-law 4 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

## by-law 5 Use of water — public standpipes

(1) Water shall be taken from public standpipes only in buckets or other suitable receptacles and in such a manner as to prevent waste.

(2) No hosepipe, pipe, tube, chute or other contrivance of any nature whatsoever shall be attached, either temporarily or permanently, to any public standpipe.

(3) No automatic self-closing valve or other automatic appliance attached to or forming part of any public standpipe shall be interfered with so as to prevent, either temporarily or permanently, its automatic action.

(4) Any person who acts in contravention of the provisions of this by-law shall be guilty of an offence.

## by-law 6 Connections

(1) The kind and size of connection and its position shall be determined by the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji.

[para (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

(2) The connection shall be taken from the main to a meter box, which shall be placed inside the boundary of the applicant's premises, or in such position as the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji may determine, and shall there terminate in a stopcock and meter.

[para (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

(3) This work shall be done by the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji.

[para (3) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

(4) Nothing hereinbefore contained shall be deemed to prohibit the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji from installing more than 1 meter where water is supplied to the same premises for different purposes.

[para (4) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

(5) The Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji may, as a condition of installing a new connection, require the applicant for the supply to contribute to the cost of the connection where the nearest main is at a greater distance than 15 metres from the boundary of the premises to be served or where the connection is larger than 25 mm bore.

[para (5) am LN 126 of 1978 by-law 2 and Sch, effective 8 September 1978; am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

(6) All such connections, stopcocks, meters and meter boxes shall remain the property of the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji and shall not be interfered with nor the stopcock turned on or off by any person on any pretext whatsoever, except with the authority of the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji.

[para (6) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

## by-law 7 Service to be fixed by licensed fitter

(1) All pipes required for the purpose of domestic, trade or building supplies shall start from the meter above referred to, and, together with all fittings, taps or other apparatus connected therewith, so far as they are subject to internal water pressure from the street mains, shall be fixed only by a fitter licenced under by-law 14.

(2) Any person, other than a licensed fitter, who fixes any such pipe, fitting, tap or other apparatus shall be guilty of an offence.

[para (2) insrt LN 88 of 1956 by-law 2, opn 5 Oct 1956]

## by-law 8 Premises supplied from tanks etc, may be connected to service

In cases where private houses or other premises are already provided with a system of water supply from tanks, wells, or other sources, such system may be connected with the mains, provided that—

(a) the tanks, wells and other sources of supply are disconnected; and

(b) the pipes, so far as they will be subjected to the pressure from the mains, are, in the opinion of the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji, of sufficient strength to sustain the pressure.

[by-law 8 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

## by-law 9 Separate connection to each house

(1) Every house or separate premises supplied with water from the mains shall have a separate connection and, except with the permission of the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji, no such house or separate premises shall have more than one connection.

[para (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

(2) Blocks or rows of houses or premises belonging to the same owner may, however, at the discretion of the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji, be supplied by one connection, stopcock, and meter, with branch pipes to each of the houses or premises in such block or row, or with one or more taps for use of the whole.

[para (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

(3) Any such arrangement shall apply only so long as the whole of the block or row of houses or premises so supplied remains under one ownership.

(4) On the transfer of ownership of any house or premises served by a sub-connection under these By-laws, the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji may disconnect the transferred premises.

[para (4) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

## by-law 9A Election in relation to one or more rates of payment

Where one or more rates of payment have been fixed in respect of domestic, trade or building supplies, the owner of premises may elect—

(a) by giving 14 days’ notice in writing to the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji in the case of the first connection of any one supply;

(b) by giving at least 3 months’ notice in writing to the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji in any other case,

to pay for water at any one of such fixed rates, which may or may not include meter rents as the case may be.

[by-law 9A insrt LN 24 of 1960 by-law 2, effective 1 March 1960; am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

## by-law 10 Accounts

(1) Except in the case of the disconnection of a supply under by-law 13, when the meter shall be read immediately prior to disconnection, all meters shall be read at such times as the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji may direct.

[para (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

(2) Accounts shall be rendered quarterly.

(3) Where a water supply shall have been disconnected, the charge for re-connection of the same shall be $10.00.

[para (3) am LN 45 of 1978 by-law 3, effective 1 April 1978; LN 83 of 1978 by-law 3, effective 1 July 1978; LN 185 of 1979 by-law 3, effective 1 November 1979; LN 158 of 1980 by-law 3, effective 1 November 1980; LN 31 of 1984 by-law 4, effective 1 April 1984; LN 4 of 1998 by-law 4, effective 1 January 1998]

(4) All payments due under these By-laws shall be made at the Water Authority of Fiji or at an authorised branch office.

[para (4) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

## by-law 11 Testing of meters

(1) Any consumer who desires the accuracy of his or her meter to be tested shall deposit with the local sub-accountant the sum of $12.74 before such meter shall be tested.

[para (1) am LN 45 of 1978 by-law 4, effective 1 April 1978; LN 83 of 1978 by-law 4, effective 1 July 1978; LN 185 of 1979 by-law 4, effective 1 November 1979; LN 158 of 1980 by-law 4, effective 1 November 1980; LN 31 of 1984 by-law 5, effective 1 April 1984; LN 4 of 1998 by-law 5, effective 1 January 1998]

(2) The Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji shall give such consumer notice of the time and place for the carrying out of such test and shall allow such person to be present.

[para (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

(3) If, as a result of the test, the meter is found to be registering fast by more than 5%—

(a) the deposit shall be refunded to the consumer;

(b) the meter shall be replaced without charge; and

(c) the consumer shall be allowed any inaccuracy deduced from the test aforesaid,

provided that such allowance shall only be in respect of the quantity in question before payment therefore shall have been made.

(4) If, as a result of the test, the meter is found to be registering slow by more than 5%—

(a) the consumer shall forfeit the deposit; and

(b) any inaccuracy deduced from the test aforesaid shall be charged to the consumer,

provided that such charge shall only be in respect of the quantity in question before payment therefore shall have been made.

(5) If, as a result of the test, the meter is found to be registering 5% fast or slow, or less than 5% fast or slow—

(a) it shall be deemed to be correct; and

(b) the consumer shall—

(i) forfeit the deposit; and

(ii) be charged with any cost incurred in testing the meter.

[para (5) am LN 45 of 1978 by-law 4, effective 1 April 1978; LN 83 of 1978 by-law 4, effective 1 July 1978]

(6) Should any meter cease registering altogether, or be found to register so incorrectly that in the opinion of the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji the true quantity for any period cannot be deduced therefrom, then the consumption for that period shall be the average deduced from the quantity supplied during the previous period of not less than one month and not more than 6 months.

[para (6) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

(7) No question of the accuracy of the meter shall exempt the consumer from payment within the time prescribed by these By-laws of the quantity registered prima facie by such meter.

(8) In the case of emergency or of a temporary supply—

(a) the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji may, in his or her discretion—

(i) dispense with the use of a meter; and

(ii) assess the quantity of water supplied by computation, which shall be binding upon the owner; and

(b) the cost of making the connection and removing the same shall be paid by the owner.

[para (8) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

## by-law 11A Special meter reading

Where a special reading is made at the request of the consumer the charge therefore shall be $9.88.

[para (1) am LN 45 of 1978 by-law 5, effective 1 April 1978; LN 83 of 1978 by-law 5, effective 1 July 1978; LN 185 of 1979 by-law 5, effective 1 November 1979; LN 158 of 1980 by-law 5, effective 1 November 1980; LN 31 of 1984 by-law 6, effective 1 April 1984; LN 4 of 1998 by-law 6, effective 1 January 1998]

## by-law 12 Repairs and renewals

(1) All repairs and renewals which may, at any time, be required to pipes, taps, fittings, or other apparatus subject to internal water pressure from the mains, shall be carried out by a fitter licensed under by-law 14.

(2) Such repairs and renewals may, however, at the discretion of the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji, be carried out by the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji, in which case the consumer shall pay the cost of such repairs or renewals.

[para (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

(3) Any person who causes or permits any repair or renewal to be carried out in contravention of the provisions of this by-law shall be guilty of an offence.

## by-law 13 Discontinuance of supplies

Any consumer who may be desirous of discontinuing a domestic, trade or building supply shall give 7 days’ notice in writing to the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji, who will then arrange for the service to be disconnected.

[by-law 13 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

## by-law 14 Licensing of waterworks fitter

(1) Any workman desiring a licence to carry out work in connection with water supplies to which these By-laws apply shall make application in the appropriate form.

[para (1) am LN 23 of 1977 by-law 3, effective 4 February 1977]

(2) Licences in the appropriate form shall be issued free of cost by the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji on his or her being satisfied that the applicant is a fit and proper person to be entrusted with work as a waterworks fitter.

[para (2) am LN 8 of 1976 by-law 2, effective 9 July 1976; am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

(3) Any authorised waterworks fitter who is shown, to the satisfaction of the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji, to have acted in any way contrary to these By-laws or the Act shall be liable to have his licence cancelled by the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji.

[para (3) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

(4) The names of all waterworks fitters so licensed shall, from time to time, be published in the Gazette.

## by-law 15 Conservation of water

(1) No person shall use water in contravention of any prohibition or limitation imposed by the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji at any time on the use of water supplied by him or her in any locality for the purpose of conserving the water supply in that locality on account of any existing or threatened shortage of water.

[para (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 304, effective 1 December 2016]

(2) Any person who uses or causes or permits to be used any water in contravention of any such limitation or prohibition shall be guilty of an offence.

## by-law 16 Penalty

Any person convicted of an offence under these By-laws shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $400 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months, or to both such fine and imprisonment.

[by-law 16 am LN 20 of 1979 by-law 2, effective 19 January 1979]
