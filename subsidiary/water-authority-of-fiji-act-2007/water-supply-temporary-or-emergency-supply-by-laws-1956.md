## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Water Supply (Temporary or Emergency Supply) By-laws 1956.

## by-law 2 Calculation of water used

In the case of unmetered emergency or temporary supplies, the measurement of water supplied shall be calculated as follows—

(a) where the premises supplied with the emergency or temporary supply have, during the preceding 6 months, been supplied with a metered supply of water for one or more continuous periods of 30 or more days, the calculation of water supplied under the emergency or temporary supply shall be made on the assumption that the daily supply under the emergency or temporary supply equals the daily average of water supplied during the most recent of the continuous periods aforesaid;

(b) in cases other than those referred to in paragraph (a), the calculation shall be based upon the assumption that the daily supply is as follows—

(i) domestic supply, 225 litres for each person resident on the premises;

(ii) trade supply, 115 litres for each person employed on the premises;

(iii) building supply, 20 litres per $2 of the estimated cost of the works.

[by-law 2 am LN 126 of 1978 by-law 2 and Sch, effective 8 September 1978]

## by-law 3 Application

Any person desirous of obtaining a temporary supply shall make application on the printed form contained in the Schedule which may be obtained from the Chief Executive Officer of Water Authority of Fiji.

[by-law 3 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 303, effective 1 December 2016]

## by-law 4 Fees

(1) The application form for a temporary supply shall be accompanied by a fee of $12 which will be returned if, for any reason, the application is refused.

[para (1) am LN 47 of 1978 by-law 2, effective 1 April 1978; LN 85 of 1978 by-law 2, effective 1 July 1978; LN 184 of 1979 by-law 2, effective 1 November 1979; LN 156 of 1980 by-law 2, effective 1 November 1980; LN 32 of 1984 by-law 2, effective 1 April 1984]

(2) The kind, size and position of the connection shall be determined by the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji.

[para (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 303, effective 1 December 2016]

(3) Where the application is granted, the applicant shall pay a further fee as determined in each case by the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji.

[para (3) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 301, effective 1 December 2016]

(4) Upon the removal of the connection, the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji shall return to the applicant any part of the fee referred to in paragraph (3) in excess of the cost to the Chief Executive Officer of the Water Authority of Fiji of installing and removing the connection.

[para (4) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 301, effective 1 December 2016]
