## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Industrial Associations Regulations 1942.

## reg 2 Restriction on registration

The Registrar shall not register an association under a name identical with that of any existing industrial association, or so nearly resembling such name as to be likely to deceive the members of the public.

## reg 3 Form of application

Every application by an association for registration shall be made in Form A in the Schedule, and shall be accompanied by 3 typed or printed copies of the constitution and rules, marked and signed as mentioned in the said Form.

## reg 4 Form of certificate

The certificate or registration of an industrial association shall be in Form B in the Schedule.

## reg 5 Notice of change of address

Notice of change in the address of the head office of an industrial association shall be given to the Registrar in Form C in the Schedule.

## reg 6 Annual returns

The annual returns required by section 11 of the Act, shall be in Forms D, E, F and G in the Schedule, and shall contain the particulars indicated therein.
