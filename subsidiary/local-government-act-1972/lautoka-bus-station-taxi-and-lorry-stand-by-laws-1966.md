## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Lautoka (Bus Station, Taxi and Lorry Stand) By-laws 1966.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**bus shelter** means any shelter owned and controlled by the Council for the use of bus passengers in the city of Lautoka;

**bus station** means the bus station at Lautoka maintained and controlled by the Council situated at Namoli Park, Lautoka and contained in State Lease 2143;

[def am LGN 9 of 1968 by-law 2, 9 August 1968]

**carrier** means a closed panel van with a front seat designed only to carry no more than one passenger with goods;

[def insrt LN 77 of 1994 by-law 3, 26 August 1994]

**carrier stand** means all carrier stands at Lautoka city maintained and controlled by the Council;

[def insrt LN 77 of 1994 by-law 3, 26 August 1994]

**Council** means the Lautoka City Council;

**lorry** means any light or heavy goods vehicle plying for hire for the carriage of any cargo for reward;

**lorry stand** means all lorry stands at Lautoka maintained and controlled by the Council;

**motor omnibus** means a public service vehicle constructed or adapted to carry more than 6 passengers excluding the driver;

**operator** includes any person, firm, company or organisation who or which owns or operates a motor omnibus, a taxi or a lorry;

**taxi** means a public service vehicle other than a motor omnibus constructed or adapted to carry passengers; and

**taxi stand** means all taxi stands at Lautoka maintained and controlled by the Council.

[def insrt LGN 9 of 1968 by-law 2, 9 August 1968]

## by-law 3 Management of bus station etc

The bus station, taxi stand, carrier stand and lorry stand shall be under the immediate management and control of the town clerk, provided that the Council may appoint an attendant or attendants to assist the town clerk to exercise control over the bus station and any one or more stands and with such functions with regard thereto as the Council may by resolution determine.

[by-law 3 am LN 77 of 1994 by-law 4, 26 August 1994]

## by-law 4 Licence to use bus station etc

Each operator desiring to use the bus station, the taxi stand, the carrier stand or the lorry stand shall make application in the form set out in Schedule 1 , for a licence for his or her motor omnibus, taxi, carrier or lorry (as the case may be) to use the bus station, the taxi stand, the carrier stand or the lorry stand.

[by-law 4 am LN 77 of 1994 by-law 5, 26 August 1994]

## by-law 5 Licences to be issued by town clerk

All licences to use the bus station, taxi stand, carrier stand or lorry stand shall be issued on behalf of the Council by the town clerk in the form of licence set out in Schedule 2 and shall have effect in respect only of the motor omnibus, taxi, carrier or lorry described therein.

[by-law 5 am LN 77 of 1994 by-law 6, 26 August 1994]

## by-law 5A Stickers

(1) The town clerk, acting on behalf of the Council, may issue a sticker in respect of a vehicle referred to in a licence issued under these By-laws.

(2) A sticker issued under paragraph (1) shall be in a form set out in Schedule 4.

(3) The operator of a vehicle in respect of which a sticker is issued under paragraph (1) shall, immediately on receipt of that sticker, affix it to the bottom corner of the windscreen of that vehicle which is furthest from the driver's seat.

(4) A sticker issued under paragraph (1) in respect of a vehicle is valid until—

(a) the date specified on the sticker; or

(b) where the licence issued under these By-laws in respect of that vehicle is revoked by the Council under by-law 9 , the date of revocation of that licence,

whichever is the earlier.

(5) Where the Council revokes a licence under by-law 9 , any sticker issued under paragraph (1) in respect of a vehicle referred to in that licence shall be removed from that vehicle by the operator of the vehicle.

(6) A person shall not remove a sticker issued under paragraph (1) during its period of validity.

[by-law 5A insrt LN 32 of 1985 by-law 3, 10 May 1985]

## by-law 6 Fees

(1) The licence fees for the use of the bus station, taxi stand, carrier stand or lorry stand by motor omnibuses, taxis, carriers or lorries shall be as set out in Schedule 3 and shall be collected and charged in accordance with the provisions of the succeeding paragraphs of this by-law.

[para (1) am LN 77 of 1994 by-law 7, 26 August 1994]

(2) The licence fees payable in respect of each motor omnibus, taxi, carrier or lorry using the bus station, taxi stand, carrier stand or lorry stand shall be charged in respect of each calendar month and in arrear.

[para (2) am LN 77 of 1994 by-law 7, 26 August 1994]

(3) In respect of motor omnibuses, the licence fees to be charged for any such monthly period shall be computed on the total number of visits of the motor omnibus to the bus station during the month and shall be calculated from the timetable approved by the Land Transport Authority in respect of the road service licence under which such motor omnibus shall have been operating during the month or from any other information which the Land Transport Authority shall think fit to supply to the town clerk from its records or from any other information obtained by the town clerk and the provisions of paragraph (4) .

[para (3) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 142, 1 December 2016]

(4) If the town clerk considers that the number of visits to the bus station of any motor omnibus cannot be satisfactorily calculated from any timetable, he or she may, by written notice, require the bus operator concerned to supply him or her with particulars of the numbers of visits to the bus station and the times thereof of each motor omnibus operating under the operator’s road service licence during the month the subject of the computation; and any bus operator so required to supply such particulars shall, within 7 days after the receipt by him or her of such notice, deliver to the town clerk a written statement, signed by such operator or under his or her authority, containing a true and correct statement of the total number of visits to the bus station and the times thereof of each such motor omnibus during the said month.

(5) All licence fees payable to the Council under the provisions of these By-laws shall be recoverable by the Council as civil debts by due process of law.

## by-law 7 Payment of fees

Each operator shall pay to the town clerk the licence fees payable by him or her within 15 days after he or she has been notified of the amount thereof.

## by-law 8 Bus station not to be used without licence

(1) No operator shall be entitled to use the bus station, taxi stand, carrier stand or lorry stand for the purpose of any motor omnibus, taxi, carrier or lorry unless he or she holds a valid licence issued under the provisions of these By-laws in respect of such motor omnibus, taxi, carrier or lorry.

[para (1) am LN 77 of 1994 by-law 8, 26 August 1994]

(2) No motor omnibus, taxi, carrier or lorry shall enter or use the bus station, taxi stand, carrier stand or lorry stand at any time unless a valid licence issued under the provisions of these By-laws is held by the operator in respect thereof and any person who contravenes the provisions of this paragraph shall be guilty of an offence.

[para (2) am LN 77 of 1994 by-law 8, 26 August 1994]

(3) No operator shall be entitled to use the bus station, taxi stand, carrier stand or lorry stand while any licence fees owing by him or her under the provisions of these By-laws remain unpaid after the due date.

[para (3) am LN 77 of 1994 by-law 8, 26 August 1994]

## by-law 9 Revocation of licence

The Council may revoke the licence of any operator who fails to pay by the due date any licence fees payable by him or her or who is convicted of any offence under the provisions of these By-laws.

## by-law 10 Control of bus station etc

(1) No person shall without the written authority of the Council sell or offer or expose for sale any produce or goods in or around the bus station, taxi stand, carrier stand or lorry stand.

[para (1) am LN 77 of 1994 by-law 9, 26 August 1994]

(2) No person shall place or deposit in or on any motor omnibus, taxi, carrier or lorry standing in the bus station, taxi stand, carrier stand or lorry stand any placard, handbill, advertisement or notice without the prior consent of the owner or driver of such motor omnibus, taxi, carrier or lorry.

[para (2) am LN 77 of 1994 by-law 9, 26 August 1994]

(3) No person shall display in or on the bus station, taxi stand, carrier stand or lorry stand or any bus shelter any placard, handbill, advertisement or notice, pamphlet, book or paper without the prior consent of the Council and on such terms and conditions as the Council shall, by resolution, determine.

[para (3) am LN 77 of 1994 by-law 9, 26 August 1994]

(4) Any person contravening the provisions of this by-law shall be guilty of an offence.

## by-law 11 Parking of motor omnibus

(1) No person shall park any motor omnibus in the bus station except for the purpose of loading or unloading passengers and their luggage and in no case for longer than—

(a) 5 minutes immediately prior to the scheduled time of departure from the said bus station of such motor omnibus; or

(b) is reasonably necessary for the purpose of setting down passengers and their luggage.

(2) Any person contravening the provisions of this by-law shall be guilty of an offence.

## by-law 12 Penalty

Any person who is guilty of an offence under the provisions of these By-laws shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine not exceeding $20.
