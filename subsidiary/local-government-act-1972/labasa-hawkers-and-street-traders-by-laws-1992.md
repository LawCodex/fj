# LABASA (HAWKERS AND STREET TRADERS) BY-LAWS 1992

Labasa (Hawkers and Street Traders) By-laws 1992 (LN 141 of 1992) commenced on 13 November 1992, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## by-law 1 Short Title

These By-laws may be cited as the Labasa (Hawkers and Street Traders) By-laws 1992.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires:

**Council** means the Labasa Town Council;

**goods** means:

(a) any food or drink other than liquor;

(b) cigarettes, cigars, tobacco and matches;

(c) curios and handicrafts, including mats, basket fans, wood carvings, article made of straw and grass skirts;

(d) plants, ferns and cut flowers;

(e) jewellery, ornaments and souvenirs;

(f) live poultry and animals and eggs;

(g)

(i) books;

(ii) filigree jewellery, imitation jewellery and ladies’ evening bags;

(iii) dolls dressed in traditional costumes or portraying Fijian Police officers or soldiers;

(iv) postcards;

(v) fruits and vegetables;

(vi) scarves, cushion covers, beaded purses, men’s belts with the words “Fiji” on the outside thereof;

(vii) wares of other Pacific Island origin such as curios and handicrafts,

not including any produce, article or thing made, manufacture, prepared in, or originating from any territory outside Fiji with the exception of the articles specified in paragraph (g) ;

**hawker** means a person who by going from place to place sells goods carried on his person or form a handcart, box, basket, tray and the like or from a boat, horse-drawn vehicle, bicycle, motor vehicle, or any other type of conveyance;

**officer** means the town clerk, deputy town or any health inspector appointed by the Council in the due performance of any duty under these By-laws;

**public and public place** shall have the same meanings as are ascribed to them in the Public Order Act 1969 ;

**street** has the meaning as in ascribed to it in the Act;

**street trader** means a person who sells goods in any street or other public place from a stationary pitch allotted to him by the Council;

**town** means the town of Labasa; and

**town clerk** means the town clerk for the time being of the Council and includes a deputy town clerk or any person duly authorised by the Council to act as town clerk.

## by-law 3 Hawkers and street traders to hold permit

No person shall within the town follow the calling of either a hawker or a street trader unless he shall be the holder in his own name of a valid permit under these By-laws issued by the Council.

## by-law 4 Application for permit

Every person desirous of obtaining either a hawker’s permit or a street trader’s permit shall make application in writing to the town clerk in the form in Schedule 1 , provided that no person may apply for or hold at any one time more than one permit under these By-laws.

## by-law 5 Refusal, cancellation or suspension of permits

The Council may refuse to grant a hawker’s permit or a street trader’s permit to any person and any permit already granted may be suspended or cancelled by the Council for any good cause after giving the holder thereof an opportunity of being heard.

## by-law 6 Issue of permits

Upon receiving an application mentioned in by-law 4 and subject to such direction as he or she may receive from the Council, the town clerk may issue to the applicant, without payment of fee, a permit, valid for a period not exceeding 12 months, in the form set out in Schedule 2 or 3 as the case may be, provided that in the case of a street trader the Council may, with the prior approval of the Minister, by resolution prescribe a fee to be paid for the occupation of the stationary pitch allotted to such street trader by the Council and may prescribe different fees for different pitches.

## by-law 7 Duration and condition of permits

(a) Every permit issued under these By-laws shall be valid for the period stated in the permit and shall only entitle the holder to sell goods as defined in these By-laws. The minimum period shall be 3 months.

(b) No permit shall be transferable and it shall be valid only for the person named in the permit.

(c) Notwithstanding any of the other provisions of these By-laws any permit may be restricted to authorise only the hawking of or trading in and sale of such goods or classes of goods as are endorsed on the permit and may be subject to such other general or special conditions as may be prescribed by the Council.

## by-law 8 Register of permits

The town clerk shall maintain or to be maintained a register of all permits issued under these By-laws containing the information referred to in Schedule 4 and such register shall open for inspection without payment of fee to any member of the public during the normal office hours of the Council.

## by-law 9 Duties of hawkers

A hawker holding a permit under these By-laws shall at all times when proceeding to hawking or returning from hawking or when carrying any goods for hawking—

(a) carry his permit with him and shall upon demand produce the permit to any officer of police officer;

(b) not enter or remain in any municipal market;

(c) not enter or remain in any retail and wholesale shop or any other premises without prior consent of the proprietor or manager of such shop or other premises;

(d) not obstruct any doorway, display window or driveway nor prevent nor stop the free passage or cause any hindrance to any persons in any place or public place nor obstruct, stop or cause hindrance to any vehicle;

(e) operate alone;

(f) not use for hawking a conveyance of any type not fitted with efficient handbrakes capable of stopping and holding such conveyance on a one foot in 6 incline and not fitted with pneumatic or solid rubber tyres as approved by the Council and if used at night not fitted with 2 white lights at the front outer extremities and one red lamp at the rear and shall when at any time on streets used by motor vehicles only move or operate such conveyance in accordance with traffic laws;

(g) move on immediately when directed so to do by any officer or police officer and obey all lawful orders or directions for the maintenance of public movement and good order and discipline.

## by-law 10 Duties of street traders

A street trader holding a permit under these By-laws shall at all times when selling his goods—

(a) have his permit with him and shall upon demand produce such permit to any officer or police officer;

(b) remain on the stationary pitch allotted to him by the Council;

(c) not call out to any person for the purpose of attracting business but may cry out his goods in general advertisements stating what they are and that they are for sale.

## by-law 11 Duties of hawkers and street traders

No hawker or street trader holding a permit under these By-laws shall at any time—

(a) behave in any manner that is or appears to be aggressive or menacing nor force or attempt to force any sale nor act in a noisy, offensive, disturbing or persistent manner;

(b) thrust any object at, nor point either by hand, or with any object therein, at, any person;

(c) touch either with his person or with any object he is holding any other person or part of any other person’s clothing or anything any other person is carrying.

## by-law 12 Penalties

(1) If any hawker or street trader contravenes or fails to comply with any of the provisions of these By-laws, he or she shall be guilty of an offence and shall upon a first or second conviction for any such contravention of failure to comply be liable to a fine not exceeding $50 and upon a third or subsequent conviction to a fine not exceeding $100.

(2) If any person other than the holder of a valid permit under these By-laws engages in hawking or street trading within the town boundaries he or she shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable upon conviction to a fine not exceeding $100 and any such proceedings the onus of proof of issue of such valid permit to him or her shall be on that person.
