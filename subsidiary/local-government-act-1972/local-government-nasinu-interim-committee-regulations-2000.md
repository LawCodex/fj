# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (NASINU) (INTERIM COMMITTEE) REGULATIONS 2000

Local Government (Nasinu) (Interim Committee) Regulations 2000 (LN 99 of 2000) commenced on 25 August 2000, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Local Government (Nasinu) (Interim Committee) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 (LN 49 of 2001) | 1 July 2001 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Nasinu) (Interim Committee) Regulations 2000.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, “Interim Committee” against the nominated members constituting the Interim Committee appointed by the Minister in exercise of powers conferred by section 5(2) of the Local Government Act 1972.

## reg 3 Interim Committee

The Interim Committee is deemed to be the duly constituted Council of the town of Nasinu and may perform and discharge all the rights, privileges, powers, duties and functions listed in or conferred or imposed on the Council by the Act except Parts 2 and 3 and is deemed to be the Council for the purpose of any other written law.

[reg 3 am LN 49 of 2001 reg 2, 1 July 2001]

## reg 4 Directions

The Interim Committee must in the exercise of its powers, functions and duties under these Regulations act in accordance with any general or special directions issued by the Minister.

## reg 5 Duration

These Regulations remain in force until a mayor is elected after an election is held under the Local Government Act 1972 to elect the councillors of the Nasinu Town Council.

## reg 6 First election

The first election of the town of Nasinu must be held before the end of year 2001.
