# NADI (CONTROL AND USE OF STREETS) BY-LAWS 1976

Nadi (Control and Use of Streets) By-laws 1976 (LGN 3 of 1976) commenced on 13 February 1976, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Nadi (Control and Use of Streets) By-laws 1976.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Council** means the Nadi Town Council;

**town clerk** means the town clerk appointed by the Council; and

**town engineer** means the town engineer appointed by the Council.

## by-law 3 Action prohibited

No person shall in any street—

(a) place, leave or deposit or permit to be placed, left or deposited any derelict vehicle, glass, refuse, rubbish or any noisome or offensive matter, save under the authority of the Council or, in accordance with the requirements of any law for the time being in force;

(b) wash, repair, grease, dismantle or assemble any vehicle otherwise than in the case of an emergency;

(c) paint any vehicle;

(d) leave any vehicle or any box, crate, barrel or package so as to form an obstruction;

(e) leave any matter or material of an inflammable nature;

(f) wilfully or maliciously damage or destroy any tree, shrub, plant, building or structure, the property of the Council;

(g) wilfully or maliciously extinguish or damage any street lamp or damage any post or power pole;

(h) park or stop an omnibus on any road, street or laneway other than a road, street, laneway or area specified for such parking or stopping;

(i) encumber or obstruct such street in any manner not hereinbefore described.

## by-law 4 Articles falling from vehicles

(1) It shall be the duty of the driver of any vehicle to take all reasonable precautions to ensure that no article, substance or material shall fall from or be deposited by such vehicle on any street.

(2) In the event of any such article, substance or material falling from or becoming deposited by any vehicle the driver thereof shall take all reasonable steps to safeguard other traffic on the street and to remove such article, substance or material immediately thereafter.

(3) In the event of failure to remove any such article, substance or material the Council may remove it and the cost thereof may be recovered from the person responsible for its removal.

## by-law 5 Crossing of gutters etc

(1) Without the prior written permission of the town engineer, no planks or other material shall be placed by any person in or across any gutter or ditch or against any kerb for the purpose of enabling vehicles to make a crossing of such gutter, ditch or kerb. In granting any such permission, the town engineer may, in his or her discretion, require that the applicant therefore furnish security to an amount considered by the town engineer to be reasonable.

(2) No person shall drive any vehicle across any gutter or ditch or on any footpath unless a suitable crossing has been constructed for the purpose and has been approved by the town engineer under the provisions of paragraph (1) .

## by-law 6 Control of motor vehicles without pneumatic tyres

No person shall drive or take any motor vehicle without pneumatic tyres upon any street unless authorised by the Council.

## by-law 7 Control of fireworks

No person shall discharge any firework, cracker or explosive material in a street or public place without having first obtained permission in writing from the Council, provided that no permit shall be required under this by-law for the discharge of fireworks or crackers on New Year's Day, 5 November, Diwali and the Hindu New Year.

## by-law 8 Street trading

(1) Without a permit in writing obtained from the town clerk and payment of such fee as the Council may by resolution prescribe, no person shall use any street or public place for the purpose of—

(a) distributing any pamphlet or advertising matter;

(b) the sale of tickets; or

(c) the carrying on of the business of commercial photography.

(2) Any permit granted under the provisions of paragraph (1) may be granted for such period of time and upon such terms and conditions as the Council may consider appropriate.
