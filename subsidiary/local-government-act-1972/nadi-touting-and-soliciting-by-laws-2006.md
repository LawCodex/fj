# NADI (TOUTING AND SOLICITING) BY-LAWS 2006

Nadi (Touting and Soliciting) By-laws 2006 (LN 80 of 2006) commenced on 4 August 2006, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Nadi (Touting and Soliciting) By-laws 2006.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws—

**authorised person** means an officer of the council appointed in that behalf in writing to give effect to these By-laws and includes a police officer;

**business** means employment, occupation, profession, trade or any commercial activity carried out or undertaken by a person for gainful or valuable livelihood;

**Council** means Nadi Town Council;

**employ** includes employment of a person for payment of salaries, wages or allowances for any other direct or indirect benefit or reward;

**person** includes any company or association or body of persons, corporate or unincorporated; and

**public place** has the same meaning as in the Public Order Act 1969 .

## by-law 3 Touting and soliciting

(1) No person shall—

(a) directly or indirectly tout or solicit for any kind of business in a public place for his or her personal benefit; or

(b) employ, hire, procure or in any manner counsel another person to overtly tout or solicit for any kind of business in a public place whether for his or her own benefit or for any other person.

(2) Paragraph (1)(a) and (b) do not apply to any person who distributes printed—

(a) notices or pamphlets;

(b) advertisement booklets, or

(c) any other useful information material;

in an orderly conduct in a public place.

## by-law 4 Directive by authorised person

An authorised person may direct any person reasonably suspected to be touting or soliciting or attempting to tout or solicit for business of any kind in a public place to desist from doing so and leave the place.

[by-law 4 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 170, 1 December 2016]

## by-law 5 Offence and penalty

Any person who contravenes by-law 3(1)(a) or (b) or fails or refuses to comply with the directions given by an authorised person under by-law 4 commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $400 or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding 6 months.

## by-law 6 Evidence in legal proceedings

In any proceedings for an offence against these By-laws, a statement in a complaint that a place is a public place shall be evidence of that fact.
