# BA WARDS ORDER 1968

Ba Wards Order 1968 (LGN 12 of 1968) commenced on 18 October 1968, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Ba Wards Order 1973 (LGN 29 of 1973) | 26 October 1973 |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Ba Wards Order 1968.

## O 2 Division of Ba into wards

The town of Ba is hereby divided into 5 wards, designated as the Varodoli Ward, the Central Ward, the Rarawai Ward, the Yalalevu Ward and the Namosau Ward.

## O 3 Boundaries of wards

The boundaries of the said wards shall be as follows—

(a) Varadoli Ward — comprising all that portion of Ba situated on the eastern side of the Ba River and to the east of Kings Road, Nukudrala Street and Koronubu Street and its prolongation southward to the southern boundary of the town;

(b) Central Ward — comprising all that portion of the town of Ba, situated on the eastern side of the Ba River and to the west of Nukudrala Street and Koronubu Street and its prolongation southward to the southern boundary of the town, and to the north of a line being 4 chains south of Kings Road and running parallel to the said Kings Road;

(c) Rarawai Ward — comprising all that portion of the town of Ba situated on the eastern side of the Ba River and to the south of a line being 4 chains south of the Kings Road and running parallel to the said Kings Road;

(d) Yalalevu Ward — comprising all that portion of the town of Ba situated on the western side of the Ba River and to the north of Namosau Creek;

(e) Namosau Ward — Comprising all that portion of the town of Ba situated on the western side of the Ba River and the south of Namosau Creek.
