# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (TAVUA TOWN COUNCIL) (FINANCIAL LOSS ALLOWANCES) REGULATIONS 2006

Local Government (Tavua Town Council) (Financial Loss Allowances) Regulations 2006 (LN 113 of 2006) commenced on 29 September 2006, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Tavua Town Council) (Financial Loss Allowances) Regulations 2006.

## reg 2 Financial loss allowance

A councillor of the Tavua Town Council is entitled to be paid an allowance at the rate of $15 per ordinary meeting or standing committee meeting as financial loss allowance in respect of loss of earnings suffered or incurred for the purpose of enabling the councillor to perform any approved duty as a councillor.
