# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (BA TOWN COUNCIL) (FINANCIAL LOSS ALLOWANCES AND TRAVELLING ALLOWANCES) REGULATIONS 2006

Local Government (Ba Town Council) (Financial Loss Allowances and Travelling Allowances) Regulations 2006 (LN 110 of 2006) commenced on 29 September 2006, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Ba Town Council) (Financial Loss Allowances and Travelling Allowances) Regulations 2006.

## reg 2 Financial loss allowances

A councillor of the Ba Town Council is entitled to an allowance at the rate of $15 per ordinary meeting or standing committee meeting as financial loss allowance in respect of loss earnings suffered or incurred for the purpose of enabling a councillor to perform any approved duty as a member of the Council.

## reg 3 Travelling allowances

A councillor of the Ba Town Council is entitled to an allowance at the rate of $10 for transportation for each ordinary meeting or standing committee meeting, if the Council does not provide for the transport for a councillor.
