# LABASA TOWN (NOISE PREVENTION AND ABATEMENT) BY-LAWS 2002

Labasa Town (Noise Prevention and Abatement) By-laws 2002 (LN 105 of 2002) commenced on 20 December 2002, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |
| Business Licensing (Repeal) Act 2020 (No 28 of 2020) | 1 August 2020 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Labasa Town (Noise Prevention and Abatement) By-laws 2002.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

(1) In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Council** means the Labasa Town Council;

**loud speaker** includes a megaphone or any other similar device for amplifying sound;

**noise** includes sound or vibration;

**noise level** includes the volume or intensity of the noise;

**occupier** means—

(a) the person in occupation or control of the premises; or

(b) where different parts of premises are occupied or controlled by different persons, the person in occupation or control of such part;

**offensive noise** means noise that, by reason of its level, nature, character or quality, or the time at which it is made, or any other circumstances, is likely—

(a) to be harmful to;

(b) to be offensive to; or

(c) to interfere unreasonably with the comfort or repose of a person who is—

(i) if the noise is made in premises that is not a public place, outside those premises;

(ii) if the noise is made in premises that is a public place, within or outside those premises;

**officer** means the town clerk and includes any person acting as the town clerk or any employee of the Council in the performance of any duty under these By-laws;

**public places** has the same meaning given to it under the Public Order Act 1969 ; and

**town rangers** means the employees of the Council appointed under the Local Government Act 1972 .

(2) In these By-laws, a reference to making or emitting noise includes a reference to causing, permitting or allowing the making or emission of noise.

## by-law 3 Musical instruments

(1) No person shall sound or play upon any musical or noisy instrument or operate a loud speaker so as to cause offensive noise.

(2) For the purpose of paragraph (1) , sound measure level of 70 dB measured one metre from the point of origin shall be deemed to be offensive noise.

## by-law 4 Equipment etc

(1) No person shall operate any equipment, machine, tools and plant so as to cause offensive noise.

(2) For the purpose of paragraph (1) , sound measure level of 85 dB measured one metre from the point of origin is to be deemed to be offensive noise.

## by-law 5 Application for a permit

Any person desiring to use any equipment, plant machinery, tools or musical instrument referred to in by-laws 3 and 4 for short spells in order to undertake essential work which is likely to emit excess noise shall apply to the Council in Form 1 set out in Schedule 1 with an application fee prescribed by the Council.

## by-law 6 Power to approve permit

(1) Upon receipt of an application referred in by-law 5 , the town clerk shall determine the application and may approve or reject the application.

(2) The approval shall be granted in Form 2 set out in Schedule 1.

## by-law 7 Noise abatement notice

(1) Any person committing an offence under by-law 3 or 4 may be issued with a noise abatement notice in Form 3 set out in Schedule 1 by the town ranger or the authorised officer of the Council requiring the person to cease from making such offensive noise or may require the person to do such other things as considered as necessary to reduce the noise level.

[para (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 130, 1 December 2016]

(2) Without affecting the generality of paragraph (1) , the town ranger or the authorised officer of the Council may require the person to reduce the noise level in one or more of the following ways—

(a) by cessation of the noise or by control implemented at the source of the noise;

(b) by the acoustic treatment of buildings or other structures;

(c) by the process of shielding or barriers to restrict the emission of noise from premises;

(d) by the restriction of the time or place or manner of operation giving rise to the noise; or

(e) by the progressive phasing out of noise or the progressive phasing out or control of activities that produce the noise.

(3) Where any person issued with a noise abatement notice fails to comply with the notice, the town ranger may confiscate the instrument making such offensive noise.

(4) For the purposes of investigating any complaints, the town ranger shall have the powers to enter any premises to measure the noise level and no person shall unreasonably obstruct the town ranger attending to his or her duties.

## by-law 8 Motor mowers on Sundays

No person shall operate a motor mower on a Sunday any earlier than 8.00 am and not later than 8.00 pm in residential areas.

## by-law 9 Penalty

Any person failing to comply with any part of the provisions of these By-laws shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $400 and to a further fine of $10 for every day during which such contravention or failure to comply continues.

[by-law 9 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 130, 1 December 2016]

## by-law 10 Exemption

These By-laws do not apply to noise emitting out of operations and functions stipulated in Schedule 2 .

## by-law 11 Repeal

The Labasa (Noise Prevention) by-laws are repealed.

### SCHEDULE 2 EXEMPTIONS

#### cl

(1) To the operations of a loud speaker for military or police purposes.

#### cl

(2) To the sounding or playing of any musical or noisy instrument or the operation of any loud speaker in a procession or public assembly if permission for such instrument or loud speaker has been given under the provisions of the Public Order Act 1969 .

#### cl

(3) To the operation of any loud speaker in case of emergency.
