# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (ADMINISTRATORS) REGULATIONS 1977

Local Government (Administrators) Regulations 1977 (LN 157 of 1977) commenced on 28 October 1977, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Administrators) Regulations 1977.

(2) These Regulations shall apply to any administrator appointed by order of the Minister in the exercise of his or her powers under section 131A of the Act.

## reg 2 Rights, functions and duties of administrator

(1) Subject to regulation 3 , an administrator shall be deemed to be the duly constituted council of the municipality and shall have, perform and discharge all the rights, privileges, powers, duties and functions vested in or conferred or imposed on the council, the mayor and any officer of the council by the Act or by any other written law.

(2) Where 2 or more persons are appointed to be administrators the Minister shall in the order making the appointment designate one of such administrators to be the chief administrator and the administrator so designated shall be deemed for the purposes of the Act and any other written law to be the mayor of the municipality.

## reg 3 Directions

In the exercise of his or her powers, duties and functions under these Regulations an administrator shall act in accordance with any general or special directions issued by the Minister.

## reg 4 Remuneration

(1) An administrator shall be entitled to receive such remuneration as the Minister shall from time to time determine.

(2) The remuneration of an administrator shall be met from the municipal fund of the municipality.

(3) No remuneration shall be payable to an administrator who is a public officer.
