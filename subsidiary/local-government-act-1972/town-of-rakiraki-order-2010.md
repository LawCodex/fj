# TOWN OF RAKIRAKI ORDER 2010

Town of Rakiraki Order 2010 (LN 77 of 2010) commenced on 1 July 2010, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Town of Rakiraki Order 2010.

## O 2 Declaration of the town of Rakiraki

The area defined in the Schedule to this Order is declared to be a town for the purpose of the Local Government Act 1972 and is to be known as the town of Rakiraki with effect from 1 July 2010.
