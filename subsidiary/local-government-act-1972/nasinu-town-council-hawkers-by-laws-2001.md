# NASINU TOWN COUNCIL (HAWKERS) BY-LAWS 2001

Nasinu Town Council (Hawkers) By-laws 2001 (LN 12 of 2001) commenced on 28 February 2001, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Business Licensing (Repeal) Act 2020 (No 28 of 2020) | 1 August 2020 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Nasinu Town Council (Hawkers) By-laws 2001.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Council** means the Nasinu Town Council;

**goods** include beads and curios and articles of food or produce grown in Fiji;

**hawker** means a person who by going from place to place sells goods carried on his or her person or from a handcart, box, basket, bag, tray and the like, or from boat, horse-drawn vehicle, bicycle, motor vehicle or any other type of conveyance;

**officer** means the town clerk and also any person acting as such for time being, or any health inspector appointed by the Council or any officer or employee of the Council in the performance of any duty under these By-laws; and

**town clerk** means the town clerk appointed by the Council.

## by-law 3 Hawkers to be licensed

No person shall within the town follow the calling of a hawker or hawk for sale any produce, livestock, or other goods whatsoever unless he or she holds in his or her own name an unexpired hawker’s licence issued by the Council.

## by-law 4 Sale of pigeons and wild birds prohibited

No licence issued under these By-laws permits the sale of pigeons or wild birds.

## by-law 5 Application for licence

A person desirous of obtaining a hawker’s licence may apply, in person, in writing, to the town clerk in Form 1 set out in the Schedule.

## by-law 6 Council may refuse, cancel or suspend licence

The Council may refuse to grant a hawker’s licence to any person and may suspend or cancel the licence of any hawker for any good cause after giving the holder thereof due opportunity of being heard.

## by-law 7 Effect of licence

The town clerk, upon receiving an application under by-law 5 and subject to such directions as he or she may receive from the Council, may issue to the applicant, a licence in Form 2 set out in the Schedule upon payments of relevant fees prescribed by the Council.

[by-law 7 am Act 28 of 2020 s 4 and Sch, 1 August 2020]

## by-law 8 Effect of licence

A hawker’s licence issued by the Council under by-law 7 shall authorise only the hawking and sale of goods endorsed on the licence and in the area specified in the licence.

## by-law 9 Duration of licence

A hawker’s licence issued under these By-laws is valid for one year from the date of issue.

## by-law 10 Duties and obligations of hawkers

A licensed hawker shall—

(a) at all times when hawking carry his or her licence with him or her and shall on demand produce the licence to any police officer or officer of the Council;

(b) if by remaining stationary in any road, street or place, he or she shall cause any obstruction to pedestrian or other traffic, forthwith move away from such road, street or place in such manner as effectively to remove such obstructions;

(c) when using a handcart or vehicle keep such handcart or vehicle as near as practicable to the left-hand side of the road or street;

(d) keep his or her boat, basket, tray, utensils and cart or vehicle clean to the satisfaction of any health inspector appointed by the Council;

(e) prevent any goods intended for human consumption from coming into contact with or being exposed to any dirt, filth, contagion, infection, flies or anything likely to render such goods injurious to health;

(f) cease to hawk any goods intended for human consumption while he or she is suffering from any infection, contagious or offensive disease.

## by-law 11 Penalty

A person who contravenes a provision of these By-laws, is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100.
