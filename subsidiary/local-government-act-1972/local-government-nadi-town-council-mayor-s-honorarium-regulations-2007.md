# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (NADI TOWN COUNCIL) (MAYOR'S HONORARIUM) REGULATIONS 2007

Local Government (Nadi Town Council) (Mayor's Honorarium) Regulations 2007 (LN 30 of 2007) commenced on 23 February 2007, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Nadi Town Council) (Mayor's Honorarium) Regulations 2007.

## reg 2 Mayor’s honorarium allowance

The mayor of Nadi Town Council is entitled to be paid a mayor's honorarium allowance at the rate of $3,600 per annum in respect of loss of earnings suffered or incurred for the purpose of enabling the mayor to carry out his or her duties as a mayor.
