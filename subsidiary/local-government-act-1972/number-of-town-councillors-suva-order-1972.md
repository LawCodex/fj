# NUMBER OF TOWN COUNCILLORS (SUVA) ORDER 1972

Number of Town Councillors (Suva) Order 1972 (LGN 7 of 1972) commenced on 6 October 1972, as amended by:

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Number of Town Councillors (Suva) Order 1972.

## O 2 Number of members

The number of members of the town councils named in the Schedule and, where the towns are divided into wards, the number of members for each ward shall, with effect from the next general elections to such councils, be as specified in the Schedule .
