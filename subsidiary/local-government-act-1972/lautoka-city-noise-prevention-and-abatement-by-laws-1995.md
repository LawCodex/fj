# LAUTOKA CITY (NOISE PREVENTION AND ABATEMENT) BY-LAWS 1995

Lautoka City (Noise Prevention and Abatement) By-laws 1995 (LN 111 of 1995) commenced on 13 October 1995, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Business Licensing (Repeal) Act 2020 (No 28 of 2020) | 1 August 2020 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Lautoka City (Noise Prevention and Abatement) By-laws 1995.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

(1) In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Council** means the Lautoka City Council;

**level**, in relation to noise, includes the volume or intensity of the noise;

**noise** includes sound and vibration;

**occupier**,in relation to any premises, means the person in occupation or control of the premises and, in relation to any premises where different parts are occupied or controlled by different persons, means, in relation to any such part, the person in occupation or control of that part;

**offensive noise** means noise that, by reason of its level, nature, character or quality, or the time at which it is made, or any other circumstances, is likely—

(a) to be harmful to;

(b) to be offensive to; or

(c) to interfere unreasonably with the comfort or repose of, a person who is—

(i) if the noise is made in premises that is not a public place, outside those premises; or

(ii) if the noise is made in premises that is a public place, within or outside those premises;

**public places** means those places as ascribed under the Public Order Act 1969 ; and

**town rangers** means the officers of the Council appointed under the Local Government Act 1972 .

(2) In these By-laws, a reference (however expressed) to making or emitting noise includes a reference to causing, permitting or allowing the making or emission of noise.

## by-law 3 Musical instruments etc

(1) No person shall sound or play upon any musical or noisy instrument or operate a loud speaker so as to cause offensive noise.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1) , sound measure level of 70 dB measured one metre from the point of origin shall be deemed to be offensive noise.

## by-law 4 Equipment etc

(1) No person shall operate any equipment, machine, tools and plant so as to cause offensive noise.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1) , sound measure level of 90 dB measured one metre from the point of origin shall be deemed to be offensive noise.

## by-law 5 Schedule 1

Any person desiring to use any equipment, plant machinery, tools or musical instrument referred to in by-laws 3 and 4 for short spells in order to undertake essential work which is likely to emit excess noise shall make an application to the Council in the form prescribed in Schedule 1 with an application fee prescribed by the Council.

## by-law 6 Schedule 2

Upon receipt of the application referred in by-law 5 , the town clerk shall determine the application and may approve or reject the same and approval shall be granted in the form prescribed in Schedule 2 .

## by-law 7 Noise Abatement Notice — Schedule 3

(1) Any person committing an offence under by-laws 3 and 4 may be issued with a noise abatement notice in the form prescribed in Schedule 3 by the town ranger of the Council requiring the person to cease forthwith making such offensive noise or may require the person to do such other things as considered necessary to reduce the noise level.

(2) Without in anyway affecting the generality of paragraph (1) , the town ranger may require the person to reduce the noise level in one or more of the following ways—

(a) by cessation of the noise or by control implemented at the source of the noise;

(b) by the acoustic treatment of buildings or other structures;

(c) by a process of shielding or barriers to restrict the emission of noise from premises;

(d) by the restriction of the time or place or manner of operations giving rise to the noise; or

(e) by the progressive phasing out of the noise or the progressive phasing out or control activities that produce the noise.

(3) Where any person issued with a noise abatement notice fails to comply with the notice, the town ranger may confiscate the instrument making such offensive noise.

(4) For the purposes of investigating any complaint, the town ranger shall have the powers to enter any premises to measure the noise level and no person shall unreasonably obstruct the town ranger attending to his or her duties.

## by-law 8 Motor mowers on Sundays

No person shall operate a motor mower on a Sunday any earlier than 8.00 am and no later than 8.00 pm in residential areas.

## by-law 9 Penalty

Any person failing to comply with any of the provisions of these By-laws shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $500 and to a further fine of $20 for every day during which period such contravention or failure to comply continues.

## by-law 10 Exemptions

These By-laws shall not apply to noise emitting out of operations and functions stipulated in Schedule 4 .

## by-law 11 Repeal

The Lautoka (Noise Prevention) By-laws 1966 is hereby repealed.

### SCHEDULE 4 EXEMPTIONS

(1) To the operation of a loud speaker for military or police purposes.

(2) To the surrounding or playing of any musical or noisy instrument or the operation of any loud speaker in a procession or public assembly if permission for such instrument or loud speaker has been given under the provisions of the Public Order Act 1969 .

(3) To the operation of any loud speaker in case of emergency.
