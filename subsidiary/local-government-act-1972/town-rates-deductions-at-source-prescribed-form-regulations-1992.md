# TOWN RATES (DEDUCTIONS AT SOURCE) (PRESCRIBED FORM) REGULATIONS 1992

Town Rates (Deductions at Source) (Prescribed Form) Regulations 1992 (LN 51 of 1992) commenced on 8 May 1992, as amended by:

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as Town Rates (Deductions at Source) (Prescribed Form) Regulations 1992.

## reg 2 Form of order requiring deduction of town rates at source

A notice directed to and served upon any person in pursuance to section 78A(1) of the Local Government Act 1972, requiring the person to deduct town rates at source shall be in the form set out in the Schedule.

## 

### SCHEDULE FORM OF NOTICE REQUIRING DEDUCTION OF TOWN RATES AT SOURCE

................................ CITY COUNCIL

To: ................................................

................................................

................................................

Dear Sir,

1. In accordance with the provisions of section 78A(1) of the Local Government Act 1972 which is printed on the back hereof and forms part of this notice, you are required to deduct:

$................................................

from any moneys including salary or wages payable or to become payable from the date of this notice, to the owner of land whose name is shown below.

2. The amounts so deducted must be paid to the Council within one week of the end of each calendar month. Please forward your remittance with a schedule showing the full name and amount deducted to each ratepayer.

3. Deductions must be made notwithstanding any objection by the rate payer.

4. If the owner of land leaves your employment or you have no moneys payable to him or her you are required to notify the Council within 14 days. If possible please also advise the name and address of his or her new employer.

5. Please acknowledge this notice by completing and returning the duplicate copy of this notice.
