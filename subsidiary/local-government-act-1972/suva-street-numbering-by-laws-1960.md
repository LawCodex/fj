# SUVA (STREET NUMBERING) BY-LAWS 1960

Suva (Street Numbering) By-laws 1960 (LN 58 of 1960) commenced on 27 May 1960, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Suva (Street Numbering) By-laws 1960.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**city** means the city of Suva;

**city engineer** means the city engineer, or any person duly authorised by the Council to act as city engineer;

**Council** means the Suva City Council;

**occupier** means the person in occupation of the holding or building in respect of which the word is used, or having the charge, management or control thereof either on his or her own account or as agent of another person, but does not include a lodger;

**owner** in relation to land whereon there are premises requiring to be numbered means a person other than a mortgagee not in possession who is for the time being entitled to dispose of the fee simple of the land, whether in possession or in reversion, and includes also a person holding or entitled to the rents, profits or use of the land under a registered lease or registered agreement; and

**street** means any road, lane, footpath, square, alley or passage whether a thoroughfare or not, over which the public has a right of way and includes any road, footway or passage, open court or open alley, used or intended to be used as a means of access to 2 or more houses or shops whether the public has a right of way thereover or not.

## by-law 3 Regulation of numbering of buildings

The city engineer of the Council or his or her designated assistant shall have the power and it shall be his or her duty to plan and regulate the numbering of all buildings in the city.

## by-law 4 Buildings to have distinguishing numbers

The owners or occupiers of land on which buildings are erected and which have entrances or frontages to a street shall for the purpose of distinguishing them, mark the buildings or fences or street frontages with such numbers as the Council may from time to time direct or approve, so that such markings may be readily seen from the road, and shall renew such markings as often as they are destroyed, obliterated or defaced.

## by-law 5 Entrances to buildings to be numbered

All main entrances to building premises shall be numbered and the city engineer or his or her designated assistant shall assign to each building or premises its proper number or numbers and furnish, free of charge to the owner or occupier, a certificate designating each number and location.

## by-law 6 Numbering at owner's expense

All buildings shall be numbered at the expense of the owner.

## by-law 7 Offence

If any such owner or occupier neglects for 30 days after written notice to him or her from the Council to mark any such building, or fence or road frontage with such number and form as the Council may direct or approve, or to renew the marking thereof as aforesaid or who shall place, maintain or allow to maintain thereon any number or numbers other than that assigned by the city engineer or his or her designated assistant, shall be guilty of an offence under these By-laws.

## by-law 8 Occupier may recover expense of numbering from owner

Where the occupier of any land incurs expenses in complying with the requirements of any notice issued to him or her under by-law 7 he or she shall be entitled to recover such expenses from the owner of the land.

## by-law 9 Destruction etc of marking

Any person who without the authority of the Council destroys, pulls down, obliterates or defaces any street number marking shall be guilty of an offence under these By-laws.

## by-law 10 Penalty

Any person who is convicted of any offence under these By-laws shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $10 for each offence and in the case of a continuing offence to a fine not exceeding $2 for each day during which the offence continues after conviction.
