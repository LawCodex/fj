## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Sigatoka (Market Fees) By-laws 1985.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

Words and phrases defined in the Sigatoka (Market and Merchandising) By-laws 1985 shall have the same meaning in these By-laws.

## by-law 3 Fees

The fees specified in the Schedule shall apply in the market.
