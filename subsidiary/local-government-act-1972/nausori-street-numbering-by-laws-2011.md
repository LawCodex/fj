# NAUSORI (STREET NUMBERING) BY-LAWS 2011

Nausori (Street Numbering) By-laws 2011 (LN 126 of 2011) commenced on 1 November 2011, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## by-law 1 Short title and commencement

These By-laws may be cited as the Nausori (Street Numbering) By-laws 2011 and shall commence on 1 November 2011.

## by-law 2 Application

These By-laws shall apply only to such part or parts of the Nausori town as the Council shall direct by notice published in the Gazette and in a newspaper circulated within the Nausori town boundary.

## by-law 3 Buildings to have distinguishing numbers

The owners or occupiers of land, on which buildings are erected or which are subdivided into building allotments and have frontages to or entrances from roads, shall for the purpose of distinguishing them—

(a) mark the buildings, fences or road frontages with such numbers and in such manner and form as the Nausori Town Council may direct or approve so that such markings may be readily seen from the road; and

(b) renew such markings as often as they are destroyed, obliterated or defaced.

## by-law 4 Offence

If any such owner or occupier of land, after being served with a written notice from the Nausori Town Council to—

(a) mark any such building, fence or road frontage with such number and in such a manner and form as the Council directs; or

(b) renew such markings,

fails to comply with the written notice within 7 days of its service, he or she shall be guilty of an offence under these By-laws.

## by-law 5 Numbering at owner's expense

If the occupier of land incurs an expense when complying with the requirements of any notice given to him or her under by-law 4, he or she shall be entitled to recover such expenses from the owner of the land.

## by-law 6 Destruction etc of marking

Any person who, without the authority of the Council, destroys, pulls down, obliterates or defaces any such marking or who makes such number other than in accordance with this by-law, shall be guilty of an offence under these By-laws and the Council may cause any unlawful marking to be obliterated or destroyed.

## by-law 7 Penalty

Any owner or occupier of land, who is convicted of any offence under these By-laws, shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $100 for each offence and in the case of a continuing offence, to a fine not exceeding $200 each day during which the offence continues after conviction.
