## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Suva City Boundaries (Alterations) Order 1994.

## O 2 Alteration of Suva City Boundaries

(1) The boundaries of the City of Suva are hereby altered by expansion to include:

All the area of land in the Tikina of Suva in the Province of Rewa and in the Tikina of Naitasiri in the Province of Naitasiri lying adjacent to the existing northern boundaries of the City of Suva and bounded generally by:

Commencing from the southest east point of survey plan R562 and the following the northern boundary of the City of Suva in westerly direction up to the western boundary of Bal S371; thence on the west by the western boundaries of Tamavua Heights; on the north bounded by the southern boundary of Wailoku Road to Princess Road junction; thence in the northerly direction following the western side of Princes Road up to the southern point of Bal R1552; thence along the eastern boundary of Bal R1166; thence along the western boundary of Bal 1247; thence along the northern boundaries of Bal 1247 up to the western boundary of Princess Road; thence across Princes Road along the western boundary of R1694; thence along the northern boundary of R1694 in the easterly direction; thence along the eastern boundary of R1694; thence in the north westerly direction for a distance of 22 chains to the eastern side of Princes Road for a distance of 2 chains; thence along the north western side of Cunningham Road and a direct line from the said Cunningham Road eastward to the right bank of the Wainitarawau Creek to Kings Road; thence across Kings Road and following the eastern boundaries of the subdivision represented by surveys on survey plan 1044; thence following the existing northern boundaries of the City of Suva in the northerly direction; thence following the existing northern boundary of the City of Suva in the westerly direction for a distance 7 chains; thence in the north westerly direction along the western boundaries of survey represented by survey plan R2063 for a distance of 108 chains; thence in the northerly direction for a distance of 20 chains; thence in the north westerly direction for a distance of 5 chains to boundary R1653; thence following the eastern boundary of R1653 in the southerly direction for a distance 6 chains; thence in the westerly direction along the southern boundary of R1653 for a distance of 9 chains; thence along the eastern boundary of R1907 in the southerly direction for a distance of 16 chains; thence along the north eastern boundary of R2010 and then the following the northern and the following eastern and southern boundary of S01287 to meet the eastern boundary of R2009 for a distance of 8 chains; thence in the southerly direction for 4 chains; thence along the southern boundary of survey plan R2010 up to the southern boundary of DP2314 up to the north east point of R869; thence along the eastern boundary of R869 in the easterly direction along the northern boundary of R562; thence along the south eastern boundary of R562 to meet the City of Suva Town Planning Boundary, being the starting point. The above areas are more particularly delineated in red on PP564 kept at the office of the Director of Lands and Surveyor General and copies are available for viewing at the offices of the permanent secretary responsible for local government, Suva City Council, Commissioner Central Division and the District Officer, Suva.
