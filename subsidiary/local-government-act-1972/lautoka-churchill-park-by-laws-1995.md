# LAUTOKA (CHURCHILL PARK) BY-LAWS 1995

Lautoka (Churchill Park) By-laws 1995 (LN 82 of 1995) commenced on 7 July 1995, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Lautoka (Churchill Park) (Amendment) By-laws 1996 (LN 83 of 1996) | 9 August 1996 |
| Lautoka (Churchill Park) (Amendment) By-laws 1998 (LN 41 of 1998) | 12 March 1998 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as Lautoka (Churchill Park) By-laws 1995.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws unless the context otherwise requires—

**Council** means Lautoka City Council;

**park** means Churchill Park contained in State Lease No 3163 and bounded by Narara Parade on the northern side, Yawini Street on the eastern side, Verona Street on the south side and outfall drain on the western side; and

**park ranger** means park leading hand appointed by the Council.

## by-law 3 Application to use park

(1) Any person desiring to use the park shall apply to the Council at least 14 days prior to the park's proposed usage.

(2) All applications to use the park shall be in a form set out in the Schedule to these By-laws.

## by-law 4 Determination of application

The town clerk, acting on behalf of the Council, shall approve or reject the application without giving any reason for his or her decision.

## by-law 5 Condition approval of application

Any approval granted by the Council through the town clerk shall be subject to such condition as the Council may from time to time decide.

## by-law 6 Revocation of approval

The Council may, by giving 7 days notice, revoke any approval granted in cases where a national or international event coincides on the same date.

## by-law 7 Fee

(1) There shall be a fee charged for the use of the park which shall be paid in advance.

(2) The fee shall be as determined by the Council from time to time and such fee maybe in form of a percentage of gatetakings, a fixed fee, a fee on per hour of use or a fee based on cost to the Council for such hire plus on-cost as determined by the Council.

(3) The Council may where it considers expedient for proper accounting purposes sell the tickets for entry into the park on behalf of the hirer at the hirer’s expense and remit the sum collected less Council's fee with unsold tickets to the hirer within 7 days.

[para (3) insrt LN 41 of 1998 by-law 2, 12 March 1998]

## by-law 8 Hire charge not able to be determined in advance

Where the hire charge of the park is not able to be determined in advance the Council may require the hirer to pay a cash deposit or obtain a bank bond of such amount as the Council considers reasonable.

## by-law 9 Use of other facilities

The hirer of the park may use other facilities in the park with the prior approval of the Council and on such terms and conditions as decided by the Council.

## by-law 10 Opening of gates

The hirer of the park shall ensure that gates are opened before the scheduled time of commencement of the event so that the patrons may enter without any undue inconvenience.

## by-law 11 Rainy days

No person shall be allowed to use the park on wet days, and every approval granted shall be deemed to be cancelled if the park is wet for use on the day of the event.

## by-law 12 Public liability insurance cover

The hirer shall take out a public liability insurance cover from a reputable under writer to cover any mishaps, and a copy of the insurance cover shall be given to the Council in advance.

## by-law 13 Restricted use

The hirer shall use the park for the purpose it was proposed by the Council and shall vacate the park as soon as practicable upon completion of business in a neat and tidy condition to the satisfaction of the Council.

## by-law 14 Closure of park for maintenance

The Council may close the park for maintenance purposes including watering of turf, maintenance to pavilion or other amenities and during such closure no person shall be allowed to enter the park.

## by-law 15 Public address system

The public address system may be used with prior approval of the Council and unless approved otherwise the sound level shall not exceed 100 dB measured 100 m from the speakers between the hours of 8:00 am to 8:00 pm and 70 dB between the hours of 8:00 pm to 8:00 am on the following morning.

## by-law 16 Prohibition

(1) No person shall cut, deface, remove, or otherwise injure or damage any of the lawns, trees, shrubs, flowers, plants, seats, gates, posts, fences, hessian or other erections upon or bounding the park or write thereon or affix any bill or stencil mark thereon.

(2) No person shall bring into the park any animal or vehicle (including motor cars and motorcycles) or any dog which is not physically held in control.

(3) No person, not being a player, shall be allowed to enter or remain upon any cricket ground, tennis court, football, or hockey ground or other places set apart by the Council for the purpose of playing cricket, tennis, football, hockey or other game or sport, within the said park during the continuance of a game or sport if, by entering or remaining, he or she shall in any way interfere with the game or sport.

(4) No performance or entertainment of any kind or public meeting of assemblage of persons shall be permitted in the said park without the permission of the Council.

(5) No person shall remove any rock, stone or other material from the Park without the leave of the Council.

(6) No person shall cast away, throw or deposit, or cause or suffer to be cast away, thrown or deposited in the park or reserve except in receptacles provided for that purpose any fruit skins, rind or peel, or any bottles, tins or cans or the like, or any handbills, printed papers, wastepaper, or paper of any kind whatsoever or plastic wrapper, or any other refuse or waste materials.

(7) No tents, booths or stall shall be erected nor any article sold or exposed for sale, on the park without the consent of the Council and except upon such terms and conditions as may be determined by the Council.

(8) No person shall bring into the park any glass, bottles or containers and no person shall sell or offer to sell any soft drink or other matter in glass containers in the park.

[para (8) am LN 83 of 1996 by-law 2, 9 August 1996]

## by-law 17 Disorderly behaviour

Every person who shall, within the park, be in a state of intoxication, or shall behave in a disorderly manner, or shall create, or take part in, any disturbance or shall obstruct any workman, or, not being a player, shall interfere with any game or sport may be removed by the ranger, overseer or servant of the Council or by a police officer, and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200.

## by-law 18 Non-payment of charges

Any person entering, or remaining upon the said park or upon any building or pavilion thereon, without having paid any lawful charge made for admission, or who shall neglect or refuse to pay that charge on demand, may be forthwith removed therefrom and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200.

## by-law 19 Loitering

Any person or people found loitering or acting in a suspicious manner in the park during working hours or after hours, and those who do not have proper permission to be there at the park shall be liable to be evicted from the park without notice and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200 or imprisonment or both the fine and imprisonment.

## by-law 20 Park ranger

The park shall be under the immediate control of the park ranger.

## by-law 21 Penalty

Any person who offends against any of the provisions of these By-laws shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200 and to a further fine of $20 for every day during which such contravention or failure to comply continues.

## by-law 22 By-laws repealed

The Churchill Park By-laws 1972 is hereby repealed.
