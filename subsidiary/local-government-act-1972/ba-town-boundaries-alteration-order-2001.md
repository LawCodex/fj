# BA TOWN BOUNDARIES (ALTERATION) ORDER 2001

Ba Town Boundaries (Alteration) Order 2001 (LN 22 of 2001) commenced on 12 April 2001, as amended by:

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Ba Town Boundaries (Alteration) Order 2001.

## O 2 Boundaries of the town of Ba

The boundaries of the town of Ba are hereby altered to include the following areas namely all that area of land in the Tikina of Ba and in the Province of Ba the boundaries of which are described hereunder.
