# LAUTOKA (RECREATIONAL RESERVE) BY-LAWS 1997

Lautoka (Recreational Reserve) By-laws 1997 (LN 106 of 1997)1 commenced on 3 October 1997, as amended by:

| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

1
This was rectified by the Corrigenda published on 24 October 2007.

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Lautoka (Recreational Reserve) By-laws 1997.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

(1) In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Council** means Lautoka City Council;

**litter** has the same meaning as ascribed under the Litter Act 2008 ;

[def am LN 99 of 2016 reg 145, 1 December 2016]

**recreational reserve** means all the parks and recreation reserves shown as such in the approved Lautoka Town Planning Scheme Town Plan in subdivided areas which is under the control, care and management of the Council but excludes Churchill Park;

**Town Clerk and Park Rangers** means officers of the Council appointed under the Local Government Act 1972.

(2) Any decision made by the Town Clerk under these By-laws shall be deemed to be a decision made by the Council.

## by-law 3 Recreational reserve open to all

Subject to other provisions in these By-laws, all recreational reserves shall be open for unhindered enjoyment by individual members of the public.

## by-law 4 Opening hours

The Council may by resolution decide on opening hours for any recreational reserves and no persons shall enter the recreational reserve whilst such recreational reserve is closed to the public.

## by-law 5 Prohibitions

No person shall in any recreational reserve—

(a) do any act or organise or play any game in a recreational reserve which would be likely to interfere with the safety or conduct of any person in the recreational reserve or surrounding residents;

(b) wilfully break a bottle, glass or glass receptable;

(c) throw, place or leave any bottle, glass or glass receptable, broken glass, nail or any other matter or things likely to endanger the safety of or cause injury to any person or animal or damage to any vehicle or property;

(d) do any such act which would be likely to injure, displace, pollute, foul, deface or disorder anything appertaining to a recreational reserve;

(e) injure or unnecessarily disturb by tree, shrub, flora, fauna;

(f) make or erect any building, fence, ditch or other structure of any kind;

(g) plant any tree, shrub, crop or vegetable of any kind;

(h) deposit any litter other than in litter bins or depositing their household garbage in litter bins provided for use by park patrons;

(i) drive, park or repair motor vehicles;

(j) light any fire;

(k) tether animals;

(l) drink liquor or any intoxicating substance;

(m) wilfully commit any act in violation of public decency or use profane, indecent, or obscene language to the annoyance of any other person.

## by-law 6 Additional prohibitions

No person shall without prior approval of the Council in any recreational reserve—

(a) set up or operate or use a loudspeaker or sound in any recreational reserve;

(b) hold public assembly for any purpose;

(c) exhibit advertisement, engage in a trade or business, distribute handbills or erect any structure.

## by-law 7 Regulation by notice

The Council may by resolution make special rules for individual parks and such rules shall be put in a notice board at a conspicuous place. The rules in the notice board shall be deemed to be rules made under these By-laws.

## by-law 8 Use of playground equipment

The Council may by notice in the board—

(a) set apart the whole or any part of a playground either generally or during specified days or times solely for the use by children of specified ages or of children accompanied by adults; and

(b) specify by whom, in what order, and for how long, at any one time, any applicant in a playground may be used, and regulate the orderly use of a playground.

## by-law 9 Special privileges

(1) The Council may from time to time by resolution grant special privileges and rights of user of, over the or in connection with the recreational reserve or portion thereof, or games, sports and purposes of recreation or amusement on payment of fees or otherwise.

(2) Where an area has been set apart pursuant to paragraph (1) a person using the area for which it is set apart may exclude any other person from the area to the extent that exclusion is reasonably necessary for that use.

## by-law 10 Maintenance of reserves

So far as is reasonably necessary for the purpose of carrying out improvements or repairs to or maintenance of a recreational reserve, or due to inclement weather, the Council may prohibit the entry into the recreational reserve and the park ranger may direct persons to leave the recreational reserve.

## by-law 11 Festivals

(1) Where any recreational reserve is hired for festivals, it shall be the responsibility of the hirer to ensure that all necessary permits, licence, approval is obtained for all structures, equipments and fittings.

(2) Without in any way affecting the generality of paragraph (1) , the following permit, licence and approval shall be deemed to be included—

(a) approval of Council’s Director Engineering Services for all structures;

(b) approval for temporary power supply;

(c) approval of Factories Inspector for all equipments, including ferris wheels;

(d) insurance cover from reputable underwriters.

## by-law 12 Park Rangers

(1) The recreational reserves shall be under the immediate management and control of Park Rangers approved by and responsible to the Council.

(2) Where the Park Ranger sees any infringement of these By-laws by any person, he or she may require such person to give his or her full name and full address.

(3) All persons using or being within the precincts of any recreational reserve shall obey all reasonable directions given by the Park Ranger for the purposes of observing the good order for the convenience and enjoyment of the public.

## by-law 13 Removal of offenders

A person committing an offence in a recreational reserve may be removed forthwith from the reserve by the park ranger or other authorised employee of the Council or any member of the police force without affecting that person’s liability to be subsequently prosecuted for that offence.

## by-law 14 Officers exempted

Nothing in these By-laws shall be deemed to prevent any officer or employee of the Council or any person authorised by the Council, from doing or pursuance of this duty any act or thing prohibited by these By-laws.

## by-law 15 Penalties

Any person who contravenes or fails to comply with any of the provisions of these By-laws shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $400 and to a further fine of $25 for every day during which such contravention or failure to comply continues.

## by-law 16 Repeal

The Lautoka (Parks and Reserves) by-laws are hereby repealed.
