# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (INQUIRIES) REGULATIONS 1977

Local Government (Inquiries) Regulations 1977 (LN 95 of 1977) commenced on 22 July 1977, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Inquiries) Regulations 1977.

(2) These Regulations shall apply to any inquiry instituted by the Minister in the exercise of his or her powers under section 130 or 131 of the Act.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**committee** means a committee of inquiry established under the provisions of regulation 3 ; and

**secretary** means the secretary to a committee.

## reg 3 Committee of inquiry

(1) For the purposes of an inquiry the Minister may appoint a committee which shall consist of not less than 2 but not more than 5 members, one of whom shall be nominated by the Minister as chairperson.

(2) The Minister may appoint a suitable person to be the secretary to a committee.

(3) Subject to the provisions of these Regulations a committee may regulate its own procedure.

## reg 4 Representation of council

(1) A council which is the subject of an inquiry shall be entitled to be heard at the inquiry and for that purpose may be represented by any member or officer of the council or by a legal practitioner.

(2) If witnesses are examined by the committee they may be cross-examined on behalf of the council.

(3) No documentary evidence shall be used against a council at an inquiry unless it has been previously supplied with a copy thereof or given access thereto.

## reg 5 Conduct of hearing

(1) An inquiry by a committee may be held either in public or in camera as the committee considers necessary or desirable in the public interest and to ensure the proper discharge of its functions under these Regulations.

(2) If any person who is to give evidence at any such inquiry held in public so requests at the inquiry or by a notice in writing served on the secretary before the date of the inquiry, the public shall be excluded from the inquiry while that person gives his or her evidence.

## reg 6 Proceedings of committee privileged

Any report, statement or other communication or record of any meeting, inquiry or proceedings which a committee may make in exercise of its functions or any member may make in performance of his or her duties, shall be privileged in that its production may not be compelled in any legal proceedings unless the chairperson of the committee certifies that such production is not against the public interest.

## reg 7 Protection of committee

No member of a committee shall be liable to any action or suit for any act done or omitted to be done in the *bona fide* execution of his or her duties under these Regulations.

## reg 8 Power to require attendance of witnesses etc

For the purposes of carrying out its powers, duties and functions under these Regulations a committee shall have the same powers and authority to summon witnesses and to admit and receive evidence as are conferred on the commissioners of a Commission of Inquiry by section 9 of the Commissions of Inquiry Act 1946 and the provisions of sections 14 and 17 of that Act shall apply *mutatis mutandis* in relation to the powers and authority vested in a committee under this regulation.

## reg 9 Attempting to influence committee

Any person who directly or indirectly, other than in the course of his or her duty, by himself or herself or by any other person in any manner whatsoever attempts to influence a committee in the proper exercise of its functions or duties shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100, or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 3 months or to both such fine and imprisonment.

## reg 10 Giving false information to committee

Any person who in connection with the exercise by a committee of its functions or duties wilfully gives to a committee any information which he or she knows to be false or does not believe to be true, or which he or she knows to be false by reason of the omission of any material particular, shall be guilty of an offence and on conviction shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $200 or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 6 months or to both such fine and imprisonment.

## reg 11 Interpretation of regulations 9 and 10

For the purpose of regulations 9 and 10 the term “committee” shall include a committee, any member of a committee, the secretary or any person or body of persons appointed to assist a committee in the exercise of its functions or duties.

## reg 12 Divulging official information

(1) No member of a committee, nor the secretary nor any other person shall, without the written permission of the Minister, publish or disclose to any person otherwise than in exercise of his or her official functions the contents of any document, communication or information whatsoever relating to or connected with the functions of a committee which has come to his or her knowledge in the course of his or her duties.

(2) Any person who knowingly acts in contravention of the provisions of this regulation shall be guilty of an offence and on conviction shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $200, or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 6 months or to both such fine and imprisonment.

## reg 13 Submission of report

As soon as practicable after the conclusion of an inquiry, a committee shall submit a written report of its findings to the Minister.
