# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (NADI TOWN COUNCIL) (DEPUTY MAYOR'S HONORARIUM) REGULATIONS 2007

Local Government (Nadi Town Council) (Deputy Mayor's Honorarium) Regulations 2007 (LN 31 of 2007) commenced on 23 February 2007, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Nadi Town Council) (Deputy Mayor's Honorarium) Regulations 2007.

## reg 2 Deputy mayor’s honorarium allowance

The deputy mayor of Nadi Town Council is entitled to be paid a deputy mayor's honorarium allowance at the rate of $1,440 per annum in respect of loss of earnings suffered or incurred for the purpose of enabling the deputy mayor to carryout duties in his or her capacity as a deputy mayor.
