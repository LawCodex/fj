1
This was rectified by the Corrigendum published on 28 July 1995.

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Labasa Town Council Financial Loss Allowance) Regulations 1995.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires “Council” means the Labasa Town Council.

## reg 3 Financial loss allowance

A member of the Council is entitled to an allowance at the rate of $25 per meeting as financial loss allowance in respect of loss of earnings suffered or incurred for the purpose of enabling a Councillor to perform any approved duty as a member of the Council.
