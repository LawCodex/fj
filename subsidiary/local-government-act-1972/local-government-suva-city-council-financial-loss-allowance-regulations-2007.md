# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (SUVA CITY COUNCIL) (FINANCIAL LOSS ALLOWANCE) REGULATIONS 2007

Local Government (Suva City Council) (Financial Loss Allowance) Regulations 2007 (LN 44 of 2007) commenced on 23 March 2007, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Suva City Council) (Financial Loss Allowance) Regulations 2007.

## reg 2 Financial loss allowance

A councillor of the Suva City Council is entitled to an allowance at the rate of $35 per ordinary meeting or standing committee meeting as financial loss allowance in respect of loss of earnings suffered or incurred for the purpose of enabling a councillor to perform any approved duty as a councillor.

## reg 3 Revocation

The Suva City Council (Financial Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations 1996 is revoked.
