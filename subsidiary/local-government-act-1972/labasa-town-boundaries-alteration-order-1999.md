# LABASA TOWN BOUNDARIES (ALTERATION) ORDER 1999

Labasa Town Boundaries (Alteration) Order 1999 (LN 82 of 1999) commenced on 18 June 1999, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Native Lands (Amendment) Decree 2011 (No 7 of 2011) | 1 March 2011 |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Labasa Town Boundaries (Alteration) Order 1999.

## O 2 Alteration of Labasa town boundaries

The boundaries of the town of Labasa are hereby altered to exclude the following—

All that area of iTaukei Reserve Claim No (46) known as Sawani (part of) owned by the mataqali Vuniwai in the district of Macuata in the province of Macuata, lying east of the Namara Road and bounded on the north by the south eastern boundary of M1205 and south boundary of M1129 (Nasea Tiri No 1), on the east by the western bank of Labasa River, on the south by northern boundary of M1368 (NL 4640) and on the west by the eastern boundary of Namara Road. It does not include the land of M1818 which is partially enclosed by this iTaukei Reserve land.

The area is more particularly delineated in yellow on the PP581 kept at the office of the Director of Lands and Surveyor General and copies are available for viewing in the office of the permanent secretary responsible for local government, Commissioner Northern, District Officer, Labasa and the Director, Town and Country Planning.
