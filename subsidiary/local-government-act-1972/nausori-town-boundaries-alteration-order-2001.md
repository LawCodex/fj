# NAUSORI TOWN BOUNDARIES (ALTERATION) ORDER 2001

Nausori Town Boundaries (Alteration) Order 2001 (LN 73 of 2001) commenced on 31 August 2001, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Nausori Town Boundaries (Alteration) Order 2001.

## O 2 Boundaries of the town Nausori

The boundaries of the town of Nausori are hereby altered to include the following areas, namely all that area of land in the Tikina of Nausori and in the Province of Tailevu, the boundaries of which are described hereunder.

## O 3 Area A

Commencing on the western boundary of TL 1782 on Vuci Road, thence along the boundaries of TL 1782 to TL 1711, thence north-westerly along TL 1711, thence north-easterly along TL 1711 and TL 1756, thence south-westerly along TL 1756, thence south along TL 1756 for a distance of approximately 100 m thence north-easterly along the boundaries of TL 1402, TL 1406, TL 1407, TL 1405, R746 SO 579, TL 1427, SO 2117, thence north-easterly to TL 1096 at a distance of 28m from Vuci Road, thence across TL 1096, TL 1037 and TL 556 to Waituri Road at a distance of 60 m from the Vuci Road/Waituri Road junction, thence south east along Waituri Road and Vuci South Road to the south-eastern boundary of Lot 2 SO 2284, thence south-westerly along Lot 2 and Lot 1 SO 2284 to the western boundary of Lot 4 TL 1187, thence in a straight line across Lot 1 TL 1187, Lot 8 to Lot 1 TL 1281, TL 108, SO 3061, ATL 120, Lot 1 SO 2881 and Lots 1 to 6 TL 1819 to the eastern boundary of Lot 1 TL 524, thence in a north-westerly direction along Lot 1 TL 524 to Vuci Road, thence across Vuci Toad to the western boundary of TL 1782 on Vuci Road being the point of commencement.

## O 4 Area B

(1) Commencing on the south western boundary of TL 887 on Wainibokasi Road, thence north-easterly along TL 887, thence generally south-westerly along TL 887, TL 1068, and TL 1275, thence along the boundaries of TL 1275 to the western boundary of Lot 1 So 1119, thence north-easterly along Lot 1 SO 1119, SO 2212, SO 1693, thence south-easterly along SO 1693, thence south-westerly along SO 1693, SO 2212, Lot 1 SO 1119, Lot 3 SO 678 the south-eastern boundary of Lot 2 So 678, thence south-easterly along TL 1854 to the north eastern boundary of TL 1548, thence south-westerly along TL 1548 to Wainibokasi Road, thence south-easterly along Wainibokasi Road to the south-eastern boundary of TL 1768, thence across Wainibokasi Road to the north-eastern boundary of TL 1932, thence south-westerly, thence south along the boundaries of Lot 2 and Lot 1 SO 2828 to Wainibokasi Road, thence westerly along Wainibokasi Road for a distance of 20 m in, thence across Wainibokasi Road to the south western boundary of TL 887 on Wainibokasi Road being the point of commencement.

(2) The above areas are shown edged in red on PP 579 kept at the office of Director of Lands and Surveyor General, copies of which are available for viewing at the offices of the permanent secretary for local government, Commisioner Central Division, Nausori and town clerk, Nausori Town Council.
