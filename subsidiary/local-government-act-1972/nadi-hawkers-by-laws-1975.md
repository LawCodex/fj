# NADI (HAWKERS) BY-LAWS 1975

Nadi (Hawkers) By-laws 1975 (LGN 17 of 1975) commenced on 19 December 1975, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Nadi (Hawkers) (Amendment) By-laws 1977 (LGN 2 of 1977) | 4 February 1977 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Nadi (Hawkers) By-laws 1975.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Council** means the Nadi Town Council;

**goods** includes beads, jewellery and curios and articles of food or produce grown in Fiji;

**hawker** means a person who by going from place to place sells goods carried on his or her person or from a handcart, box, basket, tray and the like or from a boat, horse-drawn vehicle, bicycle, motor vehicle or any other type of conveyance;

[def am LGN 2 of 1977 by-law 2, 4 February 1977]

**officer** means the town clerk and also any person acting as such for the time being, any sanitary inspector appointed by the Council, or any officer or servant of the Council in the performance of any duty under these By-laws; and

**town clerk** means the town clerk appointed by the Council.

## by-law 3 Hawkers to be licensed

No person shall within the town follow the calling of a hawker or hawk for sale any produce, livestock or other goods whatsoever unless he or she shall be the holder in his or her own name of an unexpired hawker’s licence issued by the Council.

## by-law 4 Sale of pigeons and wild birds prohibited

No licence issued under these By-laws shall permit the sale of pigeons and/or wild birds.

## by-law 5 Application for licence

Every person desirous of obtaining a hawker's licence shall make application in person in writing to the town clerk in the form in Schedule 1 .

## by-law 6 Council may refuse licence

The Council may refuse to grant a hawker's licence to any person.

## by-law 7 Council may suspend or cancel licence

The Council may suspend or cancel the licence of any hawker.

## by-law 8 Issue of licence

The town clerk shall, upon receiving the application mentioned in by-law 5 and, subject to such directions as he or she shall receive from the Council in that behalf, issue a licence in the form in the Schedule 2 to the applicant.

[by-law 8 am LGN 2 of 1977 by-law 3, 4 February 1977]

## by-law 9 Effect of licence

A hawker's licence issued by the Council under by-law 8 shall authorise only the hawking and sale of such goods as are endorsed on the licence.

## by-law 10 Duration of licence

A hawker's licence shall be issued for not more than 3 months, and shall expire on 31 March, 30 June, 30 September, and 31 December in each and every year.

## by-law 11 Duties and obligations of hawker

A licensed hawker shall—

(a) at all times when hawking carry his or her licence with him or her and shall on demand produce the same to any police officer or officer of the Council;

(b) if by remaining stationary in any road, street or place, he or she shall cause any obstruction to pedestrian or other traffic, forthwith move away from such road, street or place in such manner as effectively to remove such obstruction;

(c) when using a handcart or vehicle of any kind, keep such handcart or vehicle as near as practicable to the left hand side of the road or street;

(d) keep his or her boat, basket, tray, utensils, handcart or vehicle clean to the satisfaction of any sanitary inspector appointed by the Council;

(e) prevent any goods intended for human consumption from coming into contact with or being exposed to any dirt, filth, contagion, infection, flies or anything likely to render such goods injurious to health;

(f) cease to hawk any goods intended for human consumption while he or she is suffering from any infection, contagious or offensive disease or skin complaint;

(g) follow the directions of the authorised officers of the Council as to the location of his or her stall and his or her movements when hawking.

## by-law 12 Penalty

Any person contravening or failing to comply with these By-laws shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $20 and to a further fine of $10 for each day during which such contravention continues.
