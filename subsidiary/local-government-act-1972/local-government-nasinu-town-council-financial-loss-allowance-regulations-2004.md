# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (NASINU TOWN COUNCIL) (FINANCIAL LOSS ALLOWANCE) REGULATIONS 2004

Local Government (Nasinu Town Council) (Financial Loss Allowance) Regulations 2004 (LN 62 of 2004) commenced on 30 July 2004, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Nasinu Town Council) (Financial Loss Allowance) Regulations 2004.

## reg 2 Financial loss allowance

A member of the Nasinu Town Council is entitled to an allowance at the rate of $18 per ordinary and standing committee meeting as financial loss allowance in respect of loss of earnings suffered or incurred for the purpose of enabling a councillor to perform any approved duty as a member of the Council.
