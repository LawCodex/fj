# SAVUSAVU (BUS STATION, TAXI STAND AND LORRY STAND) BY-LAWS 2001

Savusavu (Bus Station, Taxi Stand and Lorry Stand) By-laws 2001 (LN 1 of 2001) commenced on 19 January 2001, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Savusavu (Bus Station, Taxi Stand And Lorry Stand) (Amendment) By-law 2003 (LN 12 of 2003) | 25 April 2003 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Savusavu Town Council (Bus Station, Taxi Stand and Lorry Stand) By-laws 2001.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**bus shelter** means any shelter owned and controlled by the Council for the use of bus passengers in the town of Savusavu;

**bus station** means the bus station at Savusavu contained in State Lease CL 11197, Lot 1 and 2 (part of) maintained and controlled by the Council;

**carrier** has the meaning given by the Land Transport Act 1998 ;

**Council** means the Savusavu Town Council;

**Land Transport Authority** means the Land Transport Authority established by the Land Transport Act 1998 ;

**lorry** means any light or heavy goods vehicle plying for hire for the carriage of any goods for reward and includes a carrier;

**lorry stand** means any lorry stand at Savusavu maintained and controlled by the Council;

**omnibus** has the meaning given by the Land Transport Act 1998 ;

**operator** means a person who owns or operates an omnibus, a taxi or a lorry;

**taxi** has the meaning given by the Land Transport Act 1998 ; and

**taxi stand** means any taxi stand at Savusavu maintained and controlled by the Council.

## by-law 3 Management of bus station etc

(1) The town clerk has the immediate management and control of the bus station, taxi stand and lorry stands.

(2) The Council may appoint an attendant or attendants to assist the town clerk in the control of the bus station, taxi stands and lorry stands and to perform other functions determined by the Council by resolution.

## by-law 4 Application for a permit

An operator may apply to the Council in Form 1 set out in Schedule 1, accompanied by the prescribed fee, for a permit to use the bus stand or a lorry stand.

## by-law 5 Issue of permits

(1) The town clerk may, on an application by an operator, issue a permit in Form 2 set out in Schedule 1.

(2) A permit issued under paragraph (1) is valid only for the vehicle and stand specified.

## by-law 6 Vehicle stickers

(1) If a permit is issued under by-law 5(2) , the town clerk, must issue a sticker in Form 3 set out in Schedule 1 for the vehicle referred to in the permit.

(2) The operator must, immediately on receipt of the sticker, cause the sticker to be affixed to the left bottom corner of the windscreen of the vehicle.

(3) A sticker issued under paragraph (1) is valid from the date specified on the sticker until revoked.

(4) If a permit issued under by-law 5(1) is revoked by the Council by virtue of by-law 10 —

(a) the sticker issued in relation to the permit is deemed to have been revoked from the date of the revocation of the permit; and

(b) the operator must remove the sticker from the vehicle.

(5) No person may remove a valid sticker affixed to a vehicle.

## by-law 7 Fees

(1) The fees for the purpose of these By-laws are set out in Schedule 2 .

(2) Any fees to be paid regarding a permit under these By-laws are to be charged monthly and in arrears.

(3) The monthly fee charged for an omnibus is to be calculated on the total number of visits the omnibus made to the station in the previous month based on the timetable approved by the Land Transport Authority.

(4) If the number of visits of an omnibus cannot be satisfactorily calculated under paragraph (3) , the town clerk may in writing require the operator of the omnibus to provide the town clerk with the particulars of the number of visits made by the omnibus to the bus station in a particular month.

(5) Upon receipt of the notice under paragraph (4) , the operator must within 7 days supply the town clerk the particulars required.

(6) Particulars submitted by the operator must—

(a) include the number of visits to the bus station and the time for each visit made in that month; and

(b) be signed by on behalf of the operator.

## by-law 8 Payment of fees

(1) The town clerk must, in writing, notify any operator of any fees due by the operator under these By-laws.

(2) Any fees due under these By-laws must be paid by the operator to the town clerk within 15 days from the date of receipt of notice.

## by-law 9 Bus station not to be used without permit

(1) No operator may use the bus station, a taxi stand or a lorry stand unless the operator has a valid permit issued under by-law 5(1) .

(2) A driver or person in control of an omnibus, a taxi or a lorry must not enter or use the bus station, a taxi stand or a lorry stand unless the omnibus, taxi or lorry has a valid permit.

(3) An operator must not use the bus station, a taxi stand or a lorry stand if the permit fees due under these By-laws remain unpaid after the due date.

## by-law 10 Revocation of permit

The Council may revoke a permit issued under by-law 5(1) if—

(a) the fees due under these By-laws remain unpaid after the due date; or

(b) the operator is convicted under by-law 13 .

## by-law 11 Control of bus station etc

(1) No person may without the written authority of the Council, sell or offer or expose for sale any produce or goods in or around the bus station, a taxi stand or a lorry stand.

(2) No person may place or deposit in or on any omnibus, taxi or lorry standing in the bus station, a taxi stand or a lorry stand any placard, handbill, advertisement or notice without the prior consent of the owner or driver of the omnibus, taxi or lorry.

(3) No person may display in or on the bus station, a taxi stand or a lorry stand or any bus shelter any placard, handbill, advertisement or notice, pamphlet, book or paper without prior consent of the Council and on such terms and conditions as the Council by resolution determines.

## by-law 12 Parking of omnibus

(1) A driver or person in control of an omnibus must not stop, stand or park the omnibus in the bus station except for the purpose of loading or unloading passengers and their luggage and in no case for longer than specified under paragraph (2) .

(2) The omnibus must not be stopped at the bus station for longer than—

(a) 5 minutes immediately prior to the scheduled time of departure of the omnibus; or

(b) is reasonably necessary for the purpose of setting down passengers and their luggage.

## by-law 13 Offence

A person who contravenes a provision of these By-laws commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine of $50.
