# TAVUA (HAWKERS) BY-LAWS 1993

Tavua (Hawkers) By-laws 1993 (LN 70 of 1993) commenced on 13 August 1993, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Tavua (Hawkers) By-laws 1993.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Council** means the Tavua Town Council;

**goods** include beads and curios and articles of food or produce grown in Fiji;

**hawker** means a person who by going from place to place sells goods carried on his or her person or from a handcart, box, basket, bag, tray and the like, or from a boat, horse, drawn vehicle, bicycle, motor vehicle or any other type of conveyance;

**Officer** means the town clerk and also any person acting as such for the time being, any sanitary inspector appointed by the Council, or any officer or servant of the Council in the performance of any duty under these By-laws; and

**town clerk** means the town clerk appointed by the Council.

## by-law 3 Hawkers to be licensed

No person shall within the town follow the calling of a hawker or hawk for sale any produce, livestock, or other goods whatsoever unless he or she shall be the holder in his or her own name of an unexpired hawker’s licence issued by the Council.

## by-law 4 Sale of pigeons and wild birds prohibited

No licence issued under these By-laws shall permit the sale of pigeons and/or wild birds.

## by-law 5 Application for licence

Every person desirous of obtaining a hawker’s licence shall make application, in person, in writing, to the town clerk in the form in Schedule 1 .

## by-law 6 Council may refuse, cancel or suspend licence

The Council may refuse to grant a hawker’s licence to any person and may suspend or cancel the licence of any hawker for any good cause after giving the holder thereof due opportunity of being heard.

## by-law 7 Issue of licence

The town clerk, upon receiving an application mentioned by by-law 5 and subject to such directions as he or she may receive from the Council, may issue to the applicant, without payment of fees, a licence in the form set out in Schedule 2.

## by-law 8 Effect of licence

A hawker’s licence issued by the Council under by-law 7 shall authorise only the hawking and sale of such goods as are endorsed on the licence and in such area as is prescribed by the licence.

## by-law 9 Duration of licence

A hawker’s licence under these By-laws shall be issued for a period of one year.

## by-law 10 Duties and obligations of hawkers

A licensed hawker shall—

(a) at all times when hawking carry his or her licence with his or her and shall on demand produce the same to any police officer or officer of the Council;

(b) by remaining stationary in any road, street or place, he or she shall cause any obstruction to pedestrian or other traffic, forthwith move away from such road, street or place in such manner as effectively to remove such obstruction;

(c) when using a hand-cart or vehicle keep such hand-cart or vehicle as near as practicable to the left-hand side of the road or street;

(d) keep his or her boat, basket, tray, utensils and cart or vehicle clean to the satisfaction of any sanitary inspector appointed by the Council;

(e) prevent any goods intended for human consumption from coming into contact with or being exposed to any dirt, filth, contagion, infection, lice or anything likely to render such goods injurious to health;

(f) cease to hawk any goods intended for human consumption while he or she is suffering from any infection, contagious or offensive disease or complaint.

## by-law 11 Penalty

Any person contravening or failing to comply with these By-laws, shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100.
