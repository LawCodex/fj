# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (TAVUA TOWN COUNCIL) (MAYOR'S HONORARIUM) REGULATIONS 2006

Local Government (Tavua Town Council) (Mayor's Honorarium) Regulations 2006 (LN 112 of 2006) commenced on 29 September 2006, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Tavua Town Council) (Mayor's Honorarium) Regulations 2006.

## reg 2 Mayor’s honorarium

The mayor of Tavua Town Council is entitled to be paid a mayor's honorarium allowance at the rate of $1,800 per annum in respect of loss of earnings suffered or incurred for the purpose of enabling the mayor to carry out duties in his or her capacity as a mayor.
