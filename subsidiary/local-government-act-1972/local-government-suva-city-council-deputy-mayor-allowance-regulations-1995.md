# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (SUVA CITY COUNCIL DEPUTY MAYOR ALLOWANCE) REGULATIONS 1995

Local Government (Suva City Council Deputy Mayor Allowance) Regulations 1995 (LN 129 of 1995) commenced on 10 November 1995, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Suva City Council Deputy Mayor Allowance) Regulations 1995.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, “Deputy Mayor” means the Deputy Mayor of Suva city.

## reg 3 Deputy Mayor allowance

The Deputy Mayor shall be paid a quarterly allowance based on the annual sum of $1,000.
