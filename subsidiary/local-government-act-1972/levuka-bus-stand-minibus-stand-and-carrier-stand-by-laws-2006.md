# LEVUKA (BUS STAND, MINIBUS STAND AND CARRIER STAND) BY-LAWS 2006

Levuka (Bus Stand, Minibus Stand and Carrier Stand) By-laws 2006 (LN 4 of 2006) commenced on 27 January 2006, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Levuka (Bus Stand, Minibus Stand and Carrier Stand) By-laws 2006.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**bus** means a public service vehicle which is constructed or adapted to carry not less than 9 passengers and which has a valid Public Service Licence and Road Permit;

**bus shelter** means any shelter owned and controlled by the Council for the use of bus passengers in the town of Levuka;

**bus stand** means the bus stand of Levuka maintained and controlled by the Council;

**carrier** means a motor vehicle constructed and equipped for the safe carriage of passengers and goods such that the majority of passengers are located separately from the driver's compartment;

**carrier stand** means any carrier stand at Levuka maintained and controlled by the Council;

**Council** means the Levuka Town Council;

**minibus** means a motor vehicle equipped for the conveyance of not less than 8 and not more than 15 persons excluding the driver;

**minibus stand** means the minibus stand at Levuka maintained and controlled by the Council;

**operator** includes a person who owns or operates a bus, a minibus or a carrier;

**stand** means the bus stand, minibus stand and carrier stand controlled by the Council situated at Levuka; and

**vehicle** includes any description of vehicle designed to move or to be moved on one or more wheels or revolving runners, or any truck, barrow, carriage or similar vehicle but does not include a vehicle excluded by regulations.

## by-law 3 Management of stand etc

(1) The bus stand, every minibus stand and every carrier stand come under the immediate management of the town clerk.

(2) The Council may appoint an attendant or attendants to assist the town clerk to exercise control over the bus stand or any one or more stands and to perform such functions with regard thereto as the Council may by resolution determine.

[by-law 3 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 151, 1 December 2016]

## by-law 4 Application for a permit

An operator may apply to the Council in Form 1 set out in Schedule 1 to these By-laws, accompanied by the prescribed fee, for a permit to use the bus stand, one or more minibus stands or one or more carrier stands.

## by-law 5 Issue of permit

(1) The town clerk may issue to an operator a permit in Form 2 set out in Schedule 1 to these By-laws.

(2) A permit issued under paragraph (1) is valid only for the vehicle and stand or stands specified in it.

## by-law 6 Vehicle stickers

(1) If a permit is issued under by-law 5(2) , the town clerk must issue a sticker in Form 3 set out in Schedule 1 to these By-laws in respect of the vehicle in the permit.

(2) The operator must, immediately on receipt of the sticker, cause the sticker to be affixed to the left bottom corner of the windscreen of the vehicle.

(3) A sticker issued under paragraph (1) is valid from the date specified on the sticker until revoked.

(4) If a permit issued under by-law 5(1) is revoked by the Council by virtue of by-law 10 —

(a) the sticker issued in respect of that permit is deemed to be revoked from the date of revocation of the permit; and

(b) the operator must remove the sticker from the vehicle.

(5) No person may remove a valid sticker which is affixed to a vehicle.

## by-law 7 Fees

(1) The fees for the purposes of these By-laws are prescribed in Schedule 2 to these By-laws.

(2) Any fee to be paid to the Council in respect of a bus, minibus or carrier is to be charged monthly and in arrears.

(3) The monthly fee charged for a bus, minibus and carrier is to be calculated on the total number of visits a bus, minibus or carrier made to the stand in the previous month based on the timetable approved by the Land Transport Authority.

(4) If the number of visits of a bus, minibus or carrier cannot be satisfactory calculated from the timetable, the town clerk may in writing require the operator of the bus, minibus or carrier to provide him or her with the particulars of the number of visits made by his or her bus, minibus or carrier to the stand in a particular month.

(5) Upon receipt of the notice under paragraph (4) , the operator must within 7 days supply to the town clerk the particulars required.

(6) Particulars submitted by an operator must—

(a) include the number of visits to the stand and the time for each visit made in that month; and

(b) be signed by or on behalf of the operator.

(7) Arrears of fees payable to the Council under these By-laws may be recovered as a debt in a court of competent jurisdiction.

## by-law 8 Payment of fees

(1) The town clerk must, in writing, notify the operator of any fees to be paid by the operator under these By-laws.

(2) Any fees charged and payable under these By-laws must be paid by or on behalf of the operator to the town clerk within 15 days from the date of issue of the notice.

## by-law 9 Bus, minibus and carrier stand not to be used without permit

(1) No operator may use the bus stand, a minibus stand or a carrier stand unless the operator has made a valid permit issued under by-law 5(1) .

(2) A driver or person in control of a bus, minibus or carrier must not enter or use the bus stand, a minibus stand, or a carrier stand unless that bus, minibus or carrier has a valid permit.

(3) An operator must not use the bus stand, minibus stand or a carrier stand if the fees payable under these By-laws remain unpaid after the due date.

## by-law 10 Revocation of permit

The Council may revoke a permit issued under by-law 5(1) if—

(i) a fee owed by an operator to the Council remains unpaid after the due date; or

(ii) an operator is convicted of an offence under these By-laws.

## by-law 11 Control of stand etc

(1) No person may, without the written authority of the Council, sell or offer or expose for sale any produce or goods in or around the bus stand, a minibus stand or carrier stand.

(2) No person may place or deposit in or on any bus, minibus or carrier permitted to stand in the bus stand, a minibus stand or a carrier stand a placard, handbill, advertisement or notice without the prior consent of the owner or driver of the bus, minibus or carrier.

(3) No person may display in or on the bus stand, a minibus stand, or a carrier stand any placard, handbill, advertisement, notice, pamphlet or any written material except with the written consent of the Council and on such terms and conditions as the Council may, by resolution, determine.

[by-law 11 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 151, 1 December 2016]

## by-law 12 Parking vehicles

(1) A driver or person in control of a bus, minibus or carrier must not stop, stand or park the vehicle in the bus stand except for the purposes of loading or unloading passengers and their luggage.

(2) A vehicle must not be stopped at the stand for longer than—

(a) 5 minutes immediately prior to the scheduled time for departure of the vehicle; or

(b) is reasonably necessary for the purpose of setting down passengers and their luggage.

## by-law 13 Prohibition of vehicles with oil leaks in the stand etc

No operator shall permit any vehicle which leaks oil of any description to enter, stop or stand in the bus stand, minibus stand and carrier stand.

[by-law 13 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 151, 1 December 2016]

## by-law 14 Penalty

Any person who contravenes a provision of these By-laws commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $50.
