# LAUTOKA CITY BOUNDARIES (ALTERATION) ORDER 1991

Lautoka City Boundaries (Alteration) Order 1991 (LN 33 of 1991) commenced on 7 June 1991, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Native Lands (Amendment) Decree 2011 (No 7 of 2011) | 1 March 2011 |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Lautoka City Boundaries (Alteration) Order 1991.

## O 2 Alteration of Lautoka City Boundaries

(1) The boundaries of the city of Lautoka is hereby altered by expansion to include—

(i) all that area of State Land and iTaukei land known as Part of Namoli lying on the North and North East of the existing boundary of Lautoka City;

(ii) all that area of iTaukei Land being part of NLC, Lot 39 adjoining the North East boundary of Lautoka City;

(iii) all that area of iTaukei Land lying east of the existing boundary of Lautoka City;

(iv) all that area of iTaukei land known as Waiyavi Extension lying on the East of the existing boundary of Lautoka City; and

(v) all that area of State Land being parts of Navutu, Natabua and Field 40 lying West and South West of the existing boundary of Lautoka City.

[para (1) am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, 1 March 2011]

(2) The area described in paragraph (1)(i) to (v) above are more particularly shown verged yellow on plan pp. 338 kept in the Office of the Director of Lands and Surveyor-General, copies of which are available for viewing in the offices of the permanent secretary responsible for local government; the Commissioner, Central/Western; the District Officer, Lautoka and the Lautoka City Council.

[para (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 146, 1 December 2016]
