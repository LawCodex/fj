# LAUTOKA (HANDICRAFT MARKET) BY-LAWS 1985

Lautoka (Handicraft Market) By-laws 1985 (LN 25 of 1985) commenced on 4 April 1985, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Fijian Affairs (Amendment) Decree 2010 (No 31 of 2010) | 2 July 2010 |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |
| Business Licensing (Repeal) Act 2020 (No 28 of 2020) | 1 August 2020 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Lautoka (Handicraft Market) By-laws 1985.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**attendant** means any officer of the Council appointed by the town clerk to carry out duties at the handicraft market;

**Council** means the Lautoka City Council;

**handicraft** means local curios and handicrafts and includes mats, baskets, fans, wood carving, articles made of straw, grass or synthetic grass skirts, souvenirs, post cards, books about Fiji, dolls dressed in traditional costumes or portraying Fijian police officers or soldiers, curios and handicrafts of other Pacific Islands origin, imported scarfs, cushion covers, beaded purses, men’s belts with the word “Fiji” on the outside thereof;

[def am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, 2 July 2010 LN 99 of 2016 reg 144, 1 December 2016]

**handicraft market** means the Lautoka handicraft market situated along Vitogo Parade on the northern side between Walu Street and sugar mill injection canal and along Daniva Street, maintained and controlled by the Lautoka City Council;

**stall** means any place in the handicraft market used for the sale of handicraft and includes the ground spaces from where handicrafts are being sold; and

**stall holder** means the holder of a current revenue receipt issued to him or her in accordance with by-law 14 .

## by-law 3 Attendant

The handicraft market shall be under the immediate management and control of the attendant.

## by-law 4 Sale outside the handicraft market

A person shall not sell handicraft in any place within the city of Lautoka other than at the handicraft market, provided that this by-law does not apply to the sale of handicraft at Lautoka Municipal Market established under the Lautoka (Market) By-laws 1976 and at premises in the city of Lautoka authorised for such purpose by the Council.

[by-law 4 am Act 28 of 2020 s 4 and Sch, 1 August 2020]

## by-law 5 Restriction on sale

A person shall not sell, offer or expose for sale, or display in the handicraft market any article other than handicraft.

## by-law 6 Opening of market

The handicraft market shall be open for business during such hours as are permitted by the Council and only on a day that—

(a) an international cruise ship is berthed in Lautoka harbour; or

(b) the Council permits.

## by-law 7 Directions by attendant

All persons using or being within the precincts of the handicraft market shall obey all reasonable directions given by the attendant for the purpose of observing the good order and cleanliness of the handicraft market.

## by-law 8 Sale from stalls

A person shall not sell, offer or expose for sale, display or stock any handicraft at the handicraft market except at the stall allocated to him or her by the attendant and except during the hours the handicraft market is open for business.

## by-law 9 Allocation of stalls

The allocation of stalls in the handicraft market shall be made by the attendant and his or her decision in that regard shall be final.

## by-law 10 Revenue receipt

Every stall holder shall whilst the stall is open for business, exhibit and keep exhibited on his or her stall, as the attendant may direct, his or her current revenue receipt for the payment of the handicraft market fee.

## by-law 11 Prices to be exhibited

Every stall holder shall have all handicraft for sale by him or her at a stall legibly and conspicuously marked with the prices at which he or she offers the same for sale and in a manner clearly indicating the price to the public.

## by-law 12 Alteration to stall

A person shall not make any alteration to any stall or exhibit any notice thereon or introduce any fitting, fixtures, furniture or obstructions of any kind without the authority of the Council and any such things introduced without the approval of the Council may be removed by the attendant without notice to the stall holder.

## by-law 13 Fees to be paid

(1) A person shall not occupy or use a stall whilst any charges which are due and payable for the use thereof are outstanding.

(2) If any person fails to pay the fee within the required time then the attendant may remove any goods from the stall or withhold the goods until the outstanding fee and any costs associated with the removal or withholding are fully paid.

## by-law 14 Fees

(1) The fee for the use of a stall shall be in accordance with the rates set out in the Schedule and shall be paid in advance on demand by the attendant.

(2) Upon receiving payment of the stall fee under this by-law, the attendant shall immediately issue a revenue receipt which shall contain the name of the stall holder, the date for which the fee is paid and the amount paid.

(3) A stall holder shall, on demand by any officer of the Council, produce his or her current revenue receipt.

## by-law 15 Exchange, subletting and sharing stalls

A stallholder shall not exchange, sublet, surrender or part with the possession of his or her stall or share his or her stall with any other vendor or person except with the permission of the attendant.

## by-law 16 Cleanliness

Stallholders shall keep their stalls in a clean and orderly condition and shall as and when required dispose of any litter and refuse as directed by the attendant.

## by-law 17 Litter

A person shall not abandon or cause to be abandoned any litter in or upon any part of the handicraft market except in recepticles provided for the purpose.

## by-law 18 Exclusive use and prohibition

A stall shall be exclusively used for the sale of handicraft and no person shall cook food or sell yaqona in any part of the handicraft market.

## by-law 19 Obstruction of attendant and conduct

A person shall not obstruct, prevent or hinder the attendant or any other officers of the Council in the performance of any duty under the By-laws and shall not enter the handicraft market whilst drunk or incite a riot or curse or swear or use any gross, indecent, filthy or abusive language or gamble or be guilty of any other misconduct therein, or spit or discharge any nasal fluid or mucus in the handicraft market.

## by-law 20 Obstruction

A person shall not obstruct any entrances, thoroughfares, passages or divisions of the handicraft market and shall not loiter therein.

## by-law 21 Admission

The attendant shall have the power to refuse admission to, or to eject from, the handicraft market, any person of undesirable character or any person causing any nuisance or young children unattended.

## by-law 22 Unsold goods

The unsold goods and other chattels belonging to a stall-holder shall be removed immediately upon the closure of the handicraft market.

## by-law 23 Penalty

Any person who fails to comply with any of the provisions of these By-laws shall be guilty of an offence and shall be subject on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100 and to a further fine of $10 for every day during which such offence continues.
