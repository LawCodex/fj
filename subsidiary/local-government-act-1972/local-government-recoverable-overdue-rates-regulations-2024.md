# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (RECOVERABLE OVERDUE RATES) REGULATIONS 2024

Local Government (Recoverable Overdue Rates) Regulations 2024 (LN 39 of 2024) commenced on 12 July 2024, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Recoverable Overdue Rates) Regulations 2024.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the date of publication in the Gazette.

## reg 2 Rates recoverable by Council

(1) A Council may recover rates payable to the Council.

(2) Any rate that is overdue bears an interest of 5% per annum.

(3) The interest charged on any rate that is overdue must be calculated using the simple interest method.
