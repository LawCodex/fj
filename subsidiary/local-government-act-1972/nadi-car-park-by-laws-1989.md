# NADI (CAR PARK) BY-LAWS 1989

Nadi (Car Park) By-laws 1989 (LN 12 of 1989) commenced on 17 February 1989, as amended by:

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Nadi Town Council (Car Park) By-laws 1989.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**attendant** means any person appointed by the Council to carry out duties in a car park;

**car park** means any area or building or part of a building set apart by the Council for the use of motor vehicles as a parking place under by-law 3;

**commercial vehicle** means a motor vehicle used for the carriage of goods or passengers or both goods and passengers for hire or reward or used in the course of trade or agriculture;

**Council** means the Nadi Town Council; and

**private motor vehicle** and taxi have the meanings assigned to them by the Land Transport Act 1998 .

[def am LN 99 of 2016 reg 168, 1 December 2016]

## by-law 3 Establishment of car parks

(1) The Council may from time to time, by resolution, set apart any area for the parking of motor vehicles, or any particular class or classes of motor vehicles.

(2) A description of any such area shall be advertised by the Council at least once each in the Gazette and a daily newspaper circulating in Fiji.

## by-law 4 Attendant

A car park shall be under the immediate management and control of the attendant.

## by-law 5 Duty to obey directions

All persons using or being within the precincts of a car park shall obey all reasonable directions given by the attendant for the purpose of observing good order and cleanliness in the car park.

## by-law 6 Prohibitions

No person shall make any alterations to the alignments of a car park or introduce any unauthorised fitting, fixture or obstruction into a car park and anything so introduced may be removed forthwith by the attendant.

## by-law 7 Entry and exit

All persons driving motor vehicles using a car park shall observe the directions posted at such car park relating to means of entrance thereto and egress therefrom.

## by-law 8 Maximum weight

The Council may prohibit or restrict the use of a car park by motor vehicles exceeding a specified net weight.

## by-law 9 Charges

The charges for admission to, and use of, a car park shall be as follows—

(a) casual rate (per admission) — $0.49 for each hour or part of an hour, but not exceeding $2.46 rate for a whole day.

(b) monthly unlimited use rate (per vehicle) — $36.84.

[by-law 9 am LN 108 of 1995 by-law 3, 6 October 1995 LN 2 of 2005 by-law 2, 14 January 2005]

## by-law 10 Opening hours

The Council may, by resolution, prescribe the hours of opening and closing of any car park.

## by-law 11 Parking receipt and fees

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) , the charge for the use of a car park for which the Council has not prescribed hours of opening and closing shall be paid in advance to the attendant in exchange for which a parking receipt will be issued.

(2) No fee shall be paid for entry into a car park referred to in paragraph (1) before 8.00 am on Mondays to Saturdays inclusive, and after 5.00 pm on Mondays to Fridays inclusive, and after 2.00 pm on Saturdays or on Sundays, provided that any private motor vehicle remaining in a car park after 8:00 am on week days shall be deemed to have entered the car park after that hour and fees in accordance with these By-laws shall be payable by the driver or owner of the vehicle.

## by-law 12 Parking ticket and fees

(1) On admission to a car park for which the Council has prescribed hours of opening and closing the attendant shall issue to the driver of the motor vehicle a parking ticket and payment of the charges for the use of the car park shall be made to the attendant when the motor vehicle leaves the car park.

(2) In any case where the parking ticket is not presented to the attendant before the motor vehicle leaves the car park a charge shall be made for that vehicle from the hour at which the car park opened on that day until the time the vehicle leaves the car park.

(3) No person shall remove any motor vehicle from a car park after the prescribed closing hour.

## by-law 13 Validity of parking receipt

A parking receipt issued under by-law 12 in respect of a taxi or commercial vehicle shall be valid for 7 days including the day of issue and only for the vehicle in respect of which it is issued and a parking receipt issued in respect of a private motor vehicle shall be valid for the day of issue only.

## by-law 14 Parking permits

Nothing in these By-laws shall preclude the Council from issuing a pre-paid monthly or annual permit granting the holder thereof exclusive rights to park a motor vehicle in the parking place specified in the permit.

## by-law 15 Parking places

Different classes of motor vehicles using a car park shall park only within the limits set apart for the use of vehicles of the class to which they belong as indicated by car park notices and as directed by the attendant.

## by-law 16 Production of receipt

The driver of any motor vehicle shall show for inspection his or her parking receipt, ticket or permit upon demand by the attendant.

## by-law 17 Obstruction of attendant

No person shall obstruct, prevent or interfere with an attendant in the performance of his or her duties.

## by-law 18 Obstructing entrances etc

(1) No motor vehicle shall be parked in a car park so as to obstruct any of the entrances, divisions or thoroughfares of the car park and the driver of any such vehicle, if called upon to do so by the attendant, shall forthwith remove the vehicle.

(2) If the driver of any motor vehicle fails to comply with a direction given by the attendant under paragraph (1) or if the driver is not available, the attendant may cause the vehicle to be removed to such other place, whether in the car park or not, as the attendant considers reasonable in the circumstances and the Council shall not be responsible for any loss or damage to the vehicle resulting from such removal.

(3) Any costs incurred by the Council in effecting the removal of a vehicle in terms of this by-law shall be recoverable from the registered owner of the vehicle.

## by-law 19 Loitering

No person shall loiter in any of the entrances, thoroughfares or divisions of a car park.

## by-law 20 Power to refuse admission etc

The attendant shall have power to refuse admission to or eject from a car park any persons of undesirable character or any young children unattended.

## by-law 21 Obstructing footpath

No person shall place any matter or thing on any roadway, footpath or passage in a car park so as to obstruct the free use of such roadway, footpath or passage, or neglect or refuse to remove such matter or thing on being required to do so by the attendant.

## by-law 22 Spitting

No person shall spit or discharge any nasal fluid or mucus on to any roadway, footpath or other part of a car park.

## by-law 23 Avoidance of liability

Nothing in or done under these By-laws or in any parking receipt, ticket or permit shall be deemed to make the Council responsible for any loss or damage to any motor vehicle admitted to or using a car park or for injury to any person entering or within a car park.

## by-law 24 Sale of goods

No person shall sell, offer or expose for sale any produce or goods in a car park without the prior consent in writing of the Council.

## by-law 25 Penalty

Any person who fails to comply with any of the provisions of these By-laws shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100 or to imprisonment for 3 months or to both such fine and imprisonment.
