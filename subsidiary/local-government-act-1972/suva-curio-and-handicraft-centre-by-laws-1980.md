## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Suva (Curio and Handicraft Centre) By-laws 1980.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**attendant** means any servant of the Council appointed to carry out duties at the centre;

**centre** means the Curio and Handicraft Centre maintained and controlled by the Council situated at the corner of the Central Street and Stinson Parade, and being Lot 1, on DP 2616 and being part of State Reclamation of Suva Foreshore Lease No 3359;

**Council** means the Suva City Council;

**goods** means—

(a) curios and handicrafts, including mats, baskets, fans, wood carvings, articles made of straw and grass skirts;

(b) plants, ferns and cut flowers;

(c) jewellery, ornaments and souvenirs;

(d)

(i) books;

(ii) filigree jewellery, imitation jewellery and ladies’ evening bags;

(iii) dolls dressed in traditional costume or portraying Fijian police officers or soldiers;

(iv) postcards;

(v) scarves, cushion covers, beaded purses, men's belts, with the words “Fiji” on the outside thereof;

(vi) wares of other Pacific Island origin such as curios and handicrafts, not including any produce, article or thing made, manufactured, prepared in, or originating from any territory outside Fiji with the exception of the articles specified in paragraph (d) ;

[def am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, 2 July 2010 LN 99 of 2016 reg 247, 1 December 2016]

**kiosk** means any place set aside within the centre for the cooking and sale of light meals;

**stall** includes any place in the centre used for the sale of goods; and

**stallholder** means in the case of a permanent stallholder, the person in personal charge of the stall, in whose name the receipt for the current month's stall charges is issued and who holds a valid identity card issued by the Council, provided that a person may, with the prior approval of the Council or the centre supervisor act as a temporary relief for a stallholder in his or her absence for a period not exceeding the expiry date of the current receipt for the stall on the issue to the stallholder.

## by-law 3 Centre supervisor

The centre shall be under the immediate management and control of a centre supervisor appointed by and responsible to the Council.

## by-law 4 Restriction on sales

No person shall sell or offer for sale in the centre any article other than those specified in by-law 2 .

## by-law 5 Hours

(1) The centre shall be open for business on such days and during such hours as may be determined by resolution of the Council.

(2) Every resolution made in terms of this by-law shall be published once in the Gazette and once in a newspaper published in Fiji and circulating in Suva.

## by-law 6 Directions by centre supervisor

All persons using or being within the precincts of the centre shall obey all reasonable directions given by the centre supervisor for the purpose of observing the good order and cleanliness of the centre.

## by-law 7 Sale from stalls

No person shall sell or offer or expose for sale in the centre any goods, except at a stall allotted to him or her by the Council or the centre supervisor and except during the hours that the centre is open for business.

## by-law 8 Allocation of stalls

The allocation of stalls in the centre shall be made by the Council or the centre supervisor in its or his or her discretion and the decision of the Council or centre supervisor shall be final.

## by-law 9 Receipt to be exhibited

Every stallholder shall exhibit and keep exhibited on his or her stall while the same is in use his or her current receipt for the payment of the centre charges in such manner as the centre supervisor shall direct and in such position that it can conveniently be read and inspected at all times by the centre supervisor.

## by-law 10 Price of goods

No person shall offer goods for sale without displaying a notice legibly and conspicuously marking the price at which he or she offers such goods for sale.

## by-law 11 Alterations to stalls

No person shall make any alteration to a stall or exhibit any notice thereon or introduce any fittings, fixtures, furniture or impediments or anything without the authority of the Council and any such thing introduced without such authority may be removed by the Council or the centre supervisor.

## by-law 12 Charges to be paid

No person shall occupy or use a stall so long as any charges which are due and payable for the use thereof are outstanding.

## by-law 14 Exchanging, subletting etc of stalls

No stallholder shall exchange, sublet, surrender or part with the possession of his or her stall or share his or her stall with another vendor or person without the authority of the Council or the centre supervisor.

## by-law 15 Cleanliness

(1) Stallholders shall keep their stalls in a clean and orderly condition, and shall as and when required dispose of any litter and refuse as directed by the centre supervisor.

(2) Stallholders shall keep their goods offered for sale and expose them for sale, in as acceptable a manner as is practicable and shall obey all reasonable directions of the centre supervisor in this regard.

(3) All stallholders shall abide by the directions of the centre supervisor in regard to cleaning and maintenance of the centre.

## by-law 16 Litter

No person shall abandon or cause to be abandoned any litter in or upon any part of the centre except in a receptacle provided for the purpose.

## by-law 17 Selling mixed yaqona prohibited

No person shall sell or offer for sale mixed yaqona in any part of the centre.

## by-law 18 Cooking in the centre

No person shall do any cooking in any part of the centre except at a kiosk.

## by-law 19 Shouting

No person shall in the centre advertise goods by shouting or conduct a sale by auction.

## by-law 20 Unwholesome goods

No person shall bring into or keep in the centre anything which is bad, obnoxious, unwholesome or dangerous and the centre supervisor may remove or cause to be removed any such thing brought within the precincts of the centre and the cost of such removal shall be paid by the owner.

## by-law 21 Diseases

No person suffering from any infectious or contagious disease or having recently been exposed to infection by such a disease shall engage in the sale of any goods in the centre.

## by-law 22 Dogs

No person shall bring any dog into the centre or suffer it to remain there and the centre supervisor shall have power to remove same at the risk of the owner.

## by-law 23 Entry prohibited outside business hours

No person shall enter or remain in the centre outside business hours without the authority of the centre supervisor.

## by-law 24 Obstruction of officials

No person shall obstruct, prevent, hinder or interfere with the centre supervisor or any attendant in the performance of any duty under these By-laws, and no person shall enter the centre whilst drunk or excite a riot or curse or swear or use any gross, indecent, filthy or abusive language or gamble or be guilty of any other misconduct therein.

## by-law 25 Obstruction

No person shall obstruct any of the entrances, thoroughfares, passages or divisions of the centre or shall loiter therein.

## by-law 26 Empty casks etc

(1) No person shall bring or cause to be brought into the centre any casks, crates or lumber of any kind without the consent of the centre supervisor and the centre supervisor may remove or cause to be removed all such articles and store them. Such removal and storage shall be at the risk and cost of the owner of such articles.

(2) No person shall erect any structure without the permission of the Council.

## by-law 27 Unsold goods

Goods unsold at the close of the day may be left in the centre at the owner's risk.

## by-law 28 Spitting

No person shall spit or discharge any nasal fluid or mucus on to any roadway, footpath, floor or other part of the centre or on to any utensil, goods, article or thing standing or being in the centre.

## by-law 29 Obstruction of roadway etc

No person shall place any matter or thing in any roadway, footpath, or passage in the centre so as to obstruct the free use of such roadway, footpath, or passage, or neglect or refuse to remove such matter or thing on being required so to do by the centre supervisor or any attendant.

## by-law 30 Penalty

Any person who contravenes any of the provisions of these By-laws shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $100 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months, or both, and to a further fine of $10 for every day during which such contravention continues.
