# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (HOUSE NUMBERING) REGULATIONS 1999

Local Government (House Numbering) Regulations 1999 (LN 88 of 1999) commenced on 16 July 1999, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (House Numbering) Regulations 1999.

## reg 2 Street names

Every municipal council must within 3 months of the dedication of a road to the Council or of the approval of the survey plan of a subdivision, name the streets contained in the instrument of dedication or in the survey plan.

## reg 3 Existing unnamed streets

Every municipal council must, within 6 months of the coming into force of these Regulations, name all streets within its jurisdictions which have been declared to be public roads and which are not named.

## reg 4 House numbering and schedule

Every municipal council must, in accordance with the procedures set out in the Schedule to these Regulations, allot a house number for each lot in every street under its jurisdiction.

## reg 5 Fixing house numbers

A municipal council may at any time issue a notice to the person who is the owner or occupier of a lot—

(a) informing the person of the house number allotted under these Regulations; and

(b) requiring the person to affix within 14 days the allotted house number on the building, fence or road frontage in such a manner and form as the municipal council from time to time directs or approves, so that such house number may be readily seen from the street.

## reg 6 Renewal of house number plates

Regulation 5 applies with necessary modifications to the renewal of old, obliterated, defaced, destroyed or missing number plates.

## reg 7 Standard house number plates

Standard house number plates are to be supplied by the council at a price determined by the council from time to time.

## reg 8 Failure to comply

An owner or occupier issued with a notice under regulation 5 or 6 who fails to comply with the notice commits an offence.

## reg 9 Destroying house number plates

A person who, without authority of the council and without good reason destroys, pulls down, obliterates or defaces any house number plate, including that of the person’s own premises, commits an offence.

## reg 10 Council may act in default

A municipal council may where it considers it expedient to do so, affix a house number plate to a house in accordance with these Regulations and recover from the owner or occupier the cost of doing so.

## reg 11 Penalty

A person who commits an offence under these Regulations is liable on conviction to a fine of $400 for each offence and in the case of a continuing offence to a fine of $5 for each day during which the offence continues after the date of conviction.

### SCHEDULE PROCEDURES FOR DETERMINING HOUSE NUMBERS

#### reg 1

Existing house numbering must be retained wherever possible.

#### reg 2

House numbering for every street commences from the end which is closest to the local post office except that in the case of a cul-de-sac numbering commences from the point of its intersection.

#### reg 3

House numbers are allocated in sequence with odd numbers on the left side and even numbers on the right side.

#### reg 4

In order to ensure that the odd and next even numbers are close to and opposite to each other, some numbers may be omitted from the numbering sequence.

#### reg 5

Each flat on a lot must have a number of its own.

#### reg 6

Corner lots must be assigned a number for each road and if there are 2 units, each flat will have a street number depending upon the street frontage of the flat.

#### reg 7

Where a street has unsubdivided land along it, the council must allow for future numbering in the following manner—

| | |
| --- | --- |
| *Zone* | *Allowance for Each Number* |
| Commercial | 3 metres |
| Industrial | 5 metres |
| Residential A | 6 metres |
| Residential B | 4 metres |
| Residential C | 4 metres |
| Residential D | 4 metres |
| Others | As determined by the Council |

#### reg 8

The house number plates must be of a size 120 mm x 100 mm and the number size must be 75 mm in height.
