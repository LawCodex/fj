# NASINU TOWN COUNCIL BY-LAWS 2001

## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [by-law 1-2]

### by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Nasinu Town Council By-laws 2001.

### by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**attendant** means any servant of the Council or person appointed by the Council to carry out duties in the car park;

**authorised** means duly authorised by the Council;

**book** means any book or other printed or written matter, picture, print, print photograph, film engraving, etching, map, plan, chart, record or similar contrivance for reproducing sound, music score and any article of a like nature forming part of the contents of library;

**bus operators** includes any person, firm, company or organisation which owns or operates an omnibus;

**bus shelter** means any shelter owned and controlled by the Council for the use of bus passengers in the town of Nasinu;

**bus station** means the Nasinu bus station maintained and controlled by the Council and notified as such by description published in the Gazette and in a daily newspaper published and circulating in Fiji;

**car park** means any area designated by the Council to be used for parking of commercial vehicles, private vehicles and taxis;

**commercial vehicle** means any motor vehicle not being a taxi used for the carriage of goods or passengers or both goods and passengers for hire or reward or used in the course of trade or agriculture and includes a tractor;

**Council** means the Nasinu Town Council;

**dance hall** means any hall, house, room, garden or other place kept or used, other than on an isolated occasion or during the course of religious festival, for public dancing, singing, music or other public entertainment of a similar nature, but does not include any licensed hotel or licensed cinematograph theatre;

**dump** means the area of land used by the Council for the disposal of garbage, refuse and trade waste;

**fish** means all edible aquatic marine life or food other than shellfish or turtles;

**handicrafts** means mats, baskets, earthenware or wooden vessels and other articles made ornamented, finished or adapted for sale;

**librarian** means the person appointed as such by the Council or any other person authorised in that behalf by the Council;

**library** means any public library belonging to or controlled by the Council;

**loudspeaker** includes a megaphone and any other device for amplifying sound;

**market** means the place established as a market in the town of Nasinu;

**market master** means the market master appointed by the Council and includes a deputy or assistants appointed by the Council;

**offensive matter** means and includes offal, night soil, putrid meat, entrails of fish or poultry, carrion, blood, dung, manure, shells, bones, rags, fruit or vegetable refuse, soap lees, impure water and any other matter giving off an offensive odour or being in any way a nuisance likely to be injurious to health;

**omnibus** means a public service vehicle constructed or adapted to carry more than 6 passengers excluding the driver;

**private vehicle** means any motor vehicle registered as such under the Land Transport Act 1998 ;

**poultry** means any edible live bird;

**produce** means fresh meat, fresh vegetables (including rice, split peas, dried pulses, and goods of that nature), fresh fruit, fresh dried, smoked or salted fish, shell fish, pigeons, poultry and other edible birds, eggs, locally grown tobacco, made wares, such as baskets, mats and other handicrafts, and any other agricultural or forest product being an article of food or drink sold in a natural unprocessed state;

**public park** means any park, garden, reserve, recreation area or sports ground vested in, owned or controlled or maintained by the Council;

**shellfish** means crabs, prawns, crayfish, oysters, mussels or any other edible molluscs, crustaceans;

**stall** means any place within the market where produce or handicrafts are sold or offered for sale;

**supplier** means a person who supplies produce to a miller, manufacturer or processor; and

**taxi** has the meaning given by the Land Transport Act 1998 .

## PART 2 SANITATION [by-law 3-10]

### by-law 3 Clearing of overgrowth of grass, weeds etc

(1) The Council or an authorised officer may serve upon the owner, occupier or lessee of any land (whether built upon or not) within the town a notice requiring him or her to keep the land clear of all grass, weeds and garbage and maintain the land in a sanitary condition to the satisfaction of the Council or an authorised officer thereof.

(2) Any owner, occupier or lessee who fails to comply with the provisions of paragraph (1) commits an offence.

### by-law 4 Public drains etc

(1) No person shall throw or deposit, or cause or suffer to be thrown or deposited, any refuse, garbage or offensive matter or any other kind of matter of a type likely to cause an obstruction, into any public culvert, drain, ditch, sewer or waterway.

(2) Any person who fails to comply with paragraph (1) commits an offence.

### by-law 5 Private drains

(1) The owner, occupier, or lessee of any building, premises or land, shall construct such drain as the Council may, by notice, require for the effectual removal of all storm, slop and waste water from such building, premises or land.

(2) Such drains shall be properly connected to a public drain or to such other outlet approved by the Council and shall be kept in a clean and sanitary condition.

(3) Any person who fails to comply with paragraph (1) or (2) commits an offence.

### by-law 6 Council may undertake work

If any owner, occupier or lessee of any building, premises or land shall fail to comply with a notice given under by-law 3 or 5 within such time as the Council may in such notice prescribe, the Council may undertake the work, and the cost shall be a debt due to the Council which may be recovered in a court of competent jurisdiction.

### by-law 7 Collection and disposal of garbage

(1) Garbage shall be deposited only in garbage pans supplied by the occupier and approved by the Council or an authorised officer and the garbage pans shall be in accordance with the requirements of Part 10 of the Public Health Regulations 1937 and shall be fitted with one or more handles.

(2) No person, other than an employee of the Council, shall collect garbage without the permission in writing of the Council.

(3) No person, other than an employee of the Council, shall deposit or cause or permit any other person to deposit any garbage or any other refuse at the dump without the permission in writing of the Council, or an authorised officer, and only at such times and places and in such a manner as the Council or an authorised officer may direct.

(4) Garbage receptacles shall be placed at the side of the road to be taken by the collecting vehicle or in such other places the Council or any authorised officer may permit in respect of any particular premises, in which case the owner or the occupier of such premises shall provide an easy means of access for removal of garbage.

(5) The occupier of any residence, shop, business, premises or other building where there may be an accumulation of kitchen refuse or decomposing matter of any kind shall provide the number of garbage receptacles required by the Council.

(6) A person who contravenes this by-law commits an offence and, in addition to any penalty to which he or she is liable under this by-law shall remove or cause to be removed such garbage or other refuse in such a manner and within such time as the Council or an authorised officer may direct.

(7) The Council may cause the garbage or other refuse under paragraph (6) to be removed and may recover the costs and expenses incurred in such removal from the occupier.

### by-law 8 Latrines and privies

(1) The owner of any dwelling house, business premises, lodging house or any place where persons are accommodated or labour is employed shall construct latrines or privies of such type and number as the Council may, by notice, require.

(2) A person who fails to comply with paragraph (1) commits an offence.

### by-law 9 Cleaning of streets

(1) No person shall upon any street or public place throw or deposit or cause or suffer to be thrown or deposited any offensive matter, tins or refuse of any description, gravel, sand or soil.

(2) No person shall upon any street or in the bus station permit the leakage of oil of any description from any vehicle.

(3) The occupier or owner of any business premises shall not allow trade goods, case, boxes, containers, barrels of any merchandise of any description to remain on any street.

(4) No person shall permit any cattle, horse, sheep, pig or goat to graze upon any public place without the written consent of the town clerk or any person authorised by the Council.

(5) Any person who fails to comply with this by-law commits an offence.

### by-law 10 Obstruction etc an offence

Any person who obstructs, molests or hinders any officer or employee of the Council in the execution of his or her duty or who uses violent or threatening language or attempts in any way whatsoever to intimidate such employee or who endeavours by any bribe, threat or promise to cause any such employee to neglect or exceed his or her duty, shall be guilty of an offence.

## PART 3 PUBLIC PARKS [by-law 11-19]

### by-law 11 Exclusive use of public parks

(1) Subject to by-law 1 , the Council may grant permission to any sporting, social or other organisation or body to have exclusive use of the whole or any part of any public park on any particular day or days for purposes approved by the Council and may give such organisation or body power to levy charges for admission.

(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1) , the Council may, revoke such permission to enable the public park to be used for other purposes in connection with a special occasion.

(3) The Council may, declare any public park to be unusable, in which case the flying of a red flag and the posting of notice declaring such park to be unusable, at the entrance to the park shall be deemed to be sufficient notice to all persons that the park is unusable.

(4) Any person who contravenes paragraph (3) commits an offence.

### by-law 12 Method of application and approval

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) , any application for the grant of exclusive use of any public park shall be made to the town clerk no less than 14 days when such use is required and shall be subject to approval by resolution of the Council.

(2) Where, by reason of special circumstances, it is not possible for an application to be made at least 14 days, such application may be made to the town clerk who shall consult the mayor, whose decision shall be deemed to be the decision of the Council and shall be reported to the Council at the next meeting thereof.

### by-law 13 Council may retain exclusive use of any public park

(1) The Council may, by resolution, retain to itself the exclusive use of the whole or any part of any public park on any special occasion.

(2) The Council may set aside any public park or any portion of it for use as a children's playground and install equipment for use by children in which event the Council may prohibit the use of such equipment by persons of above such age as the Council may, by resolution, determine.

### by-law 14 Charges may be made for admission

The Council may, by resolution—

(a) make a charge to be paid by the organisation or body to which permission has been granted for the exclusive use of any public park or part of it under by-law 11 , which charge may be a lump sum or a percentage of the gross gate receipts; or

(b) make a charge to be paid by members of the public on any occasion when the Council retains to itself the exclusive use of any public park or any part of it.

### by-law 15 Gatekeeper etc to be employed by the Council

(1) The Council shall provide a gatekeeper and such other persons as may be necessary for the purpose of controlling admission to any public park, the collection of admission charges and the sale of tickets whenever the Council, by resolution, makes a charge based on a percentage of gross gate receipts under by-law 14(a) .

(2) The gatekeeper shall be responsible to the Council for certifying the gross gate receipts and the charges referred to in paragraph (1) shall be calculated in accordance with such certificate and be deducted from such receipts. All such charges shall be paid by the gatekeeper to the town clerk.

### by-law 16 Failing to pay charge for admission

Any person who enters or remains within the bounds of any public park or part of it without having paid any lawful charge for admission under the authority of these By-laws or neglects or refuses to pay such charge on demand, may forthwith be removed from the park and commits an offence.

### by-law 17 Fairgrounds

(1) No fair, circus or amusement park run for profit shall be established at any place or equipment installed in any fairground or amusement park run for profit except with permission in writing of the Council and on payment of such fees as the Council may, by resolution, from time to time, prescribe, and subject to such conditions as the Council may, by resolution, impose.

(2) The Council or its officers shall at all times have access to the place prescribed and shall have the right to inspect all equipment used therein.

(3) Any person who contravenes paragraph (1) or who fails to observe any condition imposed by that paragraph commits an offence.

### by-law 18 Hours of entry

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) , any person may enter any public park at any time.

(2) The Council may, by resolution, prescribe the hours during which any public park, other than a reserve, shall remain closed, in which case the Council shall cause a notice to be posted or affixed in a prominent place in the park specifying such hours.

(3) Any person who contravenes paragraph (2) without the general or specific permission of the town clerk may forthwith be removed from it and commits an offence.

### by-law 19 Public parks

(1) No person other than an authorised officer or employee of the Council shall in any public park—

(a) cut, pluck, injure or destroy any turf, tree, bush, shrub, flower, plant or other thing in or upon any part thereof, or dig, cut or remove any sod, turf, loam, sand, gravel or other substance;

(b) negligently or wilfully, injure, deface or remove any seat, notice, notice board, post, chair, railing, fence, barrier or the thing erected or placed therein by or with the authority of the Council;

(c) post or paint any bill, placard or notice;

(d) climb any tree;

(e) wilfully obstruct any entrance, throughfare or walk;

(f) wilfully interfere with the authorised user or with any game or sport permitted by the Council;

(g) sell or offer or expose for sale any food or other goods without the prior permission of the Council or an authorised officer;

(h) deliver or read any public lecture, prayer, sermon or address of any kind or hold or take part in or cause any public assemblage without the prior permission of the Council and then only at such times and places as the Council may permit;

(i) draw, take or drive any truck, car, motorcycle, bicycle, scooter, wheel barrow, carriage or other vehicle, other than an invalid’s chair or child’s perambulator or push cart, without the prior permission of the Council or an authorised officer;

(j) bathe, wade or wash in any such pond or pool therein or wilfully take, injure or destroy, or attempt to take, injure or destroy, any fish in any such pond or pool;

(k) throw or deposit, or cause or suffer to be thrown or deposited, in a pavilion any litter, garbage or offensive matter, except in a receptacle provided by the Council for that purpose;

(l) aid in or abet any act or thing which may not be specifically mentioned in these By-laws and which may tend to the injury or disfigurement or to interfere with the use and enjoyment by the public for the purpose of exercise or recreation.

(2) No person shall in any public park—

(a) play at or engage in any game or exercise in such a manner as to injure any path, turf, tree, shrub, flower, plant, building or other structure or thing or upon any part;

(b) wilfully obstruct any officer or employee of the Council in the execution of any work in connection with such public park.

(3) Any person who contravenes or fails to comply with this by-law commits an offence.

## PART 4 BUS STATION [by-law 20-27]

### by-law 20 Management of bus station

(1) The bus station shall be under the immediate management and control of the town clerk.

(2) The Council may appoint an attendant or attendants to assist the town clerk in the discharge of this function.

(3) Subject to the overall direction of the Council, the town clerk shall have responsibility for the allocation to bus operators of stands in the bus station and for the erection of traffic signs.

(4) Any person who stops or parks an omnibus at a stand other than that allocated to the bus operator by the town clerk or who fails to comply with any direction on a traffic sign erected at the bus station commits an offence.

### by-law 21 Licence to use bus station

Each bus operator desiring to use the bus station shall make application in Form 1 set out in Schedule 1, for a licence for his or her omnibus to use the bus station.

### by-law 22 Licences

All licences to use the bus station shall be issued on behalf of the Council by the clerk and in Form 2 set out in Schedule 1 and shall have effect in respect only of the omnibus described in the licence.

### by-law 23 Fees

(1) The licence fees for the use of the bus station by an omnibus shall be in accordance with the fees set out in Part 1 of Schedule 2 and shall be collected and charged in accordance with paragraphs (2) , (3) , (4) and (5) .

(2) The licence fees payable in respect of each omnibus using the bus station shall be charged in respect of each calendar month and in arrear.

(3) In respect of omnibus, the licence fees to be charged for any such monthly period shall be computed on the total number of visits of the omnibus to the bus station during the month and shall be calculated from the timetable approved by the Land Transport Authority in respect of the road service licence under which the omnibus shall have been operating during the month or from any other information which the Land Transport Authority shall think fit to supply to the clerk from its records or from any other information obtained by the clerk under the provisions of paragraph (4) .

(4) If the clerk considers that the number of visits to the bus station of any omnibus cannot be satisfactorily calculated from any timetable, he or she may, by written notice, require the bus operator concerned to supply him or her with particulars of numbers of visits to the bus station and times thereof, of each omnibus operating under the operator’s road service licence during the month the subject of the computation and any bus operator so required to supply such particulars shall within 7 days after the receipt by him or her of such notice deliver to the clerk a written statement, signed by such operator, or under his or her authority, and containing a true and correct statement of the total number of visits to the bus station and the times thereof of each such omnibus during that month.

(5) All licence fees payable to the Council under this by-law shall be recovered by the Council as civil debts by due process of law.

### by-law 24 Payment of fees

Each bus operator shall pay to the Council the licence fees payable by him or her within 15 days after he or she has been notified of the amount.

### by-law 25 Bus station not to be used without licence

(1) No bus operator shall be entitled to use the bus station for the purpose of any omnibus unless he or she holds a valid licence issued under these By-laws in respect of such omnibus.

(2) No omnibus shall enter or use the bus station at any time unless a valid licence issued under these By-laws is held by a bus operator of the omnibus and any person who contravenes this paragraph commits an offence.

(3) No bus operator shall use the bus station while any licence fees owing by him or her under these By-laws remain unpaid after the due date.

### by-law 26 Revocation of licence

The Council may revoke the licence of any bus operator and prohibit the entry of the omnibuses into the station who fails to pay by the due date any licence fees payable by him or her or who is convicted of any offence under this Part and may prohibit the entry of omnibus and the operator on the bus station.

### by-law 27 Control of bus station

(1) No person shall without the written authority of the Council sell or offer or expose for sale any produce or goods in or around the bus station.

(2) No person shall place or deposit in or on any omnibus standing in the bus station any placard, handbill, advertisement or notice, pamphlet, books or paper without the prior consent of the owner or driver of such omnibus.

(3) No person shall display in or on the bus station or any shelter any placard, handbill, advertisement or notice, pamphlet, book or paper without the prior consent of the Council and on such terms and conditions as the Council shall, by resolution, determine.

(4) Any person who contravenes this by-law commits an offence.

## PART 5 NOISE PREVENTION [by-law 28]

### by-law 28 Noise Prevention

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) , no person shall sound or play upon any musical or noisy instrument or operate a loud speaker or radio, or a tape recorder in any street, public park or any other park, garden, recreation area, commercial complex, sports ground or reserve to which the public have access or any vehicle in any street.

(2) Subject to paragraph (2) , a person shall not, so as to cause annoyance or disturbance to the public or a member of the public—

(a) play, or cause or permit to be played, a record player, tape recorder, radio or musical instrument, loud hailer, loud speaker, or other noisy instrument; or

(b) shout, stamp, clap, sing, or otherwise cause excessive noise either personally or by way of any vehicle, machine or other contraption, except to such extent as may be reasonably necessary in connection with the pursuance of an authorised business.

(3) This by-law does not apply—

(a) to the operation of a loud speaker for military or police purposes;

(b) sounding or playing of musical instruments by a band or musicians playing with the prior permission of the Council;

(c) to the operation of a loud speaker or the sounding or playing of a musical or noisy instrument at a pleasure fair field with the prior permission of the Council;

(d) to the sounding or playing of any musical or noisy instrument or the operation of any loud speaker in a procession or public assembly if permission for such instrument or loud speaker has been given under the Public Order Act 1969 ;

(e) to the operation of any mobile cinema van by any department of Government or by any person with prior permission of the Council;

(f) to the operation of any loud speaker or the sounding or playing of any musical or noisy instrument operated or played with prior permission of the Council at any sporting, recreational, social, religious or charitable assembly, event or fixture, conducted with the prior permission of the Council, under by-law 11(1) or by-law 19(1)(h) ;

(g) to the operation of any loud speaker or the sounding or playing of any musical or noisy instrument, operated or played with the prior permission of the Council, at any sporting, recreational, social, religious or charitable assembly, event or fixture, conducted in any park, garden, recreation area, sports ground or reserve, other than a public park, to which the public have access;

(h) to the operation of any loud speaker or the sounding or playing of any noisy or musical instrument in any place in respect of which the Council has granted exemption, either generally or on any particular occasion;

(i) to the operation of any loud speaker in any case of emergency.

(4) Any person who fails to comply with the provisions of this by-law commits an offence.

## PART 6 DANCE HALL [by-law 29-34]

### by-law 29 Dance halls to be licensed

(1) No person shall, within the town keep or use or permit to be kept or used any dance hall without first obtaining from the Council a licence on payment of an annual fee prescribed by the Council.

[para (1) am Act 28 of 2020 s 4 and Sch, 1 August 2020]

(2) The premises to be used as dance hall are to be of sound construction and when required by the Council, the hall shall be air-conditioned and made soundproof and the premises shall be to the satisfaction of the Council, provided that a licence may be granted for any period not exceeding 30 days upon payment of a fee of $20.

(3) Notwithstanding paragraph (1) , no dance hall may be used on a Sunday, on Good Friday or on Christmas Day.

### by-law 30 Exemptions

(1) No licence shall be required for the use of the Nasinu town hall as a dance hall.

(2) The Council may by resolution exempt from by-law 29(1) any club the main purpose of which is to provide entertainment for its members, but which occasionally holds entertainments which are open to the public if the Council is satisfied that such exemption is not likely to result in annoyance to or disturbance of the public.

(3) The Council may by resolution revoke any exemption granted under paragraph (2) if annoyance to or disturbance of the public has occurred.

### by-law 31 Licences

(1) Any person desirous of obtaining a licence under these By-laws may apply to the town clerk in Form 3 set out in Schedule 1.

(2) The Council may grant a licence to any person as it shall think fit upon such terms and conditions and subject to such restrictions as it may consider appropriate. Any licence, other than one issued under by-law 29(1) , is valid for one year from the date of issue.

(3) A licence issued under this by-law shall be in Form 4 set out in Schedule 1.

### by-law 32 Transfer of licence

Upon the application of any licensee, the town clerk may permit the transfer of any licence issued under the provisions of these By-laws to such other person, as he or she may consider appropriate.

### by-law 33 Refusal, revocation etc of licence

(1) The Council may refuse to grant a licence to any person or in respect of any premises or may revoke or suspend or amend any licence on any grounds which it considers sufficient.

(2) Where it is proposed that a licence be refused, revoked, suspended or amended under paragraph (1) , the Council shall give to the applicant or the licensee, as the case may be, 14 days notice in writing of the meeting of the Council at which the refusal, revocation, or suspension is to be considered and in such notice the Council shall set out the grounds upon which consideration is being given to such refusal, revocation or suspension.

(3) No licence shall be refused, revoked suspended or amended by the Council unless the applicant or the licensee has been given an opportunity of being heard by the Council.

### by-law 34 Offences

Any person who contravenes by-law 29(1) or 29(3) commits an offence.

## PART 7 MARKET [by-law 35-76]

### by-law 35 Market

The Council may establish a Nasinu market or other markets.

### by-law 36 Market master

The market shall be under immediate management and control of a market master appointed by and be responsible to the Council.

### by-law 37 Prohibited sales

No person shall sell or offer for sale in the market any article other than produce.

### by-law 38 Use of market

The market may be divided into sections and different charges may be prescribed for each section by the Council’s resolution.

### by-law 39 Preference to producers

Preference for allocation of stalls or space in the market shall be given to producers or their *bona fide* agents selling direct to the public.

### by-law 40 Sale outside the market

No person not being the holder of a wholesale and retail or retail store licence or an unexpired hawker’s licence or selling from an established mini market shall sell produce other than fish, in any place at any time outside the market within the town of Nasinu, provided that the Council may by resolution approve the sale of certain items of produce outside the market within the town of Nasinu for festivals, carnivals, bazaars and functions of that nature.

### by-law 41 Sale of fish

No person shall sell by retail fresh fish at any place at any time outside the market, provided that this by-law does not apply to the sale of fish wholesale or by a butcher, fishmonger, or any premises approved by the Council.

### by-law 42 Hours

The market or any section of the market shall be open for business during such hours as the Council by resolution may determine.

### by-law 43 Direction by market master

All persons using or being within the precincts of the market shall obey reasonable directions given by the market master for the purpose of observing good order and cleanliness of the market.

### by-law 44 Sale from stall

No person shall sell or offer or expose for the sale any article in the market except at a stall allocated to him or her by the Council or market master and during the hours that the market is open for business or permitted under these By-laws.

### by-law 45 Receipts to be exhibited

Every stallholder shall exhibit and keep exhibited on the stall while the same is in use current receipt for payment of the market charges in such manner as the market master shall direct in such position that it can conveniently be read and inspected at all times by the market master or any attendant or person authorised by the Council.

### by-law 46 Allocation of stalls

The allocation, reallocation, transfer and replacement of stalls in the market shall be made by the Council or the market master in their or his or her sole discretion and the decision of the Council or market master in this respect shall be final.

### by-law 47 Alteration to stalls

No person shall make any alterations to a stall or exhibit any notice thereon or introduce any fittings, furniture or impediments of any kind without the authority of the Council or the market master and any such thing introduced without its or his or her authority may be removed by the market master and the cost shall be borne by the stall holder and the Council or the market master will not be held liable for any claim or damage arising out of such removal.

### by-law 48 Dues to be paid

(1) No person shall occupy or use a stall if the charges which are due and payable for the use of the stall are outstanding.

(2) The market master may remove the articles kept on a stall without due payment of charges at the risk of the owner of the said articles and recover all dues and costs to the Council upon selling the articles so removed and the Council will not be held liable for any claim or for damages arising out of such sale.

### by-law 49 Fish stalls

(1) No person shall sell fish in the market other than the fish section or stall set aside by the Council or market master for sale of fish.

(2) A person shall not bring into or offer for sale in the market fresh fish other than white-bait and similar small fish, shell fish or turtles that have not been gutted, gilled and cleaned.

(3) The market master may remove forthwith any fish offered for sale contrary to paragraphs (1) and (2) and destroy same in a manner he or she thinks proper and the Council or the market master may not be held liable for any claim or for damages arising out of such removal and disposal, and any expenses incurred for such removal will be recovered from the person who failed to comply with paragraph (1) or (2) .

### by-law 50 Charges

(1) The charges for the use of stall and other facilities set out in Part 2 of Schedule 2 and shall be payable in advance on demand by the market master or any attendant. The market master or attendant shall at the time of receiving payment of any charges under this by-law shall issue a receipt.

(2) On such public holidays on which under by-law 42 the market or any section of the market remains open the charges for the use of a stall be double the charges unless otherwise approved by Council resolution.

### by-law 51 Cleanliness

(1) Stallholders shall keep their stalls in a clean and orderly condition and shall dispose of any litter and refuse as and when required by the market master.

(2) A person shall place articles offered or exposed for sale in the market;

(a) on the raised concrete or wooden stalls provided in the market;

(b) on tables, benches, mats, sacking or other articles as approved by the market master.

### by-law 52 Exchange, subletting and surrendering of stall

(1) No stallholder shall exchange, sublet, surrender or part with the possession of his or her stall or any part thereof without the prior approval in writing of the Council.

(2) A person who fails to comply with paragraph (1) may be deprived of his or her stall.

### by-law 53 Sharing of stalls

(1) No stallholder shall share his or her stall with another vendor or, person without the prior approval of the market master in writing.

(2) A person who fails to comply with paragraph (1) will be deprived of his or her stall.

### by-law 54 Selling of mixed kava

(1) Mixed kava shall not be sold at any stall or in any part of the market save only at the market kava kiosks authorised for such purpose by the Council.

(2) Any person who sells mixed kava at a stall other than duly allocated for that purpose maybe deprived of his or her stall and his or her licence may be cancelled.

### by-law 55 Wholesaling

The market shall not be used by any stallholder for the conduct of a wholesale business.

### by-law 56 Hygiene

(1) Stallholders shall keep their produce or other goods offered for sale and expose them for sale in a hygienic manner as it is practicable and shall obey all reasonable directions of the market master in this regard.

(2) If a person fails to keep produce in a manner described in paragraph (1) , the market master may impound the produce so kept and all expenses for such impounding will be recovered from the stallholder or vendor concerned.

### by-law 57 Shouting etc

No person shall in the precincts of the market advertise goods by auction without the written permission of the market master and no person shall unnecessarily shout or use any abusive or bad language in the market.

### by-law 58 Unwholesome goods

No person shall bring into or keep in the market anything which is bad, obnoxious, unwholesome, or dangerous and the market master may remove or cause to be removed any such thing brought within the precincts of the market and the cost of such removal shall be paid by the owner.

### by-law 59 Dogs

No person shall bring a dog into the market or suffer it to remain there and the market master shall have power to require the removal of the same at the risk of the owner.

### by-law 60 Disease

(1) No person suffering from any infectious or contagious disease or having recently been exposed to infection by such a disease shall engage in the sale of any goods in the market.

(2) A person so found shall be prevented from selling goods unless he or she produces a medical certificate to the satisfaction of the Council.

### by-law 61 Closing hours

No person shall enter upon or remain in the market or sell or offer for sale any produce in the market outside the business hours herein before specified without the prior approval in writing of the Council or the market master save and except as provided in by-law 42 .

### by-law 62 Obstruction of officials

No person shall obstruct, prevent, hinder or interfere with the market master or any attendant in the performance of any duty under these By-laws and no person shall enter the market whilst drunk or excite a riot or public disorder or curse or swear or use any gross indecent filthy or abusive language or gamble or be guilty of any misconduct therein.

### by-law 63 Obstruction

No person shall obstruct any of the roadway, footpaths, entrances, thoroughfares, passages or divisions of the market or shall loiter therein.

### by-law 64 Use or movement of vehicles on or in the market

(1) Except where otherwise directed by the Council the driver of any vehicle shall use such entrances and exits to and from the market as shall from time to time be fixed by resolution of the Council and notified by suitable signs.

(2) A person shall not drive a vehicle into the market for purposes not connected with the market.

(3) A person shall not drive a vehicle within the market at a speed in excess of 20 kilometres per hour.

(4) A person shall not drive or use a vehicle in the market in negligent or dangerous manner or without due care or attention or reasonable consideration for the other persons using the market or are in the market premises.

(5) A driver of any vehicle within the market shall not leave the engine of any such vehicle running whilst the vehicle is parked or is being loaded or unloaded.

(6) Except where the Council approves in writing a person shall not park or stand any vehicle within the market for a period longer than duly authorised.

(7) The Council may by resolution from time to time prohibit any vehicle or class of vehicle or equipment from entering or being in the market.

(8) A person shall not drive a vehicle into or cause a vehicle to enter or be within the market contrary to a prohibition under paragraph (7) .

(9)

(1) The Council may by resolution from time to time—

(a) set apart any area of the market for the parking of vehicles;

(b) fix parking fees in respect of the area.

(2) The Council when fixing fees pursuant to subparagraph (1)(b) may—

(a) fix the fees either generally or for a specified period;

(b) fix different scales of fees for different classes of vehicles and for different area;

(c) specify the times during which the fees are payable;

(d) exempt specified classes of vehicles from payment of the fees.

(3) Where a fee has been fixed under subparagraph (1)(b) in respect of the parking of a vehicle in an area, a person shall not park or stand the vehicle in that area unless a person has paid the fee so fixed.

(4) The Council may by resolution and upon such terms as may be specified in the resolution transfer to any person its right to collect fees fixed under subparagraph (1)(b) .

### by-law 65 Admission to market

The market master shall have power to refuse admission to or reject from the market any person of undesirable character or any young children unattended and any person contravening or failing to comply with by-law 57 , 60 or 61 .

### by-law 66 Empty casks etc

(1) No person shall, without the consent of the market master bring or cause to be brought into the market any empty casks, crates or lumber of any kind and the market master may remove or cause to be removed all such articles at the risk and cost of the owner of such articles.

(2) Any such articles so removed may be sold by the Council without any liability and the Council may recover out of the process of such sale the costs and expenses incurred in such removal and sale.

### by-law 67 Unsold goods

(1) Goods unsold at the close of the day may with the permission of the market master be left in the market while the market is closed at the risk of the owner.

(2) Goods which have been left in the market when the market is closed without the market master’s permission may be removed and disposed of by the market master.

(3) The cost of the removal and disposal of such goods under paragraph (2) shall be a debt due to the Council from the person who left the goods in the market and may be recovered on sale of such goods by the market master without any liability and the Council may recover out of the proceeds of such sale the costs and expenses incurred in such removal and sale.

(4) Neither the Council nor the market master is liable for theft, loss or damage to goods left in the market with the market master's permission when it is closed unless the theft, loss or damage results from a deliberate or negligent act of the market master.

(5) Goods may be left in the care of the market master on payment in advance of the prescribed charges as determined by Council's resolution.

### by-law 68 Spitting

No person shall spit or discharge any nasal fluid or mucus on to any roadway, footpath or other part of the market or on to any utensil, produce, article or thing standing or being in the market.

### by-law 69 Obstruction to roadway

(1) No person shall place any matter or thing on any roadway, footpath or passage, or neglect or refuse to remove such matter or thing on being required so to do by the market master or any attendant.

(2) The market master or any attendant or any approved security guard engaged by the market master may remove any matter or thing so placed and the cost of such removal shall be paid by the owner or his or her agent. The market master or any person engaged in such removal is not liable for any claim or damages arising out of such removal.

### by-law 70 Permitted articles only to be sold

(1) No person shall sell any article other than the articles permitted for sale by him or her at the stall allocated to him or her.

(2) The market master may remove such articles not permitted for sale and the Council or the market master will not be held liable for any claim or damages arising out of such removal and sale.

### by-law 71 Litter

A person may be deprived of his or her stall by the market master if he or she throws, drops, deposits or otherwise parts with the possession of litter or refuse in the market except in receptacles provided for that purpose.

### by-law 72 Cooking prohibited

A person who cooks anything in a stall allocated to him or her except at the food kiosks specially allocated for the purpose commits an offence under these By-laws and shall be deprived of the stall allocated to him or her.

### by-law 73 Certain structures prohibited

The market master may demolish and remove any structure erected in the market by any person without the prior written approval of the market master and the market master or the Council is not held liable for any claim or damages of such demolition and removal.

### by-law 74 Washing of market

(1) The market master may require removal of all goods from the market for the purposes of cleaning and washing of the market and if a person fails to comply then the market master may remove goods at the owner's cost.

(2) The Council or market master may not be liable for any damages caused to goods so removed.

### by-law 75 Unauthorised occupation of stalls

The market master may remove any produce or other articles from a stall occupied without the market master’s permission and the cost of such removal must be paid by the person who occupied the stall without permission and the market master or Council is not liable for damages caused by such removal.

### by-law 76 Penalty

A person who contravenes any of these By-laws commits an offence and is liable to a fine not exceeding $200 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months and to a further fine of $10 for every day during which such contravention continues.

## PART 8 LIBRARIES [by-law 77-80]

### by-law 77 Offences

Any person who—

(a) wilfully obstructs the librarian in the performance of his or her duties;

(b) wilfully disturbs any other user of the library;

(c) causes or allows any dog or other animal belonging to him or her or under his or her control to enter or remain in the library;

(d) brings into any part of the library any wheeled vehicle or conveyance, other than an invalid’s chair or child’s perambulator;

(e) smokes any tobacco product or strikes any naked light in the library;

(f) wilfully soils, tears out, defaces, damages, injures or destroys any book forming part of the contents of the library;

(g) except with the permission of the librarian, fixes any bill, placard or notice to or upon any part of the library;

(h) except with the permission of the Council, partakes of any food or drink in the library,

commits an offence.

### by-law 78 Taking of books

No person shall, except in accordance with rules made by the Council under these By-laws, take any book away from the library, and any person who contravenes this by-law commits an offence.

### by-law 79 Return of books

Any person having charge of being in possession of any book forming part of the contents of the library shall return such book to the librarian within 7 days of the service upon him or her of a notice requiring him or her so to do, and any person who contravenes this by laws commits an offence.

### by-law 80 Rules and charges

The Council may, by resolution—

(a) make rules relating to the library or any part thereof or with regard to the borrowing of any book therefrom;

(b) levy a charge for the use of any book in the library;

(c) levy a charge in respect of the late return of any book.

## PART 9 STREET NUMBERING [by-law 81]

### by-law 81 Street numbering

(1) Where the Council decides that numbers shall be affixed on land or to building for the purpose of designating and numbering such land or building in any road or street or driveway, the owners or occupiers of such land or building shall be informed of the Council’s intention to affix such numbers at least 21 days before they are affixed by publication of a notice in the Gazette and in 2 consecutive editions of any newspaper published in Fiji and circulating within the town area.

(2) Where any employee of the Council has affixed any number on any land or onto building for the aforesaid purpose, it shall be an offence for person unlawfully to remove, deface mutilate or tamper with such number.

(3) A person who obstructs any other person who enters any land or building under the instructions of the Council in order to inspect, affix, reaffix, or change such numbers commits an offence.

## PART 10 CAR PARK [by-law 82-99]

### by-law 82 Establishment of car park

(1) The Council may from time to time by resolution, set apart any area to be used for the parking of commercial vehicles, private vehicles, taxis or other vehicles.

(2) A description of any such area shall be advertised by the Council at least once each in the Gazette and a daily newspaper circulating in Fiji.

### by-law 83 Attendant

(1) A car park shall be under the immediate management and control of the car park attendant.

(2) All persons using or being within precincts of a car park shall obey all instructions and reasonable directions given by the attendant for the purpose of observing the good order and cleanliness of the car park.

### by-law 84 Prohibitions

No person without prior approval of the Council, may make any alterations to the alignments of a car park or exhibit any notice within a car park or introduce any fittings, fixtures or impediments of any kind into a car park and any such thing introduced may be removed by the attendant.

### by-law 85 Entry to and from car park

All drivers of motor vehicles using a car park shall observe the directions posted at the car park.

### by-law 86 Charges

The charges for admission to and use of, a car park are set out in Part 3 of Schedule 2.

### by-law 87 Parking

Taxis, commercial vehicles and private vehicles using a car park shall park only within the limits set apart for the use of vehicles of the class to which they respectively belong as indicated by notices and as directed by the attendant.

### by-law 88 Allocation of stands

(1) Each operator desiring to use a commercial vehicle stand shall make an application in Form 5 set out in Schedule 1 , for a permit for his or her taxi or lorry to use the stand.

(2) All permits to use the commercial vehicle stand shall be in Form 6 set out in Schedule 1 and shall be issued by the town clerk on behalf of the Council.

### by-law 89 Production of receipts

Drivers of taxis, commercial vehicles and private vehicles shall produce for inspection their parking receipts or tickets upon demand by the attendant.

### by-law 90 Validity of receipt or ticket

A parking receipt or ticket issued in respect of a taxi, commercial vehicle or a private vehicle shall be valid for the period specified in the receipt.

### by-law 91 Obstructing attendant etc

No person shall obstruct, prevent, hinder or interfere with any attendant in the performance of any duties under these By-laws.

### by-law 92 Obstructing entrances etc

No person shall obstruct any of the entrances, thoroughfares or division of a car park or shall loiter therein.

### by-law 93 Power to refuse admission etc

The attendant shall have power to refuse admission to or to eject from a car park any persons of undesirable character or any young children unattended.

### by-law 94 Obstructing footpath

No person shall place any matter or thing on any roadway, footpath or passage in a car park so as to obstruct the free use of such roadway, footpath or passage, or neglect or refuse to remove such matter or thing on being required to do so by the attendant.

### by-law 95 Spitting

No person shall spit or discharge any nasal fluid or mucus on to any roadway, footpath or other part of a car park.

### by-law 96 Offence in connection with vehicle

The owner or driver of any taxi, commercial vehicle or a private vehicle using a car park upon which the prescribed fees have not been paid or in respect of which a valid parking receipt or ticket is not produced to the attendant on demand commits an offence.

### by-law 97 Protection from liability

Nothing contained in these By-laws or in any parking receipt or ticket issued by the Council makes the Council responsible for any loss or damage to any vehicle admitted to or using a car park or for injury to any person entering therein.

### by-law 98 Restriction on sale or exposure for the sale of articles

No person shall sell or offer or expose for sale any produce or goods in a car park without the prior consent in writing of the Council.

### by-law 99 Offences

Any person contravening or failing to comply with the provisions of this Part except by-law 83(1) , 86 , 90 , 93 or 97 commits an offence.

## PART 11 MISCELLANEOUS [by-law 100]

### by-law 100 Penalties

A person who contravenes these By-laws commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100, or in the case of a continuing offence to a fine of $10 for each day during which such offence continues, and, in addition to any such penalty, any expense incurred by the Council in consequence of any breach of these By-laws shall be paid by such person to the Council.
