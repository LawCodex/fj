# SAVUSAVU (MARKET) BY-LAWS 1998

Savusavu (Market) By-laws 1998 (LN 141 of 1998) commenced on 4 December 1998, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Savusavu (Market) By-laws 1998.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**attendant** means any employee of the Council designated to carry out duties in the market;

**Council** means the Savusavu Town Council;

**fish** means fresh fish, dried or smoked fish and includes shell fish, seaweed, sea slugs or other edible sea produce;

**market** means the Savusavu Market Building including the land contained in State lease Lot 1 and 2 (part of) maintained and controlled by the Savusavu Town Council;

**produce—** (a) means fish, fresh fruit, vegetables, rice, split peas, dried pulses, or similar goods, live poultry and other edible birds and eggs; and

(b) includes any agricultural or forest product which is an article of food or drink in an unprepared state, locally grown tobacco and locally made handicrafts, such as baskets, mats, wood carvings or similar articles;

**shell-fish** crab, prawns, crayfish, oysters, mussels, edible molluscs, crustaceans;

**stall** includes any structure or place in the market used for the sale of produce; and

**stallholder** means—

(a) in the case of a permanent stallholder, the person in personal charge of the stall, in whose name the receipt for the current month's stall charges is issued; and

(b) in the case of a casual or daily stallholder, the person in personal charge of the stall who has currently paid the fees for the stall and who is the holder of a receipt issued under by-law 11 .

## by-law 3 Market master

The market is under the management and control of a market master appointed by and responsible to the Council.

## by-law 4 Sales outside the market

(1) No person may sell produce—

(a) inside the market who is not a stallholder; or

(b) outside the market who is not a holder of a wholesale or retail store licence.

(2) No person may sell or offer for sale in the market any article other than an article included in the definition of produce.

## by-law 5 Sale of fish

(1) No person may sell fish at any place in the market except at the stall or place set aside for the purpose by the Council or the market master and the fish must not be placed in direct contact with the floor of the market building or in direct contact with any path, road, grass or ground.

(2) A person who sells fish at the market must place it on the raised concrete stalls on tables, benches, mats, sacking or other materials approved by the market master.

(3) Subject to paragraph (5) , no person may, in Savusavu Town, sell by retail fresh fish at any place other than the market.

(4) If the market is closed for business, no person may sell, by retail, fresh fish within the town of Savusavu.

(5) This by-law does not apply to fresh fish sold by—

(a) a wholesaler;

(b) a butcher;

(c) a fishmonger; or

(d) a person licensed under the provisions of any Act relating to the licensing of businesses.

## by-law 6 Fish to be gutted etc

(1) A person must not bring to, or offer for sale in, the market fresh fish unless it has been gutted, gilled and cleaned.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to white bait or similar small fish or shell fish.

## by-law 7 Price of fish

A person who sells fish at the market must display its sale price in a notice conspicuously marked in letters and figures of not less than 15 centimeters in height.

## by-law 8 Opening hours

(1) The market must be opened for business as follows—

| | |
| --- | --- |
| (a) Mondays to Fridays: | 7.00 am to 5.30 pm. |
| (b) Saturdays, not being a public holiday: | 5.30 am to 4.00 pm. |
| (c) Saturdays, being a public holiday: | 5.30 am to 12 pm. |

(2) The Council may authorise that the market be opened for business on a Sunday or a public holiday if other shops are authorised by the Council to remain open for the purpose of serving tourist cruise ships.

## by-law 9 Direction by market master

A person who uses or is within the precincts of the market must obey any reasonable directions given by the market master.

## by-law 10 Allocation of stalls

(1) ) The Council or the market master must allocate stalls in the market to stallholders and the decision of the Council or market master is final.

(2) A stallholder may, with the prior approval of the Council or the market master, nominate another person as a stall holder in his or her temporary absence.

## by-law 11 Receipt and prices to be exhibited

(1) A stallholder must exhibit and keep exhibited on the stall while it is in use the current receipt for the payment of the market charges in any manner the market master directs and in a position where it can conveniently be read and inspected at all times by the market master or any attendant.

(2) A stallholder must ensure that all produce and goods for sale at the stall are legibly and conspicuously marked with the sale price.

## by-law 12 Alteration of stalls

(1) No person may make any alteration to a stall, exhibit any notice on it or introduce any fittings, fixtures, furniture or impediments or any kind without the authority of the Council or the market master.

(2) The market or the market master may remove any thing done to a stall contrary to paragraph (1) .

## by-law 13 Charges to be paid

No person may occupy or use a stall on which any charges for its use are outstanding.

## by-law 14 Charges

(1) The charges for the use of stalls and other market facilities are as set out in the Schedule and are payable in advance to the market master or an attendant.

(2) Upon receiving payment for any charges under this by-law, the market master or an attendant, as the case may be, must immediately issue a receipt.

## by-law 15 Exchange, subletting and sharing of stalls

No stallholder may exchange, sublet, surrender or part with the possession of a stall or share a stall with another vendor or person except with the permission of the market master.

## by-law 16 Cleanliness

A stallholder must keep his or her stalls in a clean and orderly condition, and must dispose of any litter and refuse as directed by the market master.

## by-law 17 Litter

No person may abandon or cause to be abandoned any liner in or upon any part of the market except in a receptacle provided for the purpose.

## by-law 18 Hygiene

A stallholder must—

(a) keep his or her produce offered for sale, and expose it for sale, in a hygienic manner; and

(b) obey any reasonable directions relating to hygiene given by the market master.

## by-law 19 Selling mixed yaqona restricted

No person may sell or offer for sale mixed yaqona in any part of the market without the prior approval of the market master.

## by-law 20 Cooking in the market

No person may use any type of cooking, grilling or baking apparatus for the purpose of cooking, grilling or baking food in any part of the market.

## by-law 21 Shouting etc

No person may—

(a) advertise produce by shouting; or

(b) conduct a sale by auction,

in the market except with the permission of the market master.

## by-law 22 Unwholesome goods

(1) A person must not bring into or keep in the market anything which is bad, obnoxious, unwholesome, or dangerous.

(2) The market master may remove or cause to be removed any thing brought into and kept in the market contrary to paragraph (1) and the cost of removal is to be paid by the owner or person in possession or control of the thing.

## by-law 23 Diseases

A person suffering from infection or contagious disease or who has recently exposed to infection or contagious disease must not engage in the sale of produce in the market.

## by-law 24 Animal pets, birds etc

No person may bring any pet (such as bird, dog, cat etc) into the market or cause or permit it to remain there and the market master may remove any such pet from the market.

## by-law 25 Entry prohibited outside hours

(1) If the market is closed for business, no person may enter or remain in the market without the permission of the market master.

(2) No person may—

(a) enter the market when drunk;

(b) while in the market, cause a riot, curse or swear, use any gross, indecent, filthy or abusive language, or gamble or commit any other misconduct.

## by-law 26 Obstruction of officials

No person may obstruct, prevent, hinder or interfere with the market master or any attendant in the performance of a duty under these by-laws.

## by-law 27 Obstruction

No person may obstruct any of the entrances, thoroughfares, passages or divisions of the market or loiter in them.

## by-law 28 Admission to the market

The market master may refuse admission to or remove from the market any person of undesirable character or any child who is unattended.

## by-law 29 Empty casks etc

No person may bring or cause to be brought into the market empty casks, crates or lumber of any kind without the consent of the market master and the market master may remove or cause to be removed any such article and store it at the risk and costs of the owner.

## by-law 30 Unsold goods

Produce unsold at the end of the day may be left in the market at the owner's risk with the permission of the market master and any produce so left without such permission may be removed by the market master and disposed of in any manner he or she considers reasonable.

## by-law 31 Spitting etc

A person must not spit or discharge any nasal fluid or mucus on the roadway, footpath, floor or other part of the market or on to any utensil, produce, article or thing standing or being in the market except in the bins provided for collection of litter.

## by-law 32 Care of produce

Produce may, with the consent of the market master, be left by the stallholder in the care of the market master upon payment in advance of a charge prescribed by the Council.

## by-law 33 Obstruction of roadway etc

No person may place any matter or thing on any roadway, footpath or passage in a market so as to obstruct the free use of the roadway, footway, or passage, or neglect or refuse to remove any such matter or thing on being required to do so by the market master or any attendant.

## by-law 34 Prohibition on turtle meat etc

No person may sell in the market fresh turtle meat or any other produce the sale of which is prohibited under any written law.

## by-law 35 Penalty

A person who contravenes a provision of these By-laws commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100.

## by-law 36 Repeal

Part 5 of the Savusavu Town By-laws 1974 is repealed.
