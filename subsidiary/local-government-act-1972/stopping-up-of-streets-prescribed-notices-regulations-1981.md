# STOPPING UP OF STREETS (PRESCRIBED NOTICES) REGULATIONS 1981

Stopping Up of Streets (Prescribed Notices) Regulations 1981 (LN 93 of 1981) commenced on 25 September 1981, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Stopping Up of Streets (Prescribed Notices) Regulations 1981.

## reg 2 Notification of proposal to stop up street

Where a Council proposes to stop up a street and declare it no longer to be a street, in exercise of the powers conferred upon the Council by section 109(1) of the Act, the Council shall, at least 60 days before the date on which the Council’s proposal is to take effect, cause a notice of its proposal in the form set out in Part 1 of the Schedule—

(a) to be published in the Gazette and in a newspaper published and circulating in Fiji;

(b) to be displayed in a prominent position at or near the entrance to the street from any other street;

(c) to be served—

(i) on each of the owners and occupiers of any land adjoining the street; and

(ii) on any person authorised by any enactment to carry on any undertaking of public utility and having any apparatus (including any structure constructed for the lodging therein of apparatus) under, in, upon, over, along or across the street; and

(d) to be furnished, together with a copy of the plan referred to in the notice, to the permanent secretary responsible for local government, the Commissioner of Police and the Director of Town and Country Planning.

[reg 2 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 241, 1 December 2016]

## reg 3 Notification of stopping up of street

Where a Council has stopped up a street and declared it no longer to be a street, in exercise of its powers as aforesaid, the Council shall cause a notice thereof in the form set out in Part 2 of the Schedule to be published, displayed and served as mentioned in regulation 2 and to be furnished, together with a copy of the plan referred to in the notice, as mentioned in that regulation.

## reg 4 Application in relation to portions of street

The provisions of regulations 2 and 3 shall apply in relation to any portion of a street as they apply to a street, subject to such modification as may be required.
