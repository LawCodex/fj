# SAVUSAVU (MEETINGS) BY-LAWS 1998

Savusavu (Meetings) By-laws 1998 (LN 140 of 1998) commenced on 4 December 1998, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [by-law 1-2]

### by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Savusavu (Meetings) By-laws 1998.

### by-law 2 Interpretation and application

(1) In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Act** means the Local Government Act 1972 ;

**committee** means a committee appointed under section 27 of the Act and includes a subcommittee or a special committee;

**Council** means the Savusavu Town Council;

**town clerk** means the Town Clerk of the Council appointed under section 35 of the Act and includes the acting town clerk or assistant town clerk;

**whole number of members** means the total number of elected Councillors on the Council as determined by the Electoral Commission under section 9 of the Act.

(2) These By-laws apply to every meeting of the Council and of any committee, unless otherwise specified.

## PART 2 CONDUCT OF MEETINGS [by-law 3-48]

### DIVISION 1 SPECIAL MEETINGS [by-law 3]

#### by-law 3 Powers to call special meetings

(1) The Mayor may call special meetings of the Council.

(2) If the Mayor refuses or delays to call a special meeting, 3 or more members may, in writing signed by each member, request the town clerk to call a special meeting.

(3) After receiving a request under subregulation (2) , the town clerk must call a special meeting and must state the object of it.

(4) The business at a special meeting must be limited to the object stated in the notice under subregulation (3) .

### DIVISION 2 NOTICES [by-law 4-6]

#### by-law 4 Form of notices

(1) A notice to attend a meeting of the Council or of a committee must be in writing and state the place, date and hour of the meeting.

(2) A copy of every motion of which notice has been given to be moved at a meeting must be attached to the notice to attend.

#### by-law 5 Mode of service

The town clerk must send to each member of the Council or of a committee a notice under by-law 4 for each meeting, by personal delivery or by post.

#### by-law 6 Time of service

A notice served under by-law 5 must be sent to the member's last place of business or residential address at least 3 clear days before the day fixed for the meeting.

### DIVISION 3 ORDER OF BUSINESS [by-law 7]

#### by-law 7 Order and conduct of business

(1) The business at any ordinary meeting of the Council must be in the following order—

(a) Minutes,The minutes of the previous meeting, including the minutes of any special meeting, should be read and confirmed. No discussion is to be allowed concerning any minutes except as to their accuracy as a record of the proceedings.

(b) Chairperson’s minutes,The chairperson may direct attention at any meeting to any matter or subject within the power or official control of the Council by a minute signed by himself or herself, and the minute must, when introduced, take precedence over all business before or to come before the Council and its adoption may be put by the chairperson as a motion without being seconded.

(c) Petitions,A petition must be received only as the petition of the party or parties signing it. On the presentation of a petition no debate must take place until notice of its debate has been given, and the only question to be considered by the Council when first presented is whether the petition be received or referred to a committee.

(d) Correspondence,The town clerk must submit to the Council all correspondence which cannot be dealt with departmentally under any standing order for a general or special authority or direction to be made or given by the Council. The Council may require, or the chairperson may direct, that any correspondence in connection with any other matter be submitted to and read at any meeting.

(e) Reports and recommendations of committees,The recommendations of a committee must be adopted by resolution of the Council. If a report of a committee contains distinct recommendations, the Council may take decisions separately on each recommendation. The Council may amend a report or part of the report of a committee or refer it to the committee for further consideration.

(f) Reports of officers, Questions,Sufficient notice of every question must be given to allow consideration of a reply and, if necessary, reference to other persons or documents. Every question must be put without debate. No discussion must be allowed on a reply or refusal to reply to a question.

(g) Motions,A motion must be in writing signed by the member proposing it, and must be delivered to the town clerk not later than 10.00 am on the day fixed for the service of notice to attend the meeting. A copy of the motion and the notice must be sent to each member. No motion after being placed on the business paper is to be withdrawn without the consent of the Council. This by-law applies to a motion to be considered at a special meeting.

(h) Other Business,Includes any business which the chairperson thinks fit to bring under consideration without notice.

(2) Notwithstanding other provisions of this by-law, the Council may, by majority vote, determine to proceed to any particular business out of the regular order and a motion for such a vote may be made without notice and must take precedence over all other business.

### DIVISION 4 QUORUM [by-law 8-11]

#### by-law 8 Quorum

A quorum for a meeting is one half of the whole number of members of the Council or of the committee as the case may be when that number is even, or a majority of such number if it is odd.

#### by-law 9 Adjournment for no quorum

If at the end of 15 minutes after the time at which a meeting of the Council or a committee is appointed to be held a quorum is not present, the meeting must be deferred to a later time in the day or adjourned to another date and time to be fixed by the chairperson, or, in his or her absence, by majority of the members present.

#### by-law 10 Names to be recorded in minutes

If at a meeting a quorum is not present, the names of the members present must be recorded in the minutes book.

#### by-law 11 Suspension of proceedings

(1) If at any time during a meeting the attention of the chairperson is called by a member or by the town clerk to the fact that a quorum is not present, the chairperson must suspend the proceedings for 3 minutes.

(2) If at the end of 3 minutes a quorum is still not present, the chairperson must defer the meeting to a later time in the day or adjourn the meeting to another date.

### DIVISION 5 CHAIRPERSON [by-law 12-16]

#### by-law 12 Absence of chairperson etc

If at the end of 15 minutes after the time appointed for a meeting the chairperson or deputy chairperson is absent, the members present must elect a member to preside at the meeting.

#### by-law 13 Chairperson may take part in proceedings

The chairperson may take part in all proceedings of a meeting at which he or she presides unless prevented by section 17 of the Act.

#### by-law 14 Rulings by chairperson

(1) The chairperson must preserve order at any meeting.

(2) A ruling given by the chairperson must be obeyed without debate or discussion.

(3) A ruling of the chairperson is final unless a member forthwith moves a motion of dissent.

(4) A motion of dissent, if seconded, must be put without debate or discussion.

#### by-law 15 Basis or ruling may be stated

The chairperson, when deciding a point of order or practice, may state the rule or practice which he or she thinks applicable to the case without discussing or commenting on it.

#### by-law 16 Chairperson to be heard without interruption

When the chairperson rises in his or her place during the progress of a debate a member then speaking or offering to speak must immediately resume his or her seat, and every member must observe silence, so that the chairperson may be heard without interruption.

### DIVISION 6 ORDER [by-law 17-26]

#### by-law 17 Points of order

Any member may at any time call the attention of the chairperson that a member is out of order or to any other point of order.

#### by-law 18 Observance of protocol

(1) Members must, on all occasions when in the Council or a committee, address and speak to each other by their official designations, as “chairperson”, “acting chairperson”, or “member”.

(2) With the exception of the chairperson, a member must stand while speaking at a Council meeting and must address the chairperson, except that with good reason and leave of the chairperson a member may remain seated while speaking.

(3) A member may remain seated while speaking in a committee meeting.

#### by-law 19 Chairperson to call on members to speak

If 2 or more members rise to speak at the same time, the chairperson must decide the member to speak first.

#### by-law 20 Personal reflections etc

A member must not make personal reflections on, or impute improper motives to, any other member or officer of the Council or of a committee.

#### by-law 21 Acts of disorder

A member, in a meeting of the Council or of a committee, commits an act of disorder if the member—

(a) moves or attempts to move any motion or amendment on any matter beyond the power of the Council or a committee;

(b) raises or attempts to raise any question or addresses or attempts to address the Council or a committee on any matter which the Council or the committee has no legal right to entertain;

(c) uses any disorderly language;

(d) uses an expression inconsistent with good order and decorum;

(e) is offensive or abusive; or

(f) says anything or does an act calculated to bring the Council or the committee into contempt.

#### by-law 22 Withdrawal of member

If a member, after having been called to order by the chairperson for an act of disorder, commits a further act of disorder, the member must, upon the request of the chairperson, leave the council room or chamber for the remainder of the sitting.

#### by-law 23 Removal of member

If a member refuses to leave the council room or chamber after being requested by the chairperson under by-law 22 , the member commits an offence and the chairperson may order that the member be removed until the end of the sitting.

#### by-law 24 Adjournment when disorder arises

(1) If disorder arises at any meeting, the chairperson must adjourn the meeting for 15 minutes and leave the chair.

(2) The Council or committee must reassemble at the end of the 15 minutes and decide, on the question put from the chair without debate, whether to proceed with or adjourn the meeting to another day.

#### by-law 25 Rejection of matter ruled out of order

If a motion, amendment or other matter has been ruled out of order, it must be rejected.

#### by-law 26 Resumption of consideration of interrupted matter

If the consideration of a motion or business has been interrupted by lack of a quorum, the consideration of the motion or business must be resumed at the point where it was interrupted in the order of business at the next regular meeting of the Council or committee.

### DIVISION 7 VOTING AND DIVISIONS [by-law 27-28]

#### by-law 27 Voting

(1) Any decision of the Council or a committee must be by the majority of the members present.

(2) The chairperson must put all questions to be determined by majority of votes of the Council or committee as often as necessary to decide the voting.

(3) All members must vote by a show of hands unless otherwise required by the Act.

(4) If a member is entitled to vote on a question but neglects or refuses to vote, his or her vote must be counted as against.

(5) Subject to the provisions of sections 21 and 34B of the Act, in the case of equality of votes the person presiding at the meeting has a second or casting vote.

(6) A motion under by-law 44 must be passed by a majority of the whole number of members.

(7) The decision of the chairperson on the result of voting is final unless a member immediately challenges the decision and calls for a division.

#### by-law 28 Divisions

(1) If a division is called, the chairperson must first put the question in favour of and then against, and all members present (including the chairperson) must vote by show of hands and the names and votes of each member (including the chairperson) present must be recorded in the minutes.

(2) A member who is present when a division is called but does not vote on the division, not being disallowed by law from voting, is to be regarded as against.

### DIVISION 8 DEBATES [by-law 29-40]

#### by-law 29 Motions to be seconded

Subject to by-law 7(1)(b) , no motion must be debated unless it has been seconded.

#### by-law 30 Withdrawal of motions

If a motion has been proposed and seconded it becomes subject to the control of the Council or committee and must not be withdrawn without the consent of the Council or committee.

#### by-law 31 Amendment of motions

If a motion has been proposed and seconded, a member may move an amendment to it, but the amendment must not be debated until it has been seconded.

#### by-law 32 Motions as amendment to be in writing

No motion or amendment is to be debated until it has been written down, unless the chairperson so directs.

#### by-law 33 Order of moving amendments

No second or subsequent amendment is to be taken into consideration until the previous amendment has been disposed of.

#### by-law 34 Motions as amended may be amended

If an amendment has been carried, the motion as amended becomes the motion before the Council or committee, and any further amendment on the motion may be moved.

#### by-law 35 When amendment negatived, further amendment may be moved

(1) If an amendment, whether upon an original motion or as amended, has been negatived, a further amendment may be moved to the motion to which the first mentioned amendment was moved, and so on.

(2) Not more than one motion or one proposed amendment is to be before the Council or committee at any one time.

#### by-law 36 Right to speak and right to reply

(1) The mover of an original motion is entitled to the right of reply to any observation on the motion or amendment moved and to speak upon any amendment.

(2) Each member other than the mover of an original motion may speak once on the motion or amendment moved.

(3) No member may, without the consent of the Council or committee, as the case may be, speak more than once on a motion, or for longer than 10 minutes at any one time, unless he or she claims to have been misrepresented or misunderstood, in which case the member may be permitted to explain without adding any further observation than is necessary for the purpose of such explanation.

#### by-law 37 Right to reserve speech

A member who has moved a motion or amendment, other than a motion for adjournment of the debate, is treated as having spoken on it, but a member who has seconded a motion or amendment without any further observation may reserve his or her speech for a later period of the debate.

#### by-law 38 Debate confined to matter under consideration

A member while speaking must confine his or her remarks to the matter under consideration.

#### by-law 39 Member may require motion etc to be read or records to be produced

(1) Any member may request the motion or matter under discussion to be read or stated for his or her information, or may request the production of any records of the Council or committee bearing upon the motion or matter that are readily accessible.

(2) A request under paragraph (1) must not interrupt any other member speaking or materially interrupt any discussion.

(3) If it appears to the chairperson that the request is not made in good faith, the chairperson may decline the request, but the Council or committee, as the case may be, may dissent from his or her ruling.

#### by-law 40 Resolution to be reduced to writing and read aloud

Any resolution of the Council or a committee must forthwith on adoption be written down by the town clerk in attendance who must read the resolution aloud before the Council or committee moves to the next business.

### DIVISION 9 ADJOURNMENT OF MEETINGS [by-law 41-43]

#### by-law 41 Adjournment to later hour or date

A debate may be deferred to a later time on the same day or adjourned to another day.

#### by-law 42 Motions for the adjournment

(1) No discussion is to be allowed on a motion for adjournment of a meeting.

(2) If the question is put on the motion for adjournment and it is defeated, the matter then under consideration, or the next business item, must be discussed.

(3) No member is allowed to move another motion for adjournment until 30 minutes have elapsed from the time of moving of the one that has been defeated.

#### by-law 43 Resumption of debate after adjournment

On resuming a discussion that has been adjourned, the mover of the adjournment is entitled to speak first if he or she has not already spoken on the subject under consideration.

### DIVISION 10 REVOKING OR AMENDING RESOLUTIONS [by-law 44-48]

#### by-law 44 Written notice required of motion to amend or revoke a resolution

(1) A resolution which has been passed by the Council or a committee may be amended or revoked by the Council or committee respectively.

(2) The Council or a committee may, by written notice of motion, amend or revoke its resolution and the notice of motion must be served on each member of the Council or committee at least 3 days prior to the date of the meeting at which the proposal is to be moved.

(3) A committee may make a recommendation to amend or revoke a resolution of the Council and its minutes containing the recommendation must be served on each member of the Council at least 3 clear days before the meeting at which the recommendation is to be discussed.

(4) The minutes of a committee served under paragraph (3) are to be treated as the notice of motion required under paragraph (2) .

#### by-law 45 Notice to be given of motion having same effect as defeated motion

If a motion has been defeated, no other motion to the same effect may be considered unless notice has been given.

#### by-law 46 Notice of motion to be signed in certain cases

A notice of motion to amend or revoke a resolution, and a notice of motion which has the same effect as a motion which has been defeated by the Council or a committee, must be signed by 3 members of the Council or committee, as the case may be, if less than 3 months have elapsed since the resolution was passed, or the motion was defeated, as the case may be.

#### by-law 47 Restriction on motions

(1) If a motion to amend or revoke a resolution has been defeated or a motion which has the same effect as a previously defeated motion is defeated, no similar motion is to be brought forward within 6 months.

(2) This provision is not to be avoided by substituting a motion that is differently worded but which in principle has the same effect as a motion already defeated.

(3) A motion to amend or revoke a resolution of the Council may be moved at any time on the report of a committee, and the report must be stated in the minutes.

#### by-law 48 Motions of adjournment excepted

The provisions relating to defeated motions do not apply to motions of adjournment.

## PART 3 ELECTION OF MAYOR [by-law 49-50]

### by-law 49 Election of mayor

(1) A nomination for the office of mayor may be made by any member without notice.

(2) If—

(a) only one person is nominated;

(b) all the nominations except one are withdrawn; or

(c) all the persons nominated except one decline the nomination,

the person who remains nominated must be declared elected.

### by-law 50 Result of election to be notified

As soon as practicable after each election of a mayor, the town clerk must, in writing, notify the Minister responsible for local government.

## PART 4 COMMITTEES [by-law 51-61]

### DIVISION 1 COMMITTEE OF THE WHOLE [by-law 51-52]

#### by-law 51 Committee of the whole

(1) The Council may, for the consideration of any matter, resolve itself into a committee of the whole.

(2) The provisions of these By-laws, so far as they are applicable, apply to proceedings in a committee of the whole, except the provision limiting the number and duration of speeches and that requiring members to stand while speaking.

#### by-law 52 Committee to report to Council

(1) A report of the proceedings in a committee of the whole must be made to the Council by the chairperson of the committee, but it is sufficient to state the general effect of the report.

(2) All reports must be recorded in the minutes of the Council and no report is to be considered as adopted by the Council until a motion to adopt the report has been put and carried.

### DIVISION 2 STANDING COMMITTEES [by-law 53-57]

#### by-law 53 Constitution of committees

As soon as practicable after the annual election of the Mayor, the Council must appoint standing committees required by section 27 of the Act, consisting of members determined by the Council.

#### by-law 54 Chairperson of committee

(1) Every committee, other than a committee of the whole, must appoint its own chairperson.

(2) The Mayor is an *ex officio* member of every committee.

#### by-law 55 Meetings of committees

The town clerk must call meetings of a committee when directed by the Mayor or by the chairperson of the committee and must give members reasonable notice of meetings.

#### by-law 56 Absence without leave

If a member of a standing committee is absent from 3 consecutive meetings, without having obtained leave of absence from the Council or the committee, his or her seat becomes vacant, and the Council may appoint another member in his or her stead.

#### by-law 57 Finance Committee

The Finance Committee must—

(a) examine and check all accounts of the Council;

(b) generally monitor the collection and expenditure of the Council's revenue;

(c) report on any matter which, in its opinion, affects or is likely to affect the finances of the Council; and

(d) if so directed by resolution of the Council, inquire into and report on any other matter of a like nature.

### DIVISION 3 GENERAL [by-law 58-61]

#### by-law 58 Special Committees

Every special committee must continue until the duties assigned to it have been fully performed.

#### by-law 59 Reports

Committee reports submitted to the Council must be signed by the chairperson of the committee.

#### by-law 60 Communications

A committee must not disclose its terms of reference or work to any outside person or authority except through the town clerk, as the officer authorised by the Council for the purpose.

#### by-law 61 Confidential business

(1) Any matter dealt with or brought before the Council in committee, or before any committee of the Council, is confidential.

(2) Information coming to a committee must not, unless the committee otherwise determines, be disclosed to any person outside the Council except to approved press representatives, and this must be under restraint against publication until the matter has been made public by the Council.

## PART 5 MISCELLANEOUS [by-law 62-64]

### by-law 62 Keeping of minutes

The minutes of all meetings must be taken down in writing by or under the direction of the town clerk into minutes books which must be preserved as permanent records of the Council.

### by-law 63 Authentication of minutes

(1) After the minutes have been approved at any meeting, the chairperson must sign his or her name at the end and initial any correction and the date must be entered against his or her signature.

(2) If the minutes are kept in a “loose leaf” book the chairperson must sign each sheet of the minutes as confirmed.

### by-law 64 Offences

A person who contravenes by-law 23 or 61 commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine of $40 and to imprisonment for 3 months.
