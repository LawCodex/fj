## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Suva (Market) By-laws 1991.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws unless the context otherwise requires—

**attendant** means any servant of the Council appointed by the Council to carry out duties in the market;

**Council** means the Suva City Council;

[def insrt LN 99 of 2016 reg 250, 1 December 2016]

**fish** means an aquatic animal or a portion of such animal that is or purports to be edible either in whole or in part;

**market master** means the market master appointed by the Council and includes any attendant performing for the time being a function of the market master;

**poultry** means any edible live bird;

**produce** means fresh fruit and vegetables, rice, split peas, dried pulses and goods of that nature, live poultry and other edible birds and eggs and includes any agricultural or forest or animal farm product being an article of food or drink in an unprepared state and also includes locally grown tobacco and such other items as may be decided by Council resolution;

**stall** includes any place within the market used for the sale of produce or any other goods; and

**stallholder** means in the case of a permanent stallholder the person in personal charge of the stall, in whose name the receipt for the current month’s stall charges is issued and in the case of casual or daily stallholder, the person in personal charge of the stall who has currently paid the fees for the stall and who is the holder of the receipt in accordance with by-law 13 , provided that a person may, with the prior approval of the Council or the market master, act as a temporary relief for a stallholder in his or her absence for a period not exceeding the expiry date of the current receipt for the stall on issue to the stallholder.

## by-law 3 Market

In these By-laws, “market” means the Suva market established, maintained and controlled by the Council situated at the corner of Usher Street, Harris Road and Rodwell Road in the city of Suva and being the land comprised in State Lease No 2737 delineated on plan S 1057 containing an area of 2 ac 3 rd 26.84 perches (1.18 ha) and Eastern portion of Harris Road, commencing at the junction of Usher Street (enclosed between the fence) to a length of 33.7 meters and depth of 6.7 meters and includes “mini market” established by the Council.

## by-law 4 Market master

The market shall be under immediate management and control of a market master appointed by and responsible to the Council.

## by-law 5 Prohibited sales

No person shall sell or offer for sale in the market any article other than produce.

## by-law 6 Use of market

(1) The Council may by resolution designate any section or part of the market for any specific usage or establish kiosks for such usage upon such terms and conditions as may be decided by the Council.

(2) The market may be divided into sections and different charges may be prescribed for each section by Council resolution.

## by-law 7 Preference to producers

Preference for stalls or space in the market shall be given to producers or their *bona fide* agents selling direct to the public.

## by-law 8 Sale outside the market

No person not being the holder of a wholesale and retail or retail store licence or an unexpired hawker's licence or selling from an established mini market shall sell produce other than fish, in any place outside the market within the city of Suva, provided that the Council may approve the sale of certain items of produce outside the market within the city of Suva for festivals, carnivals, bazaars and functions of that nature.

## by-law 9 Sale of fish

During the hours in which the market is open for business no person shall sell by retail fresh fish at any place outside the market other than at the sale points established by Council resolution, provided that this by-law shall not apply to the sale of fish wholesale or by a butcher, fishmonger, or a retail or wholesale store holder, licensed under the provisions of any Act for the time being in force relating to the licensing of business.

## by-law 10 Hours

The market or any section of the market shall be open for business during such hours as the Council by resolution may determine.

## by-law 11 Directions by market master

All persons using or being within the precincts of the market shall obey reasonable directions given by the market master for the purpose of observing good order and cleanliness of the market.

## by-law 12 Sale from stall

No person shall sell or offer or expose for sale any article in the market except at a stall allocated to him or her by the Council or market master and during the hours that the market is open for business.

[by-law 12 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 250, 1 December 2016]

## by-law 13 Receipts to be exhibited

Every stallholder shall exhibit and keep exhibited on the stall while the same is in use current receipt for payment of the market charges in such manner as the market master shall direct and in such position that it can conveniently be read and inspected at all times by the market master or any attendant.

## by-law 14 Allocation of stalls

The allocation, reallocation, transfer and replacement of stalls in the market shall be made by the Council or the market master in their or his or her sole discretion and the decision of the Council or market master in this respect shall be final.

## by-law 15 Alteration to stall

No person shall make any alterations to a stall or exhibit any notice thereon or introduce any fittings, fixtures, furniture or impediments of any kind without the authority of the Council or the market master and any such thing introduced without its or his or her authority may be removed by the market master and all costs incurred by the Council or its agent for such removal shall be borne by the stallholder and the Council or the market master will not be held liable for any claim or damages arising out of such removal.

## by-law 16 Dues to be paid

(1) No person shall occupy or use a stall so long as any charges which are due and payable for the use thereof are outstanding.

(2) The market master may remove the articles kept on a stall without due payment of charges at the risk of the owner of the said articles and recover all dues and costs to the Council upon selling the articles so removed and the Council will not be held liable for any claim or for damages arising out of such sale.

## by-law 17 Fish stalls

(1) No person shall sell fish in the market other than at the place, section or stall set aside by the Council or market master for sale of fish.

(2) A person shall not bring into or offer for sale in the market fresh fish other than whitebait and similar small fish, shellfish or turtles that has not been gutted, gilled and cleaned.

(3) The market master may remove forthwith any fish offered for sale contrary to the provisions in paragraphs (1) and (2) above and destroy same in a manner he or she thinks proper and the Council or the market master may not be held liable for any claim or for damages arising out of such removal and disposal, and any expenses incurred for such removal will be recovered from the person who failed to comply with the provisions mentioned above.

## by-law 18 Charges

(1) The charges for the use of stall and other market facilities shall be in accordance with the rates prescribed by Council resolution subject to the approval of the Minister responsible for fisheries and shall be payable in advance on demand by the market master or any attendant. The market master or attendant shall at the time of receiving payment of any charges under this by-law issue a receipt therefor.

(2) On such public holidays on which under the provisions of by-law 9 the market or any section of the market remains open, the charges for the use of a stall shall be double the charges.

## by-law 19 Cleanliness

(1) Stallholders shall keep their stalls in a clean and orderly condition and shall dispose of any litter and refuse as and when required by the market master.

(2) A person shall place articles offered or exposed for sale in the market—

(a) on the raised concrete or wooden stalls provided in the market;

(b) on tables, benches, mats, sacking or other articles or materials approved by the market master.

(3) A person who fails to comply with paragraph (2) above will be deprived of occupying his or her stall and the articles may be impounded by the market master.

## by-law 20 Exchange, subletting and surrendering of stalls

(1) No stallholder shall exchange, sublet, surrender or part with the possession of his or her stall or any part thereof without the prior approval in writing of the Council.

(2) A person who fails to comply with paragraph (1) above will be deprived of his or her stall.

## by-law 21 Sharing of stalls

(1) No stallholder shall share his or her stall with another vendor or person without the prior approval of the market master in writing.

(2) A person who fails to comply with paragraph (1) above will be deprived of his or her stall.

## by-law 22 Selling of mixed kava

(1) Mixed kava shall not be sold at any stall or in any part of the market save only at the market kava kiosks authorised for such purpose by the Council.

[para (1) am Act 28 of 2020 s 4 and Sch, 1 August 2020]

(2) Any person who sells mixed kava at a stall other than duly allocated for the purpose will be deprived of his or her stall.

[para (2) am Act 28 of 2020 s 4 and Sch, 1 August 2020]

## by-law 23 Wholesaling

The market shall not be used by any stallholder for the conduct of a wholesale business.

## by-law 24 Hygiene

(1) Stallholders shall keep their produce or other goods offered for sale and expose them for sale in as hygienic a manner as is practicable and shall obey all reasonable directions of the market master in this regard.

(2) If a person fails to keep produce in a manner described in paragraph (1) above, the market master may impound the produce so kept and all expenses for such impounding will be recovered from the stallholder concerned.

## by-law 25 Shouting etc

No person shall in the precincts of the market advertise goods by shouting or conduct sale by auction without the written permission of the market master.

## by-law 26 Unwholesome goods

No person shall bring into or keep in the market anything which is bad, obnoxious, unwholesome or dangerous and the market master may remove or cause to be removed any such thing brought within the precincts of the market and the cost of such removal shall be paid by the owner.

## by-law 27 Dogs

No person shall bring a dog into the market or suffer it to remain there and the market master shall have power to remove same at the risk of the owner.

## by-law 28 Disease

(1) No person suffering from any infectious or contagious disease or having recently been exposed to infection by such a disease shall engage in the sale of any goods in the market.

(2) A person so found will be referred by the market master to the health department of the Council for further steps to be taken.

## by-law 29 Closing hours

No person shall enter upon or remain in the market or sell or offer for sale any produce in the market outside the business hours hereinbefore specified without the prior approval in writing of the Council or the market master save and except as provided in by-law 9 .

## by-law 30 Obstruction of officials

No person shall obstruct, prevent hinder or interfere with the market master or any attendant in the performance of any duty under these By-laws and no person shall enter the market whilst drunk or excite a riot or public disorder or curse or swear or use any gross indecent filthy or abusive language or gamble or be guilty of any misconduct therein.

## by-law 31 Obstruction

No person shall obstruct any of the roadways, footpaths, entrances thoroughfares, passages or divisions of the market or shall loiter therein.

## by-law 32 Use or movement of vehicles on or in the market

(1) Except where otherwise directed by the Council the driver of any vehicle shall use such entrances and exits to and from the market as shall from time to time be fixed by resolution of the Council and notified by suitable signs.

(2) A person shall not drive a vehicle into the market for purposes not connected with the market.

(3) A person shall not drive a vehicle within the market at a speed in excess of 20 kilometres per hour.

(4) A person shall not drive or use a vehicle in the market in negligent or dangerous manner or without due care or attention or reasonable consideration for the other persons using the market.

(5) A driver of any vehicle within the market shall not leave the engine of any such vehicle running whilst the vehicle is parked or is being loaded or unloaded.

(6) Except where the Council approves in writing a person shall not park or stand any vehicle within the market for a period longer than duly authorised.

(7) A person shall not stand or park any vehicle in the market in such a manner as to obstruct any entrance, exit or passage or operate a vehicle so as to prevent the free and convenient movement of vehicles or person.

(8) The Council may by resolution from time to time prohibit any vehicle or class of vehicle or equipment from entering or being in the market.

(9) A person shall not drive a vehicle into or cause a vehicle to enter or be within the market contrary to a prohibition under paragraph (8) .

(10)

(i) The Council may by resolution from time to time—

(a) set apart any area of the market for the parking of vehicles; and

(b) fix parking fees in respect of the area.

(ii) The Council when fixing fees pursuant to subparagraph (i)(b) may—

(a) fix the fees either generally or for a specified period;

(b) fix different scales of fees for different classes of vehicles and for different areas;

(c) specify the times during which the fees are payable;

(d) exempt specified classes of vehicles from payment of the fees.

(iii) Where a fee has been fixed pursuant to subparagraph (i)(b) in respect of the parking of a vehicle in an area a person shall not park or stand the vehicle in that area unless a person has paid the fee so fixed.

(iv) The Council may by resolution from time to time and upon such terms as may be specified in the resolution transfer to any person its right to collect fees fixed pursuant to subparagraph (i)(b) .

## by-law 33 Admission to market

The market master shall have power to refuse admission to or eject from the market any person of undesirable character or any young children unattended and any person contravening or failing to comply with any of the provisions of by-laws 25 , 28 and 29 .

## by-law 34 Empty casks etc

No person shall, without the consent of the market master bring or cause to be brought into the market any empty casks, crates or lumber of any kind and the market master may remove or cause to be removed all such articles at the risk and cost of the owner of such articles. Any such articles so removed may be sold by the Council without any liability and the Council may recover out of the proceeds of such sale the costs and expenses incurred in such removal and sale.

## by-law 35 Unsold goods

(1) Goods unsold at the close of the day may with the permission of the market master be left in the market while the market is closed.

(2) Goods which have been left in the market when the market is closed without the market master’s permission may be removed and disposed of by the market master.

(3) The cost of the removal and disposal of goods under paragraph (2) shall be a debt due to the Council from the person who left the goods in the Market and may be recovered on sale of such goods by the market master without any liability and the Council may recover out of the proceeds of such sale the costs and expenses incurred in such removal and sale.

(4) Neither the Council nor the market master is liable for theft, loss or damage to goods left in the market with the market master’s permission when it is closed unless the theft, loss or damage results from a deliberate or negligent act of the market master.

(5) Goods maybe left in the care of the market master on payment in advance of the prescribed charges as determined by Council resolution.

## by-law 36 Spitting

No person shall spit or discharge any nasal fluid or mucus on to any roadway, footpath, floor or other part of the market or on to any utensil, produce, article or thing standing or being in the market.

## by-law 37 Obstruction to roadway

(1) No person shall place any matter or thing on any roadway, footpath or passage in the market so as to obstruct the free use of such roadway, footpath or passage, or neglect or refuse to remove such matter or thing on being required so to do by the market master or any attendant.

(2) The market master or any attendant or any approved security guard engaged by the market master may remove any matter or thing so placed and the cost of such removal shall be paid by the owner or his or her agent. The market master or any person engaged in such removal will not be held liable for any claim or damages arising out of such removal.

## by-law 38 Permitted articles only to be sold

(1) No person shall sell any article other than the articles permitted for sale by him or her at the stall allocated to him or her.

(2) The market master may remove and sell such articles not permitted for sale and the Council or the market master will not be held liable for any claim or damages arising out of such removal and sale.

## by-law 39 Litter

A person may be deprived of his or her stall by the market master if he or she throws, drops, deposits or otherwise parts with the possession of litter or refuse in the market except in receptacles provided for that purpose.

## by-law 40 Cooking prohibited

A person who cooks anything in a stall allocated to him or her save and except at the food kiosks specifically allocated will be deprived of the stall allocated to him or her.

## by-law 41 Certain structures prohibited

The market master may demolish and remove any structure erected in the market by any person without the prior written approval of the market master and the market master or the Council will not be held liable for any claim or damages of such demolition and removal.

## by-law 42 Washing of market

(1) The market master may remove any goods kept at the market which will obstruct the routine cleaning and washing of the market if a person fails to comply with a request made by the market master to remove goods.

(2) A 7 days notice in writing should be given to all the stall holders in the market by the market master informing them to remove their goods from stalls for the purpose of general washing and cleaning of the market twice a year. The market master may remove any goods kept after due notice.

(3) The Council or market master may not be liable for any damages caused to goods so removed.

## by-law 43 Unauthorised occupation of stalls

The market master may remove any produce or other article from a stall occupied without the market master's permission and the cost of such removal must be paid by the person who occupied the stall without permission and the market master or Council may not be held liable for damages caused by such removal.

## by-law 44 Penalty

Any person who contravenes any of the provisions of these By-laws shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $200 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months or both and to a further fine of $10 for every day during which such contravention continues.

## by-law 45 Repeal

The Suva (Market) By-laws 1962 and all the subsequent by-laws and all other legal notices are repealed.
