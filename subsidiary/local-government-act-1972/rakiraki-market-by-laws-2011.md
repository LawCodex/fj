# RAKIRAKI (MARKET) BY-LAWS 2011

Rakiraki (Market) By-laws 2011 (LN 70 of 2011) commenced on 19 August 2011, as amended by:

## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [by-law 1-3]

### by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Rakiraki (Market) By-laws 2011.

### by-law 2 Objectives

The principal objectives of these By-laws are to provide a system of rules for governing the conduct of activities within the market which, if observed, are intended to minimise the risk of loss, damage, death or injury being sustained and to facilitate and encourage the growth of vendor activity within the market.

### by-law 3 Interpretations

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise indicates—

**access card** means the document allowing a vendor or person access to and use of the market facilities as specified on the access card;

**attendant** means any servant of the Council appointed by the Council to carry out duties in the market;

**Council** means the Rakiraki Town Council;

**fish** means an aquatic animal or a portion of such animal that is or purports to be edible either in whole or in part;

**general public** means all members of the public (other than a vendor, an attendant or the market manager);

**general public access hours** means the hours determined by the market manager, with the approval of Council, from time to time and set out in the Market Rules, during which the general public may have access to the markets;

**licence** means a licence issued by the market manager which entitles a Vendor to sell produce in a specifically allocated stall in the market, under certain conditions as imposed by the market manager;

**market** means the Rakiraki market established, maintained and controlled by the Council, at the banks of Vaileka Creek fronting onto Korotale Valley Road and being the land comprised in iTaukei Lease No 18374 delineated on SO Plan 357 containing an area of 4144 square metres and includes **mini market** established by the Rakiraki Town Council;

[def am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, 1 March 2011]

**market manager** means the market manager appointed by the Council and includes any Attendant performing for the time being a function of the market manager;

**market facilities** means the structures within the boundaries of the market including but not limited to stalls, bathrooms, points of water access, drains, ventilation appliances, outside floors and buildings including the roofs, walls and floors of such buildings;

**Market Rules** means the rules to be known as the “Rakiraki Market Rules”, supplementary to the Rakiraki (Market) By-laws 2011, governing the market as determined from time to time by the market manager under this by-law in accordance with any resolution of, or delegation to the market manager, by the Council;

**person** means any individual, company, corporation, body corporate, association, firm, business or partnership;

**poultry** means any edible live bird;

**produce** means any items as may be decided from time to time by Council resolution, including but not limited to the following—

(a) fish;

(b) fresh fruit and vegetables;

(c) rice;

(d) split peas;

(e) dried pulses and goods of that nature;

(f) any poultry and eggs;

(g) any agricultural or forest or animal farm product being an article of food or drink in an unprepared state;

(h) locally grown tobacco; and

(i) any other goods as specified from time to time by the Council.

**producer** means a person who grows or produces produce;

**stall** includes any place within the market used for the sale of produce or any other goods;

**structure** means any structure as defined but not limited to by-law 19 ;

**vehicle** means any car, cab, carriage, cart, lorry, motor vehicle, semi-trailer, forklift, tractor or traction engine, trailer, trolley vehicle, truck, van, wagon, motorcycle, bicycle, tricycle or other means of transport or conveyance designed for movement upon wheels whether or not such vehicle is or is not capable of being operated or used in a normal manner;

**vendor** means a person authorised to sell produce in the market who is the holder of a valid licence and access card and includes the following—

(a) permanent vendor, the person in whose name the receipt is issued and who is in personal charge of the stall for a period of one month or more;

(b) casual vendor, the person in whose name the receipt is issued and who is in personal charge of the stall for a period of less than one month; or

(c) relief vendor, the person who, with the prior written approval of the market manager, is acting as a temporary relief for a vendor in his or her absence for a period not exceeding the expiry date of the current receipt for the stall on issue to the vendor; and

**waste** means any discarded, rejected, unwanted, surplus, discharged, or abandoned liquid, material, rubbish, litter or other similar substance.

## PART 2 MARKET [by-law 4-7]

### by-law 4 Management and governing rules

(1) The market shall be under the immediate management and control of a market manager appointed by and responsible to the Council.

(2) All transactions, conduct and activities within the markets are regulated by and subject to these By-laws and the Market Rules.

### by-law 5 Use of market

(1) The Council may by resolution designate any section or part of the market for any specific usage, or establish kiosks for such usage upon such terms and conditions as may be decided by the Council.

(2) The market may be divided into sections and different charges may be prescribed for each section by the Council.

### by-law 6 Sale outside the market

(1) No person not being the holder of a wholesale and retail or retail store licence or an unexpired hawker's licence or selling from an established mini market shall sell produce other than fish, in any place outside the market within the town of Rakiraki.

(2) The Council may approve the sale of certain items of produce outside the market within the town of Rakiraki for festivals, carnivals, bazaars and functions of that nature.

### by-law 7 Sale of fish

(1) During trading hours, no person shall sell by retail fresh fish at any place outside the market other than at the sale points established by Council resolution.

(2) This by-law shall not apply to the sale of fish wholesale or by a butcher, fishmonger, or a retail or wholesale store holder, licensed under the provisions of any written law for the time being in force relating to the licensing of business.

## PART 3 MARKET MANAGER [by-law 8-12]

### by-law 8 Power to make rules

(1) The market manager shall have the power to make and amend the Market Rules to regulate and facilitate the operation and good maintenance of the market and market facilities.

(2) The market manager must make and amend rules under this by-law in accordance with any resolution of or delegation from the Council in respect of the Market Rules.

### by-law 9 Power to issue licences

(1) The market manager shall have the power to issue licences in accordance with the guidelines in the Market Rules.

(2) The market manager shall have the power to determine particular conditions on which a licence may be issued, subject to the Market Rules and in accordance with any resolution of or delegation from Council.

(3) A licence is subject to—

(a) all general provisions of these By-laws and the Market Rules;

(b) any conditions imposed by the market manager on all licences; and

(c) any particular conditions imposed by the market manager and endorsed on an individual licence.

### by-law 10 Power to issue access cards

(1) The market manager shall have the power to issue an access card in conjunction with the issue of a licence and in accordance with the guidelines prescribed in the Market Rules.

(2) The market manager shall have the power to determine particular conditions on which an access card may be issued, subject to the Market Rules and in accordance with any resolution of or delegation from the Council.

(3) An access card is subject to—

(a) all general provisions of these By-laws and the Market Rules;

(b) any conditions imposed by the market manager on all licences or on all access cards; and

(c) any particular conditions imposed by the market manager and endorsed on an individual access card.

### by-law 11 Power to issue directions

(1) The market manager shall have the power to give reasonable directions to any person using or being within the precincts of the market for the purpose of observing good order and cleanliness in the market.

(2) All persons shall obey any reasonable directions given to them by the market manager under this by-law.

### by-law 12 Other powers

The market manager shall have the power to—

(a) refuse admission to or eject from the market any—

(i) person of undesirable character;

(ii) young children unattended; or

(iii) person contravening or failing to comply with any of the provisions of these By-laws or the Market Rules;

(b) demolish and remove any structure erected in the market by any person without the prior written approval of the market manager, in which case neither the market manager nor the Council will be held liable for any claim or damages of such demolition and removal;

(c) deprive any vendor of his or her stall for contravening any provision of these By-laws; or

(d) remove any produce or other article from a stall occupied without the market manager’s permission, the cost of such removal being paid by the person who occupied the stall without permission, in which case neither the market manager nor the Council may be held liable for damages caused by such removal.

## PART 4 CONTROL OF ACCESS TO THE MARKET [by-law 13-15]

### by-law 13 Entry to the markets

(1) A person (other than an attendant) must not enter or be in any part of the market when closed, or closed to any category of vendors, or when access is not otherwise permitted as may be notified by the Council or the market manager at any time.

(2) A vehicle must not enter or be in any part of the market (other than a vehicle owned or controlled by the Council or an attendant) when closed, or when access is not otherwise permitted as may be notified by the Council or the market manager at any time.

(3) Paragraphs (1) and (2) do not apply to any person or vehicle permitted by the Council or an attendant to be in the market when it is closed.

### by-law 14 Entry and access by vendors

A vendor must not enter the market except—

(a) upon production of a valid access card;

(b) during the times and in accordance with the conditions of that vendor’s licence and access card; and

(c) while in the market, comply with all conditions imposed on the vendor’s licence and the provisions of these By-laws and the Market Rules.

### by-law 15 Entry and access by the public

A member of the public must not enter or remain within the market except—

(a) during the general public access hours as set out in the Market Rules;

(b) in accordance with the provisions of these By-laws and the Market Rules in relation to conduct; and

(c) as otherwise determined by the Council from time to time.

## PART 5 CONTROL OF PERSONS IN THE MARKET [by-law 16-18]

### by-law 16 Authorised officers

(1) Every person, while in the market, must promptly obey all orders and directions given or made by the market manager or by an attendant.

(2) A person who is a vendor must at any time while in the market and, upon request by the market manager or by an attendant, produce the vendor's access card and/or licence.

### by-law 17 Conduct of business in the market by vendors

A vendor must not, without the prior written approval of the market manager—

(a) sell or offer for sale anything other than produce in any way otherwise than in accordance with the terms and conditions of this by-law, the Market Rules and the vendor's licence;

(b) sell any goods that are prohibited from sale in the market; or

(c) advertise produce by shouting or conduct of sale by auction.

### by-law 18 Prohibited conduct generally in the market

(1) A person must not, while in the market—

(a) obstruct, prevent, hinder or interfere with the market manager or any attendant in the performance of any duty under these By-laws;

(b) bring into or keep anything in the market which is bad, obnoxious, unwholesome, or dangerous;

(c) enter the market whilst under the influence of an intoxicating substance or drug other than a drug prescribed for a medical condition;

(d) excite a riot or public disorder or curse or swear or use any gross, indecent, filthy or abusive language or gamble;

(e) generally engage in anti-social or otherwise offensive behaviour or be guilty of any misconduct therein;

(f) spit or discharge any nasal fluid or mucus on to any roadway, footpath, floor or other part of the market or on to any utensil, produce, article or thing standing or being in the market;

(g) damage or wilfully abuse any property belonging to the Council or any other person;

(h) make use of or otherwise occupy any area within the market precinct for activities not considered appropriate by the market manager, acting in his or her absolute discretion;

(i) obstruct any of the roadways, footpaths, entrances, thoroughfares, passages or divisions of the market or loiter therein;

(j) deposit, dispose of or otherwise part with waste in any way other than in accordance with the waste management provisions of these By-laws and the Market Rules;

(k) bring or cause to be brought into the market any empty casks, crates or lumber of any kind without the consent of the market manager;

(l) sell any produce unless the person is a vendor and then only at the place and during the times and in the manner expressly authorised by a written licence issued to that vendor by the market manager or an attendant;

(m) cook anything in a stall allocated to him or her save and except at the food kiosks specifically allocated for that purpose;

(n) erect any structure in the market without the prior written approval of the market manager;

(o) bring or allow any animal into the market (except as permitted to do so by law);

(p) smoke any substance, cigarette, cigar, pipe or other device in an area of the market;

(q) commit any unlawful act; or

(r) otherwise act in a manner which may, (in the absolute discretion of the market manager) adversely affect the health or safety of an individual, themselves or property of others.

(2) The market manager may remove or cause to be removed any article or thing brought within the precincts of the market that is prohibited at the risk and the cost of the owner.

(3) Any such articles so removed may be sold by the Council without any liability and the Council may recover out of the proceeds of such sale the costs and expenses incurred in such removal and sale.

## PART 6 MARKET FACILITIES AND SAFETY [by-law 19-22]

### by-law 19 Council’s obligations

The Council is required to erect, repair and maintain all structures required for the effective operation of the market. These include an obligation to repair and maintain structures including but not limited to the following structures—

(a) all physical buildings including the roofs, walls and floors;

(b) the floor of the outdoor market;

(c) stalls including stall posts and floors;

(d) bathroom facilities; and

(e) points of access to water.

### by-law 20 Obligation to ensure safety

(1) A person must comply at all times with all relevant occupational health and safety laws and behave in a manner that is consistent with the safety of all persons in the market.

(2) A person must not bring or cause or permit to be brought any produce, vehicle, plant, equipment or other property into the market other than in accordance with this by-law or the terms of the person’s licence.

(3) A person must not place any produce, vehicle, goods, plant, equipment or other property in the common areas of the market or in any other way obstruct, or cause to be obstructed, the common areas.

(4) A person must not in any way—

(a) obstruct or cause to be obstructed, any access to fire exits, fire doors, or fire-fighting equipment; and

(b) use fire-fighting equipment for a purpose other than the intended purpose.

(5) A person must comply with all written laws and directions of any attendant in regard to electrical safety and must not at any time install or connect any equipment or do any act that overloads any of the systems through with electricity that may be conveyed to or reticulated in the market.

### by-law 21 Use or movement of vehicles on or in the market

(1) Except where otherwise directed by Council the driver of any vehicle shall use such entrances and exits to and from the market as shall from time to time be fixed by resolution of the Council and notified by suitable signs.

(2) A person shall not drive a vehicle into the market for purposes not connected with the market.

(3) A person shall not drive a vehicle within the market at a speed in excess of 20 kilometres per hour.

(4) A person shall not drive or use a vehicle in the market in a negligent or dangerous manner or without due care or attention or reasonable consideration for the other persons using the market.

(5) A driver of any vehicle within the market shall not leave the engine of any such vehicle running whilst the vehicle is parked or is being loaded or unloaded.

(6) Except where the Council approves in writing a person shall not park or stand any vehicle within the market for a period longer than duly authorised.

(7) A person shall not stand or park any vehicle in the market in such a manner as to obstruct any entrance, exit or passage or operate a vehicle so as to prevent the free and convenient movement of vehicles or persons.

(8) The Council may by resolution from time to time prohibit any vehicle or class of vehicles or equipment from entering or being in the market.

(9) A person shall not drive a vehicle into or cause a vehicle to enter or be within the market contrary to a prohibition under paragraph (8) .

(10) The Council may by resolution from time to time (and notification in the Market Rules)—

(a) set apart any area of the market for the parking of vehicles; and

(b) fix parking fees in respect of the area.

(11) The Council when fixing fees pursuant to paragraph (10) may—

(a) fix the fees either generally or for a specified period;

(b) fix different scales of fees for different classes of vehicles and for different areas;

(c) specify the times during which the fees are payable; and

(d) exempt specified classes of vehicles from payment of the fees.

(12) Where a fee has been fixed pursuant to paragraph (11)(a) in respect of the parking of a vehicle in an area a person shall not park or stand the vehicle in that area unless a person has paid the fee so fixed.

(13) The Council may by resolution from time to time and upon such terms as may be specified in the resolution transfer to any person its right to collect fees fixed pursuant to this by-law.

### by-law 22 Obstruction to roadway

(1) No person shall place any matter or thing on any roadway, footpath or passage in the market so as to obstruct the free use of such roadway, footpath or passage or neglect or refuse to remove such matter or thing on being required to do so by the market manager or any attendant.

(2) The market manager or any attendant or any approved security guard engaged by the market manager may remove any matter or thing so placed and the cost of such removal shall be paid by the owner or his or her agent. The market manager or any person engaged in such removal will not be held liable for any claim or damages arising out of such removal.

## PART 7 OPERATIONAL [by-law 23-36]

### by-law 23 Licences

All vendors must obtain a licence in order to sell produce in the market.

### by-law 24 Access cards

All vendors must obtain an access card for authorised entry to the market and use of the market facilities.

### by-law 25 Charges

(1) Charges for the use of market facilities and the payment of such charges shall be in accordance with the rates and payment procedures prescribed by the Council resolution, subject to the approval of the Minister, as set out in the Market Rules.

(2) No person shall occupy or use a stall so long as any charges which are due and payable for the use thereof are outstanding.

(3) The market manager may remove the articles kept on a stall without due payment of charges at the risk of the owner of the said articles and recover all dues and costs to the Council upon selling the articles so removed and the Council will not be held liable for any claim or for damage arising out of such sale.

### by-law 26 Products sold

(1) No person shall sell or offer for sale in the market any article other than produce.

(2) The market manager may remove and sell any articles not permitted for sale and the Council or the market manager will not be held liable for any claim or damages arising out of such removal and sale.

### by-law 27 Wholesaling

The market shall not be used by any vendor for the conduct of a wholesale business.

### by-law 28 Selling of mixed kava

(1) Mixed kava shall not be sold at any stall or in any part of the market save only at the market kava kiosks authorised for such purpose by the Council.

[para (1) am Act 28 of 2020 s 4 and Sch, 1 August 2020]

(2) Any vendor who sells mixed kava at a stall other than duly allocated for the purpose will be deprived of his or her stall.

[para (2) am Act 28 of 2020 s 4 and Sch, 1 August 2020]

### by-law 29 Fish stalls

(1) No person shall sell fish in the market other than at the place, section or stall set aside by the Council or market manager for the sale of fish.

(2) A person shall not bring into or offer for sale in the market fresh fish other than whitebait and similar small fish, shellfish or turtles that have not been gutted, gilled and cleaned.

(3) The market manager may remove forthwith any fish offered for sale contrary to the provisions above and destroy same in the manner he or she thinks proper and the Council or the market manager may not be held liable for any claim or for damages arising out of such removal and disposal, and any expenses incurred for such removal will be recovered from the person who failed to comply with the provisions mentioned above.

### by-law 30 Unsold produce

(1) Produce unsold at the close of the day may, with the written permission of the market manager, be left in the market while the market is closed.

(2) Produce which has been left in the market when the market is closed without the market manager’s written permission may be removed and disposed of by the market manager.

(3) The cost of the removal and disposal of such produce shall be a debt due to the Council from the person who left the produce in the market and may be recovered on sale of such produce by the market manager without any liability and the Council may recover out of the proceeds of such sale the costs and expenses incurred in such removal and sale.

(4) Neither the Council nor the market manager is liable for theft, loss or damage to goods left in the market with the market manager’s permission when it is closed unless the theft, loss or damage results from a deliberate or negligent act of the market manager.

(5) Produce may be left in the care of the market manager on payment in advance of the prescribed charges as determined by the Council resolution and set out in the Market Rules.

### by-law 31 Allocation of stalls

(1) The allocation, reallocation, transfer and replacement of stalls in the market shall be made by the Council or the market manager in its, or his or her, sole discretion and the decision of the Council or market manager in this respect shall be final.

(2) Preference for stall or space in the market shall be given to producers or their *bona fide* agents selling direct to the public.

### by-law 32 Alterations to stalls

(1) No vendor shall make any alterations to a stall or exhibit any notice thereon or introduce any fittings, fixtures, furniture or impediments of any kind without the authority of the Council or the market manager and any such thing introduced without its or his or her authority may be removed by the market manager.

(2) Any and all costs incurred by the Council or its agent for removal of any item under this section shall be borne by the vendor and the Council or the market manager will not be held liable for any claim or damages arising out of such removal.

### by-law 33 Exchange, subletting and surrendering of stalls

(1) No vendor shall exchange, sublet, surrender or part with the possession of his or her stall or any part thereof without the prior approval in writing of the Council.

(2) A vendor who fails to comply with this provision will be deprived of his or her stall.

### by-law 34 Sharing of stalls

(1) No vendor shall share his or her stall with another vendor or person without the prior approval of the market manager in writing.

(2) A person who fails to comply with this provision will be deprived of his or her stall.

### by-law 35 Trading hours

The market or any section of the market shall be open for business during such hours as the Council by resolution may determine and set out in the Market Rules.

### by-law 36 Outside trading hours

No person shall enter upon or remain in the market or sell or offer for sale any produce in the market outside the trading hours indicated in the Market Rules without the prior approval in writing of the Council or the market manager.

## PART 8 ENVIRONMENTAL AND HYGIENE REQUIREMENTS [by-law 37-43]

### by-law 37 Protection of environment generally

A vendor or person while in the market must—

(a) comply with and observe all laws and requirements of the local and any other Government authority relating to the environment and environmental practices, hygiene, water usage and the general presentation and cleanliness of their area of business in the market;

(b) act in a way and carry on any activity in such manner so as to ensure—

(i) the protection of the environment from harm or degradation;

(ii) the protection of the health of persons;

(iii) the prevention, control and abatement of pollution or contamination and their effects; and

(iv) the use of best practices for the regulation of waste and waste disposal.

### by-law 38 Market maintenance

(1) The Council shall maintain—

(a) the market facilities in a state of good repair at all times;

(b) appropriate standards of cleanliness and hygiene throughout all market facilities;

(c) separate and discrete bathroom facilities for different genders; and

(d) appropriate sanitary facilities in the bathrooms.

(2) If a vendor becomes aware of any market facilities that require repair, or any hazards or factors that are adversely affecting the sale of produce or the conditions of the market or the market facilities, they may notify the market manager in accordance with the notice procedures in the Market Rules.

(3) If an incident, accident, injury or dangerous event happens at the market, then each person directly or indirectly involved in the event must report the event to an attendant immediately after it happens and provide information and details of the persons involved and the circumstances giving rise to the event.

### by-law 39 Washing of market

(1) The market shall be cleaned in accordance with the standards and in the manner prescribed by Council and set out in the Market Rules.

(2) The market manager may remove any goods kept at the market which will obstruct the routine cleaning and washing of the market if a person fails to comply with a request made by the market manager to remove goods.

### by-law 40 Particular environmental requirements for vendors

(1) A vendor must not dump, throw, deposit or place any waste in the market unless in a receptacle provided for that purpose.

(2) A vendor must store, handle and manage all produce and other materials in the area of their licence in a proper and efficient manner and otherwise in the manner as may be directed by the market manager from time to time.

(3) A vendor must keep the area of their licence and adjacent areas clean, tidy and free from dirt at all times.

(4) A vendor must not cause or permit—

(a) any noxious or offensive odour or smell to be created in or about the area of the licence; and

(b) any mongoose, rats, cockroaches or other vermin to harbour within the area of their licence.

(5) A vendor must not dispose of any waste in any sewer, sink, drain or pipe in the market except in the places and in the manner designated by the market manager for that purpose.

(6) A vendor must comply at all times with all relevant written laws in regard to the safety of food.

### by-law 41 Sale of items

(1) A vendor shall place articles offered or exposed for sale in the market—

(a) on the raised concrete or wood stalls provided in the market;

(b) on tables, benches, mats, sacking or other articles or materials approved by the market manager.

(2) A vendor who fails to comply with paragraph (1) will be deprived of occupying his or her stall and the articles may be impounded by the market manager.

(3) Vendors shall keep their produce and expose it for sale in as hygienic a manner as is practicable and shall obey all reasonable directions of the market manager in this regard.

(4) If a vendor fails to keep produce in a manner described in paragraph (3) , the market manager may impound the produce so kept and all expenses for such impounding will be recovered from the vendor concerned.

### by-law 42 Disease

(1) No vendor suffering from any infectious or contagious disease or having recently been exposed to infection by such a disease shall engage the sale of any produce in the market.

(2) A vendor so found will be referred by the market manager to the health department of the Council for further steps to be taken.

### by-law 43 Penalty

Any person who contravenes any of the provisions of this by-law shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable upon conviction to a fine not exceeding $400 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months or both.
