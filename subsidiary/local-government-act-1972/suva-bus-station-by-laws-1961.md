## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Suva (Bus Station) By-laws 1961.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**bus operator** includes any person, firm, company or organisation which owns or operates a motor omnibus;

**bus shelter** means any shelter owned and controlled by the Council for the use of bus passengers in the City of Suva;

**bus station** [def rep LN 15 of 1991 by-law 3, 19 April 1991]

**Council** means the Suva City Council;

**motor omnibus** means a public service vehicle constructed or adapted to carry more than 6 passengers excluding the driver; and

**town clerk** means the town clerk appointed by the Council.

## by-law 2A Suva bus station

(1) The Suva bus station, which shall be maintained and controlled by the Council shall comprise—

(a) all the parcel of land contained in—

(i) State Lease 3560 (excluding Taxi Stand) delineated on plan S1105, and

(ii) State Lease 10867 delineated as Lot 1, S778; and Lot 3 S817; and

(b) the portion of land contained in Escott Street and Western part of Rodwell Road between Renown Street and Southern boundary of CL3560.

The bus station described in subparagraphs (a) and (b) above are edged yellow and blue respectively on plan No S1105 lodged in the Department of Lands and Survey.

[by-law 2A insrt LN 15 of 1991 by-law 4, 19 April 1991]

## by-law 3 Licence to use bus station

Each bus operator desiring to use the bus station shall make application in the form prescribed in Schedule 1 for a licence for his or her motor omnibus or omnibuses to use the bus station.

## by-law 4 Licences to be issued by town clerk

All licences to use the bus station shall be issued on behalf of the Council by the town clerk and in the form prescribed in Schedule 2 and shall have effect in respect only of the vehicle or vehicles described therein.

## by-law 5 Fees and payment of fees

(1)

(a) The licence fees for use of the bus station by motor omnibuses shall be in accordance with the fees prescribed in Schedule 3;

(b) The licence fees shall be payable on demand at the time of each entry into the bus station by the bus operator;

(c) Where the town clerk directs any bus operator to be charged monthly, the charges shall be computed in accordance with by-law 6(1) .

[para (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 245, 1 December 2016]

(2) Any bus operator directed by town clerk to be charged in accordance with by-law 5(1)(c) shall pay to the Council the fees payable by him or her within 15 days of the succeeding month.

[by-law 5 subst LN 15 of 1991 by-law 5, 19 April 1991]

## by-law 6 Monthly charges

(1) The licence fees to be charged for any such monthly period shall be computed on the total number of visits of the vehicle to the bus station during the month and shall be calculated from the timetable approved by the Land Transport Authority in respect of the road service licence under which the vehicle shall have been operating during the month or from any other information which the said Authority shall think fit to supply to the town clerk from its records or from any other information obtained by the town clerk under paragraph (2) .

[para (1) renum LN 15 of 1991 by-law 6, 19 April 1991am LN 99 of 2016 reg 245, 1 December 2016]

(2) If the town clerk considers that the number of visits to the bus station of any motor omnibus cannot be satisfactorily calculated from any timetable, he or she may by written notice require the bus operator concerned to supply him or her with particulars of the number of visits to the bus station, and the times thereof, of each vehicle operating under the bus operator's road service licence during the month the subject of the computation; and any bus operator so required to supply such particulars shall, within 7 days after the receipt by him or her of such notice, deliver to the town clerk a written statement, signed by such operator or under his or her authority, and containing a true and correct statement of the total number of visits to the bus station, and the times thereof, of each such vehicle during the said month.

[para (2) renum LN 15 of 1991 by-law 6, 19 April 1991]

## by-law 8 Bus station not to be used without licence

No bus operator shall be entitled to use the bus station for the purposes of any motor omnibus unless he or she holds a valid licence issued under these By-laws in respect of such vehicle.

## by-law 9 Bus not to use bus station without licence

No motor omnibus shall enter or use the bus station at any time unless such a valid licence under these By-laws is held by a bus operator in respect thereof.

## by-law 10 Overdue fees

No bus operator shall be entitled to use the bus station while any licence fees owing by him or her under these By-laws remain unpaid after the due date thereof.

## by-law 11 Revocation of licence

The Council may revoke the licence of any bus operator who fails to pay by the due date thereof any licence fees payable by him or her or is convicted of any offence under these By-laws.

## by-law 12 Licence for shop, kiosk etc

The Council may at any time let to any person, or licence any person to use, any part of the bus station for a shop, kiosk or stall or as a booth for a telephone, or for the purpose of the installation and use of an automatic dispensing machine, for such period, at such rent or fee and upon such terms and conditions as the Council shall think fit.

## by-law 13 Selling goods etc at bus station

No person, other than licensed hawkers hawking hot peas and peanuts in baskets or hand-trays, shall sell or offer or expose for sale, any produce or goods in or around the bus station except at a shop, kiosk, stall or automatic dispensing machine, in respect of which the person selling or offering or exposing for sale produce or goods, or his or her employer, shall be the Council's tenant or the holder of a licence issued under by-law 12 hereof.

[by-law 13 subst LN 83 of 1963 by-law 2, 14 June 1963]

## by-law 14 Advertising space

The Council may at any time let to any person space in or on the bus station or any bus shelter for advertising purposes, for such period, at such rent or fee and upon such terms and conditions as the Council shall think fit.

## by-law 15 Placards etc in vehicle

No person shall place or deposit in or on any vehicle standing in the bus station any placard, print, hand-bill, card, advertisement, notice, pamphlet, book or paper without the prior consent of the owner or driver of such vehicle.

## by-law 16 Placards etc in bus station

No person shall place or deposit in or on the bus station or any bus shelter any placard, print, hand-bill, card, advertisement, notice, pamphlet, book or paper without the prior consent of the Council and on such terms and conditions as the Council shall think fit.

## by-law 17 Spitting

No person shall spit or discharge any nasal fluid or mucus on to any roadway, footpath or other part of the bus station or in or about any bus shelter.

## by-law 18 Recovery of fees

All licence fees payable to the Council under these By-laws shall be recoverable by the Council as civil debts by due process of law.

## by-law 19 Offences

Any person who shall fail to comply with any of the provisions of these By-laws (except by-law 5(2) ) or any person who shall commit any breach of these By-laws (except by-law 5(2) ) shall be guilty of an offence.

[by-law 19 am LN 15 of 1991 by-law 8, 19 April 1991]
