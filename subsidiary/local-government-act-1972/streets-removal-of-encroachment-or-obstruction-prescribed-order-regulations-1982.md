# STREETS (REMOVAL OF ENCROACHMENT OR OBSTRUCTION) (PRESCRIBED ORDER) REGULATIONS 1982

Streets (Removal of Encroachment or Obstruction) (Prescribed Order) Regulations 1982 (LN 65 of 1982) commenced on 25 June 1982, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Streets (Removal of Encroachment or Obstruction) (Prescribed Order) (Amendment) Regulations 1989 (LN 78 of 1989) | 15 December 1989 |

## O 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Streets (Removal of Encroachment or Obstruction) (Prescribed Order) Regulations 1982.

## O 2 Form of order requiring removal of encroachment on or obstruction of a street

An order directed to and served upon any person in pursuance to section 115(2) of the Act requiring that person to remove anything encroaching on, or obstructing, a street shall be in the form set out in the Schedule.

## 

### SCHEDULE FORM OF ORDER REQUIRING REMOVAL OF ENCROACHMENT ON, OR OBSTRUCTION OF, A STREET

[Sch subst LN 78 of 1989 reg 2, 15 December 1989]

\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ COUNCIL

To: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

of \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

Take notice that you are hereby required by the Council, acting in pursuance of section 115(2) of the Local Government Act 1972, to remove, within 24 hours commencing at the time of service of this order, \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ from \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ Street, being an encroachment or obstruction in contravention of section 115(1) of that Act.

And further take notice that if you fail to comply with this order, the Council may, under section 115(3) of that Act, cause the encroachment or obstruction to be removed and may recover from you the costs and expenses incurred in such removal and all costs and expenses incurred in repairing or making good any injury done to any street by reason of such encroachment or obstruction.
