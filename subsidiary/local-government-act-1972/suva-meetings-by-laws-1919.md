# SUVA (MEETINGS) BY-LAWS 1919

Suva (Meetings) By-laws 1919 (GN 174 of 1919) commenced on 1 August 1919, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Suva City Council (Meetings) (Amendment) By-laws 1979 (LN 113 of 1979) | 1 June 1979 |

## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [by-law 1]

### by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Suva (Meetings) By-laws 1919.

## PART 2 NOTICES OF MEETINGS [by-law 2-4]

### by-law 2 Form of notices

All notices to attend meetings of the Council shall be in writing and shall state the place, day and hour of the meeting. To such notices for ordinary meetings shall be attached a copy of every motion of which notice has been duly given to be moved thereat. Notices to attend a special meeting shall also contain a brief statement of the business for which the meeting is called.

### by-law 3 Mode of service

The town clerk shall send to each member one notice as aforesaid for each meeting by messenger or by post.

### by-law 4 Time of service

Such notices shall be sent so as to reach each member's last known place of business or abode in the case of an ordinary meeting 3 clear days before the day fixed for the meeting, and in the case of a special meeting so as to give as much notice as practicable which shall be not less than 3 hours.

## PART 3 ORDER OF BUSINESS [by-law 5]

### by-law 5 Order and conduct of business

The business shall be presented at all ordinary meetings of the Council in the following order, provided that the Council may deal with any particular matter on the business paper out of its order—

(a) reading and confirmation of the minutes of the last ordinary meeting and of all special meetings held subsequently thereto. No discussion shall be permitted concerning any minutes except as to their accuracy as a record of the proceedings.

(b) chairperson's minutes — The chairperson shall have the right of directing attention at any meeting to any matter or subject within the jurisdiction or official cognisance of the Council by a minute signed by himself or herself, and such minute shall, when introduced, take precedence of all business before or to come before the Council and the adoption thereof may be put by him or her from the chair as a motion, without being seconded.

(c) petitions — All petitions shall be received only as petitions of the party or parties signing the same. On the presentation of a petition no debate shall take place until notice has been given in the usual manner, and the only question that can be entertained by the Council on the day of its presentation shall be that the petition be received, or that it be referred to a committee.

(d) correspondence — All incoming correspondence to the Council shall be addressed to the town clerk and no letter shall be presented or read to the Council by any member. All outgoing correspondence from or on behalf of the Council shall be signed either by the town clerk or, with his or her authority, any other officer. The town clerk shall submit to the Council all correspondence which cannot be dealt with departmentally in pursuance of any standing order, or general, or special authority or direction made or given by the Council, provided that the Council may require or the chairperson may direct that all or any correspondence in connection with any matter be produced and read at any meeting.

(e) reports of committees — If in a report of a committee distinct recommendations are made, the decision of the Council may be taken separately on each recommendation. Any report of a committee, or any portion thereof, may be amended by the Council in any manner it may think fit, or may be referred back to the committee for further consideration. The recommendations of any committee, when adopted by the Council, shall be resolutions of the Council.

(f) reports of servants.

(g) questions — Sufficient notice of every question shall be given to permit of consideration of the reply thereto and, if necessary, reference to other persons or to documents. Every such question shall be put categorically, and without any argument. No discussion shall be permitted respecting any reply or refusal to reply to any question.

(h) motions of which due notice have been given — Each such motion shall be in writing signed by the member giving the same, and delivered to the town clerk not later than 10.00 am on the day fixed for the service of the notices to attend the Council meeting. Copies of such motions shall be sent to the members with the said notices. No motion after being placed on the business paper shall be withdrawn without the consent of the Council.

(i) any business which the chairperson may think fit to bring under consideration without notice.

## PART 4 CONDUCT OF MEETINGS OF COUNCIL AND COMMITTEES [by-law 6-46]

### DIVISION 1 WANT OF QUORUM [by-law 6-8]

#### by-law 6 Adjournment for want of quorum

If at the expiration of half an hour after the time at which any meeting of the Council is appointed to be held a quorum shall not be present, the meeting shall not be held, but shall stand adjourned until such time later in the day or until such day and time as shall be fixed by the chairperson or, in his or her absence, by the majority of the members present.

#### by-law 7 Particulars to be recorded in minutes

At all meetings of the Council when there is not a quorum of the members present, such circumstances, together with the names of the members present, shall be recorded in the minute book.

#### by-law 8 Suspension of proceedings

If at any time during the sitting of the Council the attention of the chairperson be called by a member or by the clerk to the fact that there is not a quorum present, the chairperson shall thereupon suspend the proceedings for a period of 3 minutes, and if a quorum be not present then, the names of those who are present shall be recorded upon the minutes, and the chairperson shall adjourn the sitting to some hour later in the day or to some future day, and the meeting shall stand adjourned accordingly.

### DIVISION 2 CHAIRPERSON [by-law 9-13]

#### by-law 9 Absence of chairperson etc

If at the expiration of half an hour after the time appointed for any duly convened meeting of the Council or a committee, the chairperson or acting chairperson (as the case may be) is absent, the senior member present shall if there be a quorum be the chairperson.

#### by-law 10 Chairperson may take part in proceedings

The chairperson may take part in all proceedings of the meeting at which he or she presides.

#### by-law 11 Rulings by chairperson

The chairperson shall preserve order. His or her ruling shall be given and obeyed without discussion or comment, and shall be final unless any member forthwith moves a motion of dissent therefrom. Such motion if seconded shall be put without discussion.

#### by-law 12 Basis of ruling may be stated

The chairperson, when deciding points of order or practice, may state the provision, rule, or practice which he or she deems applicable to the case without discussing or commenting upon the same.

#### by-law 13 Chairperson to be heard without interruption

When the chairperson rises in his or her place during the progress of a debate, any member then speaking or offering to speak shall immediately resume his or her seat, and every member shall preserve strict silence, so that the chairperson may be heard without interruption.

### DIVISION 3 ORDER [by-law 14-24]

#### by-law 14 Points of order etc

Any member may at any time call the attention of the chairperson to any member being out of order or to any other point of order.

#### by-law 15 Members to use official designations and to address chairperson standing

Members shall on all occasions when in Council or committee address and speak to each other by their official designations, as chairperson, acting chairperson, or member, as the case may be; and, with the exception of the chairperson, shall rise in their place and stand while speaking (except when prevented from so doing by bodily infirmity) and shall address the chair.

#### by-law 16 Chairperson to call on members to speak

If 2 or more members rise to speak at the same time the chairperson shall decide which of them shall be first heard.

#### by-law 17 Personal reflections etc

No member shall make personal reflections on, or impute improper motives to, any other member.

#### by-law 18 Acts of disorder

Any member who at any meeting of the Council or of any committee commits a breach of any Act or By-law, or who moves or attempts to move any motion or amendment embodying any matter beyond the legal jurisdiction of the Council or committee, or who in any other way raises or attempts to raise any question or addresses or attempts to address the Council or committee upon any subject which the Council or committee has no legal right to entertain or to discuss, or who uses any language which according to the common usage of gentlemen would be held disorderly, or who makes use of any expression inconsistent with good order and decorum, or who says or does anything calculated to bring the Council or committee into contempt, shall be guilty of an act of disorder.

#### by-law 19 Withdrawal of member

Any member who, having been called to order by the chairperson for any act of disorder, shall commit a further act of disorder, shall upon the request of the chairperson, withdraw from the Council room for the remainder of the sitting.

#### by-law 20 Removal of member

In the event of a member declining to withdraw from the Council room on being required so to do by the chairperson, the chairperson may order his or her removal until the termination of the sitting, and such member shall be deemed guilty of an offence against these By-laws.

#### by-law 21 Adjournment when disorder arises

If disorder arises at any meeting, the chairperson may adjourn the meeting for a period of 15 minutes and quit the chair. The Council on reassembling, shall, on question put from the chair, decide without debate whether the business shall be proceeded with or not.

#### by-law 22 Rejection of matter out of order

Whenever it has been decided that any motion, amendment or other matter is out of order, the same shall be rejected.

#### by-law 23 Resumption of consideration of interrupted matter

Whenever the consideration of any motion or matter of business has been interrupted by reason of a quorum not having been present, such consideration shall be resumed at the point at which it was so interrupted in its proper order of business at the next regular meeting of the Council.

#### by-law 24 Resolution to be reduced to writing and read aloud

Any resolution of the Council or of any committee shall forthwith on adoption be written down by the clerk in attendance who shall then read such resolution aloud before the Council or committee passes to the next business.

### DIVISION 4 VOTING AND DIVISIONS [by-law 25-26]

#### by-law 25 Voting

The chairperson shall put all questions and declare the sense of the Council thereon, and he or she may put the question as often as necessary to enable him or her to decide the voting. The members shall vote by a show of hands and if any member who is entitled to vote on the question neglects or refuses to do so his or her vote shall be counted for the negative. The decision of the chairperson as to the voting shall be final and conclusive unless it be immediately challenged and a member rise and demand a division.

#### by-law 26 Divisions

Upon a division being so called for, the question shall be first put in the affirmative and then in the negative, and the chairperson and all members present shall vote by show of hands and the names and votes of the chairperson and members present shall be recorded in the minutes by the clerk. Any member of the Council present when a division is called for who does not in the manner above indicated vote on such division, not being disabled by law from so voting, shall be guilty of an offence against these By-laws, and his or her vote shall be counted for the negative.

### DIVISION 5 DEBATES [by-law 27-37]

#### by-law 27 Motions to be seconded

No motion shall be debated unless it has been seconded.

#### by-law 28 Withdrawal of motions

When a motion has been proposed and seconded it shall become subject to the control of the Council, and shall not be withdrawn without the consent of the Council.

#### by-law 29 Amendment of motions

When a motion has been proposed and seconded, any member shall be at liberty to move an amendment thereon, but no such amendment shall be debated unless or until it has been seconded.

#### by-law 30 Motions and amendments to be in writing

No motion or amendment shall be debated unless or until it has been reduced to writing, if the chairperson so directs.

#### by-law 31 Order of moving amendments

No second or subsequent amendment shall be taken into consideration until the previous amendment has been disposed of.

#### by-law 32 Motion as amended may be amended

If an amendment has been carried, the motion as amended thereby shall become itself the motion before the Council, whereupon any further amendment upon such motion may be moved.

#### by-law 33 When amendment negatived, further amendment may be moved

If an amendment whether upon an original motion or upon any motion amended as aforesaid, has been negatived, then a further amendment may be moved to the motion to which such first mentioned amendment was moved, and so on, provided that not more than one motion and one proposed amendment thereof shall be before the Council at any one time.

#### by-law 34 Right to speak and right of reply

The mover of an original motion shall have the right of general reply to all observations which have been made in reference to such motion and to any amendment moved thereon, as well as the right to speak upon every such amendment. Every member other than the mover of an original motion shall have the right to speak once upon such motion and once upon every amendment moved thereon. No member shall, without the consent of the Council, speak more than once upon any motion, or for longer than 10 minutes at any one time unless when misrepresented or misunderstood, in which case he or she may be permitted to explain, without adding any further observation than may be necessary for the purpose of such explanation.

#### by-law 35 Right to reserve speech

A member who has moved any motion or amendment, other than a motion for adjournment of the debate, shall be deemed to have spoken thereon; but any member who has seconded any motion or amendment, without any further observation than that he or she seconds the same, may reserve his or her speech thereof for a later period of the debate.

#### by-law 36 Debate confined to matter under discussion

Every member speaking shall confine his or her remarks to the matter then under consideration.

#### by-law 37 Member may require motion etc to be read or records to be produced

Any member may request the motion or matter under discussion to be read or stated for his or her information, or may request the production of any records of the Council bearing upon such motion or matter as are readily accessible, provided that—

(a) no such request shall be so made as to interrupt any other member when speaking or to materially interrupt the discussion;

(b) that if any such request appears to the chairperson not to have been made in good faith he or she may decline to comply therewith; but the Council may dissent from his or her ruling.

### DIVISION 6 ADJOURNMENT OF MEETINGS [by-law 38-40]

#### by-law 38 Adjournment to later hour or later day

A debate may be adjourned to a later hour of the same day or to another day specified.

#### by-law 39 Motions for adjournment

No discussion shall be permitted upon any motion for adjournment of the Council. If upon the question being put on any such motion the same is negatived, the subject then under consideration, or next on the business paper, shall be discussed; and it shall not be competent for any member to again move a motion for adjournment until half an hour has elapsed from the time of moving the one that has been negatived.

#### by-law 40 Resumption of debate after adjournment

On resuming any discussion which has been adjourned the mover of such adjournment shall be entitled, if he or she has not already spoken on the subject under consideration, to speak first.

### DIVISION 7 REVOKING OR ALTERING RESOLUTIONS [by-law 41-46]

#### by-law 41 Written notice required of motion to alter or revoke a resolution

A resolution which has been passed by the Council shall not be altered or revoked except upon notice of motion duly given.

#### by-law 42 Notice to be given of motion having same effect as negatived motion

Where a motion has been negatived by the Board, no motion having the same effect shall be considered except notice thereof be duly given.

#### by-law 43 Notice of motion to be signed in certain cases

A notice of motion to alter or revoke a resolution, and a notice of motion which has the same effect as a motion which has been negatived by the Council, shall be signed by 3 members, if less than 3 months have elapsed since the resolution was passed, or the motion negatived as the case may be.

#### by-law 44 Restriction on motions

Where a motion to alter or revoke a resolution has been negatived, or where a motion which has the same effect as a previously negatived motion is negatived, no similar motion shall be brought forward within 6 months thereafter; and the effect of this provision shall not be evaded by substituting any motion differently worded but in principle the same.

#### by-law 45 Exception to by-law 44

Provided that any such motion as aforesaid may be moved at any time upon the report of a committee of the Council, and such report shall be specially mentioned in the minutes.

#### by-law 46 Motion of adjournment excepted

The foregoing provisions respecting negatived motions shall not apply to motions of adjournment.

## PART 5 SPECIAL MEETINGS AND ELECTION OF MAYOR [by-law 47-49]

### by-law 47 Special meetings

The business paper for each special meeting shall contain only such matters as have been stated in the notice thereof.

### by-law 48 Election of mayor

Nominations for the office of mayor, may be made without notice by any member. If there be only one person nominated, or if all the nominations except one be withdrawn or all the persons nominated except one decline nomination then the returning officer shall declare that one elected.

### by-law 49 Result of election to be notified

As soon as practicable after each election of mayor, the town clerk shall communicate the result in writing to the Government.

## PART 6 COMMITTEES [by-law 50-60]

### DIVISION 1 COMMITTEES OF THE WHOLE [by-law 50-51]

#### by-law 50 Application of these By-laws

The Council may, for the consideration of any matter, resolve itself into a Committee of the whole. All the provisions of these By-laws, so far as they are applicable, shall be extended to and govern all proceedings in Committee of the Whole, except the provision limiting the number and duration of speeches.

#### by-law 51 Committee to report to Council

All reports of proceedings in Committee of the Whole shall be made to the Council by the chairperson of such committee, and it shall be sufficient to state the general effect thereof. All such reports shall be recorded in the minute book; but no report shall be considered as adopted by the Council until a motion has been put and carried for such adoption.

### DIVISION 2 STANDING COMMITTEES [by-law 52-56]

#### by-law 52 Constitution of committees

Standing committees appointed in pursuance of section 27 of the Local Government Act 1972 shall be appointed annually as soon as practicable after the annual election of the mayor and shall consist of such number of members as the Council shall decide.

#### by-law 53 Chairperson of committee

Each committee other than a Committee of the Whole, shall appoint its own chairperson for its term of office, provided that the mayor, shall *ex officio* be a member of every committee and may when present preside.

#### by-law 54 Meetings of committees

The town clerk shall summon meetings of committees when directed by the mayor, or chairperson of the committee and shall give as much notice thereof as practicable to the members.

#### by-law 55 Absence

If any member of a standing committee shall absent himself or herself from 3 consecutive meetings thereof, without having obtained leave of absence from the Council or the committee, his or her seat shall become vacant, and the Council may appoint another member in his or her stead.

#### by-law 56 Finance committee

The finance committee shall examine and check all accounts, and shall watch generally over the collection and expenditure of the Council’s revenue. They shall also from time to time inquire into, and report upon all matters which, in their opinion, affect or are likely to affect the finances of the Council, and also such matters of like nature as they may be directed by resolution of the Council to inquire into and report upon.

### DIVISION 3 GENERAL [by-law 57-60]

#### by-law 57 Special committees

The appointment of every special committee shall be considered to continue until the duties for which the committee has been appointed have been fully performed.

#### by-law 58 Reports

Committee reports submitted to the Council shall be signed by the chairperson of the committee.

#### by-law 59 Communications

No committee shall communicate with any outside person or authority except through the clerk, as the servant of the Council duly authorised for the purpose.

#### by-law 60 Confidential business

All matters dealt with or brought before the Council in committee, or before any committee of the Council shall be strictly confidential and no information shall, unless the committee transacting the business, or the Council on reference thereon by such committee otherwise determines, be disclosed to any person outside the Council, except to approved Press representatives under embargo against publication until the matters in question have been made public by the Council. Any person contravening this by-law shall be guilty of an offence against these By-laws.

## PART 7 MISCELLANEOUS [by-law 61-63]

### by-law 61 Keeping of minutes

The minutes of all meetings of the Council and of all committees shall be transcribed by or under the direction of the town clerk into minute books which shall be preserved as permanent records of the Council.

### by-law 62 Authentication of minutes

Upon the minutes being approved at any meeting aforesaid the chairperson shall sign his or her name at the end thereof and initial all corrections therein and the date shall be entered against his or her signature. If the minutes be kept in a “loose leaf” book the chairperson shall sign each sheet of the minutes as confirmed.

### by-law 63 Penalty

Any person who shall offend against any of the provisions of these By-laws shall be liable on conviction to a fine of $20.
