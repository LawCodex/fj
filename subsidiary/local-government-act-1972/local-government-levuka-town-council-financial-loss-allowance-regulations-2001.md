# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (LEVUKA TOWN COUNCIL) (FINANCIAL LOSS ALLOWANCE) REGULATIONS 2001

Local Government (Levuka Town Council) (Financial Loss Allowance) Regulations 2001 (LN 21 of 2001) commenced on 12 April 2001, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Levuka Town Council) (Financial Loss Allowance) Regulations 2001.

## reg 2 Financial loss allowance

A member of the Levuka Town Council is entitled to an allowance at the rate of $5 per ordinary meeting or standing committee meeting as financial loss allowance for attending such a meeting.
