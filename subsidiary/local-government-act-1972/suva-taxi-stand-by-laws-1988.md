# SUVA (TAXI STAND) BY-LAWS 1988

Suva (Taxi Stand) By-laws 1988 (LN 26 of 1988) commenced on 5 February 1988, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Suva (Taxi Stand) (Amendment) By-laws 1989 (LN 55 of 1989) | 2 February 1989 |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Suva (Taxi Stand) By-laws 1988.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**attendant** means any person appointed by the Council to carry out the duties of managing and controlling taxi stands and includes the Council enforcement officers;

**car park** means any area or building or part of a building set apart by the Council for the use of motor vehicles as a parking place and notified as such by description published in the Gazette and in a daily newspaper published and circulating in Fiji;

**Council** means the Suva City Council;

**liquor** means any spirits, wine, ale, beer, porter, cider, perry, hop beer or any such liquor of a strength exceeding 3% of proof spirit but does not include methylated spirits;

**taxi** means a public service vehicle licensed to carry not more than 6 passengers excluding the driver;

**taxi operator** includes any owner or operator of a taxi;

**taxi space** means a parking space alloted for one or more taxis at a taxi stand;

**taxi stand** means any area set apart and duly declared, maintained and controlled by the Council for operation therefrom of taxis and includes parking spaces appointed for the use of taxis under the Land Transport Act 1998 ; and

[def am LN 99 of 2016 reg 253, 1 December 2016]

**town clerk** means the town clerk of the Council and any person performing a function of the town clerk.

## by-law 3 Management and control

A taxi stand shall be under the immediate management and control of the attendant.

## by-law 4 Duty to obey directions

(1) All persons using or being within the precincts of a taxi stand shall obey all reasonable directions given by an attendant or a police officer for the purpose of observing good order and for securing compliance with these By-laws.

(2) An attendant or a police officer may, for good cause, direct any person to remove himself or herself from the precincts of a taxi stand.

## by-law 5 Obstruction of attendant or police officer

A person shall not—

(a) obstruct, prevent or interfere with an attendant or a police officer in the performance of his or her duties or in the exercise of his or her powers;

(b) do any act which is likely to injure, endanger, obstruct, inconvenience or annoy any person in a taxi stand or in Council premises;

(c) use offensive, insulting or indecent or threatening language or make any utterances or gestures of a defamatory nature or behave in a disorderly manner in or about a taxi stand or in Council premises; or

(d) damage, deface or write on any part of the taxi stand area or other Council property.

## by-law 6 Prohibitions

No person shall make any alterations to the alignment of a taxi stand or introduce any unauthorised fitting, fixtures or obstruction into a taxi stand and anything so introduced shall be removed forthwith upon notice in writing from the Council and if a taxi operator fails forthwith to comply with such a notice the Council may proceed at the risk of the operator and at his or her expense to take down and remove the same from the area of a taxi stand and may recover the expenses incurred from the taxi operator.

## by-law 7 Illegal operation

(1) No person shall operate a taxi from an entrance, street, footpath, park, parking metered space, thoroughfare, division of an unauthorised taxi stand or Council private car park other than from the authorised taxi stand of each taxi as approved by the Council.

(2) A taxi operator shall not stop his or her taxi for the purpose of picking up passengers, whether by arrangement with such passengers or otherwise, at any taxi stand from which that taxi is not authorised to stand, wait or ply for hire.

## by-law 8 Loitering

No person shall loiter in any of the entrances, thoroughfares or divisions of any taxi stand.

## by-law 9 Consumption of liquor at taxi stands

No person shall offer, offer for sale or consume liquor at a taxi stand.

## by-law 10 Exchange, subletting and surrendering of a taxi stands

No taxi operator shall exchange, sublet, surrender or part with the possession of his or her taxi space or share his or her space with another taxi proprietor without the prior approval in writing of the town clerk.

## by-law 11 Identification stickers

Each taxi operator shall affix and keep affixed to the windscreen of his or her car the base identification sticker supplied by the Council which shall state the name of the registered proprietor of the vehicle, the registration number and the area of location of the taxi space.

## by-law 12 Application for taxi space

Applications for the use of any taxi space shall be made to the town clerk in writing and contain such particulars and attachments as may be required by him or her.

## by-law 13 Approval, refusal and cancellation

The Council or the town clerk may, after due consideration, approve or refuse any application for the use of any taxi space or at any time cancel any taxi space already allotted and the Council or the town clerk shall in refusing an application or cancelling any taxi space be bound to disclose and convey to the person affected the reasons for such refusal or cancellation.

## by-law 14 Allocation of taxi space

Allocation of taxi spaces shall be at the Council's discretion.

## by-law 15 Hours of operation

The Council may by resolution prescribe the hours of operation of a taxi stand.

## by-law 16 Offences relating to litter and damaging property

No person shall in any taxi stand—

(a) place, leave or deposit or permit to be placed, left, or deposited any derelict vehicle, glass, refuse, rubbish or any noisome or offensive matter except in accordance with the provisions of the Suva (Garbage Disposal) By-laws 2009 ;

(b) repair, grease, dismantle or assemble any vehicle otherwise than in the case of an emergency;

(c) wash or paint any vehicle;

(d) leave any vehicle or any box, rake, barrel, package or object so as to form any obstruction; or

(e) carelessly, wilfully or maliciously damage or destroy a building or structure which is the property of the Council.

## by-law 17 Parking

A taxi using an authorised taxi space shall park only within the limits set apart for it by the Council and as directed by any attendant or police officer.

## by-law 18 Obstructing entrances etc

(1) No taxi or other motor vehicle shall be parked in a taxi space or Council premises so as to obstruct any of the entrances, divisions or thoroughfares of a taxi stand and the driver of any such taxi, if called upon to so do by any attendant or police officer shall forthwith remove the vehicle.

(2) If the driver of any motor vehicle fails to comply with a direction given under paragraph (1) or if the driver is not available, the attendant may cause the vehicle to be removed to such other place as the attendant considers reasonable in the circumstances and the Council shall not be responsible for any loss or damage to the vehicle resulting from such removal.

(3) The registered proprietor of the taxi or other motor vehicle which is removed or towed away under the provisions of this by-law shall pay all charges and expenses incurred in such removal and storage of the vehicle before the vehicle is released to him or her and in default, the Council shall have the right to recover such expenses from him or her.

## by-law 19 Parking fees

(1) The Council may, by resolution, prescribe the parking fees for taxi spaces and may prescribe different fees for different taxi stands.

(2) Every resolution made by the Council pursuant to this by-law shall be published in the Gazette.

## by-law 20 Payment of parking fees

The parking fees for the use of a taxi space shall be paid in advance on the first business day of each month.

## by-law 21 Overdue fee

No taxi operator shall be entitled to use a taxi space while any fees owing by him or her under these By-laws remain unpaid.

## by-law 22 Recovery of fees and charges

All fees and charges payable to the Council under these By-laws may be recovered by the Council as a civil debt by due process of law.

## by-law 23 Inspection of permits

A taxi owner shall show for inspection his or her permit for a taxi space upon demand by an attendant or police officer.

## by-law 24 Avoidance of liability

Nothing in these By-laws shall be deemed to make the Council responsible for any loss or damage to any motor vehicle within the precincts of a taxi stand.

## by-law 25 Offences and penalties

Any operator or the driver of any vehicle who contravenes or fails to comply with any of the provisions of these By-laws shall be guilty of an offence and shall upon conviction for any such contravention or failure to comply be liable—

(a) for a first offence to a fine not exceeding $30;

(b) for a second or subsequent offence to a fine not exceeding $50; and

(c) in the case of a continuing offence, in addition to the penalties prescribed by paragraph (a) and (b) , to a fine not exceeding $10 for each day or part thereof that the offence continues.
