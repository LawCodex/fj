# SUVA (BUS STAND) BY-LAWS 1969

Suva (Bus Stand) By-laws 1969 (LGN 6 of 1969) commenced on 14 March 1969, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Suva (Bus Stand) By-laws 1969.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**bus operator** includes any person, firm, company or organisation which owns or operates a motor omnibus;

**bus shelter** means any shelter owned and controlled by the Council for the use of bus passengers in the city of Suva;

**bus stand** means a stand in any street designated by the Council as a bus stand from which a motor omnibus may operate;

**bus station** means the Suva bus station maintained and controlled by the Council and situate between Rodwell Road and Harris Street in the city of Suva the boundaries whereof are shown edged yellow on a plan lodged in the Department of Lands and Surveys under No S1105 together with the whole of the footpath situate on the southern boundary of the bus station and bordering the Rodwell Road Goods Vehicle Park and the market taxi stand;

**Council** means the Suva City Council;

**motor omnibus** means a public service vehicle constructed or adapted to carry more than 6 passengers excluding the driver; and

**town clerk** means the town clerk appointed by the Council.

## by-law 3 Licensed bus operators may use bus stands

A bus operator whose route does not necessitate the use of the bus station may use a bus stand for which he or she holds a licence under these By-laws.

## by-law 4 Application for licence

A bus operator desiring to operate from a bus stand shall make application in the form prescribed in Schedule 1 for a licence for his or her motor omnibus or omnibuses to use a bus stand.

## by-law 5 Issue of licences

All licences to use bus stands shall be issued on behalf of the Council by the town clerk and in the form prescribed in Schedule 2 and shall have effect in respect of the vehicle or vehicles described therein.

## by-law 6 Licence fees

(1) The licence fees for use of a bus stand by motor omnibuses shall be in accordance with the fees prescribed in Schedule 3 and shall be computed and charged in accordance with paragraph (2) .

(2)

(a) The licence fees payable in respect of each motor omnibus using a bus stand shall be charged in respect of each calendar month and in arrear.

(b) The licence fees to be charged for any such monthly period shall be computed on the total number of visits of the vehicle to the bus stand during the month and shall be calculated from the timetable approved by the Land Transport Authority in respect of the road service licence under which the vehicle shall have been operating during the month or from any other information which the said Authority shall think fit to supply to the town clerk from its records or from any other information obtained by the town clerk under subparagraph (c) .

(c) If the town clerk considers that the number of visits to a bus stand of any motor omnibus cannot be satisfactorily calculated from any timetable, he or she may by written notice require the bus operator concerned to supply him or her with particulars of the number of visits to the bus stand, and the times thereof, of each vehicle operating under the bus operator’s road service licence during the month the subject of the computation; and any bus operator so required to supply such particulars shall, within 7 days after the receipt by him or her of such notice, deliver to the town clerk a written statement, signed by such operator or under his or her authority, and containing a true and correct statement of the total number of visits to the bus stand and the times thereof, of each such vehicle during the said month.

[para (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 244, 1 December 2016]

(3) Each bus operator shall pay to the Council the licence fees payable by him or her within 15 days after the account thereof is rendered to him or her.

## by-law 7 Bus stands shall not to be used without licence

(1) No bus operator shall be entitled to use a bus stand for the purposes of any motor omnibus unless he or she holds a valid licence issued under these By-laws in respect of such vehicle.

(2) No motor omnibus shall enter or use a bus stand at any time unless such a valid licence under these By-laws is held by a bus operator in respect thereof.

## by-law 8 Overdue fees

No bus operator shall be entitled to use a bus stand while any licence fees owing by him or her under these By-laws or the Suva (Bus Station) By-laws 1961 remain unpaid after the due date thereof.

## by-law 9 Revocation of licence

The Council may revoke the licence of any bus operator who fails to pay by the due date thereof any licence fees payable by him or her or is convicted of any offence under these By-laws.

## by-law 10 Recovery of fees

All licence fees payable to the Council under these By-laws shall be recoverable by the Council as civil debts by due process of law.

## by-law 11 Restricted use

No vehicle other than a licensed motor omnibus may enter or use a bus stand.

## by-law 12 Offences

Any person who shall fail to comply with any of the provisions of these By-laws (except by-law 6 ) or any person who shall commit any breach of these By-laws (except by-law 6 ) shall be guilty of an offence.
