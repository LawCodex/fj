# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (LAMI TOWN COUNCIL FINANCIAL LOSS ALLOWANCE) REGULATIONS 1995

Local Government (Lami Town Council Financial Loss Allowance) Regulations 1995 (LN 53 of 1995), as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Lami Town Council Financial Loss Allowance) Regulations 1995.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, “Council” means the Lami Town Council.

## reg 3 Financial Loss Allowance

A member of the Council is entitled to an allowance af the rate of $10 per Ordinary meeting as financial loss allowance in respect of loss of earnings suffered or incurred for the purpose of enabling him or her to perform any approved duty as a member of the Council.
