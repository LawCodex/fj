# TOWN OF NASINU ORDER 2000

Town of Nasinu Order 2000 (LN 155 of 1999) commenced on 1 January 2000, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Town of Nasinu Order 2000.

## O 2 Declaration of the town of Nasinu

The area defined in the Schedule to this Order is declared to be a town for the purpose of the Local Government Act 1972 and is to be known as the town of Nasinu with effect from 1 January 2000.
