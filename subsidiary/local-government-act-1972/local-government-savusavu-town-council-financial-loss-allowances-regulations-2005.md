# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (SAVUSAVU TOWN COUNCIL) (FINANCIAL LOSS ALLOWANCES) REGULATIONS 2005

Local Government (Savusavu Town Council) (Financial Loss Allowances) Regulations 2005 (LN 18 of 2005) commenced on 11 March 2005, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Savusavu Town Council) (Financial Loss Allowances) Regulations 2005.

## reg 2 Financial loss allowance

A member of the Savusavu Town Council is entitled to an allowance at the rate of $25 per ordinary and standing committee meeting as financial loss allowance in respect of loss of earnings suffered or incurred for the purpose of enabling a councillor to perform any approved duty as a member of the Council.

## reg 3 Revocation

The Savusavu Town Council (Financial Loss Allowances) Regulations 2002 is revoked.
