# LAUTOKA (OPEN FIRES) BY-LAWS 1976

Lautoka (Open Fires) By-laws 1976 (LGN 18 of 1976) commenced on 30 July 1976, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Lautoka (Open Fires) By-laws 1976.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Council** means the Lautoka City Council;

**incinerator** means any receptacle or construction whether within a building or outside constructed or adapted for the destruction by fire of any object; and

**pollutant** means any solid, liquid or gaseous matter of a poisonous or noxious nature prejudicial to health or the safety of any person or, by odour or appearance, offensive or objectionable.

## by-law 3 Burning of objects prejudicial to health or safety

No person shall, without the consent in writing of the Council first had and obtained, burn, other than in an incinerator, any object or matter which may constitute a hazard to health or the safety of any person or property.

## by-law 4 Pollutants

No person shall, without the consent in writing of the Council first had and obtained, light any fire from which a pollutant is released into the environment.

## by-law 5 Authority to burn dead trees etc

The Council may, upon receipt of written application, authorise in writing the applicant to burn dead trees, wood, long grass, scrub, stubble, weeds or the cuttings of mowing or trimming arising out of the normal course of agriculture or horticulture, at such times and subject to such conditions as the Council may prescribe.

## by-law 6 Camp fires

Nothing in these By-laws shall affect the lighting of camp fires for the purpose of preparation of food or drink.

## by-law 7 Burning of vegetation or litter

Nothing in these By-laws shall affect a controlled burning of vegetation for the protection of life or property, or the burning of litter in an incinerator installed with the approval of the Council.

## by-law 8 Penalty

Any person who contravenes or fails to comply with the provisions of these By laws shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $20 and to a further fine of $5 for every day during which such contravention or non-compliance continues.
