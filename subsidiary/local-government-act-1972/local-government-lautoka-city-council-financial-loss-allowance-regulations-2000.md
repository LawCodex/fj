# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (LAUTOKA CITY COUNCIL) (FINANCIAL LOSS ALLOWANCE) REGULATIONS 2000

Local Government (Lautoka City Council) (Financial Loss Allowance) Regulations 2000 (LN 97 of 2000) commenced on 1 January 2000, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Local Government (Lautoka City Council) (Financial Loss Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2000 (LN 113 of 2000) | 26 October 2000 |
| Local Government (Lautoka City Council Financial Loss Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 (LN 11 of 2001) | 1 January 2001 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Lautoka City Council) (Financial Loss Allowance) Regulations 2000 and are deemed to have commenced on 1 January 2000.

[reg 1 am LN 113 of 2000 reg 2, 26 October 2000]

## reg 2 Financial loss allowance

A member of the Lautoka City Council is entitled to an allowance at the rate of $25 per ordinary and standing committee meetings as financial loss allowance in respect of loss of earning suffered or incurred for the purpose of enabling a councillor to perform any approved duty as a member of the Council.

[reg 2 am LN 11 of 2001 reg 2, 1 January 2001]
