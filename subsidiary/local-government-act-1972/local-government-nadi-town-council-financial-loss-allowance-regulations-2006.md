# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (NADI TOWN COUNCIL) (FINANCIAL LOSS ALLOWANCE) REGULATIONS 2006

Local Government (Nadi Town Council) (Financial Loss Allowance) Regulations 2006 (LN 111 of 2006) commenced on 29 September 2006, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Nadi Town Council) (Financial Loss Allowance) Regulations 2006.

## reg 2 Financial loss allowance

A councillor of the Nadi Town Council is entitled to an allowance at the rate of $25 per ordinary meeting or standing committee meeting as financial loss allowance in respect of loss of earnings suffered or incurred for the purpose of enabling a councillor to perform any approved duty as a councillor.

## reg 3 Revocation

The Nadi Town Council (Financial Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 is hereby revoked.
