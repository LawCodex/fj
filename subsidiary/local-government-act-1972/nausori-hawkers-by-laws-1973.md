## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Nausori (Hawkers) By-laws 1973.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Council** means the Nausori Town Council;

**goods** means—

(a) any food or drink other than liquor;

(b) cigarettes, cigars, tobacco and matches;

(c) curios and handicrafts, including mats, baskets, fans, articles made of straw and grass skirts;

(d) plants, ferns and cut flowers;

(e) jewellery, ornaments and souvenirs;

(f) live poultry and eggs;

(g) (i) books;

(ii) filigree jewellery, imitation jewellery and ladies’ evening bags;

(iii) dolls dressed in traditional costumes or portraying Fijian police officers;

(iv) postcards;

(v) fruit and vegetables;

(vi) scarves, cushion covers, beaded purses, men’s belts, with the word “Fiji” thereon on the outside thereof;

(vii) wares of other Pacific Islands origin, but not including any produce, article or thing made, manufactured, prepared in, or originating from any territory outside Fiji with the exception of the articles specified in paragraph (g) ;

[def am Decree 31 of 2010 s 4, 2 July 2010 LN 99 of 2016 reg 181, 1 December 2016]

**hawker** means a person who by going from place to place sells goods carried on his or her person or from a handcart, box, basket, bag, tray and the like, or from a boat, horse drawn vehicle, bicycle, motor vehicle or any other type of conveyance; and

[def subst LN 43 of 1981 by-law 2, 29 May 1981]

**officer** means the town clerk, any health inspector appointed by the Council, or any officer or servant of the Council in the performance of any duty under these By-laws.

## by-law 3 Hawkers to be licensed

No person shall within the town follow the calling of a hawker or hawk for sale any article whatsoever (whether or not such article falls within the definition of “goods” contained in by-law 2 ) unless he or she shall be the holder in his or her own name of an unexpired hawker’s licence issued by the Council.

## by-law 4 Application for licence

Every person desirous of obtaining a hawker’s licence shall make application in person in writing to the town clerk in the form in Schedule 1.

## by-law 5 Council may refuse, cancel or suspend licence

The Council may refuse to grant a hawker’s licence to any person, and the licence of any hawker may be suspended or cancelled for any good cause after giving the holder thereof due opportunity of being heard.

## by-law 6 Issue of licence

Upon receiving an application mentioned in by-law 4 and subject to such directions as he or she may receive from the Council, the town clerk may issue to the applicant, without payment of fee, a licence in the form set out in Schedule 2.

[by-law 6 subst LN 43 of 1981 by-law 3, 29 May 1981]

## by-law 7 Effect of licence

A hawker’s licence issued by the Council under by-law 6 shall authorise only the hawking and sale of such goods or classes of goods as are endorsed on the licence.

## by-law 8 Duration of licence

Hawkers’ licences shall be issued for a period of one year.

## by-law 9 Hawkers badge to be issued

With every hawker’s licence the town clerk shall issue to the licensee a badge of metal which shall be inscribed “Nausori Town Council Licensed Hawker” and with the number of the licence, for which the licensee shall deposit with the Council the sum of $2. This deposit shall be refunded to the licensee upon his or her surrendering such badge to the Council.

## by-law 10 No hawking outside specified area

No licensed hawker shall hawk in any place outside such area or areas as shall be specified by the Council.

## by-law 11 Allocation of spaces

The Council may, at its discretion, allocate or provide spaces to licensed hawkers, at a fee of $6 per month for each space.

[by-law 11 insrt LGN 8 of 1974 by-law 2, 1 August 1973am LN 27 of 1982 by-law 2, 26 March 1982]

## by-law 12 Duties and obligations of hawker

A licensed hawker shall—

(a) hawk only such goods or classes of goods as are endorsed on his or her licence;

(b) at all times when hawking carry his or her licence with him or her and shall on demand produce the same to any police officer or officer of the Council;

(c) at all times whilst hawking wear the badge referred to in by-law 9 ;

(d) not remain stationary in any road, street or place unless he or she is effecting a sale of his or her goods;

(e) if by remaining stationary in any road, street or place whilst effecting a sale of his or her goods he or she shall cause any obstruction to pedestrian or other traffic, forthwith move away from such road, street or place in such manner as effectively to remove such obstruction;

(f) when using a handcart or vehicle of any kind to keep such handcart or vehicle as near as practicable to the left hand side of the road or street;

(g) cease to hawk any goods intended for human consumption while he or she is suffering from any infection, contagious or offensive disease or skin complaint.

## by-law 13 Penalties

Any person who contravenes or fails to comply with the provisions of by-law 3, 10 or 12 shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable upon conviction to a fine not exceeding $20.
