# LOCAL GOVERNMENT (RAKIRAKI) (INTERIM ADMINISTRATION/COMMITTEE) REGULATIONS 2010

Local Government (Rakiraki) (Interim Administration/Committee) Regulations 2010 (LN 75 of 2010) commenced on 1 July 2010, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Rakiraki) (Interim Administration/Committee) Regulations 2010.

(2) These Regulations are deemed to have come into force on 1 July 2010.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Interim Administration/Committee** means the nominated members constituting the Interim Administration/Committee appointed by the Minister in exercise of powers conferred by section 5(2) of the Local Government Act 1972.

## reg 3 Interim Administration/Committee

The Interim Administration/Committee is deemed to be the duly constituted Council of the town of Rakiraki and may perform and discharge all the rights, privileges, powers, duties and functions vested in or conferred or imposed on the Council by the Act except Parts 2, 3, 5A, 7, 9, 11 and 12 (Divisions 2 and 3) and is deemed to be the Council for the purpose of any other written law.

## reg 4 Directions

The Interim Administration/Committee must in the exercise of its powers, functions and duties under these Regulations in accordance with any general or special directions issued by the Minister.

## reg 5 Duration

These Regulations remain in force until a mayor is elected after an election is held under the Local Government Act 1972 to elect the councillors of Rakiraki Town Council.

## reg 6 First election

The first election of the town of Rakiraki must be held before the end of year 2014.
