# LABASA (BUS STATION) BY-LAWS 1979

Labasa (Bus Station) By-laws 1979 (LN 15 of 1979) commenced on 19 January 1979, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Labasa (Bus Station) By-laws 1979.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws, unless the context otherwise requires—

**bus operator** includes any person who owns or operates a motor omnibus;

**bus shelter** means any shelter owned and controlled by the Council for the use of bus passengers in the town of Labasa;

**bus station** means the Labasa Bus Station maintained and controlled by the Council and notified as such by description in the Gazette and in a daily newspaper published and circulating in Fiji;

**Council** means the Labasa Town Council;

**motor omnibus** has the meaning assigned to it by the Land Transport Act 1998 ; and

[def am LN 99 of 2016 reg 127, 1 December 2016]

**town clerk** means the town clerk appointed by the Council.

## by-law 3 Management of bus station

(1) The bus station shall be under the immediate management and control of the town clerk.

(2) The Council may appoint an attendant or attendants to assist the town clerk in the discharge of his or her functions.

(3) Subject to the overall direction of the Council, the town clerk shall have responsibility for the allocation to bus operators of stands in the bus station and for the erection of traffic signs therein.

(4) Any person who stops or parks a motor omnibus at a stand other than that allocated to the bus operator by the town clerk or who fails to comply with any direction on a traffic sign erected at the bus station shall be guilty of an offence.

## by-law 4 Application for licence

Each bus operator desiring to use the bus station shall make application in the form prescribed in Schedule 1 for a licence for his or her motor omnibus to use the bus station.

## by-law 5 Licence to be issued by town clerk

All licences to use the bus station shall be issued on behalf of the Council by the town clerk in the form prescribed in Schedule 2 upon payment of the fees prescribed by the Council and shall be valid in respect only of the vehicle described in such Schedule.

## by-law 6 Fees

(1) The Council may, with the prior approval of the Minister, by resolution prescribe the charges for use of the bus station.

(2) Every resolution made by the Council in terms of this by-law shall be published in the Gazette.

## by-law 7 Fees to be charged monthly

(a) The licence fees to be charged under by-law 5 shall be charged in respect of each calendar month and in arrear.

(b) Such monthly licence fees shall be computed on the total number of visits of the motor omnibus to the bus station during the month and shall be calculated from the timetable approved by the Land Transport Authority in respect of the road service licence under which the motor omnibus shall have been operating during the month or from any other information which the said Authority shall think fit to supply to the town clerk from its record or from any other information obtained by the town clerk under paragraph (c) .

(c) If the town clerk considers that the number of visits to the bus station of any motor omnibus cannot be satisfactorily calculated from any timetable, he or she may by written notice require the bus operator concerned to supply him or her with particulars of the numbers of visits to the bus station, and the times thereof, of each motor omnibus operating under the bus operator’s road service licence during the month the subject of the computation; and any bus operator so required to supply such particulars shall, within 7 days after the receipt by him or her of such notice, deliver to the town clerk a written statement, signed by such operator or under his or her authority, and containing a true and correct statement of the total number of visits to the bus station, and the times thereof, of each such motor omnibus during the said month.

[by-law 7 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 127, 1 December 2016]

## by-law 8 Payment of fees

Each bus operator shall pay to the Council the licence fees payable by him or her within 15 days after the account thereof is rendered to him or her.

## by-law 9 Bus station not to be used without licence

(1) No bus operator shall be entitled to use the bus station for the purpose of any motor omnibus unless he or she holds a valid licence issued under these By-laws in respect of such motor omnibus.

(2) No motor omnibus shall enter or use the bus station at any time unless such a valid licence under these By-laws is held by a bus operator in respect thereof.

## by-law 10 Overdue fees

No bus operator shall be entitled to use the bus station while any licence fees owing by him or her under these By-laws remain unpaid after the due date thereof.

## by-law 11 Revocation of licence

The Council may revoke the licence of any bus operator who fails to pay by the due date thereof any licence fees payable by him or her or is convicted of any offence under these By-laws.

## by-law 12 Licence for shop, kiosk etc

The Council may at any time let to any person, or licence any person to use, any part of the bus station as a shop, kiosk or stall or as a booth for a telephone or for the purpose of the installation and use of an automatic dispensing machine, for such period, at such rent or fee and upon such terms and conditions as the Council shall think fit.

## by-law 13 Selling goods etc at bus station

No person not being the holder of a valid hawker’s licence shall sell, or offer or expose for sale, any produce or goods in or around the bus station except at a shop, kiosk, stall or automatic dispensing machine, in respect of which the person selling or offering or exposing for sale produce or goods, or his or her employer shall be the Council’s tenant or the holder of a licence issued under by-law 12 .

## by-law 14 Advertising space

The Council may at any time let to any person space in or at the bus station or any bus shelter for advertising purposes, for such period, at such rent or fee and upon such terms and conditions as the Council shall think fit.

## by-law 15 Placards etc in motor omnibus

No person shall place or deposit in or on any motor omnibus standing in the bus station any placard, print, hand-bill, card, advertisement, notice, pamphlet, book or paper without the prior consent of the owner or driver of such vehicle.

## by-law 16 Placards etc in bus station

No person shall place or deposit in or at the bus station or any bus shelter any placard, print, handbill, card, advertisement, notice, pamphlet, book or paper without the prior consent of the Council and on such terms and conditions as the Council shall think fit.

## by-law 17 Restricted use

No vehicle other than a licensed motor omnibus may enter or use the bus station.

## by-law 18 Recovery fees

All licence fees payable to the Council under these By-laws shall be recoverable by the Council as civil debts by due process of law.

## by-law 19 Offences

Any person who fails to comply with any of the provisions of these By-laws shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100 or to imprisonment for 3 months or to both such fine and imprisonment.

### INCREASE IN LABASA (BUS STATION) CHARGES

Pursuant to by-law 6 of the Labasa (Bus Station) By-laws 1979, notice is hereby given that in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by section 122 of the Local Government Act 1972, the Labasa Town Council with the approval of the Minister responsible for local government has revoked the last previous charges for the use of the bus station and replaced it with the following—

Bus station charges — $0.13 per bus per visit to the bus station.
