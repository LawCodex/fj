# NADI TOWN (NOISE PREVENTION AND ABATEMENT) BY-LAWS 1997

Nadi Town (Noise Prevention and Abatement) By-laws 1997 (LN 5 of 1997) commenced on 10 January 1997, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |
| Business Licensing (Repeal) Act 2020 (No 28 of 2020) | 1 August 2020 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Nadi Town (Noise Prevention and Abatement) By-laws 1997.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

(1) In these By-laws unless the context otherwise requires—

**Council** means the Nadi Town Council;

**level** in relation to noise, includes the volume or intensity of the noise;

**noise** includes sound and vibration;

**occupier** in relation to any premises, means the person in occupation or control of the premises and, in relation to any premises where different parts are occupied or controlled by different persons, means, in relation to any such part, the person in occupation or control of that part;

**offensive noise** means noise that, by reason of its level, nature, character or quality, or the time at which it is made, or any other circumstances, is likely—

(a) to be harmful to;

(b) to be offensive to; or

(c) to interfere unreasonably with the comfort or repose of a person (who is)—

(i) if the noise is made in premises that is not a public place, outside those premises; or

(ii) if the noise is made in premises that is a public place, within or outside those premises;

**public places** mean those places as ascribed under the Public Order Act 1969 ; and

**town rangers** mean the officers of the Council appointed under the Local Government Act 1972 .

(2) In these By-laws, a reference (however expressed) to making or emitting noise includes a reference to causing, permitting or allowing the making or emission of noise.

## by-law 3 Musical Instruments etc

(1) No person shall sound or play upon any musical or noisy instrument or operate a loud speaker so as to cause offensive noise.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1) of this by-law, sound measure level of 70 dB measured one metre from the point of origin shall be deemed to be offensive noise.

## by-law 4 Equipment etc

(1) No person shall operate any equipment, machine, tools and plant so as to cause offensive noise.

(2) For the purpose of paragraph (1) of this by-law, sound measure level of 90 dB measured one metre from the point of origin shall be deemed to be offensive noise.

## by-law 5 Use of special musical equipment

Any person desiring to use any equipment, plant machinery, tools or musical instrument referred to in by-laws 3 and 4 for short spells in order to undertake essential work which is likely to emit excess noise shall make an application to the Council in the form prescribed in Schedule 1 with an application fee prescribed by the Council.

## by-law 6 Permission to use special musical equipment

Upon receipt of the application referred to in by-law 5 , the town clerk shall determine the application and may approve or reject the same and approval shall be granted in the form prescribed in Schedule 2 .

## by-law 7 Noise abatement notice

(1) Any person committing an offence under by-law 3 or 4 may be issued with a noise abatement notice in the form prescribed in Schedule 3 by the Town Ranger of the Council requiring the person to cease forthwith making such offensive noise or may require the person to do such other things as considered necessary to reduce the noise level.

[para (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 172, 1 December 2016]

(2) Without in anyway affecting the generality of paragraph (1) , the town ranger may require the person to reduce the noise level in one or more of the following ways—

(a) by cessation of the noise or by control implemented at the source of the noise;

(b) by the acoustic treatment of buildings or other structures;

(c) by the process of shielding or barriers to restrict the emission of noise from premises;

(d) by the restriction of the time or place or manner of operation giving rise to the noise; or

(e) by the progressive phasing out of the noise or the progressive phasing out or control of activities that produce the noise.

(3) Where any person issued with a noise abatement notice fails to comply with the notice, the town ranger may confiscate the instrument making such offensive noise.

(4) For the purposes of investigating any complaints, the town ranger shall have the powers to enter any premises to measure the noise level and no person shall unreasonably obstruct the town ranger attending to his or her duties.

## by-law 8 Motor mowers on Sundays

No person shall operate a motor mower on a Sunday any earlier than 8:00 am and no later than 8:00 pm in residential areas.

## by-law 9 Penalty

Any person failing to comply with any of the provisions of these By-laws shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $500 and to a further fine of $2 for every day during which period such contravention or failure to comply continues.

## by-law 10 Exemptions

These By-laws shall not apply to noise emitting out of operations and functions stipulated in the Schedule 4 .

## by-law 11 Repeal

The Nadi (Noise Prevention) By-laws are hereby repealed.
