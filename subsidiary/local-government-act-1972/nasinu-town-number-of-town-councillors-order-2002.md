# NASINU TOWN (NUMBER OF TOWN COUNCILLORS) ORDER 2002

Nasinu Town (Number of Town Councillors) Order 2002 (LN 53 of 2002) commenced on 6 September 2002, as amended by:

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Nasinu Town (Number of Town Councillors) Order 2002.

## O 2 Number of Members

The number of town councillors to be elected to the Nasinu Town Council for each ward for each general election to the Council are set out in the Schedule.
