# NUMBER OF TOWN COUNCILLORS (BA) ORDER 1973

Number of Town Councillors (Ba) Order 1973 (LGN 34 of 1973) commenced on 26 October 1973, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Number of Town Councillors (Ba) Order 1973.

## O 2 Number of members

The number of members of the town councils named in the Schedule and, where the towns are divided into wards, the number of members for each ward shall, with effect from the next general elections to such councils, be as specified in the Schedule .

## 

### SCHEDULE NUMBER OF TOWN COUNCILLORS

Ba Town Council — 15 members, as follows—

Central Ward — 3 members

Varadoli Ward — 3 members

Rarawai Ward — 3 members

Yalalevu Ward — 3 members
