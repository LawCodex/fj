# LABASA (STREET NUMBERING) BY-LAWS 1976

Labasa (Street Numbering) By-laws 1976 (LGN 2 of 1976) commenced on 30 January 1976, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as the Labasa (Street Numbering) By-laws 1976.

## by-law 2 Marking of buildings etc with numbers

The owners or occupiers of lands on which buildings are erected or which are subdivided into building allotments and which have frontages to or entrances from roads shall for the purpose of distinguishing them mark the buildings or fences or road frontages with such numbers and in such manner and form as the Labasa Town Council may from time to time direct or approve, so that such markings may be readily seen from the road, and shall renew such markings as often as they are destroyed, obliterated or defaced.

## by-law 3 Failure to mark

If any such owner or occupier neglects for one week after written notice to him or her from the Labasa Town Council to mark any such building, or fence or road frontage with such number and in such manner and form as the Council may direct or approve, or to renew the marking thereof as aforesaid, he or she shall be guilty of an offence under these By-laws.

## by-law 4 Expenses of marking

Where the occupier of any land incurs expenses in complying with the requirements of any notice given to him or her under by-law 3 he or she shall be entitled to recover such expenses from the owner of the land.

## by-law 5 Destruction etc of marking

Any person who without the authority of the Council destroys, pulls down, obliterates or defaces any such marking or who marks any such number otherwise than in accordance with these By-laws shall be guilty of an offence under these By-laws and the Council may cause any unlawful marking to be obliterated or destroyed.

## by-law 6 Application

These By-laws shall apply only to such part or parts of the town of Labasa as the Labasa Town Council shall from time to time direct by notice published in the Gazette and a newspaper circulating within the Labasa town area.

## by-law 7 Penalties

Any owner or occupier aforesaid who is convicted of any offence under these By-laws shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $10 for each offence and in the case of a continuing offence to a fine not exceeding $2 for each day during which the offence continues after conviction.
