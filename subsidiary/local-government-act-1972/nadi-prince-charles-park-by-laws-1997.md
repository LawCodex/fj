# NADI (PRINCE CHARLES PARK) BY-LAWS 1997

Nadi (Prince Charles Park) By-laws 1997 (LN 161 of 1997) commenced on 19 December 1997, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Nadi (Prince Charles Park) (Amendment) By-laws 1998 (LN 130 of 1998) | 30 October 1998 |
| Native Lands (Amendment) Decree 2011 (No 7 of 2011) | 1 March 2011 |

## by-law 1 Short title

These By-laws may be cited as Nadi (Prince Charles Park) By-laws 1997.

## by-law 2 Interpretation

In these By-laws unless the context otherwise requires—

**Council** means Nadi Town Council;

**park** means Prince Charles Park contained in iTaukei Lease No 264696 and bounded by Naicker Street on the eastern side, Hospital and Mukerji Road on the southern side and Raniga Street on the western side; and

[def am Decree 7 of 2011 s 4, 1 March 2011]

**park ranger** means park leading hand appointed by the Council.

## by-law 3 Application to use park

(1) Any person desiring to use the park may apply to the Council at least 14 days prior to the park’s proposed usage.

(2) All applications to use the park shall be in a form prescribed in the Schedule to these By-laws.

## by-law 4 Determination of application

The town clerk, acting on behalf of the Council may approve or reject the application without giving any reason for his or her decision.

## by-law 5 Condition approval of application

Any approval granted by the Council through the town clerk shall be subject to such condition as the Council may from time to time decide.

## by-law 6 Revocation of approval

The Council may, by giving 7 days notice, revoke any approval granted in cases where a national or international event coincides on the same date.

## by-law 7 Fee

(1) There shall be a fee charged for the use of the park which shall be paid in advance.

(2) The fee shall be determined by the Council from time to time and such fee may be in form of a percentage of gate takings, a fixed fee, a fee on per hour of use or a fee based on cost to the Council for such hire plus on-cost as determined by the Council.

(3) The Council may if it considers expedient for proper accounting purposes sell the tickets for entry into the park on behalf of the hirer at the hirer’s expense and remit the sum collected less the Council’s fee with unsold tickets to the hirer within 7 days.

[para (3) insrt LN 130 of 1998 by-law 2, 30 October 1998]

## by-law 8 Hire charge not able to determine in advance

Where the hire charge of the park is not able to be determined in advance the Council may require the hirer to pay cash deposit or obtain a bank bond of such amount as the Council considers reasonable.

## by-law 9 Use of other facilities

The hirer of the park may use other facilities in the park with the prior approval of the Council and on such terms and conditions as decided by the Council.

## by-law 10 Opening of the gates

The hirer of the park shall ensure that gates are opened before the schedule time of commencement of the event so that the patrons may enter without any undue inconvenience.

## by-law 11 Rainy days

No person shall be allowed to use the park on wet days, and every approval granted shall be deemed to be cancelled if the park is wet for use on the day of the event.

## by-law 12 Public liability insurance cover

The hirer shall take out a public liability insurance cover from a reputable under writer to cover any mishaps, and a copy of the insurance cover shall be given to the Council in advance.

## by-law 13 Restricted use

The hirer shall use the park for the purpose it was proposed by the Council and shall vacate the park as soon as practicable upon completion of business in a neat and tidy condition to the satisfaction of the Council.

## by-law 14 Closure of park for maintenance

The Council may close the park for maintenance purposes including watering of turf, maintenance to pavilion or other amenities and during such closure no person shall be allowed to enter the park.

## by-law 15 Public address system

The public address system may be used with the prior approval of the Council and unless approved otherwise the sound level shall not exceed 100 dB measured 100 m from the speakers between the hours of 8.00 am and 8.00 pm and 70 dB between the hours of 8.00 pm and 8.00 am on the following morning.

## by-law 16 Prohibition

(1) No person shall cut, deface, remove or otherwise injure or damage any of the lawns, trees, shrubs, flowers, plants, seats, gates, posts, fences, hessian or other erections upon or bounding the said park or write thereon or affix any bill or stencil mark thereon.

(2) No person shall bring into the said park any animal or vehicle (including motor cars and motor cycles) or any dog which is not physically held in control.

(3) No person, not being a player, shall be allowed to enter or remain upon any cricket ground, tennis court, football or hockey ground or other places set apart by the Council for the purpose of playing cricket, tennis, football, hockey or other game or sport, within the park during the continuance of a game or sport if, by entering or remaining, he or she shall in a way interfere with the game or sport.

(4) No performance or entertainment of any kind or public meeting of assemblage or persons shall be permitted in the park without the permission of the Council.

(5) No person shall remove any rock, stone or other material from the said park without the leave of the Council.

(6) No person shall cast away, throw or deposit or cause or suffer to be cast away, thrown or deposited in the park or reserve except in receptacles provided for that purpose any fruit skins, rind or peel, or any bottles, tins or cans or the like, or any handbills, printed papers, waste paper, or paper of any kind whatsoever or plastic wrapper, or any other refuse or waste materials.

(7) No tents, booths or stall be erected nor any article sold or exposed for sale, on the park without the consent of the Council and except upon such terms and conditions as may be determined by the Council.

(8) No person shall bring into the park any glass, bottles or containers and no person shall sell or offer to sell any soft drink or other matter in glass containers in the park.

## by-law 17 Disorderly behaviour

Every person who, within the park is in a state of intoxication, or behaves in a disorderly manner, or creates, or takes part in any disturbance or obstructs any workmen, or, not being a player, interferes with any game or sport, may be removed by the ranger, overseer or servant of the Council or by a police officer, and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200.

## by-law 18 Non payment of charges

Any person entering, or remaining upon the park or upon any building or pavilion thereon, without having paid any lawful charge made for admission, or neglects or refuses to pay that charge on demand, may be forthwith removed therefrom and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200.

## by-law 19 Loitering

Any person found loitering or acting in a suspicious manner in the park during working hours or after hours, and does not have proper permission to be at the park is liable to be evicted from the park without notice and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200 or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months or both.

## by-law 20 Park ranger

The park shall be under the immediate control of the park ranger.

## by-law 21 Penalty

Any person who contravenes a provision of these By-laws is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $400 and to a further fine of $25 for every day during which such contravention or failure to comply continues.

## by-law 22 By-laws repealed

The Prince Charles Park By-laws are hereby repealed.
