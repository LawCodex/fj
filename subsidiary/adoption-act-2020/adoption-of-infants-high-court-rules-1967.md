# ADOPTION OF INFANTS (HIGH COURT) RULES 1967

Adoption of Infants (High Court) Rules 1967 (LN 17 of 1967), as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## r 1 Short title

These Rules may be cited as the Adoption of Infants (High Court) Rules 1967.

## r 2 Application for adoption order

(1) An application to the High Court for an adoption order shall be made by originating summons intituled in the matter of the infant and in the matter of the Act.

(2) The originating summons shall ask for the appointment of a guardian *ad litem* for the purposes of safeguarding the interests of the infant.

(3) The originating summons shall be accompanied by a statement, verified on oath or affirmation, of the particulars required to be stated in Form 1 set out in the Schedule to the Adoption of Infants (Magistrates Court) Rules 1946 and shall be accompanied also by the documents and copy documents referred to in rule 3 of those Rules.

## r 3 Parties to the adoption order application

The proposed adopter shall be the applicant, and the following persons shall be made respondents, namely, the infant in respect of whom the application is made, every person or body who is a parent or guardian of the infant or has the actual custody of the infant or is liable to contribute to the support of the infant, and the spouse, if any, of the applicant, except in the case of a joint application by 2 spouses.

## r 4 Proceedings for the adoption order

All proceedings in the High Court under the Act shall be heard and disposed of by a Judge in Chambers, subject to the power of the Judge to adjourn the proceedings into open court for the purpose of giving judgment or for such other purpose as he or she may think fit.

## r 5 Copy of the adoption order to be sent to the Registrar-General

Within 7 days after an adoption order has been passed and entered the Chief Registrar of the High Court shall send an office copy of the order to the Registrar-General.

## r 6 Entry of adoption order into the general register

The Chief Registrar of the High Court shall enter the particulars of all adoption orders made by the High Court in the general register for which provision is made by rule 17(3) of the Adoption of Infants (Magistrates Court) Rules 1946.

## r 7 Amendment of rules

Subject to these Rules, the Adoption of Infants (Magistrates Court) Rules 1946, as from time to time amended or replaced but with all necessary modifications, shall apply to proceedings in the High Court under the Act.
