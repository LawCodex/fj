# FIJI SPECIAL CONSTABULARY MEDAL REGULATIONS 1979

**Table of Amendments**

Fiji Special Constabulary Medal Regulations 1979 (LN 192 of 1979) commenced on 26 October 1979 as amended by:

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Fiji Special Constabulary Medal Regulations 1979.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, “Reserve” means the former Fiji Police Force Reserve.

## reg 3 Award of Special Constabulary medal

(1) The Fiji Special Constabulary Medal Regulations 1979 will be granted as a reward for long service and good conduct to members of the Fiji Special Constabulary who, on or after 10 October 1970, shall have completed 15 years’ continuous service as hereinafter defined.

(2) A Clasp will also be granted to a recipient of the Medal on his or her completing 25 years’ qualifying service, and a further Clasp on completing 30 years’ service. For each Clasp so awarded a small silver rose may be added to the ribbon of the medal when worn alone.

## reg 4 Qualification for the award of Special Constabulary medal

(1) Qualifying service in the Reserve and the Fiji Special Constabulary before 10 October 1970, may be allowed to reckon towards the required period of qualifying service. Service in a Special Constabulary which was qualifying service for the Colonial Special Constabulary Medal may be aggregated with service in the Reserve and the Fiji Special Constabulary and allowed to count towards the required period of 15 years’ service provided no award for the Colonial Special Constabulary Medal has been made.

(2) Where service has been rendered in the Fiji Special Constabulary and in one or more other Special Constabulary 12 months between the 2 periods of service shall not be regarded as breaking the continuity of such service.

(3) A break in service in any one Special Constabulary Force not exceeding 6 calendar months shall not be regarded as breaking the continuity of such service.

## reg 5 Other qualification for the award of Special Constabulary medal

(1) For the purpose of these Regulations, service shall be reckoned as qualifying service only if it is certified that the character and conduct of the person recommended for the grant of the Medal or Clasp has been exemplary. No member of the Special Constabulary shall be deemed to be of exemplary character who during the last 7 years of his or her service has been convicted of—

(a) any criminal offence, excluding offences under the Land Transport Act 1998 (but including the offences of driving under the influence of drinks or drugs);

(b) a disciplinary offence of drunkenness or incapacity for duty due to alcoholism; or

(c) a disciplinary offence and sentenced to a punishment of reduction in rank.

[subreg (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 82, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 6 Recommendations for the award of Special Constabulary medal

Recommendation for the award of the Medal or Clasp shall be submitted to the President by the Prime Minister on the advice of the Commissioner of Police. The Medal will be awarded on the authority of the Prime Minister and a notification of such award shall be published in the Gazette.

[reg 6 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 82, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 7 Forfeiture of the medal when the recipient has been convicted of a criminal offence, dismissed or removed from Police service for misconduct

(1) A recipient of the Medal or Clasp who is convicted of a criminal offence or is dismissed or removed from the Fiji Special Constabulary for misconduct shall forfeit the Medal or Clasp unless the President shall otherwise direct.

(2) A Medal or Clasp so forfeited may be restored to the recipient by the President on the advice of the Prime Minister.

(3) A notice of forfeiture or restoration shall in every case be published in the Gazette.

[subreg (3) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 82, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 8 Replacement of medal

(1) When a Medal is lost through carelessness or neglect, the Commissioner of Police may recommend that the loser be provided with a new Medal at his or her own expense. If the loss is accidental, it may be recommended that the loser be supplied with a new Medal, either at his or her own or public expense, according to the circumstances of the case.

(2) An application for a new Medal to replace one which has been lost must be accompanied by a declaration as to the circumstances under which the original Medal was lost. A new Medal will not be issued until a period of 6 months has elapsed since the date on which the loss occurred.
