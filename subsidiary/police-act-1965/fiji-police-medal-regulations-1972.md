# FIJI POLICE MEDAL REGULATIONS 1972

**Table of Amendments**

Fiji Police Medal Regulations 1972 (LN 61 of 1972) commenced on 28 July 1972, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Fiji Police Medal Regulations 1972.

## reg 2 Medal opened to all ranks and file of the Fiji Police Force

The Medal shall be open to all ranks of the Fiji Police Force.

## reg 3 Recommendations for the award of the medal

Recommendations for the award of the Medal shall be submitted to the President by the Prime Minister on the advice of the Commissioner of Police. The Medal will be awarded on the authority of the President and notification of such award shall be published in the Gazette.

[reg 3 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 80, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 4 Qualification for the award of the medal

The qualifications for the Medal shall be as follows—

(a) Conspicuous gallantry.

(b) Valuable service characterised by resource and devotion to duty, including prolonged services marked by exceptional ability, merit and exemplary conduct.

## reg 5 Medal award for conspicuous gallantry

Any award of the Medal for conspicuous gallantry shall be made as soon as possible after the event occasioning the grant, and all other awards shall be made biannually, provided that a recommendation may be made at any time when the President so commands.

[reg 5 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 80, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 6 Forfeiture of the medal when the recipient has been convicted of a criminal offence, dismissed or removed from the Police service for misconduct

(1) A recipient of the Medal or Clasp who is convicted of a criminal offence or is dismissed or removed from the Fiji Police Force for misconduct shall forfeit the Medal or Clasp unless the President shall otherwise direct.

[subreg (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 80, effective 1 December 2016]

(2) A Medal or Clasp so forfeited may be restored to the recipient by the President on behalf of the State on the advice of the Prime Minister.

(3) A notice of forfeiture or restoration shall in every case be published in the Gazette.

[subreg (3) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 80, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 7 Replacement of a lost medal

(1) When a Medal is lost through carelessness or neglect, the Commissioner of Police may recommend that the loser be provided with a new Medal at the loser's expense. If the loss is accidental, it may be recommended that the loser be supplied with a new Medal, either at his or her own or at the public expense, according to the circumstances of the case.

(2) An application for a new Medal to replace one which has been lost must be accompanied by a declaration as to the circumstances under which the original Medal was lost. A new Medal will not be issued until a period of 6 months has elapsed since the date on which the loss occurred.

## reg 8 Award of Queen’s Police medal

The award of the Medal will not be a bar to subsequent recommendation for the award of the Queen’s Police Medal.
