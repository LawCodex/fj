## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [regs 1, 2]

### reg 1 Short title and commencement date

These Regulations may be cited as the Police (Inspectorate and Subordinate Officers’ Leave) Regulations 1993 and shall be deemed to have come into effect on 1 January 1993.

### reg 2 Interpretations

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**salary** means basic salary;

**leave year** means a period of 12 months of resident service according to the anniversary of the date on which the officer joined the service or after 1 January 1993 (for those joining the service after the commencement of these Regulations) or the anniversary of the date of resumption of duty after their last vacation/ long service leave under the 1972 Leave Conditions for officers who joined the service prior to 1 January 1993, excluding any of the following periods of leave taken by the officers—

(i) Long Service leave;

(ii) Training courses, attachments or visits of more than 3 months duration;

(iii) Maternity Leave;

(iv) Sporting leave;

(v) Military leave without pay; and

**officer** means and includes all Police Inspectorate and subordinate officers.

## PART 2 RIGHT TO REGULAR LEAVE AND THEIR CLASSES [regs 3–10]

### reg 3 Leave etc to be right

Leave and other conditions under these Regulations shall be deemed to be the right of officers.

### reg 4 Categories of officers

For the purpose of these Regulations officers shall be divided into 2 categories as follows—

| | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| | Category A: | Officers in grades with salary maxima up to and including the PL05 maximum. |
| | Category B: | Officers in grades with salary maxima above the PL05 maximum and up to and including the PL04 maximum. |

### reg 5 Annual leave

(1) Annual leave shall be granted as follows—

(a) Category A — 18 working days

(b) Category B — 21 working days

(2) Annual leave may be taken on a daily basis on in multiples by arrangement with the Commissioner of Police during the leave year.

(3) Officers may, if they so desire, be allowed to carry over up to a maximum of 10 working days leave entitlement from one leave year to the next.

(4) Officers shall be allowed to anticipate up to 5 working days leave from the following leave year's entitlement during the final 6 months of any leave year; provided this may not be linked with a long leave of absence (2 weeks or more) save in exceptional circumstances and with prior approval of the Commissioner of Police.

(5)

(a) Annual leave shall be taken within 3 months after completion of leave year.

(b) The Commissioner of Police shall use his or her discretion in rescheduling of leave to avoid bulging of leave entitlement at particular times of the year and for this purpose leave entitlements shall be assessed and become due in the leave year as defined in regulation 2.

(6) Any officer who has his or her annual leave deferred either at his or her own request or by management for a period not exceeding 6 months, shall not be granted any additional leave nor shall he or she forfeit any leave from his or her leave entitlement as specified above for the period of deferment. The approval for such deferment shall be at the discretion of the Commissioner of Police.

(7) Any deferment of leave beyond 6 months shall be with the written consent of the officer and with the prior approval of the Commissioner of Police.

### reg 6 Long service leave

(1) In addition to annual leave as prescribed above officers shall be granted long service leave as follows—

(a) Categories A and B

(i) on completion of 12 continuous years service — 30 consecutive days;

(ii) on completion of 15 continuous years service — 42 consecutive days;

(iii) on completion of 20 continuous years service — 56 consecutive days;

(iv) on completion of 25 continuous years service — 84 consecutive days;

(v) on completion of 30 continuous years service — 112 consecutive days.

(2)

(a) Long service leave shall be taken together with an officer's annual leave due on completion of the officer's leave year.

(b) In exceptional cases or in case of retirement, long service leave may be granted before the end of the officer's leave year.

(c) Long service leave shall be regarded as non leave earning for annual leave purposes.

(3) An officer who has taken long service leave in his or her 12th, 15th, 20th or 25th year of service shall be entitled to take the difference between the long service leave he or she has already taken and the leave prescribed for completing 15, 20, 25 or 30 years.

### reg 7 Leave on resignation etc

In the event of resignation, termination of appointment on any grounds or death of an officer, he or she or his or her legal representative shall be paid—

(a) Pro rata compensation for—

(i) annual leave, due to the officer up to the date of his or her resignation, death etc;

(ii) leave allowance;

(iii) passages under regulation 13 if applicable.

(b) compensation for long service leave which shall be in accordance with his or her entitlement at the time of his or her resignation, termination of appointment on any grounds or death (see regulation 12).

### reg 8 Leave and passage on retirement

(1) In the event of retirement from the Force whether at the officer's own request or at the instigation of Government, the officer shall be granted—

(a) annual leave on pro rata basis;

(b) leave allowance on pro rata basis, provided that if the officer has completed 21 years 6 months service he or she shall be granted the full amount of leave allowance.

(2) In addition to subregulation (1), an officer who has completed 12 years or more continuous service shall be paid the full long service leave allowance due to him or her under regulations 8(a) and 8(b) provided he or she had not already taken it.

(3) An officer who is entitled to Auckland Passages, in accordance with regulation 13 and has served continuously for 20 years and has completed more than one and a half leave years shall, when proceeding on pre-retirement leave, be granted full Auckland passages.

### reg 9 Leave and passage before due date

In the event of an officer being authorised to proceed on leave before the due date, on medical grounds if recommended by a medical board or on grounds of urgent private affairs or if required by Government to take leave, he or she may be granted—

(a) annual leave on pro rata basis;

(b) leave allowance or, if entitled, Auckland Passages calculated on pro rata basis, provided that, if the officer is required by Government to proceed on leave before the due date on which he or she will become entitled to leave allowance under regulation 11 or Auckland Passage under regulation 13, the officer shall qualify for full leave allowance under regulation 11 or full passage in accordance with regulation 13.

### reg 10 Authority for grant of leave

Except as otherwise provided in these Regulations authority for the grant of annual and long service leave in accordance with these Regulations is vested in the Commissioner of Police.

## PART 3 LEAVE ALLOWANCES [regs 11, 12]

### reg 11 Leave allowance

(1) Officers are eligible to receive leave allowance (on written request during the 6 months immediately preceding the qualifying date) 7 days before the qualifying date as follows—

(a) Category A—

(i) $275 payable before long service leave is taken in accordance with regulation 6;

(ii) $125 every third leave year after qualifying for long service leave;

(iii) If long service leave is taken with annual leave and the officer qualifies for the leave allowance under (i) and (ii) then both allowances ($400) shall be payable.

(b) Category B—

(i) $275 payable before long service leave is taken in accordance with regulation 6;

(ii) $275 every third leave year after qualifying for long service leave;

(iii) If long service leave is taken with annual leave and the officer qualifies for the leave allowance under (i) and (ii) then both allowances ($550) shall be payable.

(2) Leave allowance for long service leave under Categories A and B shall be payable once only and no such allowance shall be payable when an officer utilises the balance of his or her long service leave under regulation 6(3).

(3) If the officer proceeds on long service leave before qualifying for leave allowance in accordance with regulation 11(a)(ii) and regulation 11(b)(ii), leave allowance shall be calculated on a pro rata basis, unless the officer is required by Government to proceed on leave before qualifying for such allowance, in which case he or she shall qualify for full leave allowance.

### reg 12 Pro rata leave allowance

For the purpose of this Regulation, the calculation of leave allowance on pro rata, shall be based on completed months served in the leave year and leave allowance or passage earning period respectively.

## PART 4 CLASSES OF AD HOC LEAVE [regs 13–25]

### reg 13 Overseas passages

(1) An officer who was eligible for overseas leave and passages under his or her pre-1972 Leave Conditions and had opted for future Auckland Passages shall receive up to 3 adult economy class passages for himself, his wife and children below the age of 18 years to Auckland, New Zealand, at the end of every sixth leave year on becoming entitled to long service leave under Category B.

(2) Any officer under Category B, who was eligible for Australian passages every 3 years under his or her pre-1972 conditions, shall further enjoy future Auckland, New Zealand Passages.

(3) No compensation shall be payable to any officer who elects to receive passages to Auckland as described in regulation 13(1) if he or she does not qualify for the full 3 adult passages or does not take the passages.

(4) Passage entitlement referred to in subregulation (1) above shall not be used for travel within Fiji.

### reg 14 Sick leave

(1) The Commissioner may, on the recommendation of a Government medical officer, grant at any time up to 3 months’ sick leave in any period of 12 months to any Inspectorate or Subordinate Officer. Such sick leave shall be on full pay, unless necessitated at the Officer's own indiscretion or the sickness is occasioned by the Officer's own negligence, in which case such leave may be without salary or on such reduced salary as may be decided by the Commissioner.

(2) If at the expiration of 3 months’ sick leave, any Inspectorate or Subordinate Officer is still unfit for service, the Commissioner may grant such officer any annual leave, he or she has earned and if at the expiration of such annual leave the officer is then certified by a medical board to be unfit for duty, his or her latter period of annual leave may be extended by a further period of 180 days which, subject to the provisions of subregulation (1), shall be on full salary. Where there is any reason to believe that the officer will ultimately be fit for duty, this period may be further extended by an additional 180 days which, subject as aforesaid, shall be on half salary.

[subreg (2) am LN 56 of 1994 reg 3, effective 1 January 1993]

### reg 15 Study leave

(1) When an officer is required to attend or attends any conference or is required to undertake or undertakes, with the prior approval of Government, any course of instruction, attachment or visit, the time spent on any such conference, course, attachment or visit shall not absorb any annual leave or long service leave due to the officer.

(2) Training courses, attachments or visits of more than 3 months duration shall be non-leave earning except for the purposes of qualifying for long service leave.

(3) If at the end of a training course, attachment or a visit of 3 to 12 months’ duration, the officer has no annual leave to his or her credit, he or she shall be granted 5 working days leave on full salary before being required to resume duty.

(4) If the training course, attachment or visit is for more than 12 months’ duration and the officer has no annual leave to his or her credit, he or she shall be granted 10 working days’ leave on full salary before being required to resume duty.

(5) An officer who is sitting examinations in connection with a course approved by Government shall be granted leave of absence for such examinations and such leave shall not count against any leave due to the officer.

(6) An officer who is granted study leave with salary shall be required to sign an undertaking to serve the Government for a further period in accordance with the bonding rules governing the recipients of in-service training award.

(7) An officer who is sent overseas for training shall travel by air and by the most direct route and where the normal period of the air journey is less than 24 hours, travel leave shall not be granted, but in other cases travel leave shall be limited to the period of the voyage up to a maximum of 2 days (based on air travel to the United Kingdom) and a similar period for the return journey shall be allowed.

### reg 16 Military service

Officers released by the Commissioner of Police for military service and for any military purpose other than military training shall have their leave and pay conditions governed by such administrative policy directives as may be issued by the Commissioner of Police at the time of their release.

[reg 16 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 204, effective 1 December 2016]

### reg 17 Military training leave

(1) An officer who is released for short-term military training in Fiji (weekend territorial training and annual camps) shall draw his or her military pay in addition to his or her civil pay, provided that a release for extended military training in Fiji in preparation for service overseas under the auspices of the United Nations mandate, shall be treated as leave without pay and officers so released shall not be entitled to civilian pay in addition to military pay.

(2) If, however, the military pay and allowances are less than the civil pay which the officer was receiving at the time of his or her release for military training, Government will pay the difference.

(3) Any period of military training shall not count against any leave due to an officer.

### reg 18 Maternity leave

(1) An officer shall not be permitted to return to work within a period of 42 days following her confinement.

(2) For the purpose of this Regulation—

(a) On the first 3 occasions of confinement, officers, both permanent and temporary shall be granted maternity leave on full salary up to a maximum absence of up to 84 days covering the period before and after confinement, provided that if the officer is unfit to return to duty as a result of illness arising out of pregnancy or confinement at the end of this time, she may be granted, by the Commissioner of Police, leave of absence without salary for a further period of up to 60 days and no further extension of leave shall be granted without the prior approval of the Commissioner of Police.

(b) After the first 3 occasions of confinement officers, both permanent and temporary, shall be granted leave without salary up to a maximum absence of up to 84 days before and after confinement, but during period of leave without salary, the officers shall be paid an allowance of $5 per day including Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays and, in the event of the officer being unfit to return to duty as a result of illness arising out of pregnancy or confinement at the end of this period, she may be granted, by the Commissioner of Police, leave of absence without salary for a further period of up to 60 days, but no allowances will be paid during this period and no further extension of leave shall be granted without the prior approval of the Commissioner of Police.

(c) An officer, whether permanent or temporary who has had maternity leave on 3 occasions under the previous conditions shall be subject to the provisions of (b) above in respect of future occasions and if she has had such leave on less than 3 occasions under the previous terms, she shall be subject to the provisions of (a) above for the appropriate number of occasions in future, before she is subject to the provisions of (b) above.

(d) An officer who has rejoined the service and has had maternity leave on at least 3 occasions during her previous service or has had 3 or more children, shall be subject to the provisions of (b) above and, if she has had such leave on less than 3 occasions or has less than 3 children, she shall be subject to the provisions of (a) above, in respect of the appropriate number of such occasions before she is subject to the provisions of (b) above.

(e) An officer who, on joining the service for the first time, has 3 or more children, shall be subject to the provisions of (b) above or, if she has less than 3 children, she will be subject to the provisions of (a) above, in respect of the appropriate number of such occasions before she is subject to the provisions of (b) above.

(f) Any leave arising out of pregnancy or a miscarriage before the officer takes her maternity leave shall be counted against sick leave for which the officer may be eligible and it will be counted against maternity leave if the officer has taken all sick leave for which she may be entitled.

(g) Where the allowance referred to in (b) above is payable, payment shall be made in one sum in advance.

(h) Officers shall not be allowed to resume duty before the expiry of their maternity leave.

(i) Any annual leave due to the officer before commencement of maternity leave shall not be absorbed into the maternity leave.

(j) An officer who takes maternity leave shall be granted annual leave on a pro rata basis for the number of months service from the date of resumption of duty after maternity leave to the end of the officer's leave year.

[subreg (2) am LN 56 of 1994 reg 4, effective 1 January 1993; LN 99 of 2016 reg 204, effective 1 December 2016]

### reg 19 Leave for sporting tour

(1) An officer who is selected to be an official or a member of a Fiji national sporting team shall be granted leave on full salary for up to 60 days in any one year and the period shall include local training sessions, but leave in excess of 60 days shall be debited to the officer's future annual leave.

(2) The period of sporting leave shall not earn any annual leave.

### reg 20 Leave without salary

No leave without salary shall be granted to any officer except in very special circumstances and application for such leave shall be made to the Commissioner of Police.

[reg 20 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 204, effective 1 December 2016]

### reg 21 Leave without salary in public interest

(1) Application for leave under this regulation shall be made to the Commissioner of Police.

[subreg (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 204, effective 1 December 2016]

(2) Leave granted under this regulation shall be deemed to be on the grounds of public policy for the purposes of regulation 13 of Schedule 2 of the Pensions Act 1983, but shall not earn annual or long service leave or any other leave or count as merit increase earning period.

(3) An officer granted leave under this regulation for a period which is to extend beyond twelve months shall be transferred to a Reserve List, whereupon, his or her post will become vacant.

(4) An officer granted leave under this regulation will be deemed to be the occupant of a supernumerary post and, upon resumption of duty, the officer shall be entitled to transfer to a suitable vacancy in his or her substantive or lower grade.

### reg 22 Leave without salary not in public interest

Application for leave under this regulation shall be made to the Commissioner of Police.

[reg 22 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 204, effective 1 December 2016]

### reg 23 Bereavement leave

The Commissioner of Police may grant an officer bereavement leave of up to 3 days in any one leave year on application and this leave shall be additional to any other leave to which an officer is entitled under these Regulations.

### reg 24 Unexpired leave

(1) Whenever possible officers shall be allowed to enjoy the full leave earned by them before resuming duty.

(2) An officer who is requested to resume duty before the expiry of his or her leave shall, if he or she so desires, be paid appropriate compensation for the total of his or her unutilised leave no later than the end of his or her leave year immediately following his or her recall.

(3) No officer shall be recalled from leave without his or her consent and the prior authority of the Commissioner of Police.

### reg 25 Leave in respect of public holidays for shift workers

(1) A shift worker who is required to work on a public holiday, shall be granted a day off in lieu of the public holiday in addition to the payment at the standard hourly rate of pay for the day in question.

(2) A shift worker, who does not work on a public holiday because it is his or her rostered off duty day, shall be granted a day off in lieu of the public holiday.

(3) A shift worker who is eligible for overtime and is on a rostered day off, recalled to duty on a public holiday and not rostered for an additional off-duty day to compensate shall be granted a day off in lieu of the public holiday in addition to payment at Rate 2 for the day in question.

(4) The days granted in lieu of the public holidays shall be in addition to the officer's annual leave entitlement.

## PART 5 MISCELLANEOUS [regs 26–28]

### reg 26 Extension of leave

(1) Extension of leave may be granted with the prior approval of the Commissioner of Police, on any of the following grounds—

(a) of ill health in accordance with regulation 14, on full salary;

(b) of public interest and, unless otherwise directed, on full salary.

(2) Extension of leave granted on any grounds other than those set out above shall be without salary unless decided otherwise by the Commissioner of Police.

### reg 27 Application for leave, leave rosters, leave schedules

Subject to the provisions of these Regulations, the Commissioner of Police shall give directives relating to application for long service leave, leave rosters and leave schedules applicable to the Inspectorate and Subordinate Officers.

### reg 28 Repeal and savings

(1) The Police (Inspectorate and Subordinate Officers’ Leave) Regulations, Chapter 85 is hereby repealed.

(2) Vacation and annual leaves earned by the Inspectorate and Subordinate Officers up to 31 December 1992 shall be calculated on pro rata basis and taken on the next anniversary of the date they resumed duty after their last vacation/long service leave under the Leave Conditions 1972 together with leave earned after the commencement of and under these Regulations.
