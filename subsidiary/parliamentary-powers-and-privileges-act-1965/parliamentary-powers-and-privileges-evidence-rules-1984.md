# PARLIAMENTARY POWERS AND PRIVILEGES (EVIDENCE) RULES 1984

**Table of Amendments**

## r 1 Short title

These Rules may be cited as the Parliamentary Powers and Privileges (Evidence) Rules 1984.

## r 2 Interpretation

A reference in these rules to a form by number shall be read as a reference to the form so numbered in the Schedule.

## r 3 Orders to attend

An order to attend to give evidence or to produce documents before Parliament or a committee thereof shall be in accordance with Form 1.

[r 3 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 197, effective 1 December 2016]

## r 4 Warrants

A warrant to apprehend a witness and bring him or her before Parliament or a committee thereof shall be in accordance with Form 2.

[r 4 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 197, effective 1 December 2016]

## r 5 Expenses

The sum for expenses which shall be paid or tendered in accordance with section 11(2) of the Act to a person summoned in accordance with that section shall be as follows—

(1) Travelling expenses—

When official transport is not provided the amount of the fares by public service bus but, in respect of expert witnesses, professional persons, merchants, bankers, persons in business on their own account, company directors and managers, and in respect of other persons where public service buses are not available, the actual or reasonable cost of transport incurred.

(2) Subsistence expenses—

(a) If necessarily absent overnight from place of residence the actual cost of board and lodging reasonably incurred.

(b) If not necessarily absent overnight but the attendance exceeds 4 hours or includes a luncheon adjournment, $1.50 in respect of each meal.

## r 6 Other powers

Parliament or a committee may—

(a) order any witness present to give evidence notwithstanding that a payment to which he or she may be entitled has not been paid or tendered to him or her;

(b) disallow any payment of expenses, for good cause which shall be noted on the record;

(c) allow an additional payment not exceeding a total increase of $10 in respect of any one person for any one day where it is satisfied that such person has suffered other expenses or additional pecuniary loss in excess of the expenses granted under Rule 5.

[r 6 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 197, effective 1 December 2016]
