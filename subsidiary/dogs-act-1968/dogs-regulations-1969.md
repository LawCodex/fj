## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Dogs Regulations 1969.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**authorised person** means a person who is, for the time being, authorised in writing by a responsible authority to lay poisons for the destruction of dogs;

**Permanent Secretary** means the permanent secretary responsible for primary industries; and

**responsible authority** means—

(a) the Permanent Secretary; and

(b) a person for the time being authorised in writing by the Permanent Secretary to perform the functions of a responsible authority under these Regulations.

[def am LN 134 of 1982 reg 2, effective 17 December 1982]

## reg 7 Control of use of poisons

The Permanent Secretary shall have control of all measures taken for the destruction of dogs by poison.

[reg 7 am LN 134 of 1982 reg 4, effective 17 December 1982]

## reg 8 Poisoned baits

(1) Poisons shall only be laid by authorised persons in the form of poisoned baits and any person who lays poisons otherwise than in accordance with the provisions of this paragraph, shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $100.

[subreg (1) am LN 134 of 1982 reg 5, effective 17 December 1982]

(2) No poisoned bait shall be laid for the purpose of poisoning dogs except between the hours of 8pm on any one day and 5am on the following day.

(3) No poisoned bait shall be laid within a distance of 25 yards from the outside walls of any building used exclusively as a dwelling house.

(4) Every authorised person shall count and record the total number of poisoned baits in his or her possession immediately prior to every occasion upon which such baits shall be laid.

(5) All poisoned baits which shall not have been consumed before 5am on the morning following the laying thereof, shall be collected by the authorised person who laid them and shall be preserved in the manner provided for in regulation 9 or shall be disposed of in such a manner as the Permanent Secretary may direct.

[subreg (5) am LN 134 of 1982 reg 5, effective 17 December 1982]

(6) The Permanent Secretary shall cause notice of any proposal for a poisoning campaign under this regulation to be given to the Commissioner of Police not later than 7 days before the intended date of the commencement of the campaign.

[subreg (6) insrt LN 134 of 1982 reg 5, effective 17 December 1982]

(7) The Permanent Secretary shall cause to be given to the Commissioner of Police, not later than 7 days after the conclusion of a poisoning campaign under this regulation, a report setting out, in relation to that campaign, the total number of poisoned baits prepared and, as far as is practicable, the movement and final disposal of each such bait.

[subreg (7) insrt LN 134 of 1982 reg 5, effective 17 December 1982]

## reg 9 Preservation of baits

(1) Every poisoned bait shall be kept in a locked container clearly marked in red so as to indicate that such container contains poisoned baits for the destruction of dogs.

(2) The Permanent Secretary shall cause to be kept a register showing the total number of poisoned baits prepared, after the commencement of this paragraph, for the purposes of these Regulations and, as far as is practicable, the movement and final disposal of each such bait.

[subreg (2) subst LN 134 of 1982 reg 6, effective 17 December 1982]

## reg 10 Disposal of carcasses

The carcasses of all dogs poisoned in accordance with these Regulations shall be collected as soon as possible after the death of such dogs and shall be burnt and buried forthwith.
