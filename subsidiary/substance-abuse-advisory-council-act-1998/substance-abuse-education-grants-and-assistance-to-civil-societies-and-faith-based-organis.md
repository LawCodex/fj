# SUBSTANCE ABUSE EDUCATION (GRANTS AND ASSISTANCE TO CIVIL SOCIETIES AND FAITH BASED ORGANISATIONS) REGULATIONS 2012

Substance Abuse Education (Grants and Assistance to Civil Societies and Faith Based Organisations) Regulations 2012 (LN 44 of 2012) commenced on 24 April 2012, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Substance Abuse Education (Grants and Assistance to Civil Societies and Faith Based Organisations) Regulations 2012 and shall commence on the date of its publication in the Gazette.

## reg 2 Objectives

The objectives of these Regulations are to—

(a) provide clear criteria, procedures and guidelines on how the Council can provide grants to civil societies and faith based organisations to enable these organisations to carry out education and training programmes;

(b) strengthen collaboration between Government, civil societies and faith based organisations in addressing drugs and substance abuse;

(c) decentralise some of the Council’s service delivery functions and meet the needs of rural communities;

(d) protect and safeguard against the abuse of Government funds by recipient organisations; and

(e) maintain transparency and clear accountability in the use of Government funds.

## reg 3 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**certificated religious leaders** means religious leaders who have attained a certificate or written statement of qualification to testify that they have the necessary qualification to practice as a religious leader in a religious organisation;

**civil societies** includes but is not limited to any—

(a) community based organisations;

(b) charitable organisations;

(c) village associations;

(d) environmental groups;

(e) human rights groups;

(f) professional associations; and

(g) other associations or organisations approved by the Council;

**controlling authority** means the body or organisation registered under the Charitable Trusts Act 1945 or the Religious Bodies Registration Act 1881 having overall command or control to check, direct or oversee the operations of other affiliated bodies or organisations under it;

**Council** means the Substance Abuse Advisory Council as established by section 3 of the Substance Abuse Advisory Council Act 1998; and

**faith based organisations** means any organisation or association based on religious beliefs whose controlling authority is registered under the Religious Bodies Registration Act 1881 .

## reg 4 Method of application

(1) Any civil society or faith based organisation may apply to the Council for a substance abuse education grant or assistance under these Regulations.

(2) All applications for substance abuse education grants must be made to the Council on the prescribed forms set out in the Schedule .

## reg 5 Eligibility of grants for civil societies and faith based organisations

(1) The Council may, in its discretion, make grants to civil societies and faith based organisations for the conduct of preventative programmes relating to alcohol, drug and substance abuse to members of the communities.

(2) Where a civil society, faith based organisation or its controlling authority eligible for a grant under subsection (1) has been registered under the Charitable Trusts Act 1945 or Religious Bodies Registration Act 1881 , the Council may make provision for the grant to cater for all or any of the following forms of assistance—

(a) the assistance towards the cost of workshops, training and awareness programmes and related matters;

(b) the assistance towards the payment of allowances of volunteers and certificated religious leaders engaged by the organisation or its controlling authority and who have attained relevant qualifications from any recognised tertiary institutions;

(c) the issue of office equipment designed to facilitate the conduct of drug awareness and training program in the communities;

(d) the issue of books and other educational materials and equipment; and

(e) such other forms of assistance as may from time to time be deemed desirable by the Council.

## reg 6 Conditions may be imposed

In granting any form of assistance under the provisions of these Regulations, the Council may impose such terms and conditions as it considers necessary in the circumstances on faith based organisations and civil societies who are the recipients of such assistance.

## reg 7 Suspension or cancellation of assistance

The Council may suspend, vary or cancel any form of assistance to any faith based organisation, civil society or controlling authority if, after due warning, it is satisfied that the conditions governing the award of such assistance have not been complied with.
