# SCRAP METAL TRADE (FORMS AND FEES) REGULATIONS 2011

**Table of Amendments**

Scrap Metal Trade (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2011 (LN 47 of 2011) commenced on 16 May 2011, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Scrap Metal Trade (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2011 and shall come into force on the commencement date of the Scrap Metal Trade Act 2011.

## reg 2 Application for licence to trade as a scrap metal dealer

(1) For the purpose of section 7(2)(a) of the Act, the prescribed application form is set out as Form 1 in Schedule 1.

(2) For the purpose of section 7(2)(b) of the Act the relevant fee is prescribed in Schedule 2.

## reg 3 Licence

For the purpose of section 8 of the Act, the prescribed licence form is set out as Form 2 in Schedule 1.

## reg 4 Appeal Fees

For the purpose of section 11(1) of the Act, the prescribed appeal fee is set out in Schedule 2.
