## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Post and Telecommunications (Callback Schemes) Regulations 2002.

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**callback scheme** means a telecommunications service which involves the use by any person of a telecommunications system or apparatus as part of a contract, arrangement, understanding or scheme with another person who would, but for such contract, arrangement, understanding or scheme, have made an outbound or inbound call himself or herself;

**inbound call** means the use by any person of a telecommunications system or apparatus to transmit voice or data content from a point outside of Fiji to a telecommunications system or apparatus within Fiji; and

**outbound call** means the use by any person of a telecommunications system or apparatus to transmit voice or data content from a point within Fiji to a telecommunications system or apparatus outside of Fiji.

## reg 3 Prohibition of callback schemes

(1) No person shall establish, use or operate a callback scheme within Fiji or from Fiji to another country or another place outside Fiji without a licence granted by the Minister following consultation with the relevant licensed telecommunication system operator or operators.

(2) Any person who contravenes subregulation (1) commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $5,000 or to imprisonment not exceeding 3 years or both.

(3) In addition to a fine or imprisonment imposed under subregulation (2), the court may order the confiscation and forfeiture of any apparatus or thing used in the commission of the offence.

## reg 4 Variation of licence

Any licence granted by the Minister under regulation 3(1) to a licensed telecommunications system operator shall be deemed to be a variation of the conditions of its licence.
