## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [regs 1–3]

### reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the National and Trade Measurement (Units and Standards) Regulations 1989.

### reg 2 Commencement

These Regulations shall come into force on 4 August 1989.

### reg 3 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**verify** includes reverify and verification includes reverification; and

**verifying agent** means a person who is permitted under these Regulations to verify standards of measurement.

## PART 2 UNITS OF MEASUREMENT [regs 4]

### reg 4 Fiji legal units of measurement

(1) The Fiji legal units of measurement for the quantities specified in Part 4 are—

(a) the base, supplementary and derived units of measurement belonging to the International System of Units (SI) prescribed by these Regulations; and

(b) such units of measurement not belonging to the International System of Units (SI) as may be permitted to be used pursuant to these Regulations.

(2) A Fiji legal unit of measurement may be referred to, and shall not be referred to other than as follows that is to say—

(a) a Fiji legal unit of measurement may be referred to by the symbol specified for that unit in these Regulations;

(b) a Fiji legal unit of measurement may be preceded by a prefix word or symbol;

(c) prefix words shall only be attached to words naming units of measurement and prefix symbols shall only be attached to symbols designating units of measurement;

(d) the word or symbol of a prefix shall be placed before and shall be contiguous with the word or symbol respectively of the unit to which it directly applies;

(e) not more than one prefix word or symbol shall be joined to the name or symbol respectively of the unit of measurement to which it directly applies;

(f) the use of a prefix with a Fiji legal unit of measurement means that the value of the unit is to be multiplied or divided by the factor specified opposite that prefix in Table 1—

## PART 3 STANDARDS OF PHYSICAL MEASUREMENT [regs 5–17]

### reg 5 Verifying agents

(1) The measurement laboratories set out in the Schedule to these Regulations are authorised to verify primary standards of measurement.

(2) The Director may appoint in writing a person to be a verifying agent to verify secondary or reference standards of measurement.

[subreg (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 178, effective 1 December 2016]

(3) An appointment under this regulation may be in relation to the verifying of standards of physical measurement generally or the verification of standards of physical measurement included in a particular class or particular classes of standards of physical measurement.

### reg 6 Certification, care and use of primary standards

Every Fiji primary standard of measurement shall—

(a) subject to paragraph (b) and regulation 14, be certified by the Director as a Fiji primary standard of measurement;

(b) not be so certified by the Director unless he or she is satisfied that the standard has been verified by a verifying agent and that the standard complies with the certificate issued by that verifying agent;

(c) be placed in the care of a person nominated in writing under the hand of the Director who shall be solely responsible for the care of that standard;

(d) not in any way be adjusted, cleaned, polished, repaired or otherwise dealt with in any manner whatsoever which could compromise the true value of the standard;

(e) be at all times retained in a place of safety when not in use and not be exposed to risk of damage through exposure to heat, fume, dust, corrosion or any similar hazard; and

(f) not be used except for the purpose of verifying secondary standards of measurement.

[reg 6 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 178, effective 1 December 2016]

### reg 7 Certification and use of secondary standards

Every Fiji secondary standard of measurement shall—

(a) subject to paragraph (b) and regulation 14, be certified by the Director as a Fiji secondary standard of measurement;

(b) not be so certified by the Director unless he or she is satisfied that the Fiji secondary standard of measurement has been verified against a Fiji primary standard of measurement; and

(c) be used only by persons approved by the Director to verify reference standards of measurement.

[reg 7 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 178, effective 1 December 2016]

### reg 8 Certification and use of reference standards

Every reference standard of measurement shall—

(a) subject to paragraph (b) and regulation 14, be certified by the Director as a Fiji reference standard of measurement;

(b) not be so certified by the Director unless he or she is satisfied that the reference standard of measurement has been verified against a Fiji secondary standard of measurement and has been found to comply with the limits of error specified in Table 3 of regulation 17 to permit the reference standard to be deemed to be of a value equal to its denomination;

(c) be used only by persons approved by the Director to verify standards of measurement and measuring instruments that are in use for trade or other prescribed instruments;

(d) be used only for the purpose of verification of standards and measuring instruments that are in use for trade or other prescribed instruments, or for measurements necessarily made in the course of science, engineering, manufacturing, construction, sporting events or similar purposes.

[reg 8 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 178, effective 1 December 2016]

### reg 9 Recognised-value standards

Every recognised-value standard of measurement shall—

(a) be issued as a recognised-value standard of measurement by the Director;

(b) not be so issued by the Director unless he or she is satisfied that the values stated are correct and to this end he or she may adopt by reference published recognised values into the standard.

[reg 9 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 178, effective 1 December 2016]

### reg 10 Certificates

(1) Subject to subregulation (2) every certificate relating to a standard of measurement issued by the Director pursuant to section 14 of the Act shall be sequentially numbered and shall—

(a) state the quantity that the standard purports to measure and the person responsible for the standard;

(b) describe the category of standard;

(c) state whether identifying marks or numbers were observed on the standard and describe any such marks or numbers;

(d) state the date on which—

(i) the certificate was issued;

(ii) the standard was verified; and

(iii) the certificate will expire;

(e) state in terms of Fiji legal units of measurement the numerical value of the standard as ascertained on the date when the standard was verified;

(f) estimate the uncertainty of the numerical value of the standard by stating the limits of possible error in the measurement of that value and giving a statistical probability that the true value will be within the limits of the possible errors so stated;

(g) confirm that the limits of possible error are such that the standard may under the provisions of regulation 17 be deemed to be without error and of a value equal to its denomination for all purposes related to the verification of measurements used for trade or in evidence given in a court of law in relation to the verification of a measurement or a measuring instrument used for trade;

(h) state whether the standard is recognised as a primary, secondary or reference standard of measurement;

(i) state the place where the standard was verified;

(j) where the standard was verified in Fiji, state the name of the person who verified the standard and the organisation employing that person;

(k) where the standard was verified otherwise than in Fiji, state the name of the organisation undertaking the verification;

(l) be signed by the Director or a person duly delegated by him or her to sign certificates.

[subreg (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 178, effective 1 December 2016]

(2) A certificate relating to a recognised-value standard of measurement is not subject to the provisions of subregulation (1)(c), (d)(ii), (d)(iii), (h), (i), (j) and (k).

### reg 11 Register of standards

The Director shall—

(a) retain a copy of every certificate issued for at least 3 years beyond the date stated under regulation 10(d)(iii) to be the date of expiry of the certificate and shall file these, for so long as retained, in numerical order to constitute the register of standards of measurement available in Fiji;

(b) index the register appropriately;

(c) in writing in the register forthwith cancel the certificate of any standard of measurement the value of which he or she reasonably believes may be untrue by an amount exceeding the possible errors stated in the certificate and shall inform the person responsible for the standard of the cancellation; and

(d) effect the cancellation in the register by endorsing on the certificate the word “Cancelled” and the date of cancellation.

[reg 11 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 178, effective 1 December 2016]

### reg 12 Cancellation of certificates

(1) A certificate may be cancelled at any time by the Director or by a person authorised by him or her in writing to do so.

[subreg (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 178, effective 1 December 2016]

(2) A standard of measurement of which the certificate has been cancelled shall not be used as a standard until that standard has again been verified and certified.

### reg 13 Physical quantity to be stated on certificates

The name of the physical quantity to be stated on certificates shall be that given in Part 4 of these Regulations, or, if not there stated, such name as may be determined by the Director.

[reg 13 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 178, effective 1 December 2016]

### reg 14 Stamping and marking of standards

(1) Subject to subregulation (2), a standard of measurement shall not be verified and certificated under these Regulations unless—

(a) a distinguishing mark has been stamped or otherwise legibly and permanently affixed on or to it before it is verified; or

(b) there is stamped or otherwise legibly marked on or affixed to it immediately after it has been verified, a mark indicating the date on which it was verified.

(2) This regulation does not apply to a standard of measurement if it is impractical by reason of its size or nature so to stamp or affix a mark referred to in subregulation (1) on or to it, or if the Director exempts a standard from this regulation.

[subreg (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 178, effective 1 December 2016]

### reg 15 Time for reverification

(1) Every standard of measurement shall be reverified within such periods of time as the certificate relating to that standard requires.

(2) Certificates issued in Fiji relating to a standard of measurement shall state a period of time, which shall not exceed the period shown in Table 2, within which that standard shall be reverified—

### reg 16 Portable balances

A portable balance used by an Inspector shall be certificated as if it were a reference standard of Mass and shall be again verified within one year of the date of the last verification of that balance.

### reg 17 Permissible variations

(1) Subject to subregulation (2), a standard of measurement that on verification is found not to be greater or less than its denomination by an amount exceeding the amount of permissible variation specified in Table 3 and which is used to verify standards or measuring instruments in use for trade—

(a) may, if it is a primary or secondary standard of measurement, be deemed to be of value equal to its denominations;

(b) shall, if it is a reference standard of measurement be deemed to be of value equal to its denomination, unless the certificate issued under regulation 10 states otherwise.

(2) The variation of the standard of measurement from its denomination stated in the certificate issued by the Director shall be determined to an uncertainty that is equal to or better than the maximum permissible uncertainty for that standard prescribed in Table 3.

## PART 4 FIJI LEGAL UNITS OF MEASUREMENT [regs 18–49]

### reg 18 Length

(1) For the quantity length, the Fiji legal unit of measurement is the metre, symbol m.

(2) The metre is the length of the path travelled by light in a vacuum during a time interval of 1/299 792 458 of a second.

(3) When not used for trade, permitted multiples and submultiples of the metre are the units specified in Table 4—

(1) For the quantity temperature, the Fiji legal unit of measurement is the kelvin, symbol K.

(2) The kelvin is 1/273.16 of the thermodynamic temperature of the triple point of water.

(3) When not in use for trade—

(a) permitted multiples and submultiples of the kelvin are the units specified in Table 9.

### reg 27 Velocity and speed

(1) For the physical quantity velocity and speed, the Fiji legal unit of measurement is the metre per second, symbol m/s.

### reg 33 Viscosity

(1) For the physical quantity dynamic viscosity, the Fiji legal unit of measurement is the pascal second, symbol Pa.s.

(2) The pascal second is the viscosity of a fluid for which there is a tangential force of one newton on one square metre of either of 2 infinite parallel planes one metre apart when—

(a) the space between those planes is filled with the fluid;

(b) 1 of the planes moves with a velocity of one metre per second in its own plane relative to the other; and

(c) the movement of the fluid is laminar.

(3) For the physical quantity kinematic viscosity, the Fiji legal unit of measurement is the square metre per second, symbol m2/s.

(4) The square metre per second is the kinematic viscosity of a fluid having a viscosity of one pascal second and a density of one kilogram per cubic metre.

## PART 5 NON-SI UNITS [regs 50–53]

### reg 50 Prescribed units not belonging to the International System of Units (SI)

(1) Units of measurement in this Part are Fiji legal units of measurement but are not within the International System of Units (SI).

(2) Unless permitted by these Regulations, units of measurement in this Part shall not be used for trade without the consent of the Director given in writing and published in the Gazette.

[subreg (2) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 178, effective 1 December 2016]

(3) A consent of the Director to use units of measurement in this Part for trade may be subject to such conditions as the Director sees fit.

[subreg (3) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 178, effective 1 December 2016]

(4) Notwithstanding the requirements of subregulation (2), where a unit of measurement which is within the International System of Units (SI) and which is permitted to be used for trade is duly used, a unit of measurement from this Part may be used in addition if the expression of the units in each case is of an equivalent value.

### reg 51 Measurement by appropriate units

Where the use of a unit of measurement other than a Fiji legal unit of measurement is essential for articles prepared solely for export from Fiji measurements may be made in any system of units appropriate to the measurement.

### reg 52 International conventions and government agreements

Nothing in these Regulations shall prevent the use of units of measurement expressed in international conventions or agreements between governments as being required for use—

(a) in the art of navigation by sea, air or rail; or

(b) for any other purpose to which an agreement ratified by the Government of the Republic of Fiji applies.

### reg 53 Conversion factors

Where measurements are expressed in a system of units other than the International System of Units (SI), whether pursuant to these Regulations or not—

(a) a name and symbol expressed in this Part, or in any publication referred to in this Part, that appears to be similar to the name and symbol of the unit of measurement so used, shall be deemed to be that unit of measurement; and

(b) the conversion factors to the International System of Units (SI) stated in this Part shall apply; or

(c) where a conversion factor is not stated in these Regulations, the value of the unit of measurement shall be determined from the publication of the Standards Association of Australia entitled Australian Standard 1376-1973 “Conversion Factors” and if the value of the unit of measurement is not therein available, from the publication of the British Standards Institute entitled British Standard 350 — 1974 “Conversion Factors and Tables”, or if the value of the unit of measurement is not therein available from any publication deemed by the Director to be appropriate.
