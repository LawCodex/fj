# NATIONAL AND TRADE MEASUREMENT (BREAD) REGULATIONS 1989

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the National and Trade Measurement (Bread) Regulations 1989.

## reg 2 Commencement

These Regulations shall come into force on 4 August 1989.

## reg 3 Interpretation and application

(1) In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**bread** includes milk bread, but does not include a bread roll;

**bread roll** means bread baked as a unit that does not exceed 170 grams in mass;

**loaf of bread** means the whole portion of bread baked from a single mass of dough or from 2 or more masses of dough fused together during baking for the purpose of producing a single article, and includes a packaged loaf; and

**orpackaged loafpackaged loaf of bread** means a loaf of bread (whether sliced or unsliced) that is enclosed in a package before sale.

(2) The provisions of these Regulations do not apply to bread that contains raisins, currants or sultanas or similar products, either singly or in aggregate.

## reg 4 Denominations of bread

(1) A loaf of bread or a packaged loaf of bread shall be denominated in the following denominations—

(a) 200 grams;

(b) 400 grams;

(c) 600 grams;

(d) 800 grams; or

(e) 1,200 grams.

(2) A person shall not—

(a) make for sale;

(b) sell;

(c) cause, permit or suffer to be made for sale or to be sold,

a loaf of bread or a packaged loaf of bread—

(d) that is not of a denomination prescribed by subregulation (1); and

(e) purporting to be of a denomination prescribed by subregulation (1), is of a true measurement that is less than of the measurement specified by that subregulation.

## reg 5 Bread packages to be marked

A person shall not package a loaf of bread before sale unless the package is marked as prescribed by regulation 6.

## reg 6 Marking of packaged loaves of bread

(1) Every packaged loaf of bread shall be marked on the main display portion of the package and, if there are 2 or more main display portions on at least 2 of those main display portions with the following—

(i) in the case of a 200 gram package, “Net 200 grams” or “200 g Net”;

(ii) in the case of a 400 gram package, “Net 400 grams” or “400 g Net”;

(iii) in the case of a 600 gram package, “Net 600 grams” or “600 g Net”;

(iv) in the case of a 800 gram package, “Net 800 grams” or “800 g Net”;

(v) in the case of a 1,200 gram package, “Net 1,200 grams” or “1,200 g Net”; or

(vi) in each case, letters and numerals to similar effect.

(2) Every packaged loaf of bread which contains sliced bread, shall also be marked with the word “sliced” adjacent to the marking of the quantity.

(3) The marks prescribed by subregulations (1) and (2) shall be printed in a colour of distinct contrast to the colour of the background, in letters and figures not less than 4.8 millimeters in height, so that they can be readily seen and easily read by any person when the packaged loaf of bread is exposed for sale.

(4) Any packaged loaf of bread packed for sale shall, in addition be clearly and legibly marked in accordance with section 43 of this Act, except that where a packaged loaf of bread is sold and delivered on the premises where such bread is manufactured and packed, no name and address of the packer is required.

## reg 7 Powers of inspectors with respect of weighing loaves of bread or packaged loaves of bread

(1) Any inspector may at any time by day or night—

(i) enter any place in which bread is sold or any bakehouse and therein search for and in the case of a bakehouse, in the presence of the person, or if more than one, any of the persons, carrying on the business thereat; or of any servant or agent of such person or persons, or in any case, in the presence of any person apparently then in charge of that place, select, inspect and test any loaves of bread or packaged loaves of bread therein;

(ii) stop any person or vehicle engaged in the distribution or delivery of loaves of bread or packaged loaves of bread and search for, select, inspect and test any loaf of bread or packaged loaf of bread in or on any vehicle or other thing used in connection with such distribution or delivery;

(iii) seize any loaf of bread or any packaged loaf of bread in respect of which a contravention of these Regulations appears to him or her, to have been committed;

and if any person in whose presence any bread referred to in subparagraph (i) of this subregulation maybe selected, inspected and tested when requested by the inspector to be present at that selection, inspection and testing, refuses, or fails to be present then, notwithstanding the provisions of that subparagraph, the inspector may select, inspect and test the bread in the absence of any person in that subparagraph specified.

(2) Notwithstanding anything in these Regulations contained, an inspector shall not include in any weighing any loaf which is proved to the inspector to have been baked more than 24 hours before such weighing.

(3) If on weighing any loaf of bread or any packaged loaf of bread, any deficiency is found from the true measurement prescribed therefore, the inspector shall give to the person who pursuant to this regulation is present at the weighing, a copy of a certificate in writing and signed by him or her showing the correct particulars of such weighing or, where any bread is weighed under the authority of this regulation in the absence of any such person, the inspector shall give such copy to some person employed or apparently employed at the place where any bread was made or sold, or in connection with the business carried on thereat, or if some such person cannot be found, the inspector shall forward such copy by registered letter addressed to the person or persons, at the place, who made or sold the bread so selected and weighed.

(4) Every person carrying on the business of making or selling bread and their agents and servants shall furnish the means required by any inspector for any entry, inspection, or any enquiry, or the exercise of his or her powers under these Regulations.

(5) If upon an inspector demanding entry into any place for the purpose of exercising his or her powers under these Regulations, that demand is not answered forthwith or the person answering that demand does not admit the inspector to that place forthwith, then that inspector, having the prior approval of the Permanent Secretary, may enter that place with such assistance as may be deemed requisite and may use such force as is necessary, for effecting that entry.

## reg 8 Savings

Save as herein expressly provided, the provisions of these Regulations are in addition to and not in derogation of any other provisions of this Act.

## reg 9 Offences

Any person who contravenes or fails to comply with any of the provisions of these regulations shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable upon conviction to the penalties as specified in section 54 of the Act.
