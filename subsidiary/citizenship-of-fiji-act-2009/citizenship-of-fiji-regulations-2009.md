# CITIZENSHIP OF FIJI REGULATIONS 2009

Citizenship of Fiji Regulations 2009 (LN 38 of 2009) commenced on 10 April 2009, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| | |

## PART 1 PRELIMINARY [regs 1-2]

### reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Citizenship of Fiji Regulations 2009.

(2) These Regulations shall be deemed to have come into force at the same time as the Act.

### reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Act** means the Citizenship of Fiji Act 2009.

**approved form** means the form approved by the Minister for the purpose of an application for citizenship pursuant to section 9(1) or 12(1) of the Act as specified in Schedule 1 ;

**certificate of naturalisation** means a certificate issued under section 13 of the Act and the provisions of these Regulations;

**certificate of registration** means a certificate issued under section 10 of the Act and the provisions of these Regulations; and

**prescribed fee** means a fee prescribed in Schedule 2 ;

## PART 2 APPLICATIONS FOR CITIZENSHIP BY REGISTRATION [regs 3-9]

### reg 3 Application to be made on approved form

An application for registration as a citizen of Fiji under the provisions of Part 3 of the Act shall be made to the Minister in the approved form.

### reg 4 Application by a child born abroad of a parent who was then a citizen

For the purposes of an application under section 8(1) of the Act the applicant must submit with the application—

(a) 2 recent certified passport sized photographs of the applicant;

(b) the original of the applicant's birth certificate;

(c) certified copies of the birth certificates of both the applicant's parents;

(d) the Fijian passport of any parent of the applicant who is a citizen of Fiji;

(e) where a parent of the applicant who was a citizen of Fiji on the date of the applicant's birth is either deceased or no longer a citizen of Fiji on the date the application is made, documentary evidence that the parent was a citizen of Fiji on the date of the applicant's birth;

(f) a certified copy of the marriage certificate of the applicant's parents; and

(g) the prescribed fee.

### reg 5 Application by a foreign child adopted by a citizen

For the purposes of an application under section 8(2) of the Act the applicant must submit with the application—

(a) 2 recent certified passport sized photographs of the applicant;

(b) the original of the applicant's birth certificate;

(c) certified copies of the birth certificates of both the applicant's parents;

(d) a certified copy of the marriage certificate of the applicant's parents;

(e) the Fijian passport of any parent of the applicant who is a citizen of Fiji;

(f) where a parent of the applicant is either deceased or no longer a citizen of Fiji on the date the application is made, documentary evidence that the parent was a citizen of Fiji on the date when the adoption took effect;

(g) a certified copy of the adoption papers relating to the applicant; and

(h) the prescribed fee.

### reg 6 Application by child who was a minor when a parent acquired citizenship

For the purposes of an application under section 8(3) of the Act the applicant must submit with the application—

(a) 2 recent certified passport-sized photographs of the applicant;

(b) the original of the applicant's birth certificate;

(c) certified copies of the birth certificates of both the applicant's parents;

(d) a certified copy of the marriage certificate of the applicant's parents;

(e) the Fijian passport of any parent who is a citizen of Fiji;

(f) a certified copy of the citizenship certificate of any parent;

(g) the prescribed fee.

### reg 7 Application on the basis of parentage by an applicant who is an adult

Where an application to which section 8(2) , (3) or (4) of the Act relates is made by a person who has reached the age of 18, then in addition to the documents required to be submitted under whichever of regulations 4 , 5 or 6 is relevant to the applicant, the applicant must also submit evidence that the applicant has been lawfully present in Fiji for a total of at least 3 years within the 5 years immediately preceding the application.

### reg 8 Application by an adult former citizen who is a citizen of another country

For the purposes of an application under section 8(6) of the Act the applicant must submit with the application—

(a) 2 recent passport sized photographs of the applicant;

(b) the original of the applicant's birth certificate;

(c) documentary evidence that the applicant was formerly a citizen of Fiji;

(d) documentary evidence of the applicant's citizenship of another country;

(e) where the applicant possesses the status of a Fijian permanent resident under the provisions of the Immigration Act 2003 , documentary evidence of that status;

(f) a police report concerning the applicant from the country or countries in which the applicant has formerly been resident;

(g) a local police report concerning the applicant;

(h) subject to section 22 of the Act, the prescribed fee.

### reg 9 Application by spouse or former spouse of a citizen

For the purposes of an application under section 8(7) of the Act the applicant must submit with the application—

(a) 2 recent passport-sized photographs of the applicant;

(b) the original of the applicant's birth certificate;

(c) the original of the applicant's marriage certificate;

(d) documentary evidence to establish that the applicant has been lawfully present in Fiji for a total of at least 3 years within the 5 years immediately preceding the date of the application;

(e) documentary evidence that the applicant's spouse or former spouse is, or if deceased, was a citizen of Fiji;

(f) the original birth certificates of any children of the applicant;

(g) the prescribed fee.

## PART 3 APPLICATIONS FOR CITIZENSHIP BY NATURALISATION [regs 10-11]

### reg 10 Application to be made on approved form

An application for the grant of citizenship of Fiji by naturalisation under the provisions of Part 4 of the Act shall be made to the Minister in the approved form.

### reg 11 Procedure for making application

For the purposes of an application to which regulation 10 refers the applicant must submit with the application—

(a) 2 recent certified passport sized photographs of the applicant;

(b) the original of the applicant's birth certificate;

(c) evidence to establish that the applicant has been lawfully present in Fiji for a total of at least 5 years within the 10 years immediately preceding the date of the application;

(d) a police report concerning the applicant from the country or countries in which the applicant has formerly been resident;

(e) a local police report concerning the applicant;

(f) a medical report on the applicant's health;

(g) letters of support from 3 referees who have indicated their willingness to act as such by signing the application form;

(h) the prescribed fee.

## PART 4 OATH OR AFFIRMATION OF ALLEGIANCE [regs 12]

### reg 12 Evidence of oath or affirmation of allegiance

(1) Where under the Act it is a condition of granting a certificate of registration or naturalisation that an applicant for citizenship make an oath or affirmation of allegiance, the applicant may make that oath or affirmation after the applicant has been notified that the Minister is satisfied that the applicant meets all other conditions for granting the certificate.

(2) After an applicant for citizenship has been notified by the Minister in accordance with subregulation (1) and has made the required oath or affirmation, the applicant shall submit documentary evidence of having done so to the Minister.

(3) Upon receiving such evidence as is required by subregulation (2) the Minister shall grant the applicant a certificate of registration or naturalisation, as the case may be.

## PART 5 RENUNCIATION OF CITIZENSHIP [regs 13]

### reg 13 Procedure for cancelling citizenship documents after renunciation

(1) Where the Minister has served a written notice under section 16(5) of the Act on a person who has made and registered a declaration of renunciation of citizenship that person must surrender to the Minister any document referred to in the notice within 21 days of receiving that notice.

(2) A document surrendered pursuant to subregulation (1) shall be cancelled by the Minister.

## PART 6 DEPRIVATION OF CITIZENSHIP [regs 14-15]

### reg 14 Procedure for making an order of deprivation of citizenship

(1) Where a person has been served with notice in writing in accordance with section 17(3) of the Act that the Minister proposes to make an order to deprive the person of his or her citizenship the person has 21 days from the receipt of the notice in which to make any representations to the Minister why the proposed order should not be made.

(2) Upon the expiration of the period referred to in subregulation (1) the Minister, after considering any representations submitted pursuant to that subregulation, may proceed to make the proposed order.

### reg 15 Procedure for cancelling citizenship documents after deprivation of citizenship

(1) Where the Minister has served on a person who has been deprived of his or her citizenship a notice pursuant to section 17(6) the Act, that person must surrender to the Minister within 21 days of receiving that notice any Fijian passport or emergency travel document of current validity and any certificate of registration or naturalisation which he or she holds.

(2) A document surrendered pursuant to subregulation (1) shall be cancelled by the Minister.

## PART 7 REGISTERS [regs 16]

### reg 16 Form of registers

Any register maintained for the purposes of section 20 of the Act may be kept—

(a) in writing;

(b) in the form of information stored by electronic means and readily retrievable; or

(c) in a combination of methods (a) and (b) .
