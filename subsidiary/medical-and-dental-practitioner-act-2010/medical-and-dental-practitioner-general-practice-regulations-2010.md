# MEDICAL AND DENTAL PRACTITIONER (GENERAL PRACTICE) REGULATIONS 2010

Medical and Dental Practitioner (General Practice) Regulations 2010 (LN 129 of 2010) commenced on 24 December 2010, as amended by:

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Medical and Dental Practitioner (General Practice) Regulations 2010.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the date of their Gazettal.

## reg 2 Application

These Regulations shall apply to medical practitioners in the civil service, or others who may want to enter general practice.

[reg 2 am Act 2 of 2016 s 20, 16 February 2016]

## reg 3 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**College** means the Fiji College of General Practitioners established under the Medical and Dental Practitioner Act 2010 ;

**general practice** means the discipline of clinical scientific medicine, to patients in the community regardless of the age of the patient;

**general practitioner** means a registered medical practitioner who engages in general practice;

**medical practitioner** means a person who holds a degree, diploma or similar qualification in medicine or surgery; and

**sole practice** means a general practitioner practising on his or her own account.

## reg 4 Requirements for medical practitioners in civil service for 3 to 5 years

(1) Any medical practitioner that has been employed in the civil service for 3 years but not exceeding 5 years, following his or her internship, and wishes to enter general practice must—

(a) apply to and become a member of the College;

(b) undertake the required postgraduate course as a minimum requirement; and

(c) acquire a certificate of good standing from the Fiji Medical Council.

[subreg (1) subst LN 81 of 2022 reg 2, 23 June 2022]

(2) The College must upon the attainment of the requirements in subregulation (1) above, identify a mentor for the new general practitioner.

(3) Having completed the requirements as stipulated in subregulation (1) above, the new general practitioner must be attached to the mentor’s practice for a minimum of 6 months.

(4) At the end of the 6 months period of attachment, the new general practitioner must practise 18 months under the supervision of the mentor either—

(a) in partnership with the mentor from the mentor’s current practice, or

(b) open a new practice on his/her own expense under the mentor’s name or style of practice.

(5) At the end of the 18 months period of mentorship, the mentor must submit a report to the College who will than make a decision as to the ability of the new general practitioner to do sole practice. If required, the mentoring process may be extended for a further 6 months.

(6) Upon the successful completion of the mentoring process, the medical practitioner may then open sole practice in general practice.

[reg 4 am LN 81 of 2022 reg 2, 23 June 2022]

## reg 4A Requirements for medical practitioners in civil service for 3 years with relevant qualifications

(1) Any medical practitioner that has been employed in the civil service for 3 years following his or her internship and has a postgraduate qualification in family medicine, public health, emergency medicine or internal medicine, and wishes to enter general practice must—

(a) apply to and become a member of the College; and

(b) acquire a certificate of good standing from the Fiji Medical Council.

(2) Having completed the requirements as stipulated in subregulation (1) , the medical practitioner may then open sole practice in general practice.

[reg 4A insrt LN 81 of 2022 reg 3, 23 June 2022]

## reg 4B Requirements for medical practitioners in civil service for 3 years with other qualifications

(1) Any medical practitioner that has been employed in the civil service for 3 years following his or her internship and has a postgraduate qualification in a field other than family medicine, public health, emergency medicine or internal medicine, and wishes to enter general practice must—

(a) apply to and become a member of the College; and

(b) acquire a certificate of good standing from the Fiji Medical Council.

(2) The College must, upon the attainment of the requirements in subregulation (1) , identify a mentor for the new general practitioner.

(3) Having completed the requirements as stipulated in subregulation (1) , the new general practitioner must be attached to the mentor’s practice for a minimum of 6 months.

(4) At the end of the 6 months period of attachment, the new general practitioner must practise 6 months under the supervision of the mentor either—

(a) in partnership with the mentor from the mentor’s current practice; or

(b) open a new practice on his or her own expense under the mentor’s name or style of practice.

(5) At the end of the 6 months period of mentorship, the mentor must submit a report to the College which will then make a decision as to the ability of the new general practitioner to do sole practice, and if required, the mentoring process may be extended for a further 6 months.

(6) Upon the successful completion of the mentoring process, the medical practitioner may then open sole practice in general practice.

[reg 4B insrt LN 81 of 2022 reg 3, 23 June 2022]

## reg 4C Requirements for medical practitioners in civil service for 5 to 7 years

(1) Any medical practitioner that has been employed in the civil service for 5 years but not exceeding 7 years, following his or her internship, and wishes to enter general practice must—

(a) apply to and become a member of the College;

(b) undertake the required postgraduate course as a minimum requirement; and

(c) acquire a certificate of good standing from the Fiji Medical Council.

(2) The College must, upon the attainment of the requirements in subregulation (1) , identify a mentor for the new general practitioner.

(3) Having completed the requirements as stipulated in subregulation (1) , the new general practitioner must be attached to the mentor’s practice for a minimum of 6 months.

(4) At the end of the 6 months period of attachment, the mentor must submit a report to the College which will then make a decision as to the ability of the new general practitioner to do sole practice, and if required, the attachment may be extended for a further 6 months.

(5) Upon the successful completion of the attachment, the medical practitioner may then open sole practice in general practice.

[reg 4C insrt LN 81 of 2022 reg 3, 23 June 2022]

(a) apply to and become a member of the College;

(b) undertake the required postgraduate course as a minimum requirement; and

(c) acquire a certificate of good standing from the Fiji Medical Council.

(2) Having completed the requirements as stipulated in subregulation (1) , the medical practitioner may then open sole practice in general practice.

[reg 4D insrt LN 81 of 2022 reg 3, 23 June 2022]

## reg 4E Transitional

(1) Any medical practitioner that was employed in the civil service for 3 years but not exceeding 5 years following his or her internship, and who prior to the commencement of the Medical and Dental Practitioner (General Practice) (Amendment) Regulations 2022 is undertaking the required period of attachment or mentorship under regulation 4 , must—

(a) acquire a certificate of good standing from the Fiji Medical Council in order to continue such attachment or mentorship; and

(b) successfully complete the mentoring process under regulation 4 in order to open sole practice in general practice.

(2) Any medical practitioner that was employed in the civil service for 3 years following his or her internship and has a postgraduate qualification in family medicine, public health, emergency medicine or internal medicine, and who prior to the commencement of the Medical and Dental Practitioner (General Practice) (Amendment) Regulations 2022 is undertaking the required period of attachment or mentorship under regulation 4 , is upon the commencement of the Medical and Dental Practitioner (General Practice) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, subject to the requirements of regulation 4A .

(3) Any medical practitioner that was employed in the civil service for 3 years following his or her internship and has a postgraduate qualification in a field other than family medicine, public health, emergency medicine or internal medicine, and who prior to the commencement of the Medical and Dental Practitioner (General Practice) (Amendment) Regulations 2022 is undertaking the required period of attachment or mentorship under regulation 4 , is upon the commencement of the Medical and Dental Practitioner (General Practice) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, subject to the requirements of regulation 4B .

(4) Any medical practitioner that was employed in the civil service for 5 years but not exceeding 7 years following his or her internship, and who prior to the commencement of the Medical and Dental Practitioner (General Practice) (Amendment) Regulations 2022 is undertaking the required period of attachment or mentorship under regulation 4 , is upon the commencement of the Medical and Dental Practitioner (General Practice) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, subject to the requirements of regulation 4C .

(5) Any medical practitioner that was employed in the civil service for 7 years following his or her internship, and who prior to the commencement of the Medical and Dental Practitioner (General Practice) (Amendment) Regulations 2022 is undertaking the required period of attachment or mentorship under regulation 4 , is upon the commencement of the Medical and Dental Practitioner (General Practice) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, subject to the requirements of regulation 4D .

[reg 4E insrt LN 81 of 2022 reg 3, 23 June 2022]

## reg 5 Requirements for medical practitioners outside the civil service

(1) Medical practitioners outside the ambit of the civil service who may want to enter general practice must be assessed for entry into general practice by the Fiji Medical Council and must fulfil the following requirements—

(a) a minimum of 5 years practice within the private health care system following internship to ensure enhanced clinical skills; and

(b) within these 5 years of practice as mentioned in paragraph (a) the medical practitioner must apply and become a member of the College and undertake the required postgraduate course as a minimum requirement.

(2) The College must upon the attainment of the requirements in subregulation (1) above, identify a mentor for the new general practitioner.

(3) Having completed the requirements as stipulated in subregulation (1) above, the new general practitioner must be attached to the mentor’s practice for a minimum of 6 months.

(4) At the end of the 6 months period of attachment, the new general practitioner must practise 18 months under the supervision of the mentor either—

(a) in partnership with the mentor from the mentor’s current practice, or

(b) open a new practice under the mentor’s name or style of practice.

(5) At the end of the 18 months period of mentorship, the mentor must submit a report to the College who will than make a decision as to the ability of the new general practitioner to do sole practice. If required, the mentoring process may be extended by the College for a further 6 months.

(6) Upon the successful completion of the mentoring process, the medical practitioner may then open a sole practice as a general practitioner.
