# MEDICAL AND DENTAL PRACTITIONER (FEES) REGULATIONS 2010

Medical and Dental Practitioner (Fees) Regulations 2010 (LN 131 of 2010) commenced on 4 January 2011, as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Medical and Dental Practitioner (Fees) Regulations 2010.

[subreg (1) am LN 99 of 2016 reg 72, 1 December 2016]

(2) These Regulations comes into force on 4 day January 2011.
