## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Patents Regulations 1965.

## reg 2 Advertisement of application for registration

Applications made under the provisions of section 23 of the Act for registration in Fiji of patents granted in the United Kingdom shall be advertised once in the Gazette and once in a newspaper published and circulating in Fiji, in Form 1 contained in the Schedule.

## reg 3 Application for entry in register

Applications for entry in the register under the provisions of section 30 of the Act shall be made in Form 2 contained in the Schedule, provided that the Solicitor-General may in any particular case permit an application in any other form.

[reg 3 am Decree 55 of 2012 s 3, effective 9 July 2012]

## reg 4 Production of documents of title

Every document giving effect to or being evidence of the assignment, transmission or devolution of the privileges and rights or interest specified in section 30 of the Act or affecting the proprietorship thereof, or any interest therein as claimed by an application under the said section, except such documents as are matters of record in Fiji, shall, unless the Solicitor-General otherwise directs, be produced to him or her together with the application and he or she may call for such other proof of title as he or she may think fit.

[reg 4 am Decree 55 of 2012 s 3, effective 9 July 2012]
