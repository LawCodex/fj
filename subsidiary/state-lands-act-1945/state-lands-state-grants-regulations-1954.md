## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the State Lands (State Grants) Regulations 1954.

## reg 2 Form of State grant, section 6

The form of State grant for the purposes of section 6 of the Act shall be as in Form 1 of the Schedule.

## reg 3 Form of State grant, section 9

The form of State grant for the purposes of section 9 of the Act shall be as in Form 2 of the Schedule.
