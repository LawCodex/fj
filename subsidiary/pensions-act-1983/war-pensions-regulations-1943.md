# WAR PENSIONS REGULATIONS 1943

**Table of Amendments**

War Pensions Regulations 1943 commenced on 27 April 1945 (Vol 4 of 1945), as amended by:

| Amending Legislation | Date of Commencement |
| --- | --- |
| Revised Edition of the Laws (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 (LN 99 of 2016) | 1 December 2016 |

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the War Pensions Regulations 1943.

[reg 1 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 298, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Act** [def rep LN 99 of 2016 reg 298, effective 1 December 2016]

**Board** means the Minister or such person appointed by the Minister;

[def am LN 99 of 2016 reg 298, effective 1 December 2016]

**claim** means a claim for a pension in accordance with the War Pensions Act 1943;

[def am LN 99 of 2016 reg 298, effective 1 December 2016]

**claimant** means a person who has made a claim for a pension on behalf of himself, or on behalf of a person under the age of 16 years, or on behalf of a person of unsound mind;

**pensioner** means a person to whom a pension has been granted and includes a person under the age of 16 years as well as a person of unsound mind; and

**trustee** means a person appointed to collect and disburse a pension payable on behalf of a pensioner under the age of 16 years, or on behalf of a pensioner of unsound mind, or on behalf of a pensioner who consents to the payment of his pension to such person.

## reg 3 Claim on behalf of child or person of unsound mind

Where a person under the age of 16 years or a person of unsound mind is entitled to a pension, a claim may be made on behalf of such person by any other person over the age of 16 years, and the Minister may direct that payment of the pension be made to a trustee for the benefit of the pensioner, provided that the appointment of a trustee may at any time be cancelled by the Minister.

## reg 4 Claim by member of the forces — Form A

Every member of the forces who claims a pension on the ground that he suffers from an incapacity shall prepare a claim in accordance with Form A in the Schedule.

## reg 5 Claim by dependant — Forms B and C

Every person who claims a pension on the ground that such person was or would have been a dependant of a member of the forces shall prepare a claim in accordance with Form B in the Schedule, provided that where a dependant or dependants is or are under the age of 16 years, the claim shall be made on Form C in the Schedule, and the declaration on that form may be made by any person who has a knowledge of the facts and who is more than 16 years of age.

## reg 6 Claim to be sent to Board (Revoked) (Repealed)

(Repealed)

## reg 7 Investigation of claim

The Board—

(a) upon receipt of a claim;

(b) whenever it is necessary or expedient to review a grant of pension, may arrange for the claim or case to be investigated by such other person as it may appoint, who shall ascertain from the claimant, pensioner or any other person, such information as may be necessary to enable the Board to determine such of the questions as have a bearing on the claim or case, or to ascertain the name and address of a person suitable for appointment as a trustee in respect of a pensioner under the age of 16 years or of unsound mind.

[reg 7 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 298, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 8 Board may summon claimant etc — Form D

The Board may at any time, for the purpose of ascertaining the truth of any facts stated in any claim or under regulation 7, summon before it the claimant, pensioner or any other person to give such evidence or produce such documents as may be necessary or expedient. Such summons shall be in Form D in the Schedule.

## reg 9 Inquiries

For the purpose of ascertaining or confirming any information it may require, the Board may address to the Commissioner of Police a request for inquiries and report, and may in like manner request such information from any other proper official or person whether in Fiji or abroad.

## reg 10 Report to Minister — Form E (Revoked) (Repealed)

(Repealed)

## reg 11 Confidential report may be required

The Board may require any person whom it believes to be in a position to do so, to furnish a confidential written report as to the circumstances of, the names, ages and means of livelihood of the dependants of, the family and blood relationships of, the means of livelihood of, and the age of any person who claims a pension or for whom a pension is claimed; and any person who, being a resident in Fiji, on being required to do so by the Board, fails to furnish a report within a reasonable time, shall be guilty of an offence, and shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $100.

## reg 12 Military record and medical investigation

The Board shall obtain from the proper military authority a copy of the record of a member of the forces who is referred to in the claim. In the case of a member of the forces who, it is claimed, suffers from an incapacity, the Board may accept such evidence of incapacity as is part of the member's record; but if not satisfied by that evidence, it shall take other steps as to it shall seem desirable in order to ascertain the nature and extent of the incapacity. For this purpose, it may request a Government medical officer to make an examination, and to report the result of such examination to the Board.

## reg 13 Recommendation to Minister (Revoked) (Repealed)

(Repealed)

## reg 14 Declarations

Declarations made under these Regulations may be made before any of the following persons: a Magistrate, a district officer, a Justice of the Peace, a minister of religion, a barrister and solicitor or a medical practitioner.

## reg 15 Record of claims — Form F

The ministry shall keep a record, in accordance with Form F in the Schedule of war pension claims received by it.

[reg 15 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 298, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 16 Certificate of incapacity — Form G

A medical certificate of incapacity may be in accordance with Form G in the Schedule.

## reg 17 Pension certificate and identification card — Forms H and I

On the grant of a pension, a pension certificate in accordance with Form H in the Schedule, shall be forwarded to the pensioner or trustee for the pensioner, and an identification card in accordance with Form I in the Schedule, shall be forwarded to the Chief Accountant.

## reg 18 Payment of pension

The issue by the Board to the Chief Accountant or to any sub-accountant of a voucher authorising payment to the pensioner or trustee of the quarterly instalment then payable and the production to the Chief Accountant or the sub-accountant by the pensioner or trustee of the pension certificate shall be the authority of the Chief Accountant or the sub-accountant to make payment of the instalment of pension therein described.

## reg 19 Pension payable quarterly

Pensions shall be payable quarterly in advance on the third day of the months of August, November, February and May in each year.

## reg 20 Receipt

The pensioner or the trustee shall give a receipt for each instalment in accordance with Form J in the Schedule.

## reg 21 Authority to receive payment

No payment shall be made to any person other than the pensioner or the trustee, unless such person is the holder of an authority in accordance with the Treasury form for the time being in force.

## reg 22 Declaration as to marriage

An instalment of pension granted to any female dependant of a member of the forces, being over the age of 16 years and being unmarried, widowed or divorced, shall not in any case be paid unless there is presented to the Chief Accountant or sub-accountant at the time of payment a declaration in Form K in the Schedule showing that, on the due date of such instalment, such female dependant has not married or remarried.

## reg 23 Declaration by trustee

An instalment of a pension payable to a trustee shall not in any case be paid unless there is presented to the Chief Accountant or sub-accountant at the time of payment a declaration in Form L in the Schedule, showing that, on the due date of such instalment, the person on behalf of whom the pension is payable is alive.

## reg 24 Suspension of pension

Where—

(a) a pensioner is admitted to hospital as an inpatient; or

(b) the Minister is satisfied that a pensioner is misspending money,

the Minister may, if he or she considers it in the interests of the pensioner so to do, suspend payment of the pension, provided that in such case the amount of the pension accrued during the period of suspension may at a later date be paid to the pensioner or trustee or, in the event of the pensioner's decease, may be dealt with under regulation 28.

## reg 25 Payment to trustee

The Minister may, with the consent of the pensioner, make payment of the pension to a trustee.

## reg 26 Lost certificate

If a pensioner or a trustee loses his pension certificate, he may make application in accordance with Form M in the Schedule, to the Board for a duplicate certificate; and if the Board issues a duplicate it shall notify the Chief Accountant or other officer who is authorised to pay the pension. The duplicate shall bear across its face the word “duplicate”, together with a note in writing stating the date on which the duplicate is issued.

[reg 26 am LN 99 of 2016 reg 298, effective 1 December 2016]

## reg 27 Change of place of payment

If a pensioner or a trustee desires to change the place of payment of pension, he may make application in Form N in the Schedule, to the officer authorised to pay the pension, and if such officer is satisfied that the request is a reasonable one, he shall make a note on the pension certificate as follows — “Payment to be made in future at “ and add his signature.

## reg 28 Payment to legal representative etc

Any undrawn instalment of a pension due at the date of a pensioner's decease, if applied for within 6 months after such decease, may be paid to such person as in the opinion of the Board has a legal or equitable claim thereto, provided that the Board shall not authorise payment to any person who is not a legal representative of the deceased, until it is satisfied that probate of will or letters of administration will not be applied for.

## reg 29 Inquiries and report by Registrar of Deaths

(1) Whenever the death of any person is reported to the Registrar of Deaths for registration, the Registrar of Deaths may inquire whether the deceased was a pensioner under the Act and the person reporting the death shall state, to the best of his or her knowledge, whether the deceased was a pensioner or not. If the deceased was a pensioner, the Registrar of Deaths should send to the Board a notice of death, in accordance with Form O in the Schedule.

(2) In this regulation, “Registrar of Deaths” means any officer charged with the duty of registering deaths.
