## O 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Pensions (Republic of Fiji Military Forces Eligibility) Order 1986.

## O 2 Part 4 to cease to apply

Part 4 of the Pensions Act 1983 (other than section 37 and so much of that Part as relates to section 37) shall not apply to a person who joins the Republic of Fiji Military Forces, either as an officer or a soldier, after 1 November 1986.
