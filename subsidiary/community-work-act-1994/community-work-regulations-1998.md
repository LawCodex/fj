## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Community Work Regulations 1998.

## reg 2 Commencement

These Regulations come into force on 1 December 1998.

## reg 3 Community work order

A community work order required to be drawn up under section 12 must be in the Form 1 in the Schedule.

## reg 4 Register of supervising officers

(1) There is established a Register of supervising officers.

(2) The Register must be kept and maintained by the Director of Social Welfare.

(3) A list of supervising officers and their addresses must be given to the Resident Magistrate of a court, Chief Magistrate and the Chief Registrar by the Director of Social Welfare.

## reg 5 Duties of supervising officer

(1) For the purpose of section 8 of the Act the supervising officer must, in relation to an offender referred to him or her for supervision, keep and maintain the following records—

(a) Suitability/personal information record in the Form 2 in the Schedule to include the following—

(i) address;

(ii) skills;

(iii) work preference;

(iv) health and medical state; and

(v) preferred time and day to perform community work.

(b) Job Card to record the following information—

(i) the sentence (total number of hours);

(ii) hours completed in each session;

(iii) supervisor's comments; and

(iv) other relevant matters including whether any counselling was done during the period of the sentence.

(c) Monthly Progress Report to record the progress of the offender which is to include a summary of the information recorded in the Job Card and any other matter of interest such as change of address and attitude and behaviour changes.

(d) Final Report in the Form 3 in the Schedule.

(2) The requirements in subregulation (1) must be submitted to the community-based corrections officer and the Court, in addition to any reports or information on the offender that the court may require from a supervising officer under section 8(c) of the Act.

[subreg (2) am Act 10 of 2018 s 21, effective 23 November 2022]

(3) A supervising officer is also vested with the duty of giving counsel to the offender under his or her charge.

## reg 6 Revocation of appointment of supervising officer

A supervising officer's appointment may be revoked for—

(a) neglect of duty;

(b) misconduct or unethical conduct.

## reg 7 Report on breach of the Act or condition of community work

For the purpose of monitoring the performance of an offender the supervising officer must report any breach of a community work order to a community-based corrections officer and to the Court in the Form 4 in the Schedule.

[reg 7 am Act 10 of 2018 s 21, effective 23 November 2022]
