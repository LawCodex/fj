# BURIAL (NARIKOSO) REGULATIONS 1929

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Burial (Narikoso) Regulations 1929.

## reg 2 Appointment of manager

The burial-ground at Narikoso (hereinafter referred to as “the cemetery”), licensed and proclaimed under the Act, as a private burial-ground, shall be under the care of a manager to be appointed by the Minister.

[s 2 am LN 112 of 1970 O 234, effective 8 October 1970]

## reg 3 Graves and vaults numbered

Places prepared for interment shall be either ordinary graves, not exceeding 9 feet by 4 feet, hereinafter called “graves”, or family vaults, hereinafter called “vaults”. Every grave and vault shall be distinguished by a separate number, to be recorded in the plan and register of burials hereinafter mentioned.

## reg 4 Depth of graves

The manager shall cause all graves to be dug, and each grave shall be dug to a depth of at least 5 feet with the exception of the graves of children under 10 years of age, which need not exceed 4 feet in depth. Vaults shall be constructed by the owners to the satisfaction of the manager.

## reg 5 One corpse only in each grave

One corpse only shall be placed in each grave, save and except in the cases following—

(a) in the case of the interment of children under 10 years of age more than one corpse may be interred at the same time in one grave provided the top of the uppermost coffin is at least 30 inches below the ground surface;

(b) more than one member of the same family may be interred at the same time in one grave provided the top of the uppermost coffin is at least 30 inches below the ground surface.

## reg 6 Graves

The manager shall have a discretion to authorise or refuse the opening of a grave for the purpose of a second or subsequent interment therein, provided that no such second or subsequent interment shall take place without the consent of the owner or of the parties having a present interest in such grave or vault.

## reg 7 Re-opening of graves

No grave shall be re-opened for the purpose of burying therein the body of a person until after the expiration of 8 years from the date of the burial in such grave of the body of a person whose age at the time of death did not exceed 20 years or until after the expiration of 14 years from the date of the burial in such grave of the body of a person whose age at the time of death exceeded 20 years.

## reg 8 Space between coffins

The space between 2 coffins (except when buried in a single grave as in regulation 5) at any point shall be at least 18 inches.

## reg 9 Approval of manager to burial in vault

No interment shall be permitted in any vault unless such vault shall have been first approved by the manager as a fit and proper place of interment.

## reg 10 Manager's consent necessary before interment

No interment shall take place in the cemetery without the knowledge and consent of the manager, or of the person who for the time being may be acting in his or her place. A person committing or being accessory to the commission of a breach of this regulation shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine not exceeding $4. In the allotment of every grave or vault there shall be delivered to the allottee, a certificate in Schedule 1 form signed by the manager, in which every grave or vault shall be distinguished by a separate number. A duplicate of every such certificate shall be filed by the manager in book form.

## reg 11 Register to be kept

It shall be the duty of the manager to keep a register of burials in Schedule 2 form, wherein each interment shall be distinguished consecutively by a separate number.

## reg 12 Plan to be kept

A plan of the cemetery approved by the Central Board of Health shall be kept by the manager, in which the position and number of every grave and vault and the registered number of every interment in every grave or vault shall be indicated.

## reg 13 Penalty for damage to monuments

Any person who shall unlawfully remove, or cause to be removed, or who shall wilfully injure, move, alter or destroy any monument, tablet, fence or other memorial or building, except for the purpose of repair or restoration, shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine not exceeding $4.

## reg 14 Manager or his or her deputy to be present at all funerals

The manager, or some person deputed by him or her for the purpose, shall be present at all funerals.

## reg 15 Certificate of burial

A certificate of burial in Schedule 3 form shall be furnished by the officiating minister, or by the manager, as the case may be, in all cases where the Registrar-General shall require him or her to do so.

## reg 16 Seizure of trespassing animals

The manager, or any person authorised by him or her, shall have power to seize any poundable animal found trespassing in the cemetery, and to impound the same in accordance with the provisions of the Pound Act 1877.
