# BURIAL (SUVA MILITARY CEMETERY) REGULATIONS 1950

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Burial (Suva Military Cemetery) Regulations 1950.

## reg 2 Application

These Regulations shall apply to the public burial-ground situated in the tikina of Suva and set apart and licensed as a public burial-ground and crematorium.

## reg 3 Name

The said burial-ground shall be known as “The Suva Military Cemetery”.

## reg 4 Control and management

The control and management of the Suva Military Cemetery shall be vested in the Commonwealth War Graves Commission.

[reg 4 am LN 75 of 1960 reg 2, effective 1 July 1960]

## reg 5 Use

The said burial-ground is hereby appropriated to the burial of members of His or Her Majesty’s Forces who died or were killed in the Second World War.For the purposes of these Regulations, the Second World War shall be deemed to have begun on 4 September 1939 and to have ended on 31 December 1947.

## reg 6 Graves

All graves shall be numbered and uniformly laid out in rows with a distance of 3 feet between every 2 adjacent rows. Each plot shall be 6.5 feet long and 3 feet wide with a distance of one foot between every 2 adjacent plots. Each grave shall be not less than 5 feet deep. There shall be a distance of not less than 1.5 feet between every 2 adjacent coffins.

## reg 7 Only one body

Not more than one body shall be buried in any one grave.

## reg 8 Levelling

All graves shall be levelled and planted with grass which shall be kept mown.

## reg 9 Flowers and monuments

No flowers or shrubs shall be planted on any grave except as may be directed by the Commonwealth War Graves Commission. No monument or marker shall be placed on any grave except as may be authorised by the Commonwealth War Graves Commission.

[reg 9 am LN 75 of 1960 reg 2, effective 1 July 1960]

## reg 10 Register

A register of graves shall be kept in which the name, sex and age of the person buried in each grave, and the date of such interment shall be duly registered.

## reg 11 Re-opening grave

No grave shall be re-opened without the permission in writing of both the Commonwealth War Graves Commission and the Medical Officer of Health at Suva, or by virtue of a warrant under the hand of a Magistrate, or by permission of the Minister pursuant to section 12 of the Act.

[reg 9 am LN 75 of 1960 reg 2, effective 1 July 1960; LN 112 of 1970 O 235, effective 8 October 1970]

## reg 12 Disorderly behaviour

No person shall by any violent, abusive or indecent language or behaviour cause any disturbance in any part of the said burial-ground.

## reg 13 Penalty

Any person who contravenes any of the provisions of regulations 7, 9, 11 and 12 shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $4 or in default to imprisonment not exceeding one month.
