## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Methylated Spirits Regulations 1983.

## reg 2 Form of licence

The form of licence to sell or store for sale methylated spirits shall be as in Form 1 in the Schedule.

## reg 3 Form of book

The form of book to be kept by every licensee upon his or her licensed premises shall be as in Form 2 in the Schedule.

## reg 4 Revocation

The Methylated Spirits Regulations are revoked.
