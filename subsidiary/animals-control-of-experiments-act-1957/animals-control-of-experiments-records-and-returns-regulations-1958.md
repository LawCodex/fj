# ANIMALS (CONTROL OF EXPERIMENTS) (RECORDS AND RETURNS) REGULATIONS 1958

## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals (Control of Experiments) (Records and Returns) Regulations 1958.

## reg 2 Records

Records of painful experiments required to be kept pursuant to the provisions of section 10(1) of the Animals (Control of Experiments) Act 1957, (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) shall be kept in the form contained in the Schedule.

## reg 3 Returns

Returns required to be rendered pursuant to the provisions of section 11 of the Act shall consist of an extract from the form referred to in regulation 2 covering the relevant period, and shall be rendered in respect of every year so as to reach the Minister not later than 31 January in the succeeding year.

[reg 3 am LN 112 of 1970 O 265, effective 8 October 1970]
