## reg 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Rivers and Streams (Fees) Regulations 1966.

## reg 2 Fees

The fees specified in the Schedule shall be charged by the Registrar of Titles or the Director, as the case may be, in respect of the several matters specified in the Schedule.
