## r 1 Short title

These Rules may be cited as the Bankruptcy (Fees) Rules 1969.

## r 2 Interpretation

In the Schedule any reference to a numbered rule or form is a reference to the rule or form so numbered in the Bankruptcy Rules 1915, as from time to time amended up to the commencement of the Act.

## r 3 Present scale of fees not to remain in force

The scale of fees prescribed under the Bankruptcy Act 1914 of the Imperial Parliament, shall forthwith be no longer in force in Fiji.

## r 4 Fees in High Court

The fees to be taken in the High Court Registry for and in respect of proceedings in bankruptcy shall be those set out in Part 1 of the Schedule and, where no fees are prescribed in Part 1, such of those fees set out in Part 1 of Appendix 2 of the High Court Rules as may be applicable, mutatis mutandis.

## r 5 Fees of Official Receiver

The fees to be taken and percentages to be charged by the Official Receiver for and in respect of proceedings in bankruptcy shall be those set out in Part 2 of the Schedule.
