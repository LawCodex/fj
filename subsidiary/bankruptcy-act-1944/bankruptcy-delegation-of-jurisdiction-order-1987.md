## O 1 Short title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Bankruptcy (Delegation of Jurisdiction) Order 1987, and shall come into force on 1 August 1987.

## O 2 Delegation of jurisdiction

All of the jurisdiction of the High Court in bankruptcy is delegated to the courts of a Resident Magistrate.

[O 2 subst LN 65 of 1994 O 2, effective 15 July 1994]

## O 3 Fees

The fees to be taken by the clerk of the court pursuant to the exercise by the court of a Resident Magistrate of the jurisdiction delegated to it by this Order shall be the same as those prescribed for the High Court Registry in terms of rule 4 of the Bankruptcy (Fees) Rules 1996.
