## O 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Telecommunications (Interconnection Levy) Order 2016.

(2) This Order is deemed to have come into force on 17 November 2010.

## O 2 Interconnection levy

For the purpose of regulation 3 of the Telecommunications (Trust Fund) Regulations 2016, a carrier is liable to pay to the Ministry the levy at the maximum rate specified by the Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission from time to time.
